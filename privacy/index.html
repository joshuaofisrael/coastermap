Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Coaster Map (coastermap.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across sites; it records basic, aggregated information such as the page visited, referrer, browser type and country.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, so we can reply. We use them only to respond and keep them only as long as needed.

Email sign-up

If you sign up for new-coaster alerts, we store your email address, the date and your consent in a database run on Cloudflare Workers. We use it only to send Coaster Map updates, never sell it, and delete it when you unsubscribe or ask us to. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Maps

Interactive maps load tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Games and tools

The height checker and quiz run in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us. The trivia game may save your best streak in your browser's local storage.

Advertising

To keep Coaster Map free, we show or may show ads served by Google (including Google AdSense) and other third-party advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu. Learn more in how Google uses cookies in advertising. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent through a certified consent management platform before any advertising cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Coaster Map itself sets no cookies for analytics. Advertising partners, if ads are shown, may set cookies or use similar identifiers as described above. The trivia game may store your best score in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you via the contact form. Residents of some US states and other countries may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The height checker does not collect or store a child's height.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
