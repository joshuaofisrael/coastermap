About Coaster Map

Coaster Map is an independent guide to the roller coasters at the world's big theme parks. Our goal is simple: one clear page for every coaster, answering the questions families and thrill seekers actually ask, such as the height requirement, how intense the ride is, and where to sit, plus a map of where each coaster sits in its park.

How we work

Height requirements come from official park pages. Ride statistics, opening dates, manufacturers and map positions come from the Roller Coaster DataBase (RCDB). We write every description ourselves. If a fact can't be verified, we leave it out. See sources and methods.

Independence

Coaster Map is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any park, operator or ride manufacturer. Park and ride names are used only to identify the rides we describe.
