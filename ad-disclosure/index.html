Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated 10 Nov 2026. Coaster Map is free to use. To pay for running it, we plan to show advertising, for example through Google AdSense or a managed ad network. At the moment the site shows no ads and contains no affiliate links.

When ads appear they will be clearly separate from our content and labelled as advertising. Advertisers will have no influence over our coaster descriptions, ratings or recommendations. If we ever add affiliate links (for example to tickets or hotels), we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you, and we will label those links.

How ads would be chosen

Ads from networks such as Google AdSense are picked automatically by the network, sometimes based on cookies, as explained in our privacy policy. We do not choose individual advertisers, and an ad appearing on a page is not an endorsement by Coaster Map. Ads will never be placed inside height tables or made to look like ride information.

Editorial independence

Stats come from RCDB and height rules from official park sources, as described on our sources and methods page. Intensity labels, seat tips and "who it suits" notes are our own editorial guidance, written the same way for every coaster whatever its park.

No park, operator or ride manufacturer pays for coverage on Coaster Map. Questions: use our contact form.
