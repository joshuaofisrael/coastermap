Roller coasters in Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 46 roller coasters at 7 parks in Pennsylvania. The tallest is Steel Curtain (Kennywood) at 220 ft (67 m) and the fastest is Phantom's Revenge (Kennywood) at 85 mph (137 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), on Trailblazer (Hersheypark).

Hersheypark has the most coasters of the Pennsylvania parks we cover, with 14. The coaster with the most inversions is Steel Curtain (Kennywood), with 8. 13 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
