Roller coasters in Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 67 roller coasters at 10 parks in Germany. The tallest is Silver Star (Europa-Park) at 239.5 ft (73 m) and the fastest is Silver Star (Europa-Park) at 78.9 mph (127 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on FirleFranz (Bayern-Park).

Europa-Park has the most coasters of the Germany parks we cover, with 14. The coaster with the most inversions is Voltron Nevera (Europa-Park), with 7. 4 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
