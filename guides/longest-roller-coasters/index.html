Longest roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The longest roller coaster on Coaster Map is Falcons Flight (Six Flags Qiddiya City) at 13943.6 ft (4,250 m). Below are the top 25 of the 790 coasters we cover that have a track length figure on RCDB, with each ride's height requirement so you can see who can ride.

Track length is the length of the circuit listed on RCDB. Long coasters are not always the longest rides; launches, brakes and lift speeds change the ride time. Records change slowly, but they do change when a park opens a new ride or a record holder closes, so check the date at the top of this guide.
