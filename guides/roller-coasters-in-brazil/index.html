Roller coasters in Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 7 roller coasters at 2 parks in Brazil. The tallest is Katapul (Hopi Hari) at 137.8 ft (42 m) and the fastest is Montezum (Hopi Hari) at 64 mph (103 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 80 cm (about 31.5 in), on Jacaré Dum-Dum (Beto Carrero World).

Hopi Hari has the most coasters of the Brazil parks we cover, with 4. The coaster with the most inversions is FireWhip (Beto Carrero World), with 5. 1 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
