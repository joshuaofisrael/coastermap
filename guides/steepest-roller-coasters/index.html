Steepest roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The steepest roller coaster on Coaster Map is Takabisha (Fuji-Q Highland) at 121°. Below are the top 25 of the 147 coasters we cover that have a drop angle figure on RCDB, with each ride's height requirement so you can see who can ride.

Drop angle is the steepest angle of descent listed on RCDB. Anything over 90 degrees is a beyond-vertical drop that curls back under the hill. Records change slowly, but they do change when a park opens a new ride or a record holder closes, so check the date at the top of this guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the steepest roller coaster on Coaster Map?

Takabisha at Fuji-Q Highland, with a drop angle of 121° according to RCDB, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Which of these coasters has the lowest height requirement?

Of the top 25, Cannibal at Lagoon has the lowest ride-alone minimum, 48 in (122 cm).

Is this every coaster in the world?

No. It ranks the 147 coasters with a drop angle figure at the 102 parks Coaster Map covers. RCDB lists records for every coaster worldwide.
