Gerstlauer roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Gerstlauer is a coaster manufacturer based in Münsterhausen, Germany. Coaster Map covers 78 Gerstlauer coasters at 56 parks, the tallest being Der Schwur des Kärnan (Hansa-Park) at 239.5 ft (73 m).

About Gerstlauer

Gerstlauer is a German manufacturer known for Euro-Fighter coasters with beyond-vertical drops, infinity coasters, spinning coasters and family coasters. RCDB lists Gerstlauer as based in Münsterhausen, Germany, in business since 1981.

Gerstlauer coasters by the numbers

  • Opened between 1998 and 2026.
  • Tallest: Der Schwur des Kärnan (Hansa-Park), 239.5 ft (73 m).
