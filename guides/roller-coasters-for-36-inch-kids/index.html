Roller coasters a 36-inch (91 cm) child can ride

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

A child who is 36 inches (91 cm) tall meets the ride-alone height on 42 of the 1047 coasters with a verified height rule on Coaster Map, across 36 parks. A further 91 can be ridden with a companion. The lists below are grouped by park.

Height rules are set by each park and the ride's manufacturer, based on how the restraint fits and the forces on the ride, not on age or bravery. A child exactly at the posted minimum can ride; a child even slightly under it will be turned away at the measuring post, so it is worth measuring at home in the shoes they will wear. Parks outside North America publish their rules in centimetres. We compare against the official centimetre figure, which is why a ride listed at 107 cm does not appear on the 42-inch list (42 inches is 106.7 cm).

At 36 inches the tallest coaster on the list is Cornwall Coaster (Hansa-Park) at 59.1 ft (18 m).

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters can a 36-inch child ride?

On Coaster Map, a 36-inch (91 cm) rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 42 coasters and can ride 91 more with a companion, based on official park rules last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Do kids get measured with shoes on?

Yes, parks measure guests as they arrive at the ride, normally in the shoes they are wearing. Thick soles or hairstyles that add height may be checked, and staff have the final say.

Can a child ride if they are just under the height?

No. The posted minimum is a safety limit tied to how the restraint fits, so ride staff do not make exceptions. Some family coasters offer a lower limit when riding with a companion; those are listed separately above.

What is the biggest coaster a 36-inch child can ride?

By height, Cornwall Coaster at Hansa-Park, which is 59.1 ft tall (RCDB) with a ride-alone minimum of 90 cm.
