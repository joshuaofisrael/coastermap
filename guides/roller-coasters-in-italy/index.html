Roller coasters in Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 31 roller coasters at 5 parks in Italy. The tallest is iSpeed (Mirabilandia) at 180.4 ft (55 m) and the fastest is iSpeed (Mirabilandia) at 68.4 mph (110 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on Gold Digger (Mirabilandia).
