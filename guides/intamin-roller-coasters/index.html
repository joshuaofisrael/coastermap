Intamin roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Intamin is a coaster manufacturer based in Schaan, Liechtenstein. Coaster Map covers 66 Intamin coasters at 48 parks, the tallest being Top Thrill 2 (Cedar Point) at 420 ft (128 m).

About Intamin

Intamin has built many record-breaking coasters, including hydraulic and LSM launch coasters, giga coasters and wooden coasters with prefabricated track. RCDB lists Intamin as based in Schaan, Liechtenstein, in business since 1967.

Intamin coasters by the numbers

  • 66 coasters on Coaster Map at 48 parks.
  • Opened between 1979 and 2026.
  • Tallest: Top Thrill 2 (Cedar Point), 420 ft (128 m).
  • Fastest: Top Thrill 2 (Cedar Point), 120 mph (193 km/h).
  • Most inversions: Fahrenheit (Hersheypark), 6.
  • Lowest height requirement: Wilderness Run (Cedar Point), 36 in (91 cm).
