Roller coasters in South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 17 roller coasters at 4 parks in South Korea. The tallest is Draken (Gyeongju World) at 206.7 ft (63 m) and the fastest is Giant Revolution (Lotte World Adventure Busan) at 68.4 mph (110 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 80 cm (about 31.5 in), on Tobot Train (Seoul Land).

Gyeongju World has the most coasters of the South Korea parks we cover, with 5. The coaster with the most inversions is Phaethon (Gyeongju World), with 6. None of them are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
