Tallest roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The tallest roller coaster on Coaster Map is Top Thrill 2 (Cedar Point) at 420 ft (128 m). Below are the top 25 of the 410 coasters we cover that have a height figure on RCDB, with each ride's height requirement so you can see who can ride.

Height is the coaster's highest point above the ground, as listed on RCDB. A coaster with a drop into a trench or tunnel can have a longer drop than its height. Records change slowly, but they do change when a park opens a new ride or a record holder closes, so check the date at the top of this guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the tallest roller coaster on Coaster Map?

Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, with a height of 420 ft (128 m) according to RCDB, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Of the top 25, Millennium Force at Cedar Point has the lowest ride-alone minimum, 48 in (122 cm).

Is this every coaster in the world?

No. It ranks the 410 coasters with a height figure at the 62 parks Coaster Map covers. RCDB lists records for every coaster worldwide.
