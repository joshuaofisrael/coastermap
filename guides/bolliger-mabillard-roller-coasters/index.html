About Bolliger & Mabillard (B&M)

Bolliger & Mabillard, usually called B&M, is a Swiss firm known for smooth steel coasters: inverted coasters, hypercoasters, dive coasters, wing coasters, floorless and flying coasters. RCDB lists Bolliger & Mabillard (B&M) as based in Monthey, Switzerland, in business since 1988.
