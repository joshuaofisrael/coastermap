What is a hybrid roller coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

A hybrid roller coaster combines a wooden-style structure with steel track, or replaces an old wooden track with a steel rail system such as I-Box or Topper track. RCDB tags these rides as hybrid.

What it feels like

Hybrids keep the out-of-control feel and airtime of a wooden coaster but run far smoother, which lets designers add steep drops, overbanked turns and even inversions that a traditional wooden track could not handle.

Who it suits

If you enjoy airtime but find classic wooden coasters too rough, a hybrid is a good next step.

Hybrid roller coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 52 hybrid roller coasters at 45 parks. Height requirements range from 42 in (107 cm) on Mini Mine Train (Six Flags Over Texas) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on El Diablo: Tren de la Mina (PortAventura Park). The tallest is Iron Gwazi (Busch Gardens Tampa Bay) at 206 ft (63 m) and the fastest is Iron Gwazi (Busch Gardens Tampa Bay) at 76 mph (122 km/h).
