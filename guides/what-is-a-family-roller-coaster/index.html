What is a family roller coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

A family roller coaster is designed so children and adults can ride together: a lower height requirement, moderate speeds and drops, and usually no inversions. RCDB classes each coaster on a scale of kiddie, family, thrill or extreme; our examples are those RCDB classes as family.

What it feels like

Family coasters range from gentle mine trains to fast launches with small inversions, so check the stats and height rule on each ride's page.

Who it suits

They are the best place for a first coaster and for mixed groups.

Family roller coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 354 family roller coasters at 168 parks. Height requirements range from 80 cm (about 31.5 in) on Jacaré Dum-Dum (Beto Carrero World) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on The Walking Dead: The Ride (Thorpe Park). The tallest is Luna (Liseberg) at 109.9 ft (33 m) and the fastest is Dragon Roller Coaster (Energylandia) at 47 mph (76 km/h).

Frequently asked questions

What is a family roller coaster?

A family roller coaster is designed so children and adults can ride together: a lower height requirement, moderate speeds and drops, and usually no inversions. RCDB classes each coaster on a scale of kiddie, family, thrill or extreme; our examples are those RCDB classes as family.

What does a family roller coaster feel like?

Family coasters range from gentle mine trains to fast launches with small inversions, so check the stats and height rule on each ride's page.

What is the lowest height requirement for a family roller coaster?

Of the family roller coasters we cover, Jacaré Dum-Dum at Beto Carrero World has the lowest ride-alone minimum, 80 cm (about 31.5 in).
