Mack Rides roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Mack Rides is a coaster manufacturer based in Waldkirch, Germany. Coaster Map covers 83 Mack Rides coasters at 52 parks, the tallest being Hyperia (Thorpe Park) at 236 ft (72 m).

About Mack Rides

Mack Rides is a German family company known for launched coasters, mega coasters, family coasters, wild mouse rides and water coasters. RCDB lists Mack Rides as based in Waldkirch, Germany, in business since 1780.

Mack Rides coasters by the numbers

  • 83 coasters on Coaster Map at 52 parks.
  • Opened between 1985 and 2026.
  • Tallest: Hyperia (Thorpe Park), 236 ft (72 m).
  • Fastest: Hyperia (Thorpe Park), 80 mph (129 km/h).
  • Most inversions: Helix (Liseberg), 7.
  • Lowest height requirement: Slinky Dog Dash (Disney's Hollywood Studios), 38 in (97 cm).
