How a coaster gets built

A park usually chooses a manufacturer and works with its engineers on a layout that fits the site, budget and target riders. Some firms sell standard models, such as Vekoma's Boomerang or junior coasters, which are built many times; others mostly build custom layouts. The manufacturer designs the track, trains and control system, and the ride is assembled on site on new foundations. Rides are tested, often with weighted dummies in the seats, before riders board.

Frequently asked questions

Who makes the most roller coasters?

Among the coasters on Coaster Map, Vekoma leads with 139, followed by Bolliger & Mabillard with 97 and Intamin with 83, as of 10 Nov 2026.

What are the main roller coaster manufacturers?

Bolliger & Mabillard, Intamin, Vekoma, Mack Rides, Gerstlauer, Rocky Mountain Construction and Premier Rides are among the best-known active manufacturers; Arrow Dynamics built many classic coasters and is listed by RCDB as defunct.

Where are B&M roller coasters made?

RCDB lists Bolliger & Mabillard as based in Monthey, Switzerland.
