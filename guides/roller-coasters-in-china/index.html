Roller coasters in China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 12 roller coasters at 3 parks in China. The tallest is Circus Roller Coaster (Chimelong Paradise) at 196.8 ft (60 m) and the fastest is Circus Roller Coaster (Chimelong Paradise) at 69.6 mph (112 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 97 cm (about 38.2 in), on Seven Dwarfs Mine Train (Shanghai Disneyland).

Chimelong Ocean Kingdom has the most coasters of the China parks we cover, with 5. The coaster with the most inversions is Parrot Coaster (Chimelong Ocean Kingdom), with 3. None of them are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
