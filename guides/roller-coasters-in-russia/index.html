Roller coasters in Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 20 roller coasters at 4 parks in Russia. The tallest is Serpent Dragon (Sochi Park) at 124.7 ft (38 m) and the fastest is Nesterov Loop (Divo Ostrov) at 62.1 mph (100 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), on Whirl Wind Looping Coaster (Divo Ostrov).

Skazka Family Park has the most coasters of the Russia parks we cover, with 7. The coaster with the most inversions is Nesterov Loop (Divo Ostrov), with 4. None of them are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
