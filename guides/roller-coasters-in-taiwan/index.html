Roller coasters in Taiwan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 10 roller coasters at 3 parks in Taiwan. The tallest is Big Air (E-DA Theme Park) at 209.3 ft (64 m) and the fastest is Screaming Condor (Leofoo Village Theme Park) at 70 mph (113 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on Little Rattler (Leofoo Village Theme Park).

E-DA Theme Park has the most coasters of the Taiwan parks we cover, with 4. The coaster with the most inversions is Insane Speed (Janfusun Fancyworld), with 4. None of them are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
