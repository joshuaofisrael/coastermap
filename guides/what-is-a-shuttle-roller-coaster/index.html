What is a shuttle roller coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

A shuttle roller coaster does not complete a full circuit. The train runs forwards through the course and then back through it in reverse, as on a Boomerang, or launches up a tower and falls back. RCDB tags these as shuttle coasters.

What it feels like

Riding the same elements backwards is disorienting because you cannot see what is coming. Shuttle coasters are usually short but intense.

Who it suits

Riders who dislike surprises may prefer a conventional circuit coaster.

Shuttle roller coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 49 shuttle roller coasters at 39 parks. Height requirements range from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Raik (Phantasialand) to 56 in (142 cm) on The Flash: Vertical Velocity (Six Flags Great Adventure). The tallest is Mr. Freeze (Six Flags Over Texas) at 218 ft (66 m) and the fastest is The Flash: Vertical Velocity (Six Flags Great America) at 70 mph (113 km/h).
