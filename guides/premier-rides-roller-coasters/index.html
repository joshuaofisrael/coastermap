Premier Rides roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Premier Rides is a coaster manufacturer based in Baltimore, Maryland, United States. Coaster Map covers 25 Premier Rides coasters at 20 parks, the tallest being Mr. Freeze (Six Flags Over Texas) at 218 ft (66 m).

About Premier Rides

Premier Rides is known for LIM and LSM launch coasters, including indoor launched coasters and Sky Rocket designs. RCDB lists Premier Rides as based in Baltimore, Maryland, United States, in business since 1994.

Premier Rides coasters by the numbers

  • 25 coasters on Coaster Map at 20 parks.
  • Opened between 1996 and 2025.
  • Tallest: Mr. Freeze (Six Flags Over Texas), 218 ft (66 m).
  • Fastest: AlpenFury (Canada's Wonderland), 71.5 mph (115 km/h).
  • Most inversions: AlpenFury (Canada's Wonderland), 7.
  • Lowest height requirement: Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness (Silver Dollar City), 48 in (122 cm).

Most common models
