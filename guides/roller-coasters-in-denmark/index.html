Roller coasters in Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 38 roller coasters at 7 parks in Denmark. The tallest is Fønix (Fårup Sommerland) at 131.2 ft (40 m) and the fastest is Fønix (Fårup Sommerland) at 59 mph (95 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 105 cm (about 41.3 in), on Mine-Expressen (Fårup Sommerland).

Djurs Sommerland has the most coasters of the Denmark parks we cover, with 8. The coaster with the most inversions is Dæmonen (Tivoli Gardens), with 3. 3 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
