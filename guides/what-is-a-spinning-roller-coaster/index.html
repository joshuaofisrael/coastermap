What is a spinning roller coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

On a spinning roller coaster each car can rotate freely on its own axis as it travels along the track, so riders spin depending on the weight in the car and the forces in each turn. RCDB tags these as having spinning cars.

What it feels like

No two rides feel quite the same: a car loaded unevenly spins more. Spinning coasters are usually compact with tight turns and small drops.

Who it suits

They suit families and riders who enjoy spinning rides; those who get motion sick should be cautious.

Spinning roller coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 46 spinning roller coasters at 41 parks. Height requirements range from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Cétautomatix (Parc Astérix) to 59 in (150 cm) on Exterminator (Kennywood). The tallest is Steel Taipan (Dreamworld) at 128 ft (39 m) and the fastest is Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift (Universal Studios Hollywood) at 72 mph (116 km/h).
