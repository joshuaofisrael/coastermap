What is a wild mouse coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

A wild mouse coaster uses small, single cars on a compact track with sharp, unbanked hairpin turns and short steep drops. RCDB tags these rides as wild mouse.

What it feels like

Because the cars hang out over the track before turning, riders feel as though the car will run off the edge at each corner. The drops are short but sudden.

Who it suits

A classic family-to-thrill step up: no inversions, but more jolting than most family coasters.

Wild mouse coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 79 wild mouse coasters at 77 parks. Height requirements range from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Gold Digger (Mirabilandia) to 150 cm (about 59.1 in) on Flagermusen (Fårup Sommerland). The tallest is Mad Mouse (Michigan's Adventure) at 68 ft (21 m) and the fastest is Apple Zapple (Kings Dominion) at 35 mph (56 km/h).
