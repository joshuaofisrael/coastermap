Vekoma roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Vekoma is a coaster manufacturer based in Vlodrop, Netherlands. Coaster Map covers 167 Vekoma coasters at 90 parks, the tallest being Iron Rattler (Six Flags Qiddiya City) at 208 ft (63 m).

About Vekoma

Vekoma is one of the most prolific coaster builders, known for family coasters, the Boomerang shuttle and the Suspended Looping Coaster (SLC), as well as launched and family boomerang designs. RCDB lists Vekoma as based in Vlodrop, Netherlands, in business since 1926.

Vekoma coasters by the numbers

  • 167 coasters on Coaster Map at 90 parks.
  • Opened between 1981 and 2026.
  • Tallest: Iron Rattler (Six Flags Qiddiya City), 208 ft (63 m).
  • Fastest: Iron Rattler (Six Flags Qiddiya City), 73.3 mph (118 km/h).
  • Most inversions: Goudurix (Parc Astérix), 7.
  • Lowest height requirement: The Barnstormer (Magic Kingdom), 35 in (89 cm).
