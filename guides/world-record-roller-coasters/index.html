World record roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: the fastest and tallest roller coaster in the world is Falcons Flight at Six Flags Qiddiya City in Saudi Arabia: 155.3 mph and 534.8 ft, according to RCDB (last verified 10 Nov 2026). In the US, Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point leads at 120 mph and 420 ft.

These tables copy RCDB's Record Holders rankings for operating roller coasters, last verified 10 Nov 2026. RCDB does not assign ranks to coasters that are not operating, so closed rides and rides not yet open are left out. Records change when new rides open, so check the date above. Where we cover a coaster, its name links to our page with its height requirement.

Other world records on RCDB

  • Longest: Falcons Flight at Six Flags Qiddiya City, 13,943.6 ft (4,250 m), ahead of Steel Dragon 2000 at 8,133.2 ft and The Beast at Kings Island at 7,361 ft.
  • Biggest drop: Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, 400 ft (122 m), ahead of Red Force at 345 ft.
  • Most inversions: The Smiler at Alton Towers, 14.
  • Steepest: TMNT Shellraiser at Nickelodeon Universe in New Jersey, 121.5 degrees, then Takabisha at Fuji-Q Highland at 121 degrees and Green Lantern Coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World at 120.5 degrees.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest roller coaster in the world?

Falcons Flight at Six Flags Qiddiya City in Saudi Arabia, at 155.3 mph (250 km/h), according to RCDB's record holders list for operating coasters, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster in the world?

Falcons Flight at Six Flags Qiddiya City, at 534.8 ft (163 m), according to RCDB, last verified 10 Nov 2026. Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point is second at 420 ft.

What is the fastest roller coaster in the US?

Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Ohio, at 120 mph, according to RCDB's record holders list, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Which roller coaster has the most inversions?

The Smiler at Alton Towers, with 14, according to RCDB, last verified 10 Nov 2026.
