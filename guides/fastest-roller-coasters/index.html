Fastest roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The fastest roller coaster on Coaster Map is Falcons Flight (Six Flags Qiddiya City) at 155.3 mph (250 km/h). Below are the top 25 of the 887 coasters we cover that have a top speed figure on RCDB, with each ride's height requirement so you can see who can ride.

Top speed is the maximum speed listed on RCDB, usually reached at the bottom of the first drop or the end of a launch. Records change slowly, but they do change when a park opens a new ride or a record holder closes, so check the date at the top of this guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest roller coaster on Coaster Map?

Falcons Flight at Six Flags Qiddiya City, with a top speed of 155.3 mph (250 km/h) according to RCDB, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Which of these coasters has the lowest height requirement?

Of the top 25, Millennium Force at Cedar Point has the lowest ride-alone minimum, 48 in (122 cm).

Is this every coaster in the world?

No. It ranks the 887 coasters with a top speed figure at the 183 parks Coaster Map covers. RCDB lists records for every coaster worldwide.
