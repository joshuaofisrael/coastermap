Roller coasters in Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 16 roller coasters at 4 parks in Japan. The tallest is Fujiyama (Fuji-Q Highland) at 259.2 ft (79 m) and the fastest is Fujiyama (Fuji-Q Highland) at 80.8 mph (130 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on Gadget's Go Coaster (Tokyo Disneyland).
