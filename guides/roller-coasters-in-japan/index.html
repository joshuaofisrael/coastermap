Roller coasters in Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 98 roller coasters at 20 parks in Japan. The tallest is Steel Dragon 2000 (Nagashima Spa Land) at 318.2 ft (97 m) and the fastest is Steel Dragon 2000 (Nagashima Spa Land) at 95 mph (153 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on Gadget's Go Coaster (Tokyo Disneyland).

Nagashima Spa Land has the most coasters of the Japan parks we cover, with 11. The coaster with the most inversions is Takabisha (Fuji-Q Highland), with 7. 2 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
