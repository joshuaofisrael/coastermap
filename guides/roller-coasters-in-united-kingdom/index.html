Roller coasters in the United Kingdom

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 58 roller coasters at 9 parks in the United Kingdom. The tallest is Hyperia (Thorpe Park) at 236 ft (72 m) and the fastest is Stealth (Thorpe Park) at 80 mph (129 km/h). The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), on DUPLO Dino Coaster (Legoland Windsor).

Alton Towers has the most coasters of the the United Kingdom parks we cover, with 11. The coaster with the most inversions is Infusion (Blackpool Pleasure Beach), with 5. 4 of the coasters are wooden. Use the park guides for maps, the full height lists and where to sit on each ride.
