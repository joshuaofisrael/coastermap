Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC) roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC) is a coaster manufacturer based in Hayden, Idaho, United States. Coaster Map covers 28 Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC) coasters at 24 parks, the tallest being Iron Gwazi (Busch Gardens Tampa Bay) at 206 ft (63 m).

About Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC)

Rocky Mountain Construction, listed on RCDB as Rocky Mountain Coasters, is best known for I-Box steel track that turns wooden coasters into hybrids, and for Raptor single-rail coasters. Its rides are known for airtime. RCDB lists Rocky Mountain Construction (RMC) as based in Hayden, Idaho, United States, in business since 2001.
