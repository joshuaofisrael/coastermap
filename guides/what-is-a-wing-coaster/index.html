What is a wing coaster?

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

On a wing coaster the seats sit on either side of the track, like the wings of a plane, with nothing above or below the riders. RCDB tags these as wing coasters.

What it feels like

Wing seats give a feeling of flying beside the track, and the slow rolls and near-miss elements, such as passing close to a building or rock, make the most of it. The outer seats feel the most exposed.

Who it suits

Wing coasters usually feel smooth and graceful rather than violent, though most still go upside down.

Wing coasters by the numbers

Coaster Map covers 23 wing coasters at 21 parks. Height requirements range from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Mandrill Mayhem (Chessington World of Adventures) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on The Swarm (Thorpe Park). The tallest is Eejanaika (Fuji-Q Highland) at 249.3 ft (76 m) and the fastest is Furius Baco (PortAventura Park) at 83.9 mph (135 km/h).
