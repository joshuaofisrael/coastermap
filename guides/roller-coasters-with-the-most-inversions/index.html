Roller coasters with the most inversions

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The coaster with the most inversions on Coaster Map is Dragon Khan (PortAventura Park) at 8. Below are the top 25 of the 162 coasters we cover that have a inversions figure on RCDB, with each ride's height requirement so you can see who can ride.

An inversion is any point where riders turn upside down, such as a loop, corkscrew, cobra roll or zero-g roll. The count is from RCDB. Records change slowly, but they do change when a park opens a new ride or a record holder closes, so check the date at the top of this guide.
