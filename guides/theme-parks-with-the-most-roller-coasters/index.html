Theme parks with the most roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Of the 256 theme parks on Coaster Map, Energylandia has the most operating roller coasters, with 19, going by RCDB's list of operating coasters at each park. The table ranks the 30 parks with the most coasters and shows how many of each park's coasters a 48-inch (122 cm) child can ride alone under the park's official height rules.

Counts include kiddie and family coasters as well as the big thrill rides, because RCDB lists every roller coaster a park operates. A park with many small coasters can therefore outrank one with fewer, bigger rides. Of the top 30, Canada's Wonderland has the most coasters a 48-inch rider can ride alone (12); the park pages list every coaster with its height rule.

Parks with the same number of coasters share a rank. "Ride alone at 48 in" counts coasters whose official ride-alone minimum is 48 inches (122 cm) or less, excluding rides with a maximum below that height. "Tallest by listed height" uses the structure height RCDB gives for each coaster; coasters without a listed height (such as some rides built into the ground) are left out of that column.

Frequently asked questions

Which theme park has the most roller coasters?

Of the 256 parks Coaster Map covers, Energylandia has the most, with 19 operating roller coasters on RCDB's list, last verified 10 Nov 2026.

Do kiddie coasters count?

Yes. RCDB lists every roller coaster a park operates, from kiddie coasters to giga coasters, and the counts here follow RCDB. Water coasters and alpine coasters count where RCDB lists them as roller coasters.

Which park in Europe has the most roller coasters?

Of the European parks we cover, Energylandia, with 19 operating coasters.

Which park in Asia has the most roller coasters?

Of the Asian parks we cover, Nagashima Spa Land, with 11 operating coasters.

How many roller coasters does Coaster Map cover?

1,410 operating roller coasters at 256 theme parks, each with its own page, RCDB statistics and the park's official height rule where the park publishes one.
