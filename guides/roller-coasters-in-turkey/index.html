Roller coasters in Turkey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Coaster Map covers 7 roller coasters at 2 parks in Turkey. The tallest is Hyper Coaster (The Land of Legends) at 200.2 ft (61 m) and the fastest is Hyper Coaster (The Land of Legends) at 71.5 mph (115 km/h).
