Arrow Dynamics roller coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Quick answer: Arrow Dynamics is a coaster manufacturer based in Clearfield, Utah, United States. Coaster Map covers 56 Arrow Dynamics coasters at 38 parks, the tallest being The Big One (Blackpool Pleasure Beach) at 213 ft (65 m).

About Arrow Dynamics

Arrow Dynamics built many classic mine trains, corkscrews and looping coasters on tubular steel track. RCDB lists the company as defunct. RCDB lists Arrow Dynamics as based in Clearfield, Utah, United States, operating from 1945 to 2002.

Arrow Dynamics coasters by the numbers

  • 56 coasters on Coaster Map at 38 parks.
  • Opened between 1959 and 2012.
  • Tallest: The Big One (Blackpool Pleasure Beach), 213 ft (65 m).
  • Fastest: Phantom's Revenge (Kennywood), 85 mph (137 km/h).
  • Most inversions: Viper (Six Flags Magic Mountain), 7.
  • Lowest height requirement: Trailblazer (Hersheypark), 36 in (91 cm).
