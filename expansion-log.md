## Height rules omitted (unverified)

- Dorney Park, Woodstock Express: official page lists only 'Min Height Accompanied 42 in' and no ride-alone height.
- Kennywood, Lil' Phantom: official page says no minimum, riders under 36 in need a companion; the site's model needs a ride-alone minimum, so omitted rather than stating a 36 in minimum.
- Lake Compounce, Kiddie Coaster: official page says no minimum, max 56 in; omitted for the same reason.
- Silver Dollar City, Thunderation: no height on the official page (final 2026 season).
- Pre-existing: Great American Revolution, Canyon Cruiser (Magic Mountain), Pteranodon Flyers (Islands of Adventure).
