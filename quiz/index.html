How the quiz picks your coasters

The quiz works through the same verified data as the rest of Coaster Map. First it removes every coaster your height does not meet, using each park's official minimum (and any maximum). Then it matches your answer on bravery to our intensity labels, which are worked out from each ride's RCDB height, speed, inversions and type, the same way for every park. If you say no to going upside down, any coaster with an inversion is dropped.

The results are a starting point, not a promise. Two rides with the same numbers can feel very different, so read the "How intense is it?" and "Who is it for?" notes on each coaster page before you queue.

Frequently asked questions

Is the quiz based on real ride rules?

Yes. It only uses height rules from official park sources and stats from RCDB. Coasters without a verified height rule are left out of the matches.

Why didn't my favourite coaster come up?

It may be outside the thrill level you chose, have an inversion when you said no to going upside down, or have no verified height rule on Coaster Map yet.

Where do the trivia heights come from?

From RCDB, the Roller Coaster DataBase. Height is the coaster's highest point above the ground.
