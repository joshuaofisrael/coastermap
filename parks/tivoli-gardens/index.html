Tivoli Gardens height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Copenhagen, Denmark · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen, Denmark has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Kamelen to 132 cm (about 52 in) on Dæmonen. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Tivoli Gardens are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Tivoli Gardens a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Rutschebanen).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rutschebanen, which opened in 1914.

Tivoli Gardens publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Tivoli Gardens? By height

At Tivoli Gardens the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Tivoli Gardens for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Tivoli Gardens, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Kamelen, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. The big one: Dæmonen, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 91.9 ft tall, 48 mph, 3 inversions, 4 g).
