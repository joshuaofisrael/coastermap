Dæmonen

Tivoli Gardens · Copenhagen, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 48 mph; 91.9 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2004.

Dæmonen is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen, Denmark, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 16 Apr 2004. The ride stands 91.9 ft tall, drops 65.6 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 1850.4 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Dæmonen is like

Dæmonen pairs a 91.9 ft structure with 1,850.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,850.4 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Tivoli Gardens.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dæmonen sits near the middle of Tivoli's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kamelen, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mælkevejen at about 140 m. Dæmonen layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Dæmonen's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Immelmann
  4. Zero-G Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Dæmonen

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Tivoli Gardens app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dæmonen?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Dæmonen, according to Tivoli Gardens, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dæmonen?

Dæmonen is 91.9 ft tall, with a drop of 65.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dæmonen go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dæmonen go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Dæmonen scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dæmonen open?

Dæmonen opened on 16 Apr 2004.

Who built Dæmonen?

Dæmonen was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Dæmonen?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 46 seconds over 1850.4 ft of track.

How does Dæmonen get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dæmonen at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Dæmonen good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 48 mph; the minimum height is 132 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
