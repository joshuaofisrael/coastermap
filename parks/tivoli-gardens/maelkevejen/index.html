Mælkevejen

Tivoli Gardens · Copenhagen, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 22.4 mph; 50.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Mælkevejen is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen, Denmark, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 28 Nov 2019. The ride stands 50.2 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1025.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mælkevejen is like

At 50.2 ft tall with 1,025.4 ft of track, Mælkevejen balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 1.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Tivoli Gardens.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mælkevejen sits on the south-west side of Tivoli's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rutschebanen, roughly 110 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dæmonen at about 140 m.
