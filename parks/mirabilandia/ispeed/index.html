iSpeed

Mirabilandia · Ravenna, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 68.4 mph; 180.4 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2009.

iSpeed is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mirabilandia in Ravenna, Italy, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 20 May 2009. The ride stands 180.4 ft tall, reaches 68.4 mph and runs 3215.2 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding iSpeed is like

iSpeed pairs a 180.4 ft structure with 3,215.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 3,215.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2009, it is the 5th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Mirabilandia.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Mirabilandia, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, iSpeed sits on the south-west side of Mirabilandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cowabunga Carts, roughly 70 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is PAW Patrol To The Rescue at about 130 m. iSpeed layout, trains and ride system

iSpeed starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How iSpeed compares

On our intensity ranking iSpeed is number 2 of 9 at Mirabilandia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride iSpeed

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Mirabilandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for iSpeed?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride iSpeed. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Mirabilandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is iSpeed?

iSpeed is 180.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does iSpeed go?

Its top speed is 68.4 mph (110 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does iSpeed go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is iSpeed scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did iSpeed open?

iSpeed opened on 20 May 2009.

Who built iSpeed?

iSpeed was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on iSpeed?

RCDB lists 3215.2 ft of track.

Does iSpeed launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride iSpeed at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is iSpeed good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 68.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
