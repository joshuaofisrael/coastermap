Rexplorer

Mirabilandia · Ravenna, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); top speed 21.8 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

Rexplorer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mirabilandia in Ravenna, Italy, built by S.D.C. It first opened in 1992 and was previously known as Explorer. The ride reaches 21.8 mph and runs 820.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rexplorer is like

Opened in 1992, it is the oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Mirabilandia. It has run under other names (Explorer), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Mirabilandia (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rexplorer sits on the north-east side of Mirabilandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gold Digger, roughly 100 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Desmo Race at about 180 m. Before you ride Rexplorer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 90 cm (about 35.4 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park requires an adult companion for riders between 3 and 10 years old.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Mirabilandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rexplorer?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Rexplorer. The park requires an adult companion for riders between 3 and 10 years old, according to Mirabilandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Rexplorer go?

Its top speed is 21.8 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rexplorer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rexplorer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Rexplorer open?

Rexplorer opened in 1992 (previously known as Explorer).

Who built Rexplorer?

Rexplorer was manufactured by S.D.C.

How long is the ride on Rexplorer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 820.3 ft of track.

How many people ride Rexplorer at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Rexplorer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.8 mph; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
