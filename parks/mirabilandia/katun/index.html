Katun

Mirabilandia · Ravenna, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 64.6 mph; 164 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2000.

Katun is a steel inverted roller coaster at Mirabilandia in Ravenna, Italy, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 1 Apr 2000. The ride stands 164 ft tall, drops 147.6 ft, reaches 64.6 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding Katun is like

Katun pairs a 164 ft structure with 3,937 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 6 times across 3,937 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Mirabilandia.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Mirabilandia, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Katun sits on the east side of Mirabilandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gold Digger, roughly 140 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rexplorer at about 230 m. Katun layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 7 elements RCDB records on Katun's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Zero-G Roll
  4. Cobra Roll
  5. Block Brake
  6. Corkscrew ×2

RCDB counts 6 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Katun

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Mirabilandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Katun?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Katun, according to Mirabilandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Katun?

Katun is 164 ft tall, with a drop of 147.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Katun go?

Its top speed is 64.6 mph (104 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Katun go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Katun scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Katun open?

Katun opened on 1 Apr 2000.

Who built Katun?

Katun was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Katun?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 22 seconds over 3,937 ft of track.

How does Katun get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Katun at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,700 riders.

Is Katun good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 64.6 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
