Mirabilandia height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ravenna, Italy · 9 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Mirabilandia in Ravenna, Italy has 9 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Gold Digger to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Katun. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 4, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Mirabilandia are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Mirabilandia a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 9 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 9 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (iSpeed).

The most common builder is L&T Systems, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rexplorer, which opened in 1992.

Mirabilandia publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Mirabilandia? By height

At Mirabilandia the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 9 coasters with a verified rule: Gold Digger, Rexplorer, PAW Patrol To The Rescue and Cowabunga Carts. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Master Thai at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 9 coasters with a verified rule: Gold Digger, Rexplorer, PAW Patrol To The Rescue and Cowabunga Carts. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Master Thai at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 9 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Master Thai and Desmo Race. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Divertical at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 9 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Divertical. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Katun at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: iSpeed (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Desmo Race (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Mirabilandia for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Mirabilandia, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: PAW Patrol To The Rescue, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 19.7 ft tall, 21.3 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Master Thai, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Katun, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 164 ft tall, 64.6 mph, 6 inversions, 4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
