Gold Digger

Mirabilandia · Ravenna, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); 47.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Gold Digger is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mirabilandia in Ravenna, Italy, built by L&T Systems. It first opened in 1998 and was previously known as Wild Mouse and later Pakal. The ride stands 47.6 ft tall and runs 1183.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Digger is like

At 47.6 ft tall with 1,183.4 ft of track, Gold Digger balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 2nd oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Mirabilandia. It has run under other names (Wild Mouse, then Pakal), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Mirabilandia (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Digger sits on the east side of Mirabilandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rexplorer, roughly 100 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Katun at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Gold Digger compares

On our intensity ranking Gold Digger is number 5 of 9 at Mirabilandia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Master Thai (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Desmo Race (72.2 ft tall, 49.7 mph, no inversions, 1.2 g; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Gold Digger

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 90 cm (about 35.4 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Digger?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Gold Digger. The park requires an adult companion for riders between 3 and 10 years old, according to Mirabilandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gold Digger?

Gold Digger is 47.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Gold Digger go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Digger scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Gold Digger open?

Gold Digger opened in 1998 (previously known as Wild Mouse and later Pakal).

Who built Gold Digger?

Gold Digger was manufactured by L&T Systems.

How long is the ride on Gold Digger?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 25 seconds over 1183.4 ft of track.

How many people ride Gold Digger at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Gold Digger good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 90 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
