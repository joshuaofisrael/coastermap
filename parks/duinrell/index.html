Duinrell height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Wassenaar, Netherlands · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Duinrell in Wassenaar, Netherlands has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Dragonfly to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Falcon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Duinrell are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Duinrell a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Falcon, which opened in 2009.

Duinrell publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
