Space Twister

Doha Quest · Doha, Qatar

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Space Twister is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Doha Quest in Doha, Qatar, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 1 Jul 2021. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Space Twister is like

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Doha Quest. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Krazy Koaster at Silverwood, Whirlwind at Waldameer, Spin-O-Rama at Cliff's and Texas Twister at Julianatoren. Height rules for this model differ by park: Doha Quest sets 130 cm, while Silverwood sets 48 in, Waldameer sets 48 in and Cliff's sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Doha Quest, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Space Twister layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 190 cm (about 74.8 in).

Before you ride Space Twister

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Space Twister?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Space Twister, or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 190 cm (about 74.8 in), according to Doha Quest, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Space Twister go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Space Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Space Twister open?

Space Twister opened on 1 Jul 2021.

Who built Space Twister?

Space Twister was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Space Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Space Twister at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Space Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Space Twister at Doha Quest the same as Krazy Koaster at Silverwood?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A coasters on RCDB; the Doha Quest ride opened in 2021 and the Silverwood ride in 2014; Doha Quest sets 130 cm and Silverwood sets 48 in.
