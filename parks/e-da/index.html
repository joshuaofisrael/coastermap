E-DA Theme Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Dashu, Kaohsiung, Taiwan · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

E-DA Theme Park in Dashu, Kaohsiung, Taiwan has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Dark Ride to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Big Air. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at E-DA Theme Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes E-DA Theme Park a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 232 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Roof Junior Coaster, which opened in 2010.

E-DA Theme Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at E-DA Theme Park? By height

At E-DA Theme Park the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at E-DA Theme Park

These are the rules E-DA Theme Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Dark Ride: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Roof Junior Coaster: riders 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at E-DA Theme Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for E-DA Theme Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dark Ride, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 49.3 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Flume Ride, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 98.4 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Big Air, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 209.3 ft tall, 67.1 mph, no inversions, 3.5 g).
