Roof Junior Coaster

E-DA Theme Park · Dashu, Kaohsiung, Taiwan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 49.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Roof Junior Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at E-DA Theme Park in Dashu, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, built by Fabbri. It opened in 2010. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Roof Junior Coaster is like

RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2010, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at E-DA Theme Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Fabbri's Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mad Mouse at Tosselilla. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at E-DA, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Roof Junior Coaster sits near the middle of E-DA's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dark Ride, roughly 40 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flume Ride at about 110 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Roof Junior Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as 旋轉飛車 (RCDB: 雲霄旋轉飛車); 120 to 130 cm may ride with a parent.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the E-DA Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Roof Junior Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Roof Junior Coaster, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as 旋轉飛車 (RCDB: 雲霄旋轉飛車); 120 to 130 cm may ride with a parent, according to E-DA Theme Park, official facility page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Roof Junior Coaster?

Roof Junior Coaster is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Roof Junior Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Roof Junior Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Roof Junior Coaster open?

Roof Junior Coaster opened in 2010.

Who built Roof Junior Coaster?

Roof Junior Coaster was manufactured by Fabbri.

How many people ride Roof Junior Coaster at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Roof Junior Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Roof Junior Coaster at E-DA the same as Mad Mouse at Tosselilla?

Both are Fabbri Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse coasters on RCDB; the E-DA ride opened in 2010 and the Tosselilla ride in 2009; both list a 130 cm minimum.
