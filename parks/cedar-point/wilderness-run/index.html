Wilderness Run

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); top speed 6 mph; 19 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1979.

Wilderness Run is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened in 1979 and was previously known as Jr. Gemini. The ride stands 19 ft tall, reaches 6 mph and runs 443 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wilderness Run is like

Wilderness Run pairs a 19 ft structure with 443 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1979, it is the 5th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point. It has run under other names (Jr. Gemini), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Children's Roller Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at California's Great America. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cedar Point sets 36 in, while California's Great America sets 46 in.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Cedar Point, which makes it a natural first coaster there.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wilderness Run sits on the north-west side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock Express, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gemini at about 70 m. How intense is it?

Gentle (kiddie)

RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster; Cedar Point rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall, and no taller than 54 in (137 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wilderness Run?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Wilderness Run. The maximum rider height is 54 in (137 cm), according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wilderness Run?

Wilderness Run is 19 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wilderness Run go?

Its top speed is 6 mph (10 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wilderness Run go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wilderness Run scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster; Cedar Point rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is one of the gentlest coasters you can ride.

When did Wilderness Run open?

Wilderness Run opened in 1979 (previously known as Jr. Gemini).

Who built Wilderness Run?

Wilderness Run was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Wilderness Run?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 443 ft of track.

How does Wilderness Run get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wilderness Run at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Wilderness Run good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as kiddie; it has no inversions; it tops out at 6 mph; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wilderness Run at Cedar Point the same as Woodstock Express at California's Great America?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Children's Roller Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Cedar Point ride opened in 1979 and the California's Great America ride in 1987; Cedar Point sets 36 in and California's Great America sets 46 in.
