Maverick

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 70 mph; 105 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2007.

Maverick is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 26 May 2007. The ride stands 105 ft tall, drops 100 ft, reaches 70 mph and runs 4,450 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 95°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Maverick?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Maverick. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Maverick?

Maverick is 105 ft tall, with a drop of 100 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Maverick go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Maverick go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Maverick scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Maverick open?

Maverick opened on 26 May 2007.

Who built Maverick?

Maverick was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
