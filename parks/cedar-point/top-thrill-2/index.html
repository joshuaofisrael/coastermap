Top Thrill 2

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 120 mph; 420 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Top Thrill 2 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 4 May 2003 and was previously known as Top Thrill Dragster. The ride stands 420 ft tall, drops 400 ft, reaches 120 mph and runs 3,422 ft of track. That height makes it a strata coaster (a coaster 400 ft or taller). Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Top Thrill 2 is like

For a 420 ft ride, 3,422 ft of track is short: Top Thrill 2 is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2003, it is the 12th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point. It has run under other names (Top Thrill Dragster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Xcelerator at Knott's, Rita at Alton Towers and Stealth at Thorpe Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cedar Point sets 52 in, while Hersheypark sets 54 in, Alton Towers sets 140 cm and Thorpe Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 15 other coasters at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Top Thrill 2 sits near the middle of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Iron Dragon, roughly 140 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rougarou at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Top Thrill 2?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Top Thrill 2, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Top Thrill 2?

Top Thrill 2 is 420 ft tall, with a drop of 400 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Top Thrill 2 go?

Its top speed is 120 mph (193 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Top Thrill 2 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Top Thrill 2 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Top Thrill 2 open?

Top Thrill 2 opened on 4 May 2003 (previously known as Top Thrill Dragster).

Who built Top Thrill 2?

Top Thrill 2 was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Is Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point the same as Xcelerator at Knott's?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Cedar Point ride opened in 2003 and the Knott's ride in 2002; both list a 52 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 420 ft and 205 ft.
