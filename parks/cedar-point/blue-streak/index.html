Blue Streak

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 78 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1964.

Blue Streak is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened on 23 May 1964. The ride stands 78 ft tall, drops 72 ft, reaches 40 mph and runs 2,558 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Blue Streak is like

Blue Streak spreads 2,558 ft of track across a layout only 78 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1964, it is the oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Blue Streak sits on the south-east side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Raptor, roughly 150 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is GateKeeper at about 310 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Cedar Point rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Blue Streak?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Blue Streak, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Blue Streak?

Blue Streak is 78 ft tall, with a drop of 72 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Blue Streak go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Blue Streak go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Blue Streak scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Cedar Point rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Blue Streak open?

Blue Streak opened on 23 May 1964.

Who built Blue Streak?

Blue Streak was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the ride on Blue Streak?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 2,558 ft of track.

How does Blue Streak get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Blue Streak at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Blue Streak good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
