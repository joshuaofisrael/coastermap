Millennium Force

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 93 mph; 310 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Millennium Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 13 May 2000. The ride stands 310 ft tall, drops 300 ft, reaches 93 mph and runs 6,595 ft of track. That height makes it a giga coaster (300 to 399 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Millennium Force is like

Millennium Force pairs a 310 ft structure with 6,595 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2000, it is the 11th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Giga Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Pantherian at Kings Dominion. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cedar Point sets 48 in, while Kings Dominion sets 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Millennium Force sits on the south-west side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rougarou, roughly 150 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Iron Dragon at about 260 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Millennium Force?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Millennium Force. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Millennium Force?

Millennium Force is 310 ft tall, with a drop of 300 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Millennium Force go?

Its top speed is 93 mph (150 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Millennium Force go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Millennium Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Millennium Force open?

Millennium Force opened on 13 May 2000.

Who built Millennium Force?

Millennium Force was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Millennium Force?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 6,595 ft of track.

How does Millennium Force get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a cable lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Millennium Force at once?

RCDB's train details give 36 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,300 riders.

What restraints does Millennium Force use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Millennium Force good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 93 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Millennium Force at Cedar Point the same as Pantherian at Kings Dominion?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Giga Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Cedar Point ride opened in 2000 and the Kings Dominion ride in 2010; Cedar Point sets 48 in and Kings Dominion sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 310 ft and 305 ft.
