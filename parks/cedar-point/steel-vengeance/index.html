Steel Vengeance

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 74 mph; 205 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2018.

Steel Vengeance is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 5 May 2018. The ride stands 205 ft tall, drops 200 ft, reaches 74 mph and runs 5,740 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 4 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Steel Vengeance is like

At 205 ft tall with 5,740 ft of track, Steel Vengeance balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 4 times across 5,740 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the 16th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 15 other coasters at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Vengeance sits on the north-west side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Maverick, roughly 240 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cedar Creek Mine Ride at about 270 m. Steel Vengeance layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill (205 ft tall, with a 200 ft drop at 90 °).

These are the 8 elements RCDB records on Steel Vengeance's track, in RCDB's order:

  2. Outward Banked Airtime Hill
  3. Zero-G Roll (variant)
  4. Zero-G Stall
  5. Block Brake
  6. Zero-G Roll ×2
  7. Over-Banked Curve

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Steel Vengeance

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Vengeance?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Steel Vengeance, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Vengeance?

Steel Vengeance is 205 ft tall, with a drop of 200 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Vengeance go?

Its top speed is 74 mph (119 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Vengeance go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Steel Vengeance scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Steel Vengeance open?

Steel Vengeance opened on 5 May 2018.

Who built Steel Vengeance?

Steel Vengeance was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the ride on Steel Vengeance?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 5,740 ft of track.

How does Steel Vengeance get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steel Vengeance at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Steel Vengeance good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 74 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
