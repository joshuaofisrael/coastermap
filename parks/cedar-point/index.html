Cedar Point roller coasters

Sandusky, Ohio · 18 coasters

Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio has 18 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Top Thrill 2 at 420 ft, which is also the fastest at 120 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Wilderness Run.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Cedar Point have?

Cedar Point has 18 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Cedar Point?

Top Thrill 2, at 420 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Cedar Point?

Top Thrill 2, with a top speed of 120 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Cedar Point?

By height requirement, Wilderness Run has the lowest minimum at 36 inches (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Cedar Point coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Wilderness Run, Iron Dragon. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Cedar Point coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Magnum XL-200, Blue Streak, Corkscrew, Gemini, Wilderness Run, Woodstock Express, Millennium Force, Iron Dragon, Cedar Creek Mine Ride, Wild Mouse, Siren’s Curse. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Cedar Point do not go upside down?

Magnum XL-200, Blue Streak, Gemini, Wilderness Run, Woodstock Express, Millennium Force, Iron Dragon, Cedar Creek Mine Ride, Top Thrill 2, Wild Mouse have no inversions, per RCDB.
