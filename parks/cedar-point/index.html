Cedar Point height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Sandusky, Ohio · 18 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio has 18 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Wilderness Run to 54 in (137 cm) on Raptor. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 11 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 18 of them.

What the coasters at Cedar Point are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 5 as thrill and 11 as extreme, which makes Cedar Point a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 61% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 16 of the 18 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 8 of the 18 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Blue Streak); 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Maverick and Top Thrill 2).

The most common builder is Arrow Dynamics, with 5 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Blue Streak, which opened in 1964.

Millennium Force is the 6th tallest of the 918 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Millennium Force is the 6th fastest of the 893 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Cedar Point? By height

At Cedar Point the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 9 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 18 coasters with a verified rule: Wilderness Run. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 16 coasters, the nearest being Iron Dragon at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Iron Dragon. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Wild Mouse (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 14 coasters, the nearest being Magnum XL-200 at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 11 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 9 more than at the height below: Magnum XL-200, Blue Streak, Corkscrew, Gemini, Woodstock Express, Millennium Force, Cedar Creek Mine Ride, Wild Mouse and Siren’s Curse. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Maverick at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 18 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 7 more than at the height below: Raptor, Rougarou, Maverick, GateKeeper, Valravn, Steel Vengeance and Top Thrill 2. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Rougarou (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Wilderness Run (up to 54 in (137 cm)), Millennium Force (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Maverick (up to 78 in (198 cm)), GateKeeper (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Siren’s Curse (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Cedar Point

These are the rules Cedar Point states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Woodstock Express: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wild Mouse: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Cedar Point for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Cedar Point, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wilderness Run, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; 19 ft tall, 6 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Iron Dragon, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 76 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Top Thrill 2, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 420 ft tall, 120 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Cedar Point?

Roller coaster height requirements at Cedar Point run from 36 in (91 cm) (Wilderness Run) to 54 in (137 cm) (Raptor), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Cedar Point have?

Cedar Point has 18 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Cedar Point?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Top Thrill 2 (420 ft tall, 120 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Cedar Point?

Top Thrill 2, at 420 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Cedar Point?

Top Thrill 2, with a top speed of 120 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Cedar Point for first-timers?

Wilderness Run is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 36 in (91 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Cedar Point?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Wilderness Run, Iron Dragon. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express, Wild Mouse.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Cedar Point?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Magnum XL-200, Blue Streak, Corkscrew, Gemini, Wilderness Run, Woodstock Express, Millennium Force, Iron Dragon, Cedar Creek Mine Ride, Wild Mouse, Siren’s Curse.

Which coasters at Cedar Point do not go upside down?

Magnum XL-200, Blue Streak, Gemini, Wilderness Run, Woodstock Express, Millennium Force, Iron Dragon, Cedar Creek Mine Ride, Top Thrill 2, Wild Mouse have no inversions, per RCDB.
