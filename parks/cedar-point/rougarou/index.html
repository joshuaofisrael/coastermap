Rougarou

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 60 mph; 145 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1996.

Rougarou is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 11 May 1996 and was previously known as Mantis. The ride stands 145 ft tall, drops 137 ft, reaches 60 mph and runs 3,900 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Rougarou is like

At 145 ft tall with 3,900 ft of track, Rougarou balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,900 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1996, it is the 9th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point. It has run under other names (Mantis), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Stand-Up Coaster design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Riddler's Revenge at Magic Mountain, Georgia Scorcher at Six Flags Over Georgia, Vortex at Carowinds and Patriot at California's Great America. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Cedar Point, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rougarou sits on the south-west side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Iron Dragon, roughly 120 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Millennium Force at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Rougarou

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 78 in (198 cm). See can you be too tall?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rougarou?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Rougarou. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rougarou?

Rougarou is 145 ft tall, with a drop of 137 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rougarou go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rougarou go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Rougarou scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Rougarou open?

Rougarou opened on 11 May 1996 (previously known as Mantis).

Who built Rougarou?

Rougarou was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Rougarou?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 40 seconds over 3,900 ft of track.

How does Rougarou get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

What restraints does Rougarou use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Rougarou good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rougarou at Cedar Point the same as Patriot at California's Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Stand-Up Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Cedar Point ride opened in 1996 and the California's Great America ride in 1991; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 145 ft and 91 ft.
