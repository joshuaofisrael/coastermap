Iron Dragon

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 40 mph; 76 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1987.

Iron Dragon is a steel suspended roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 11 Jun 1987. The ride stands 76 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 2,800 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Iron Dragon is like

With 2,800 ft of track for a 76 ft structure, Iron Dragon is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1987, it is the 6th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Suspended Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Ninja at Magic Mountain, The Bat at Kings Island, Vortex at Canada's Wonderland and Vampire at Chessington. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cedar Point sets 42 in, while Chessington sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Iron Dragon sits on the south-east side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rougarou, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Siren’s Curse at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Cedar Point rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Dragon?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Iron Dragon, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Dragon?

Iron Dragon is 76 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Dragon go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Dragon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Iron Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Cedar Point rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Iron Dragon open?

Iron Dragon opened on 11 Jun 1987.

Who built Iron Dragon?

Iron Dragon was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Iron Dragon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 2,800 ft of track.

How does Iron Dragon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Iron Dragon at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,000 riders.

What restraints does Iron Dragon use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Iron Dragon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Iron Dragon at Cedar Point the same as Ninja at Magic Mountain?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Suspended Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Cedar Point ride opened in 1987 and the Magic Mountain ride in 1988; both list a 42 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 76 ft and 60 ft.
