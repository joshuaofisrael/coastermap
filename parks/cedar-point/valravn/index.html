Valravn

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 75 mph; 223 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2016.

Valravn is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 May 2016. The ride stands 223 ft tall, drops 214 ft, reaches 75 mph and runs 3,415 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Valravn?

Riders must be at least 52 inches (132 cm) tall to ride Valravn, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Valravn?

Valravn is 223 ft tall, with a drop of 214 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Valravn go?

Its top speed is 75 mph (121 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Valravn go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Valravn scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Valravn open?

Valravn opened on 7 May 2016.

Who built Valravn?

Valravn was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
