Corkscrew

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 48 mph; 85 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1976.

Corkscrew is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 15 May 1976. The ride stands 85 ft tall, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,050 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Corkscrew is like

At 85 ft tall with 2,050 ft of track, Corkscrew balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,050 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1976, it is the 3rd oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Cedar Point.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Cedar Point, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Corkscrew sits on the east side of Cedar Point's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Iron Dragon, roughly 140 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Siren’s Curse at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Corkscrew

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Cedar Point app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Corkscrew?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Corkscrew, according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Corkscrew?

Corkscrew is 85 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Corkscrew go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Corkscrew go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Corkscrew scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Corkscrew open?

Corkscrew opened on 15 May 1976.

Who built Corkscrew?

Corkscrew was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

Is Corkscrew at Cedar Point the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Cedar Point ride opened in 1976 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 85 ft and 70 ft.
