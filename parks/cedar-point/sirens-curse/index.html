Siren’s Curse

Cedar Point · Sandusky, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 58.4 mph; 160 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2025.

Siren’s Curse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 Jun 2025. The ride stands 160 ft tall, reaches 58.4 mph and runs 2965.9 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

On our intensity ranking Siren’s Curse is number 10 of 18 at Cedar Point, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 18 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Siren’s Curse?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Siren’s Curse. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to Cedar Point, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Siren’s Curse?

Siren’s Curse is 160 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Siren’s Curse go?

Its top speed is 58.4 mph (94 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Siren’s Curse go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Siren’s Curse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Cedar Point rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Siren’s Curse open?

Siren’s Curse opened on 28 Jun 2025.

Who built Siren’s Curse?

Siren’s Curse was manufactured by Vekoma.
