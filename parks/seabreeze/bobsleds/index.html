Bobsleds

Seabreeze Amusement Park · Rochester, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 23 mph; 31 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1954.

Bobsleds is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Seabreeze Amusement Park in Rochester, New York. It first opened in 1954 and was previously known as Junior Coaster. The ride stands 31 ft tall, reaches 23 mph and runs 1,240 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bobsleds is like

With 1,240 ft of track for a 31 ft structure, Bobsleds is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1954, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Seabreeze Amusement Park. It has run under other names (Junior Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Seabreeze, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bobsleds sits on the north side of Seabreeze's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Whirlwind, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Bear Trax at about 270 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Bobsleds

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 38 to 48 in must ride with a responsible person who meets the ride criteria.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bobsleds?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Bobsleds, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion. Riders 38 to 48 in must ride with a responsible person who meets the ride criteria, according to Seabreeze, official ride requirements page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Bobsleds?

Bobsleds is 31 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Bobsleds go?

Its top speed is 23 mph (37 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Bobsleds go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bobsleds scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Bobsleds open?

Bobsleds opened in 1954 (previously known as Junior Coaster).

How long is the track on Bobsleds?

RCDB lists 1,240 ft (378 m) of track.

How does Bobsleds get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Bobsleds at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Bobsleds good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 23 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
