Runaway Train

Gulliver's Land · Milton Keynes, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Runaway Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gulliver's Land in Milton Keynes, England, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2002. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Runaway Train is like

At 13 ft tall with 262.5 ft of track, Runaway Train balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Gulliver's Land. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gulliver's Land sets 120 cm, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 42 in and Silver Dollar City sets 42 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Gulliver's Land, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Runaway Train sits near the middle of Gulliver's Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Crazy Mouse, roughly 60 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grand Prix Racers at about 100 m. Who is it for?

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Runaway Train?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Runaway Train, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Gulliver's Land Resort, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Runaway Train?

Runaway Train is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Runaway Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Runaway Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Runaway Train open?

Runaway Train opened in 2002.

Who built Runaway Train?

Runaway Train was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Runaway Train?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Runaway Train get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Runaway Train at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Runaway Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Runaway Train at Gulliver's Land the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Gulliver's Land ride opened in 2002 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; Gulliver's Land sets 120 cm and Holiday World sets 42 in.
