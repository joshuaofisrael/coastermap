Wriggler

Gulliver's Land · Milton Keynes, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Wriggler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gulliver's Land in Milton Keynes, England, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 13 Feb 2026. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wriggler is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Gulliver's Land. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Lokolo at Lost Island, Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle and Family Coaster at Plohn. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gulliver's Land sets 120 cm, while Winnoland sets 80 cm and Didi'Land sets 130 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Gulliver's Land, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Before you ride Wriggler

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wriggler?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Wriggler, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Gulliver's Land Resort, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Wriggler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wriggler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wriggler open?

Wriggler opened on 13 Feb 2026.

Who built Wriggler?

Wriggler was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Wriggler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wriggler at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wriggler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wriggler at Gulliver's Land the same as Family Coaster at Plohn?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Gulliver's Land ride opened in 2026 and the Plohn ride in 2006.
