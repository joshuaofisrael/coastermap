Mumbo Jumbo

Flamingo Land · Kirby Misperton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in); 98.4 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2009.

Mumbo Jumbo is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Flamingo Land in Kirby Misperton, England, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 4 Jul 2009. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 112°, past vertical.

What riding Mumbo Jumbo is like

The steepest drop is 112°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2009, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Flamingo Land. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's El Loco design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Green Lantern Coaster at Movie World, Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach and Timber Drop at Fraispertuis-City. Height rules for this model differ by park: Flamingo Land sets 122 cm, while Movie World sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 122 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Flamingo Land, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mumbo Jumbo sits on the north side of Flamingo Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kumali, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hero at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Mumbo Jumbo compares

On our intensity ranking Mumbo Jumbo is number 2 of 8 at Flamingo Land, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Mumbo Jumbo

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Flamingo Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mumbo Jumbo?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Mumbo Jumbo, according to Flamingo Land, official ride restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mumbo Jumbo?

Mumbo Jumbo is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Mumbo Jumbo go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Mumbo Jumbo scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mumbo Jumbo open?

Mumbo Jumbo opened on 4 Jul 2009.

Who built Mumbo Jumbo?

Mumbo Jumbo was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How does Mumbo Jumbo get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mumbo Jumbo at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Mumbo Jumbo good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 122 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Mumbo Jumbo at Flamingo Land the same as Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach?

Both are S&S Worldwide El Loco coasters on RCDB; the Flamingo Land ride opened in 2009 and the Indiana Beach ride in 2008; both list a 122 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 98.4 ft and 96 ft.
