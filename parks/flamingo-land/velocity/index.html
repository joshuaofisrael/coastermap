Velocity

Flamingo Land · Kirby Misperton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 54 mph; 57 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Velocity is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Flamingo Land in Kirby Misperton, England, built by Vekoma. It opened on 2 Jul 2005. The ride stands 57 ft tall, reaches 54 mph and runs 2152.2 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Velocity is like

Velocity spreads 2,152.2 ft of track across a layout only 57 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2005, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Flamingo Land.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Flamingo Land, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Velocity sits on the west side of Flamingo Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sik, roughly 60 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hero at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 198 cm (about 78 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Velocity?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Velocity. The maximum rider height is 198 cm (about 78 in), according to Flamingo Land, official ride restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Velocity?

Velocity is 57 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Velocity go?

Its top speed is 54 mph (87 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Velocity go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Velocity scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Velocity open?

Velocity opened on 2 Jul 2005.

Who built Velocity?

Velocity was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Velocity?

RCDB lists 2152.2 ft of track.

Does Velocity launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Velocity at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 518 riders.

Is Velocity good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 54 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
