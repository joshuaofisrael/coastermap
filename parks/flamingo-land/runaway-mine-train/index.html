Runaway Mine Train

Flamingo Land · Kirby Misperton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Runaway Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Flamingo Land in Kirby Misperton, England, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2020. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Runaway Mine Train is like

At 13 ft tall with 262.5 ft of track, Runaway Mine Train balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2020, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Flamingo Land. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Runaway Mine Train sits on the south-east side of Flamingo Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Zooom!, roughly 30 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twistosaurus at about 120 m. Frequently asked questions

How tall is Runaway Mine Train?

Runaway Mine Train is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Runaway Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Runaway Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Runaway Mine Train open?

Runaway Mine Train opened in 2020.

Who built Runaway Mine Train?

Runaway Mine Train was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Runaway Mine Train?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Runaway Mine Train get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Runaway Mine Train at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Runaway Mine Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Runaway Mine Train operate before?

RCDB records it at Gulliver's World Resort (as Runaway Train); American Adventure Theme Park (as Mini Mine Rush).

Is Runaway Mine Train at Flamingo Land the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Flamingo Land ride opened in 2020 and the Holiday World ride in 1999.
