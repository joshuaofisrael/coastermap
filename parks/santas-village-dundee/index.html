Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

East Dundee, Illinois · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park in East Dundee, Illinois has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Wacky Worm to 52 in (132 cm) on Super Cyclone. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Wacky Worm, which opened in 2015.

What can my child ride at Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park? By height

At Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Wacky Worm (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)) and Farmers Fling (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Super Cyclone at 52 in (132 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Wacky Worm. With a companion they can also ride Farmers Fling (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Super Cyclone at 52 in (132 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Farmers Fling. With a companion they can also ride Super Cyclone (48 to 52 in (122 to 132 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Super Cyclone. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Farmers Fling (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park

These are the rules Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Wacky Worm: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Super Cyclone: riders 48 to 52 in (122 to 132 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Farmers Fling: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
