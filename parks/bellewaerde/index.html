Bellewaerde Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ypres, Belgium · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Bellewaerde Park in Ypres, Belgium has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Huracan to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Boomerang. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Bellewaerde Park are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Bellewaerde Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 20% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Boomerang, which opened in 1984.

Bellewaerde Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Bellewaerde Park? By height

At Bellewaerde Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Brazilian Buggies (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Huracan at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Best coasters at Bellewaerde Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Bellewaerde Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Brazilian Buggies, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wakala, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 68.9 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Boomerang, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
