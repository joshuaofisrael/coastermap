Boomerang

Bellewaerde Park · Ypres, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1984.

Boomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bellewaerde Park in Ypres, Belgium, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1984. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Boomerang is like

For a 116.5 ft ride, 935 ft of track is short: Boomerang is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1984, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Bellewaerde Park. It is one of 20 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the first of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bellewaerde sets 130 cm, while Hersheypark sets 48 in, Carowinds sets 48 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 48 in.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Bellewaerde. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Boomerang sits on the south-east side of Bellewaerde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Huracan, roughly 130 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dawson Duel at about 650 m. Boomerang layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Boomerang's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Catch Car Lift Hill
  2. Cobra Roll
  3. Loop
  4. Chain Lift Hill

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Boomerang compares

On our intensity ranking Boomerang is number 1 of 5 at Bellewaerde Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Dawson Duel (82 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Boomerang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Bellewaerde Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Boomerang?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Boomerang, according to Bellewaerde, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Boomerang?

Boomerang is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Boomerang go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Boomerang go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Boomerang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Boomerang open?

Boomerang opened in 1984.

Who built Boomerang?

Boomerang was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Boomerang?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Boomerang get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Boomerang at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Boomerang good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Boomerang at Bellewaerde the same as Boomerang at La Ronde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Bellewaerde ride opened in 1984 and the La Ronde ride in 1984; Bellewaerde sets 130 cm and La Ronde sets 48 in.
