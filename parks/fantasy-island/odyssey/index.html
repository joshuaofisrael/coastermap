Odyssey

Fantasy Island · Ingoldmells, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 62 mph; 167 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2002.

Odyssey is a steel inverted roller coaster at Fantasy Island in Ingoldmells, England, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 25 May 2002 and was previously known as Jubilee Odyssey. The ride stands 167 ft tall, drops 141 ft, reaches 62 mph and runs 2923.2 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Odyssey is like

At 167 ft tall with 2,923.2 ft of track, Odyssey balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,923.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.8 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Fantasy Island. It has run under other names (Jubilee Odyssey), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Fantasy Island, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Odyssey sits on the south-west side of Fantasy Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jellikins Coaster, roughly 120 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Family Roller Coaster at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Odyssey

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders under 1.4 m must be accompanied by an adult (minimum 1.3 m).
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fantasy Island app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Odyssey?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Odyssey, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riders under 1.4 m must be accompanied by an adult (minimum 1.3 m), according to Fantasy Island, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Odyssey?

Odyssey is 167 ft tall, with a drop of 141 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Odyssey go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Odyssey go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Odyssey scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Odyssey open?

Odyssey opened on 25 May 2002 (previously known as Jubilee Odyssey).

Who built Odyssey?

Odyssey was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Odyssey?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 52 seconds over 2923.2 ft of track.

How does Odyssey get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Odyssey at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Odyssey good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 62 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
