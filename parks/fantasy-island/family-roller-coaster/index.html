Family Roller Coaster

Fantasy Island · Ingoldmells, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2021.

Family Roller Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fantasy Island in Ingoldmells, England, built by Güven Amusement Rides Factory. It opened in 2021. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Roller Coaster is like

Opened in 2021, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Fantasy Island.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Roller Coaster sits on the west side of Fantasy Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jellikins Coaster, roughly 160 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Odyssey at about 170 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Frequently asked questions

Does Family Roller Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Roller Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Family Roller Coaster open?

Family Roller Coaster opened in 2021.

Who built Family Roller Coaster?

Family Roller Coaster was manufactured by Güven Amusement Rides Factory.

How does Family Roller Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Family Roller Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Family Roller Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
