Fantasy Island height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ingoldmells, England · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Fantasy Island in Ingoldmells, England has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Jellikins Coaster to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Millennium. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Fantasy Island are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Fantasy Island a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down.

The most common builder is WGH Transportation, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Rhombus Express, which opened in 1995.

Fantasy Island publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • The Rhombus Express: Riders under 1.2 m must be accompanied by an adult (minimum 1.0 m).
  • Millennium: Riders under 1.4 m must be accompanied by an adult (minimum 1.2 m).

What can my child ride at Fantasy Island? By height

At Fantasy Island the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Jellikins Coaster at 100 cm (about 39.4 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Jellikins Coaster. With a companion they can also ride The Rhombus Express (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Millennium at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: The Rhombus Express. With a companion they can also ride Millennium (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Odyssey at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: The Rhombus Express and Jellikins Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Millennium (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)) and Odyssey (130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Jellikins Coaster (up to 160 cm (about 63 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Fantasy Island

These are the rules Fantasy Island states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • The Rhombus Express: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Millennium: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Odyssey: riders 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Fantasy Island for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Fantasy Island, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Jellikins Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (100 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: The Rhombus Express, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 17.1 ft tall, 28.8 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Odyssey, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 167 ft tall, 62 mph, 5 inversions, 4.8 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
