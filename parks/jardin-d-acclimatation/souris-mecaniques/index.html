Souris Mécaniques

Jardin d'Acclimatation · Paris, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 18.6 mph; 23 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Souris Mécaniques is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Jardin d'Acclimatation in Paris, France, built by Reverchon. It first opened on Feb 2002 and was previously known as Papillons d'Alice. The ride stands 23 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 711.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Souris Mécaniques is like

With 711.9 ft of track for a 23 ft structure, Souris Mécaniques is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2002, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Jardin d'Acclimatation. It has run under other names (Papillons d'Alice), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Jardin d'Acclimatation, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Souris Mécaniques sits on the east side of Jardin d'Acclimatation's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speed Rockets, roughly 30 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Défi du Dragon at about 210 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

How Souris Mécaniques compares

On our intensity ranking Souris Mécaniques is number 2 of 4 at Jardin d'Acclimatation, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Speed Rockets (36.1 ft tall, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Défi du Dragon (39.4 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Souris Mécaniques

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Souris Mécaniques?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Souris Mécaniques, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Les Souris Mécaniques, according to Jardin d'Acclimatation, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Souris Mécaniques?

Souris Mécaniques is 23 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Souris Mécaniques go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Souris Mécaniques go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Souris Mécaniques scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Souris Mécaniques open?

Souris Mécaniques opened on Feb 2002 (previously known as Papillons d'Alice).

Who built Souris Mécaniques?

Souris Mécaniques was manufactured by Reverchon.

How long is the ride on Souris Mécaniques?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 711.9 ft of track.

How does Souris Mécaniques get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Souris Mécaniques good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
