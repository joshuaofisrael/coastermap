SpeedSnake FREE

Fort Fun Abenteuerland · Bestwig, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 64 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1981.

SpeedSnake FREE is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fort Fun Abenteuerland in Bestwig, Germany, built by Vekoma. It first opened on Jun 1981 and was previously known as Wirbelwind and later Speed Snake. The ride stands 64 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1148.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding SpeedSnake FREE is like

SpeedSnake FREE pairs a 64 ft structure with 1,148.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,148.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1981, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fort Fun Abenteuerland. It has run under other names (Wirbelwind, then Speed Snake), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Fort Fun. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, SpeedSnake FREE sits on the north-east side of Fort Fun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Marienkäferbahn, roughly 190 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Trapper Slider at about 420 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride SpeedSnake FREE

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 130 to 140 cm must ride with an adult.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fort Fun Abenteuerland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for SpeedSnake FREE?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride SpeedSnake FREE, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riders 130 to 140 cm must ride with an adult, according to Fort Fun Abenteuerland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is SpeedSnake FREE?

SpeedSnake FREE is 64 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does SpeedSnake FREE go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does SpeedSnake FREE go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is SpeedSnake FREE scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did SpeedSnake FREE open?

SpeedSnake FREE opened on Jun 1981 (previously known as Wirbelwind and later Speed Snake).

Who built SpeedSnake FREE?

SpeedSnake FREE was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on SpeedSnake FREE?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 1148.3 ft of track.

How does SpeedSnake FREE get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride SpeedSnake FREE at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,350 riders.

Is SpeedSnake FREE good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
