Marienkäferbahn

Fort Fun Abenteuerland · Bestwig, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

Marienkäferbahn is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fort Fun Abenteuerland in Bestwig, Germany, built by Zierer. It opened in 1986. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Marienkäferbahn is like

With 1,181.1 ft of track for a 26.3 ft structure, Marienkäferbahn is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1986, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fort Fun Abenteuerland. It is one of 11 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure, Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom and Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fort Fun sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Fort Fun (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Marienkäferbahn sits on the north-east side of Fort Fun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is SpeedSnake FREE, roughly 190 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Trapper Slider at about 520 m. Marienkäferbahn layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Marienkäferbahn

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Marienkäferbahn?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Marienkäferbahn, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Fort Fun Abenteuerland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Marienkäferbahn?

Marienkäferbahn is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Marienkäferbahn go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Marienkäferbahn go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Marienkäferbahn scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Marienkäferbahn open?

Marienkäferbahn opened in 1986.

Who built Marienkäferbahn?

Marienkäferbahn was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Marienkäferbahn?

RCDB lists 1181.1 ft of track.

How does Marienkäferbahn get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Marienkäferbahn good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Marienkäferbahn operate before?

RCDB records it at Löwensafari und Freizeit-Park Tüddern (as unknown).

Is Marienkäferbahn at Fort Fun the same as #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Fort Fun ride opened in 1986 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1976; both list a 120 cm minimum.
