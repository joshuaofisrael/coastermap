Fort Fun Abenteuerland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bestwig, Germany · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Fort Fun Abenteuerland in Bestwig, Germany has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Devil's Mine to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on SpeedSnake FREE. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Fort Fun Abenteuerland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Fort Fun Abenteuerland a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is SpeedSnake FREE, which opened in 1981.

Fort Fun Abenteuerland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Fort Fun Abenteuerland? By height

At Fort Fun Abenteuerland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Fort Fun Abenteuerland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Fort Fun Abenteuerland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Marienkäferbahn, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Trapper Slider, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: SpeedSnake FREE, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 64 ft tall, 37.3 mph, 2 inversions).
