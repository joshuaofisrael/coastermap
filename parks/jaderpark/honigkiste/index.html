Honigkiste

Jaderpark · Jaderberg, Lower Saxony, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); 5.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Honigkiste is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Jaderpark in Jaderberg, Lower Saxony, Germany, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on Apr 2014 and was previously known as Seifenkiste. The ride stands 5.8 ft tall and runs 75.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Honigkiste is like

Honigkiste pairs a 5.8 ft structure with 75.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Jaderpark. It has run under other names (Seifenkiste), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Kiddy Coasters / Kiddy Racer design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Huracanito at Belantis, Kiddy-Racer at Potts Park, Herkules at Familypark and Cobralino at Conny-Land. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 90 cm.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Jaderpark, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among kiddie-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 36 in (91 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Honigkiste sits near the middle of Jaderpark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Holzwurm-Achterbahn, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ziegelblitz at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Gentle (kiddie)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall, and no taller than 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Honigkiste?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Honigkiste. The maximum rider height is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). A ride for children aged 3 to 5, from 90 to 120 cm, according to Jaderpark, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Honigkiste?

Honigkiste is 5.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Honigkiste go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Honigkiste scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster. It is one of the gentlest coasters you can ride.

When did Honigkiste open?

Honigkiste opened on Apr 2014 (previously known as Seifenkiste).

Who built Honigkiste?

Honigkiste was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Honigkiste?

RCDB lists 75.5 ft of track.

How does Honigkiste get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Honigkiste at once?

Up to 1 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Honigkiste good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as kiddie; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Honigkiste at Jaderpark the same as Huracanito at Belantis?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Kiddy Coasters / Kiddy Racer coasters on RCDB; the Jaderpark ride opened in 2014 and the Belantis ride in 2014.
