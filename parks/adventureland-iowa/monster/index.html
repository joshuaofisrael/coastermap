Monster

Adventureland Park · Altoona, Iowa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 65 mph; 133 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2016.

Monster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventureland Park in Altoona, Iowa, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 4 Jun 2016. The ride stands 133 ft tall, reaches 65 mph and runs 2,500 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 5 times. Its steepest drop is 101°, past vertical.

What riding Monster is like

At 133 ft tall with 2,500 ft of track, Monster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 101°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,500 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2016, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Adventureland Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Adventureland, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Monster sits on the north-west side of Adventureland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Underground, roughly 90 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon Slayer at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Monster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Adventureland Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Monster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Monster, according to Adventureland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Monster?

Monster is 133 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Monster go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Monster go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Monster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Monster open?

Monster opened on 4 Jun 2016.

Who built Monster?

Monster was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Monster?

RCDB lists 2,500 ft (762 m) of track.

How does Monster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Monster at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Monster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Monster at Adventureland the same as Monster at Gröna Lund?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Adventureland ride opened in 2016 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2021; Adventureland sets 48 in and Gröna Lund sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 133 ft and 111.5 ft.
