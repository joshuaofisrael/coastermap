Adventureland Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Altoona, Iowa · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Adventureland Park in Altoona, Iowa has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 48 in (122 cm) on Outlaw. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Adventureland Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Adventureland Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 29% of its lineup, against 50% across all 88 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 7 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Outlaw, Underground and Tornado).

The most common builder is Custom Coasters International, Inc., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tornado, which opened in 1978.

What can my child ride at Adventureland Park? By height

At Adventureland Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Flying Viking (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Outlaw at 48 in (122 cm).

Best coasters at Adventureland Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Adventureland Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Flying Viking, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 50 ft tall, 26.1 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Outlaw, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 67 ft tall, 48 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Monster, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 133 ft tall, 65 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

What are the height requirements at Adventureland Park?

Adventureland Park has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.
