Gold Rush

Rainbow's End · Auckland, New Zealand

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 80 to 130 cm (about 31.5 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Gold Rush is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Rainbow's End in Auckland, New Zealand. It opened on Dec 1995. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Rush is like

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Rainbow's End.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Rainbow's End. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 80 to 130 cm (about 31.5 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Rush sits near the middle of Rainbow's End's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Choco Express, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew Coaster at about 130 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 80 to 130 cm (about 31.5 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Gold Rush

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 80 to 130 cm (about 31.5 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum 80 cm; children under 130 cm must sit next to a responsible person over 160 cm tall.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Rainbow's End app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Rush?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Gold Rush, or 80 to 130 cm (about 31.5 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Minimum 80 cm; children under 130 cm must sit next to a responsible person over 160 cm tall, according to Rainbow's End, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Gold Rush go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Rush scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Gold Rush open?

Gold Rush opened on Dec 1995.

How does Gold Rush get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Gold Rush at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Gold Rush good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Gold Rush at Rainbow's End the same as Gold Rush at OK Corral?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Rainbow's End ride opened in 1995 and the OK Corral ride in 2012; Rainbow's End sets 130 cm and OK Corral sets 120 cm.
