Corkscrew Coaster

Rainbow's End · Auckland, New Zealand

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 90 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1986.

Corkscrew Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Rainbow's End in Auckland, New Zealand, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 1986 and was previously known as Coca Cola Rollercoaster. The ride stands 90 ft tall. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Corkscrew Coaster is like

Opened in 1986, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Rainbow's End. It has run under other names (Coca Cola Rollercoaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Loop & Corkscrew design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Steamin' Demon at Great Escape and Halilintar at Dufan. Height rules for this model differ by park: Rainbow's End sets 120 cm, while Great Escape sets 48 in and Dufan sets 125 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Rainbow's End (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Corkscrew Coaster sits on the west side of Rainbow's End's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gold Rush, roughly 130 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Choco Express at about 220 m. Corkscrew Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 3 elements RCDB records on Corkscrew Coaster's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Double Corkscrew

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Corkscrew Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Corkscrew Roller Coaster.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Corkscrew Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Corkscrew Coaster. The park lists it as Corkscrew Roller Coaster, according to Rainbow's End, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Corkscrew Coaster?

Corkscrew Coaster is 90 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Corkscrew Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Corkscrew Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Corkscrew Coaster open?

Corkscrew Coaster opened in 1986 (previously known as Coca Cola Rollercoaster).

Who built Corkscrew Coaster?

Corkscrew Coaster was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How does Corkscrew Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Corkscrew Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Corkscrew Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Corkscrew Coaster at Rainbow's End the same as Steamin' Demon at Great Escape?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Loop & Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Rainbow's End ride opened in 1986 and the Great Escape ride in 1984; Rainbow's End sets 120 cm and Great Escape sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 90 ft and 85 ft.
