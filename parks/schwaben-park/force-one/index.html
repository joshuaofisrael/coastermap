Force One

Schwaben Park · Kaisersbach, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 40.4 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Force One is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Schwaben Park in Kaisersbach, Germany, built by Zierer. It opened on 18 May 2010. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 1738.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Force One is like

Force One pairs a 72.2 ft structure with 1,738.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.7 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2010, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Schwaben Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's ESC / 535 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Spirou Racing at Parc Spirou. Height rules for this model differ by park: Schwaben Park sets 140 cm, while Parc Spirou sets 130 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Schwaben Park, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Force One sits near the middle of Schwaben Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hummel Brummel, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Raupen Express at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Force One

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riding alone needs 1.40 m and age 8; from 120 cm and age 6 riders must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Force One?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Force One, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riding alone needs 1.40 m and age 8; from 120 cm and age 6 riders must be accompanied by an adult, according to Schwaben Park, official age and height table (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Force One?

Force One is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Force One go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Force One go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Force One scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Force One open?

Force One opened on 18 May 2010.

Who built Force One?

Force One was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Force One?

RCDB lists a ride time of 38 seconds over 1738.8 ft of track.

How does Force One get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Force One at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Force One good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Force One at Schwaben Park the same as Spirou Racing at Parc Spirou?

Both are Zierer ESC / 535 coasters on RCDB; the Schwaben Park ride opened in 2010 and the Parc Spirou ride in 2018; Schwaben Park sets 140 cm and Parc Spirou sets 130 cm.
