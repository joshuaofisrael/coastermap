Hummel Brummel

Schwaben Park · Kaisersbach, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 105 to 140 cm (about 41.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Hummel Brummel is a steel suspended roller coaster at Schwaben Park in Kaisersbach, Germany, built by Wiegand. It opened on 15 Aug 2020. The ride runs 1640.4 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hummel Brummel is like

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Schwaben Park.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Schwaben Park, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 105 to 140 cm (about 41.3 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hummel Brummel sits on the north-west side of Schwaben Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Force One, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Raupen Express at about 210 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hummel Brummel?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Hummel Brummel, or 105 to 140 cm (about 41.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riding alone needs 1.40 m and age 8; from 105 cm and age 4 riders must be accompanied by an adult, according to Schwaben Park, official age and height table (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Hummel Brummel go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hummel Brummel scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Hummel Brummel open?

Hummel Brummel opened on 15 Aug 2020.

Who built Hummel Brummel?

Hummel Brummel was manufactured by Wiegand.

How long is the track on Hummel Brummel?

RCDB lists 1640.4 ft of track.

Is Hummel Brummel good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 140 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
