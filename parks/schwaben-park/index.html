Schwaben Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Kaisersbach, Germany · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Schwaben Park in Kaisersbach, Germany has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Raupen Express. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 0 of them.

What the coasters at Schwaben Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Schwaben Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Raupen Express, which opened in 2003.

Schwaben Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
