Phobia Phear Coaster

Lake Compounce · Bristol, Connecticut

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) with a companion; top speed 62 mph; 150 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2016.

Phobia Phear Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lake Compounce in Bristol, Connecticut, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 7 May 2016. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 62 mph and runs 863 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Phobia Phear Coaster is like

For a 150 ft ride, 863 ft of track is short: Phobia Phear Coaster is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2016, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Lake Compounce. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's Sky Rocket / II design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Tigris at Busch Gardens Tampa, Tempesto at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom and Electric Eel at SeaWorld San Diego. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lake Compounce sets 54 in, while Särkänniemi sets 140 cm, Plopsaland Deutschland sets 140 cm and Chimelong Paradise sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Lake Compounce. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Phobia Phear Coaster sits near the middle of Lake Compounce's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wildcat, roughly 120 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kiddie Coaster at about 130 m. Phobia Phear Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Phobia Phear Coaster starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (3 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. In-Line Twist
  3. Non Inverting Loop

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Phobia Phear Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm), and riders from 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lake Compounce app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Phobia Phear Coaster?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Phobia Phear Coaster, or 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) with a companion, according to Lake Compounce, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Phobia Phear Coaster?

Phobia Phear Coaster is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Phobia Phear Coaster go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Phobia Phear Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Phobia Phear Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Phobia Phear Coaster open?

Phobia Phear Coaster opened on 7 May 2016.

Who built Phobia Phear Coaster?

Phobia Phear Coaster was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the track on Phobia Phear Coaster?

RCDB lists 863 ft (263 m) of track.

Does Phobia Phear Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Phobia Phear Coaster at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Phobia Phear Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 62 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Phobia Phear Coaster at Lake Compounce the same as Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Premier Rides Sky Rocket / II coasters on RCDB; the Lake Compounce ride opened in 2016 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2012; both list a 54 in minimum.
