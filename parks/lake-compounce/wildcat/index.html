Wildcat

Lake Compounce · Bristol, Connecticut

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 48 mph; 85 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1927.

Wildcat is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Lake Compounce in Bristol, Connecticut, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened in 1927. The ride stands 85 ft tall, drops 78 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,746 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wildcat is like

With 2,746 ft of track for a 85 ft structure, Wildcat is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1927, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Lake Compounce.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Lake Compounce (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wildcat sits near the middle of Lake Compounce's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Phobia Phear Coaster, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kiddie Coaster at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wildcat?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wildcat, according to Lake Compounce, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wildcat?

Wildcat is 85 ft tall, with a drop of 78 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wildcat go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wildcat go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wildcat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wildcat open?

Wildcat opened in 1927.

Who built Wildcat?

Wildcat was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the ride on Wildcat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 2,746 ft of track.

How many people ride Wildcat at once?

Up to 14 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Wildcat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 48 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wildcat at Lake Compounce the same as Wildcat at Frontier City?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Lake Compounce ride opened in 1927 and the Frontier City ride in 1991; Lake Compounce sets 48 in and Frontier City sets 42 in; RCDB lists heights of 85 ft and 75 ft.
