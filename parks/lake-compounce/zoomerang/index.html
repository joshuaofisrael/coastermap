Zoomerang

Lake Compounce · Bristol, Connecticut

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1997.

Zoomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lake Compounce in Bristol, Connecticut, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Jun 1997. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Zoomerang is like

For a 116.5 ft ride, 935 ft of track is short: Zoomerang is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1997, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Lake Compounce. It is one of 17 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lake Compounce sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Walibi Belgium sets 130 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Lake Compounce (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Zoomerang sits on the north side of Lake Compounce's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kiddie Coaster, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Phobia Phear Coaster at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Zoomerang compares

On our intensity ranking Zoomerang is number 3 of 5 at Lake Compounce, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Wildcat (85 ft tall, 48 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Boulder Dash (60 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum).

Before you ride Zoomerang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lake Compounce app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Zoomerang?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Zoomerang, according to Lake Compounce, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Zoomerang?

Zoomerang is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Zoomerang go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Zoomerang go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Zoomerang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Zoomerang open?

Zoomerang opened on 27 Jun 1997.

Who built Zoomerang?

Zoomerang was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Zoomerang at Lake Compounce the same as Boomerang at La Ronde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Lake Compounce ride opened in 1997 and the La Ronde ride in 1984; both list a 48 in minimum.
