Lake Compounce roller coasters

Bristol, Connecticut · 5 coasters

Lake Compounce in Bristol, Connecticut has 5 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Phobia Phear Coaster at 150 ft, which is also the fastest at 62 mph. The lowest height requirement is 48 in (122 cm), for Wildcat.
