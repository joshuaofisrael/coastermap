Marsh Mayhem

Wild Adventures Theme Park · Valdosta, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 49.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Marsh Mayhem is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Wild Adventures Theme Park in Valdosta, Georgia, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It first opened in 2000 and was previously known as Bug Out and later Go Bananas!. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Marsh Mayhem is like

Marsh Mayhem pairs a 49.3 ft structure with 1,213.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Wild Adventures Theme Park. It has run under other names (Bug Out, then Go Bananas!), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Wild Maus Classic / Right design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum at Six Flags New England and Wild Mouse at Dorney Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Wild Adventures sets 50 in, while Six Flags New England sets 54 in and Dorney Park sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 50 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Wild Adventures, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Marsh Mayhem sits on the north-east side of Wild Adventures' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Outpost Express, roughly 50 m away (44 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Swamp Thing at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Marsh Mayhem compares

On our intensity ranking Marsh Mayhem is number 3 of 6 at Wild Adventures Theme Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Swamp Thing (48.6 ft tall, 26 mph, no inversions, 2.2 g; 44 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Boomerang (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 48 in minimum).

Before you ride Marsh Mayhem

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 50 in (127 cm), and riders from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 46 to 50 in must ride with someone at least 14 years old.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Wild Adventures Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Marsh Mayhem?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Marsh Mayhem, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion. Riders 46 to 50 in must ride with someone at least 14 years old, according to Wild Adventures, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Marsh Mayhem?

Marsh Mayhem is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Marsh Mayhem go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Marsh Mayhem go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Marsh Mayhem scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Marsh Mayhem open?

Marsh Mayhem opened in 2000 (previously known as Bug Out and later Go Bananas!).

Who built Marsh Mayhem?

Marsh Mayhem was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Marsh Mayhem?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 10 seconds over 1213.9 ft of track.

How does Marsh Mayhem get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Marsh Mayhem at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Marsh Mayhem good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Marsh Mayhem at Wild Adventures the same as Wild Mouse at Dorney Park?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Wild Maus Classic / Right coasters on RCDB; the Wild Adventures ride opened in 2000 and the Dorney Park ride in 2000; Wild Adventures sets 50 in and Dorney Park sets 48 in.
