Swampwater Snake

Wild Adventures Theme Park · Valdosta, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Swampwater Snake is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Wild Adventures Theme Park in Valdosta, Georgia, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2019. The ride stands 8 ft tall and runs 262 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Swampwater Snake is like

With 262 ft of track for a 8 ft structure, Swampwater Snake is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Wild Adventures Theme Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Mini Mouse design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund and Mini Mouse at Luna Park.

Swampwater Snake layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Swampwater Snake:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Swampwater Snake trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 8 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 16 riders.

Swampwater Snake has been relocated: RCDB records it operating at Wild Adventures as Fiesta Express and Wild Adventures as Swampwater Snake before Wild Adventures.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Swampwater Snake?

Swampwater Snake is 8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Swampwater Snake go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Swampwater Snake scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Swampwater Snake open?

Swampwater Snake opened in 2019.

Who built Swampwater Snake?

Swampwater Snake was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Swampwater Snake?

RCDB lists 262 ft (80 m) of track.

How does Swampwater Snake get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Swampwater Snake at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Swampwater Snake good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Swampwater Snake operate before?

RCDB records it at Wild Adventures (as Fiesta Express); Wild Adventures (as Swampwater Snake).

Is Swampwater Snake at Wild Adventures the same as Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund?

Both are Zamperla Mini Mouse coasters on RCDB; the Wild Adventures ride opened in 2019 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2010.
