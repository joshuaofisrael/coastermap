Universal Islands of Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Orlando, Florida · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Flight of the Hippogriff to 54 in (137 cm) on The Incredible Hulk Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Islands of Adventure are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Universal Islands of Adventure a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Jurassic World VelociCoaster).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Pteranodon Flyers, which opened in 1999.

What can my child ride at Universal Islands of Adventure? By height

At Universal Islands of Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Universal Islands of Adventure for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Islands of Adventure, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Flight of the Hippogriff, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 28.5 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 65 ft tall, 50 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Jurassic World VelociCoaster, the most intense coaster in the park (51 in minimum; 155 ft tall, 70 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
