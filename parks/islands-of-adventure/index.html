Universal Islands of Adventure roller coasters

Orlando, Florida · 5 coasters

Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida has 5 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Jurassic World VelociCoaster at 155 ft, which is also the fastest at 70 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Flight of the Hippogriff.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Universal Islands of Adventure have?

Universal Islands of Adventure has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure?

Jurassic World VelociCoaster, at 155 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure?

Jurassic World VelociCoaster, with a top speed of 70 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Universal Islands of Adventure?

By height requirement, Flight of the Hippogriff has the lowest minimum at 36 in (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Universal Islands of Adventure coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Flight of the Hippogriff. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Universal Islands of Adventure coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Flight of the Hippogriff, Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Universal Islands of Adventure do not go upside down?

Pteranodon Flyers, Flight of the Hippogriff, Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure have no inversions, per RCDB.
