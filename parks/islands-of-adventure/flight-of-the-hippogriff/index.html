Flight of the Hippogriff

Universal Islands of Adventure · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); top speed 28.5 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Flight of the Hippogriff is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 29 Jun 2000 and was previously known as Flying Unicorn. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 28.5 mph and runs 1099.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flight of the Hippogriff is like

Flight of the Hippogriff pairs a 42.7 ft structure with 1,099.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Islands of Adventure. It has run under other names (Flying Unicorn), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 14 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 335m design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Cedar Point, Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Japan, The Dragon at Legoland Florida and Energuś at Energylandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Islands of Adventure sets 36 in, while Cedar Point sets 48 in, Universal Studios Japan sets 122 cm and Legoland Florida sets 48 in.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Islands of Adventure, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flight of the Hippogriff sits on the north-west side of Islands of Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jurassic World VelociCoaster at about 190 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Flight of the Hippogriff layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flight of the Hippogriff?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Flight of the Hippogriff, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flight of the Hippogriff?

Flight of the Hippogriff is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flight of the Hippogriff go?

Its top speed is 28.5 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flight of the Hippogriff go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Flight of the Hippogriff open?

Flight of the Hippogriff opened on 29 Jun 2000 (previously known as Flying Unicorn).

Who built Flight of the Hippogriff?

Flight of the Hippogriff was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Flight of the Hippogriff?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 6 seconds over 1099.1 ft of track.

How does Flight of the Hippogriff get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Flight of the Hippogriff at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.5 mph; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff at Islands of Adventure the same as K3 Roller Skater at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 335m coasters on RCDB; the Islands of Adventure ride opened in 2000 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1991; Islands of Adventure sets 36 in and Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm.
