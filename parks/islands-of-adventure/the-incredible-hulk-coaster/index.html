The Incredible Hulk Coaster

Universal Islands of Adventure · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 67 mph; 110 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 1999.

The Incredible Hulk Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 28 May 1999. The ride stands 110 ft tall, drops 104.9 ft, reaches 67 mph and runs 3,700 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding The Incredible Hulk Coaster is like

With 3,700 ft of track for a 110 ft structure, The Incredible Hulk Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 7 times across 3,700 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Islands of Adventure. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Sitting Coaster design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Wildfire at Silver Dollar City and Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Islands of Adventure sets 54 in, while Silver Dollar City sets 52 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Islands of Adventure. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Incredible Hulk Coaster sits on the east side of Islands of Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jurassic World VelociCoaster, roughly 420 m away (51 in minimum). Next closest is Pteranodon Flyers at about 440 m. The Incredible Hulk Coaster layout, trains and ride system

The Incredible Hulk Coaster starts with a tyre-driven launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 9 elements RCDB records on The Incredible Hulk Coaster's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Tunnel (150 ft long)
  2. Tire Propelled Launch (from 0 mph - 40 mph in 2 s)
  3. Zero-G Roll
  4. Cobra Roll
  5. Loop
  6. Corkscrew
  7. Loop
  8. Block Brake
  9. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 7 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How The Incredible Hulk Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking The Incredible Hulk Coaster is number 2 of 5 at Universal Islands of Adventure, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride The Incredible Hulk Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Islands of Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Incredible Hulk Coaster?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride The Incredible Hulk Coaster, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Incredible Hulk Coaster?

The Incredible Hulk Coaster is 110 ft tall, with a drop of 104.9 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Incredible Hulk Coaster go?

Its top speed is 67 mph (108 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Incredible Hulk Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is The Incredible Hulk Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Incredible Hulk Coaster open?

The Incredible Hulk Coaster opened on 28 May 1999.

Who built The Incredible Hulk Coaster?

The Incredible Hulk Coaster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on The Incredible Hulk Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 15 seconds over 3,700 ft of track.

Does The Incredible Hulk Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride The Incredible Hulk Coaster at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,920 riders.

Is The Incredible Hulk Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 7 times; it tops out at 67 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is The Incredible Hulk Coaster at Islands of Adventure the same as Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Sitting Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Islands of Adventure ride opened in 1999 and the PortAventura Park ride in 1995; Islands of Adventure sets 54 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 110 ft and 148 ft.
