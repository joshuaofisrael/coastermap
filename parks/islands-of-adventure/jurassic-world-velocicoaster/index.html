Jurassic World VelociCoaster

Universal Islands of Adventure · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 51 in (130 cm); top speed 70 mph; 155 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2021.

Jurassic World VelociCoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 10 Jun 2021. The ride stands 155 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 4,700 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Jurassic World VelociCoaster is like

Jurassic World VelociCoaster spreads 4,700 ft of track across a layout only 155 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 4 times across 4,700 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Islands of Adventure.

A rider who just meets its 51 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Islands of Adventure, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jurassic World VelociCoaster sits on the south-west side of Islands of Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pteranodon Flyers, roughly 110 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flight of the Hippogriff at about 190 m. Jurassic World VelociCoaster layout, trains and ride system

Jurassic World VelociCoaster starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists 7 track elements for Jurassic World VelociCoaster, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 50 mph in 2 s)
  2. Immelmann
  3. Dive Loop
  4. LSM Boost (from 40 mph - 70 mph in 2.4 s)
  5. Top Hat - Outside (155 ft tall)
  6. Zero-G Stall (100 ft long)
  7. Barrel Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 51 in (130 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Jurassic World VelociCoaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 51 in (130 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Islands of Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Jurassic World VelociCoaster?

Riders must be at least 51 in (130 cm) tall to ride Jurassic World VelociCoaster, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Jurassic World VelociCoaster?

Jurassic World VelociCoaster is 155 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Jurassic World VelociCoaster go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Jurassic World VelociCoaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Jurassic World VelociCoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Jurassic World VelociCoaster open?

Jurassic World VelociCoaster opened on 10 Jun 2021.

Who built Jurassic World VelociCoaster?

Jurassic World VelociCoaster was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Jurassic World VelociCoaster?

RCDB lists 4,700 ft (1,433 m) of track.

Does Jurassic World VelociCoaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Jurassic World VelociCoaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Jurassic World VelociCoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 51 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
