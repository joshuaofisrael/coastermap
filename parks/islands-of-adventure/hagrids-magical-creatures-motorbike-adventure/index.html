Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure

Universal Islands of Adventure · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Islands of Adventure in Orlando, Florida, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 13 Jun 2019. The ride stands 65 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 5,053 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure is like

With 5,053 ft of track for a 65 ft structure, Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2019, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Islands of Adventure.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Islands of Adventure, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure sits on the north-west side of Islands of Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flight of the Hippogriff, roughly 140 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jurassic World VelociCoaster at about 290 m. Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure layout, trains and ride system

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 5 booster sections (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (9 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (rolling)
  2. LSM Boost ×4
  3. Vertical Rollback
  4. LSM Boost
  5. Vertical Drop Track (with a 17 ft drop)
  6. LSM Launch (rolling)

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure?

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure is 65 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure open?

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure opened on 13 Jun 2019.

Who built Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure?

Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure?

RCDB lists 5,053 ft (1,540 m) of track.

Does Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure at once?

RCDB's train details give 14 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,700 riders.

Is Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
