OK Corral height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Cuges-les-Pins, France · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

OK Corral in Cuges-les-Pins, France has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Gold Rush to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Serpent Hopi. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at OK Corral are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes OK Corral a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Serpent Hopi, which opened in 1984.

OK Corral publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at OK Corral? By height

At OK Corral the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at OK Corral

These are the rules OK Corral states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Serpent Hopi: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Gold Rush: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at OK Corral for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for OK Corral, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Gold Rush, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Serpent Hopi, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
