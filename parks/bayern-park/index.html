Bayern-Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Reisbach, Germany · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Bayern-Park in Reisbach, Germany has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on FirleFranz to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Freischütz. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Bayern-Park are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Bayern-Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 20% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 37.4 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Freischütz).

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Froschbahn, which opened in 1995.

Bayern-Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Bayern-Park? By height

At Bayern-Park the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: FirleFranz. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Froschbahn at 95 cm (about 37.4 in).

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Froschbahn. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Freischütz at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Best coasters at Bayern-Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Bayern-Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Froschbahn, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (95 cm minimum; 10.8 ft tall, 16.2 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: FirleFranz, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (90 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 29.2 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Freischütz, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 78.7 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 4 inversions, 4.4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
