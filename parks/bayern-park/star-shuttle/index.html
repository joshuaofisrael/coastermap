Star-Shuttle

Bayern-Park · Reisbach, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

20 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Star-Shuttle is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bayern-Park in Reisbach, Germany, built by Sunkid GmbH. It opened on Aug 2009. The ride stands 20 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Star-Shuttle is like

Opened in 2009, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Bayern-Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Sunkid GmbH's Butterfly / Original design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Vlinder at Julianatoren.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Star-Shuttle sits on the north side of Bayern-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is FirleFranz, roughly 50 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Froschbahn at about 160 m. How Star-Shuttle compares

On our intensity ranking Star-Shuttle is number 4 of 5 at Bayern-Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Raupenachterbahn (no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Froschbahn (10.8 ft tall, 16.2 mph, no inversions; 95 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Star-Shuttle?

Star-Shuttle is 20 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Star-Shuttle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Star-Shuttle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Star-Shuttle open?

Star-Shuttle opened on Aug 2009.

Who built Star-Shuttle?

Star-Shuttle was manufactured by Sunkid GmbH.

How many people ride Star-Shuttle at once?

RCDB's train details give 2 seats per train.

Is Star-Shuttle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Star-Shuttle operate before?

RCDB records it at Potts Park (as Pendelbahn); Funhall Mering (as Dschungel-Bahn).

Is Star-Shuttle at Bayern-Park the same as Vlinder at Julianatoren?

Both are Sunkid GmbH Butterfly / Original coasters on RCDB; the Bayern-Park ride opened in 2009 and the Julianatoren ride in 2000.
