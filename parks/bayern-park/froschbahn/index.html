Froschbahn

Bayern-Park · Reisbach, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); top speed 16.2 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Froschbahn is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bayern-Park in Reisbach, Germany, built by Zierer. It opened in 1995. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 16.2 mph and runs 197.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Froschbahn is like

Froschbahn pairs a 10.8 ft structure with 197.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1995, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Bayern-Park. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Small design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Hoot N Holler at Six Flags Darien Lake, Puff the Little Fire Dragon at Lagoon, Nyckelpigan at Gröna Lund and Mariehønen at Bakken. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bayern-Park sets 95 cm, while Six Flags Darien Lake sets 36 in and Glenwood Caverns sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 95 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Bayern-Park, out of 2 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Froschbahn sits near the middle of Bayern-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Raupenachterbahn, roughly 140 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Freischütz at about 140 m. Froschbahn layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Froschbahn?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Froschbahn. The park lists the ride from age 3, according to Bayern-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Froschbahn?

Froschbahn is 10.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Froschbahn go?

Its top speed is 16.2 mph (26 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Froschbahn go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Froschbahn scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Froschbahn open?

Froschbahn opened in 1995.

Who built Froschbahn?

Froschbahn was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Froschbahn?

RCDB lists 197.5 ft of track.

How does Froschbahn get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Froschbahn at once?

Each train carries 10 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Froschbahn good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 16.2 mph; the minimum height is 95 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Froschbahn at Bayern-Park the same as Nyckelpigan at Gröna Lund?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Small coasters on RCDB; the Bayern-Park ride opened in 1995 and the Gröna Lund ride in 1976.
