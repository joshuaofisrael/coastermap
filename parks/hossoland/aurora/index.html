Aurora

Hossoland · Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Aurora is a steel suspended roller coaster at Hossoland in Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Jun 2025. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1295.9 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Aurora is like

Aurora pairs a 65.6 ft structure with 1,295.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2025, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hossoland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 395m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Freedom Flyer at Fun Spot Orlando and Kvasten at Gröna Lund. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hossoland sets 130 cm, while Fun Spot Orlando sets 52 in and Gröna Lund sets 120 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Hossoland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Aurora sits on the north side of Hossoland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Veyron, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Thor at about 200 m. Aurora layout, trains and ride system

Who is it for?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Aurora?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Aurora, or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park describes it as a suspended coaster, according to Hossoland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Aurora?

Aurora is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Aurora go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Aurora go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Aurora scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Aurora open?

Aurora opened on 27 Jun 2025.

Who built Aurora?

Aurora was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Aurora?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 3 seconds over 1295.9 ft of track.

How does Aurora get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Aurora at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Aurora good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Aurora at Hossoland the same as Kvasten at Gröna Lund?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 395m coasters on RCDB; the Hossoland ride opened in 2025 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2007; Hossoland sets 130 cm and Gröna Lund sets 120 cm.
