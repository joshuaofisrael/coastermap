Hossoland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Hossoland in Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Mermaid Ride to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Thor. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Hossoland are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Hossoland a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Thor, which opened in 2025.

Hossoland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Hossoland? By height

At Hossoland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Veyron (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Mermaid Ride at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Thor (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)), Aurora (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)), Mermaid Ride (95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in)) and Veyron (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Mermaid Ride and Veyron. With a companion they can also ride Thor (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)) and Aurora (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Thor and Aurora. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Hossoland

These are the rules Hossoland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Thor: riders 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Aurora: riders 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Mermaid Ride: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Veyron: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Hossoland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Hossoland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Veyron, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Thor, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 41 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Aurora, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
