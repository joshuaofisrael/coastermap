Thor

Hossoland · Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 24.9 mph; 41 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Thor is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hossoland in Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Jun 2025. The ride stands 41 ft tall, reaches 24.9 mph and runs 1148.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Thor is like

Thor pairs a 41 ft structure with 1,148.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2025, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hossoland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Coaster / Horus design, and the first of them to open. Others include Twilight Express at Six Flags Qiddiya. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Hossoland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thor sits on the east side of Hossoland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mermaid Ride, roughly 180 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aurora at about 200 m. Thor layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Thor compares

On our intensity ranking Thor is number 2 of 4 at Hossoland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Thor?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Thor, or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Hossoland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Thor?

Thor is 41 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Thor go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thor go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Thor scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Thor open?

Thor opened on 27 Jun 2025.

Who built Thor?

Thor was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Thor?

RCDB lists 1148.3 ft of track.

How does Thor get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Thor at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Thor good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.9 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Thor at Hossoland the same as Twilight Express at Six Flags Qiddiya?

Both are Vekoma Family Coaster / Horus coasters on RCDB; the Hossoland ride opened in 2025 and the Six Flags Qiddiya ride in 2025; both list a 130 cm minimum.
