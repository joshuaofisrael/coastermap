Veyron

Hossoland · Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Veyron is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hossoland in Brojce, West Pomerania, Poland, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 27 Jun 2025. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Veyron is like

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Hossoland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Preston & Barbieri's Family Coaster 30 x 14 m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Draken at Energylandia.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Hossoland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Veyron sits on the north-west side of Hossoland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Aurora, roughly 100 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mermaid Ride at about 210 m. Veyron layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Veyron:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

On kid height: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36 and 42 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

Before you ride Veyron

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Veyron?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Veyron, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Hossoland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Veyron go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Veyron scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Veyron open?

Veyron opened on 27 Jun 2025.

Who built Veyron?

Veyron was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How does Veyron get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Veyron good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Veyron at Hossoland the same as Draken at Energylandia?

Both are Preston & Barbieri Family Coaster 30 x 14 m coasters on RCDB; the Hossoland ride opened in 2025 and the Energylandia ride in 2019.
