Silver Star

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 78.9 mph; 239.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Silver Star is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 23 Mar 2002. The ride stands 239.5 ft tall, drops 219.8 ft, reaches 78.9 mph and runs 5,315 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Silver Star is like

At 239.5 ft tall with 5,315 ft of track, Silver Star balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 6th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Europa-Park. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island and Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Europa-Park sets 140 cm, while SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in, Kings Island sets 54 in and Kings Island sets 54 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Europa-Park. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Silver Star sits on the north side of Europa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pegasus, roughly 230 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Poseidon at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Silver Star?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Silver Star, according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Silver Star?

Silver Star is 239.5 ft tall, with a drop of 219.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Silver Star go?

Its top speed is 78.9 mph (127 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Silver Star go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Silver Star scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Silver Star open?

Silver Star opened on 23 Mar 2002.

Who built Silver Star?

Silver Star was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Silver Star?

RCDB lists 5,315 ft (1,620 m) of track.

How does Silver Star get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Silver Star at once?

RCDB's train details give 36 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,750 riders.

Is Silver Star good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 78.9 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Silver Star at Europa-Park the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Europa-Park ride opened in 2002 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; Europa-Park sets 140 cm and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 239.5 ft and 170 ft.
