Silver Star

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 78.9 mph; 239.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Silver Star is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 23 Mar 2002. The ride stands 239.5 ft tall, drops 219.8 ft, reaches 78.9 mph and runs 5,315 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Silver Star?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Silver Star, according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Silver Star?

Silver Star is 239.5 ft tall, with a drop of 219.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Silver Star go?

Its top speed is 78.9 mph (127 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Silver Star go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Silver Star scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Silver Star open?

Silver Star opened on 23 Mar 2002.

Who built Silver Star?

Silver Star was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
