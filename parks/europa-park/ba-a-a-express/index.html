Ba-a-a-Express

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 9.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Ba-a-a-Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by ART Engineering GmbH. It opened on 13 Jul 2016. The ride stands 9.8 ft tall and runs 219.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ba-a-a-Express is like

At 9.8 ft tall with 219.8 ft of track, Ba-a-a-Express balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2016, it is the 12th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Europa-Park.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Europa-Park (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ba-a-a-Express sits on the north-east side of Europa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Swiss Bob Run, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Eurosat CanCan Coaster at about 230 m. Ba-a-a-Express layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ba-a-a-Express?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Ba-a-a-Express, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ba-a-a-Express?

Ba-a-a-Express is 9.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Ba-a-a-Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ba-a-a-Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster. It is one of the gentlest coasters you can ride.

When did Ba-a-a-Express open?

Ba-a-a-Express opened on 13 Jul 2016.

Who built Ba-a-a-Express?

Ba-a-a-Express was manufactured by ART Engineering GmbH.

How long is the track on Ba-a-a-Express?

RCDB lists 219.8 ft of track.

How does Ba-a-a-Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ba-a-a-Express at once?

Up to 10 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Ba-a-a-Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as kiddie; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
