Euro Mir

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 92 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Euro Mir is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 12 Jun 1997. The ride stands 92 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 3215.3 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Euro Mir is like

Euro Mir spreads 3,215.3 ft of track across a layout only 92 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1997, it is the 3rd oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Europa-Park.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 12 other coasters at Europa-Park, out of 13 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Euro Mir sits near the middle of Europa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Voltron Nevera, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Swiss Bob Run at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Euro Mir?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Euro Mir. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Euro Mir?

Euro Mir is 92 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Euro Mir go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Euro Mir go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Euro Mir scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Euro Mir open?

Euro Mir opened on 12 Jun 1997.

Who built Euro Mir?

Euro Mir was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Euro Mir?

RCDB lists a ride time of 4 minutes 33 seconds over 3215.3 ft of track.

How does Euro Mir get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a spiral lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Euro Mir at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Euro Mir good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
