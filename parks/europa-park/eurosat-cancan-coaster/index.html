Eurosat CanCan Coaster

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 83.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1989.

Eurosat CanCan Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 5 Aug 1989 and was previously known as Eurosat. The ride stands 83.7 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 3024.8 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Eurosat CanCan Coaster is like

With 3,024.8 ft of track for a 83.7 ft structure, Eurosat CanCan Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1989, it is the 2nd oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Europa-Park. It has run under other names (Eurosat), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 12 other coasters at Europa-Park, out of 13 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Eurosat CanCan Coaster sits on the north side of Europa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Matterhorn Blitz, roughly 50 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Poseidon at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Eurosat CanCan Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Eurosat CanCan Coaster, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Eurosat CanCan Coaster?

Eurosat CanCan Coaster is 83.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Eurosat CanCan Coaster go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Eurosat CanCan Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Eurosat CanCan Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Eurosat CanCan Coaster open?

Eurosat CanCan Coaster opened on 5 Aug 1989 (previously known as Eurosat).

Who built Eurosat CanCan Coaster?

Eurosat CanCan Coaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
