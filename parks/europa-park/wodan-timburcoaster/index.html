Wodan Timburcoaster

Europa-Park · Rust, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 62.1 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Wodan Timburcoaster is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Europa-Park in Rust, Germany, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 31 Mar 2012. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3444.9 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wodan Timburcoaster is like

At 131.2 ft tall with 3,444.9 ft of track, Wodan Timburcoaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2012, it is the 10th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Europa-Park.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 12 other coasters at Europa-Park, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wodan Timburcoaster sits on the south side of Europa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Atlantica SuperSplash, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Blue Fire Megacoaster at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wodan Timburcoaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Wodan Timburcoaster, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Europa-Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wodan Timburcoaster?

Wodan Timburcoaster is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wodan Timburcoaster go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wodan Timburcoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wodan Timburcoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wodan Timburcoaster open?

Wodan Timburcoaster opened on 31 Mar 2012.

Who built Wodan Timburcoaster?

Wodan Timburcoaster was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Wodan Timburcoaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 25 seconds over 3444.9 ft of track.

How does Wodan Timburcoaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wodan Timburcoaster at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,250 riders.

What restraints does Wodan Timburcoaster use?

RCDB lists: t-bar.

Is Wodan Timburcoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
