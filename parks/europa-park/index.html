Europa-Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Rust, Germany · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Europa-Park in Rust, Germany has 14 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Arthur to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Silver Star. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 13 of them.

What the coasters at Europa-Park are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 6 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Europa-Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 29% of its lineup, against 37% across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 12 of the 14 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 14 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Wodan Timburcoaster); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Blue Fire Megacoaster).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 11 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Swiss Bob Run, which opened in 1985.

Europa-Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Europa-Park? By height

At Europa-Park the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 11 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Ba-a-a-Express (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and Alpine Express Enzian (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 12 coasters, the nearest being Arthur at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Atlantica SuperSplash (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Pegasus (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Arthur (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)), Ba-a-a-Express (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and Alpine Express Enzian (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Matterhorn Blitz at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 14 coasters with a verified rule: Arthur and Ba-a-a-Express. With a companion they can also ride Matterhorn Blitz (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)), Eurosat CanCan Coaster (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)), Swiss Bob Run (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)), Poseidon (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)), Atlantica SuperSplash (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Pegasus (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Wodan Timburcoaster (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)) and Alpine Express Enzian (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Euro Mir at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 13 of 14 coasters with a verified rule, 11 more than at the height below: Matterhorn Blitz, Euro Mir, Eurosat CanCan Coaster, Swiss Bob Run, Poseidon, Atlantica SuperSplash, Pegasus, Blue Fire Megacoaster, Voltron Nevera, Wodan Timburcoaster and Alpine Express Enzian. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Silver Star at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Euro Mir (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Eurosat CanCan Coaster (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Europa-Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Europa-Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Ba-a-a-Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 9.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Matterhorn Blitz, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Voltron Nevera, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 106.6 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Europa-Park?

Roller coaster height requirements at Europa-Park run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Arthur) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Silver Star), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Europa-Park have?

Europa-Park has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Europa-Park?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Voltron Nevera (106.6 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Europa-Park?

Silver Star, at 239.5 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Europa-Park?

Silver Star, with a top speed of 78.9 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Europa-Park for first-timers?

Ba-a-a-Express is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 120 cm (about 47.2 in) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Europa-Park?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Atlantica SuperSplash, Pegasus, Arthur, Ba-a-a-Express, Alpine Express Enzian.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Europa-Park?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Arthur, Ba-a-a-Express. With a companion they can also ride Matterhorn Blitz, Eurosat CanCan Coaster, Swiss Bob Run, Poseidon, Atlantica SuperSplash, Pegasus, Wodan Timburcoaster, Alpine Express Enzian.

Which coasters at Europa-Park do not go upside down?
