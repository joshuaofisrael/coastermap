Europa-Park roller coasters

Rust, Germany · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Europa-Park in Rust, Germany has 14 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Silver Star at 239.5 ft, which is also the fastest at 78.9 mph. The lowest height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), for Arthur.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Europa-Park have?

Europa-Park has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Europa-Park?

Silver Star, at 239.5 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Europa-Park?

Silver Star, with a top speed of 78.9 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Europa-Park?

By height requirement, Arthur has the lowest minimum at 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Europa-Park coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Arthur, Ba-a-a-Express. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Europa-Park do not go upside down?

Matterhorn Blitz, Euro Mir, Eurosat CanCan Coaster, Swiss Bob Run, Silver Star, Poseidon, Atlantica SuperSplash, Pegasus, Wodan Timburcoaster, Arthur, Ba-a-a-Express, Alpine Express Enzian have no inversions, per RCDB.
