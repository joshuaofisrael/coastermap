Frontier City height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Oklahoma City, Oklahoma · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Frontier City in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 38 in (97 cm) on Steel Lasso to 48 in (122 cm) on Silver Bullet. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Frontier City are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Frontier City a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 42 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Wildcat); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Diamondback).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Silver Bullet, which opened in 1986.

What can my child ride at Frontier City? By height

At Frontier City the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Steel Lasso at 38 in (97 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Wildcat, Steel Lasso and Frankie's Mine Train. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Silver Bullet at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Silver Bullet and Diamondback. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Silver Bullet, Wildcat, Diamondback, Steel Lasso and Frankie's Mine Train. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Steel Lasso (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Frontier City for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Frontier City, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Frankie's Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wildcat, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 75 ft tall, 46 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Diamondback, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 56 ft tall, 45 mph, 1 inversion, 4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Frontier City?

Roller coaster height requirements at Frontier City run from 38 in (97 cm) (Steel Lasso) to 48 in (122 cm) (Silver Bullet), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

Frontier City has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Frontier City?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Diamondback (56 ft tall, 45 mph, 1 inversion, 4 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Frontier City?

What is the best coaster at Frontier City for first-timers?
