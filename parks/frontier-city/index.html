Frontier City roller coasters

Oklahoma City, Oklahoma · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Frontier City in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma has 5 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Silver Bullet at 80 ft, which is also the fastest at 47.8 mph. The lowest height requirement is 38 in (97 cm), for Steel Lasso.
