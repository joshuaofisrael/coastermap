Steel Lasso

Frontier City · Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 38 in (97 cm); top speed 30 mph; 49 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Steel Lasso is a steel suspended roller coaster at Frontier City in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, built by Vekoma. It opened on 18 Jul 2008. The ride stands 49 ft tall, reaches 30 mph and runs 967.8 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Steel Lasso is like

At 49 ft tall with 967.8 ft of track, Steel Lasso balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2008, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Frontier City. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 294m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer at Movie Park Germany. Height rules for this model differ by park: Frontier City sets 38 in, while Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

Its 38 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Frontier City, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Lasso sits on the south-east side of Frontier City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Silver Bullet, roughly 100 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wildcat at about 180 m. Steel Lasso layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Frontier City rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

How Steel Lasso compares

On our intensity ranking Steel Lasso is number 4 of 5 at Frontier City, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Lasso?

Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall to ride Steel Lasso. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to Frontier City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Lasso?

Steel Lasso is 49 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Lasso go?

Its top speed is 30 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Lasso go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steel Lasso scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Frontier City rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Steel Lasso open?

Steel Lasso opened on 18 Jul 2008.

Who built Steel Lasso?

Steel Lasso was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Steel Lasso?

RCDB lists 967.8 ft of track.

How does Steel Lasso get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steel Lasso at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Steel Lasso good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 30 mph; the minimum height is 38 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Steel Lasso at Frontier City the same as Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer at Movie Park Germany?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 294m coasters on RCDB; the Frontier City ride opened in 2008 and the Movie Park Germany ride in 2007; Frontier City sets 38 in and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm; RCDB lists heights of 49 ft and 42.7 ft.
