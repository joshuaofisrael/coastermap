Wildcat

Frontier City · Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 46 mph; 75 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1991.

Wildcat is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Frontier City in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, built by National Amusement Device Company. It opened on 20 Apr 1991. The ride stands 75 ft tall, drops 65 ft, reaches 46 mph and runs 2,653 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wildcat is like

Wildcat spreads 2,653 ft of track across a layout only 75 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1991, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Frontier City.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Frontier City, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wildcat sits on the south-west side of Frontier City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Silver Bullet, roughly 160 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Steel Lasso at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Frontier City rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wildcat?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Wildcat, according to Frontier City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wildcat?

Wildcat is 75 ft tall, with a drop of 65 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wildcat go?

Its top speed is 46 mph (74 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wildcat go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wildcat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Frontier City rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wildcat open?

Wildcat opened on 20 Apr 1991.

Who built Wildcat?

Wildcat was manufactured by National Amusement Device Company.

How long is the ride on Wildcat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 2,653 ft of track.

How does Wildcat get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wildcat at once?

Each train carries 18 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wildcat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 46 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Wildcat operate before?

RCDB records it at Fairyland Park (as Wildcat).

Is Wildcat at Frontier City the same as Wildcat at Lake Compounce?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Frontier City ride opened in 1991 and the Lake Compounce ride in 1927; Frontier City sets 42 in and Lake Compounce sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 75 ft and 85 ft.
