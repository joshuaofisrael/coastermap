Diamondback

Frontier City · Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 45 mph; 56 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1994.

Diamondback is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Frontier City in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on May 1994. The ride stands 56 ft tall, drops 47 ft, reaches 45 mph and runs 635 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Frontier City rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Diamondback?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Diamondback, according to Frontier City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Diamondback?

Diamondback is 56 ft tall, with a drop of 47 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Diamondback go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Diamondback go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Diamondback scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Frontier City rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Diamondback open?

Diamondback opened on May 1994.

Who built Diamondback?

Diamondback was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.
