Car Racing

Kiftzuba · Kibbutz Tzuba, near Jerusalem, Israel

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 26.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Car Racing is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kiftzuba in Kibbutz Tzuba, near Jerusalem, Israel, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on Aug 2024. The ride stands 26.2 ft tall and runs 426.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Car Racing is like

Car Racing pairs a 26.2 ft structure with 426.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Kiftzuba. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Race Coaster (MX611) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land, Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's World, Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's Land and Zippy Zappy Coaster at Land of Legends. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Kiftzuba (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Car Racing layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Car Racing?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Car Racing, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as its car-race coaster: children from 95 cm to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Kiftzuba, official ride page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Car Racing?

Car Racing is 26.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Car Racing go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Car Racing scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Car Racing open?

Car Racing opened on Aug 2024.

Who built Car Racing?

Car Racing was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Car Racing?

RCDB lists 426.5 ft of track.

How does Car Racing get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Car Racing at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Car Racing good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Car Racing at Kiftzuba the same as Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land?

Both are SBF Visa Group Race Coaster (MX611) coasters on RCDB; the Kiftzuba ride opened in 2024 and the Ferrari Land ride in 2018; both list a 120 cm minimum.
