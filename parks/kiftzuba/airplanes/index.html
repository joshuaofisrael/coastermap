Airplanes

Kiftzuba · Kibbutz Tzuba, near Jerusalem, Israel

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Airplanes is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kiftzuba in Kibbutz Tzuba, near Jerusalem, Israel, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in Sep 2022. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Airplanes is like

Opened in 2022, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Kiftzuba. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Double Coaster (MX605) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Choco Express at Rainbow's End. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kiftzuba sets 130 cm, while Rainbow's End sets 120 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Kiftzuba. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Airplanes layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Airplanes' track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill
  2. Helix (540 °)
  3. Booster Wheel Lift Hill
  4. Helix (540 °)

Airplanes trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 5 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 20 riders.
Riders per train20

On kid height: it appears on our 54-inch list as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36, 42 and 48 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Airplanes

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as its airplanes coaster: for children from 90 cm, with riders up to 130 cm accompanied by an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Airplanes?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Airplanes, or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as its airplanes coaster: for children from 90 cm, with riders up to 130 cm accompanied by an adult, according to Kiftzuba, official ride page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Airplanes go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Airplanes scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Airplanes open?

Airplanes opened in Sep 2022.

Who built Airplanes?

Airplanes was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Airplanes get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Airplanes at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Airplanes good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Airplanes at Kiftzuba the same as Choco Express at Rainbow's End?

Both are SBF Visa Group Double Coaster (MX605) coasters on RCDB; the Kiftzuba ride opened in 2022 and the Rainbow's End ride in 2012; Kiftzuba sets 130 cm and Rainbow's End sets 120 cm.
