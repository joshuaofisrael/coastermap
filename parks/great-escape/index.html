Great Escape height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Formerly Six Flags Great Escape · Queensbury, New York · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Great Escape (formerly Six Flags Great Escape) in Queensbury, New York has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Frankie's Mine Train to 48 in (122 cm) on Comet. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Great Escape are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Great Escape a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 47% across all 113 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Comet and The Bobcat).

The most common builder is Arrow Dynamics, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Steamin' Demon, which opened in 1984.

What can my child ride at Great Escape? By height

At Great Escape the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Frankie's Mine Train at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Frankie's Mine Train. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Comet at 48 in (122 cm).

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Comet, Steamin' Demon, Flashback, Canyon Blaster and The Bobcat. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Comet, Steamin' Demon, Flashback, Canyon Blaster, Frankie's Mine Train and The Bobcat. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Frankie's Mine Train (up to 54 in (137 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Great Escape for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Great Escape, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Frankie's Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Canyon Blaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 56 ft tall, 45 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Flashback, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is Great Escape the same park as Six Flags Great Escape?

Yes. Great Escape in Queensbury, New York is the park formerly called Six Flags Great Escape; sixflags.com/greatescape redirects to the Enchanted Parks site, and RCDB lists the park as Great Escape (checked 10 Nov 2026).

What are the height requirements at Great Escape?

Roller coaster height requirements at Great Escape run from 42 in (107 cm) (Frankie's Mine Train) to 48 in (122 cm) (Comet), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Great Escape have?

Great Escape has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Great Escape?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Flashback (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Great Escape?

Flashback, at 116.5 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Great Escape?

Comet, with a top speed of 55 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Great Escape for first-timers?

Frankie's Mine Train is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 42 in (107 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Great Escape?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Frankie's Mine Train. With a companion they can also ride Canyon Blaster, The Bobcat.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Great Escape?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Comet, Steamin' Demon, Flashback, Canyon Blaster, Frankie's Mine Train, The Bobcat.

Which coasters at Great Escape do not go upside down?

Comet, Canyon Blaster, Frankie's Mine Train, The Bobcat have no inversions, per RCDB.
