Steamin' Demon

Great Escape · Queensbury, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 85 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1984.

Steamin' Demon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Great Escape in Queensbury, New York, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 26 May 1984. The ride stands 85 ft tall, drops 65 ft, reaches 40 mph and runs 1,565 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Steamin' Demon is like

Steamin' Demon pairs a 85 ft structure with 1,565 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,565 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1984, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Great Escape. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Loop & Corkscrew design, and the first of them to open. Others include Halilintar at Dufan and Corkscrew Coaster at Rainbow's End. Height rules for this model differ by park: Great Escape sets 48 in, while Dufan sets 125 cm and Rainbow's End sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Great Escape, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steamin' Demon sits on the west side of Great Escape's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Canyon Blaster, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Frankie's Mine Train at about 300 m. Steamin' Demon layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 3 elements RCDB records on Steamin' Demon's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Double Corkscrew

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Great Escape rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Steamin' Demon compares

On our intensity ranking Steamin' Demon is number 2 of 6 at Great Escape, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Comet (95 ft tall, 55 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Flashback (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 48 in minimum).

Measured against every coaster on Coaster Map with a listed figure, Steamin' Demon is taller than 56% and faster than 38% of them.

Before you ride Steamin' Demon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Great Escape app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steamin' Demon?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Steamin' Demon, according to Great Escape, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steamin' Demon?

Steamin' Demon is 85 ft tall, with a drop of 65 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steamin' Demon go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steamin' Demon go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Steamin' Demon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Great Escape rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Steamin' Demon open?

Steamin' Demon opened on 26 May 1984.

Who built Steamin' Demon?

Steamin' Demon was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Steamin' Demon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1,565 ft of track.

How does Steamin' Demon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steamin' Demon at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Steamin' Demon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Steamin' Demon operate before?

RCDB records it at Pontchartrain Beach (as Ragin' Cajun).

Is Steamin' Demon at Great Escape the same as Corkscrew Coaster at Rainbow's End?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Loop & Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Great Escape ride opened in 1984 and the Rainbow's End ride in 1986; Great Escape sets 48 in and Rainbow's End sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 85 ft and 90 ft.
