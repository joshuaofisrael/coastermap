Frankie's Mine Train

Great Escape · Queensbury, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Frankie's Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Great Escape in Queensbury, New York, built by Zamperla. It first opened on May 2005 and was previously known as Road Runner Express. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Frankie's Mine Train is like

At 13 ft tall with 262.5 ft of track, Frankie's Mine Train balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2005, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Great Escape. It has run under other names (Road Runner Express), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Great Escape sets 42 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Great Escape, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Frankie's Mine Train sits near the middle of Great Escape's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Bobcat, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flashback at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Great Escape rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, and no taller than 54 in (137 cm).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Frankie's Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Frankie's Mine Train. The maximum rider height is 54 in (137 cm), according to Great Escape, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Frankie's Mine Train?

Frankie's Mine Train is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Frankie's Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Frankie's Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Great Escape rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Frankie's Mine Train open?

Frankie's Mine Train opened on May 2005 (previously known as Road Runner Express).

Who built Frankie's Mine Train?

Frankie's Mine Train was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Frankie's Mine Train?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Frankie's Mine Train get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Frankie's Mine Train at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Frankie's Mine Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Frankie's Mine Train at Great Escape the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Great Escape ride opened in 2005 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; both list a 42 in minimum.
