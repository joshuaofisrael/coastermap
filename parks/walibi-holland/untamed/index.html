Untamed

Walibi Holland · Biddinghuizen, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 57.2 mph; 119.8 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2019.

Untamed is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Holland in Biddinghuizen, Netherlands, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 1 Jul 2019. The ride stands 119.8 ft tall, drops 116.1 ft, reaches 57.2 mph and runs 3559.7 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Untamed is like

Untamed pairs a 119.8 ft structure with 3,559.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,559.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the 7th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Walibi Holland.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Walibi Holland, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Untamed sits on the north-west side of Walibi Holland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Eat My Dust, roughly 250 m away (105 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Condor at about 330 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Untamed

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walibi Holland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Untamed?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Untamed, according to Walibi Holland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Untamed?

Untamed is 119.8 ft tall, with a drop of 116.1 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Untamed go?

Its top speed is 57.2 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Untamed go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Untamed scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Untamed open?

Untamed opened on 1 Jul 2019.

Who built Untamed?

Untamed was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the ride on Untamed?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 46 seconds over 3559.7 ft of track.

How does Untamed get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Untamed at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Untamed good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 57.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Untamed at Walibi Holland the same as Untamed at Canobie Lake?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Walibi Holland ride opened in 2019 and the Canobie Lake ride in 2011; Walibi Holland sets 120 cm and Canobie Lake sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 119.8 ft and 72.2 ft.
