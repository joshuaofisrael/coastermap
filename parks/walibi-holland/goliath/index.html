Goliath

Walibi Holland · Biddinghuizen, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 65.9 mph; 153.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Goliath is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Holland in Biddinghuizen, Netherlands, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 29 Mar 2002. The ride stands 153.7 ft tall, drops 150.9 ft, reaches 65.9 mph and runs 3982.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Goliath is like

Goliath pairs a 153.7 ft structure with 3,982.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the 5th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Walibi Holland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Walibi Holland, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Goliath sits on the south side of Walibi Holland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is YoY, roughly 160 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Xpress: Platform 13 at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Goliath?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Goliath, according to Walibi Holland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Goliath?

Goliath is 153.7 ft tall, with a drop of 150.9 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Goliath go?

Its top speed is 65.9 mph (106 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Goliath go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Goliath scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Goliath open?

Goliath opened on 29 Mar 2002.

Who built Goliath?

Goliath was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Is Goliath at Walibi Holland the same as Goliath at Magic Mountain?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Walibi Holland ride opened in 2002 and the Magic Mountain ride in 2000; Walibi Holland sets 140 cm and Magic Mountain sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 153.7 ft and 235 ft.
