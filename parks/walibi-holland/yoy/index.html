YoY

Walibi Holland · Biddinghuizen, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 51 mph; 95.1 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2025.

YoY is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Holland in Biddinghuizen, Netherlands, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 5 Apr 2025. The ride stands 95.1 ft tall, drops 88.6 ft, reaches 51 mph and runs 2,149 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding YoY is like

YoY pairs a 95.1 ft structure with 2,149 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. With 6 inversions in 2,149 ft, it flips riders roughly every 358 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 9 coasters we list at Walibi Holland.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Walibi Holland, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, YoY sits on the south side of Walibi Holland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Xpress: Platform 13, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Condor at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride YoY

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walibi Holland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for YoY?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride YoY, according to Walibi Holland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is YoY?

YoY is 95.1 ft tall, with a drop of 88.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does YoY go?

Its top speed is 51 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does YoY go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is YoY scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did YoY open?

YoY opened on 5 Apr 2025.

Who built YoY?

YoY was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on YoY?

RCDB lists 2,149 ft (655 m) of track.

How does YoY get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride YoY at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is YoY good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 51 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
