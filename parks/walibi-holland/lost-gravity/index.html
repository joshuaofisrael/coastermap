Lost Gravity

Walibi Holland · Biddinghuizen, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 54.1 mph; 105 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2016.

Lost Gravity is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Holland in Biddinghuizen, Netherlands, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 24 Mar 2016. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 54.1 mph and runs 2,231 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Lost Gravity is like

At 105 ft tall with 2,231 ft of track, Lost Gravity balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,231 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2016, it is the 6th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Walibi Holland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Walibi Holland, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Lost Gravity sits on the north-east side of Walibi Holland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Drako, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Speed of Sound at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Lost Gravity compares

Before you ride Lost Gravity

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walibi Holland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Lost Gravity?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Lost Gravity, according to Walibi Holland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Lost Gravity?

Lost Gravity is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Lost Gravity go?

Its top speed is 54.1 mph (87 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Lost Gravity go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Lost Gravity scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Lost Gravity open?

Lost Gravity opened on 24 Mar 2016.

Who built Lost Gravity?

Lost Gravity was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Lost Gravity?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 23 seconds over 2,231 ft of track.

How does Lost Gravity get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Lost Gravity at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Lost Gravity good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 54.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
