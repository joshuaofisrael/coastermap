Big Thunder Mountain Railroad

Magic Kingdom · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 38 in (97 cm); top speed 36 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1980.

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida. It opened on 15 Nov 1980. The ride reaches 36 mph and runs 2,780 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Thunder Mountain Railroad is like

Opened in 1980, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Magic Kingdom.

A rider who just meets its 38 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Magic Kingdom, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad sits on the west side of Magic Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, roughly 490 m away (38 in minimum). Next closest is The Barnstormer at about 620 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Thunder Mountain Railroad?

Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall to ride Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, according to Walt Disney World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad go?

Its top speed is 36 mph (58 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Thunder Mountain Railroad open?

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad opened on 15 Nov 1980.

How long is the ride on Big Thunder Mountain Railroad?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 30 seconds over 2,780 ft of track.

How does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at once?

Up to 30 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 36 mph; the minimum height is 38 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at Magic Kingdom the same as Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at Disneyland?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Magic Kingdom ride opened in 1980 and the Disneyland ride in 1979; Magic Kingdom sets 38 in and Disneyland sets 40 in.
