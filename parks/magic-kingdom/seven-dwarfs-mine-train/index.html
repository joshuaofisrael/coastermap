Seven Dwarfs Mine Train

Magic Kingdom · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 38 in (97 cm); top speed 34 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Seven Dwarfs Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 May 2014. The ride drops 41 ft, reaches 34 mph and runs 2001.3 ft of track. The cars rock from side to side as the train takes the curves. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall to ride Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, according to Walt Disney World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Seven Dwarfs Mine Train go?

Its top speed is 34 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Seven Dwarfs Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Seven Dwarfs Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Seven Dwarfs Mine Train open?

Seven Dwarfs Mine Train opened on 28 May 2014.

Who built Seven Dwarfs Mine Train?

Seven Dwarfs Mine Train was manufactured by Vekoma.
