Space Mountain

Magic Kingdom · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 44 in (112 cm); top speed 27 mph; 90 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1975.

Space Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida. It opened on 15 Jan 1975. The ride stands 90 ft tall, reaches 27 mph and runs 3,196 ft of track on each track. It runs on two tracks side by side. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Space Mountain is like

Space Mountain spreads 3,196 ft of track across a layout only 90 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1975, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Magic Kingdom.

A rider who just meets its 44 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Magic Kingdom, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Space Mountain sits on the south-east side of Magic Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is TRON Lightcycle / Run, roughly 170 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Barnstormer at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Space Mountain?

Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall to ride Space Mountain, according to Walt Disney World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Space Mountain?

Space Mountain is 90 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Space Mountain go?

Its top speed is 27 mph (43 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Space Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Space Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Space Mountain open?

Space Mountain opened on 15 Jan 1975.

How long is the ride on Space Mountain?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 35 seconds over 3,196 ft of track.

How many people ride Space Mountain at once?

Up to 6 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Space Mountain good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 27 mph; the minimum height is 44 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Space Mountain at Magic Kingdom the same as Space Mountain at Disneyland?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Magic Kingdom ride opened in 1975 and the Disneyland ride in 1977; Magic Kingdom sets 44 in and Disneyland sets 40 in.
