The Barnstormer

Magic Kingdom · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 35 in (89 cm); top speed 25 mph; 30 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

The Barnstormer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1996. The ride stands 30 ft tall, reaches 25 mph and runs 794 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Barnstormer is like

The Barnstormer pairs a 30 ft structure with 794 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1996, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Magic Kingdom.

Its 35 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Magic Kingdom, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Barnstormer sits on the east side of Magic Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, roughly 120 m away (38 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is TRON Lightcycle / Run at about 180 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Barnstormer?

Riders must be at least 35 in (89 cm) tall to ride The Barnstormer, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Barnstormer?

The Barnstormer is 30 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Barnstormer go?

Its top speed is 25 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Barnstormer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Barnstormer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did The Barnstormer open?

The Barnstormer opened in 1996.

Who built The Barnstormer?

The Barnstormer was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on The Barnstormer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 794 ft of track.

How does The Barnstormer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Barnstormer at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is The Barnstormer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 25 mph; the minimum height is 35 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is The Barnstormer at Magic Kingdom the same as The Barnstormer at Adventure Island?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Magic Kingdom ride opened in 1996 and the Adventure Island ride in 2000; Magic Kingdom sets 35 in and Adventure Island sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 30 ft and 29.5 ft.
