Magic Kingdom roller coasters

Lake Buena Vista, Florida · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida has 5 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Space Mountain at 90 ft and the fastest is TRON Lightcycle / Run at 59.3 mph. The lowest height requirement is 35 in (89 cm), for The Barnstormer.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Magic Kingdom have?

Magic Kingdom has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Magic Kingdom?

Space Mountain, at 90 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Magic Kingdom?

TRON Lightcycle / Run, with a top speed of 59.3 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Magic Kingdom?

By height requirement, The Barnstormer has the lowest minimum at 35 in (89 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Magic Kingdom coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Magic Kingdom coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Space Mountain, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, TRON Lightcycle / Run. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Magic Kingdom do not go upside down?

Space Mountain, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, TRON Lightcycle / Run have no inversions, per RCDB.
