Magic Kingdom height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Lake Buena Vista, Florida · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 35 in (89 cm) on The Barnstormer to 48 in (122 cm) on TRON Lightcycle / Run. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Magic Kingdom are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Magic Kingdom a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 47% of coasters across all 113 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 38 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Space Mountain, which opened in 1975.

What can my child ride at Magic Kingdom? By height

At Magic Kingdom the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: The Barnstormer. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at 38 in (97 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Big Thunder Mountain Railroad and Seven Dwarfs Mine Train. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Space Mountain at 44 in (112 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Space Mountain and TRON Lightcycle / Run. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Space Mountain, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and TRON Lightcycle / Run. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Magic Kingdom for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Magic Kingdom, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Barnstormer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (35 in minimum; 30 ft tall, 25 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (38 in minimum; 36 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: TRON Lightcycle / Run, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 78.1 ft tall, 59.3 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Magic Kingdom?

Roller coaster height requirements at Magic Kingdom run from 35 in (89 cm) (The Barnstormer) to 48 in (122 cm) (TRON Lightcycle / Run), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Magic Kingdom have?

Magic Kingdom has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Magic Kingdom?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is TRON Lightcycle / Run (78.1 ft tall, 59.3 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Magic Kingdom?

Space Mountain, at 90 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Magic Kingdom?

TRON Lightcycle / Run, with a top speed of 59.3 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Magic Kingdom for first-timers?

The Barnstormer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 35 in (89 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Magic Kingdom?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Magic Kingdom?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Space Mountain, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, TRON Lightcycle / Run.

Which coasters at Magic Kingdom do not go upside down?

Space Mountain, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, The Barnstormer, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, TRON Lightcycle / Run have no inversions, per RCDB.
