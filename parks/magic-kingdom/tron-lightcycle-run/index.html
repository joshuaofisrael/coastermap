TRON Lightcycle / Run

Magic Kingdom · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 59.3 mph; 78.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

TRON Lightcycle / Run is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Kingdom in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It opened on 4 Apr 2023. The ride stands 78.1 ft tall, reaches 59.3 mph and runs 3169.3 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

What is the height requirement for TRON Lightcycle / Run?

TRON Lightcycle / Run is 78.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does TRON Lightcycle / Run go?

No. It has no inversions.

Is TRON Lightcycle / Run scary?
