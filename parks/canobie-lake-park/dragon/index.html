Dragon

Canobie Lake Park · Salem, New Hampshire

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 0 inversions; opened 1991.

Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canobie Lake Park in Salem, New Hampshire, built by Zamperla. It opened in 1991. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dragon is like

Opened in 1991, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Canobie Lake Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Powered Coaster / Twin Helix design, and the first of them to open. Others include Big Red Boat at Dreamworld and Speedi '64 at Hopi Hari. Height rules for this model differ by park: Canobie Lake sets 42 in, while Dreamworld sets 105 cm and Hopi Hari sets 120 cm.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Canobie Lake, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragon sits on the south-west side of Canobie Lake's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Untamed, roughly 280 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Yankee Cannonball at about 290 m. Before you ride Dragon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 42 in (107 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park says riders under 42 in may ride with a supervising companion.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Canobie Lake Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragon?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Dragon. The park says riders under 42 in may ride with a supervising companion, according to Canobie Lake Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Dragon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Dragon open?

Dragon opened in 1991.

Who built Dragon?

Dragon was manufactured by Zamperla.

How many people ride Dragon at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Dragon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Dragon operate before?

RCDB records it at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park (as Dragon).

Is Dragon at Canobie Lake the same as Speedi '64 at Hopi Hari?

Both are Zamperla Powered Coaster / Twin Helix coasters on RCDB; the Canobie Lake ride opened in 1991 and the Hopi Hari ride in 1999; Canobie Lake sets 42 in and Hopi Hari sets 120 cm.
