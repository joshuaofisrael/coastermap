Canobie Lake Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Salem, New Hampshire · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Canobie Lake Park in Salem, New Hampshire has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Dragon to 48 in (122 cm) on Untamed. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Canobie Lake Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Canobie Lake Park a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 42% across all 183 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Yankee Cannonball).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Yankee Cannonball, which opened in 1936.

What can my child ride at Canobie Lake Park? By height

At Canobie Lake Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Canobie Lake Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Canobie Lake Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dragon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Yankee Cannonball, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 63.5 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Untamed, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 72.2 ft tall, 43.5 mph, 3 inversions, 4.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
