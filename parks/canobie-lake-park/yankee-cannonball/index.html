Yankee Cannonball

Canobie Lake Park · Salem, New Hampshire

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 35 mph; 63.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1936.

Yankee Cannonball is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Canobie Lake Park in Salem, New Hampshire, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It first opened in 1936 and was previously known as Greyhound. The ride stands 63.5 ft tall, reaches 35 mph and runs 2,000 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Yankee Cannonball is like

Yankee Cannonball spreads 2,000 ft of track across a layout only 63.5 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1936, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Canobie Lake Park. It has run under other names (Greyhound), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Canobie Lake, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Yankee Cannonball sits on the east side of Canobie Lake's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Untamed, roughly 150 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon at about 290 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Yankee Cannonball?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Yankee Cannonball, according to Canobie Lake Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Yankee Cannonball?

Yankee Cannonball is 63.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Yankee Cannonball go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Yankee Cannonball go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Yankee Cannonball scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Yankee Cannonball open?

Yankee Cannonball opened in 1936 (previously known as Greyhound).

Who built Yankee Cannonball?

Yankee Cannonball was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the track on Yankee Cannonball?

RCDB lists 2,000 ft (610 m) of track.

How many people ride Yankee Cannonball at once?

Each train carries 18 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Yankee Cannonball use?

RCDB lists: single position lap bar, headrest.

Is Yankee Cannonball good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Yankee Cannonball operate before?

RCDB records it at Lakewood Park (as Meteor).
