Hiccup's Wing Gliders

Universal Epic Universe · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Hiccup's Wing Gliders is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 22 May 2025. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hiccup's Wing Gliders is like

Opened in 2025, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Epic Universe.

Its 40 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Epic Universe (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Hiccup's Wing Gliders layout, trains and ride system

Hiccup's Wing Gliders starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hiccup's Wing Gliders?

Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall to ride Hiccup's Wing Gliders, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Hiccup's Wing Gliders go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hiccup's Wing Gliders scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Hiccup's Wing Gliders open?

Hiccup's Wing Gliders opened on 22 May 2025.

Who built Hiccup's Wing Gliders?

Hiccup's Wing Gliders was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Hiccup's Wing Gliders launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Hiccup's Wing Gliders at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

What restraints does Hiccup's Wing Gliders use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Hiccup's Wing Gliders good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 40 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
