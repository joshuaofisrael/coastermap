Universal Epic Universe roller coasters

Orlando, Florida · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida has 4 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Stardust Racers at 133 ft, which is also the fastest at 62 mph. The lowest height requirement is 40 in (102 cm), for Hiccup's Wing Gliders.

How many roller coasters does Universal Epic Universe have?

What is the tallest roller coaster at Universal Epic Universe?

Stardust Racers, with a top speed of 62 mph (RCDB).
