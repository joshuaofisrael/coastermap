Universal Epic Universe height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Orlando, Florida · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 40 in (102 cm) on Hiccup's Wing Gliders to 48 in (122 cm) on Stardust Racers. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Epic Universe are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Universal Epic Universe a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 52% across all 80 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 40 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Stardust Racers, which opened in 2025.

What can my child ride at Universal Epic Universe? By height

At Universal Epic Universe the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Hiccup's Wing Gliders at 40 in (102 cm).

Best coasters at Universal Epic Universe for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Epic Universe, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mine-Cart Madness, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (40 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Curse of the Werewolf, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (40 in minimum; 37 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Stardust Racers, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 133 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
