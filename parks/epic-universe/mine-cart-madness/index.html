Mine-Cart Madness

Universal Epic Universe · Orlando, Florida

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Mine-Cart Madness is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida, built by Setpoint. It opened on 22 May 2025. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

Mine-Cart Madness stats

Minimum height40 in (102 cm)
Inversions0
Opened22 May 2025
ManufacturerSetpoint
ModelOver-The-Top / Custom
TypeSteel, Sit Down, Thrill, Onboard Sound

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Universal Epic Universe app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mine-Cart Madness?

Riders must be at least 40 inches (102 cm) tall to ride Mine-Cart Madness, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mine-Cart Madness go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mine-Cart Madness scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Mine-Cart Madness open?

Mine-Cart Madness opened on 22 May 2025.

Who built Mine-Cart Madness?

Mine-Cart Madness was manufactured by Setpoint.
