Curse of the Werewolf

Universal Epic Universe · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); top speed 37 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Curse of the Werewolf is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 22 May 2025. The ride reaches 37 mph. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Curse of the Werewolf is like

Opened in 2025, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Epic Universe.

Its 40 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Epic Universe (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Curse of the Werewolf?

Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall to ride Curse of the Werewolf, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Curse of the Werewolf go?

Its top speed is 37 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Curse of the Werewolf go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Curse of the Werewolf scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Curse of the Werewolf open?

Curse of the Werewolf opened on 22 May 2025.

Who built Curse of the Werewolf?

Curse of the Werewolf was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
