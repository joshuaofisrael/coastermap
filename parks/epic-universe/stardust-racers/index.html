Stardust Racers

Universal Epic Universe · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 62 mph; 133 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2025.

Stardust Racers is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 22 May 2025. The ride stands 133 ft tall and reaches 62 mph on each track. It runs on two tracks side by side. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Stardust Racers is like

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Epic Universe.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Epic Universe. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Stardust Racers layout, trains and ride system

Stardust Racers starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 8 elements RCDB records on Stardust Racers' track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch (rolling)
  2. Hill
  3. LSM Boost
  4. Barrel Roll Downdrop
  5. LSM Launch (rolling)
  6. Hill
  7. LSM Boost
  8. Zero-G Roll

It has two tracks, and RCDB may list an element once for each track.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Stardust Racers?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Stardust Racers, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Stardust Racers?

Stardust Racers is 133 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Stardust Racers go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Stardust Racers go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Stardust Racers scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Stardust Racers open?

Stardust Racers opened on 22 May 2025.

Who built Stardust Racers?

Stardust Racers was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

Does Stardust Racers launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Stardust Racers at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Stardust Racers good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 62 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
