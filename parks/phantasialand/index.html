Phantasialand roller coasters

Brühl, Germany · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany has 7 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Taron at 98.4 ft, which is also the fastest at 72.7 mph. The lowest height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), for Colorado Adventure.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Phantasialand have?

Phantasialand has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Phantasialand?

Taron, at 98.4 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Phantasialand?

Taron, with a top speed of 72.7 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Phantasialand?

By height requirement, Colorado Adventure has the lowest minimum at 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Phantasialand coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Colorado Adventure, Crazy Bats, Raik. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Phantasialand do not go upside down?

Colorado Adventure, Crazy Bats, Winja's Fear & Force, Taron, Raik have no inversions, per RCDB.
