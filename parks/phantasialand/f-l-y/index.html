F.L.Y.

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 48.5 mph; 64 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2020.

F.L.Y. is a steel flying roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Vekoma. It opened on 17 Sep 2020. The ride stands 64 ft tall, reaches 48.5 mph and runs 4048.6 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding F.L.Y. is like

With 4,048.6 ft of track for a 64 ft structure, F.L.Y. is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 2 times across 4,048.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Phantasialand.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Phantasialand, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, F.L.Y. sits on the west side of Phantasialand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Winja's Fear & Force, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Crazy Bats at about 160 m. F.L.Y. layout, trains and ride system

F.L.Y. starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on F.L.Y.'s track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Dark/Show Section
  2. LSM Launch (rolling)
  3. Corkscrew
  4. LSM Boost
  5. Corkscrew

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

How F.L.Y. compares

On our intensity ranking F.L.Y. is number 3 of 7 at Phantasialand, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Colorado Adventure (85.3 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Black Mamba (85.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 4 inversions, 4 g; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride F.L.Y.

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Children aged 8 to 10 must ride with an adult; children over 10 who meet the height may ride alone.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Phantasialand app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for F.L.Y.?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride F.L.Y., or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Children aged 8 to 10 must ride with an adult; children over 10 who meet the height may ride alone, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is F.L.Y.?

F.L.Y. is 64 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does F.L.Y. go?

Its top speed is 48.5 mph (78 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does F.L.Y. go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is F.L.Y. scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did F.L.Y. open?

F.L.Y. opened on 17 Sep 2020.

Who built F.L.Y.?

F.L.Y. was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on F.L.Y.?

RCDB lists 4048.6 ft of track.

Does F.L.Y. launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride F.L.Y. at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,440 riders.

Is F.L.Y. good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 48.5 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
