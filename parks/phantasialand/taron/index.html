Taron

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 72.7 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Taron is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 30 Jun 2016. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 72.7 mph and runs 4330.8 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Taron?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Taron, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Taron?

Taron is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Taron go?

Its top speed is 72.7 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Taron go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Taron scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Taron open?

Taron opened on 30 Jun 2016.

Who built Taron?

Taron was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
