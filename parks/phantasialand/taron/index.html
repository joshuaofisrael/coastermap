Taron

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 72.7 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Taron is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 30 Jun 2016. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 72.7 mph and runs 4330.8 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Taron is like

Taron spreads 4,330.8 ft of track across a layout only 98.4 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2016, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Phantasialand.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Phantasialand, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Taron sits on the east side of Phantasialand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Raik, roughly 50 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Colorado Adventure at about 110 m. Taron layout, trains and ride system

Taron starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Taron?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Taron, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Taron?

Taron is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Taron go?

Its top speed is 72.7 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Taron go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Taron scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Taron open?

Taron opened on 30 Jun 2016.

Who built Taron?

Taron was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Taron?

RCDB lists 4330.8 ft of track.

Does Taron launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Taron at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Taron good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 72.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
