Winja's Fear & Force

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 57.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Winja's Fear & Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 30 Mar 2002. The ride stands 57.1 ft tall, drops 52.5 ft, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1345.1 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Winja's Fear & Force is like

Winja's Fear & Force pairs a 57.1 ft structure with 1,345.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Phantasialand. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Dragon's Fury at Chessington, Salama at Linnanmäki, Naga Bay at Bobbejaanland and Tarántula at Parque de Atracciones. Height rules for this model differ by park: Phantasialand sets 140 cm, while Chessington sets 120 cm, Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm and MagicLand sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Phantasialand, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Winja's Fear & Force sits on the west side of Phantasialand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Crazy Bats, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is F.L.Y. at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

How Winja's Fear & Force compares

On our intensity ranking Winja's Fear & Force is number 5 of 7 at Phantasialand, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Crazy Bats (38.4 ft tall, 28.9 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Colorado Adventure (85.3 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Site-wide, Winja's Fear & Force is taller than 35% and faster than 31% of the 1082 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Winja's Fear & Force

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Children under 6 may not ride; children aged 6 to 10 must ride with an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Phantasialand app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Winja's Fear & Force?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Winja's Fear & Force, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Children under 6 may not ride; children aged 6 to 10 must ride with an adult, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Winja's Fear & Force?

Winja's Fear & Force is 57.1 ft tall, with a drop of 52.5 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Winja's Fear & Force go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Winja's Fear & Force go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Winja's Fear & Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Winja's Fear & Force open?

Winja's Fear & Force opened on 30 Mar 2002.

Who built Winja's Fear & Force?

Winja's Fear & Force was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Winja's Fear & Force?

RCDB lists 1345.1 ft of track.

How does Winja's Fear & Force get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Winja's Fear & Force at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,440 riders.

Is Winja's Fear & Force good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand the same as Dragon's Fury at Chessington?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 coasters on RCDB; the Phantasialand ride opened in 2002 and the Chessington ride in 2004; Phantasialand sets 140 cm and Chessington sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 57.1 ft and 50.8 ft.
