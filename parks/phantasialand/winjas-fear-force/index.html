Winja's Fear & Force

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 57.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Winja's Fear & Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 30 Mar 2002. The ride stands 57.1 ft tall, drops 52.5 ft, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1345.1 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Winja's Fear & Force?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Winja's Fear & Force, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Children under 6 may not ride; children aged 6 to 10 must ride with an adult, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Winja's Fear & Force?

Winja's Fear & Force is 57.1 ft tall, with a drop of 52.5 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Winja's Fear & Force go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Winja's Fear & Force go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Winja's Fear & Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Winja's Fear & Force open?

Winja's Fear & Force opened on 30 Mar 2002.

Who built Winja's Fear & Force?

Winja's Fear & Force was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.
