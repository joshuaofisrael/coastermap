Black Mamba

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 85.3 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2006.

Black Mamba is a steel inverted roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 24 May 2006. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall, drops 88.6 ft, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2519.7 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Black Mamba is like

Black Mamba pairs a 85.3 ft structure with 2,519.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,519.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2006, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Phantasialand.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Phantasialand, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Black Mamba sits on the south side of Phantasialand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Colorado Adventure, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is F.L.Y. at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Black Mamba

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Children under 10 may not ride.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Phantasialand app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Black Mamba?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Black Mamba. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Children under 10 may not ride, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Black Mamba?

Black Mamba is 85.3 ft tall, with a drop of 88.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Black Mamba go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Black Mamba go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Black Mamba scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Black Mamba open?

Black Mamba opened on 24 May 2006.

Who built Black Mamba?

Black Mamba was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Black Mamba?

RCDB lists 2519.7 ft of track.

How does Black Mamba get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Black Mamba at once?

Each train carries 32 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,500 riders.

Is Black Mamba good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
