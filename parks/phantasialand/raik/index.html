Raik

Phantasialand · Brühl, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 38.5 mph; 82 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Raik is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Phantasialand in Brühl, Germany, built by Vekoma. It opened on 30 Jun 2016. The ride stands 82 ft tall, reaches 38.5 mph and runs 685.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Raik is like

Raik packs its 685.7 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (82 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together.

Opened in 2016, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Phantasialand.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Phantasialand (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Raik sits on the east side of Phantasialand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Taron, roughly 50 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Colorado Adventure at about 120 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Raik

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Phantasialand app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Raik?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Raik, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Phantasialand, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Raik?

Raik is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Raik go?

Its top speed is 38.5 mph (62 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Raik go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Raik scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Raik open?

Raik opened on 30 Jun 2016.

Who built Raik?

Raik was manufactured by Vekoma.
