Plopsaland Deutschland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Formerly Holiday Park · Haßloch, Germany · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Plopsaland Deutschland (formerly Holiday Park) in Haßloch, Germany has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Tabaluga's Achterbahn to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Expedition GeForce. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Plopsaland Deutschland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Plopsaland Deutschland a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Sky Scream).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Expedition GeForce, which opened in 2001.

Plopsaland Deutschland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Plopsaland Deutschland? By height

At Plopsaland Deutschland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Plopsaland Deutschland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Plopsaland Deutschland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Tabaluga's Achterbahn, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 29.5 ft tall, 24.2 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: 100% Wolf, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 57.4 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Sky Scream, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plopsaland Deutschland the same park as Holiday Park?

Yes. Plopsaland Deutschland in Haßloch, Germany is the park formerly called Holiday Park; RCDB lists Holiday Park as a former name, and holidaypark.de redirects to the Plopsaland Deutschland page on plopsa.com (checked 10 Nov 2026).

What are the height requirements at Plopsaland Deutschland?

Roller coaster height requirements at Plopsaland Deutschland run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Tabaluga's Achterbahn) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Expedition GeForce), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Plopsaland Deutschland have?

Plopsaland Deutschland has 4 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.
