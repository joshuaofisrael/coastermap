Swamp Fox

Family Kingdom Amusement Park · Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 50 mph; 72 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1966.

Swamp Fox is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Family Kingdom Amusement Park in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened in 1966. The ride stands 72 ft tall, drops 62 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,640 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Swamp Fox is like

With 2,640 ft of track for a 72 ft structure, Swamp Fox is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1966, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Family Kingdom Amusement Park.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Swamp Fox sits near the middle of Family Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon, roughly 80 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twist 'n' Shout at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Swamp Fox?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Swamp Fox, according to Family Kingdom Amusement Park, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Swamp Fox?

Swamp Fox is 72 ft tall, with a drop of 62 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Swamp Fox go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Swamp Fox go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Swamp Fox scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Swamp Fox open?

Swamp Fox opened in 1966.

Who built Swamp Fox?

Swamp Fox was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the track on Swamp Fox?

RCDB lists 2,640 ft (805 m) of track.

How does Swamp Fox get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Swamp Fox at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

What restraints does Swamp Fox use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, single position lap bar.

Is Swamp Fox good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
