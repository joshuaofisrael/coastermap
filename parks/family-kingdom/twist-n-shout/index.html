Twist 'n' Shout

Family Kingdom Amusement Park · Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2013.

Twist 'n' Shout is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Family Kingdom Amusement Park in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, built by Zamperla. It opened on 2 Apr 2013. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twist 'n' Shout is like

Opened in 2013, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Family Kingdom Amusement Park. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Zig Zag Coaster / 270m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Toboggan Nordique at La Ronde, Doo Wopper at Morey's Piers and Woodstock Express at Walibi Rhône-Alpes.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twist 'n' Shout sits near the middle of Family Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon, roughly 80 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Swamp Fox at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

Does Twist 'n' Shout go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twist 'n' Shout scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Twist 'n' Shout open?

Twist 'n' Shout opened on 2 Apr 2013.

Who built Twist 'n' Shout?

Twist 'n' Shout was manufactured by Zamperla.

How does Twist 'n' Shout get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twist 'n' Shout at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Twist 'n' Shout good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Twist 'n' Shout operate before?

RCDB records it at Gillian's Wonderland Pier (as Wild Wonder); Magic Springs Theme and Water Park (as Twist 'n' Shout).

Is Twist 'n' Shout at Family Kingdom the same as Woodstock Express at Walibi Rhône-Alpes?

Both are Zamperla Zig Zag Coaster / 270m coasters on RCDB; the Family Kingdom ride opened in 2013 and the Walibi Rhône-Alpes ride in 2002.
