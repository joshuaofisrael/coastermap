Family Kingdom Amusement Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Myrtle Beach, South Carolina · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Family Kingdom Amusement Park in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 47 in (119 cm) on Dragon to 52 in (132 cm) on Swamp Fox. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Family Kingdom Amusement Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Family Kingdom Amusement Park a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Swamp Fox).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Swamp Fox, which opened in 1966.
