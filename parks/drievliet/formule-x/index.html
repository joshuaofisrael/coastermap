Formule X

Drievliet · The Hague, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 49.3 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2007.

Formule X is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Drievliet in The Hague, Netherlands, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 6 Apr 2007. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1164.7 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Formule X is like

At 49.3 ft tall with 1,164.7 ft of track, Formule X balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,164.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Drievliet. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's X-Car / Launch Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Shock at MagicLand. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Drievliet, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Formule X sits near the middle of Drievliet's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Twistrix, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dynamite Express at about 80 m. Formule X layout, trains and ride system

Formule X starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 43.5 mph in 2 s)
  2. Immelmann
  3. Heartline Roll
  4. Over-Banked Curve (135 °)

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Formule X

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Drievliet app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Formule X?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Formule X, according to Drievliet, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Formule X?

Formule X is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Formule X go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Formule X go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Formule X scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Formule X open?

Formule X opened on 6 Apr 2007.

Who built Formule X?

Formule X was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Formule X?

RCDB lists a ride time of 47 seconds over 1164.7 ft of track.

Does Formule X launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Formule X at once?

RCDB's train details give 6 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 650 riders.

Is Formule X good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Formule X at Drievliet the same as Shock at MagicLand?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH X-Car / Launch Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Drievliet ride opened in 2007 and the MagicLand ride in 2011; both list a 130 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 49.3 ft and 114.8 ft.
