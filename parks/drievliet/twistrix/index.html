Twistrix

Drievliet · The Hague, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 16.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Twistrix is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Drievliet in The Hague, Netherlands, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened in 2001. The ride stands 16.4 ft tall and runs 508.5 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twistrix is like

With 508.5 ft of track for a 16.4 ft structure, Twistrix is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2001, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Drievliet.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Drievliet (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twistrix sits on the south side of Drievliet's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Formule X, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dynamite Express at about 120 m. Twistrix layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Twistrix

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Drievliet app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twistrix?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Twistrix, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Drievliet, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twistrix?

Twistrix is 16.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Twistrix go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twistrix scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Twistrix open?

Twistrix opened in 2001.

Who built Twistrix?

Twistrix was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Twistrix?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 508.5 ft of track.

How does Twistrix get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twistrix at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Twistrix good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
