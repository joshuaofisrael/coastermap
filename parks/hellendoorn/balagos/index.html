Balagos

Avonturenpark Hellendoorn · Hellendoorn, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 82 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1990.

Balagos is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Avonturenpark Hellendoorn in Hellendoorn, Netherlands, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1990 and was previously known as Tornado. The ride stands 82 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1509.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Balagos is like

At 82 ft tall with 1,509.2 ft of track, Balagos balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,509.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1990, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Avonturenpark Hellendoorn. It has run under other names (Tornado), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Tornado design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Loopen at TusenFryd. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hellendoorn sets 120 cm, while TusenFryd sets 110 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Hellendoorn, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Balagos sits on the south-east side of Hellendoorn's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Donderstenen, roughly 230 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rioolrat at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Balagos

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Avonturenpark Hellendoorn app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Balagos?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Balagos, according to Avonturenpark Hellendoorn, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Balagos?

Balagos is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Balagos go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Balagos go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Balagos scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Balagos open?

Balagos opened in 1990 (previously known as Tornado).

Who built Balagos?

Balagos was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Balagos?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 8 seconds over 1509.2 ft of track.

How does Balagos get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Balagos at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Balagos good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Balagos at Hellendoorn the same as Loopen at TusenFryd?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Tornado coasters on RCDB; the Hellendoorn ride opened in 1990 and the TusenFryd ride in 1988; Hellendoorn sets 120 cm and TusenFryd sets 110 cm; RCDB lists heights of 82 ft and 68.9 ft.
