Magic Castle

E-World · Daegu, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Magic Castle is a steel sit-down roller coaster at E-World in Daegu, South Korea, built by EOS Rides. It opened on Mar 1995. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Magic Castle is like

Opened in 1995, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at E-World.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at E-World (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Magic Castle sits on the north-west side of E-World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurricane, roughly 230 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Camel Back at about 330 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Magic Castle

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as 매직열차 (Magic Train), the family coaster inside the Magic Castle. Riders 90 to 110 cm ride with a guardian aged 18 or over.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the E-World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Magic Castle?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Magic Castle, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. The park lists it as 매직열차 (Magic Train), the family coaster inside the Magic Castle. Riders 90 to 110 cm ride with a guardian aged 18 or over, according to E-World, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Magic Castle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Magic Castle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Magic Castle open?

Magic Castle opened on Mar 1995.

Who built Magic Castle?

Magic Castle was manufactured by EOS Rides.

How long is the ride on Magic Castle?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds.

Is Magic Castle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
