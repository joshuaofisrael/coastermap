E-World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Daegu, South Korea · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

E-World in Daegu, South Korea has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Magic Castle to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Hurricane. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at E-World are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes E-World a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 43.3 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Hurricane, which opened in 1995.

E-World publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at E-World? By height

At E-World the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Magic Castle (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Camel Back at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).
