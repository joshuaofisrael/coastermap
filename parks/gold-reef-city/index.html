Gold Reef City height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Johannesburg, South Africa · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Gold Reef City in Johannesburg, South Africa has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Golden Loop. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Gold Reef City are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Gold Reef City a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Anaconda, which opened in 1999.

Gold Reef City publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Gold Reef City? By height

At Gold Reef City the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Gold Reef City for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Gold Reef City, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Run A Way Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Jozi Express, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 78.8 ft tall, 42.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Anaconda, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 111.6 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 5 inversions, 3.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
