Tower of Terror

Gold Reef City · Johannesburg, South Africa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 59 mph; 111.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Tower of Terror is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gold Reef City in Johannesburg, South Africa. It opened on Dec 2001. The ride stands 111.6 ft tall, drops 164.1 ft and reaches 59 mph. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Tower of Terror is like

Its 164.1 ft drop is longer than its 111.6 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. RCDB lists a peak force of 6.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2001, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Gold Reef City.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Gold Reef City, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tower of Terror sits on the south side of Gold Reef City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Shongololo, roughly 110 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Golden Loop at about 250 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Tower of Terror compares

On our intensity ranking Tower of Terror is number 3 of 6 at Gold Reef City, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tower of Terror?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Tower of Terror, according to Gold Reef City, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tower of Terror?

Tower of Terror is 111.6 ft tall, with a drop of 164.1 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tower of Terror go?

Its top speed is 59 mph (95 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tower of Terror go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tower of Terror scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tower of Terror open?

Tower of Terror opened on Dec 2001.

How does Tower of Terror get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tower of Terror at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Tower of Terror good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 59 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
