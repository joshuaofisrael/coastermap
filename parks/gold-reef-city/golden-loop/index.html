Golden Loop

Gold Reef City · Johannesburg, South Africa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 57 mph; 137.8 ft tall; 1 inversion.

Golden Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gold Reef City in Johannesburg, South Africa, built by Schwarzkopf. The ride stands 137.8 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 863 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Golden Loop is like

Golden Loop packs its 863 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (137.8 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Schwarzkopf's Shuttle Loop / Weight Drop design. Others include Katapul at Hopi Hari. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gold Reef City sets 130 cm, while Hopi Hari sets 140 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Gold Reef City, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Golden Loop sits on the north-west side of Gold Reef City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Anaconda, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Run A Way Mine Train at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Golden Loop compares

On our intensity ranking Golden Loop is number 2 of 6 at Gold Reef City, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Tower of Terror (111.6 ft tall, 59 mph, no inversions, 6.3 g; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Anaconda (111.6 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 5 inversions, 3.5 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Golden Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Gold Reef City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Golden Loop?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Golden Loop, according to Gold Reef City, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Golden Loop?

Golden Loop is 137.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Golden Loop go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Golden Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Golden Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

Who built Golden Loop?

Golden Loop was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Golden Loop?

RCDB lists a ride time of 30 seconds over 863 ft of track.

Does Golden Loop launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a launch.

How many people ride Golden Loop at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Golden Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 57 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Golden Loop operate before?

RCDB records it at Carowinds (as White Lightnin').

Is Golden Loop at Gold Reef City the same as Katapul at Hopi Hari?

Both are Schwarzkopf Shuttle Loop / Weight Drop coasters on RCDB; Gold Reef City sets 130 cm and Hopi Hari sets 140 cm.
