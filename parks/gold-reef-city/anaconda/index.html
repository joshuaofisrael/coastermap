Anaconda

Gold Reef City · Johannesburg, South Africa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 111.6 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1999.

Anaconda is a steel inverted roller coaster at Gold Reef City in Johannesburg, South Africa, built by Giovanola. It opened in 1999. The ride stands 111.6 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 2447.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Anaconda is like

At 111.6 ft tall with 2,447.5 ft of track, Anaconda balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,447.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Gold Reef City.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Gold Reef City, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Anaconda sits on the north-west side of Gold Reef City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Golden Loop, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Run A Way Mine Train at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Anaconda

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Gold Reef City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Anaconda?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Anaconda, according to Gold Reef City, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Anaconda?

Anaconda is 111.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Anaconda go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Anaconda go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Anaconda scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Anaconda open?

Anaconda opened in 1999.

Who built Anaconda?

Anaconda was manufactured by Giovanola.

How long is the ride on Anaconda?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds over 2447.5 ft of track.

How many people ride Anaconda at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Anaconda good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
