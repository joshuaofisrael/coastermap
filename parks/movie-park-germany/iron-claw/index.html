Iron Claw

Movie Park Germany · Bottrop, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2001.

Iron Claw is a steel inverted roller coaster at Movie Park Germany in Bottrop, Germany, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 6 Apr 2001 and was previously known as Eraser and later FX and later MP-Xpress. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Iron Claw is like

At 109.3 ft tall with 2,260.5 ft of track, Iron Claw balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2001, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Movie Park Germany. It has run under other names (Eraser, then FX, then MP-Xpress), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 22 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 14th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm, while Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in, Six Flags New England sets 52 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 52 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Movie Park Germany, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Iron Claw sits on the north side of Movie Park Germany's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Bandit, roughly 150 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Van Helsing's Factory at about 310 m. Iron Claw layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Iron Claw's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Double In-Line Twist

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Iron Claw

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Movie Park Germany app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Claw?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Iron Claw. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Movie Park Germany, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Claw?

Iron Claw is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Claw go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Claw go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Iron Claw scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Iron Claw open?

Iron Claw opened on 6 Apr 2001 (previously known as Eraser and later FX and later MP-Xpress).

Who built Iron Claw?

Iron Claw was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Iron Claw?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Iron Claw get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Iron Claw at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Iron Claw good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Iron Claw at Movie Park Germany the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Movie Park Germany ride opened in 2001 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm and Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in.
