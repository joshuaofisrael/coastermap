Ghost Chasers

Movie Park Germany · Bottrop, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 28 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Ghost Chasers is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Movie Park Germany in Bottrop, Germany, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 2000 and was previously known as Mad Manor and later Tom and Jerry's Mouse in the House. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ghost Chasers is like

At 45.9 ft tall with 1,213.9 ft of track, Ghost Chasers balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Movie Park Germany. It has run under other names (Mad Manor, then Tom and Jerry's Mouse in the House), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Park design, and the first of them to open. Others include Ricochet at Carowinds. Height rules for this model differ by park: Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm, while Carowinds sets 54 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Movie Park Germany (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ghost Chasers sits on the south-east side of Movie Park Germany's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, roughly 50 m away (135 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Studio Tour at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Ghost Chasers compares

On our intensity ranking Ghost Chasers is number 4 of 8 at Movie Park Germany, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ghost Chasers?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Ghost Chasers. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Movie Park Germany, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ghost Chasers?

Ghost Chasers is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ghost Chasers go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ghost Chasers go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ghost Chasers scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Ghost Chasers open?

Ghost Chasers opened on 2000 (previously known as Mad Manor and later Tom and Jerry's Mouse in the House).

Who built Ghost Chasers?

Ghost Chasers was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

Is Ghost Chasers at Movie Park Germany the same as Ricochet at Carowinds?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Park coasters on RCDB; the Movie Park Germany ride opened in 2000 and the Carowinds ride in 2002; Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm and Carowinds sets 54 in.
