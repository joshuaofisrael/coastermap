Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer

Movie Park Germany · Bottrop, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer is a steel suspended roller coaster at Movie Park Germany in Bottrop, Germany, built by Vekoma. It opened on Mar 2007. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall and runs 967.8 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer is like

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer pairs a 42.7 ft structure with 967.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2007, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Movie Park Germany. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 294m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Steel Lasso at Frontier City. Height rules for this model differ by park: Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm, while Frontier City sets 38 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Movie Park Germany, out of 7 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer sits on the south side of Movie Park Germany's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Studio Tour, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Backyardigans: Mission to Mars at about 150 m. Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 205 cm (about 80.7 in).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer, or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 205 cm (about 80.7 in), according to Movie Park Germany, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer?

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer open?

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer opened on Mar 2007.

Who built Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer?

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 48 seconds over 967.8 ft of track.

How does Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer at Movie Park Germany the same as Steel Lasso at Frontier City?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 294m coasters on RCDB; the Movie Park Germany ride opened in 2007 and the Frontier City ride in 2008; Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm and Frontier City sets 38 in; RCDB lists heights of 42.7 ft and 49 ft.
