Movie Park Germany height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bottrop, Germany · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Movie Park Germany in Bottrop, Germany has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Ghost Chasers to 135 cm (about 53.1 in) on Backyardigans: Mission to Mars. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at Movie Park Germany are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Movie Park Germany a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 47% across all 113 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Bandit); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Star Trek: Operation Enterprise).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, which opened in 1996.

Movie Park Germany publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Movie Park Germany? By height

At Movie Park Germany the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Backyardigans: Mission to Mars (90 to 135 cm (about 35.4 to 53.1 in)). Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Ghost Chasers at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Backyardigans: Mission to Mars (90 to 135 cm (about 35.4 to 53.1 in)) and Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Ghost Chasers at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Ghost Chasers, Bandit and Van Helsing's Factory. With a companion they can also ride Backyardigans: Mission to Mars (90 to 135 cm (about 35.4 to 53.1 in)), Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer (95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in)) and Studio Tour (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Iron Claw at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, Iron Claw, Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer, Star Trek: Operation Enterprise and Studio Tour. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Ghost Chasers (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Iron Claw (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Movie Park Germany

These are the rules Movie Park Germany states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Backyardigans: Mission to Mars: riders 90 to 135 cm (about 35.4 to 53.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer: riders 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Studio Tour: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Movie Park Germany for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Movie Park Germany, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Van Helsing's Factory, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Star Trek: Operation Enterprise, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 55.9 mph, 3 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Movie Park Germany?

Roller coaster height requirements at Movie Park Germany run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Ghost Chasers) to 135 cm (about 53.1 in) (Backyardigans: Mission to Mars), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Movie Park Germany have?

Movie Park Germany has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Movie Park Germany?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Star Trek: Operation Enterprise (55.9 mph, 3 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Movie Park Germany?

Iron Claw, at 109.3 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Movie Park Germany?

Star Trek: Operation Enterprise, with a top speed of 55.9 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Movie Park Germany for first-timers?

Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 130 cm (about 51.2 in) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Movie Park Germany?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Movie Park Germany?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Ghost Chasers, Bandit, Van Helsing's Factory. With a companion they can also ride Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer, Studio Tour.

Which coasters at Movie Park Germany do not go upside down?

Ghost Chasers, Bandit, Backyardigans: Mission to Mars, Jimmy Neutron's Atomic Flyer, Van Helsing's Factory, Studio Tour have no inversions, per RCDB.
