Crazy 8 Spin Coaster

Quassy Amusement Park · Middlebury, Connecticut

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Crazy 8 Spin Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Quassy Amusement Park in Middlebury, Connecticut, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in Jul 2025. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Crazy 8 Spin Coaster is like

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Quassy Amusement Park. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model B design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Farmers Fling at Santa's Village, Spinning Coaster at Monkey Park and Whirlwind in the Jungle at Dream Island. Height rules for this model differ by park: Quassy sets 47 in, while Santa's Village sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 47 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Quassy, out of 2 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Crazy 8 Spin Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Crazy 8 Spin Coaster?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Crazy 8 Spin Coaster, or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm). The park lists it as Crazy 8 Family Roller Coaster: minimum 36 inches, 47 inches to ride alone, according to Quassy Amusement Park, official rides and attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Crazy 8 Spin Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Crazy 8 Spin Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Crazy 8 Spin Coaster open?

Crazy 8 Spin Coaster opened in Jul 2025.

Who built Crazy 8 Spin Coaster?

Crazy 8 Spin Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Crazy 8 Spin Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Crazy 8 Spin Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Crazy 8 Spin Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 47 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Crazy 8 Spin Coaster at Quassy the same as Farmers Fling at Santa's Village?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model B coasters on RCDB; the Quassy ride opened in 2025 and the Santa's Village ride in 2023; Quassy sets 47 in and Santa's Village sets 48 in.
