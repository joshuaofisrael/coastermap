Wooden Warrior

Quassy Amusement Park · Middlebury, Connecticut

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 32 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Wooden Warrior is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Quassy Amusement Park in Middlebury, Connecticut. It opened on 23 Apr 2011. The ride stands 32 ft tall, drops 36 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 1,239 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wooden Warrior is like

Wooden Warrior spreads 1,239 ft of track across a layout only 32 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2011, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Quassy Amusement Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Quassy. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is Little Dipper, roughly 130 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wooden Warrior?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wooden Warrior, or 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) with a companion. Minimum 40 inches; 48 inches to ride alone, according to Quassy Amusement Park, official rides and attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wooden Warrior?

Wooden Warrior is 32 ft tall, with a drop of 36 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wooden Warrior go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wooden Warrior go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wooden Warrior scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wooden Warrior open?

Wooden Warrior opened on 23 Apr 2011.

How long is the track on Wooden Warrior?

RCDB lists 1,239 ft (378 m) of track.

How does Wooden Warrior get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wooden Warrior at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Wooden Warrior good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
