Braus & Saus

Potts Park · Minden, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 31.1 mph; 62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Braus & Saus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Potts Park in Minden, Germany, built by ART Engineering GmbH. It opened on 12 Jul 2025. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 721.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Braus & Saus is like

Braus & Saus pairs a 62.3 ft structure with 721.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Potts Park.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Potts Park, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Braus & Saus sits near the middle of Potts Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Säbelsaurus, roughly 50 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Potts Blitz at about 60 m. Braus & Saus layout, trains and ride system

Braus & Saus starts with a tyre-driven launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Tire Propelled Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (3 entries):

  1. Tire Propelled Launch
  2. Tire Propelled Boost
  3. Spiral Rollback

Before you ride Braus & Saus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riding alone needs 120 cm and age 6; from 90 cm and age 3 riders must be accompanied.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Potts Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Braus & Saus?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Braus & Saus, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riding alone needs 120 cm and age 6; from 90 cm and age 3 riders must be accompanied, according to Potts Park, official access restrictions (Zugangsbeschränkungen, PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Braus & Saus?

Braus & Saus is 62.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Braus & Saus go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Braus & Saus go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Braus & Saus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Braus & Saus open?

Braus & Saus opened on 12 Jul 2025.

Who built Braus & Saus?

Braus & Saus was manufactured by ART Engineering GmbH.

How long is the track on Braus & Saus?

RCDB lists 721.8 ft of track.

Does Braus & Saus launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Braus & Saus at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 500 riders.

Is Braus & Saus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
