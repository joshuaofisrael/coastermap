Potts Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Minden, Germany · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Potts Park in Minden, Germany has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Kiddy-Racer to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Turbo-Drachen. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Potts Park are like

RCDB classes 5 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Potts Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 49.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Potts Blitz, which opened in 1993.

Potts Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Turbo-Drachen: Riding alone needs 130 cm and age 8; from 105 cm and age 3 riders must be accompanied.
  • Kiddy-Racer: Minimum age 3, ridden under supervision; maximum weight 40 kg.
  • Säbelsaurus: Riding alone needs 125 cm and age 7; from 105 cm and age 4 riders must be accompanied.
  • Braus & Saus: Riding alone needs 120 cm and age 6; from 90 cm and age 3 riders must be accompanied.

We could not verify a height rule on an official Potts Park source for Potts Blitz, so it is left out of the height lists below. Ask the park before you queue.

What can my child ride at Potts Park? By height

At Potts Park the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Kiddy-Racer. With a companion they can also ride Braus & Saus (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Säbelsaurus at 125 cm (about 49.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Kiddy-Racer. With a companion they can also ride Turbo-Drachen (105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in)), Säbelsaurus (105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in)) and Braus & Saus (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Potts Park

These are the rules Potts Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Turbo-Drachen: riders 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Säbelsaurus: riders 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Braus & Saus: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Kiddy-Racer: Minimum age 3, ridden under supervision; maximum weight 40 kg.

Best coasters at Potts Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Potts Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Kiddy-Racer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 5.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Turbo-Drachen, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Braus & Saus, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 62.3 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
