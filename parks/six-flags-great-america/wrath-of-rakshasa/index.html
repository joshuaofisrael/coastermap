Wrath of Rakshasa

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 67 mph; 180 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2025.

Wrath of Rakshasa is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 31 May 2025. The ride stands 180 ft tall, drops 171 ft, reaches 67 mph and runs 3,239 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 5 times. Its steepest drop is 96°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wrath of Rakshasa?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wrath of Rakshasa. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wrath of Rakshasa?

Wrath of Rakshasa is 180 ft tall, with a drop of 171 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wrath of Rakshasa go?

Its top speed is 67 mph (108 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wrath of Rakshasa go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Wrath of Rakshasa scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wrath of Rakshasa open?

Wrath of Rakshasa opened on 31 May 2025.

Who built Wrath of Rakshasa?

Wrath of Rakshasa was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
