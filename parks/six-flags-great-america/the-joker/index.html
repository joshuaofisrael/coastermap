The Joker

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 38 mph; 120 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

The Joker is a steel wing roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 27 May 2017. The ride stands 120 ft tall, drops 54 ft, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,019 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Joker is like

The Joker packs its 1,019 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (120 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. RCDB lists a 54 ft drop against a 120 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2017, it is the 14th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Great America. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's 4D Free Spin / 110 design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include The Joker at Six Flags Great Adventure, The Joker at Six Flags Over Texas, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas and The Joker at Six Flags New England. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Great America, out of 15 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Joker sits on the north-east side of Six Flags Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman The Ride, roughly 80 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Flash: Vertical Velocity at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Joker?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Joker. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Joker?

The Joker is 120 ft tall, with a drop of 54 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Joker go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Joker go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Joker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Joker open?

The Joker opened on 27 May 2017.

Who built The Joker?

The Joker was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the track on The Joker?

RCDB lists 1,019 ft (311 m) of track.

How does The Joker get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Joker at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is The Joker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 38 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is The Joker at Six Flags Great America the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Both are S&S Worldwide 4D Free Spin / 110 coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great America ride opened in 2017 and the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride in 2015; both list a 48 in minimum.
