Demon

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50 mph; 103.7 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1976.

Demon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened on 29 May 1976 and was previously known as Turn Of The Century. The ride stands 103.7 ft tall, drops 90 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,130 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Demon is like

At 103.7 ft tall with 2,130 ft of track, Demon balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,130 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1976, it is the oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Great America. It has run under other names (Turn Of The Century), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Great America, out of 15 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Demon sits on the south-west side of Six Flags Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is X-Flight, roughly 130 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Raging Bull at about 160 m. Demon layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Demon's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop (70 ft tall)
  3. Loop (55 ft tall)
  4. Double Corkscrew (35 ft tall)

RCDB counts 4 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Demon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Great America app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Demon?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Demon, according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Demon?

Demon is 103.7 ft tall, with a drop of 90 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Demon go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Demon go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Demon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Demon open?

Demon opened on 29 May 1976 (previously known as Turn Of The Century).

Who built Demon?

Demon was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Demon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 2,130 ft of track.

How does Demon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Demon at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,300 riders.

What restraints does Demon use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Demon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Demon at Six Flags Great America the same as Demon at California's Great America?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Custom Looping Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great America ride opened in 1976 and the California's Great America ride in 1976; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 103.7 ft and 102 ft.
