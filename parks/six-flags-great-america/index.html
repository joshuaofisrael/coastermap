Six Flags Great America height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Gurnee, Illinois · 16 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois has 16 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Whizzer to 54 in (137 cm) on Batman The Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 11 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 16 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Great America are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 12 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Great America a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 75% of its lineup, against 50% across all 96 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 13 of the 16 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 7 of the 16 go upside down; 4 are wooden coasters (Little Dipper, Goliath, American Eagle and Viper); 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Maxx Force and The Flash: Vertical Velocity).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 5 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Whizzer, which opened in 1976.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Great America? By height

At Six Flags Great America the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 9 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Little Dipper (36 to 44 in (91 to 112 cm)), Whizzer (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)) and Sprocket Rockets (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 13 coasters, the nearest being The Dark Knight Coaster at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 16 coasters with a verified rule: Whizzer and Sprocket Rockets. With a companion they can also ride The Dark Knight Coaster (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Little Dipper (36 to 44 in (91 to 112 cm)). Still too short for 12 coasters, the nearest being Goliath at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 11 of 16 coasters with a verified rule, 9 more than at the height below: The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper, Goliath, The Joker, Maxx Force, Wrath of Rakshasa, Demon, American Eagle and Viper. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being X-Flight at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 16 of 16 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: X-Flight, Batman The Ride, Raging Bull, The Flash: Vertical Velocity and Superman - Ultimate Flight. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: X-Flight (up to 78 in (198 cm)), The Joker (up to 77 in (196 cm)), Wrath of Rakshasa (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and The Flash: Vertical Velocity (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Six Flags Great America

These are the rules Six Flags Great America states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • The Dark Knight Coaster: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Little Dipper: riders 36 to 44 in (91 to 112 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Whizzer: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Sprocket Rockets: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags Great America for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Great America, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Little Dipper, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (44 in minimum; 28 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: The Dark Knight Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Maxx Force, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 175 ft tall, 78 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Six Flags Great America?

Roller coaster height requirements at Six Flags Great America run from 42 in (107 cm) (Whizzer) to 54 in (137 cm) (Batman The Ride), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Great America have?

Six Flags Great America has 16 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags Great America?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Maxx Force (175 ft tall, 78 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Great America?

Raging Bull, at 202 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Great America?

Maxx Force, with a top speed of 78 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Six Flags Great America for first-timers?

Little Dipper is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 44 in (112 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Six Flags Great America?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets. With a companion they can also ride The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Six Flags Great America?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper, Goliath, The Joker, Maxx Force, Wrath of Rakshasa, Demon, American Eagle, Viper, Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets.

Which coasters at Six Flags Great America do not go upside down?

The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper, The Joker, American Eagle, Viper, Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets, Raging Bull, The Flash: Vertical Velocity have no inversions, per RCDB.
