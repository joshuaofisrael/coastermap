Six Flags Great America roller coasters

Gurnee, Illinois · 16 coasters

Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois has 16 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Raging Bull at 202 ft and the fastest is Maxx Force at 78 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Whizzer.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Great America have?

Six Flags Great America has 16 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Great America?

Raging Bull, at 202 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Great America?

Maxx Force, with a top speed of 78 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Great America?

By height requirement, Whizzer has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Great America coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Great America coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper, Goliath, The Joker, Maxx Force, Wrath of Rakshasa, Demon, American Eagle, Viper, Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Great America do not go upside down?

The Dark Knight Coaster, Little Dipper, The Joker, American Eagle, Viper, Whizzer, Sprocket Rockets, Raging Bull, The Flash: Vertical Velocity have no inversions, per RCDB.
