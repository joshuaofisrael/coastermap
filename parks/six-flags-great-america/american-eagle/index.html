American Eagle

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 66 mph; 127 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1981.

American Eagle is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 23 May 1981. The ride stands 127 ft tall, drops 147 ft, reaches 66 mph and runs 4,650 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding American Eagle is like

With 4,650 ft of track for a 127 ft structure, American Eagle is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 147 ft drop is longer than its 127 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 1981, it is the 3rd oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Great America.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Great America, out of 15 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, American Eagle sits on the south side of Six Flags Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is X-Flight, roughly 120 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Demon at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for American Eagle?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride American Eagle, according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is American Eagle?

American Eagle is 127 ft tall, with a drop of 147 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does American Eagle go?

Its top speed is 66 mph (106 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does American Eagle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is American Eagle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did American Eagle open?

American Eagle opened on 23 May 1981.

Who built American Eagle?

American Eagle was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
