Sprocket Rockets

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Sprocket Rockets is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1998 and was previously known as Spacely's Sprocket Rockets. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sprocket Rockets is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Sprocket Rockets balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 6th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Great America. It has run under other names (Spacely's Sprocket Rockets), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland and Tami-Tami at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Great America sets 42 in, while Magic Mountain sets 36 in, Universal Studios Florida sets 36 in and Disneyland sets 35 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Great America (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sprocket Rockets sits on the east side of Six Flags Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Goliath, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Flash: Vertical Velocity at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sprocket Rockets?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Sprocket Rockets, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sprocket Rockets?

Sprocket Rockets is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sprocket Rockets go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sprocket Rockets go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sprocket Rockets scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Sprocket Rockets open?

Sprocket Rockets opened in 1998 (previously known as Spacely's Sprocket Rockets).

Who built Sprocket Rockets?

Sprocket Rockets was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great America ride opened in 1998 and the Disneyland ride in 1993; Six Flags Great America sets 42 in and Disneyland sets 35 in.
