Superman - Ultimate Flight

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 51 mph; 106 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2003.

Superman - Ultimate Flight is a steel flying roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 3 May 2003. The ride stands 106 ft tall, drops 100 ft, reaches 51 mph and runs 2,798 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Superman - Ultimate Flight?

Riders must be at least 54 inches (137 cm) tall to ride Superman - Ultimate Flight, according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Superman - Ultimate Flight?

Superman - Ultimate Flight is 106 ft tall, with a drop of 100 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Superman - Ultimate Flight go?

Its top speed is 51 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Superman - Ultimate Flight go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Superman - Ultimate Flight scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Superman - Ultimate Flight open?

Superman - Ultimate Flight opened on 3 May 2003.

Who built Superman - Ultimate Flight?

Superman - Ultimate Flight was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
