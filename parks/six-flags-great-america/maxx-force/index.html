Maxx Force

Six Flags Great America · Gurnee, Illinois

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 78 mph; 175 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2019.

Maxx Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, Illinois, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 4 Jul 2019. The ride stands 175 ft tall, reaches 78 mph and runs 1,800 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Maxx Force is like

At 175 ft tall with 1,800 ft of track, Maxx Force balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 1,800 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the 15th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Great America.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Great America, out of 15 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Maxx Force sits on the north-west side of Six Flags Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Whizzer, roughly 80 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Viper at about 140 m. Maxx Force layout, trains and ride system

Maxx Force starts with a compressed-air launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Maxx Force

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Great America app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Maxx Force?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Maxx Force, according to Six Flags Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Maxx Force?

Maxx Force is 175 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Maxx Force go?

Its top speed is 78 mph (126 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Maxx Force go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Maxx Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Maxx Force open?

Maxx Force opened on 4 Jul 2019.

Who built Maxx Force?

Maxx Force was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the ride on Maxx Force?

RCDB lists a ride time of 23 seconds over 1,800 ft of track.

Does Maxx Force launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a compressed-air launch.

How many people ride Maxx Force at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Maxx Force good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 78 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
