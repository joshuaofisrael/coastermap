Emperor

SeaWorld San Diego · San Diego, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 60 mph; 153 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2022.

Emperor is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld San Diego in San Diego, California, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 12 Mar 2022. The ride stands 153 ft tall, drops 143 ft, reaches 60 mph and runs 2,411 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Emperor is like

Emperor pairs a 153 ft structure with 2,411 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,411 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2022, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at SeaWorld San Diego.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at SeaWorld San Diego, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Emperor sits on the south-east side of SeaWorld San Diego's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Journey to Atlantis, roughly 150 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Arctic Rescue at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

How Emperor compares

Before you ride Emperor

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the SeaWorld San Diego app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Emperor?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Emperor, according to SeaWorld San Diego, Rides and Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Emperor?

Emperor is 153 ft tall, with a drop of 143 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Emperor go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Emperor go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Emperor scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Emperor open?

Emperor opened on 12 Mar 2022.

Who built Emperor?

Emperor was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Emperor?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 35 seconds over 2,411 ft of track.

How does Emperor get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Emperor at once?

Each train carries 18 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Emperor good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
