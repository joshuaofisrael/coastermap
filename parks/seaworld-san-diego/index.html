SeaWorld San Diego roller coasters

San Diego, California · 5 coasters

SeaWorld San Diego in San Diego, California has 5 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Emperor at 153 ft and the fastest is Electric Eel at 62 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Journey to Atlantis.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does SeaWorld San Diego have?

SeaWorld San Diego has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Diego?

Emperor, at 153 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Diego?

Electric Eel, with a top speed of 62 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at SeaWorld San Diego?

By height requirement, Journey to Atlantis has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which SeaWorld San Diego coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which SeaWorld San Diego coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis, Manta, Arctic Rescue. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at SeaWorld San Diego do not go upside down?

Journey to Atlantis, Manta, Arctic Rescue have no inversions, per RCDB.
