SeaWorld San Diego height requirements and roller coasters ranked

San Diego, California · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

SeaWorld San Diego in San Diego, California has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Journey to Atlantis to 54 in (137 cm) on Electric Eel. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at SeaWorld San Diego are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes SeaWorld San Diego a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 52% across all 69 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down; 3 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Manta, Electric Eel and Arctic Rescue).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Journey to Atlantis, which opened in 2004.

What can my child ride at SeaWorld San Diego? By height

At SeaWorld San Diego the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at SeaWorld San Diego for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for SeaWorld San Diego, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Journey to Atlantis, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 95 ft tall, 42 mph, no inversions, 3 g).
  2. Step up: Arctic Rescue, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Electric Eel, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
