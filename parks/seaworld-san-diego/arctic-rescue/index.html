Arctic Rescue

SeaWorld San Diego · San Diego, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Arctic Rescue is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld San Diego in San Diego, California, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 2 Jun 2023. The ride reaches 40 mph and runs 2,800 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Arctic Rescue?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Arctic Rescue, according to SeaWorld San Diego, Rides and Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Arctic Rescue go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Arctic Rescue go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Arctic Rescue scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Arctic Rescue open?

Arctic Rescue opened on 2 Jun 2023.

Who built Arctic Rescue?

Arctic Rescue was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
