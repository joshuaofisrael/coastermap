PortAventura Park roller coasters

Salou, Spain · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Shambhala at 249.3 ft and the fastest is Furius Baco at 83.9 mph. The lowest height requirement is 105 cm (about 41.3 in), for Tomahawk.
