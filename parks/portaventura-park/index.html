PortAventura Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Salou, Spain · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on Tomahawk to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Dragon Khan. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at PortAventura Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes PortAventura Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 46% across all 122 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Stampida and Tomahawk).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Dragon Khan, which opened in 1995.

Shambhala is the 12th tallest of the 712 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Furius Baco is the 14th fastest of the 704 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

PortAventura Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at PortAventura Park? By height

At PortAventura Park the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Tomahawk at 105 cm (about 41.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Tomahawk. With a companion they can also ride Tami-Tami (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Stampida at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Stampida. With a companion they can also ride El Diablo: Tren de la Mina (110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in)) and Tami-Tami (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Dragon Khan at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Tami-Tami. With a companion they can also ride El Diablo: Tren de la Mina (110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Dragon Khan at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Dragon Khan (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Tomahawk (up to 185 cm (about 72.8 in)) and Furius Baco (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at PortAventura Park

These are the rules PortAventura Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • El Diablo: Tren de la Mina: riders 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Tami-Tami: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at PortAventura Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for PortAventura Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Tami-Tami, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.7 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: El Diablo: Tren de la Mina, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 54 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Dragon Khan, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 148 ft tall, 65 mph, 8 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
