Stampida

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 46 mph; 84 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Stampida is a wood sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc.. It opened on 17 Mar 1997. The ride stands 84 ft tall, reaches 46 mph and runs 3,127 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Stampida is like

With 3,127 ft of track for a 84 ft structure, Stampida is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1997, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at PortAventura Park.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at PortAventura Park, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Stampida sits on the north-west side of PortAventura Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tomahawk, roughly 80 m away (105 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence at about 140 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

What is the height requirement for Stampida?

Stampida is 84 ft tall, according to RCDB.
