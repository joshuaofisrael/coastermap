El Diablo: Tren de la Mina

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion; 54 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 2 May 1995. The ride stands 54 ft tall and runs 3,306 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.
