El Diablo: Tren de la Mina

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion; 54 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 2 May 1995. The ride stands 54 ft tall and runs 3,306 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding El Diablo: Tren de la Mina is like

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina spreads 3,306 ft of track across a layout only 54 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at PortAventura Park. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 11th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Adventure Express at Kings Island and Trailblazer at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: PortAventura Park sets 140 cm, while Cedar Point sets 48 in, Magic Mountain sets 48 in and Kings Island sets 48 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at PortAventura Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, El Diablo: Tren de la Mina sits on the north side of PortAventura Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tomahawk, roughly 140 m away (105 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Stampida at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for El Diablo: Tren de la Mina?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride El Diablo: Tren de la Mina, or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to PortAventura World, PortAventura Park height requirements page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is El Diablo: Tren de la Mina?

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina is 54 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does El Diablo: Tren de la Mina go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is El Diablo: Tren de la Mina scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did El Diablo: Tren de la Mina open?

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina opened on 2 May 1995.

Who built El Diablo: Tren de la Mina?

El Diablo: Tren de la Mina was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

Is El Diablo: Tren de la Mina at PortAventura Park the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the PortAventura Park ride opened in 1995 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; PortAventura Park sets 140 cm and Six Flags Over Texas sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 54 ft and 35 ft.
