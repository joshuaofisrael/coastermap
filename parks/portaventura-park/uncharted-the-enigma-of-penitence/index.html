Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 17 Jun 2023. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, drops 49.2 ft and runs 2296.6 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence is like

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence spreads 2,296.6 ft of track across a layout only 39.4 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. Its 49.2 ft drop is longer than its 39.4 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at PortAventura Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Multi Dimension Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include TH13TEEN at Alton Towers and Studio Tour at Movie Park Germany.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence sits on the north-west side of PortAventura Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Stampida, roughly 140 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tomahawk at about 160 m. Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence layout, trains and ride system

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 3 booster sections (LSM Boost (multi-pass) and LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

How Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence compares

On our intensity ranking Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence is number 6 of 8 at PortAventura Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence?

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence is 39.4 ft tall, with a drop of 49.2 ft, according to RCDB.

Does Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence open?

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence opened on 17 Jun 2023.

Who built Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence?

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence?

RCDB lists 2296.6 ft of track.

Does Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence at PortAventura Park the same as TH13TEEN at Alton Towers?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Multi Dimension Coaster coasters on RCDB; the PortAventura Park ride opened in 2023 and the Alton Towers ride in 2010; RCDB lists heights of 39.4 ft and 65.6 ft.
