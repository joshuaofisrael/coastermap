Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 17 Jun 2023. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, drops 49.2 ft and runs 2296.6 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.
