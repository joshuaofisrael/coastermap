Dragon Khan

PortAventura Park · Salou, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 65 mph; 148 ft tall; 8 inversions; opened 1995.

Dragon Khan is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PortAventura Park in Salou, Spain, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 2 May 1995. The ride stands 148 ft tall, drops 161 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 4,165 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 8 times.

What riding Dragon Khan is like

At 148 ft tall with 4,165 ft of track, Dragon Khan balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 161 ft drop is longer than its 148 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. It goes upside down 8 times across 4,165 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1995, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at PortAventura Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Sitting Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include The Incredible Hulk Coaster at Islands of Adventure and Wildfire at Silver Dollar City. Height rules for this model differ by park: PortAventura Park sets 140 cm, while Islands of Adventure sets 54 in and Silver Dollar City sets 52 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at PortAventura Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragon Khan sits on the east side of PortAventura Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Shambhala, roughly 110 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tami-Tami at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 8 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Dragon Khan

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 8 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the PortAventura Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragon Khan?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Dragon Khan. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to PortAventura World, PortAventura Park height requirements page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dragon Khan?

Dragon Khan is 148 ft tall, with a drop of 161 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dragon Khan go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dragon Khan go upside down?

Yes. It has 8 inversions.

Is Dragon Khan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dragon Khan open?

Dragon Khan opened on 2 May 1995.

Who built Dragon Khan?

Dragon Khan was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Dragon Khan?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 4,165 ft of track.

How does Dragon Khan get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dragon Khan at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Dragon Khan good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 8 times; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park the same as The Incredible Hulk Coaster at Islands of Adventure?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Sitting Coaster coasters on RCDB; the PortAventura Park ride opened in 1995 and the Islands of Adventure ride in 1999; PortAventura Park sets 140 cm and Islands of Adventure sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 148 ft and 110 ft.
