Fireball

Adventureland (Long Island) · Farmingdale, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; 68.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Fireball is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventureland (Long Island) in Farmingdale, New York, built by RES Rides AG. It opened on 21 May 2022. The ride stands 68.9 ft tall and runs 288.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Fireball is like

For a 68.9 ft ride, 288.7 ft of track is short: Fireball is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Adventureland (Long Island). It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to RES Rides AG's Roller Ball / S design, and the first of them to open. Others include Wilde Hilde at Geiselwind. Height rules for this model differ by park: Adventureland Long Island sets 48 in, while Geiselwind sets 130 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Adventureland Long Island, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fireball sits on the west side of Adventureland Long Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Turbulence, roughly 170 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rattlesnake Coaster at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 75 in (190 cm).

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

How Fireball compares

On our intensity ranking Fireball is number 2 of 3 at Adventureland (Long Island), where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Rattlesnake Coaster (19.7 ft tall, 19.9 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Turbulence (55 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum).

Before you ride Fireball

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 75 in (190 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • The park says anyone 6 feet 4 inches or taller cannot ride.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fireball?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Fireball, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 75 in (190 cm). The park says anyone 6 feet 4 inches or taller cannot ride, according to Adventureland (Long Island), official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fireball?

Fireball is 68.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Fireball go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fireball scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Fireball open?

Fireball opened on 21 May 2022.

Who built Fireball?

Fireball was manufactured by RES Rides AG.

How long is the track on Fireball?

RCDB lists 288.7 ft of track.

How many people ride Fireball at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 360 riders.

Is Fireball good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Fireball at Adventureland Long Island the same as Wilde Hilde at Geiselwind?

Both are RES Rides AG Roller Ball / S coasters on RCDB; the Adventureland Long Island ride opened in 2022 and the Geiselwind ride in 2026; Adventureland Long Island sets 48 in and Geiselwind sets 130 cm.
