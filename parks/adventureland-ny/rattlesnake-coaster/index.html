Rattlesnake Coaster

Adventureland (Long Island) · Farmingdale, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 19.9 mph; 19.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Rattlesnake Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventureland (Long Island) in Farmingdale, New York, built by Zierer. It first opened in 2000 and was previously known as Paul Bunyan Express and later Ladybug Coaster and later Ladybug Rollercoaster. The ride stands 19.7 ft tall, reaches 19.9 mph and runs 652.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rattlesnake Coaster is like

Rattlesnake Coaster spreads 652.9 ft of track across a layout only 19.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2000, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Adventureland (Long Island). It has run under other names (Paul Bunyan Express, then Ladybug Coaster, then Ladybug Rollercoaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Medium design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Drako at Walibi Holland, SOS Tournevis at Parc Astérix, Ladybird at Lightwater Valley and Children Coaster at Nagashima Spa Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Adventureland Long Island sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Lightwater Valley sets 90 cm and Dufan sets 100 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Adventureland Long Island, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rattlesnake Coaster sits on the east side of Adventureland Long Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Turbulence, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Fireball at about 220 m. Rattlesnake Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Rattlesnake Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park's page is still at its old Lady Bug Coaster address: 36 inches to ride with an adult, 48 inches alone.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Adventureland (Long Island) app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rattlesnake Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Rattlesnake Coaster, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion. The park's page is still at its old Lady Bug Coaster address: 36 inches to ride with an adult, 48 inches alone, according to Adventureland (Long Island), official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rattlesnake Coaster?

Rattlesnake Coaster is 19.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rattlesnake Coaster go?

Its top speed is 19.9 mph (32 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rattlesnake Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rattlesnake Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Rattlesnake Coaster open?

Rattlesnake Coaster opened in 2000 (previously known as Paul Bunyan Express and later Ladybug Coaster and later Ladybug Rollercoaster).

Who built Rattlesnake Coaster?

Rattlesnake Coaster was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Rattlesnake Coaster?

RCDB lists 652.9 ft of track.

How does Rattlesnake Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rattlesnake Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

What restraints does Rattlesnake Coaster use?

RCDB lists: single position lap bar.

Is Rattlesnake Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 19.9 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Rattlesnake Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Navel Land (as Family Coaster); Adventureland (as Rattlesnake Coaster).

Is Rattlesnake Coaster at Adventureland Long Island the same as Children Coaster at Nagashima Spa Land?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Medium coasters on RCDB; the Adventureland Long Island ride opened in 2000 and the Nagashima Spa Land ride in 1983.
