Parc Ange Michel height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parc Ange Michel in Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 95 cm (about 37.4 in) on Tacot en Folie to 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Tornado. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Parc Ange Michel are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Parc Ange Michel a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 37.4 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tacot en Folie, which opened in 2008.

Parc Ange Michel publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parc Ange Michel? By height

At Parc Ange Michel the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
