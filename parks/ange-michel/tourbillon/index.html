Tourbillon

Parc Ange Michel · Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Tourbillon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Ange Michel in Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France, built by Gosetto. It opened on 22 Apr 2018. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tourbillon is like

Opened in 2018, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Parc Ange Michel. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gosetto's Spinning Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Bumble Blast at Calaway Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Ange Michel sets 95 cm, while Calaway Park sets 48 in.

Its 95 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Ange Michel (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tourbillon sits near the middle of Ange Michel's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tacot en Folie, roughly 70 m away (95 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tornado at about 190 m. Before you ride Tourbillon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 95 cm (about 37.4 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Le Tourbillon and does not state a companion rule.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parc Ange Michel app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tourbillon?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Tourbillon. The park lists it as Le Tourbillon and does not state a companion rule, according to Ange Michel, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Tourbillon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tourbillon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tourbillon open?

Tourbillon opened on 22 Apr 2018.

Who built Tourbillon?

Tourbillon was manufactured by Gosetto.

How many people ride Tourbillon at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Tourbillon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 95 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Tourbillon at Ange Michel the same as Bumble Blast at Calaway Park?

Both are Gosetto Spinning Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Ange Michel ride opened in 2018 and the Calaway Park ride in 2019; Ange Michel sets 95 cm and Calaway Park sets 48 in.
