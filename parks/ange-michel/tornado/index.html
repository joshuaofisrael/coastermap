Tornado

Parc Ange Michel · Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Tornado is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Ange Michel in Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France, built by Zamperla. It opened on 20 Apr 2013. The ride runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tornado is like

Opened in 2013, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Parc Ange Michel. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Twister Coaster / 420m design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Sidewinder Safari at Discovery Kingdom, Galaxy Spin at Fun Spot Kissimmee, The Tickler at Luna Park and Wild Mouse at Beech Bend. Height rules for this model differ by park: Ange Michel sets 110 cm, while Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in, Fun Spot Kissimmee sets 48 in and Luna Park sets 48 in.

Its 110 cm minimum is the strictest at Ange Michel. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tornado sits on the south-west side of Ange Michel's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tourbillon, roughly 190 m away (95 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tacot en Folie at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tornado?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Tornado. The park lists it as Le Tornado, according to Ange Michel, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Tornado go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tornado scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tornado open?

Tornado opened on 20 Apr 2013.

Who built Tornado?

Tornado was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Tornado?

RCDB lists 1377.9 ft of track.

How does Tornado get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tornado at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Tornado good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Tornado at Ange Michel the same as Wild Mouse at Beech Bend?

Both are Zamperla Twister Coaster / 420m coasters on RCDB; the Ange Michel ride opened in 2013 and the Beech Bend ride in 2005; Ange Michel sets 110 cm and Beech Bend sets 48 in.
