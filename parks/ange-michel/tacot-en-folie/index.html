Tacot en Folie

Parc Ange Michel · Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Tacot en Folie is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Ange Michel in Saint-Martin-de-Landelles, Normandy, France, built by Soquet. It opened on 12 Apr 2008. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tacot en Folie is like

Opened in 2008, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Parc Ange Michel.

Its 95 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Ange Michel (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tacot en Folie sits on the north-east side of Ange Michel's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tourbillon, roughly 70 m away (95 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tornado at about 240 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tacot en Folie?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Tacot en Folie. The park does not state a companion rule, according to Ange Michel, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Tacot en Folie go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tacot en Folie scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tacot en Folie open?

Tacot en Folie opened on 12 Apr 2008.

Who built Tacot en Folie?

Tacot en Folie was manufactured by Soquet.

How many people ride Tacot en Folie at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Tacot en Folie good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 95 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Tacot en Folie operate before?

RCDB records it at Fraispertuis City (as Express); Ange Michel (as Tacot en Folie).
