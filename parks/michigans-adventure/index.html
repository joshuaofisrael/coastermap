Michigan's Adventure roller coasters

Muskegon, Michigan · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Michigan's Adventure in Muskegon, Michigan has 7 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Shivering Timbers at 122 ft, which is also the fastest at 57 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Woodstock Express.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Michigan's Adventure have?

Michigan's Adventure has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Michigan's Adventure?

Shivering Timbers, at 122 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Michigan's Adventure?

Shivering Timbers, with a top speed of 57 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Michigan's Adventure?

By height requirement, Woodstock Express has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Michigan's Adventure coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Woodstock Express. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Michigan's Adventure coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Corkscrew, Wolverine Wildcat, Zach's Zoomer, Shivering Timbers, Mad Mouse, Woodstock Express. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Michigan's Adventure do not go upside down?

Wolverine Wildcat, Zach's Zoomer, Shivering Timbers, Mad Mouse, Woodstock Express have no inversions, per RCDB.
