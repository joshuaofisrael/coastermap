Woodstock Express

Michigan's Adventure · Muskegon, Michigan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 16.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Woodstock Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Michigan's Adventure in Muskegon, Michigan, built by Chance Rides. It opened on 29 May 2021. The ride stands 16.1 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Woodstock Express is like

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Michigan's Adventure. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Chance Rides's Big Dipper / Standard design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster at Six Flags Over Texas and Joust at Dutch Wonderland. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 42 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Michigan's Adventure, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Woodstock Express sits on the west side of Michigan's Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Zach's Zoomer, roughly 30 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 120 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Woodstock Express layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Michigan's Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Woodstock Express?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Woodstock Express, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to Michigan's Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Woodstock Express?

Woodstock Express is 16.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Woodstock Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Woodstock Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Michigan&#x27;s Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Woodstock Express open?

Woodstock Express opened on 29 May 2021.

Who built Woodstock Express?

Woodstock Express was manufactured by Chance Rides.

How does Woodstock Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Woodstock Express at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Woodstock Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Woodstock Express at Michigan's Adventure the same as Joust at Dutch Wonderland?

Both are Chance Rides Big Dipper / Standard coasters on RCDB; the Michigan's Adventure ride opened in 2021 and the Dutch Wonderland ride in 1998; both list a 42 in minimum.
