Zach's Zoomer

Michigan's Adventure · Muskegon, Michigan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm), or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion; 41 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1994.

Zach's Zoomer is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Michigan's Adventure in Muskegon, Michigan, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It opened on 22 Jul 1994. The ride stands 41 ft tall and runs 1,400 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Zach's Zoomer is like

With 1,400 ft of track for a 41 ft structure, Zach's Zoomer is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1994, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Michigan's Adventure.

A rider who just meets its 46 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Michigan's Adventure, out of 6 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Zach's Zoomer sits on the west side of Michigan's Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock Express, roughly 30 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 130 m. Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Michigan's Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Zach's Zoomer?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Zach's Zoomer, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion, according to Michigan's Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Zach's Zoomer?

Zach's Zoomer is 41 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Zach's Zoomer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Zach's Zoomer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Michigan&#x27;s Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Zach's Zoomer open?

Zach's Zoomer opened on 22 Jul 1994.

Who built Zach's Zoomer?

Zach's Zoomer was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.

How long is the track on Zach's Zoomer?

RCDB lists 1,400 ft (427 m) of track.

How does Zach's Zoomer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Zach's Zoomer at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Zach's Zoomer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 46 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
