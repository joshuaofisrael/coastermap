Thunderhawk

Michigan's Adventure · Muskegon, Michigan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2008.

Thunderhawk is a steel inverted roller coaster at Michigan's Adventure in Muskegon, Michigan, built by Vekoma. It opened on 17 May 2008. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Thunderhawk is like

Thunderhawk pairs a 109.3 ft structure with 2,260.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2008, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Michigan's Adventure. It is one of 18 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 15th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Mind Eraser at Six Flags Darien Lake. Height rules for this model differ by park: Michigan's Adventure sets 52 in, while Energylandia sets 140 cm, Heide Park sets 130 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Michigan's Adventure. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thunderhawk sits on the north-east side of Michigan's Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wolverine Wildcat, roughly 170 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 320 m. Thunderhawk layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Thunderhawk's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Double In-Line Twist

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Michigan's Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Thunderhawk

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Michigan's Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Thunderhawk?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Thunderhawk, according to Michigan's Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Thunderhawk?

Thunderhawk is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Thunderhawk go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thunderhawk go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Thunderhawk scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Michigan&#x27;s Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Thunderhawk open?

Thunderhawk opened on 17 May 2008.

Who built Thunderhawk?

Thunderhawk was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Thunderhawk?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Thunderhawk get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Thunderhawk at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Thunderhawk good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Thunderhawk operate before?

RCDB records it at Geauga Lake & Wildwater Kingdom (as Thunderhawk).

Is Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Michigan's Adventure ride opened in 2008 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; both list a 52 in minimum.
