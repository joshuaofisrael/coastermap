Rollie's Coaster

Morey's Piers · Wildwood, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 44 to 50 in (112 to 127 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Rollie's Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Morey's Piers in Wildwood, New Jersey, built by Pinfari. It opened in 1999. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rollie's Coaster is like

Opened in 1999, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Morey's Piers.

A rider who just meets its 50 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Morey's Piers, out of 5 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 44 to 50 in (112 to 127 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rollie's Coaster sits on the south-west side of Morey's Piers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sea Serpent, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wild Whizzer at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 44 to 50 in (112 to 127 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 75 in (190 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rollie's Coaster?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Rollie's Coaster, or 44 to 50 in (112 to 127 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 75 in (190 cm). Riders 44 to 50 in must ride with an adult, according to Morey's Piers, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Rollie's Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rollie's Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rollie's Coaster open?

Rollie's Coaster opened in 1999.

Who built Rollie's Coaster?

Rollie's Coaster was manufactured by Pinfari.

How does Rollie's Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Rollie's Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
