Doo Wopper

Morey's Piers · Wildwood, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; 35 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Doo Wopper is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Morey's Piers in Wildwood, New Jersey, built by Zamperla. It opened on 14 May 2005. The ride stands 35 ft tall and runs 885 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Doo Wopper is like

At 35 ft tall with 885 ft of track, Doo Wopper balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2005, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Morey's Piers. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Zig Zag Coaster / 270m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Toboggan Nordique at La Ronde and Woodstock Express at Walibi Rhône-Alpes. Height rules for this model differ by park: Morey's Piers sets 48 in, while Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Morey's Piers (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Doo Wopper sits on the north-east side of Morey's Piers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Runaway Tram, roughly 20 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Great Nor'Easter at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Doo Wopper?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Doo Wopper, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Morey's Piers, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Doo Wopper?

Doo Wopper is 35 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Doo Wopper go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Doo Wopper scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Doo Wopper open?

Doo Wopper opened on 14 May 2005.

Who built Doo Wopper?

Doo Wopper was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Doo Wopper?

RCDB lists 885 ft (270 m) of track.

How does Doo Wopper get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Doo Wopper at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 500 riders.

Is Doo Wopper good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Doo Wopper at Morey's Piers the same as Woodstock Express at Walibi Rhône-Alpes?

Both are Zamperla Zig Zag Coaster / 270m coasters on RCDB; the Morey's Piers ride opened in 2005 and the Walibi Rhône-Alpes ride in 2002; Morey's Piers sets 48 in and Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm.
