Morey's Piers height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Wildwood, New Jersey · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Morey's Piers in Wildwood, New Jersey has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Sea Serpent to 52 in (132 cm) on The Great Nor'Easter. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Morey's Piers are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Morey's Piers a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 43% of its lineup, against 37% across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Great White).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sea Serpent, which opened in 1984.

What can my child ride at Morey's Piers? By height

At Morey's Piers the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Doo Wopper (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Wild Whizzer (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Sea Serpent at 48 in (122 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: The Great Nor'Easter (up to 79 in (201 cm)), Sea Serpent (up to 81 in (206 cm)), Rollie's Coaster (up to 75 in (190 cm)) and Wild Whizzer (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Morey's Piers

These are the rules Morey's Piers states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Rollie's Coaster: riders 44 to 50 in (112 to 127 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Doo Wopper: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wild Whizzer: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Runaway Tram: riders 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Morey's Piers for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Morey's Piers, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wild Whizzer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Rollie's Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (50 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: The Great Nor'Easter, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 115 ft tall, 55 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Morey's Piers?

Roller coaster height requirements at Morey's Piers run from 48 in (122 cm) (Sea Serpent) to 52 in (132 cm) (The Great Nor'Easter), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Morey's Piers have?

Morey's Piers has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Morey's Piers?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is The Great Nor'Easter (115 ft tall, 55 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Morey's Piers?

Sea Serpent, at 116.5 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Morey's Piers?

The Great Nor'Easter, with a top speed of 55 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Morey's Piers for first-timers?

Wild Whizzer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Morey's Piers?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Doo Wopper, Wild Whizzer, Runaway Tram.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Morey's Piers?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Sea Serpent, Doo Wopper, Wild Whizzer, Runaway Tram. With a companion they can also ride Rollie's Coaster.

Which coasters at Morey's Piers do not go upside down?

Rollie's Coaster, Great White, Doo Wopper, Wild Whizzer, Runaway Tram have no inversions, per RCDB.
