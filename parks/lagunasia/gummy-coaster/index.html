Gummy Coaster

Lagunasia · Gamagori, Aichi, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 21.1 mph; 39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Gummy Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagunasia in Gamagori, Aichi, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It first opened on 25 Apr 2002 and was previously known as Stellar Coaster. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, reaches 21.1 mph and runs 951.4 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gummy Coaster is like

Gummy Coaster pairs a 39.4 ft structure with 951.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Lagunasia. It has run under other names (Stellar Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Lagunasia, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gummy Coaster sits on the south-east side of Lagunasia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pirates' Blast, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aqua Wind at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Gummy Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders from 90 cm may ride with a guardian. The park map lists it under its former name, Stella Coaster.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lagunasia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gummy Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Gummy Coaster, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Riders from 90 cm may ride with a guardian. The park map lists it under its former name, Stella Coaster, according to Lagunasia, official guide map with attraction limits (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gummy Coaster?

Gummy Coaster is 39.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gummy Coaster go?

Its top speed is 21.1 mph (34 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gummy Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gummy Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Gummy Coaster open?

Gummy Coaster opened on 25 Apr 2002 (previously known as Stellar Coaster).

Who built Gummy Coaster?

Gummy Coaster was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the track on Gummy Coaster?

RCDB lists 951.4 ft of track.

How does Gummy Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Gummy Coaster at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Gummy Coaster use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, single position lap bar.

Is Gummy Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.1 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
