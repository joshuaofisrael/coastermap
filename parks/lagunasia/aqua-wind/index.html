Aqua Wind

Lagunasia · Gamagori, Aichi, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 49.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Aqua Wind is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagunasia in Gamagori, Aichi, Japan, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 24 Apr 2004. The ride stands 49.2 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1246.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Aqua Wind is like

At 49.2 ft tall with 1,246.7 ft of track, Aqua Wind balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Lagunasia.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Lagunasia, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Aqua Wind sits on the north-west side of Lagunasia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pirates' Blast, roughly 140 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gummy Coaster at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Aqua Wind

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders from 110 cm may ride with a guardian; it runs forwards and backwards.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lagunasia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Aqua Wind?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Aqua Wind, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders from 110 cm may ride with a guardian; it runs forwards and backwards, according to Lagunasia, official guide map with attraction limits (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Aqua Wind?

Aqua Wind is 49.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Aqua Wind go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Aqua Wind go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Aqua Wind scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Aqua Wind open?

Aqua Wind opened on 24 Apr 2004.

Who built Aqua Wind?

Aqua Wind was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Aqua Wind?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds over 1246.7 ft of track.

How does Aqua Wind get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Aqua Wind at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Aqua Wind good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
