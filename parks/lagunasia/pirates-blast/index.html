Pirates' Blast

Lagunasia · Gamagori, Aichi, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 26.7 mph; 39.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Pirates' Blast is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagunasia in Gamagori, Aichi, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It opened on 17 Mar 2007. The ride stands 39.3 ft tall, reaches 26.7 mph and runs 1568.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pirates' Blast is like

With 1,568.3 ft of track for a 39.3 ft structure, Pirates' Blast is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2007, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Lagunasia.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Lagunasia, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pirates' Blast sits near the middle of Lagunasia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gummy Coaster, roughly 80 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aqua Wind at about 140 m. How Pirates' Blast compares

On our intensity ranking Pirates' Blast is number 2 of 3 at Lagunasia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Gummy Coaster (39.4 ft tall, 21.1 mph, no inversions; 110 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Aqua Wind (49.2 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pirates' Blast?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Pirates' Blast. No accompanied option is listed, according to Lagunasia, official guide map with attraction limits (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pirates' Blast?

Pirates' Blast is 39.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pirates' Blast go?

Its top speed is 26.7 mph (43 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pirates' Blast go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pirates' Blast scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Pirates' Blast open?

Pirates' Blast opened on 17 Mar 2007.

Who built Pirates' Blast?

Pirates' Blast was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the track on Pirates' Blast?

RCDB lists 1568.3 ft of track.

How many people ride Pirates' Blast at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Pirates' Blast good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 26.7 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
