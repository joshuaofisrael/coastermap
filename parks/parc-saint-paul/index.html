Parc Saint Paul height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Saint-Paul, Oise, France · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parc Saint Paul in Saint-Paul, Oise, France has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Mini Mouse Cartoon to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Aérotrain. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Parc Saint Paul are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Parc Saint Paul a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Wood Express).

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Mini Mouse Cartoon, which opened in 2004.

Parc Saint Paul publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parc Saint Paul? By height

At Parc Saint Paul the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Pomme (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Mini Mouse Cartoon at 100 cm (about 39.4 in).

Companion rules at Parc Saint Paul

These are the rules Parc Saint Paul states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Pomme: riders 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Aérotrain: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wood Express: riders 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Mini Mouse Cartoon: Riders under 100 cm must be accompanied; no lower limit is listed.

Best coasters at Parc Saint Paul for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parc Saint Paul, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mini Mouse Cartoon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (100 cm minimum; 10 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Aérotrain, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 36.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Wood Express, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 50 ft tall, 38.2 mph, no inversions).
