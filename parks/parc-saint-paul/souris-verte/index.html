Souris Verte

Parc Saint Paul · Saint-Paul, Oise, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Souris Verte is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Saint Paul in Saint-Paul, Oise, France, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2007. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Souris Verte is like

With 1,377.9 ft of track for a 42.7 ft structure, Souris Verte is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parc Saint Paul. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Twister Coaster / 420m design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Sidewinder Safari at Discovery Kingdom, Galaxy Spin at Fun Spot Kissimmee, The Tickler at Luna Park and Wild Mouse at Beech Bend.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Souris Verte sits on the north-west side of Parc Saint Paul's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wood Express, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pomme at about 230 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Souris Verte?

Souris Verte is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Souris Verte go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Souris Verte go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Souris Verte scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Souris Verte open?

Souris Verte opened in 2007.

Who built Souris Verte?

Souris Verte was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Souris Verte?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1377.9 ft of track.

How does Souris Verte get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Souris Verte at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Souris Verte good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Souris Verte at Parc Saint Paul the same as Wild Mouse at Beech Bend?

Both are Zamperla Twister Coaster / 420m coasters on RCDB; the Parc Saint Paul ride opened in 2007 and the Beech Bend ride in 2005.
