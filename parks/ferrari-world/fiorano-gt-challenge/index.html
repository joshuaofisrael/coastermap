Fiorano GT Challenge

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi · Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 59 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Fiorano GT Challenge is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 4 Nov 2010. The ride reaches 59 mph and runs 3280.8 ft of track on each track. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Fiorano GT Challenge is like

Opened in 2010, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Ferrari World, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Flying Aces, roughly 290 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Fiorano GT Challenge layout, trains and ride system

Fiorano GT Challenge starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 6 booster sections (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (8 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (rolling)
  2. LSM Boost ×3
  3. LSM Launch (rolling)
  4. LSM Boost ×3

It has two tracks, and RCDB may list an element once for each track.

How intense is it?

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fiorano GT Challenge?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Fiorano GT Challenge. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Fiorano GT Challenge go?

Its top speed is 59 mph (95 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fiorano GT Challenge go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fiorano GT Challenge scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Fiorano GT Challenge open?

Fiorano GT Challenge opened on 4 Nov 2010.

Who built Fiorano GT Challenge?

Fiorano GT Challenge was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Fiorano GT Challenge?

RCDB lists 3280.8 ft of track.

Does Fiorano GT Challenge launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Fiorano GT Challenge at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Fiorano GT Challenge good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 59 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
