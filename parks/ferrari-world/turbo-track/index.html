Turbo Track

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi · Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 63.4 mph; 210 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Turbo Track is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 29 Mar 2017. The ride stands 210 ft tall, reaches 63.4 mph and runs 590.6 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Turbo Track is like

Turbo Track packs its 590.6 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (210 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together.

Opened in 2017, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Ferrari World, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Written from RCDB statistics and the park's published height rule; it describes the layout, not a review.

Turbo Track layout, trains and ride system

Turbo Track starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (3 entries):

  1. LSM Launch
  2. LSM Boost
  3. Twisted Vertical Rollback

For families checking heights: it appears on our 54-inch list as a ride-alone coaster.

Elsewhere, Intamin Amusement Rides also built Piraten (Djurs Sommerland), Divertical (Mirabilandia), Speed (Energylandia) and Ride of Steel (Six Flags Darien Lake). See all Intamin Amusement Rides coasters.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Turbo Track compares

On our intensity ranking Turbo Track is number 2 of 5 at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Mission Ferrari (44.7 mph, 2 inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Flying Aces (206.7 ft tall, 74.6 mph, 1 inversion, 5 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Turbo Track

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Turbo Track?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Turbo Track. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in), according to Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Turbo Track?

Turbo Track is 210 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Turbo Track go?

Its top speed is 63.4 mph (102 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Turbo Track go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Turbo Track scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Turbo Track open?

Turbo Track opened on 29 Mar 2017.

Who built Turbo Track?

Turbo Track was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Turbo Track?

RCDB lists 590.6 ft of track.

Does Turbo Track launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

Is Turbo Track good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 63.4 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
