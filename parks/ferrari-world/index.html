Ferrari World Abu Dhabi height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Formula Rossa Junior to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Fiorano GT Challenge. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Ferrari World Abu Dhabi a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 60% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Fiorano GT Challenge, which opened in 2010.

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi? By height

At Ferrari World Abu Dhabi the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Fiorano GT Challenge (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Turbo Track (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)) and Flying Aces (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Formula Rossa Junior, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 38.7 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Fiorano GT Challenge, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 59 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Flying Aces, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 206.7 ft tall, 74.6 mph, 1 inversion, 5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
