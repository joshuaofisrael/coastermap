Moto Coaster

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 37.3 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Moto Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Zamperla. It first opened on 3 May 2008 and was previously known as Orange County Choppers MotoCoaster. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1194.2 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Moto Coaster is like

At 42.7 ft tall with 1,194.2 ft of track, Moto Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2008, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Darien Lake. It has run under other names (Orange County Choppers MotoCoaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Moto Coaster / 364m design, and the first of them to open. Others include MotoGee at Särkänniemi and Steeplechase at Luna Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Darien Lake sets 48 in, while Särkänniemi sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Six Flags Darien Lake, out of 7 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Moto Coaster sits on the north-east side of Six Flags Darien Lake's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Boomerang, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tantrum at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Moto Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Moto Coaster, according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Moto Coaster?

Moto Coaster is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Moto Coaster go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Moto Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Moto Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Moto Coaster open?

Moto Coaster opened on 3 May 2008 (previously known as Orange County Choppers MotoCoaster).

Who built Moto Coaster?

Moto Coaster was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Moto Coaster?

RCDB lists 1194.2 ft of track.

Does Moto Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a flywheel launch.

How many people ride Moto Coaster at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Moto Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Moto Coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake the same as MotoGee at Särkänniemi?

Both are Zamperla Moto Coaster / 364m coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Darien Lake ride opened in 2008 and the Särkänniemi ride in 2010; Six Flags Darien Lake sets 48 in and Särkänniemi sets 120 cm.
