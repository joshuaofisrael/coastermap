Ride of Steel

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 73 mph; 208 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Ride of Steel is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 15 May 1999 and was previously known as Superman - Ride Of Steel. The ride stands 208 ft tall, drops 205 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 5,400 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ride of Steel?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Ride of Steel. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ride of Steel?

Ride of Steel is 208 ft tall, with a drop of 205 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ride of Steel go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ride of Steel go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ride of Steel scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ride of Steel open?

Ride of Steel opened on 15 May 1999 (previously known as Superman - Ride Of Steel).

Who built Ride of Steel?

Ride of Steel was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
