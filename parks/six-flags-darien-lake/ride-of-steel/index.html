Ride of Steel

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 73 mph; 208 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Ride of Steel is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 15 May 1999 and was previously known as Superman - Ride Of Steel. The ride stands 208 ft tall, drops 205 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 5,400 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ride of Steel is like

Ride of Steel pairs a 208 ft structure with 5,400 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1999, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Darien Lake. It has run under other names (Superman - Ride Of Steel), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Darien Lake. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ride of Steel sits on the west side of Six Flags Darien Lake's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Predator, roughly 210 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tantrum at about 370 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Ride of Steel compares

On our intensity ranking Ride of Steel is number 4 of 8 at Six Flags Darien Lake, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ride of Steel?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Ride of Steel. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ride of Steel?

Ride of Steel is 208 ft tall, with a drop of 205 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ride of Steel go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ride of Steel go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ride of Steel scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ride of Steel open?

Ride of Steel opened on 15 May 1999 (previously known as Superman - Ride Of Steel).

Who built Ride of Steel?

Ride of Steel was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Ride of Steel?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 2 seconds over 5,400 ft of track.

How does Ride of Steel get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ride of Steel at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train.

Is Ride of Steel good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 73 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
