Six Flags Darien Lake height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Darien Center, New York · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Hoot N Holler to 54 in (137 cm) on Ride of Steel. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Darien Lake are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Darien Lake a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 75% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 8 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Hoot N Holler, which opened in 1981.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Darien Lake? By height

At Six Flags Darien Lake the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Hoot N Holler. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Predator at 48 in (122 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Mind Eraser and Ride of Steel. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Mind Eraser (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Ride of Steel (up to 76 in (193 cm)), Hoot N Holler (up to 54 in (137 cm)) and Tantrum (up to 76 in (193 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Six Flags Darien Lake for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Darien Lake, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Hoot N Holler, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; 10.8 ft tall, 16.2 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Moto Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Viper, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 121 ft tall, 50 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
