Six Flags Darien Lake roller coasters

Darien Center, New York · 8 coasters

Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Ride of Steel at 208 ft, which is also the fastest at 73 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Hoot N Holler.
