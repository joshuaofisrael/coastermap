Boomerang

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1998.

Boomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Vekoma. It opened on 16 May 1998. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Boomerang?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Boomerang, according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Boomerang?

Boomerang is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Boomerang go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Boomerang go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

Boomerang opened on 16 May 1998.
