Boomerang

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1998.

Boomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Vekoma. It opened on 16 May 1998. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Boomerang is like

Boomerang packs its 935 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (116.5 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Darien Lake. It is one of 28 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 13th of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Darien Lake sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Walibi Belgium sets 130 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Six Flags Darien Lake, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Boomerang sits on the north side of Six Flags Darien Lake's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Moto Coaster, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tantrum at about 100 m. Boomerang layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Boomerang's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Catch Car Lift Hill
  2. Cobra Roll
  3. Loop
  4. Chain Lift Hill

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Boomerang compares

On our intensity ranking Boomerang is number 5 of 8 at Six Flags Darien Lake, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Boomerang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Darien Lake app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Boomerang?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Boomerang, according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Boomerang?

Boomerang is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Boomerang go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Boomerang go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Boomerang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Boomerang open?

Boomerang opened on 16 May 1998.

Who built Boomerang?

Boomerang was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Boomerang?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Boomerang get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Boomerang at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Boomerang good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Boomerang at Six Flags Darien Lake the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Darien Lake ride opened in 1998 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; Six Flags Darien Lake sets 48 in and Bellewaerde sets 130 cm.
