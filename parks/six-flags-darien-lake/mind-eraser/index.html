Mind Eraser

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1997.

Mind Eraser is a steel inverted roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Vekoma. It opened on 17 May 1997. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mind Eraser?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Mind Eraser. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mind Eraser?

Mind Eraser is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mind Eraser go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mind Eraser go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Mind Eraser scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mind Eraser open?

Mind Eraser opened on 17 May 1997.
