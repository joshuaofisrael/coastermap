Hoot N Holler

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); top speed 16.2 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1981.

Hoot N Holler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Zierer. It first opened on 1981 and was previously known as Brain Teaser and later Nessie the Dreamy Dragon and later Ladybug. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 16.2 mph and runs 197.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall, and no taller than 54 in.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hoot N Holler?

Riders must be at least 36 inches (91 cm) tall to ride Hoot N Holler. The maximum rider height is 54 inches, according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hoot N Holler?

Hoot N Holler is 10.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hoot N Holler go?

Its top speed is 16.2 mph (26 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hoot N Holler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hoot N Holler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Hoot N Holler open?

Hoot N Holler opened on 1981 (previously known as Brain Teaser and later Nessie the Dreamy Dragon and later Ladybug).

Who built Hoot N Holler?

Hoot N Holler was manufactured by Zierer.
