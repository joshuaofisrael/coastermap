Tantrum

Six Flags Darien Lake · Darien Center, New York

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52.8 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2018.

Tantrum is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake in Darien Center, New York, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 25 May 2018. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 52.8 mph and runs 1246.7 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tantrum?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Tantrum. The maximum rider height is 76 inches, according to Six Flags Darien Lake, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tantrum?

Tantrum is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tantrum go?

Its top speed is 52.8 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tantrum go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Tantrum scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Darien Lake rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tantrum open?

Tantrum opened on 25 May 2018.

Who built Tantrum?

Tantrum was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.
