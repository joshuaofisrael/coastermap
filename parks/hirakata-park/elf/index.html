Elf

Hirakata Park · Hirakata, Osaka, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 36.1 mph; 60.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Elf is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Hirakata Park in Hirakata, Osaka, Japan, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on Mar 2001. The ride stands 60.7 ft tall, reaches 36.1 mph and runs 2282.2 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Elf is like

Elf spreads 2,282.2 ft of track across a layout only 60.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2001, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hirakata Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Wooden Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include American Eagle at Six Flags Great America and Jupiter at Kijima Kogen. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hirakata Park sets 110 cm, while Six Flags Great America sets 48 in and Kijima Kogen sets 120 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Hirakata Park, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Elf sits near the middle of Hirakata Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Crazy Mouse, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Korottruck at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Elf compares

On our intensity ranking Elf is number 2 of 5 at Hirakata Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Elf?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Elf, according to Hirakata Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Elf?

Elf is 60.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Elf go?

Its top speed is 36.1 mph (58 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Elf go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Elf scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Elf open?

Elf opened on Mar 2001.

Who built Elf?

Elf was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Elf?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 38 seconds over 2282.2 ft of track.

How does Elf get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Elf at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Elf good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 36.1 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Elf at Hirakata Park the same as American Eagle at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Wooden Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Hirakata Park ride opened in 2001 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1981; Hirakata Park sets 110 cm and Six Flags Great America sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 60.7 ft and 127 ft.
