Booster Bike

Toverland · Sevenum, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 46.6 mph; 49.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Booster Bike is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Toverland in Sevenum, Netherlands, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Jul 2004. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 1988.2 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Booster Bike is like

Booster Bike spreads 1,988.2 ft of track across a layout only 49.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Toverland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Toverland. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Booster Bike sits on the south-west side of Toverland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Troy, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Fēnix at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Booster Bike?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Booster Bike, according to Toverland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Booster Bike?

Booster Bike is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Booster Bike go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Booster Bike go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Booster Bike scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.
