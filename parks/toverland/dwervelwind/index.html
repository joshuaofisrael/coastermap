Dwervelwind

Toverland · Sevenum, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 43.5 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Dwervelwind is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Toverland in Sevenum, Netherlands, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 29 Sep 2012 and was previously known as d'wervelwind. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1515.7 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dwervelwind is like

Dwervelwind pairs a 65.6 ft structure with 1,515.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2012, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Toverland. It has run under other names (d'wervelwind), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Spinning Coaster / Twist design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Twist at Le PAL. Height rules for this model differ by park: Toverland sets 120 cm, while Le PAL sets 140 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Toverland (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dwervelwind sits on the north-east side of Toverland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Fēnix, roughly 190 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Booster Bike at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Dwervelwind

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult (16+).
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Toverland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dwervelwind?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Dwervelwind, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult (16+), according to Toverland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dwervelwind?

Dwervelwind is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dwervelwind go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dwervelwind go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dwervelwind scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Dwervelwind open?

Dwervelwind opened on 29 Sep 2012 (previously known as d'wervelwind).

Who built Dwervelwind?

Dwervelwind was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Dwervelwind?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 52 seconds over 1515.7 ft of track.

How does Dwervelwind get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dwervelwind at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Dwervelwind good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Dwervelwind at Toverland the same as Twist at Le PAL?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Spinning Coaster / Twist coasters on RCDB; the Toverland ride opened in 2012 and the Le PAL ride in 2011; Toverland sets 120 cm and Le PAL sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 65.6 ft and 72.2 ft.
