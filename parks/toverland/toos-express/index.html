Toos-Express

Toverland · Sevenum, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 39.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Toos-Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Toverland in Sevenum, Netherlands, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 19 May 2001 and was previously known as Achtbaan and later Boomerang. The ride stands 39.3 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1049.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Toos-Express is like

At 39.3 ft tall with 1,049.9 ft of track, Toos-Express balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2001, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Toverland. It has run under other names (Achtbaan, then Boomerang), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Toverland (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Toos-Express layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Toos-Express?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Toos-Express, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Toverland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Toos-Express?

Toos-Express is 39.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Toos-Express go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Toos-Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Toos-Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Toos-Express open?

Toos-Express opened on 19 May 2001 (previously known as Achtbaan and later Boomerang).

Who built Toos-Express?

Toos-Express was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Toos-Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 52 seconds over 1049.9 ft of track.

How does Toos-Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Toos-Express at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Toos-Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
