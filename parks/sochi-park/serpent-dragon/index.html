Serpent Dragon

Sochi Park · Sochi, Krasnodar Krai, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 124.7 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2014.

Serpent Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Sochi Park in Sochi, Krasnodar Krai, Russia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 124.7 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3464.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Serpent Dragon is like

Serpent Dragon pairs a 124.7 ft structure with 3,464.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,464.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2014, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Sochi Park. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Launch Coaster / Blue Fire design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park, Velociraptor at IMG Worlds, Giant Digger at Lotte World Busan and Nesterov Loop at Divo Ostrov. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Serpent Dragon sits on the south side of Sochi Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Forest Mail, roughly 180 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sharolet at about 340 m. Serpent Dragon layout, trains and ride system

Serpent Dragon starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 62.1 mph in 2.5 s)
  2. Loop (105 ft tall)
  3. Twisted Horseshoe Roll
  4. In-Line Twist

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Serpent Dragon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Змей Горыныч: from 130 cm and 7 years old; children aged 7 to 10 ride with an adult.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Sochi Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Serpent Dragon?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Serpent Dragon. The park lists it as Змей Горыныч: from 130 cm and 7 years old; children aged 7 to 10 ride with an adult, according to Sochi Park, official ride page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Serpent Dragon?

Serpent Dragon is 124.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Serpent Dragon go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Serpent Dragon go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Serpent Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Serpent Dragon open?

Serpent Dragon opened in 2014.

Who built Serpent Dragon?

Serpent Dragon was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Serpent Dragon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 3464.5 ft of track.

Does Serpent Dragon launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Serpent Dragon at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 830 riders.

Is Serpent Dragon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Serpent Dragon at Sochi Park the same as Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Launch Coaster / Blue Fire coasters on RCDB; the Sochi Park ride opened in 2014 and the Europa-Park ride in 2009; both list a 130 cm minimum.
