Sharolet

Sochi Park · Sochi, Krasnodar Krai, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2014.

Sharolet is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Sochi Park in Sochi, Krasnodar Krai, Russia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened in 2014. The ride runs 1,213 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sharolet is like

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Sochi Park. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Large Park design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Coast Rider at Knott's, Apple Zapple at Kings Dominion, The Fly at Canada's Wonderland and The Great LEGO Race at Legoland Florida.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sharolet sits on the north-west side of Sochi Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Forest Mail, roughly 320 m away (120 cm minimum). Next closest is Serpent Dragon at about 340 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Sharolet compares

On our intensity ranking Sharolet is number 2 of 3 at Sochi Park, where 1 is the most intense.

Frequently asked questions

Does Sharolet go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sharolet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Sharolet open?

Sharolet opened in 2014.

Who built Sharolet?

Sharolet was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Sharolet?

RCDB lists 1,213 ft (370 m) of track.

How does Sharolet get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Sharolet at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Sharolet good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Sharolet at Sochi Park the same as The Fly at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Large Park coasters on RCDB; the Sochi Park ride opened in 2014 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1999.
