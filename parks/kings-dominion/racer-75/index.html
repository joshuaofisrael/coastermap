Racer 75

Kings Dominion · Doswell, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 56 mph; 85 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1975.

Racer 75 is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It first opened on 3 May 1975 and was previously known as Rebel Yell. The ride stands 85 ft tall, reaches 56 mph and runs 3368.5 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Racer 75 is like

Racer 75 spreads 3,368.5 ft of track across a layout only 85 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1975, it is the 2nd oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Kings Dominion. It has run under other names (Rebel Yell), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 8 other coasters at Kings Dominion, out of 12 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Racer 75 sits on the south-east side of Kings Dominion's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Backlot Stunt Coaster, roughly 210 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pantherian at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Racer 75?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Racer 75, according to Kings Dominion, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Racer 75?

Racer 75 is 85 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Racer 75 go?

Its top speed is 56 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Racer 75 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Racer 75 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Racer 75 open?

Racer 75 opened on 3 May 1975 (previously known as Rebel Yell).

Who built Racer 75?
