Kings Dominion roller coasters

Doswell, Virginia · 13 coasters

Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia has 13 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Pantherian at 305 ft, which is also the fastest at 90 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for The Great Pumpkin Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Kings Dominion have?

Kings Dominion has 13 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kings Dominion?

Pantherian, at 305 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kings Dominion?

Pantherian, with a top speed of 90 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Kings Dominion?

By height requirement, The Great Pumpkin Coaster has the lowest minimum at 36 inches (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Kings Dominion coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: The Great Pumpkin Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Kings Dominion coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Racer 75, Woodstock Express, Grizzly, Reptilian, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Twisted Timbers, Tumbili, Rapterra. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Kings Dominion do not go upside down?

Racer 75, Woodstock Express, Grizzly, Reptilian, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Apple Zapple, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Pantherian, Tumbili have no inversions, per RCDB.
