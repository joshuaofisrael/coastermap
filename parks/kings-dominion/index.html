Kings Dominion height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Doswell, Virginia · 13 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia has 13 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on The Great Pumpkin Coaster to 54 in (137 cm) on Flight of Fear. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 9 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 13 of them.

What the coasters at Kings Dominion are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 8 as extreme, which makes Kings Dominion a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 62% of its lineup, against 40% across all 232 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 10 of the 13 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 13 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Racer 75, Woodstock Express and Grizzly); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Flight of Fear).

The most common builder is Premier Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Woodstock Express, which opened in 1974.

Pantherian is the 9th tallest of the 1023 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Pantherian is the 9th fastest of the 979 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Kings Dominion? By height

At Kings Dominion the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 8 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 13 coasters with a verified rule: The Great Pumpkin Coaster. Still too short for 12 coasters, the nearest being Woodstock Express at 46 in (117 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 13 coasters with a verified rule: The Great Pumpkin Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)) and Reptilian (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)). Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being Racer 75 at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 9 of 13 coasters with a verified rule, 8 more than at the height below: Racer 75, Woodstock Express, Grizzly, Reptilian, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Twisted Timbers, Tumbili and Rapterra. With a companion they can also ride Apple Zapple (44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Flight of Fear at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 13 of 13 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Flight of Fear, Apple Zapple, Dominator and Pantherian. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Reptilian (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Pantherian (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Tumbili (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Kings Dominion

These are the rules Kings Dominion states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Woodstock Express: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Reptilian: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Apple Zapple: riders 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Kings Dominion for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Kings Dominion, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Great Pumpkin Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Backlot Stunt Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 45.2 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Pantherian, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 305 ft tall, 90 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Kings Dominion?

Roller coaster height requirements at Kings Dominion run from 36 in (91 cm) (The Great Pumpkin Coaster) to 54 in (137 cm) (Flight of Fear), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Kings Dominion have?

Kings Dominion has 13 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Kings Dominion?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Pantherian (305 ft tall, 90 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kings Dominion?

Pantherian, at 305 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kings Dominion?

Pantherian, with a top speed of 90 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Kings Dominion for first-timers?

The Great Pumpkin Coaster is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 36 in (91 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Kings Dominion?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: The Great Pumpkin Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express, Reptilian.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Kings Dominion?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Racer 75, Woodstock Express, Grizzly, Reptilian, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Twisted Timbers, Tumbili, Rapterra. With a companion they can also ride Apple Zapple.

Which coasters at Kings Dominion do not go upside down?

Racer 75, Woodstock Express, Grizzly, Reptilian, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Apple Zapple, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Pantherian, Tumbili have no inversions, per RCDB.
