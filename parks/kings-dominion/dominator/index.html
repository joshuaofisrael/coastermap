Dominator

Kings Dominion · Doswell, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 65 mph; 157 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2008.

Dominator is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 24 May 2008. The ride stands 157 ft tall, drops 148 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 4,210 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Dominator is like

At 157 ft tall with 4,210 ft of track, Dominator balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 4,210 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2008, it is the 9th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Kings Dominion.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Kings Dominion, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dominator sits on the north side of Kings Dominion's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock Express, roughly 110 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Great Pumpkin Coaster at about 120 m. Dominator layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Dominator's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop (135 ft tall)
  3. Cobra Roll
  4. Block Brake
  5. Interlocking Corkscrews

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

How Dominator compares

On our intensity ranking Dominator is number 2 of 13 at Kings Dominion, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 11 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Dominator

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Kings Dominion app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dominator?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Dominator, according to Kings Dominion, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dominator?

Dominator is 157 ft tall, with a drop of 148 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dominator go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dominator go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Dominator scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dominator open?

Dominator opened on 24 May 2008.

Who built Dominator?

Dominator was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Dominator?

RCDB lists 4,210 ft (1,283 m) of track.

How does Dominator get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dominator at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Dominator good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Dominator operate before?

RCDB records it at Geauga Lake & Wildwater Kingdom (as Dominator).
