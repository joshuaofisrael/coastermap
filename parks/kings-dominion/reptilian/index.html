Reptilian

Kings Dominion · Doswell, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm), or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion; top speed 41 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1988.

Reptilian is a steel roller coaster at Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 26 Mar 1988 and was previously known as Avalanche. The ride reaches 41 mph. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Reptilian is like

Opened in 1988, it is the 4th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Kings Dominion. It has run under other names (Avalanche), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 46 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Kings Dominion, out of 12 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Reptilian sits on the north-east side of Kings Dominion's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tumbili, roughly 100 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flight of Fear at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Reptilian

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 46 in (117 cm), and riders from 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Reptilian?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Reptilian, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Kings Dominion, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Reptilian go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Reptilian go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Reptilian scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Reptilian open?

Reptilian opened on 26 Mar 1988 (previously known as Avalanche).

Who built Reptilian?

Reptilian was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How does Reptilian get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Reptilian at once?

RCDB's train details give 14 seats per train.

Is Reptilian good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 41 mph; the minimum height is 46 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
