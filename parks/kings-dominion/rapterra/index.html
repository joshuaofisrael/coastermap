Rapterra

Kings Dominion · Doswell, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 65 mph; 145 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2025.

Rapterra is a steel wing roller coaster at Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 29 Mar 2025. The ride stands 145 ft tall, reaches 65 mph and runs 3,086 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Rapterra is like

Rapterra pairs a 145 ft structure with 3,086 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,086 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 13 coasters we list at Kings Dominion.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 8 other coasters at Kings Dominion, out of 12 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Rapterra layout, trains and ride system

Rapterra starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 4 track elements for Rapterra, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (rolling) (from 0 mph - 65 mph in 4 s)
  2. Dive Loop
  3. Corkscrew
  4. Heartline Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Rapterra

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Kings Dominion app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rapterra?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Rapterra, according to Kings Dominion, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rapterra?

Rapterra is 145 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rapterra go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rapterra go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Rapterra scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Rapterra open?

Rapterra opened on 29 Mar 2025.

Who built Rapterra?

Rapterra was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Rapterra?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 29 seconds over 3,086 ft of track.

Does Rapterra launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Rapterra at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Rapterra good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
