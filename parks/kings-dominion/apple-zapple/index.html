Apple Zapple

Kings Dominion · Doswell, Virginia

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 44 to 54 in with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 51.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Apple Zapple is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kings Dominion in Doswell, Virginia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 23 Mar 2002 and was previously known as Ricochet. The ride stands 51.8 ft tall, drops 49.8 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 44 to 54 in with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Apple Zapple?

Riders must be at least 54 inches (137 cm) tall to ride Apple Zapple, or 44 to 54 inches with a companion, according to Kings Dominion, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Apple Zapple?

Apple Zapple is 51.8 ft tall, with a drop of 49.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Apple Zapple go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Apple Zapple go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Apple Zapple scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Dominion rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Apple Zapple open?

Apple Zapple opened on 23 Mar 2002 (previously known as Ricochet).

Who built Apple Zapple?

Apple Zapple was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
