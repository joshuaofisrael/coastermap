Capitol Bullet Train

Motiongate Dubai · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 2 inversions; opened 2017.

Capitol Bullet Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Motiongate Dubai in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 20 Oct 2017. The ride runs 1,870 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Capitol Bullet Train is like

It goes upside down 2 times across 1,870 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2017, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Motiongate Dubai.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Motiongate, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Capitol Bullet Train sits on the south-east side of Motiongate's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is John Wick: Open Contract, roughly 70 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Green Hornet: High Speed Chase at about 170 m. Capitol Bullet Train layout, trains and ride system

Capitol Bullet Train starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 4 track elements for Capitol Bullet Train, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. Vertical Rollback
  3. Loop
  4. Zero-G Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Capitol Bullet Train

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Motiongate Dubai app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Capitol Bullet Train?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Capitol Bullet Train, according to Motiongate Dubai, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Capitol Bullet Train go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Capitol Bullet Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Capitol Bullet Train open?

Capitol Bullet Train opened on 20 Oct 2017.

Who built Capitol Bullet Train?

Capitol Bullet Train was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Capitol Bullet Train?

RCDB lists 1,870 ft (570 m) of track.

Does Capitol Bullet Train launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Capitol Bullet Train at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Capitol Bullet Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
