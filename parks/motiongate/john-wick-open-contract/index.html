John Wick: Open Contract

Motiongate Dubai · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 38 mph; 120 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

John Wick: Open Contract is a steel wing roller coaster at Motiongate Dubai in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 21 Jan 2022. The ride stands 120 ft tall, drops 54 ft, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,019 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding John Wick: Open Contract is like

John Wick: Open Contract packs its 1,019 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (120 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. RCDB lists a 54 ft drop against a 120 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2022, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Motiongate Dubai. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's 4D Free Spin / 110 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include The Joker at Six Flags Great Adventure, The Joker at Six Flags Great America, The Joker at Six Flags Over Texas and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Motiongate sets 125 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags Great America sets 48 in and Six Flags Over Texas sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Motiongate, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, John Wick: Open Contract sits on the south side of Motiongate's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Capitol Bullet Train, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Now You See Me: High Roller at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for John Wick: Open Contract?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride John Wick: Open Contract. The park also states that children must be accompanied by an adult, according to Motiongate Dubai, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is John Wick: Open Contract?

John Wick: Open Contract is 120 ft tall, with a drop of 54 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does John Wick: Open Contract go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does John Wick: Open Contract go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is John Wick: Open Contract scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did John Wick: Open Contract open?

John Wick: Open Contract opened on 21 Jan 2022.

Who built John Wick: Open Contract?

John Wick: Open Contract was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the track on John Wick: Open Contract?

RCDB lists 1,019 ft (311 m) of track.

How does John Wick: Open Contract get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride John Wick: Open Contract at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is John Wick: Open Contract good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 38 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is John Wick: Open Contract at Motiongate the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Both are S&S Worldwide 4D Free Spin / 110 coasters on RCDB; the Motiongate ride opened in 2022 and the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride in 2015; Motiongate sets 125 cm and Six Flags Fiesta Texas sets 48 in.
