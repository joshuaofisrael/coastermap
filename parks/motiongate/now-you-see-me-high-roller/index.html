Now You See Me: High Roller

Motiongate Dubai · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Now You See Me: High Roller is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Motiongate Dubai in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 21 Jan 2022. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1702.8 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Now You See Me: High Roller is like

Now You See Me: High Roller spreads 1,702.8 ft of track across a layout only 55.8 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Motiongate Dubai.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Motiongate, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Now You See Me: High Roller sits on the south-west side of Motiongate's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is John Wick: Open Contract, roughly 120 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Capitol Bullet Train at about 180 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Before you ride Now You See Me: High Roller

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park also states that children must be accompanied by an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Motiongate Dubai app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Now You See Me: High Roller?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Now You See Me: High Roller. The park also states that children must be accompanied by an adult, according to Motiongate Dubai, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Now You See Me: High Roller?

Now You See Me: High Roller is 55.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Now You See Me: High Roller go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Now You See Me: High Roller go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Now You See Me: High Roller scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Now You See Me: High Roller open?

Now You See Me: High Roller opened on 21 Jan 2022.

Who built Now You See Me: High Roller?

Now You See Me: High Roller was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Now You See Me: High Roller?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 43 seconds over 1702.8 ft of track.

How does Now You See Me: High Roller get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Now You See Me: High Roller at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 680 riders.

Is Now You See Me: High Roller good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
