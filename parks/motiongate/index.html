Motiongate Dubai height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Dubai, United Arab Emirates · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Motiongate Dubai in Dubai, United Arab Emirates has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Now You See Me: High Roller to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Green Hornet: High Speed Chase. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Motiongate Dubai are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Motiongate Dubai a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 43% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 49.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 7 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Capitol Bullet Train).

The most common builder is Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Green Hornet: High Speed Chase, which opened in 2016.

Motiongate Dubai publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Motiongate Dubai? By height

At Motiongate Dubai the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Motiongate Dubai for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Motiongate Dubai, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dragon Gliders, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (122 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Green Hornet: High Speed Chase, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: John Wick: Open Contract, the most intense coaster in the park (125 cm minimum; 120 ft tall, 38 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

What are the height requirements at Motiongate Dubai?

Roller coaster height requirements at Motiongate Dubai run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Now You See Me: High Roller) to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) (Green Hornet: High Speed Chase), according to official park sources checked 11 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is John Wick: Open Contract (120 ft tall, 38 mph, no inversions).
