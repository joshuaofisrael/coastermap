Madagascar Mad Pursuit

Motiongate Dubai · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 51.6 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Madagascar Mad Pursuit is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Motiongate Dubai in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened in 2017. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 51.6 mph and runs 2224.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Madagascar Mad Pursuit is like

Madagascar Mad Pursuit spreads 2,224.3 ft of track across a layout only 72.2 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2017, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Motiongate Dubai.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Motiongate, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Madagascar Mad Pursuit sits on the north-west side of Motiongate's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon Gliders, roughly 120 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Smurf Village Express at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

General guidance from how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule. See best seat on a roller coaster.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Madagascar Mad Pursuit?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Madagascar Mad Pursuit, according to Motiongate Dubai, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Madagascar Mad Pursuit?

Madagascar Mad Pursuit is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Madagascar Mad Pursuit go?

Its top speed is 51.6 mph (83 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Madagascar Mad Pursuit go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Madagascar Mad Pursuit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Madagascar Mad Pursuit open?

Madagascar Mad Pursuit opened in 2017.

Who built Madagascar Mad Pursuit?

Madagascar Mad Pursuit was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Madagascar Mad Pursuit?

RCDB lists 2224.3 ft of track.

Does Madagascar Mad Pursuit launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Madagascar Mad Pursuit at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Madagascar Mad Pursuit good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 51.6 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
