Pipeline: The Surf Coaster

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 60 mph; 110 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2023.

Pipeline: The Surf Coaster is a steel stand-up roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 27 May 2023. The ride stands 110 ft tall, reaches 60 mph and runs 2,950 ft of track. On a stand-up coaster riders travel standing, supported by a bicycle-style seat and a shoulder harness. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Pipeline: The Surf Coaster is like

At 110 ft tall with 2,950 ft of track, Pipeline: The Surf Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,950 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2023, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at SeaWorld Orlando.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at SeaWorld Orlando, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pipeline: The Surf Coaster sits on the west side of SeaWorld Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ice Breaker, roughly 240 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Manta at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Pipeline: The Surf Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Pipeline: The Surf Coaster is number 4 of 8 at SeaWorld Orlando, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Ice Breaker (93 ft tall, 52 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Mako (200 ft tall, 73 mph, no inversions; 54 in minimum).

Before you ride Pipeline: The Surf Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 78 in (198 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the SeaWorld Orlando app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pipeline: The Surf Coaster?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Pipeline: The Surf Coaster. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to SeaWorld Orlando, Pipeline: The Surf Coaster ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pipeline: The Surf Coaster?

Pipeline: The Surf Coaster is 110 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pipeline: The Surf Coaster go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pipeline: The Surf Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Pipeline: The Surf Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Pipeline: The Surf Coaster open?

Pipeline: The Surf Coaster opened on 27 May 2023.

Who built Pipeline: The Surf Coaster?

Pipeline: The Surf Coaster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Pipeline: The Surf Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds over 2,950 ft of track.

Does Pipeline: The Surf Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Pipeline: The Surf Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Pipeline: The Surf Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
