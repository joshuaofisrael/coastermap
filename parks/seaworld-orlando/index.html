SeaWorld Orlando height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Orlando, Florida · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 38 in (97 cm) on Super Grover's Box Car Derby to 54 in (137 cm) on Kraken. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at SeaWorld Orlando are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes SeaWorld Orlando a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 45% across all 131 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 54 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 8 go upside down.

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 5 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Journey to Atlantis, which opened in 1998.

What can my child ride at SeaWorld Orlando? By height

At SeaWorld Orlando the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Super Grover's Box Car Derby at 38 in (97 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Journey to Atlantis, Super Grover's Box Car Derby and Penguin Trek. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Ice Breaker at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Ice Breaker. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Kraken at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Kraken, Manta, Mako and Pipeline: The Surf Coaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Pipeline: The Surf Coaster (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Penguin Trek (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at SeaWorld Orlando for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for SeaWorld Orlando, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Super Grover's Box Car Derby, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (38 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Journey to Atlantis, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Kraken, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 149 ft tall, 65 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at SeaWorld Orlando?

Roller coaster height requirements at SeaWorld Orlando run from 38 in (97 cm) (Super Grover's Box Car Derby) to 54 in (137 cm) (Kraken), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does SeaWorld Orlando have?

SeaWorld Orlando has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Kraken (149 ft tall, 65 mph, 7 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando?

Mako, at 200 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando?

Mako, with a top speed of 73 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at SeaWorld Orlando for first-timers?

What can a 42-inch child ride at SeaWorld Orlando?

Which coasters at SeaWorld Orlando do not go upside down?
