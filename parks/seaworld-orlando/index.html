SeaWorld Orlando roller coasters

Orlando, Florida · 8 coasters

SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Mako at 200 ft, which is also the fastest at 73 mph. The lowest height requirement is 38 in (97 cm), for Super Grover's Box Car Derby.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does SeaWorld Orlando have?

SeaWorld Orlando has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando?

Mako, at 200 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando?

Mako, with a top speed of 73 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at SeaWorld Orlando?

By height requirement, Super Grover's Box Car Derby has the lowest minimum at 38 in (97 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which SeaWorld Orlando coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis, Super Grover's Box Car Derby, Penguin Trek. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which SeaWorld Orlando coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis, Super Grover's Box Car Derby, Ice Breaker, Penguin Trek. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at SeaWorld Orlando do not go upside down?

Journey to Atlantis, Super Grover's Box Car Derby, Mako, Ice Breaker, Penguin Trek have no inversions, per RCDB.
