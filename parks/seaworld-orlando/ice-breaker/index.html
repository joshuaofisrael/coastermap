Ice Breaker

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52 mph; 93 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Ice Breaker is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 18 Feb 2022. The ride stands 93 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,900 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 100°, past vertical.

What riding Ice Breaker is like

Ice Breaker pairs a 93 ft structure with 1,900 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 100°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down.

Opened in 2022, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at SeaWorld Orlando.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at SeaWorld Orlando, out of 7 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ice Breaker sits on the south-west side of SeaWorld Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Super Grover's Box Car Derby, roughly 230 m away (38 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pipeline: The Surf Coaster at about 240 m. Ice Breaker layout, trains and ride system

Ice Breaker starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Ice Breaker, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. Jr. Scorpion Tail (93 ft tall)
  3. Top Hat - Outside (80 ft tall)

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ice Breaker?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Ice Breaker, according to SeaWorld Orlando, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ice Breaker?

Ice Breaker is 93 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ice Breaker go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ice Breaker go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ice Breaker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Ice Breaker open?

Ice Breaker opened on 18 Feb 2022.

Who built Ice Breaker?

Ice Breaker was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the track on Ice Breaker?

RCDB lists 1,900 ft (579 m) of track.

Does Ice Breaker launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Ice Breaker at once?

Each train carries 18 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Ice Breaker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
