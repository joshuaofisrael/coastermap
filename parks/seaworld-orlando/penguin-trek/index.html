Penguin Trek

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 43 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Penguin Trek is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 Jul 2024. The ride stands 65 ft tall, reaches 43 mph and runs 3,020 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Penguin Trek?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Penguin Trek. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to SeaWorld Orlando, Penguin Trek ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Penguin Trek?

Penguin Trek is 65 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Penguin Trek go?

Its top speed is 43 mph (69 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Penguin Trek go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Penguin Trek scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Penguin Trek open?

Penguin Trek opened on 7 Jul 2024.

Who built Penguin Trek?

Penguin Trek was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
