Penguin Trek

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 43 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Penguin Trek is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 Jul 2024. The ride stands 65 ft tall, reaches 43 mph and runs 3,020 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Penguin Trek is like

Penguin Trek spreads 3,020 ft of track across a layout only 65 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at SeaWorld Orlando.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at SeaWorld Orlando, out of 7 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Penguin Trek layout, trains and ride system

Penguin Trek starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Penguin Trek, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. Dark/Show Section
  2. LSM Launch (rolling)
  3. LSM Boost

Penguin Trek trains and ride system

Trains9 cars per train. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 18 riders per train.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Penguin Trek?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Penguin Trek. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to SeaWorld Orlando, Penguin Trek ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Penguin Trek?

Penguin Trek is 65 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Penguin Trek go?

Its top speed is 43 mph (69 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Penguin Trek go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Penguin Trek scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Penguin Trek open?

Penguin Trek opened on 7 Jul 2024.

Who built Penguin Trek?

Penguin Trek was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Penguin Trek?

RCDB lists 3,020 ft (920 m) of track.

Does Penguin Trek launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Penguin Trek at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Penguin Trek good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
