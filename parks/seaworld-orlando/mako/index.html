Mako

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 73 mph; 200 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Mako is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 10 Jun 2016. The ride stands 200 ft tall, drops 200 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 4,760 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mako is like

Mako pairs a 200 ft structure with 4,760 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2016, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at SeaWorld Orlando. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the 14th of them to open. Others include Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island, Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg and Candymonium at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, Europa-Park sets 140 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at SeaWorld Orlando, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mako sits on the east side of SeaWorld Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kraken, roughly 180 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Journey to Atlantis at about 400 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Mako compares

On our intensity ranking Mako is number 3 of 8 at SeaWorld Orlando, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Pipeline: The Surf Coaster (110 ft tall, 60 mph, 1 inversion; 54 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Manta (140 ft tall, 56 mph, 4 inversions; 54 in minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mako?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Mako, according to SeaWorld Orlando, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mako?

Mako is 200 ft tall, with a drop of 200 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mako go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mako go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mako scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mako open?

Mako opened on 10 Jun 2016.

Who built Mako?

Mako was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

Is Mako at SeaWorld Orlando the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the SeaWorld Orlando ride opened in 2016 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 200 ft and 170 ft.
