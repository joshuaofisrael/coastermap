Kraken

SeaWorld Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 65 mph; 149 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2000.

Kraken is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 1 Jun 2000 and was previously known as Kraken Unleashed. The ride stands 149 ft tall, drops 144 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 4,177 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding Kraken is like

Kraken pairs a 149 ft structure with 4,177 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 7 times across 4,177 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2000, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at SeaWorld Orlando. It has run under other names (Kraken Unleashed), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at SeaWorld Orlando, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kraken sits on the east side of SeaWorld Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mako, roughly 180 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Journey to Atlantis at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Kraken

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the SeaWorld Orlando app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kraken?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Kraken, according to SeaWorld Orlando, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kraken?

Kraken is 149 ft tall, with a drop of 144 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kraken go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kraken go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Kraken scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Kraken open?

Kraken opened on 1 Jun 2000 (previously known as Kraken Unleashed).

Who built Kraken?

Kraken was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
