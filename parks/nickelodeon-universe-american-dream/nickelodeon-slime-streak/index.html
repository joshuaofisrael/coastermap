Nickelodeon Slime Streak

Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream · East Rutherford, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 37 mph; 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Nickelodeon Slime Streak is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey, built by Chance Rides. It opened on 25 Oct 2019. The ride stands 70 ft tall, reaches 37 mph and runs 1,263 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Nickelodeon Slime Streak is like

Nickelodeon Slime Streak pairs a 70 ft structure with 1,263 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2019, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nickelodeon Universe NJ (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.
