TMNT Shellraiser

Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream · East Rutherford, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 62.1 mph; 141 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2019.

TMNT Shellraiser is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 25 Oct 2019. The ride stands 141 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3280.8 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 7 times. Its steepest drop is 121.5°, past vertical.

What riding TMNT Shellraiser is like

At 141 ft tall with 3,280.8 ft of track, TMNT Shellraiser balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 121.5°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 7 times across 3,280.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Euro-Fighter / 1000 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Takabisha at Fuji-Q Highland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nickelodeon Universe NJ sets 48 in, while Fuji-Q Highland sets 125 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nickelodeon Universe NJ (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

TMNT Shellraiser layout, trains and ride system

TMNT Shellraiser starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. It also uses a vertical chain lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (9 entries):

  1. Heartline Roll
  2. LSM Launch (rolling) (from 0 mph - 62.1 mph in 2 s)
  3. Corkscrew
  4. Banana Roll
  5. Corkscrew
  6. Vertical Chain Lift Hill
  7. Dive Loop
  8. Top Hat - Inside
  9. Immelmann

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride TMNT Shellraiser

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for TMNT Shellraiser?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride TMNT Shellraiser, according to Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is TMNT Shellraiser?

TMNT Shellraiser is 141 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does TMNT Shellraiser go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does TMNT Shellraiser go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is TMNT Shellraiser scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did TMNT Shellraiser open?

TMNT Shellraiser opened on 25 Oct 2019.

Who built TMNT Shellraiser?

TMNT Shellraiser was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on TMNT Shellraiser?

RCDB lists 3280.8 ft of track.

Does TMNT Shellraiser launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride TMNT Shellraiser at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is TMNT Shellraiser good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 7 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is TMNT Shellraiser at Nickelodeon Universe NJ the same as Takabisha at Fuji-Q Highland?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Euro-Fighter / 1000 coasters on RCDB; the Nickelodeon Universe NJ ride opened in 2019 and the Fuji-Q Highland ride in 2011; Nickelodeon Universe NJ sets 48 in and Fuji-Q Highland sets 125 cm.
