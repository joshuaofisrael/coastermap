Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc

Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream · East Rutherford, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 7 Dec 2019. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc is like

Opened in 2019, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Surfrider / 20m design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Flash: Speed Force at Movie World, Avatar Airbender at Nickelodeon Universe MOA and Ipanema Skate Ride at VinWonders Phu Quoc. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nickelodeon Universe NJ sets 48 in, while VinWonders Phu Quoc sets 140 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nickelodeon Universe NJ (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc layout, trains and ride system

Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists one element for Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc open?

Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc opened on 7 Dec 2019.

Who built Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc?

Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc at Nickelodeon Universe NJ the same as Avatar Airbender at Nickelodeon Universe MOA?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Surfrider / 20m coasters on RCDB; the Nickelodeon Universe NJ ride opened in 2019 and the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride in 2008; both list a 48 in minimum.
