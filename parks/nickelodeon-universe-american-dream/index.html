Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream height requirements and roller coasters ranked

East Rutherford, New Jersey · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on TMNT Shellraiser to 52 in (132 cm) on The Shredder. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 45% across all 131 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is TMNT Shellraiser, which opened in 2019.

At Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Nickelodeon Slime Streak, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 70 ft tall, 37 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  2. Step up: The Shredder, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (52 in minimum; 85 ft tall, 36 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: TMNT Shellraiser, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 141 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions).
