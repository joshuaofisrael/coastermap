Sandy's Blasting Bronco

Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream · East Rutherford, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 47.8 mph; 85.3 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2020.

Sandy's Blasting Bronco is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 1 Oct 2020. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall, reaches 47.8 mph and runs 1607.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Sandy's Blasting Bronco is like

Sandy's Blasting Bronco pairs a 85.3 ft structure with 1,607.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 1,607.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Nickelodeon Universe NJ, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Sandy's Blasting Bronco layout, trains and ride system

Sandy's Blasting Bronco starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (5 entries):

  1. LSM Launch
  2. Immelmann ×2
  3. Roll Over
  4. Turntable

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sandy's Blasting Bronco?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Sandy's Blasting Bronco. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Nickelodeon Universe at American Dream, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sandy's Blasting Bronco?

Sandy's Blasting Bronco is 85.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sandy's Blasting Bronco go?

Its top speed is 47.8 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sandy's Blasting Bronco go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Sandy's Blasting Bronco scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Sandy's Blasting Bronco open?

Sandy's Blasting Bronco opened on 1 Oct 2020.

Who built Sandy's Blasting Bronco?

Sandy's Blasting Bronco was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Sandy's Blasting Bronco?

RCDB lists 1607.6 ft of track.

Does Sandy's Blasting Bronco launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Sandy's Blasting Bronco at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Sandy's Blasting Bronco good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 47.8 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
