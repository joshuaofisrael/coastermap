Blackpool Pleasure Beach roller coasters

Blackpool, England · 9 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England has 9 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is The Big One at 213 ft, which is also the fastest at 74 mph. The lowest height requirement is 117 cm (about 46.1 in), for Nickelodeon Streak.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Blackpool Pleasure Beach have?

Blackpool Pleasure Beach has 9 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach?

The Big One, at 213 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach?

The Big One, with a top speed of 74 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Blackpool Pleasure Beach?

By height requirement, Nickelodeon Streak has the lowest minimum at 117 cm (about 46.1 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Blackpool Pleasure Beach coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Nickelodeon Streak. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Blackpool Pleasure Beach do not go upside down?

The Big One, Avalanche, Grand National, Nickelodeon Streak, Steeplechase, Blue Flyer have no inversions, per RCDB.
