Blackpool Pleasure Beach height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Blackpool, England · 9 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England has 9 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 117 cm (about 46.1 in) on Nickelodeon Streak to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Grand National. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Blackpool Pleasure Beach are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Blackpool Pleasure Beach a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 44% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 52 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 9 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Grand National, Nickelodeon Streak and Blue Flyer); 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Revolution and ICON).

The most common builder is Arrow Dynamics, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Nickelodeon Streak, which opened in 1933.

Blackpool Pleasure Beach publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Blackpool Pleasure Beach? By height

At Blackpool Pleasure Beach the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Blackpool Pleasure Beach for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Blackpool Pleasure Beach, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Steeplechase, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (127 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Avalanche, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (132 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: The Big One, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 213 ft tall, 74 mph, no inversions, 3.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
