Avalanche

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in), or 112 to 132 cm (about 44.1 to 52 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1988.

Avalanche is a steel roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 22 Jun 1988. The ride runs 1,490 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall, or 112 to 132 cm (about 44.1 to 52 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Blackpool Pleasure Beach app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Avalanche?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Avalanche, or 112 to 132 cm (about 44.1 to 52 in) with a companion. Below 132 cm, riders must be with a responsible person aged 12 or over who meets the height requirement, according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Avalanche go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Avalanche scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Avalanche open?

Avalanche opened on 22 Jun 1988.

Who built Avalanche?

Avalanche was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
