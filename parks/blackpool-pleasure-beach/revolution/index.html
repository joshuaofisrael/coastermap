Revolution

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 127 cm (about 50 in); top speed 45 mph; 56 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1979.

Revolution is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 1979 and was previously known as Irn-Bru Revolution. The ride stands 56 ft tall, drops 47 ft, reaches 45 mph and runs 635 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Revolution is like

Revolution pairs a 56 ft structure with 635 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. A free fall of 47 ft would give about 38 mph; Revolution reaches 45 mph because its launch adds the speed rather than the drop. It goes upside down 1 time across 635 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1979, it is the 5th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Blackpool Pleasure Beach. It has run under other names (Irn-Bru Revolution), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Launched Loop design, and the first of them to open. Others include Diamondback at Frontier City and Sidewinder at Elitch Gardens. Height rules for this model differ by park: Blackpool Pleasure Beach sets 127 cm, while Frontier City sets 48 in and Elitch Gardens sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 127 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Blackpool Pleasure Beach, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Revolution sits near the middle of Blackpool Pleasure Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Avalanche, roughly 60 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Big One at about 70 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 127 cm (about 50 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Revolution

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 127 cm (about 50 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Blackpool Pleasure Beach app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Revolution?

Riders must be at least 127 cm (about 50 in) tall to ride Revolution, according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Revolution?

Revolution is 56 ft tall, with a drop of 47 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Revolution go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Revolution go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Revolution scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Revolution open?

Revolution opened in 1979 (previously known as Irn-Bru Revolution).

Who built Revolution?

Revolution was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Revolution?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 6 seconds over 635 ft of track.

Does Revolution launch?

Yes. RCDB lists an electric winch launch.

How many people ride Revolution at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Revolution good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 45 mph; the minimum height is 127 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Revolution at Blackpool Pleasure Beach the same as Diamondback at Frontier City?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Launched Loop coasters on RCDB; the Blackpool Pleasure Beach ride opened in 1979 and the Frontier City ride in 1994; Blackpool Pleasure Beach sets 127 cm and Frontier City sets 48 in.
