Grand National

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 40 mph; 62 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1935.

Grand National is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England. It opened in 1935. The ride stands 62 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 3,302 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Grand National is like

With 3,302 ft of track for a 62 ft structure, Grand National is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1935, it is the 3rd oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Blackpool Pleasure Beach.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Blackpool Pleasure Beach. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Grand National sits on the north-east side of Blackpool Pleasure Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Nickelodeon Streak, roughly 140 m away (117 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Blue Flyer at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Grand National compares

On our intensity ranking Grand National is number 5 of 9 at Blackpool Pleasure Beach, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Grand National?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Grand National, according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Grand National?

Grand National is 62 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Grand National go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Grand National go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Grand National scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Grand National open?

Grand National opened in 1935.

How long is the ride on Grand National?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 3,302 ft of track.

How many people ride Grand National at once?

Each train carries 18 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Grand National use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar, seat divider, seatbelt.

Is Grand National good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
