ICON

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 52.8 mph; 88.5 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2018.

ICON is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 25 May 2018. The ride stands 88.5 ft tall, drops 82 ft, reaches 52.8 mph and runs 3,750 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Blackpool Pleasure Beach app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for ICON?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride ICON, according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is ICON?

ICON is 88.5 ft tall, with a drop of 82 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does ICON go?

Its top speed is 52.8 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does ICON go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is ICON scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did ICON open?

ICON opened on 25 May 2018.

Who built ICON?

ICON was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
