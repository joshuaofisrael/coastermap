Infusion

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2007.

Infusion is a steel inverted roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England, built by Vekoma. It opened on 2 May 2007. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Infusion is like

Infusion pairs a 109.3 ft structure with 2,260.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2007, it is the 8th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Blackpool Pleasure Beach. It is one of 14 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 11th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Blackpool Pleasure Beach sets 132 cm, while Energylandia sets 140 cm, Heide Park sets 130 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 132 cm minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Blackpool Pleasure Beach, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Infusion sits on the west side of Blackpool Pleasure Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is ICON, roughly 60 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Avalanche at about 90 m. Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Infusion

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Blackpool Pleasure Beach app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Infusion?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Infusion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in), according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Infusion?

Infusion is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Infusion go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Infusion go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Infusion scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Infusion open?

Infusion opened on 2 May 2007.

Who built Infusion?

Infusion was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Infusion at Blackpool Pleasure Beach the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Blackpool Pleasure Beach ride opened in 2007 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; both list a 132 cm minimum.
