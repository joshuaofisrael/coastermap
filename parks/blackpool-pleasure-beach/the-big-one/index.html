The Big One

Blackpool Pleasure Beach · Blackpool, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 74 mph; 213 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1994.

The Big One is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Blackpool Pleasure Beach in Blackpool, England, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened on 28 May 1994 and was previously known as Pepsi Max Big One. The ride stands 213 ft tall, drops 205 ft, reaches 74 mph and runs 5,497 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Big One?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride The Big One, according to Pleasure Beach Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Big One?

The Big One is 213 ft tall, with a drop of 205 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Big One go?

Its top speed is 74 mph (119 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Big One go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Big One scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Big One open?

The Big One opened on 28 May 1994 (previously known as Pepsi Max Big One).

Who built The Big One?

The Big One was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.
