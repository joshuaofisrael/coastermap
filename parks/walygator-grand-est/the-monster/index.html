The Monster

Walygator Grand Est · Maizières-lès-Metz, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2010.

The Monster is a steel inverted roller coaster at Walygator Grand Est in Maizières-lès-Metz, France, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 9 Jul 2010. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding The Monster is like

The Monster spreads 3,937 ft of track across a layout only 131.2 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 6 times across 3,937 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2010, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Walygator Grand Est. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Inverted Coaster / Raptor design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Raptor at Cedar Point. Height rules for this model differ by park: Walygator sets 140 cm, while Cedar Point sets 54 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Walygator. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Monster sits on the north side of Walygator's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Family Coaster, roughly 170 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Comet at about 220 m. The Monster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

RCDB lists 8 track elements for The Monster, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop (100 ft tall)
  3. Zero-G Roll
  4. Cobra Roll
  5. Helix
  6. Corkscrew ×2
  7. Helix

RCDB counts 6 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride The Monster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walygator Grand Est app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Monster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride The Monster, according to Walygator Grand Est, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Monster?

The Monster is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Monster go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Monster go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is The Monster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Monster open?

The Monster opened on 9 Jul 2010.

Who built The Monster?

The Monster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on The Monster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 28 seconds over 3,937 ft of track.

How does The Monster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Monster at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does The Monster use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is The Monster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did The Monster operate before?

RCDB records it at Expoland (as Orochi).

Is The Monster at Walygator the same as Raptor at Cedar Point?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Inverted Coaster / Raptor coasters on RCDB; the Walygator ride opened in 2010 and the Cedar Point ride in 1994; Walygator sets 140 cm and Cedar Point sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 131.2 ft and 137 ft.
