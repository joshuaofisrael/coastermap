Family Coaster

Walygator Grand Est · Maizières-lès-Metz, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 80 to 120 cm (about 31.5 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Family Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walygator Grand Est in Maizières-lès-Metz, France. It opened on 6 Apr 2007. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Coaster is like

Opened in 2007, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Walygator Grand Est.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walygator (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 80 to 120 cm (about 31.5 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Coaster sits near the middle of Walygator's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Monster, roughly 170 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Comet at about 190 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 80 to 120 cm (about 31.5 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Family Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 80 to 120 cm (about 31.5 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 80 to 120 cm must be accompanied.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Family Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Family Coaster, or 80 to 120 cm (about 31.5 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 80 to 120 cm must be accompanied, according to Walygator Grand Est, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Family Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Family Coaster open?

Family Coaster opened on 6 Apr 2007.

Is Family Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Family Coaster at Walygator the same as Family Coaster at Pleasurewood Hills?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Walygator ride opened in 2007 and the Pleasurewood Hills ride in 2026.
