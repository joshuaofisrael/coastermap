Storm

Magic Park Land · Ensuès-la-Redonne, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 36.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Storm is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Park Land in Ensuès-la-Redonne, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France, built by Pinfari. It opened on 25 Apr 2015. The ride stands 36.1 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1066.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Storm is like

At 36.1 ft tall with 1,066.3 ft of track, Storm balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2015, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Magic Park Land.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Magic Park Land, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Storm sits near the middle of Magic Park Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Shark Trip, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Formula 1 at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Storm?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Storm, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Strom, according to Magic Park Land, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Storm?

Storm is 36.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Storm go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Storm go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Storm scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Storm open?

Storm opened on 25 Apr 2015.

Who built Storm?

Storm was manufactured by Pinfari.

How long is the ride on Storm?

RCDB lists a ride time of 55 seconds over 1066.3 ft of track.

How does Storm get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Storm at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 960 riders.

Is Storm good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Storm operate before?

RCDB records it at EuroPark Milano Idroscalo (as Roller Coaster).
