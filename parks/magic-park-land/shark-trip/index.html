Shark Trip

Magic Park Land · Ensuès-la-Redonne, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Shark Trip is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Park Land in Ensuès-la-Redonne, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France, built by Kılıç Lunapark. It opened on 13 Jun 2020. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Shark Trip is like

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Magic Park Land.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Magic Park Land, shared with 2 other coasters. Children from 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Shark Trip sits on the east side of Magic Park Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Storm, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Formula 1 at about 300 m. Shark Trip layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Shark Trip trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 5 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 20 riders.
Riders per train20

For families checking heights: it appears on our 54-inch list as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36, 42 and 48 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Shark Trip

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Le Shark Coaster.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Shark Trip?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Shark Trip, or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Le Shark Coaster, according to Magic Park Land, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Shark Trip go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

When did Shark Trip open?

Shark Trip opened on 13 Jun 2020.

Who built Shark Trip?

Shark Trip was manufactured by Kılıç Lunapark.

How does Shark Trip get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Shark Trip at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.
