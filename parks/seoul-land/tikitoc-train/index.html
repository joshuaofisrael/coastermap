Tikitoc Train

Seoul Land · Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 100 cm (about 39.4 in); top speed 9.9 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Tikitoc Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Seoul Land in Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea, built by Interpark. It first opened on 23 Sep 1996 and was previously known as Kiddy Coaster. The ride reaches 9.9 mph and runs 367.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tikitoc Train is like

Opened in 1996, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Seoul Land. It has run under other names (Kiddy Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 100 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Seoul Land, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tikitoc Train sits on the south-west side of Seoul Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tobot Train, roughly 310 m away (80 cm minimum). Next closest is Crazy Mouse at about 310 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tikitoc Train?

Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall to ride Tikitoc Train. The park lists it as 티키톡열차, according to Seoul Land, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Tikitoc Train go?

Its top speed is 9.9 mph (16 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tikitoc Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tikitoc Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tikitoc Train open?

Tikitoc Train opened on 23 Sep 1996 (previously known as Kiddy Coaster).

Who built Tikitoc Train?

Tikitoc Train was manufactured by Interpark.

How long is the ride on Tikitoc Train?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 26 seconds over 367.4 ft of track.

Is Tikitoc Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 9.9 mph; the minimum height is 100 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
