Tobot Train

Seoul Land · Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 80 cm (about 31.5 in); 0 inversions; opened 1990.

Tobot Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Seoul Land in Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea, built by Zamperla. It first opened on 21 Sep 1990 and was previously known as Rudolph 2 Loop Coaster. The ride runs 390.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tobot Train is like

Opened in 1990, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Seoul Land. It has run under other names (Rudolph 2 Loop Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Powered Coaster / Twin Helix design, and the first of them to open. Others include Big Red Boat at Dreamworld, Dragon at Canobie Lake, Speedi '64 at Hopi Hari and Montaña Dragón at Fantasilandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Seoul Land sets 80 cm, while Dreamworld sets 105 cm, Canobie Lake sets 42 in and Hopi Hari sets 120 cm.

Its 80 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Seoul Land, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tobot Train sits on the south-east side of Seoul Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Crazy Mouse, roughly 130 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Black Hole 2000 at about 160 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tobot Train?

Riders must be at least 80 cm (about 31.5 in) tall to ride Tobot Train. The park lists it as 또봇트레인, according to Seoul Land, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Tobot Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tobot Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tobot Train open?

Tobot Train opened on 21 Sep 1990 (previously known as Rudolph 2 Loop Coaster).

Who built Tobot Train?

Tobot Train was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Tobot Train?

RCDB lists a ride time of 56 seconds over 390.4 ft of track.

Is Tobot Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 80 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Tobot Train at Seoul Land the same as Dragon at Canobie Lake?

Both are Zamperla Powered Coaster / Twin Helix coasters on RCDB; the Seoul Land ride opened in 1990 and the Canobie Lake ride in 1991; Seoul Land sets 80 cm and Canobie Lake sets 42 in.
