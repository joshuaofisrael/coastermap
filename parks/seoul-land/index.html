Seoul Land height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Seoul Land in Gwacheon, Gyeonggi, South Korea has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 80 cm (about 31.5 in) on Tobot Train to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Columbia Double Loop Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Seoul Land are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Seoul Land a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Columbia Double Loop Coaster, which opened in 1988.

Seoul Land publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Seoul Land? By height

At Seoul Land the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Tobot Train. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Tikitoc Train at 100 cm (about 39.4 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Tikitoc Train. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Columbia Double Loop Coaster at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Columbia Double Loop Coaster, Black Hole 2000 and Crazy Mouse. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Columbia Double Loop Coaster, Black Hole 2000, Tobot Train, Tikitoc Train and Crazy Mouse. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Seoul Land for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Seoul Land, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Tobot Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (80 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Crazy Mouse, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 28 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Black Hole 2000, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 113.8 ft tall, 52.8 mph, 2 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
