Camel Creek Adventure Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Tredinnick, Cornwall, England · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Camel Creek Adventure Park in Tredinnick, Cornwall, England has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Clown Coaster to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Magic Dragon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Camel Creek Adventure Park are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Camel Creek Adventure Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 43.3 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Pinfari, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Morgawr, which opened in 2010.

Camel Creek Adventure Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Camel Creek Adventure Park? By height

At Camel Creek Adventure Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Clown Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Magic Dragon (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Morgawr at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Clown Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Morgawr (92 to 110 cm (about 36.2 to 43.3 in)) and Magic Dragon (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Morgawr and Magic Dragon. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Morgawr, Clown Coaster and Magic Dragon. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Camel Creek Adventure Park

These are the rules Camel Creek Adventure Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Morgawr: riders 92 to 110 cm (about 36.2 to 43.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Magic Dragon: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Clown Coaster: The park does not state a companion rule.
