Magic Dragon

Camel Creek Adventure Park · Tredinnick, Cornwall, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Magic Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Camel Creek Adventure Park in Tredinnick, Cornwall, England, built by Pinfari. It opened in 2021. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Magic Dragon is like

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Camel Creek Adventure Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Pinfari's Super Dragon MD31 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Dragen at Friheden.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Camel Creek. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Magic Dragon sits on the east side of Camel Creek's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Clown Coaster, roughly 120 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Morgawr at about 160 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Magic Dragon?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Magic Dragon, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as The Dragon, according to Camel Creek, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Magic Dragon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Magic Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Magic Dragon open?

Magic Dragon opened in 2021.

Who built Magic Dragon?

Magic Dragon was manufactured by Pinfari.

Is Magic Dragon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Magic Dragon operate before?

RCDB records it at Drayton Manor (as Super Dragon); Funland Amusement Park (as Funlandasaurus).

Is Magic Dragon at Camel Creek the same as Dragen at Friheden?

Both are Pinfari Super Dragon MD31 coasters on RCDB; the Camel Creek ride opened in 2021 and the Friheden ride in 2001.
