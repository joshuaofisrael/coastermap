Big Red Boat

Dreamworld · Coomera, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 105 cm (about 41.3 in), or 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Big Red Boat is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dreamworld in Coomera, Australia, built by Zamperla. It opened on 22 Dec 2023. The ride runs 374 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Red Boat is like

Opened in 2023, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Dreamworld. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Powered Coaster / Twin Helix design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Dragon at Canobie Lake, Speedi '64 at Hopi Hari, Montaña Dragón at Fantasilandia and Wizard Race at Gyeongju World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Dreamworld sets 105 cm, while Canobie Lake sets 42 in, Hopi Hari sets 120 cm and Fantasilandia sets 90 cm.

Its 105 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Dreamworld, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Red Boat?

Riders must be at least 105 cm (about 41.3 in) tall to ride Big Red Boat, or 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) with a companion, according to Dreamworld, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Big Red Boat go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Red Boat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Big Red Boat open?

Big Red Boat opened on 22 Dec 2023.

Who built Big Red Boat?

Big Red Boat was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Big Red Boat?

RCDB lists 374 ft (114 m) of track.

Is Big Red Boat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 105 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Big Red Boat at Dreamworld the same as Tobot Train at Seoul Land?

Both are Zamperla Powered Coaster / Twin Helix coasters on RCDB; the Dreamworld ride opened in 2023 and the Seoul Land ride in 1990; Dreamworld sets 105 cm and Seoul Land sets 80 cm.
