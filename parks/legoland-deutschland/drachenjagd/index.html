Drachenjagd

Legoland Deutschland · Günzburg, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 20.5 mph; 18.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Drachenjagd is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Deutschland in Günzburg, Germany, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on Jun 2003. The ride stands 18.1 ft tall, reaches 20.5 mph and runs 492.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Drachenjagd is like

At 18.1 ft tall with 492.2 ft of track, Drachenjagd balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2003, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland Deutschland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Junior Coaster / Drachenjagd design, and the first of them to open. Others include Coastersaurus at Legoland California. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm, while Legoland California sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Legoland Deutschland (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Drachenjagd sits on the north-east side of Legoland Deutschland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Feuerdrache, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters at about 210 m. Drachenjagd layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Drachenjagd

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum age 4 with an adult, 6 without.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Drachenjagd?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Drachenjagd, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Minimum age 4 with an adult, 6 without, according to Legoland Deutschland, official age and height restrictions (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Drachenjagd?

Drachenjagd is 18.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Drachenjagd go?

Its top speed is 20.5 mph (33 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Drachenjagd go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Drachenjagd scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Drachenjagd open?

Drachenjagd opened on Jun 2003.

Who built Drachenjagd?

Drachenjagd was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Drachenjagd?

RCDB lists a ride time of 35 seconds over 492.2 ft of track.

How does Drachenjagd get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Drachenjagd good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 20.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Drachenjagd at Legoland Deutschland the same as Coastersaurus at Legoland California?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Junior Coaster / Drachenjagd coasters on RCDB; the Legoland Deutschland ride opened in 2003 and the Legoland California ride in 2004; Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm and Legoland California sets 48 in.
