Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters

Legoland Deutschland · Günzburg, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 33.6 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2023.

Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters is a steel wing roller coaster at Legoland Deutschland in Günzburg, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 25 Mar 2023. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, reaches 33.6 mph and runs 1499.3 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters is like

Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters pairs a 55.8 ft structure with 1,499.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,499.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland Deutschland.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Legoland Deutschland. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters sits on the east side of Legoland Deutschland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Drachenjagd, roughly 210 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Feuerdrache at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Minimum age 6 with an adult, 8 without.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland Deutschland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Minimum age 6 with an adult, 8 without, according to Legoland Deutschland, official age and height restrictions (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters?

Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters is 55.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters go?

Its top speed is 33.6 mph (54 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters open?

Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters opened on 25 Mar 2023.

Who built Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters?

Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 17 seconds over 1499.3 ft of track.

How does Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Maximus: Der Flug des Wächters good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 33.6 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
