Feuerdrache

Legoland Deutschland · Günzburg, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 35.1 mph; 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Feuerdrache is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Deutschland in Günzburg, Germany, built by Zierer. It opened in 2002. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall, reaches 35.1 mph and runs 1476.4 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Feuerdrache is like

Feuerdrache pairs a 52.5 ft structure with 1,476.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland Deutschland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Force / Five design, and the first of them to open. Others include The Dragon at Legoland New York. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm, while Legoland New York sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Legoland Deutschland (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Feuerdrache sits on the north side of Legoland Deutschland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Drachenjagd, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Das Große LEGO Rennen (The Great LEGO Race) at about 290 m. Feuerdrache layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

How Feuerdrache compares

On our intensity ranking Feuerdrache is number 3 of 4 at Legoland Deutschland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Drachenjagd (18.1 ft tall, 20.5 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Das Große LEGO Rennen (The Great LEGO Race) (51.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Feuerdrache

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum age 6 with an adult, 8 without.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland Deutschland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Feuerdrache?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Feuerdrache, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Minimum age 6 with an adult, 8 without, according to Legoland Deutschland, official age and height restrictions (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Feuerdrache?

Feuerdrache is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Feuerdrache go?

Its top speed is 35.1 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Feuerdrache go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Feuerdrache scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Feuerdrache open?

Feuerdrache opened in 2002.

Who built Feuerdrache?

Feuerdrache was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Feuerdrache?

RCDB lists 1476.4 ft of track.

How does Feuerdrache get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Feuerdrache at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Feuerdrache good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35.1 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Feuerdrache at Legoland Deutschland the same as The Dragon at Legoland New York?

Both are Zierer Force / Five coasters on RCDB; the Legoland Deutschland ride opened in 2002 and the Legoland New York ride in 2021; Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm and Legoland New York sets 48 in.
