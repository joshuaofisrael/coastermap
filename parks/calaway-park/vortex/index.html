Vortex

Calaway Park · Calgary, Alberta

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 2 inversions; opened 1982.

Vortex is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Calaway Park in Calgary, Alberta, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 1982 and was previously known as Corkscrew. The ride drops 70 ft. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Vortex is like

Opened in 1982, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Calaway Park. It has run under other names (Corkscrew), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure, Corkscrew at Silverwood, Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land and Corkscrew at Rusutsu. Height rules for this model differ by park: Calaway Park sets 48 in, while Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm, Rusutsu sets 125 cm and Salitre Mágico sets 135 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Calaway Park, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vortex sits near the middle of Calaway Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Express, roughly 80 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Bumble Blast at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Vortex

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as The Vortex.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vortex?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Vortex. The park lists it as The Vortex, according to Calaway Park, official height guide (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Vortex go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Vortex scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Vortex open?

Vortex opened in 1982 (previously known as Corkscrew).

Who built Vortex?

Vortex was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How does Vortex get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vortex at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Vortex good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Vortex at Calaway Park the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Calaway Park ride opened in 1982 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; both list a 48 in minimum.
