Bumble Blast

Calaway Park · Calgary, Alberta

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Bumble Blast is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Calaway Park in Calgary, Alberta, built by Gosetto. It opened in 2019. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bumble Blast is like

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Calaway Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Calaway Park, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bumble Blast sits near the middle of Calaway Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Express, roughly 60 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Vortex at about 100 m. Bumble Blast layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Bumble Blast

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The height guide lists it as accompanied by an adult from 42 inches and alone from 48 inches.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Calaway Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bumble Blast?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Bumble Blast, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion. The height guide lists it as accompanied by an adult from 42 inches and alone from 48 inches, according to Calaway Park, official height guide (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Bumble Blast go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bumble Blast scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Bumble Blast open?

Bumble Blast opened in 2019.

Who built Bumble Blast?

Bumble Blast was manufactured by Gosetto.

How does Bumble Blast get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Bumble Blast at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Bumble Blast good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
