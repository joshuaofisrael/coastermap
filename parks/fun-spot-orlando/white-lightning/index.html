White Lightning

Fun Spot America Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 44.3 mph; 69.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

White Lightning is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Fun Spot America Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on May 2013. The ride stands 69.7 ft tall, drops 67.2 ft, reaches 44.3 mph and runs 2,032 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding White Lightning is like

At 69.7 ft tall with 2,032 ft of track, White Lightning balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2013, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Fun Spot America Orlando.

A rider who just meets its 46 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Fun Spot Orlando, out of 2 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, White Lightning sits near the middle of Fun Spot Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Freedom Flyer, roughly 30 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sea Serpent at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for White Lightning?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride White Lightning, according to Fun Spot America Orlando, official roller coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is White Lightning?

White Lightning is 69.7 ft tall, with a drop of 67.2 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does White Lightning go?

Its top speed is 44.3 mph (71 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does White Lightning go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is White Lightning scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did White Lightning open?

White Lightning opened on May 2013.

Who built White Lightning?

White Lightning was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on White Lightning?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 2,032 ft of track.

How does White Lightning get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride White Lightning at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is White Lightning good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 44.3 mph; the minimum height is 46 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
