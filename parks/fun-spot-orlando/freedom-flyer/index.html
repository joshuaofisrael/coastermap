Freedom Flyer

Fun Spot America Orlando · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm), or 38 to 52 in (97 to 132 cm) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 64.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Freedom Flyer is a steel suspended roller coaster at Fun Spot America Orlando in Orlando, Florida, built by Vekoma. It opened on May 2013. The ride stands 64.3 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1295.9 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Freedom Flyer is like

At 64.3 ft tall with 1,295.9 ft of track, Freedom Flyer balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2013, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Fun Spot America Orlando. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 395m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Kvasten at Gröna Lund. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fun Spot Orlando sets 52 in, while Gröna Lund sets 120 cm.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Fun Spot Orlando. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 52 in (97 to 132 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Freedom Flyer sits near the middle of Fun Spot Orlando's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is White Lightning, roughly 30 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sea Serpent at about 150 m. Freedom Flyer layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, or 38 to 52 in (97 to 132 cm) with a companion.

How Freedom Flyer compares

On our intensity ranking Freedom Flyer is number 2 of 3 at Fun Spot America Orlando, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Sea Serpent (no inversions; 36 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is White Lightning (69.7 ft tall, 44.3 mph, no inversions; 46 in minimum).

Before you ride Freedom Flyer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm), and riders from 38 to 52 in (97 to 132 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 38 to 52 in must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Freedom Flyer?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Freedom Flyer, or 38 to 52 in (97 to 132 cm) with a companion. Riders 38 to 52 in must ride with an adult, according to Fun Spot America Orlando, official roller coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Freedom Flyer?

Freedom Flyer is 64.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Freedom Flyer go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Freedom Flyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Freedom Flyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Freedom Flyer open?

Freedom Flyer opened on May 2013.

Who built Freedom Flyer?

Freedom Flyer was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Freedom Flyer?

RCDB lists 1295.9 ft of track.

How does Freedom Flyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Freedom Flyer at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 758 riders.

Is Freedom Flyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Freedom Flyer at Fun Spot Orlando the same as Kvasten at Gröna Lund?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 395m coasters on RCDB; the Fun Spot Orlando ride opened in 2013 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2007; Fun Spot Orlando sets 52 in and Gröna Lund sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 64.3 ft and 65.6 ft.
