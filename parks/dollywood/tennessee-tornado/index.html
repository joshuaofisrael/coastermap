Tennessee Tornado

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 63 mph; 163 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1999.

Tennessee Tornado is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 17 Apr 1999. The ride stands 163 ft tall, drops 128 ft, reaches 63 mph and runs 2,682 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Tennessee Tornado is like

Tennessee Tornado pairs a 163 ft structure with 2,682 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,682 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.7 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 2nd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Dollywood.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Dollywood, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tennessee Tornado sits on the south-east side of Dollywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Blazing Fury, roughly 140 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is FireChaser Express at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Tennessee Tornado compares

On our intensity ranking Tennessee Tornado is number 3 of 11 at Dollywood, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Tennessee Tornado

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Dollywood app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tennessee Tornado?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Tennessee Tornado, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tennessee Tornado?

Tennessee Tornado is 163 ft tall, with a drop of 128 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tennessee Tornado go?

Its top speed is 63 mph (101 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tennessee Tornado go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Tennessee Tornado scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tennessee Tornado open?

Tennessee Tornado opened on 17 Apr 1999.

Who built Tennessee Tornado?

Tennessee Tornado was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Tennessee Tornado?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 2,682 ft of track.

How does Tennessee Tornado get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tennessee Tornado at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,360 riders.

Is Tennessee Tornado good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 63 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
