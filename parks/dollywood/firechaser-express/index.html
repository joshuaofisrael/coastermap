FireChaser Express

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 34.5 mph; 78 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

FireChaser Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 22 Mar 2014. The ride stands 78 ft tall, reaches 34.5 mph and runs 2,427 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion.

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for FireChaser Express?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride FireChaser Express, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is FireChaser Express?

FireChaser Express is 78 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does FireChaser Express go?

Its top speed is 34.5 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does FireChaser Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is FireChaser Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did FireChaser Express open?

FireChaser Express opened on 22 Mar 2014.

Who built FireChaser Express?

FireChaser Express was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.
