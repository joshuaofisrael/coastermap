Dragonflier

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Dragonflier is a steel suspended roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Vekoma. It opened on 10 May 2019. The ride runs 1486.2 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in with a companion, and no taller than 81 in.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragonflier?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Dragonflier, or 39 to 48 inches with a companion. The maximum rider height is 81 inches, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Dragonflier go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dragonflier scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Dragonflier open?

Dragonflier opened on 10 May 2019.

Who built Dragonflier?

Dragonflier was manufactured by Vekoma.
