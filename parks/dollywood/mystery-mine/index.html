Mystery Mine

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 43.5 mph; 85.3 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2007.

Mystery Mine is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 13 Apr 2007. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1804.5 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 95°, past vertical.

What riding Mystery Mine is like

Mystery Mine pairs a 85.3 ft structure with 1,804.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 95°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,804.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2007, it is the 4th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Dollywood.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Dollywood, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mystery Mine sits near the middle of Dollywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Eagle, roughly 100 m away (50 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Whistle Punk Chaser at about 160 m. Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Before you ride Mystery Mine

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Dollywood app or official site on the day.
