Blazing Fury

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 22 mph; 22 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1978.

Blazing Fury is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee. It opened on 6 Jun 1978. The ride stands 22 ft tall, reaches 22 mph and runs 1,520 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Blazing Fury?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Blazing Fury, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Blazing Fury?

Blazing Fury is 22 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Blazing Fury go?

Its top speed is 22 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Blazing Fury go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Blazing Fury scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Blazing Fury open?

Blazing Fury opened on 6 Jun 1978.
