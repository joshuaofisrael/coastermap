Dollywood height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Pigeon Forge, Tennessee · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 39 in (99 cm) on NightFlight Expedition to 50 in (127 cm) on Wild Eagle. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 10 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 11 of them.

What the coasters at Dollywood are like

RCDB classes 5 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Dollywood a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 45% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 8 of the 11 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 11 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Thunderhead); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Big Bear Mountain).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Blazing Fury, which opened in 1978.

What can my child ride at Dollywood? By height

At Dollywood the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 7 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Whistle Punk Chaser (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being NightFlight Expedition at 39 in (99 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 11 coasters with a verified rule: Blazing Fury, Whistle Punk Chaser and NightFlight Expedition. With a companion they can also ride FireChaser Express (39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm)), Dragonflier (39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm)) and Big Bear Mountain (39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Tennessee Tornado at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 10 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 7 more than at the height below: Tennessee Tornado, Thunderhead, Mystery Mine, FireChaser Express, Lightning Rod, Dragonflier and Big Bear Mountain. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Wild Eagle at 50 in (127 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 11 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Wild Eagle. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

A ride here also has an official maximum height: Wild Eagle (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Family and kiddie coasters with an upper limit: Dragonflier (up to 81 in (206 cm)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Dollywood

These are the rules Dollywood states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • FireChaser Express: riders 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Whistle Punk Chaser: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Dragonflier: riders 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Big Bear Mountain: riders 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Dollywood for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Dollywood, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dragonflier, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: NightFlight Expedition, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (39 in minimum; 29 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Wild Eagle, the most intense coaster in the park (50 in minimum; 61 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Dollywood?

Roller coaster height requirements at Dollywood run from 39 in (99 cm) (NightFlight Expedition) to 50 in (127 cm) (Wild Eagle), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Dollywood have?

Dollywood has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Dollywood?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Wild Eagle (61 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Dollywood?

Tennessee Tornado, at 163 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Dollywood?

Lightning Rod, with a top speed of 73 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Dollywood for first-timers?

Dragonflier is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Dollywood?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Blazing Fury, Whistle Punk Chaser, NightFlight Expedition. With a companion they can also ride FireChaser Express, Dragonflier, Big Bear Mountain.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Dollywood?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Blazing Fury, Tennessee Tornado, Thunderhead, Mystery Mine, FireChaser Express, Lightning Rod, Whistle Punk Chaser, Dragonflier, Big Bear Mountain, NightFlight Expedition.

Do any coasters at Dollywood have a maximum height?

Yes. Wild Eagle has a maximum of 78 in (198 cm); Dragonflier has a maximum of 81 in (206 cm), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026.

Which coasters at Dollywood do not go upside down?

Blazing Fury, Thunderhead, FireChaser Express, Lightning Rod, Whistle Punk Chaser, Dragonflier, Big Bear Mountain, NightFlight Expedition have no inversions, per RCDB.
