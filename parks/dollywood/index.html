Dollywood roller coasters

Pigeon Forge, Tennessee · 11 coasters

Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee has 11 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Tennessee Tornado at 163 ft and the fastest is Lightning Rod at 73 mph. The lowest height requirement is 39 in (99 cm), for NightFlight Expedition.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Dollywood have?

Dollywood has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Dollywood?

Tennessee Tornado, at 163 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Dollywood?

Lightning Rod, with a top speed of 73 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Dollywood?

By height requirement, NightFlight Expedition has the lowest minimum at 39 in (99 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Dollywood coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Blazing Fury, Whistle Punk Chaser, NightFlight Expedition. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Dollywood coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Blazing Fury, Tennessee Tornado, Thunderhead, Mystery Mine, FireChaser Express, Lightning Rod, Whistle Punk Chaser, Dragonflier, Big Bear Mountain, NightFlight Expedition. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Dollywood do not go upside down?

Blazing Fury, Thunderhead, FireChaser Express, Lightning Rod, Whistle Punk Chaser, Dragonflier, Big Bear Mountain, NightFlight Expedition have no inversions, per RCDB.
