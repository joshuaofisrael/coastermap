Big Bear Mountain

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 48 mph; 66 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Big Bear Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Vekoma. It opened on 12 May 2023. The ride stands 66 ft tall, reaches 48 mph and runs 3,990 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Bear Mountain?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Big Bear Mountain, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Big Bear Mountain?

Big Bear Mountain is 66 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Big Bear Mountain go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Bear Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Bear Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Bear Mountain open?

Big Bear Mountain opened on 12 May 2023.

Who built Big Bear Mountain?

Big Bear Mountain was manufactured by Vekoma.
