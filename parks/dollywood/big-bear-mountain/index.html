Big Bear Mountain

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 48 mph; 66 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Big Bear Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Vekoma. It opened on 12 May 2023. The ride stands 66 ft tall, reaches 48 mph and runs 3,990 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Bear Mountain is like

Big Bear Mountain spreads 3,990 ft of track across a layout only 66 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2023, it is the 10th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Dollywood.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Dollywood, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Bear Mountain sits on the north side of Dollywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragonflier, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Whistle Punk Chaser at about 220 m. Big Bear Mountain layout, trains and ride system

Big Bear Mountain starts with a linear induction motor (LIM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (LIM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 3 elements RCDB records on Big Bear Mountain's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LIM Launch
  2. LIM Boost ×2

Big Bear Mountain trains and ride system

Trains10 cars per train. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 20 riders per train.
Riders per train20
RestraintsIndividual hydraulic lap bar

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Big Bear Mountain

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Bear Mountain?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Big Bear Mountain, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Big Bear Mountain?

Big Bear Mountain is 66 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Big Bear Mountain go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Bear Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Bear Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Bear Mountain open?

Big Bear Mountain opened on 12 May 2023.

Who built Big Bear Mountain?

Big Bear Mountain was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Big Bear Mountain?

RCDB lists 3,990 ft (1,216 m) of track.

Does Big Bear Mountain launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

How many people ride Big Bear Mountain at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Big Bear Mountain use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Big Bear Mountain good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 48 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
