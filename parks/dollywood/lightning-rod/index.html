Lightning Rod

Dollywood · Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 73 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Lightning Rod is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 13 Jun 2016. The ride drops 165 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 3,800 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Lightning Rod is like

RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2016, it is the 7th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Dollywood.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Dollywood, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Lightning Rod sits on the west side of Dollywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Thunderhead, roughly 500 m away (48 in minimum). Next closest is Whistle Punk Chaser at about 570 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Lightning Rod?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Lightning Rod, according to Dollywood, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Lightning Rod go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Lightning Rod go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Lightning Rod scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Lightning Rod open?

Lightning Rod opened on 13 Jun 2016.

Who built Lightning Rod?

Lightning Rod was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Lightning Rod?

RCDB lists 3,800 ft (1,158 m) of track.

How does Lightning Rod get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Lightning Rod at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Lightning Rod good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 73 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
