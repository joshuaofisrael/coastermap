Rex's RC Racer

Shanghai Disneyland · Shanghai, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 47 mph; 91.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Rex's RC Racer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Shanghai Disneyland in Shanghai, China, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 26 Apr 2018. The ride stands 91.2 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 269 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rex's RC Racer is like

For a 91.2 ft ride, 269 ft of track is short: Rex's RC Racer is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Shanghai Disneyland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Surfrider / RC Racer design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include RC Racer at Hong Kong Disneyland and RC Racer at Disney Adventure World. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Shanghai Disneyland, out of 2 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rex's RC Racer sits on the west side of Shanghai Disneyland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is TRON Lightcycle Power Run, roughly 160 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Seven Dwarfs Mine Train at about 380 m. Rex's RC Racer layout, trains and ride system

Rex's RC Racer starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists one element for Rex's RC Racer:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Rex's RC Racer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Shanghai Disneyland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rex's RC Racer?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Rex's RC Racer, according to Shanghai Disney Resort, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rex's RC Racer?

Rex's RC Racer is 91.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rex's RC Racer go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rex's RC Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rex's RC Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rex's RC Racer open?

Rex's RC Racer opened on 26 Apr 2018.

Who built Rex's RC Racer?

Rex's RC Racer was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Rex's RC Racer?

RCDB lists 269 ft (82 m) of track.

Does Rex's RC Racer launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Rex's RC Racer at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Rex's RC Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rex's RC Racer at Shanghai Disneyland the same as RC Racer at Disney Adventure World?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Surfrider / RC Racer coasters on RCDB; the Shanghai Disneyland ride opened in 2018 and the Disney Adventure World ride in 2010; both list a 120 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 91.2 ft and 82 ft.
