Shanghai Disneyland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Shanghai, China · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Shanghai Disneyland in Shanghai, China has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 97 cm (about 38.2 in) on Seven Dwarfs Mine Train to 122 cm (about 48 in) on TRON Lightcycle Power Run. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Shanghai Disneyland are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Shanghai Disneyland a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 42% of coasters across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is TRON Lightcycle Power Run, which opened in 2016.

Shanghai Disneyland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Shanghai Disneyland? By height

At Shanghai Disneyland the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Shanghai Disneyland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Shanghai Disneyland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (97 cm minimum; 44.3 ft tall, 28.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: TRON Lightcycle Power Run, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (122 cm minimum; 78.1 ft tall, 59.3 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
