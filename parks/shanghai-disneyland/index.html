Shanghai Disneyland roller coasters

Shanghai, China · 3 coasters

Shanghai Disneyland in Shanghai, China has 3 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Rex's RC Racer at 91.2 ft and the fastest is TRON Lightcycle Power Run at 59.3 mph. The lowest height requirement is 97 cm (about 38.2 in), for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train.
