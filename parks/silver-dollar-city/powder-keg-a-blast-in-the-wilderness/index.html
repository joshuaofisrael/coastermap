Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness

Silver Dollar City · Branson, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 64 mph; 98 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri, built by Premier Rides. It first opened in 1999 and was previously known as BuzzSaw Falls. The ride stands 98 ft tall, drops 110 ft, reaches 64 mph and runs 3,506 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness is like

With 3,506 ft of track for a 98 ft structure, Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 110 ft drop is longer than its 98 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.9 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silver Dollar City. It has run under other names (BuzzSaw Falls), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Silver Dollar City, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness sits on the west side of Silver Dollar City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wildfire, roughly 160 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Outlaw Run at about 200 m. Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness layout, trains and ride system

Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness starts with a compressed-air launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. It also uses a chain lift hill.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Silver Dollar City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness?

Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness is 98 ft tall, with a drop of 110 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness go?

Its top speed is 64 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness open?

Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness opened in 1999 (previously known as BuzzSaw Falls).

Who built Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness?

Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 53 seconds over 3,506 ft of track.

Does Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a compressed-air launch.

How many people ride Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 64 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
