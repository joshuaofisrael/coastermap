Outlaw Run

Silver Dollar City · Branson, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 68 mph; 107 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2013.

Outlaw Run is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 15 Mar 2013. The ride stands 107 ft tall, drops 162 ft, reaches 68 mph and runs 2,937 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Outlaw Run is like

At 107 ft tall with 2,937 ft of track, Outlaw Run balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 162 ft drop is longer than its 107 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. At 81°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,937 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2013, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silver Dollar City.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Silver Dollar City, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Outlaw Run sits on the north-west side of Silver Dollar City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, roughly 200 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wildfire at about 330 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Outlaw Run

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Silver Dollar City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Outlaw Run?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Outlaw Run, according to Silver Dollar City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Outlaw Run?

Outlaw Run is 107 ft tall, with a drop of 162 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Outlaw Run go?

Its top speed is 68 mph (109 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Outlaw Run go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Outlaw Run scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Outlaw Run open?

Outlaw Run opened on 15 Mar 2013.

Who built Outlaw Run?

Outlaw Run was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the ride on Outlaw Run?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 27 seconds over 2,937 ft of track.

How does Outlaw Run get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Outlaw Run at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Outlaw Run good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 68 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
