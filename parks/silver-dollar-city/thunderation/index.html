Thunderation

Silver Dollar City · Branson, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 48 mph; 81 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Thunderation is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 10 Apr 1993. The ride stands 81 ft tall, reaches 48 mph and runs 3,022 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Thunderation is like

Thunderation spreads 3,022 ft of track across a layout only 81 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1993, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silver Dollar City. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 10th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Adventure Express at Kings Island and Trailblazer at Hersheypark.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thunderation sits on the south-east side of Silver Dollar City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Time Traveler, roughly 100 m away (51 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grand Exposition Coaster at about 250 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Thunderation?

Thunderation is 81 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Thunderation go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thunderation go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Thunderation scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Thunderation open?

Thunderation opened on 10 Apr 1993.

Who built Thunderation?

Thunderation was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

Is Thunderation at Silver Dollar City the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Silver Dollar City ride opened in 1993 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; RCDB lists heights of 81 ft and 35 ft.
