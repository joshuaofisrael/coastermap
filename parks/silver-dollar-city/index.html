Silver Dollar City height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Branson, Missouri · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Grand Exposition Coaster to 52 in (132 cm) on Wildfire. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Silver Dollar City are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Silver Dollar City a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 57% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Outlaw Run).

The most common builder is Rocky Mountain Coasters, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Thunderation, which opened in 1993.

What can my child ride at Silver Dollar City? By height

At Silver Dollar City the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Grand Exposition Coaster (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)) and Fire In The Hole (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Grand Exposition Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Fire In The Hole (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Outlaw Run at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, Outlaw Run and Fire In The Hole. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Time Traveler at 51 in (130 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Wildfire and Time Traveler. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Silver Dollar City

These are the rules Silver Dollar City states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Grand Exposition Coaster: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Fire In The Hole: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Silver Dollar City for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Silver Dollar City, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Grand Exposition Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Fire In The Hole, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 26 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Wildfire, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 120 ft tall, 66 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Silver Dollar City?

Roller coaster height requirements at Silver Dollar City run from 42 in (107 cm) (Grand Exposition Coaster) to 52 in (132 cm) (Wildfire), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Silver Dollar City have?

Silver Dollar City has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Silver Dollar City?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Wildfire (120 ft tall, 66 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Silver Dollar City?

Wildfire, at 120 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Silver Dollar City?

What is the best coaster at Silver Dollar City for first-timers?

What can a 42-inch child ride at Silver Dollar City?
