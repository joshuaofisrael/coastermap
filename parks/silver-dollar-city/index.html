Silver Dollar City roller coasters

Branson, Missouri · 7 coasters

Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri has 7 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Wildfire at 120 ft and the fastest is Outlaw Run at 68 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Grand Exposition Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Silver Dollar City have?

Silver Dollar City has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Silver Dollar City?

Wildfire, at 120 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Silver Dollar City?

Outlaw Run, with a top speed of 68 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Silver Dollar City?

By height requirement, Grand Exposition Coaster has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Silver Dollar City coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Grand Exposition Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Silver Dollar City coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, Grand Exposition Coaster, Outlaw Run, Fire In The Hole. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Silver Dollar City do not go upside down?

Thunderation, Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, Grand Exposition Coaster, Fire In The Hole have no inversions, per RCDB.
