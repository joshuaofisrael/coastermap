Wildfire

Silver Dollar City · Branson, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 66 mph; 120 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2001.

Wildfire is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 4 Apr 2001. The ride stands 120 ft tall, drops 155 ft, reaches 66 mph and runs 3,073 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Wildfire is like

Wildfire pairs a 120 ft structure with 3,073 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. Its 155 ft drop is longer than its 120 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. It goes upside down 5 times across 3,073 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2001, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silver Dollar City. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Sitting Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include The Incredible Hulk Coaster at Islands of Adventure and Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Silver Dollar City sets 52 in, while Islands of Adventure sets 54 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Silver Dollar City. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wildfire sits on the south-west side of Silver Dollar City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Powder Keg: A Blast in the Wilderness, roughly 160 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Outlaw Run at about 330 m. Wildfire layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Wildfire's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Immelmann
  3. Loop
  4. Cobra Roll
  5. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Wildfire

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Silver Dollar City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wildfire?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Wildfire, according to Silver Dollar City, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wildfire?

Wildfire is 120 ft tall, with a drop of 155 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wildfire go?

Its top speed is 66 mph (106 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wildfire go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Wildfire scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wildfire open?

Wildfire opened on 4 Apr 2001.

Who built Wildfire?

Wildfire was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Wildfire?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 16 seconds over 3,073 ft of track.

How does Wildfire get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wildfire at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,300 riders.

Is Wildfire good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 66 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wildfire at Silver Dollar City the same as Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Sitting Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Silver Dollar City ride opened in 2001 and the PortAventura Park ride in 1995; Silver Dollar City sets 52 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 120 ft and 148 ft.
