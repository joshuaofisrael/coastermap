Lagoon height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Farmington, Utah · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Lagoon in Farmington, Utah has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on The Bat to 50 in (127 cm) on Spider. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 9 of them.

What the coasters at Lagoon are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 4 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Lagoon a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 30% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 50 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 8 of the 9 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 10 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Roller Coaster); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Wicked).

The most common builder is ART Engineering GmbH, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Roller Coaster, which opened in 1921.

What can my child ride at Lagoon? By height

At Lagoon the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Primordial (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Bombora (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being The Bat at 42 in (107 cm).

Best coasters at Lagoon for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Lagoon, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Bat, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 48.6 ft tall, 26 mph, no inversions, 2.2 g).
  2. Step up: Spider, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (50 in minimum; 50.8 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Cannibal, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 197 ft tall, 72 mph, 3 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Lagoon?

Roller coaster height requirements at Lagoon run from 42 in (107 cm) (The Bat) to 50 in (127 cm) (Spider), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Lagoon have?

Lagoon has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Lagoon?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Cannibal (197 ft tall, 72 mph, 3 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Lagoon?

Cannibal, at 197 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Lagoon?

Cannibal, with a top speed of 72 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Lagoon for first-timers?

The Bat is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 42 in (107 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Lagoon?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: The Bat. With a companion they can also ride Primordial, Bombora.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Lagoon?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Cannibal, Primordial, The Bat, Bombora. With a companion they can also ride Spider, Roller Coaster, Colossus the Fire Dragon, Wild Mouse, Wicked.

Which coasters at Lagoon do not go upside down?

Spider, Primordial, Roller Coaster, Puff the Little Fire Dragon, Wild Mouse, The Bat, Bombora have no inversions, per RCDB.
