Wicked

Lagoon · Farmington, Utah

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion; top speed 55 mph; 110 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2007.

Wicked is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagoon in Farmington, Utah, built by Zierer. It opened on 1 Jun 2007. The ride stands 110 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 2050.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wicked?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Wicked, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion. Riders 46 to 50 in must ride in a designated seat, according to Lagoon, official Rides & Attractions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wicked?

Wicked is 110 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wicked go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wicked go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Wicked scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wicked open?

Wicked opened on 1 Jun 2007.

Who built Wicked?

Wicked was manufactured by Zierer.
