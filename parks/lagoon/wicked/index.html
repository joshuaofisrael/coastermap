Wicked

Lagoon · Farmington, Utah

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion; top speed 55 mph; 110 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2007.

Wicked is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagoon in Farmington, Utah, built by Zierer. It opened on 1 Jun 2007. The ride stands 110 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 2050.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Wicked is like

At 110 ft tall with 2,050.5 ft of track, Wicked balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,050.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.8 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 7th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Lagoon.

Its 50 in minimum is the strictest at Lagoon, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wicked sits on the south-west side of Lagoon's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Colossus the Fire Dragon, roughly 50 m away (50 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wild Mouse at about 110 m. Wicked layout, trains and ride system

Wicked starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Wicked compares

On our intensity ranking Wicked is number 2 of 10 at Lagoon, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Colossus the Fire Dragon (86.9 ft tall, 49.1 mph, 2 inversions; 50 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Cannibal (197 ft tall, 72 mph, 3 inversions; 48 in minimum).

Site-wide, Wicked is taller than 70% and faster than 68% of the 917 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Wicked

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 50 in (127 cm), and riders from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 46 to 50 in must ride in a designated seat.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lagoon app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wicked?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Wicked, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion. Riders 46 to 50 in must ride in a designated seat, according to Lagoon, official Rides & Attractions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wicked?

Wicked is 110 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wicked go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wicked go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Wicked scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wicked open?

Wicked opened on 1 Jun 2007.

Who built Wicked?

Wicked was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Wicked?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 46 seconds over 2050.5 ft of track.

Does Wicked launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Wicked at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Wicked good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
