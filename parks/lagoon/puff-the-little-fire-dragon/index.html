Puff the Little Fire Dragon

Lagoon · Farmington, Utah

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 16.2 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1985.

Puff the Little Fire Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagoon in Farmington, Utah, built by Zierer. It opened on May 1985. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 16.2 mph and runs 197.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Puff the Little Fire Dragon is like

At 10.8 ft tall with 197.5 ft of track, Puff the Little Fire Dragon balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1985, it is the 3rd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Lagoon. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Small design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Hoot N Holler at Six Flags Darien Lake.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Puff the Little Fire Dragon sits on the south-east side of Lagoon's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Bombora, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Bat at about 160 m.
