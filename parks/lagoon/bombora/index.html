Bombora

Lagoon · Farmington, Utah

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 31 mph; 45 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Bombora is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagoon in Farmington, Utah, built by ART Engineering GmbH. It opened on 2 Apr 2011. The ride stands 45 ft tall, reaches 31 mph and runs 1,040 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bombora is like

At 45 ft tall with 1,040 ft of track, Bombora balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2011, it is the 8th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Lagoon.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Lagoon, out of 8 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bombora sits on the east side of Lagoon's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Bat, roughly 90 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Puff the Little Fire Dragon at about 90 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bombora?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Bombora, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Lagoon, official Rides & Attractions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Bombora?

Bombora is 45 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Bombora go?

Its top speed is 31 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Bombora go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bombora scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Bombora open?

Bombora opened on 2 Apr 2011.

Who built Bombora?

Bombora was manufactured by ART Engineering GmbH.
