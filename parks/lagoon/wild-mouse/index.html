Wild Mouse

Lagoon · Farmington, Utah

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 49.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Wild Mouse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lagoon in Farmington, Utah, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened in 1998. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wild Mouse is like

At 49.3 ft tall with 1,213.9 ft of track, Wild Mouse balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 4th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Lagoon. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Wild Maus Classic / Left design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Rattlesnake at Chessington, Crazy Mine at Hansa-Park and Kopermijn at Drievliet. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lagoon sets 50 in, while Chessington sets 140 cm and Drievliet sets 130 cm.

Its 50 in minimum is the strictest at Lagoon, shared with 4 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wild Mouse sits on the south side of Lagoon's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Colossus the Fire Dragon, roughly 60 m away (50 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spider at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Wild Mouse compares

On our intensity ranking Wild Mouse is number 7 of 10 at Lagoon, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Bombora (45 ft tall, 31 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Spider (50.8 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions; 50 in minimum).

Site-wide, Wild Mouse is taller than 28% and faster than 15% of the 1335 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Wild Mouse

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 50 in (127 cm), and riders from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wild Mouse?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Wild Mouse, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion, according to Lagoon, official Rides & Attractions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wild Mouse?

Wild Mouse is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wild Mouse go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wild Mouse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wild Mouse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wild Mouse open?

Wild Mouse opened in 1998.

Who built Wild Mouse?

Wild Mouse was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Wild Mouse?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 10 seconds over 1213.9 ft of track.

How does Wild Mouse get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wild Mouse at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Wild Mouse good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wild Mouse at Lagoon the same as Kopermijn at Drievliet?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Wild Maus Classic / Left coasters on RCDB; the Lagoon ride opened in 1998 and the Drievliet ride in 1996; Lagoon sets 50 in and Drievliet sets 130 cm.
