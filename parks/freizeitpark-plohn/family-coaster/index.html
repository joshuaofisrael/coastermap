Family Coaster

Freizeitpark Plohn · Lengenfeld, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2006.

Family Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Freizeitpark Plohn in Lengenfeld, Germany, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2006. The ride runs 452.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Coaster is like

Opened in 2006, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Freizeitpark Plohn. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Wriggler at Gulliver's Land, Lokolo at Lost Island and Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Coaster sits on the south side of Plohn's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Miniwah, roughly 70 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dynamite at about 110 m. Frequently asked questions

Does Family Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Family Coaster open?

Family Coaster opened in 2006.

Who built Family Coaster?

Family Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the ride on Family Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 55 seconds over 452.8 ft of track.

How does Family Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Family Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is Family Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Family Coaster at Plohn the same as Grosse Pomme at Winnoland?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Plohn ride opened in 2006 and the Winnoland ride in 2010.
