Disney California Adventure Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Anaheim, California · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Disney California Adventure Park in Anaheim, California has 2 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Goofy's Sky School to 48 in (122 cm) on Incredicoaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Disney California Adventure Park are like

Of the ride types, 1 of the 2 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Incredicoaster, which opened in 2001.

What can my child ride at Disney California Adventure Park? By height

At Disney California Adventure Park the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Goofy's Sky School at 42 in (107 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Incredicoaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Incredicoaster and Goofy's Sky School. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Disney California Adventure Park roller coasters ranked, most intense first

We rank the coasters by intensity using only measurable facts: RCDB's kiddie, family, thrill or extreme class first, then height, top speed, inversions, launches and beyond-vertical drops. It is a guide to how hard each ride hits, not a review of how good it is.

Best coasters at Disney California Adventure Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Disney California Adventure Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Goofy's Sky School, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 27 mph, no inversions).
  2. The big one: Incredicoaster, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 55 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
