Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Disney California Adventure Park have?

Disney California Adventure Park has 2 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the fastest roller coaster at Disney California Adventure Park?

Incredicoaster, with a top speed of 55 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Disney California Adventure Park?

By height requirement, Goofy's Sky School has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Disney California Adventure Park coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Goofy's Sky School. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Disney California Adventure Park coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Incredicoaster, Goofy's Sky School. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Disney California Adventure Park do not go upside down?

Goofy's Sky School have no inversions, per RCDB.
