Goofy's Sky School

Disney California Adventure Park · Anaheim, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 27 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Goofy's Sky School is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disney California Adventure Park in Anaheim, California, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 8 Feb 2001 and was previously known as Mulholland Madness. The ride reaches 27 mph. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Goofy's Sky School is like

It has run under other names (Mulholland Madness), so older guides and photos may use those.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Incredicoaster, roughly 210 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Goofy's Sky School?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Goofy's Sky School, according to Disneyland Resort, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Goofy's Sky School go?

Its top speed is 27 mph (43 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Goofy's Sky School go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Goofy's Sky School scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Goofy's Sky School open?

Goofy's Sky School opened on 8 Feb 2001 (previously known as Mulholland Madness).

Who built Goofy's Sky School?

Goofy's Sky School was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How does Goofy's Sky School get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Goofy's Sky School at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Goofy's Sky School good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 27 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
