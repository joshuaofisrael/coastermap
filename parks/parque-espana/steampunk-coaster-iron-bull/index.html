Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull

Shima Spain Village Parque España · Shima, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 32.3 mph; 39.3 ft tall; 0 inversions.

Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Shima Spain Village Parque España in Shima, Mie, Japan, built by Sansei Technologies. The ride stands 39.3 ft tall, reaches 32.3 mph and runs 1604.3 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull is like

With 1,604.3 ft of track for a 39.3 ft structure, Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

It has run under other names (Super Express Iberia, then Bullfight Roller Coaster Matador), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 110 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Parque España, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull sits on the north side of Parque España's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kiddy Montserrat, roughly 220 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gran Montserrat at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull. Not for guests aged 65 or older, according to Shima Spain Village Parque España, official attraction rules page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull?

Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull is 39.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull go?

Its top speed is 32.3 mph (52 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

Who built Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull?

Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull was manufactured by Sansei Technologies.

How long is the track on Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull?

RCDB lists 1604.3 ft of track.

How does Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Steampunk Coaster Iron Bull good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 32.3 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
