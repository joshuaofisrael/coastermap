Shima Spain Village Parque España height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Shima, Mie, Japan · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Shima Spain Village Parque España in Shima, Mie, Japan has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Kiddy Montserrat to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Pyrenees. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Shima Spain Village Parque España are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Shima Spain Village Parque España a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 43.3 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Gran Montserrat, which opened in 1994.

Shima Spain Village Parque España publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Shima Spain Village Parque España? By height

At Shima Spain Village Parque España the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Shima Spain Village Parque España for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Shima Spain Village Parque España, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Kiddy Montserrat, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.8 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Gran Montserrat, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (110 cm minimum; 72.2 ft tall, 41 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Pyrenees, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 147.7 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 6 inversions).
