Pyrenees

Shima Spain Village Parque España · Shima, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 147.7 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 1997.

Pyrenees is a steel inverted roller coaster at Shima Spain Village Parque España in Shima, Mie, Japan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 1 Mar 1997. The ride stands 147.7 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 4048.6 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding Pyrenees is like

At 147.7 ft tall with 4,048.6 ft of track, Pyrenees balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 6 times across 4,048.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1997, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Shima Spain Village Parque España.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Parque España. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pyrenees sits on the south-east side of Parque España's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kiddy Montserrat, roughly 100 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gran Montserrat at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Pyrenees compares

On our intensity ranking Pyrenees is number 1 of 4 at Shima Spain Village Parque España, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Pyrenees

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Not for children under 10. Not for guests aged 65 or older.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Shima Spain Village Parque España app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pyrenees?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Pyrenees. Not for children under 10. Not for guests aged 65 or older, according to Shima Spain Village Parque España, official attraction rules page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pyrenees?

Pyrenees is 147.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pyrenees go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pyrenees go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Pyrenees scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Pyrenees open?

Pyrenees opened on 1 Mar 1997.

Who built Pyrenees?

Pyrenees was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Pyrenees?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 53 seconds over 4048.6 ft of track.

How does Pyrenees get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pyrenees at once?

Each train carries 32 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Pyrenees use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Pyrenees good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
