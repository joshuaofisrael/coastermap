Kiddy Montserrat

Shima Spain Village Parque España · Shima, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); top speed 21.8 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Kiddy Montserrat is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Shima Spain Village Parque España in Shima, Mie, Japan, built by Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd. It opened on 13 Feb 2016. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.8 mph and runs 708.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kiddy Montserrat is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 708.7 ft of track, Kiddy Montserrat balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2016, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Shima Spain Village Parque España.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Parque España, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kiddy Montserrat sits near the middle of Parque España's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pyrenees, roughly 100 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gran Montserrat at about 130 m. Before you ride Kiddy Montserrat

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 90 cm (about 35.4 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Children below school age must ride with a guardian.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Shima Spain Village Parque España app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kiddy Montserrat?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Kiddy Montserrat. Children below school age must ride with a guardian, according to Shima Spain Village Parque España, official attraction rules page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kiddy Montserrat?

Kiddy Montserrat is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kiddy Montserrat go?

Its top speed is 21.8 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kiddy Montserrat go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kiddy Montserrat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Kiddy Montserrat open?

Kiddy Montserrat opened on 13 Feb 2016.

Who built Kiddy Montserrat?

Kiddy Montserrat was manufactured by Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the ride on Kiddy Montserrat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 708.7 ft of track.

How many people ride Kiddy Montserrat at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Kiddy Montserrat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.8 mph; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
