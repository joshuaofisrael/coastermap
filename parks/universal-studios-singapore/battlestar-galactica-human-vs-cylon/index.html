Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon

Universal Studios Singapore · Sentosa, Singapore

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 140.1 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2010.

Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon is a steel inverted roller coaster at Universal Studios Singapore in Sentosa, Singapore, built by Vekoma. It opened on 18 Mar 2010. The ride stands 140.1 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 3185.7 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.
