Canopy Flyer

Universal Studios Singapore · Sentosa, Singapore

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in), or 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Canopy Flyer is a steel suspended roller coaster at Universal Studios Singapore in Sentosa, Singapore, built by Setpoint. It opened on 18 Mar 2010. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Canopy Flyer is like

Opened in 2010, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Studios Singapore.

Its 122 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Singapore (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Canopy Flyer sits on the south-east side of Universal Studios Singapore's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Revenge of the Mummy, roughly 110 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Puss In Boots' Giant Journey at about 130 m. Who is it for?

Before you ride Canopy Flyer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in), and riders from 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Canopy Flyer?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Canopy Flyer, or 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) with a companion, according to Universal Studios Singapore, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Canopy Flyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Canopy Flyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Canopy Flyer open?

Canopy Flyer opened on 18 Mar 2010.

Who built Canopy Flyer?

Canopy Flyer was manufactured by Setpoint.

How does Canopy Flyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Canopy Flyer at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Canopy Flyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 122 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
