Puss In Boots' Giant Journey

Universal Studios Singapore · Sentosa, Singapore

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in), or 102 to 122 cm (about 40.2 to 48 in) with a companion; top speed 24.9 mph; 56.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Puss In Boots' Giant Journey is a steel inverted roller coaster at Universal Studios Singapore in Sentosa, Singapore, built by Zamperla. It opened on 8 Apr 2015. The ride stands 56.8 ft tall, reaches 24.9 mph and runs 1404.2 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Puss In Boots' Giant Journey is like

At 56.8 ft tall with 1,404.2 ft of track, Puss In Boots' Giant Journey balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2015, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Universal Studios Singapore.

Its 122 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Singapore (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 102 to 122 cm (about 40.2 to 48 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Puss In Boots' Giant Journey sits on the east side of Universal Studios Singapore's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Canopy Flyer, roughly 130 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Enchanted Airways at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall, or 102 to 122 cm (about 40.2 to 48 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Puss In Boots' Giant Journey?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Puss In Boots' Giant Journey, or 102 to 122 cm (about 40.2 to 48 in) with a companion, according to Universal Studios Singapore, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Puss In Boots' Giant Journey?

Puss In Boots' Giant Journey is 56.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Puss In Boots' Giant Journey go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Puss In Boots' Giant Journey go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Puss In Boots' Giant Journey scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Puss In Boots' Giant Journey open?

Puss In Boots' Giant Journey opened on 8 Apr 2015.

Who built Puss In Boots' Giant Journey?

Puss In Boots' Giant Journey was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Puss In Boots' Giant Journey?

RCDB lists 1404.2 ft of track.

How does Puss In Boots' Giant Journey get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a spiral lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Puss In Boots' Giant Journey at once?

Each train carries 9 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 875 riders.

Is Puss In Boots' Giant Journey good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.9 mph; the minimum height is 122 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
