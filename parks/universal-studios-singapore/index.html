Universal Studios Singapore height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Sentosa, Singapore · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Singapore in Sentosa, Singapore has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 122 cm (about 48 in) on Enchanted Airways to 125 cm (about 49.2 in) on Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Studios Singapore are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Universal Studios Singapore a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 47% across all 113 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Enchanted Airways, which opened in 2010.

Universal Studios Singapore publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Universal Studios Singapore? By height

At Universal Studios Singapore the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Enchanted Airways (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Universal Studios Singapore

These are the rules Universal Studios Singapore states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Enchanted Airways: riders 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Canopy Flyer: riders 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Puss In Boots' Giant Journey: riders 102 to 122 cm (about 40.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Universal Studios Singapore for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Studios Singapore, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Canopy Flyer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (122 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Puss In Boots' Giant Journey, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (122 cm minimum; 56.8 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Battlestar Galactica: Human vs. Cylon, the most intense coaster in the park (125 cm minimum; 140.1 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
