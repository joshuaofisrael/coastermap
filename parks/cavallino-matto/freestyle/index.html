Freestyle

Cavallino Matto · Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 51 mph; 88 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2015.

Freestyle is a steel stand-up roller coaster at Cavallino Matto in Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy, built by Togo. It opened on 18 Jul 2015. The ride stands 88 ft tall, drops 84 ft, reaches 51 mph and runs 2,210 ft of track. On a stand-up coaster riders travel standing, supported by a bicycle-style seat and a shoulder harness. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Freestyle is like

Freestyle pairs a 88 ft structure with 2,210 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,210 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2015, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Cavallino Matto. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Togo's Standing Coaster / Astro Comet design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster at Washuzan Highland. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 140 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Cavallino Matto. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Freestyle sits on the west side of Cavallino Matto's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jurassic River, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wild Mine at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Freestyle

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Minimum age 8; maximum weight 110 kg.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Cavallino Matto app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Freestyle?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Freestyle. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Minimum age 8; maximum weight 110 kg, according to Cavallino Matto, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Freestyle?

Freestyle is 88 ft tall, with a drop of 84 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Freestyle go?

Its top speed is 51 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Freestyle go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Freestyle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Freestyle open?

Freestyle opened on 18 Jul 2015.

Who built Freestyle?

Freestyle was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the ride on Freestyle?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 32 seconds over 2,210 ft of track.

How does Freestyle get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Freestyle at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Freestyle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 51 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Freestyle operate before?

RCDB records it at Canada's Wonderland (as SkyRider).

Is Freestyle at Cavallino Matto the same as Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster at Washuzan Highland?

Both are Togo Standing Coaster / Astro Comet coasters on RCDB; the Cavallino Matto ride opened in 2015 and the Washuzan Highland ride in 1986; both list a 140 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 88 ft and 95.1 ft.
