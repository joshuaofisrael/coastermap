Wild Mine

Cavallino Matto · Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Wild Mine is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cavallino Matto in Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy, built by L&T Systems. It first opened in 1997 and was previously known as Fantasy Mouse. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wild Mine is like

Opened in 1997, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Cavallino Matto. It has run under other names (Fantasy Mouse), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to L&T Systems's Wild Mouse / Wild Mouse 30x20 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Cheddar Chase at Alabama Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm, while Alabama Adventure sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Cavallino Matto, out of 5 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wild Mine sits on the north-east side of Cavallino Matto's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Project 1, roughly 100 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jurassic River at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wild Mine?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Wild Mine, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Minimum 100 cm; children up to 130 cm ride accompanied, according to Cavallino Matto, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Wild Mine go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wild Mine scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wild Mine open?

Wild Mine opened in 1997 (previously known as Fantasy Mouse).

Who built Wild Mine?

Wild Mine was manufactured by L&T Systems.

How many people ride Wild Mine at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wild Mine good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wild Mine at Cavallino Matto the same as Cheddar Chase at Alabama Adventure?

Both are L&T Systems Wild Mouse / Wild Mouse 30x20 coasters on RCDB; the Cavallino Matto ride opened in 1997 and the Alabama Adventure ride in 2022; Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm and Alabama Adventure sets 48 in.
