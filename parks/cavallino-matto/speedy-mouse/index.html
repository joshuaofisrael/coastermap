Speedy Mouse

Cavallino Matto · Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 21.3 mph; 19.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Speedy Mouse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cavallino Matto in Marina di Castagneto Carducci, Italy, built by L&T Systems. It opened in 2026. The ride stands 19.7 ft tall, reaches 21.3 mph and runs 442.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Speedy Mouse is like

At 19.7 ft tall with 442.9 ft of track, Speedy Mouse balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Cavallino Matto. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to L&T Systems's Junior Coaster / Junior Coaster 30x14 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include PAW Patrol To The Rescue at Mirabilandia and Speedy Gonzales at Bocasse. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cavallino Matto sets 110 cm, while Mirabilandia sets 90 cm and Bocasse sets 120 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Cavallino Matto (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How Speedy Mouse compares

On our intensity ranking Speedy Mouse is number 5 of 6 at Cavallino Matto, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Topo Zorro (no inversions; 110 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Wild Mine (no inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Speedy Mouse?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Speedy Mouse, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Minimum 90 cm; children up to 110 cm ride accompanied, according to Cavallino Matto, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Speedy Mouse?

Speedy Mouse is 19.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Speedy Mouse go?

Its top speed is 21.3 mph (34 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Speedy Mouse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Speedy Mouse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Speedy Mouse open?

Speedy Mouse opened in 2026.

Who built Speedy Mouse?

Speedy Mouse was manufactured by L&T Systems.

How long is the track on Speedy Mouse?

RCDB lists 442.9 ft of track.

How many people ride Speedy Mouse at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Speedy Mouse good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.3 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Speedy Mouse at Cavallino Matto the same as PAW Patrol To The Rescue at Mirabilandia?

Both are L&T Systems Junior Coaster / Junior Coaster 30x14 coasters on RCDB; the Cavallino Matto ride opened in 2026 and the Mirabilandia ride in 2005; Cavallino Matto sets 110 cm and Mirabilandia sets 90 cm.
