Mine Blower

Fun Spot America Kissimmee · Kissimmee, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 48.5 mph; 83 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2017.

Mine Blower is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Fun Spot America Kissimmee in Kissimmee, Florida, built by Gravitykraft Corporation. It opened on 23 Jun 2017. The ride stands 83 ft tall, drops 80.5 ft, reaches 48.5 mph and runs 2,290 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Mine Blower is like

Mine Blower pairs a 83 ft structure with 2,290 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,290 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2017, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fun Spot America Kissimmee.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Fun Spot Kissimmee, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mine Blower sits near the middle of Fun Spot Kissimmee's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sea Serpent, roughly 70 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Galaxy Spin at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mine Blower?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Mine Blower, according to Fun Spot America Kissimmee, official roller coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mine Blower?

Mine Blower is 83 ft tall, with a drop of 80.5 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mine Blower go?

Its top speed is 48.5 mph (78 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mine Blower go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Mine Blower scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mine Blower open?

Mine Blower opened on 23 Jun 2017.

Who built Mine Blower?

Mine Blower was manufactured by Gravitykraft Corporation.

How long is the track on Mine Blower?

RCDB lists 2,290 ft (698 m) of track.

How does Mine Blower get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mine Blower at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Mine Blower good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 48.5 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
