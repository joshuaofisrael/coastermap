PowerPark height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Härmä, Kauhava, Finland · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

PowerPark in Härmä, Kauhava, Finland has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Mine Train to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Cobra. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at PowerPark are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes PowerPark a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 43% of its lineup, against 43% across all 159 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Thunderbird).

The most common builder is Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Joyride, which opened in 2003.

PowerPark publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Mine Train: The park lists 110 cm as the safety limit and says riders under 110 cm may ride only with a guardian (15 or older); no lower limit is stated.
  • Neo's Twister: Riders 120 to 130 cm must ride with a guardian (15 or older).
  • Junker: Riders aged 8 to 10 (over 130 cm) must ride with a guardian.

What can my child ride at PowerPark? By height

At PowerPark the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at PowerPark for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for PowerPark, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Joyride, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Junker, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 131.3 ft tall, 64.9 mph, 3 inversions, 4.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
