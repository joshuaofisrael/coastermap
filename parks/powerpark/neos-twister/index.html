Neo's Twister

PowerPark · Härmä, Kauhava, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Neo's Twister is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PowerPark in Härmä, Kauhava, Finland, built by Fabbri. It opened in 2011. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Neo's Twister is like

Opened in 2011, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at PowerPark.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at PowerPark, shared with 4 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Neo's Twister sits near the middle of PowerPark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pitts Special, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Junker at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Neo's Twister

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 120 to 130 cm must ride with a guardian (15 or older).
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the PowerPark app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Neo's Twister?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Neo's Twister, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 120 to 130 cm must ride with a guardian (15 or older), according to PowerPark, official safety limits table (Turvarajat) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Neo's Twister go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Neo's Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Neo's Twister open?

Neo's Twister opened in 2011.

Who built Neo's Twister?

Neo's Twister was manufactured by Fabbri.

How does Neo's Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Neo's Twister at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Neo's Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
