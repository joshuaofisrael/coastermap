Junker

PowerPark · Härmä, Kauhava, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 64.9 mph; 131.3 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2015.

Junker is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PowerPark in Härmä, Kauhava, Finland, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 30 May 2015. The ride stands 131.3 ft tall, reaches 64.9 mph and runs 2821.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Junker is like

At 131.3 ft tall with 2,821.5 ft of track, Junker balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,821.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2015, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at PowerPark.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at PowerPark, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Junker sits on the north-west side of PowerPark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pitts Special, roughly 40 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Neo's Twister at about 90 m. Junker layout, trains and ride system

Junker starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (7 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (rolling) (from 0 mph - 64.9 mph in 1.9 s)
  2. Top Hat - Outside
  3. Finnish Loop
  4. Block Brake
  5. Over-Banked Curve
  6. Dive Loop
  7. Corkscrew

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Junker

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders aged 8 to 10 (over 130 cm) must ride with a guardian.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the PowerPark app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Junker?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Junker. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders aged 8 to 10 (over 130 cm) must ride with a guardian, according to PowerPark, official safety limits table (Turvarajat) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Junker?

Junker is 131.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Junker go?

Its top speed is 64.9 mph (104 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Junker go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Junker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Junker open?

Junker opened on 30 May 2015.

Who built Junker?

Junker was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Junker?

RCDB lists 2821.5 ft of track.

Does Junker launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Junker at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is Junker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 64.9 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
