Pitts Special

PowerPark · Härmä, Kauhava, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 142.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Pitts Special is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PowerPark in Härmä, Kauhava, Finland, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 24 Jun 2020. The ride stands 142.7 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 2273.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pitts Special is like

At 142.7 ft tall with 2,273.6 ft of track, Pitts Special balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 85°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at PowerPark.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at PowerPark, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pitts Special sits on the north-west side of PowerPark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Junker, roughly 40 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Neo's Twister at about 70 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Pitts Special compares

On our intensity ranking Pitts Special is number 3 of 7 at PowerPark, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Pitts Special

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders aged 8 to 10 (over 130 cm) must ride with a guardian.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the PowerPark app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pitts Special?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Pitts Special. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders aged 8 to 10 (over 130 cm) must ride with a guardian, according to PowerPark, official safety limits table (Turvarajat) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pitts Special?

Pitts Special is 142.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pitts Special go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pitts Special go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pitts Special scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Pitts Special open?

Pitts Special opened on 24 Jun 2020.

Who built Pitts Special?

Pitts Special was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Pitts Special?

RCDB lists 2273.6 ft of track.

How does Pitts Special get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pitts Special at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Pitts Special good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
