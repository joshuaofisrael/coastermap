Joyride

PowerPark · Härmä, Kauhava, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Joyride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at PowerPark in Härmä, Kauhava, Finland, built by L&T Systems. It opened in 2003. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall and runs 918.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Joyride is like

Joyride pairs a 52.5 ft structure with 918.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2003, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at PowerPark. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to L&T Systems's Compact Coaster / Compact Coaster 47x21 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Project 1 at Cavallino Matto. Height rules for this model differ by park: PowerPark sets 120 cm, while Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at PowerPark, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Joyride sits on the south-east side of PowerPark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cobra, roughly 60 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Neo's Twister at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Joyride?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Joyride, according to PowerPark, official safety limits table (Turvarajat) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Joyride?

Joyride is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Joyride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Joyride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Joyride open?

Joyride opened in 2003.

Who built Joyride?

Joyride was manufactured by L&T Systems.

How long is the ride on Joyride?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 918.7 ft of track.

How does Joyride get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Joyride at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Joyride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Joyride at PowerPark the same as Project 1 at Cavallino Matto?

Both are L&T Systems Compact Coaster / Compact Coaster 47x21 coasters on RCDB; the PowerPark ride opened in 2003 and the Cavallino Matto ride in 2006; PowerPark sets 120 cm and Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm.
