Bûche Dansante

Winnoland · Saint-Pierre-du-Perray, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 105 cm (about 41.3 in); 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Bûche Dansante is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Winnoland in Saint-Pierre-du-Perray, France, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 6 Oct 2018. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bûche Dansante is like

Opened in 2018, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Winnoland. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Happy Loops at Energylandia, Kids Spin at Skyline Park, Spinning Out at Beech Bend and Bisværmen at Friheden. Height rules for this model differ by park: Winnoland sets 105 cm, while Skyline Park sets 120 cm, Beech Bend sets 47 in and Friheden sets 120 cm.

Its 105 cm minimum is the strictest at Winnoland. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Grosse Pomme, roughly 130 m away (80 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Bûche Dansante layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 105 cm (about 41.3 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bûche Dansante?

Riders must be at least 105 cm (about 41.3 in) tall to ride Bûche Dansante. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). The park lists it as La Bûche Dansante, according to Winnoland, official attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Bûche Dansante go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bûche Dansante scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Bûche Dansante open?

Bûche Dansante opened on 6 Oct 2018.

Who built Bûche Dansante?

Bûche Dansante was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Bûche Dansante get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Bûche Dansante at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Bûche Dansante good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 105 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Bûche Dansante at Winnoland the same as Spinning Out at Beech Bend?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A coasters on RCDB; the Winnoland ride opened in 2018 and the Beech Bend ride in 2016; Winnoland sets 105 cm and Beech Bend sets 47 in.
