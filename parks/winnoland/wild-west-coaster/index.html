Wild West Coaster

Winnoland · Saint-Pierre-du-Perray, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); top speed 28.6 mph; 38.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Wild West Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Winnoland in Saint-Pierre-du-Perray, France, built by Vekoma. It opened on 15 Sep 2025. The ride stands 38.4 ft tall, reaches 28.6 mph and runs 833.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wild West Coaster is like

Wild West Coaster pairs a 38.4 ft structure with 833.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.9 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Winnoland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Coaster / Kalypso design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Honey Harbour at Energylandia and Dino Dash at Emerald Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Winnoland sets 95 cm, while Emerald Park sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 95 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Winnoland, out of 2 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Wild West Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wild West Coaster?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Wild West Coaster, according to Winnoland, official attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wild West Coaster?

Wild West Coaster is 38.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wild West Coaster go?

Its top speed is 28.6 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wild West Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wild West Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wild West Coaster open?

Wild West Coaster opened on 15 Sep 2025.

Who built Wild West Coaster?

Wild West Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Wild West Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 56 seconds over 833.3 ft of track.

How does Wild West Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wild West Coaster at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Wild West Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.6 mph; the minimum height is 95 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wild West Coaster at Winnoland the same as Dino Dash at Emerald Park?

Both are Vekoma Family Coaster / Kalypso coasters on RCDB; the Winnoland ride opened in 2025 and the Emerald Park ride in 2022; Winnoland sets 95 cm and Emerald Park sets 120 cm.
