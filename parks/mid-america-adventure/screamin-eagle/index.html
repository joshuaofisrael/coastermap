Screamin' Eagle

Mid America Adventure · Eureka, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 46 to 48 in (117 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 62 mph; 110 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1976.

Screamin' Eagle is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened on 10 Apr 1976. The ride stands 110 ft tall, drops 92 ft, reaches 62 mph and runs 3,872 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Screamin' Eagle is like

With 3,872 ft of track for a 110 ft structure, Screamin' Eagle is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1976, it is the 2nd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Mid America Adventure.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Mid America Adventure, out of 9 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 46 to 48 in (117 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Screamin' Eagle sits on the north-west side of Mid America Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rookie Racer, roughly 180 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pandemonium at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 46 to 48 in (117 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Screamin' Eagle?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Screamin' Eagle, or 46 to 48 in (117 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Mid America Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Screamin' Eagle?

Screamin' Eagle is 110 ft tall, with a drop of 92 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Screamin' Eagle go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Screamin' Eagle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Screamin' Eagle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Screamin' Eagle open?

Screamin' Eagle opened on 10 Apr 1976.

Who built Screamin' Eagle?

Screamin' Eagle was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.
