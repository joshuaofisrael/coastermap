River King Mine Train

Mid America Adventure · Eureka, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 37 mph; 32 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1971.

River King Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on Jun 1971. The ride stands 32 ft tall, drops 41 ft, reaches 37 mph and runs 2,500 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding River King Mine Train is like

With 2,500 ft of track for a 32 ft structure, River King Mine Train is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 41 ft drop is longer than its 32 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 1971, it is the oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Mid America Adventure. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Adventure Express at Kings Island and Trailblazer at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Mid America Adventure sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 44 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Mid America Adventure, out of 9 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, River King Mine Train sits on the east side of Mid America Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ninja, roughly 60 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Boomerang at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for River King Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride River King Mine Train, according to Mid America Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is River King Mine Train?

River King Mine Train is 32 ft tall, with a drop of 41 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does River King Mine Train go?

Its top speed is 37 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does River King Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is River King Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did River King Mine Train open?

River King Mine Train opened on Jun 1971.

Who built River King Mine Train?

River King Mine Train was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

Is River King Mine Train at Mid America Adventure the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Mid America Adventure ride opened in 1971 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 32 ft and 35 ft.
