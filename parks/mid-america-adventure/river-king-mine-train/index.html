River King Mine Train

Mid America Adventure · Eureka, Missouri

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 37 mph; 32 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1971.

River King Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on Jun 1971. The ride stands 32 ft tall, drops 41 ft, reaches 37 mph and runs 2,500 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for River King Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride River King Mine Train, according to Mid America Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is River King Mine Train?

River King Mine Train is 32 ft tall, with a drop of 41 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does River King Mine Train go?

Its top speed is 37 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does River King Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is River King Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did River King Mine Train open?

River King Mine Train opened on Jun 1971.

Who built River King Mine Train?
