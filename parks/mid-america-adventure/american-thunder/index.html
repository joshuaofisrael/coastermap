American Thunder

Mid America Adventure · Eureka, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 48 mph; 82 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

American Thunder is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri, built by Great Coasters International. It first opened on 20 Jun 2008 and was previously known as Evel Knievel. The ride stands 82 ft tall, drops 80 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,713 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding American Thunder is like

With 2,713 ft of track for a 82 ft structure, American Thunder is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2008, it is the 8th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Mid America Adventure. It has run under other names (Evel Knievel), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Mid America Adventure, out of 9 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, American Thunder sits on the south side of Mid America Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, roughly 250 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batman The Ride at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for American Thunder?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride American Thunder, according to Mid America Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is American Thunder?

American Thunder is 82 ft tall, with a drop of 80 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does American Thunder go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does American Thunder go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is American Thunder scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did American Thunder open?

American Thunder opened on 20 Jun 2008 (previously known as Evel Knievel).

Who built American Thunder?

American Thunder was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the track on American Thunder?

RCDB lists 2,713 ft (827 m) of track.

How does American Thunder get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride American Thunder at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is American Thunder good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 48 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
