Rookie Racer

Mid America Adventure · Eureka, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 27 mph; 41 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Rookie Racer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri, built by Vekoma. It opened on 30 Sep 2023. The ride stands 41 ft tall, reaches 27 mph and runs 843 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rookie Racer is like

Rookie Racer pairs a 41 ft structure with 843 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 10 coasters we list at Mid America Adventure. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 247m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Frida at Energylandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Mid America Adventure sets 48 in, while Energylandia sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Mid America Adventure, out of 9 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rookie Racer sits on the west side of Mid America Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Boss, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast at about 150 m. Rookie Racer layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rookie Racer?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Rookie Racer, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Mid America Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rookie Racer?

Rookie Racer is 41 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rookie Racer go?

Its top speed is 27 mph (43 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rookie Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rookie Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Mid America Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Rookie Racer open?

Rookie Racer opened on 30 Sep 2023.

Who built Rookie Racer?

Rookie Racer was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Rookie Racer?

RCDB lists 843 ft (257 m) of track.

How does Rookie Racer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rookie Racer at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Rookie Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 27 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Rookie Racer at Mid America Adventure the same as Frida at Energylandia?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 247m coasters on RCDB; the Mid America Adventure ride opened in 2023 and the Energylandia ride in 2019; Mid America Adventure sets 48 in and Energylandia sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 41 ft and 42.7 ft.
