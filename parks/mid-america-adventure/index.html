Mid America Adventure roller coasters

Formerly Six Flags St. Louis · Eureka, Missouri · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Mid America Adventure (formerly Six Flags St. Louis) in Eureka, Missouri has 10 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast at 218 ft, which is also the fastest at 70 mph. The lowest height requirement is 47 in (119 cm), for Pandemonium.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mid America Adventure the same park as Six Flags St. Louis?

Yes. Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri is the park formerly called Six Flags St. Louis; sixflags.com/stlouis now redirects to the park's Enchanted Parks website, and RCDB lists the park as Mid America Adventure (checked 10 Nov 2026).

How many roller coasters does Mid America Adventure have?

Mid America Adventure has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Mid America Adventure?

Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, at 218 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Mid America Adventure?

Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, with a top speed of 70 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Mid America Adventure?

By height requirement, Pandemonium has the lowest minimum at 47 in (119 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Mid America Adventure coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Ninja, River King Mine Train, Screamin' Eagle, The Boss, Pandemonium, American Thunder, Boomerang, Rookie Racer. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Mid America Adventure do not go upside down?

River King Mine Train, Screamin' Eagle, The Boss, Pandemonium, American Thunder, Rookie Racer have no inversions, per RCDB.
