Mid America Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Formerly Six Flags St. Louis · Eureka, Missouri · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Mid America Adventure (formerly Six Flags St. Louis) in Eureka, Missouri has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 47 in (119 cm) on Pandemonium to 54 in (137 cm) on Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 8 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 10 of them.

What the coasters at Mid America Adventure are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes Mid America Adventure a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 60% of its lineup, against 38% across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 10 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 10 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Screamin' Eagle, The Boss and American Thunder).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is River King Mine Train, which opened in 1971.

What can my child ride at Mid America Adventure? By height

At Mid America Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 8 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Rookie Racer (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Pandemonium at 47 in (119 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Pandemonium (42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm)) and Rookie Racer (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Ninja at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 10 coasters with a verified rule: Ninja, River King Mine Train, Screamin' Eagle, The Boss, Pandemonium, American Thunder, Boomerang and Rookie Racer. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 10 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast and Batman The Ride. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Mid America Adventure

These are the rules Mid America Adventure states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Screamin' Eagle: riders 46 to 48 in (117 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Pandemonium: riders 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Rookie Racer: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Mid America Adventure for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Mid America Adventure, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Rookie Racer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 41 ft tall, 27 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: River King Mine Train, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 32 ft tall, 37 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 218 ft tall, 70 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mid America Adventure the same park as Six Flags St. Louis?

Yes. Mid America Adventure in Eureka, Missouri is the park formerly called Six Flags St. Louis; sixflags.com/stlouis now redirects to the park's Enchanted Parks website, and RCDB lists the park as Mid America Adventure (checked 10 Nov 2026).

What are the height requirements at Mid America Adventure?

Roller coaster height requirements at Mid America Adventure run from 47 in (119 cm) (Pandemonium) to 54 in (137 cm) (Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Mid America Adventure have?

Mid America Adventure has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Mid America Adventure?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast (218 ft tall, 70 mph, 1 inversion).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Mid America Adventure?

Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, at 218 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Mid America Adventure?

Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast, with a top speed of 70 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Mid America Adventure for first-timers?

Rookie Racer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Mid America Adventure?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Pandemonium, Rookie Racer.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Mid America Adventure?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Ninja, River King Mine Train, Screamin' Eagle, The Boss, Pandemonium, American Thunder, Boomerang, Rookie Racer.

Which coasters at Mid America Adventure do not go upside down?

River King Mine Train, Screamin' Eagle, The Boss, Pandemonium, American Thunder, Rookie Racer have no inversions, per RCDB.
