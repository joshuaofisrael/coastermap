Hyperia

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 80 mph; 236 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2024.

Hyperia is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 24 May 2024. The ride stands 236 ft tall, reaches 80 mph and runs 3265.7 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It turns riders upside down 2 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hyperia?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Hyperia, according to Thorpe Park Resort, Ride Restrictions Guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hyperia?

Hyperia is 236 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hyperia go?

Its top speed is 80 mph (129 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hyperia go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Hyperia scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hyperia open?

Hyperia opened on 24 May 2024.

Who built Hyperia?

Hyperia was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
