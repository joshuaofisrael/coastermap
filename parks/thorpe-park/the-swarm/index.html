The Swarm

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 59 mph; 127 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2012.

The Swarm is a steel wing roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 15 Mar 2012. The ride stands 127 ft tall, reaches 59 mph and runs 2,543 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Swarm?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride The Swarm. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Thorpe Park Resort, Ride Restrictions Guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Swarm?

The Swarm is 127 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Swarm go?

Its top speed is 59 mph (95 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Swarm go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is The Swarm scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Swarm open?

The Swarm opened on 15 Mar 2012.

Who built The Swarm?

The Swarm was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
