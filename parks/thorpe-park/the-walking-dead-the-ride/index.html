The Walking Dead: The Ride

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 27.7 mph; 41.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

The Walking Dead: The Ride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 23 Mar 1996 and was previously known as X:\ No Way Out and later X. The ride stands 41.7 ft tall, reaches 27.7 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Walking Dead: The Ride is like

With 1,312.3 ft of track for a 41.7 ft structure, The Walking Dead: The Ride is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1996, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Thorpe Park. It has run under other names (X:\ No Way Out, then X), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Thorpe Park, shared with 5 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Walking Dead: The Ride sits near the middle of Thorpe Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Colossus, roughly 50 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SAW: The Ride at about 130 m. The Walking Dead: The Ride layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Walking Dead: The Ride?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride The Walking Dead: The Ride. The park's ride guide lists an age requirement of 13+, according to Thorpe Park Resort, Ride Restrictions Guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Walking Dead: The Ride?

The Walking Dead: The Ride is 41.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Walking Dead: The Ride go?

Its top speed is 27.7 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Walking Dead: The Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Walking Dead: The Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did The Walking Dead: The Ride open?

The Walking Dead: The Ride opened on 23 Mar 1996 (previously known as X:\ No Way Out and later X).

Who built The Walking Dead: The Ride?

The Walking Dead: The Ride was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on The Walking Dead: The Ride?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 1312.3 ft of track.

How does The Walking Dead: The Ride get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Walking Dead: The Ride at once?

Up to 10 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is The Walking Dead: The Ride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 27.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
