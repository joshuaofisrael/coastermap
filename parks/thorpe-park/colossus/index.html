Colossus

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 44.7 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2002.

Colossus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 22 Mar 2002. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 44.7 mph and runs 2788.7 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Colossus is like

At 98.4 ft tall with 2,788.7 ft of track, Colossus balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,788.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Thorpe Park.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Thorpe Park, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Colossus sits on the south-east side of Thorpe Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Walking Dead: The Ride, roughly 50 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SAW: The Ride at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Colossus compares

On our intensity ranking Colossus is number 6 of 8 at Thorpe Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Colossus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 196 cm (about 77.2 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Thorpe Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Colossus?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Colossus. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in), according to Thorpe Park Resort, Ride Restrictions Guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Colossus?

Colossus is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Colossus go?

Its top speed is 44.7 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Colossus go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Colossus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Colossus open?

Colossus opened on 22 Mar 2002.

Who built Colossus?

Colossus was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Colossus?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 32 seconds over 2788.7 ft of track.

How does Colossus get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Colossus at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,300 riders.

Is Colossus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 44.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
