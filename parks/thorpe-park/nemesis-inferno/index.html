Nemesis Inferno

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 47.8 mph; 95.2 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2003.

Nemesis Inferno is a steel inverted roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 5 Apr 2003. The ride stands 95.2 ft tall, reaches 47.8 mph and runs 2460.7 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.
