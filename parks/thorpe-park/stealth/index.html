Stealth

Thorpe Park · Chertsey, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 80 mph; 205.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2006.

Stealth is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 15 Mar 2006. The ride stands 205.1 ft tall, reaches 80 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Stealth is like

For a 205.1 ft ride, 1,312.3 ft of track is short: Stealth is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2006, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Thorpe Park. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Xcelerator at Knott's and Rita at Alton Towers. Height rules for this model differ by park: Thorpe Park sets 140 cm, while Cedar Point sets 52 in, Hersheypark sets 54 in and Knott's sets 52 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Thorpe Park, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Stealth sits on the north-west side of Thorpe Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Swarm, roughly 120 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flying Fish at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Stealth compares

On our intensity ranking Stealth is number 2 of 8 at Thorpe Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is The Swarm (127 ft tall, 59 mph, 4 inversions, 4.5 g; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Hyperia (236 ft tall, 80 mph, 2 inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Stealth?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Stealth. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in), according to Thorpe Park Resort, Ride Restrictions Guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Stealth?

Stealth is 205.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Stealth go?

Its top speed is 80 mph (129 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Stealth go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Stealth scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Stealth open?

Stealth opened on 15 Mar 2006.

Who built Stealth?

Stealth was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Stealth?

RCDB lists 1312.3 ft of track.

Does Stealth launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Stealth at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Stealth good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 80 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Stealth at Thorpe Park the same as Xcelerator at Knott's?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Thorpe Park ride opened in 2006 and the Knott's ride in 2002; Thorpe Park sets 140 cm and Knott's sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 205.1 ft and 205 ft.
