Thorpe Park roller coasters

Chertsey, England · 8 coasters

Thorpe Park in Chertsey, England has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Hyperia at 236 ft and the fastest is Stealth at 80 mph. The lowest height requirement is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), for Flying Fish.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Thorpe Park have?

Thorpe Park has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Thorpe Park?

Hyperia, at 236 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Thorpe Park?

Stealth, with a top speed of 80 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Thorpe Park?

By height requirement, Flying Fish has the lowest minimum at 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Thorpe Park coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Flying Fish. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Thorpe Park do not go upside down?

The Walking Dead: The Ride, Flying Fish, Stealth have no inversions, per RCDB.
