Medusa

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 61 mph; 142 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 1999.

Medusa is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 2 Apr 1999 and was previously known as Bizarro. The ride stands 142 ft tall, drops 132 ft, reaches 61 mph and runs 3,985 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding Medusa is like

Medusa pairs a 142 ft structure with 3,985 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 7 times across 3,985 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1999, it is the 5th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Six Flags Great Adventure. It has run under other names (Bizarro), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Floorless Coaster / Medusa design, and the first of them to open. Others include Scream! at Magic Mountain. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 54 in minimum can also ride 11 other coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Medusa sits on the east side of Six Flags Great Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Runaway Mine Train, roughly 170 m away (44 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is El Toro at about 180 m. Medusa layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 7 elements RCDB records on Medusa's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Dive Loop
  4. Zero-G Roll
  5. Cobra Roll
  6. Block Brake
  7. Interlocking Corkscrews

RCDB counts 7 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Medusa

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Great Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Medusa?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Medusa, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Medusa?

Medusa is 142 ft tall, with a drop of 132 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Medusa go?

Its top speed is 61 mph (98 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Medusa go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Medusa scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Medusa open?

Medusa opened on 2 Apr 1999 (previously known as Bizarro).

Who built Medusa?

Medusa was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Medusa?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 3,985 ft of track.

How does Medusa get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Medusa at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train.

Is Medusa good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 7 times; it tops out at 61 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Medusa at Six Flags Great Adventure the same as Scream! at Magic Mountain?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Floorless Coaster / Medusa coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great Adventure ride opened in 1999 and the Magic Mountain ride in 2003; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 142 ft and 150 ft.
