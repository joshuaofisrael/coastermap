Barrels O' Fun

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 59 in (150 cm), or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Barrels O' Fun is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Reverchon. It opened on 8 Jul 2026. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Barrels O' Fun is like

Barrels O' Fun spreads 1,377.9 ft of track across a layout only 42.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 14 coasters we list at Six Flags Great Adventure. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Reverchon's Spinning Coaster / Classic design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Exterminator at Kennywood, Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park, RidderStrijd at Hellendoorn and Flagermusen at Fårup. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Great Adventure sets 59 in, while Hirakata Park sets 130 cm, Hellendoorn sets 140 cm and Bagatelle sets 130 cm.

Its 59 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Great Adventure. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall, or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Barrels O' Fun?

Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall to ride Barrels O' Fun, or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Barrels O' Fun?

Barrels O' Fun is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Barrels O' Fun go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Barrels O' Fun go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Barrels O' Fun scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Barrels O' Fun open?

Barrels O' Fun opened on 8 Jul 2026.

Who built Barrels O' Fun?

Barrels O' Fun was manufactured by Reverchon.

How long is the ride on Barrels O' Fun?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1377.9 ft of track.

How does Barrels O' Fun get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Barrels O' Fun at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Barrels O' Fun good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph; the minimum height is 59 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Barrels O' Fun operate before?

RCDB records it at Six Flags Great America (as Ragin' Cajun); Six Flags America (as Ragin' Cajun).

Is Barrels O' Fun at Six Flags Great Adventure the same as Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park?

Both are Reverchon Spinning Coaster / Classic coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great Adventure ride opened in 2026 and the Hirakata Park ride in 1998; Six Flags Great Adventure sets 59 in and Hirakata Park sets 130 cm.
