Barrels O' Fun

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 59 in (150 cm), or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Barrels O' Fun is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Reverchon. It opened on 8 Jul 2026. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall, or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Barrels O' Fun?

Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall to ride Barrels O' Fun, or 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Barrels O' Fun?

Barrels O' Fun is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Barrels O' Fun go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Barrels O' Fun go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Barrels O' Fun scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Barrels O' Fun open?

Barrels O' Fun opened on 8 Jul 2026.
