Harley Quinn Crazy Train

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Harley Quinn Crazy Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1999 and was previously known as Blackbeard's Lost Treasure Train. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Harley Quinn Crazy Train is like

With 1,181.1 ft of track for a 26.3 ft structure, Harley Quinn Crazy Train is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1999, it is the 4th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Six Flags Great Adventure. It has run under other names (Blackbeard's Lost Treasure Train), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 12 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom, Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico and Tom y Jerry at Parque Warner. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, while Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm, Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm and Fort Fun sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure, out of 13 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Harley Quinn Crazy Train sits on the south side of Six Flags Great Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Joker, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Skull Mountain at about 170 m. Harley Quinn Crazy Train layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Harley Quinn Crazy Train?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Harley Quinn Crazy Train, or 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Harley Quinn Crazy Train?

Harley Quinn Crazy Train is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Harley Quinn Crazy Train go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Harley Quinn Crazy Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Harley Quinn Crazy Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Harley Quinn Crazy Train open?

Harley Quinn Crazy Train opened in 1999 (previously known as Blackbeard's Lost Treasure Train).

Who built Harley Quinn Crazy Train?

Harley Quinn Crazy Train was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Harley Quinn Crazy Train?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 5 seconds over 1181.1 ft of track.

How does Harley Quinn Crazy Train get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Harley Quinn Crazy Train at once?

RCDB's train details give 40 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,250 riders.

Is Harley Quinn Crazy Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure the same as #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great Adventure ride opened in 1999 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1976; Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in and Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm.
