Nitro

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 80 mph; 230 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Nitro is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 Apr 2001. The ride stands 230 ft tall, drops 215 ft, reaches 80 mph and runs 5,394 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Nitro?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Nitro, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Nitro?

Nitro is 230 ft tall, with a drop of 215 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nitro go?

Its top speed is 80 mph (129 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nitro go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Nitro scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Nitro open?

Nitro opened on 7 Apr 2001.

Who built Nitro?

Nitro was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
