Six Flags Great Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Jackson, New Jersey · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey has 14 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Lil' Devil Coaster to 59 in (150 cm) on Barrels O' Fun. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 8 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 12 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 8 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Great Adventure a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 57% of its lineup, against 52% across all 80 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 12 of the 14 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 14 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (El Toro).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Runaway Mine Train, which opened in 1974.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Great Adventure? By height

At Six Flags Great Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 7 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 14 coasters, the nearest being Lil' Devil Coaster at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 14 coasters with a verified rule: Lil' Devil Coaster. With a companion they can also ride The Dark Knight Coaster (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Harley Quinn Crazy Train (41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Runaway Mine Train at 44 in (112 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 14 coasters with a verified rule, 7 more than at the height below: El Toro, The Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Jersey Devil Coaster, Skull Mountain, Runaway Mine Train and Harley Quinn Crazy Train. With a companion they can also ride Barrels O' Fun (48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm)) and The Flash: Vertical Velocity (48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Superman - Ultimate Flight at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 12 of 14 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Superman - Ultimate Flight, Batman The Ride, Medusa and Nitro. With a companion they can also ride Barrels O' Fun (48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm)) and The Flash: Vertical Velocity (48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: The Joker (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and Skull Mountain (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Six Flags Great Adventure

These are the rules Six Flags Great Adventure states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Barrels O' Fun: riders 48 to 59 in (122 to 150 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Dark Knight Coaster: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Flash: Vertical Velocity: riders 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Skull Mountain: riders 44 to 48 in (112 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Harley Quinn Crazy Train: riders 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Great Adventure, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Lil' Devil Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: The Dark Knight Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Medusa, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 142 ft tall, 61 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Roller coaster height requirements at Six Flags Great Adventure run from 42 in (107 cm) (Lil' Devil Coaster) to 59 in (150 cm) (Barrels O' Fun), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Great Adventure have?

Six Flags Great Adventure has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Medusa (142 ft tall, 61 mph, 7 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Nitro, at 230 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Nitro, with a top speed of 80 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure for first-timers?

Lil' Devil Coaster is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 42 in (107 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Lil' Devil Coaster. With a companion they can also ride The Dark Knight Coaster, Harley Quinn Crazy Train.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Which coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure do not go upside down?
