Six Flags Great Adventure roller coasters

Jackson, New Jersey · 14 coasters

Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey has 14 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Nitro at 230 ft, which is also the fastest at 80 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Lil' Devil Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Great Adventure have?

Six Flags Great Adventure has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Nitro, at 230 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure?

Nitro, with a top speed of 80 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Great Adventure?

By height requirement, Lil' Devil Coaster has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Great Adventure coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Lil' Devil Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Great Adventure coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: El Toro, The Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Jersey Devil Coaster, Lil' Devil Coaster, Skull Mountain, Runaway Mine Train, Harley Quinn Crazy Train. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure do not go upside down?

Barrels O' Fun, El Toro, The Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Lil' Devil Coaster, Skull Mountain, Runaway Mine Train, Harley Quinn Crazy Train, Nitro have no inversions, per RCDB.
