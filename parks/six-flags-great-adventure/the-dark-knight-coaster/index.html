The Dark Knight Coaster

Six Flags Great Adventure · Jackson, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

The Dark Knight Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure in Jackson, New Jersey, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 15 May 2008. The ride runs 1,213 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Dark Knight Coaster is like

Opened in 2008, it is the 9th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Six Flags Great Adventure. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile, Rev. 2 design, and the first of them to open. Others include The Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Great America and Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, while Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Six Flags Great Adventure, out of 13 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Dark Knight Coaster sits on the west side of Six Flags Great Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman The Ride, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Lil' Devil Coaster at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Dark Knight Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Dark Knight Coaster, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Great Adventure, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does The Dark Knight Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Dark Knight Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Great Adventure rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Dark Knight Coaster open?

The Dark Knight Coaster opened on 15 May 2008.

Who built The Dark Knight Coaster?

The Dark Knight Coaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

Is The Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure the same as The Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile, Rev. 2 coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Great Adventure ride opened in 2008 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 2008; both list a 48 in minimum.
