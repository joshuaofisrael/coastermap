Götterblitz

Familypark Neusiedlersee · St. Margarethen, Austria

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Götterblitz is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Familypark Neusiedlersee in St. Margarethen, Austria, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened in 2008. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1574.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Götterblitz is like

Götterblitz pairs a 52.5 ft structure with 1,574.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2008, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Familypark Neusiedlersee. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Youngstar Coaster / Götterblitz design, and the first of them to open. Others include Correcaminos Bip, Bip at Parque Warner.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Familypark, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Götterblitz sits on the north side of Familypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Herkules, roughly 60 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Adlerflug at about 290 m. Götterblitz layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Götterblitz compares

On our intensity ranking Götterblitz is number 2 of 4 at Familypark Neusiedlersee, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Adlerflug (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Rattenmühle (62.3 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Götterblitz?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Götterblitz, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Children under 130 cm only with an adult, according to Familypark, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Götterblitz?

Götterblitz is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Götterblitz go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Götterblitz go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Götterblitz scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Götterblitz open?

Götterblitz opened in 2008.

Who built Götterblitz?

Götterblitz was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Götterblitz?

RCDB lists 1574.8 ft of track.

How does Götterblitz get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Götterblitz good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Götterblitz at Familypark the same as Correcaminos Bip, Bip at Parque Warner?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Youngstar Coaster / Götterblitz coasters on RCDB; the Familypark ride opened in 2008 and the Parque Warner ride in 2009.
