Rattenmühle

Familypark Neusiedlersee · St. Margarethen, Austria

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 31.1 mph; 62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Rattenmühle is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Familypark Neusiedlersee in St. Margarethen, Austria, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 7 Jun 2013. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 1410.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rattenmühle is like

At 62.3 ft tall with 1,410.8 ft of track, Rattenmühle balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2013, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Familypark Neusiedlersee.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Familypark, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rattenmühle sits on the south-east side of Familypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Adlerflug, roughly 260 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Götterblitz at about 410 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rattenmühle?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Rattenmühle, or 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Children under 130 cm only with an adult, according to Familypark, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rattenmühle?

Rattenmühle is 62.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rattenmühle go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rattenmühle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rattenmühle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rattenmühle open?

Rattenmühle opened on 7 Jun 2013.

Who built Rattenmühle?

Rattenmühle was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Rattenmühle?

RCDB lists 1410.8 ft of track.

How does Rattenmühle get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rattenmühle at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Rattenmühle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
