Adlerflug

Familypark Neusiedlersee · St. Margarethen, Austria

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Adlerflug is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Familypark Neusiedlersee in St. Margarethen, Austria, built by Sunkid GmbH. It opened on 5 Apr 2014. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Adlerflug is like

Opened in 2014, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Familypark Neusiedlersee. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Sunkid GmbH's Butterfly / II XL design, and the first of them to open. Others include Säbelsaurus at Potts Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Familypark sets 120 cm, while Potts Park sets 125 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Familypark, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Adlerflug sits on the south side of Familypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rattenmühle, roughly 260 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Götterblitz at about 290 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Adlerflug

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Children under 120 cm only with an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Familypark Neusiedlersee app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Adlerflug?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Adlerflug, or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Children under 120 cm only with an adult, according to Familypark, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Adlerflug go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Adlerflug scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Adlerflug open?

Adlerflug opened on 5 Apr 2014.

Who built Adlerflug?

Adlerflug was manufactured by Sunkid GmbH.

How many people ride Adlerflug at once?

Up to 2 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Adlerflug good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Adlerflug at Familypark the same as Säbelsaurus at Potts Park?

Both are Sunkid GmbH Butterfly / II XL coasters on RCDB; the Familypark ride opened in 2014 and the Potts Park ride in 2019; Familypark sets 120 cm and Potts Park sets 125 cm.
