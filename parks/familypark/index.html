Familypark Neusiedlersee height requirements and roller coasters ranked

St. Margarethen, Austria · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Familypark Neusiedlersee in St. Margarethen, Austria has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Herkules to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Götterblitz. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Familypark Neusiedlersee are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Familypark Neusiedlersee a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 232 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Götterblitz, which opened in 2008.

Familypark Neusiedlersee publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Familypark Neusiedlersee? By height

At Familypark Neusiedlersee the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Herkules. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Adlerflug at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

Companion rules at Familypark Neusiedlersee

These are the rules Familypark Neusiedlersee states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Götterblitz: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Rattenmühle: riders 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Adlerflug: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Herkules: A children's coaster: riders over 120 cm or 45 kg may not ride.

Best coasters at Familypark Neusiedlersee for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Familypark Neusiedlersee, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Herkules, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 5.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Götterblitz, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Rattenmühle, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 62.3 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
