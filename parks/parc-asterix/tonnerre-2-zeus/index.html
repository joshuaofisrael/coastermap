Tonnerre 2 Zeus

Parc Astérix · Plailly, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 52 mph; 98 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Tonnerre 2 Zeus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Astérix in Plailly, France, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It first opened on 5 Apr 1997 and was previously known as Tonnerre de Zeus. The ride stands 98 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 4,044 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tonnerre 2 Zeus is like

Tonnerre 2 Zeus spreads 4,044 ft of track across a layout only 98 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1997, it is the 4th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Parc Astérix. It has run under other names (Tonnerre de Zeus), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Parc Astérix, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tonnerre 2 Zeus sits on the south-east side of Parc Astérix's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vol d'Icare, roughly 190 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Goudurix at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tonnerre 2 Zeus?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tonnerre 2 Zeus, according to Parc Astérix, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tonnerre 2 Zeus?

Tonnerre 2 Zeus is 98 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tonnerre 2 Zeus go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tonnerre 2 Zeus go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tonnerre 2 Zeus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tonnerre 2 Zeus open?

Tonnerre 2 Zeus opened on 5 Apr 1997 (previously known as Tonnerre de Zeus).

Who built Tonnerre 2 Zeus?

Tonnerre 2 Zeus was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.

How long is the ride on Tonnerre 2 Zeus?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 5 seconds over 4,044 ft of track.

How does Tonnerre 2 Zeus get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Tonnerre 2 Zeus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
