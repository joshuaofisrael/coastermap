La Trace du Hourra

Parc Astérix · Plailly, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

La Trace du Hourra is a steel roller coaster at Parc Astérix in Plailly, France, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 31 Mar 2001. The ride reaches 37.3 mph and runs 2952.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding La Trace du Hourra is like

Opened in 2001, it is the 5th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Parc Astérix.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Parc Astérix, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, La Trace du Hourra sits on the north-west side of Parc Astérix's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is SOS Tournevis, roughly 120 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Toutatis at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for La Trace du Hourra?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride La Trace du Hourra, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Parc Astérix, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does La Trace du Hourra go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does La Trace du Hourra go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is La Trace du Hourra scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did La Trace du Hourra open?

La Trace du Hourra opened on 31 Mar 2001.

Who built La Trace du Hourra?

La Trace du Hourra was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on La Trace du Hourra?

RCDB lists 2952.8 ft of track.

How does La Trace du Hourra get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride La Trace du Hourra at once?

Each train carries 14 riders, according to RCDB.

Is La Trace du Hourra good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
