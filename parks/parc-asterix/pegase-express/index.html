Pégase Express

Parc Astérix · Plailly, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 32.3 mph; 68.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Pégase Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Astérix in Plailly, France, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 11 Jun 2017. The ride stands 68.8 ft tall, reaches 32.3 mph and runs 3044.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pégase Express is like

Pégase Express spreads 3,044.6 ft of track across a layout only 68.8 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2017, it is the 7th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Parc Astérix.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Parc Astérix, shared with 3 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pégase Express sits on the east side of Parc Astérix's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vol d'Icare, roughly 60 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tonnerre 2 Zeus at about 250 m. Pégase Express layout, trains and ride system

Pégase Express starts with a tyre-driven launch (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. It also uses a chain lift hill (68.8 ft tall) and a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels (42.5 ft tall). RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists 6 track elements for Pégase Express, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  2. Chain Lift Hill (68.8 ft tall)
  3. Tunnel
  4. Booster Wheel Lift Hill (42.5 ft tall)
  5. Dark/Show Section
  6. Tire Propelled Launch

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pégase Express?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Pégase Express, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Parc Astérix, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pégase Express?

Pégase Express is 68.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pégase Express go?

Its top speed is 32.3 mph (52 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pégase Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pégase Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Pégase Express open?

Pégase Express opened on 11 Jun 2017.

Who built Pégase Express?

Pégase Express was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Pégase Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 3044.6 ft of track.

Does Pégase Express launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Pégase Express at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Pégase Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 32.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
