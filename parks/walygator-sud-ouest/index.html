Walygator Sud-Ouest height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Roquefort, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Walygator Sud-Ouest in Roquefort, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Coccinelle to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Titan. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Walygator Sud-Ouest are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Walygator Sud-Ouest a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Boomerang, which opened in 1992.

Walygator Sud-Ouest publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Walygator Sud-Ouest? By height

At Walygator Sud-Ouest the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Walygator Sud-Ouest for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Walygator Sud-Ouest, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Coccinelle, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Mine de Thor, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Titan, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 114.8 ft tall, 59 mph, 2 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
