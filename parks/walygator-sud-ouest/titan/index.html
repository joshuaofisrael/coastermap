Titan

Walygator Sud-Ouest · Roquefort, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 59 mph; 114.8 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2026.

Titan is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walygator Sud-Ouest in Roquefort, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 12 Sep 2026. The ride stands 114.8 ft tall, reaches 59 mph and runs 1968.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Titan is like

At 114.8 ft tall with 1,968.5 ft of track, Titan balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,968.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Walygator Sud-Ouest.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Walygator Sud-Ouest. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Titan sits on the north-west side of Walygator Sud-Ouest's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mine de Thor, roughly 250 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Coccinelle at about 390 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Titan compares

On our intensity ranking Titan is number 1 of 4 at Walygator Sud-Ouest, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Boomerang (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Titan

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walygator Sud-Ouest app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Titan?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Titan, according to Walygator Sud-Ouest, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Titan?

Titan is 114.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Titan go?

Its top speed is 59 mph (95 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Titan go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Titan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Titan open?

Titan opened on 12 Sep 2026.

Who built Titan?

Titan was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Titan?

RCDB lists 1968.5 ft of track.

How does Titan get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Titan at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Titan good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 59 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Titan operate before?

RCDB records it at Oakwood Theme Park (as Speed: No Limits).
