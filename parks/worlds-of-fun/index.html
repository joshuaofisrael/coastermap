Worlds of Fun roller coasters

Kansas City, Missouri · 7 coasters

Worlds of Fun in Kansas City, Missouri has 7 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Mamba at 205 ft, which is also the fastest at 75 mph. The lowest height requirement is 48 in (122 cm), for Timber Wolf.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Worlds of Fun have?

Worlds of Fun has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Worlds of Fun?

Mamba, at 205 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Worlds of Fun?

Mamba, with a top speed of 75 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Worlds of Fun?

By height requirement, Timber Wolf has the lowest minimum at 48 inches (122 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Worlds of Fun coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Timber Wolf, Mamba, Spinning Dragons, Prowler, Zambezi Zinger, Boomerang. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Worlds of Fun do not go upside down?

Timber Wolf, Mamba, Spinning Dragons, Prowler, Zambezi Zinger have no inversions, per RCDB.
