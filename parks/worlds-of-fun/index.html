Worlds of Fun height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Kansas City, Missouri · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Worlds of Fun in Kansas City, Missouri has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Timber Wolf to 54 in (137 cm) on Patriot. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Worlds of Fun are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Worlds of Fun a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 71% of its lineup, against 40% across all 232 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 7 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Timber Wolf, Prowler and Zambezi Zinger).

The most common builder is Great Coasters International, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Timber Wolf, which opened in 1989.

What can my child ride at Worlds of Fun? By height

At Worlds of Fun the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Worlds of Fun for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Worlds of Fun, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Spinning Dragons, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 54 ft tall, 31 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Zambezi Zinger, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 74 ft tall, 45 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Patriot, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 149 ft tall, 60 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Worlds of Fun?

Roller coaster height requirements at Worlds of Fun run from 48 in (122 cm) (Timber Wolf) to 54 in (137 cm) (Patriot), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Worlds of Fun have?

Worlds of Fun has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Worlds of Fun?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Patriot (149 ft tall, 60 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Worlds of Fun?

Mamba, at 205 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Worlds of Fun?

Mamba, with a top speed of 75 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Worlds of Fun for first-timers?

Spinning Dragons is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Worlds of Fun?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Spinning Dragons.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Worlds of Fun?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Timber Wolf, Mamba, Spinning Dragons, Prowler, Zambezi Zinger, Boomerang.

Which coasters at Worlds of Fun do not go upside down?

Timber Wolf, Mamba, Spinning Dragons, Prowler, Zambezi Zinger have no inversions, per RCDB.
