Spinning Dragons

Worlds of Fun · Kansas City, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 31 mph; 54 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Spinning Dragons is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Worlds of Fun in Kansas City, Missouri, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 17 Apr 2004. The ride stands 54 ft tall, reaches 31 mph and runs 1,345 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinning Dragons is like

Spinning Dragons pairs a 54 ft structure with 1,345 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2004, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Worlds of Fun. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / 420/4 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Fairly Odd Coaster at Nickelodeon Universe MOA. Height rules for this model differ by park: Worlds of Fun sets 48 in, while Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 47 in.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Worlds of Fun (tied with 5 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spinning Dragons sits on the north side of Worlds of Fun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Patriot, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Zambezi Zinger at about 340 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Worlds of Fun rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Spinning Dragons

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Worlds of Fun app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinning Dragons?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Spinning Dragons, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Worlds of Fun, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spinning Dragons?

Spinning Dragons is 54 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spinning Dragons go?

Its top speed is 31 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spinning Dragons go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinning Dragons scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Worlds of Fun rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spinning Dragons open?

Spinning Dragons opened on 17 Apr 2004.

Who built Spinning Dragons?

Spinning Dragons was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Spinning Dragons?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1,345 ft of track.

How does Spinning Dragons get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinning Dragons at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Spinning Dragons good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Spinning Dragons at Worlds of Fun the same as Fairly Odd Coaster at Nickelodeon Universe MOA?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / 420/4 coasters on RCDB; the Worlds of Fun ride opened in 2004 and the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride in 2004; Worlds of Fun sets 48 in and Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 47 in.
