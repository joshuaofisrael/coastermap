Zambezi Zinger

Worlds of Fun · Kansas City, Missouri

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 45 mph; 74 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Zambezi Zinger is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Worlds of Fun in Kansas City, Missouri, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 19 Jun 2023. The ride stands 74 ft tall, reaches 45 mph and runs 2,428 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Zambezi Zinger is like

With 2,428 ft of track for a 74 ft structure, Zambezi Zinger is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Worlds of Fun.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Worlds of Fun (tied with 5 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Zambezi Zinger sits on the east side of Worlds of Fun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Boomerang, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Prowler at about 140 m. Zambezi Zinger layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Worlds of Fun rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Zambezi Zinger?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Zambezi Zinger, according to Worlds of Fun, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Zambezi Zinger?

Zambezi Zinger is 74 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Zambezi Zinger go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Zambezi Zinger go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Zambezi Zinger scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Worlds of Fun rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Zambezi Zinger open?

Zambezi Zinger opened on 19 Jun 2023.

Who built Zambezi Zinger?

Zambezi Zinger was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Zambezi Zinger?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 2,428 ft of track.

How does Zambezi Zinger get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Zambezi Zinger at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Zambezi Zinger good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 45 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
