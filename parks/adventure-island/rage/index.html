Rage

Adventure Island · Southend-on-Sea, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2007.

Rage is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventure Island in Southend-on-Sea, England, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 10 Feb 2007. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1184.4 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Rage is like

Rage pairs a 72.2 ft structure with 1,184.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. With 3 inversions in 1,184.4 ft, it flips riders roughly every 395 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Adventure Island. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Euro-Fighter / 320+ design, and the first of them to open. Others include Untamed at Canobie Lake, Predator at IMG Worlds and Falcon at Duinrell. Height rules for this model differ by park: Adventure Island sets 120 cm, while Canobie Lake sets 48 in and IMG Worlds sets 125 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Adventure Island, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rage sits on the east side of Adventure Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Green Scream, roughly 60 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Barnstormer at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Rage

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Adventure Island app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rage?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Rage, according to Adventure Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rage?

Rage is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rage go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rage go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Rage scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Rage open?

Rage opened on 10 Feb 2007.

Who built Rage?

Rage was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Rage?

RCDB lists 1184.4 ft of track.

How does Rage get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rage at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 850 riders.

Is Rage good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rage at Adventure Island the same as Falcon at Duinrell?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Euro-Fighter / 320+ coasters on RCDB; the Adventure Island ride opened in 2007 and the Duinrell ride in 2009; both list a 120 cm minimum.
