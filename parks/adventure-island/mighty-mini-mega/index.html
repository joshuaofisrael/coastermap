Mighty Mini Mega

Adventure Island · Southend-on-Sea, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 24.6 mph; 30.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Mighty Mini Mega is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventure Island in Southend-on-Sea, England, built by Pinfari. It opened in 2003. The ride stands 30.2 ft tall, reaches 24.6 mph and runs 764.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mighty Mini Mega is like

Mighty Mini Mega pairs a 30.2 ft structure with 764.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2003, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Adventure Island.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Adventure Island, shared with 3 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mighty Mini Mega sits near the middle of Adventure Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Barnstormer, roughly 40 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kiddi Koasta at about 40 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mighty Mini Mega?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Mighty Mini Mega, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 100 to 120 cm must be accompanied by a person aged 14 or over, according to Adventure Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mighty Mini Mega?

Mighty Mini Mega is 30.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mighty Mini Mega go?

Its top speed is 24.6 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mighty Mini Mega go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mighty Mini Mega scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Mighty Mini Mega open?

Mighty Mini Mega opened in 2003.

Who built Mighty Mini Mega?

Mighty Mini Mega was manufactured by Pinfari.

How long is the ride on Mighty Mini Mega?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 764.4 ft of track.

Is Mighty Mini Mega good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
