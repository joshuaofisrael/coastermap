Tarántula

Parque de Atracciones de Madrid · Madrid, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 42.3 mph; 83.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Tarántula is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid in Madrid, Spain, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 14 May 2005. The ride stands 83.7 ft tall, reaches 42.3 mph and runs 2066.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tarántula is like

At 83.7 ft tall with 2,066.9 ft of track, Tarántula balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2005, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand, Dragon's Fury at Chessington, Salama at Linnanmäki and Naga Bay at Bobbejaanland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm, while Phantasialand sets 140 cm, Linnanmäki sets 140 cm and Bobbejaanland sets 140 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Parque de Atracciones (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tarántula sits on the north-east side of Parque de Atracciones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Abismo, roughly 60 m away (135 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tornado at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

How Tarántula compares

On our intensity ranking Tarántula is number 3 of 6 at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is TNT Tren de la Mina (55.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Tornado (98 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 3 inversions, 4 g; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tarántula?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tarántula. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tarántula?

Tarántula is 83.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tarántula go?

Its top speed is 42.3 mph (68 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tarántula go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tarántula scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tarántula open?

Tarántula opened on 14 May 2005.

Who built Tarántula?

Tarántula was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Tarántula?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2066.9 ft of track.

How does Tarántula get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tarántula at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 870 riders.

Is Tarántula good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 42.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Tarántula at Parque de Atracciones the same as Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 coasters on RCDB; the Parque de Atracciones ride opened in 2005 and the Phantasialand ride in 2002; Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm and Phantasialand sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 83.7 ft and 57.1 ft.
