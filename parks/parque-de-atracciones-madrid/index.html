Parque de Atracciones de Madrid height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Madrid, Spain · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parque de Atracciones de Madrid in Madrid, Spain has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Tornado to 135 cm (about 53.1 in) on Abismo. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Parque de Atracciones de Madrid a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tornado, which opened in 1999.

Parque de Atracciones de Madrid publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid? By height

At Parque de Atracciones de Madrid the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Tornado (up to 193 cm (about 76 in)), Tarántula (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Abismo (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)) and TNT Tren de la Mina (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol), the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: TNT Tren de la Mina, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 55.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Abismo, the most intense coaster in the park (135 cm minimum; 160.8 ft tall, 65.2 mph, 2 inversions, 4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
