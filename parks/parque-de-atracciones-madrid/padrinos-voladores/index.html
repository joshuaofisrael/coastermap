Padrinos Voladores

Parque de Atracciones de Madrid · Madrid, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 7.8 mph; 20.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Padrinos Voladores is a steel suspended roller coaster at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid in Madrid, Spain, built by Zamperla. It first opened on May 2007 and was previously known as Turbulencia. The ride stands 20.3 ft tall, reaches 7.8 mph and runs 360.9 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Padrinos Voladores is like

Padrinos Voladores pairs a 20.3 ft structure with 360.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2007, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid. It has run under other names (Turbulencia), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Air Force / 5 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Aero 5 at Benyland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm, while Benyland sets 105 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Parque de Atracciones (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Padrinos Voladores sits on the south-west side of Parque de Atracciones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is TNT Tren de la Mina, roughly 40 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) at about 90 m. Padrinos Voladores layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

How Padrinos Voladores compares

On our intensity ranking Padrinos Voladores is number 5 of 6 at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) (13 ft tall, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is TNT Tren de la Mina (55.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Padrinos Voladores

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Padrinos Voladores?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Padrinos Voladores, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Padrinos Voladores?

Padrinos Voladores is 20.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Padrinos Voladores go?

Its top speed is 7.8 mph (13 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Padrinos Voladores go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Padrinos Voladores scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Padrinos Voladores open?

Padrinos Voladores opened on May 2007 (previously known as Turbulencia).

Who built Padrinos Voladores?

Padrinos Voladores was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Padrinos Voladores?

RCDB lists 360.9 ft of track.

How does Padrinos Voladores get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Padrinos Voladores at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Padrinos Voladores good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 7.8 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Padrinos Voladores at Parque de Atracciones the same as Aero 5 at Benyland?

Both are Zamperla Air Force / 5 coasters on RCDB; the Parque de Atracciones ride opened in 2007 and the Benyland ride in 2015; Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm and Benyland sets 105 cm.
