Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)

Parque de Atracciones de Madrid · Madrid, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid in Madrid, Spain, built by Zamperla. It first opened on 2 Jun 2007 and was previously known as Vagones Locos. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) is like

At 13 ft tall with 262.5 ft of track, Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2007, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Parque de Atracciones de Madrid. It has run under other names (Vagones Locos), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 8th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 42 in and Silver Dollar City sets 42 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Parque de Atracciones (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) sits on the south-west side of Parque de Atracciones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is TNT Tren de la Mina, roughly 60 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Padrinos Voladores at about 90 m. Before you ride Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Parque de Atracciones de Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)?

Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) open?

Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) opened on 2 Jun 2007 (previously known as Vagones Locos).

Who built Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)?

Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol)?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Patrulla Canina (PAW Patrol) at Parque de Atracciones the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Parque de Atracciones ride opened in 2007 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; Parque de Atracciones sets 120 cm and Holiday World sets 42 in.
