Backlot Stunt Coaster

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 45.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Backlot Stunt Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Premier Rides. It first opened on 1 May 2005 and was previously known as Italian Job: Stunt Track. The ride stands 45.2 ft tall, drops 31.2 ft, reaches 40 mph and runs 1,960 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Backlot Stunt Coaster is like

Backlot Stunt Coaster spreads 1,960 ft of track across a layout only 45.2 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a 31.2 ft drop against a 45.2 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2005, it is the 12th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It has run under other names (Italian Job: Stunt Track), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 11 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Backlot Stunt Coaster sits on the south side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Behemoth, roughly 70 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mighty Canadian Minebuster at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Backlot Stunt Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Backlot Stunt Coaster, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Backlot Stunt Coaster?

Backlot Stunt Coaster is 45.2 ft tall, with a drop of 31.2 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Backlot Stunt Coaster go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Backlot Stunt Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Backlot Stunt Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Backlot Stunt Coaster open?

Backlot Stunt Coaster opened on 1 May 2005 (previously known as Italian Job: Stunt Track).

Who built Backlot Stunt Coaster?

Backlot Stunt Coaster was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Backlot Stunt Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 4 seconds over 1,960 ft of track.

Does Backlot Stunt Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

How many people ride Backlot Stunt Coaster at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Backlot Stunt Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Backlot Stunt Coaster at Canada's Wonderland the same as Backlot Stunt Coaster at Kings Dominion?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Canada's Wonderland ride opened in 2005 and the Kings Dominion ride in 2006; both list a 48 in minimum.
