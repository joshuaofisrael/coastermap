Yukon Striker

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 80.8 mph; 223 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2019.

Yukon Striker is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 3 May 2019. The ride stands 223 ft tall, drops 245 ft, reaches 80.8 mph and runs 3,625 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It turns riders upside down 4 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Yukon Striker is like

At 223 ft tall with 3,625 ft of track, Yukon Striker balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 245 ft drop is longer than its 223 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,625 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the 16th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 13 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Yukon Striker sits on the south side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vortex, roughly 70 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wonder Mountain's Guardian at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Yukon Striker

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Yukon Striker?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Yukon Striker. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Yukon Striker?

Yukon Striker is 223 ft tall, with a drop of 245 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Yukon Striker go?

Its top speed is 80.8 mph (130 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Yukon Striker go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Yukon Striker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Yukon Striker open?

Yukon Striker opened on 3 May 2019.

Who built Yukon Striker?

Yukon Striker was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Yukon Striker?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 25 seconds over 3,625 ft of track.

How does Yukon Striker get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Yukon Striker at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,310 riders.

Is Yukon Striker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 80.8 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
