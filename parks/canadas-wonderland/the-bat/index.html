The Bat

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1987.

The Bat is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1987. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding The Bat is like

For a 116.5 ft ride, 935 ft of track is short: The Bat is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1987, it is the 6th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It is one of 27 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas and Flashback at Six Flags New England. Height rules for this model differ by park: Canada's Wonderland sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Walibi Belgium sets 130 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 11 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Bat sits on the north side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Fly, roughly 100 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Thunder Run at about 130 m. The Bat layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How The Bat compares

On our intensity ranking The Bat is number 7 of 18 at Canada's Wonderland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 17 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Mighty Canadian Minebuster (90 ft tall, 55.9 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Dragon Fyre (78 ft tall, 50 mph, 4 inversions; 48 in minimum).

Before you ride The Bat

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Canada's Wonderland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Bat?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Bat, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Bat?

The Bat is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Bat go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Bat go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is The Bat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Bat open?

The Bat opened in 1987.

Who built The Bat?

The Bat was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on The Bat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does The Bat get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Bat at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is The Bat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is The Bat at Canada's Wonderland the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Canada's Wonderland ride opened in 1987 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; Canada's Wonderland sets 48 in and Bellewaerde sets 130 cm.
