Dragon Fyre

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50 mph; 78 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1981.

Dragon Fyre is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 1981 and was previously known as Dragon Fire. The ride stands 78 ft tall, drops 76 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,160 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Dragon Fyre is like

At 78 ft tall with 2,160 ft of track, Dragon Fyre balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,160 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1981, it is the oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It has run under other names (Dragon Fire), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 11 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragon Fyre sits on the north side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Leviathan, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wilde Beast at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Dragon Fyre

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Canada's Wonderland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragon Fyre?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Dragon Fyre, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dragon Fyre?

Dragon Fyre is 78 ft tall, with a drop of 76 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dragon Fyre go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dragon Fyre go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Dragon Fyre scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dragon Fyre open?

Dragon Fyre opened in 1981 (previously known as Dragon Fire).

Who built Dragon Fyre?

Dragon Fyre was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Dragon Fyre?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2,160 ft of track.

How does Dragon Fyre get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dragon Fyre at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Dragon Fyre good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
