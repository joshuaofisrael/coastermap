Wonder Mountain's Guardian

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; 60 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Wonder Mountain's Guardian is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by ART Engineering GmbH. It opened on 24 May 2014. The ride stands 60 ft tall and runs 1,000 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wonder Mountain's Guardian is like

At 60 ft tall with 1,000 ft of track, Wonder Mountain's Guardian balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2014, it is the 15th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 11 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wonder Mountain's Guardian sits near the middle of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Thunder Run, roughly 40 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Fly at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wonder Mountain's Guardian?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wonder Mountain's Guardian, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wonder Mountain's Guardian?

Wonder Mountain's Guardian is 60 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Wonder Mountain's Guardian go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wonder Mountain's Guardian scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wonder Mountain's Guardian open?

Wonder Mountain's Guardian opened on 24 May 2014.

Who built Wonder Mountain's Guardian?

Wonder Mountain's Guardian was manufactured by ART Engineering GmbH.

How long is the track on Wonder Mountain's Guardian?

RCDB lists 1,000 ft (305 m) of track.

How does Wonder Mountain's Guardian get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wonder Mountain's Guardian at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 650 riders.

Is Wonder Mountain's Guardian good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
