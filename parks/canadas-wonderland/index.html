Canada's Wonderland roller coasters

Vaughan, Ontario · 18 coasters

Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario has 18 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Leviathan at 306 ft, which is also the fastest at 92 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Taxi Jam.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Canada's Wonderland have?

Canada's Wonderland has 18 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland?

Leviathan, at 306 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland?

Leviathan, with a top speed of 92 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Canada's Wonderland?

By height requirement, Taxi Jam has the lowest minimum at 36 inches (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Canada's Wonderland coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Vortex, Taxi Jam. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Canada's Wonderland coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Dragon Fyre, Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast, Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, The Bat, Vortex, Taxi Jam, Silver Streak, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Wonder Mountain's Guardian, Snoopy's Racing Railway. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Canada's Wonderland do not go upside down?

Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast, Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, Vortex, Taxi Jam, The Fly, Silver Streak, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Behemoth, Leviathan, Wonder Mountain's Guardian, Snoopy's Racing Railway have no inversions, per RCDB.
