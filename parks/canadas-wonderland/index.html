Canada's Wonderland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Vaughan, Ontario · 18 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario has 18 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Taxi Jam to 54 in (137 cm) on The Fly. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 12 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 18 of them.

What the coasters at Canada's Wonderland are like

RCDB classes 5 coasters here as kiddie or family, 4 as thrill and 9 as extreme, which makes Canada's Wonderland a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 44% across all 148 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 13 of the 18 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 18 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast and Ghoster Coaster); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Snoopy's Racing Railway).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Dragon Fyre, which opened in 1981.

Leviathan is the 8th tallest of the 800 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Leviathan is the 7th fastest of the 786 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Canada's Wonderland? By height

At Canada's Wonderland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 10 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 18 coasters with a verified rule: Taxi Jam. With a companion they can also ride Snoopy's Racing Railway (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 16 coasters, the nearest being Vortex at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Vortex. With a companion they can also ride Ghoster Coaster (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)), Thunder Run (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)), Wonder Mountain's Guardian (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Snoopy's Racing Railway (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 12 coasters, the nearest being Silver Streak at 44 in (112 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 12 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 10 more than at the height below: Dragon Fyre, Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast, Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, The Bat, Silver Streak, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Wonder Mountain's Guardian and Snoopy's Racing Railway. With a companion they can also ride The Fly (44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being DareDeviler at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 18 of 18 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: DareDeviler, The Fly, Behemoth, Leviathan, Yukon Striker and AlpenFury. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: DareDeviler (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Silver Streak (up to 76 in (193 cm)), Behemoth (up to 80 in (203 cm)), Leviathan (up to 80 in (203 cm)), Yukon Striker (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and AlpenFury (up to 81 in (206 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Canada's Wonderland

These are the rules Canada's Wonderland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Ghoster Coaster: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Thunder Run: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Fly: riders 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wonder Mountain's Guardian: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Snoopy's Racing Railway: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Canada's Wonderland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Canada's Wonderland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Taxi Jam, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wonder Mountain's Guardian, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 60 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: AlpenFury, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 164 ft tall, 71.5 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Canada's Wonderland?

Roller coaster height requirements at Canada's Wonderland run from 36 in (91 cm) (Taxi Jam) to 54 in (137 cm) (The Fly), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Canada's Wonderland have?

Canada's Wonderland has 18 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is AlpenFury (164 ft tall, 71.5 mph, 7 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland?

Leviathan, at 306 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland?

Leviathan, with a top speed of 92 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Canada's Wonderland for first-timers?

Taxi Jam is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 36 in (91 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Canada's Wonderland?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Vortex, Taxi Jam. With a companion they can also ride Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, Wonder Mountain's Guardian, Snoopy's Racing Railway.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Canada's Wonderland?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Dragon Fyre, Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast, Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, The Bat, Vortex, Taxi Jam, Silver Streak, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Wonder Mountain's Guardian, Snoopy's Racing Railway. With a companion they can also ride The Fly.

Which coasters at Canada's Wonderland do not go upside down?

Mighty Canadian Minebuster, Wilde Beast, Ghoster Coaster, Thunder Run, Vortex, Taxi Jam, The Fly, Silver Streak, Backlot Stunt Coaster, Behemoth, Leviathan, Wonder Mountain's Guardian, Snoopy's Racing Railway have no inversions, per RCDB.
