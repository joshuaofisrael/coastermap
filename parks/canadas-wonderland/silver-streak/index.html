Silver Streak

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 44 in (112 cm); top speed 26 mph; 48.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Silver Streak is a steel inverted roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Vekoma. It opened on 6 May 2001. The ride stands 48.6 ft tall, reaches 26 mph and runs 1,122 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Silver Streak is like

At 48.6 ft tall with 1,122 ft of track, Silver Streak balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2001, it is the 11th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 342m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Woodstock's Air Rail at Kings Island, Kiddy Hawk at Carowinds, The Bat at Lagoon and Kenny's Forest Flyer at Dreamworld. Height rules for this model differ by park: Canada's Wonderland sets 44 in, while Lagoon sets 42 in and Dreamworld sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 44 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Silver Streak sits on the west side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Taxi Jam, roughly 120 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ghoster Coaster at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Silver Streak?

Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall to ride Silver Streak. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Silver Streak?

Silver Streak is 48.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Silver Streak go?

Its top speed is 26 mph (42 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Silver Streak go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Silver Streak scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Silver Streak open?

Silver Streak opened on 6 May 2001.

Who built Silver Streak?

Silver Streak was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Silver Streak at Canada's Wonderland the same as Woodstock's Air Rail at Kings Island?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 342m coasters on RCDB; the Canada's Wonderland ride opened in 2001 and the Kings Island ride in 2001; both list a 44 in minimum.
