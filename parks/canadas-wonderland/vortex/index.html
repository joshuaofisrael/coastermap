Vortex

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 55 mph; 91 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1991.

Vortex is a steel suspended roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 12 May 1991. The ride stands 91 ft tall, drops 85 ft, reaches 55 mph and runs 2,361 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vortex is like

At 91 ft tall with 2,361 ft of track, Vortex balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1991, it is the 7th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Suspended Coaster design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Iron Dragon at Cedar Point, Ninja at Magic Mountain, The Bat at Kings Island and Vampire at Chessington. Height rules for this model differ by park: Canada's Wonderland sets 42 in, while Chessington sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Canada's Wonderland, out of 17 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vortex sits on the south side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Yukon Striker, roughly 70 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wonder Mountain's Guardian at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vortex?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Vortex, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vortex?

Vortex is 91 ft tall, with a drop of 85 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Vortex go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vortex go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vortex scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Vortex open?

Vortex opened on 12 May 1991.

Who built Vortex?

Vortex was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Vortex?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2,361 ft of track.

How does Vortex get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vortex at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

What restraints does Vortex use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Vortex good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Vortex at Canada's Wonderland the same as Iron Dragon at Cedar Point?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Suspended Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Canada's Wonderland ride opened in 1991 and the Cedar Point ride in 1987; both list a 42 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 91 ft and 76 ft.
