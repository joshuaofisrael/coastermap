Leviathan

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 92 mph; 306 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Leviathan is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 6 May 2012. The ride stands 306 ft tall, drops 306 ft, reaches 92 mph and runs 5,486 ft of track. That height makes it a giga coaster (300 to 399 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Leviathan is like

At 306 ft tall with 5,486 ft of track, Leviathan balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2012, it is the 14th oldest of the 18 coasters we list at Canada's Wonderland. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the 11th of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island and Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, Europa-Park sets 140 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Canada's Wonderland, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Leviathan sits on the north-east side of Canada's Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon Fyre, roughly 120 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wilde Beast at about 310 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Leviathan compares

On our intensity ranking Leviathan is number 3 of 18 at Canada's Wonderland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 17 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Leviathan?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Leviathan. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Leviathan?

Leviathan is 306 ft tall, with a drop of 306 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Leviathan go?

Its top speed is 92 mph (148 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Leviathan go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Leviathan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Leviathan open?

Leviathan opened on 6 May 2012.

Who built Leviathan?

Leviathan was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Leviathan?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 8 seconds over 5,486 ft of track.

How does Leviathan get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Leviathan at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train.

Is Leviathan good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 92 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Leviathan at Canada's Wonderland the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Canada's Wonderland ride opened in 2012 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 306 ft and 170 ft.
