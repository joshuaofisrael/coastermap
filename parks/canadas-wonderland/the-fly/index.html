The Fly

Canada's Wonderland · Vaughan, Ontario

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 51.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

The Fly is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Canada's Wonderland in Vaughan, Ontario, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 2 May 1999. The ride stands 51.8 ft tall, drops 49.8 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Fly?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride The Fly, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion, according to Canada's Wonderland, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Fly?

The Fly is 51.8 ft tall, with a drop of 49.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Fly go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Fly go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Fly scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Fly open?

The Fly opened on 2 May 1999.

Who built The Fly?

The Fly was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
