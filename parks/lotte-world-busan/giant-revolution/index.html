Giant Revolution

Lotte World Adventure Busan · Gijang, Busan, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 68.4 mph; 164 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2026.

Giant Revolution is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lotte World Adventure Busan in Gijang, Busan, South Korea, built by Zamperla. It opened on 8 Oct 2026. The ride stands 164 ft tall, reaches 68.4 mph and runs 1066.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Giant Revolution is like

For a 164 ft ride, 1,066.3 ft of track is short: Giant Revolution is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,066.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Lotte World Adventure Busan.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Lotte World Busan (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Written from RCDB statistics and the park's published height rule; it describes the layout, not a review.

Giant Revolution layout, trains and ride system

Giant Revolution starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 3 elements RCDB records on Giant Revolution's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. Immelmann
  3. Dive Drop

Giant Revolution trains and ride system

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Giant Revolution compares

On our intensity ranking Giant Revolution is number 2 of 4 at Lotte World Adventure Busan, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Giant Splash (147.6 ft tall, 62.8 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Giant Digger (124.7 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 4 inversions, 3.8 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Giant Revolution

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lotte World Adventure Busan app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Giant Revolution?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Giant Revolution, according to Lotte World Adventure Busan, official attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Giant Revolution?

Giant Revolution is 164 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Giant Revolution go?

Its top speed is 68.4 mph (110 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Giant Revolution go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Giant Revolution scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Giant Revolution open?

Giant Revolution opened on 8 Oct 2026.

Who built Giant Revolution?

Giant Revolution was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Giant Revolution?

RCDB lists a ride time of 53 seconds over 1066.3 ft of track.

Does Giant Revolution launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Giant Revolution at once?

Each train carries 32 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

What restraints does Giant Revolution use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Giant Revolution good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 68.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
