Lotte World Adventure Busan height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Gijang, Busan, South Korea · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Lotte World Adventure Busan in Gijang, Busan, South Korea has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Giant Splash to 135 cm (about 53.1 in) on Mysterious Cookie Train. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Lotte World Adventure Busan are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Lotte World Adventure Busan a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 75% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 4 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Giant Digger).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Giant Splash, which opened in 2022.

Lotte World Adventure Busan publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Lotte World Adventure Busan? By height

At Lotte World Adventure Busan the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Lotte World Adventure Busan for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Lotte World Adventure Busan, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mysterious Cookie Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (135 cm minimum; 32.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. The big one: Giant Digger, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 124.7 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 4 inversions, 3.8 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
