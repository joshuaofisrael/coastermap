Mysterious Cookie Train

Lotte World Adventure Busan · Gijang, Busan, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 135 cm (about 53.1 in); 32.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Mysterious Cookie Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lotte World Adventure Busan in Gijang, Busan, South Korea, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 31 Mar 2022. The ride stands 32.8 ft tall and runs 689 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mysterious Cookie Train is like

At 32.8 ft tall with 689 ft of track, Mysterious Cookie Train balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2022, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lotte World Adventure Busan.

Its 135 cm minimum is the strictest at Lotte World Busan. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mysterious Cookie Train?

Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall to ride Mysterious Cookie Train, according to Lotte World Adventure Busan, official attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mysterious Cookie Train?

Mysterious Cookie Train is 32.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Mysterious Cookie Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mysterious Cookie Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Mysterious Cookie Train open?

Mysterious Cookie Train opened on 31 Mar 2022.

Who built Mysterious Cookie Train?

Mysterious Cookie Train was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How long is the track on Mysterious Cookie Train?

RCDB lists 689 ft (210 m) of track.

How many people ride Mysterious Cookie Train at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Mysterious Cookie Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 135 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
