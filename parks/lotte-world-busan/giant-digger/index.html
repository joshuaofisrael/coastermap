Giant Digger

Lotte World Adventure Busan · Gijang, Busan, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 124.7 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2022.

Giant Digger is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lotte World Adventure Busan in Gijang, Busan, South Korea, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 31 Mar 2022. The ride stands 124.7 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3464.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Giant Digger is like

At 124.7 ft tall with 3,464.6 ft of track, Giant Digger balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,464.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2022, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lotte World Adventure Busan. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Launch Coaster / Blue Fire design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park and Velociraptor at IMG Worlds. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Lotte World Busan, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Giant Splash, roughly 300 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Giant Digger layout, trains and ride system

Giant Digger starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (5 entries):

  1. LSM Launch
  2. Loop (105 ft tall)
  3. Block Brake
  4. Twisted Horseshoe Roll
  5. In-Line Twist

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Giant Digger

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lotte World Adventure Busan app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Giant Digger?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Giant Digger, according to Lotte World Adventure Busan, official attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Giant Digger?

Giant Digger is 124.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Giant Digger go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Giant Digger go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Giant Digger scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Giant Digger open?

Giant Digger opened on 31 Mar 2022.

Who built Giant Digger?

Giant Digger was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Giant Digger?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 3464.6 ft of track.

Does Giant Digger launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Giant Digger at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 830 riders.

Is Giant Digger good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Giant Digger at Lotte World Busan the same as Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Launch Coaster / Blue Fire coasters on RCDB; the Lotte World Busan ride opened in 2022 and the Europa-Park ride in 2009; both list a 130 cm minimum.
