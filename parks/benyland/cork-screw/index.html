Cork Screw

Yagiyama Benyland · Sendai, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); 70 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1980.

Cork Screw is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yagiyama Benyland in Sendai, Japan, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 27 Apr 1980. The ride stands 70 ft tall and runs 1,200 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Cork Screw is like

At 70 ft tall with 1,200 ft of track, Cork Screw balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,200 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1980, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Yagiyama Benyland. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure, Corkscrew at Silverwood, Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land and Corkscrew at Rusutsu. Height rules for this model differ by park: Benyland sets 110 cm, while Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in, Silverwood sets 48 in and Rusutsu sets 125 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the strictest at Benyland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cork Screw sits on the south-east side of Benyland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Cyclone, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aero 5 at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Cork Screw

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Corkscrew. No companion is required.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Yagiyama Benyland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cork Screw?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Cork Screw. The park lists it as Corkscrew. No companion is required, according to Yagiyama Benyland, official attraction list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cork Screw?

Cork Screw is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Cork Screw go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Cork Screw scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Cork Screw open?

Cork Screw opened on 27 Apr 1980.

Who built Cork Screw?

Cork Screw was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Cork Screw?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 26 seconds over 1,200 ft of track.

How does Cork Screw get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Cork Screw at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

What restraints does Cork Screw use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Cork Screw good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Cork Screw at Benyland the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Benyland ride opened in 1980 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; Benyland sets 110 cm and Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in.
