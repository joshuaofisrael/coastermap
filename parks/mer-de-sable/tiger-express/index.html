Tiger Express

La Mer de Sable · Ermenonville, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Tiger Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at La Mer de Sable in Ermenonville, France, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 26 Jun 2011. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tiger Express is like

At 45.9 ft tall with 1,213.9 ft of track, Tiger Express balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2011, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at La Mer de Sable. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Park design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Ricochet at Carowinds and Ghost Chasers at Movie Park Germany. Height rules for this model differ by park: Mer de Sable sets 140 cm, while Carowinds sets 54 in and Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Mer de Sable. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tiger Express sits on the north-west side of Mer de Sable's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Buffalo, roughly 160 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Silver Mountain at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tiger Express?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Tiger Express, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to La Mer de Sable, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tiger Express?

Tiger Express is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tiger Express go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tiger Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tiger Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tiger Express open?

Tiger Express opened on 26 Jun 2011.

Who built Tiger Express?

Tiger Express was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Tiger Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 25 seconds over 1213.9 ft of track.

How does Tiger Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tiger Express at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,120 riders.

Is Tiger Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Tiger Express operate before?

RCDB records it at Walibi Holland (as Flying Dutchman Gold Mine); Mer de Sable (as Tiger Express).

Is Tiger Express at Mer de Sable the same as Ghost Chasers at Movie Park Germany?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Park coasters on RCDB; the Mer de Sable ride opened in 2011 and the Movie Park Germany ride in 2000; Mer de Sable sets 140 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm.
