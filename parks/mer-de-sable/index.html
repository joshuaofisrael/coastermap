La Mer de Sable height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ermenonville, France · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

La Mer de Sable in Ermenonville, France has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Bandidos to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Tiger Express. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at La Mer de Sable are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes La Mer de Sable a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Wild Buffalo).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tiger Express, which opened in 2011.

La Mer de Sable publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at La Mer de Sable? By height

At La Mer de Sable the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Bandidos at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Bandidos (95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in)) and Silver Mountain (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Wild Buffalo at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Bandidos and Silver Mountain. With a companion they can also ride Tiger Express (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)) and Wild Buffalo (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Wild Buffalo. With a companion they can also ride Tiger Express (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Bandidos (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at La Mer de Sable

These are the rules La Mer de Sable states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Tiger Express: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Bandidos: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Silver Mountain: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wild Buffalo: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at La Mer de Sable for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for La Mer de Sable, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Bandidos, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wild Buffalo, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 48.6 ft tall, 41.6 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Tiger Express, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 45.9 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
