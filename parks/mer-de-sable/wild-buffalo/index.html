Wild Buffalo

La Mer de Sable · Ermenonville, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 41.6 mph; 48.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Wild Buffalo is a wood sit-down roller coaster at La Mer de Sable in Ermenonville, France, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 26 Apr 2025. The ride stands 48.6 ft tall, reaches 41.6 mph and runs 1,643 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wild Buffalo is like

With 1,643 ft of track for a 48.6 ft structure, Wild Buffalo is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at La Mer de Sable.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Mer de Sable, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wild Buffalo sits near the middle of Mer de Sable's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tiger Express, roughly 160 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Bandidos at about 210 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wild Buffalo?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Wild Buffalo, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to La Mer de Sable, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wild Buffalo?

Wild Buffalo is 48.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wild Buffalo go?

Its top speed is 41.6 mph (67 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wild Buffalo go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wild Buffalo scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wild Buffalo open?

Wild Buffalo opened on 26 Apr 2025.

Who built Wild Buffalo?

Wild Buffalo was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Wild Buffalo?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds over 1,643 ft of track.

How does Wild Buffalo get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wild Buffalo at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Wild Buffalo good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 41.6 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
