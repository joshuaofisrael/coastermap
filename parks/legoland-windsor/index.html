Legoland Windsor height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Windsor, England · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Legoland Windsor in Windsor, England has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on DUPLO Dino Coaster to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on The Dragon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Legoland Windsor are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Legoland Windsor a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 45% of coasters across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is WGH Transportation, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Dragon, which opened in 1998.

Legoland Windsor publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Legoland Windsor? By height

At Legoland Windsor the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Legoland Windsor for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Legoland Windsor, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: DUPLO Dino Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 9.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Dragon's Apprentice, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Minifigure Speedway, the most intense coaster in the park (105 cm minimum; 34.8 mph, no inversions).
