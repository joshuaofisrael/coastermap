Olandese Volante

MagicLand · Valmontone, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Olandese Volante is a steel sit-down roller coaster at MagicLand in Valmontone, Italy, built by Vekoma. It opened on 26 May 2011. The ride runs 2234.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Olandese Volante is like

Opened in 2011, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at MagicLand.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at MagicLand, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Olandese Volante sits on the north side of MagicLand's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cagliostro, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Shock at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Olandese Volante

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders under 130 cm must be accompanied (minimum 110 cm, minimum age 4).
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Olandese Volante?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Olandese Volante, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders under 130 cm must be accompanied (minimum 110 cm, minimum age 4), according to MagicLand, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Olandese Volante go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Olandese Volante scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Olandese Volante open?

Olandese Volante opened on 26 May 2011.

Who built Olandese Volante?

Olandese Volante was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Olandese Volante?

RCDB lists 2234.3 ft of track.

How does Olandese Volante get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Olandese Volante good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
