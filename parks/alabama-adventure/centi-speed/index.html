Centi-SPEED

Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure · Bessemer, Alabama

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Centi-SPEED is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure in Bessemer, Alabama, built by Fajume. It opened in 2014. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Centi-SPEED is like

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Fajume's Wacky Worm design, and the first of them to open. Others include Wacky Worm at Santa's Village. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 42 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Alabama Adventure, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Centi-SPEED sits on the north-west side of Alabama Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cheddar Chase, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rampage at about 170 m. Before you ride Centi-SPEED

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 42 in (107 cm), and riders from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders from 36 inches may ride with a responsible person.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Centi-SPEED?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Centi-SPEED, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion. Riders from 36 inches may ride with a responsible person, according to Alabama Adventure, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Centi-SPEED go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Centi-SPEED scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Centi-SPEED open?

Centi-SPEED opened in 2014.

Who built Centi-SPEED?

Centi-SPEED was manufactured by Fajume.

How does Centi-SPEED get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Centi-SPEED good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Centi-SPEED operate before?

RCDB records it at Gillian's Wonderland Pier (as Wacky Worm); Gillian's FunLand (as Wacky Worm).

Is Centi-SPEED at Alabama Adventure the same as Wacky Worm at Santa's Village?

Both are Fajume Wacky Worm coasters on RCDB; the Alabama Adventure ride opened in 2014 and the Santa's Village ride in 2015; both list a 42 in minimum.
