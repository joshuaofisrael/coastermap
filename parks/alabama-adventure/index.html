Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bessemer, Alabama · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure in Bessemer, Alabama has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Centi-SPEED to 48 in (122 cm) on Rampage. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Rampage).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rampage, which opened in 1998.

What can my child ride at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure? By height

At Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Centi-SPEED (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Rampage at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Centi-SPEED. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Rampage at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Rampage and Cheddar Chase. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Rampage, Centi-SPEED and Cheddar Chase. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Centi-SPEED, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Cheddar Chase, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Rampage, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 120 ft tall, 56 mph, no inversions).
