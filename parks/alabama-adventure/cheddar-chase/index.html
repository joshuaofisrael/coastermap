Cheddar Chase

Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure · Bessemer, Alabama

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Cheddar Chase is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure in Bessemer, Alabama, built by L&T Systems. It opened on 2 Jul 2022. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cheddar Chase is like

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Alabama Adventure & Splash Adventure. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to L&T Systems's Wild Mouse / Wild Mouse 30x20 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Wild Mine at Cavallino Matto. Height rules for this model differ by park: Alabama Adventure sets 48 in, while Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Alabama Adventure, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cheddar Chase sits near the middle of Alabama Adventure's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rampage, roughly 130 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Centi-SPEED at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cheddar Chase?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Cheddar Chase, according to Alabama Adventure, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Cheddar Chase go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cheddar Chase scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cheddar Chase open?

Cheddar Chase opened on 2 Jul 2022.

Who built Cheddar Chase?

Cheddar Chase was manufactured by L&T Systems.

How does Cheddar Chase get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Cheddar Chase at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Cheddar Chase good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Cheddar Chase operate before?

RCDB records it at Lake Winnepesaukah (as Wild Lightnin').

Is Cheddar Chase at Alabama Adventure the same as Wild Mine at Cavallino Matto?

Both are L&T Systems Wild Mouse / Wild Mouse 30x20 coasters on RCDB; the Alabama Adventure ride opened in 2022 and the Cavallino Matto ride in 1997; Alabama Adventure sets 48 in and Cavallino Matto sets 130 cm.
