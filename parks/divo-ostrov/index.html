Divo Ostrov height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Divo Ostrov in Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Whirl Wind Looping Coaster to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Divo Ostrov are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Divo Ostrov a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 75% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 53.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 4 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Nesterov Loop).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster, which opened in 2003.

Divo Ostrov publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Maximum heights

A ride here also has an official maximum height: Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2 (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.
