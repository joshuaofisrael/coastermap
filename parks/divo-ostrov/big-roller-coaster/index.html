Big Roller Coaster

Divo Ostrov · Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 135 cm (about 53.1 in); top speed 54.1 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Big Roller Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Divo Ostrov in Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia, built by BHS. It opened in 2004. The ride reaches 54.1 mph and runs 3225.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Roller Coaster is like

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Divo Ostrov.

A rider who just meets its 135 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Divo Ostrov, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Roller Coaster sits near the middle of Divo Ostrov's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Big Roller Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 135 cm (about 53.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Большая Русская горка (the Big Russian Mountain), for riders from 1.35 m. No companion rule is stated.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Roller Coaster?

Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall to ride Big Roller Coaster. The park lists it as Большая Русская горка (the Big Russian Mountain), for riders from 1.35 m. No companion rule is stated, according to Divo Ostrov, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Big Roller Coaster go?

Its top speed is 54.1 mph (87 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Roller Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Roller Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Roller Coaster open?

Big Roller Coaster opened in 2004.

Who built Big Roller Coaster?

Big Roller Coaster was manufactured by BHS.

How long is the track on Big Roller Coaster?

RCDB lists 3225.1 ft of track.

How many people ride Big Roller Coaster at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,250 riders.

Is Big Roller Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 54.1 mph; the minimum height is 135 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Big Roller Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Kure Portopialand (as Andalusia Railroad); Wonder Island (as Big Roller Coaster).
