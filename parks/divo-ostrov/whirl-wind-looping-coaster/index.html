Whirl Wind Looping Coaster

Divo Ostrov · Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 64 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2003.

Whirl Wind Looping Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Divo Ostrov in Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia, built by Vekoma. It opened in 2003. The ride stands 64 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1148.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Whirl Wind Looping Coaster is like

Whirl Wind Looping Coaster pairs a 64 ft structure with 1,148.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,148.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2003, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Divo Ostrov. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Whirlwind design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include SpeedSnake FREE at Fort Fun, Rallus at Salitre Mágico and Bocaraca at Parque Diversiones. Height rules for this model differ by park: Divo Ostrov sets 110 cm, while Fort Fun sets 140 cm and Parque Diversiones sets 120 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Divo Ostrov, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Whirl Wind Looping Coaster sits on the east side of Divo Ostrov's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2, roughly 80 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Roller Coaster at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Whirl Wind Looping Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Whirl Wind Looping Coaster is number 3 of 4 at Divo Ostrov, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Big Roller Coaster (54.1 mph, no inversions; 135 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2 (108.3 ft tall, 52.8 mph, 1 inversion; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Whirl Wind Looping Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists this looping coaster as Русские горки (Russian Mountains) and describes a 20 m structure with loops, which matches RCDB's 64 ft (about 20 m), two-inversion Vekoma Whirlwind here. For riders from 1.1 m; no companion rule is stated.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Divo Ostrov app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Whirl Wind Looping Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Whirl Wind Looping Coaster. The park lists this looping coaster as Русские горки (Russian Mountains) and describes a 20 m structure with loops, which matches RCDB's 64 ft (about 20 m), two-inversion Vekoma Whirlwind here. For riders from 1.1 m; no companion rule is stated, according to Divo Ostrov, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster?

Whirl Wind Looping Coaster is 64 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Whirl Wind Looping Coaster go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Whirl Wind Looping Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Whirl Wind Looping Coaster open?

Whirl Wind Looping Coaster opened in 2003.

Who built Whirl Wind Looping Coaster?

Whirl Wind Looping Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Whirl Wind Looping Coaster?

RCDB lists 1148.3 ft of track.

How does Whirl Wind Looping Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Whirl Wind Looping Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Whirl Wind Looping Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Magic Land (as Corkscrew); Wonder Island (as Whirl Wind Looping Coaster).

Is Whirl Wind Looping Coaster at Divo Ostrov the same as SpeedSnake FREE at Fort Fun?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Whirlwind coasters on RCDB; the Divo Ostrov ride opened in 2003 and the Fort Fun ride in 1981; Divo Ostrov sets 110 cm and Fort Fun sets 140 cm.
