Nesterov Loop

Divo Ostrov · Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 124 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2015.

Nesterov Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Divo Ostrov in Krestovsky Island, Saint Petersburg, Russia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 15 May 2015 and was previously known as Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat. The ride stands 124 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3,464 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Nesterov Loop is like

At 124 ft tall with 3,464 ft of track, Nesterov Loop balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,464 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2015, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Divo Ostrov. It has run under other names (Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Launch Coaster / Blue Fire design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park, Velociraptor at IMG Worlds, Giant Digger at Lotte World Busan and Serpent Dragon at Sochi Park. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Divo Ostrov, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Nesterov Loop sits on the west side of Divo Ostrov's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Roller Coaster, roughly 320 m away (135 cm minimum). Next closest is Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat-2 at about 370 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Nesterov Loop layout, trains and ride system

Nesterov Loop starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Nesterov Loop's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 62.1 mph in 2.5 s)
  2. Loop (105 ft tall)
  3. Twisted Horseshoe Roll
  4. In-Line Twist

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Nesterov Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Горка «Петля Нестерова» (Nesterov Loop), for riders from 1.3 m. No companion rule is stated.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Divo Ostrov app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Nesterov Loop?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Nesterov Loop. The park lists it as Горка «Петля Нестерова» (Nesterov Loop), for riders from 1.3 m. No companion rule is stated, according to Divo Ostrov, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Nesterov Loop?

Nesterov Loop is 124 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nesterov Loop go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nesterov Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Nesterov Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Nesterov Loop open?

Nesterov Loop opened on 15 May 2015 (previously known as Velikolukskiy Myasokombinat).

Who built Nesterov Loop?

Nesterov Loop was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Nesterov Loop?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 3,464 ft of track.

Does Nesterov Loop launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Nesterov Loop at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 830 riders.

Is Nesterov Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Nesterov Loop at Divo Ostrov the same as Blue Fire Megacoaster at Europa-Park?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Launch Coaster / Blue Fire coasters on RCDB; the Divo Ostrov ride opened in 2015 and the Europa-Park ride in 2009; both list a 130 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 124 ft and 124.7 ft.
