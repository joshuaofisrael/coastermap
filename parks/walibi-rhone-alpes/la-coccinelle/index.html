La Coccinelle

Walibi Rhône-Alpes · Les Avenières, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

La Coccinelle is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes in Les Avenières, France, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1992 and was previously known as Coccinelle des Andes. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding La Coccinelle is like

With 1,181.1 ft of track for a 26.3 ft structure, La Coccinelle is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1992, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Walibi Rhône-Alpes. It has run under other names (Coccinelle des Andes), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 12 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure, Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom and Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, La Coccinelle sits on the north-east side of Walibi Rhône-Alpes' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mahuka, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Generator at about 200 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for La Coccinelle?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride La Coccinelle, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must be accompanied by an adult, according to Walibi Rhône-Alpes, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is La Coccinelle?

La Coccinelle is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does La Coccinelle go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does La Coccinelle go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is La Coccinelle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did La Coccinelle open?

La Coccinelle opened in 1992 (previously known as Coccinelle des Andes).

Who built La Coccinelle?

La Coccinelle was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on La Coccinelle?

RCDB lists 1181.1 ft of track.

How does La Coccinelle get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is La Coccinelle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is La Coccinelle at Walibi Rhône-Alpes the same as #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Walibi Rhône-Alpes ride opened in 1992 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1976; both list a 120 cm minimum.
