Walibi Rhône-Alpes height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Les Avenières, France · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Walibi Rhône-Alpes in Les Avenières, France has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on La Coccinelle to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Generator. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Walibi Rhône-Alpes are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Walibi Rhône-Alpes a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 44% across all 139 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Timber).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Generator, which opened in 1988.

Walibi Rhône-Alpes publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Walibi Rhône-Alpes? By height

At Walibi Rhône-Alpes the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride La Coccinelle (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Woodstock Express at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride La Coccinelle (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Woodstock Express at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Generator and Mystic. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Mystic (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Mahuka (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Walibi Rhône-Alpes for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Walibi Rhône-Alpes, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: La Coccinelle, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Timber, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 55.8 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Mystic, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 101.7 ft tall, 52.8 mph, 3 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
