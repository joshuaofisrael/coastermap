Woodstock Express

Walibi Rhône-Alpes · Les Avenières, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Woodstock Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes in Les Avenières, France, built by Zamperla. It first opened in 2002 and was previously known as Zig Zag and later Scratch. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Woodstock Express is like

Opened in 2002, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Walibi Rhône-Alpes. It has run under other names (Zig Zag, then Scratch), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Zig Zag Coaster / 270m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Toboggan Nordique at La Ronde, Doo Wopper at Morey's Piers and Twist 'n' Shout at Family Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm, while La Ronde sets 48 in and Morey's Piers sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Woodstock Express sits on the west side of Walibi Rhône-Alpes' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Timber, roughly 40 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Generator at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Woodstock Express compares

On our intensity ranking Woodstock Express is number 5 of 6 at Walibi Rhône-Alpes, where 1 is the most intense.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Woodstock Express?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Woodstock Express, according to Walibi Rhône-Alpes, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Woodstock Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Woodstock Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Woodstock Express open?

Woodstock Express opened in 2002 (previously known as Zig Zag and later Scratch).

Who built Woodstock Express?

Woodstock Express was manufactured by Zamperla.

How does Woodstock Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Woodstock Express at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Woodstock Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Woodstock Express at Walibi Rhône-Alpes the same as Toboggan Nordique at La Ronde?

Both are Zamperla Zig Zag Coaster / 270m coasters on RCDB; the Walibi Rhône-Alpes ride opened in 2002 and the La Ronde ride in 2003; Walibi Rhône-Alpes sets 120 cm and La Ronde sets 48 in.
