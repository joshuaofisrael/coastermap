Mahuka

Walibi Rhône-Alpes · Les Avenières, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 41.6 mph; 59.1 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2024.

Mahuka is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes in Les Avenières, France, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 15 Jun 2024. The ride stands 59.1 ft tall, reaches 41.6 mph and runs 2011.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Mahuka is like

With 2,011.2 ft of track for a 59.1 ft structure, Mahuka is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,011.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Walibi Rhône-Alpes.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Rhône-Alpes (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mahuka sits on the east side of Walibi Rhône-Alpes' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is La Coccinelle, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mystic at about 220 m. Mahuka layout, trains and ride system

Mahuka starts with a tyre-driven launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Tire Propelled Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (5 entries):

  1. Tire Propelled Launch (rolling)
  2. Top Hat - Inside
  3. Corkscrew
  4. Tire Propelled Boost
  5. Heartline Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Mahuka compares

On our intensity ranking Mahuka is number 3 of 6 at Walibi Rhône-Alpes, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Timber (55.8 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Generator (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 130 cm minimum).

Site-wide, Mahuka is taller than 33% and faster than 39% of the 917 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Mahuka

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Walibi Rhône-Alpes app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mahuka?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Mahuka. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Walibi Rhône-Alpes, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mahuka?

Mahuka is 59.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mahuka go?

Its top speed is 41.6 mph (67 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mahuka go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Mahuka scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mahuka open?

Mahuka opened on 15 Jun 2024.

Who built Mahuka?

Mahuka was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Mahuka?

RCDB lists 2011.2 ft of track.

Does Mahuka launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Mahuka at once?

Up to 9 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Mahuka good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 41.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
