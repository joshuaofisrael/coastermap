Hype

Särkänniemi · Tampere, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 62 mph; 150 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2017.

Hype is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Särkänniemi in Tampere, Finland, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 18 Jun 2017. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 62 mph and runs 863 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Hype is like

For a 150 ft ride, 863 ft of track is short: Hype is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2017, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Särkänniemi. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's Sky Rocket / II design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Tigris at Busch Gardens Tampa, Tempesto at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom and Electric Eel at SeaWorld San Diego. Height rules for this model differ by park: Särkänniemi sets 140 cm, while Busch Gardens Tampa sets 54 in, Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 54 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Särkänniemi, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hype sits on the north-east side of Särkänniemi's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vauhtimato, roughly 40 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Konect at about 90 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Hype layout, trains and ride system

Hype starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Hype, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. In-Line Twist
  3. Non Inverting Loop

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Hype

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Särkänniemi app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hype?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Hype, according to Särkänniemi, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hype?

Hype is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hype go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hype go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Hype scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hype open?

Hype opened on 18 Jun 2017.

Who built Hype?

Hype was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Hype?

RCDB lists a ride time of 55 seconds over 863 ft of track.

Does Hype launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Hype at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 500 riders.

Is Hype good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 62 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Hype at Särkänniemi the same as Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Premier Rides Sky Rocket / II coasters on RCDB; the Särkänniemi ride opened in 2017 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2012; Särkänniemi sets 140 cm and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.
