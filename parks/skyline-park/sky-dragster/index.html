Sky Dragster

Skyline Park · Bad Wörishofen, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 37.3 mph; 39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Sky Dragster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Skyline Park in Bad Wörishofen, Germany, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 6 Jun 2017. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 889.1 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sky Dragster is like

Sky Dragster pairs a 39.4 ft structure with 889.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 1.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2017, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Skyline Park.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sky Dragster sits on the south-east side of Skyline Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Berg & Tal Hetz, roughly 260 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kids Spin at about 370 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Sky Dragster?

Sky Dragster is 39.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sky Dragster go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sky Dragster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sky Dragster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Sky Dragster open?

Sky Dragster opened on 6 Jun 2017.

Who built Sky Dragster?

Sky Dragster was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Sky Dragster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 35 seconds over 889.1 ft of track.

How many people ride Sky Dragster at once?

Each train carries 2 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 400 riders.

Is Sky Dragster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
