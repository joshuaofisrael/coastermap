Skyline Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bad Wörishofen, Germany · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Skyline Park in Bad Wörishofen, Germany has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Kids Spin to 150 cm (about 59.1 in) on Sky Wheel. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Skyline Park are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Skyline Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 17% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 6 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Berg & Tal Hetz).

The most common builder is SBF Visa Group, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sky Rider, which opened in 2001.

Skyline Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Skyline Park? By height

At Skyline Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Flotter Otto (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Kids Spin at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Kids Spin (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)) and Flotter Otto (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Berg & Tal Hetz at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Kids Spin and Flotter Otto. With a companion they can also ride Berg & Tal Hetz (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)) and Sky Rider (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Sky Wheel at 150 cm (about 59.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Berg & Tal Hetz. With a companion they can also ride Sky Rider (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Sky Wheel at 150 cm (about 59.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Sky Wheel (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Skyline Park

These are the rules Skyline Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Kids Spin: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Berg & Tal Hetz: riders 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Flotter Otto: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Sky Rider: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Sky Wheel: The park lists the ride from age 12.

Best coasters at Skyline Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Skyline Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Kids Spin, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Sky Rider, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Sky Wheel, the most intense coaster in the park (150 cm minimum; 151.6 ft tall, 65.3 mph, 2 inversions, 5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Skyline Park?

Roller coaster height requirements at Skyline Park run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Kids Spin) to 150 cm (about 59.1 in) (Sky Wheel), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Skyline Park have?

Skyline Park has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Skyline Park?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Sky Wheel (151.6 ft tall, 65.3 mph, 2 inversions, 5 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Skyline Park?
