Sky Rider

Skyline Park · Bad Wörishofen, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 31.1 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Sky Rider is a steel suspended roller coaster at Skyline Park in Bad Wörishofen, Germany, built by Caripro. It opened in 2001. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 1148.3 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sky Rider is like

Sky Rider pairs a 65.6 ft structure with 1,148.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2001, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Skyline Park.

A rider who just meets its 140 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Skyline Park, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sky Rider sits on the west side of Skyline Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flotter Otto, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sky Wheel at about 180 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Sky Rider

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists the ride from age 6; riders up to 10 years old must ride with an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Skyline Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sky Rider?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Sky Rider, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The park lists the ride from age 6; riders up to 10 years old must ride with an adult, according to Skyline Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sky Rider?

Sky Rider is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sky Rider go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sky Rider go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sky Rider scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Sky Rider open?

Sky Rider opened in 2001.

Who built Sky Rider?

Sky Rider was manufactured by Caripro.

How long is the track on Sky Rider?

RCDB lists 1148.3 ft of track.

How does Sky Rider get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

Is Sky Rider good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
