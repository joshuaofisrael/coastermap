Flotter Otto

Skyline Park · Bad Wörishofen, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 19.9 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Flotter Otto is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Skyline Park in Bad Wörishofen, Germany, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 25 Mar 2023. The ride reaches 19.9 mph and runs 442.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flotter Otto is like

Opened in 2023, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Skyline Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Double Mine Train Extended design, and the first of them to open. Others include Car City at Fantasilandia. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Skyline Park (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flotter Otto sits on the west side of Skyline Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sky Rider, roughly 80 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sky Wheel at about 120 m. Flotter Otto layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flotter Otto?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Flotter Otto, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park lists the ride from age 2, according to Skyline Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Flotter Otto go?

Its top speed is 19.9 mph (32 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flotter Otto go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flotter Otto scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Flotter Otto open?

Flotter Otto opened on 25 Mar 2023.

Who built Flotter Otto?

Flotter Otto was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Flotter Otto?

RCDB lists 442.9 ft of track.

How does Flotter Otto get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Flotter Otto good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 19.9 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Flotter Otto at Skyline Park the same as Car City at Fantasilandia?

Both are SBF Visa Group Double Mine Train Extended coasters on RCDB; the Skyline Park ride opened in 2023 and the Fantasilandia ride in 2024; both list a 120 cm minimum.
