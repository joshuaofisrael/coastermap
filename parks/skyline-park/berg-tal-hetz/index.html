Berg & Tal Hetz

Skyline Park · Bad Wörishofen, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 31.3 mph; 49.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Berg & Tal Hetz is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Skyline Park in Bad Wörishofen, Germany, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 13 Aug 2024. The ride stands 49.2 ft tall, reaches 31.3 mph and runs 853 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Berg & Tal Hetz is like

At 49.2 ft tall with 853 ft of track, Berg & Tal Hetz balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Skyline Park.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Skyline Park, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Berg & Tal Hetz sits on the north-east side of Skyline Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kids Spin, roughly 190 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sky Dragster at about 260 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Berg & Tal Hetz?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Berg & Tal Hetz, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists the ride from age 6, according to Skyline Park, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Berg & Tal Hetz?

Berg & Tal Hetz is 49.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Berg & Tal Hetz go?

Its top speed is 31.3 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Berg & Tal Hetz go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Berg & Tal Hetz scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Berg & Tal Hetz open?

Berg & Tal Hetz opened on 13 Aug 2024.

Who built Berg & Tal Hetz?

Berg & Tal Hetz was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Berg & Tal Hetz?

RCDB lists 853 ft (260 m) of track.

Does Berg & Tal Hetz launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Berg & Tal Hetz at once?

Up to 14 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Berg & Tal Hetz good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
