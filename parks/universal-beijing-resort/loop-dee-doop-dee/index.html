Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee

Universal Studios Beijing · Tongzhou, Beijing, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in), or 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) with a companion; top speed 21.1 mph; 31.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Beijing in Tongzhou, Beijing, China, built by Jinma Rides. It opened on 20 Sep 2021. The ride stands 31.2 ft tall, reaches 21.1 mph and runs 761.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee is like

At 31.2 ft tall with 761.2 ft of track, Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Studios Beijing.

Its 122 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Beijing (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee sits on the south side of Universal Studios Beijing's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jurassic Flyers, roughly 240 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flight of the Hippogriff at about 310 m. Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee trains and ride system

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall, or 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) with a companion, and no taller than 190 cm (about 74.8 in).

Before you ride Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in), and riders from 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Guests must be between 92 cm and 190 cm; children between 92 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Studios Beijing app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee, or 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). Guests must be between 92 cm and 190 cm; children between 92 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg, according to Universal Beijing Resort, official ride page (accessibility information) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee?

Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee is 31.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee go?

Its top speed is 21.1 mph (34 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee open?

Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee opened on 20 Sep 2021.

Who built Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee?

Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee was manufactured by Jinma Rides.

How long is the track on Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee?

RCDB lists 761.2 ft of track.

How does Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.1 mph; the minimum height is 122 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
