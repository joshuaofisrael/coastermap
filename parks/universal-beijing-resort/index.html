Universal Studios Beijing height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Tongzhou, Beijing, China · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Beijing in Tongzhou, Beijing, China has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 122 cm (about 48 in) on Jurassic Flyers to 132 cm (about 52 in) on Decepticoaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Studios Beijing are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Universal Studios Beijing a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Decepticoaster, which opened in 2021.

Universal Studios Beijing publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Jurassic Flyers: Guests under 100 cm cannot ride; children between 100 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg.
  • Decepticoaster: Guests under 132 cm cannot ride. No companion rule is stated.
  • Flight of the Hippogriff: Guests under 99 cm cannot ride; children between 99 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg.
  • Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee: Guests must be between 92 cm and 190 cm; children between 92 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg.

What can my child ride at Universal Studios Beijing? By height

At Universal Studios Beijing the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

This family or kiddie coaster has an upper limit: Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee (up to 190 cm (about 74.8 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Universal Studios Beijing

These are the rules Universal Studios Beijing states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Jurassic Flyers: riders 100 to 122 cm (about 39.4 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Flight of the Hippogriff: riders 99 to 122 cm (about 39 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee: riders 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Decepticoaster: Guests under 132 cm cannot ride. No companion rule is stated.

Best coasters at Universal Studios Beijing for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Studios Beijing, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (122 cm minimum; 31.2 ft tall, 21.1 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Flight of the Hippogriff, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (122 cm minimum; 40 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Decepticoaster, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 121.7 ft tall, 63.8 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
