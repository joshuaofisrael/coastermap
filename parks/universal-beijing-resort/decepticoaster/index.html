Decepticoaster

Universal Studios Beijing · Tongzhou, Beijing, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 63.8 mph; 121.7 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2021.

Decepticoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Beijing in Tongzhou, Beijing, China, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 20 Sep 2021. The ride stands 121.7 ft tall, reaches 63.8 mph and runs 4150.3 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding Decepticoaster is like

Decepticoaster spreads 4,150.3 ft of track across a layout only 121.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 7 times across 4,150.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2021, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Studios Beijing. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Sitting Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include The Incredible Hulk Coaster at Islands of Adventure, Wildfire at Silver Dollar City and Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Universal Studios Beijing sets 132 cm, while Islands of Adventure sets 54 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 132 cm minimum is the strictest at Universal Studios Beijing. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Decepticoaster sits on the north-west side of Universal Studios Beijing's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jurassic Flyers, roughly 180 m away (122 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee at about 410 m. Decepticoaster layout, trains and ride system

Decepticoaster starts with a tyre-driven launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (8 entries):

  1. Tire Propelled Launch
  2. Zero-G Roll
  3. Cobra Roll
  4. Loop
  5. Corkscrew
  6. Loop
  7. Block Brake
  8. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 7 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Decepticoaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Guests under 132 cm cannot ride. No companion rule is stated.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Studios Beijing app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Decepticoaster?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Decepticoaster. Guests under 132 cm cannot ride. No companion rule is stated, according to Universal Beijing Resort, official ride page (accessibility information) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Decepticoaster?

Decepticoaster is 121.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Decepticoaster go?

Its top speed is 63.8 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Decepticoaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Decepticoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Decepticoaster open?

Decepticoaster opened on 20 Sep 2021.

Who built Decepticoaster?

Decepticoaster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Decepticoaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 35 seconds over 4150.3 ft of track.

Does Decepticoaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

Is Decepticoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 7 times; it tops out at 63.8 mph; the minimum height is 132 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Decepticoaster at Universal Studios Beijing the same as Dragon Khan at PortAventura Park?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Sitting Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Universal Studios Beijing ride opened in 2021 and the PortAventura Park ride in 1995; Universal Studios Beijing sets 132 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 121.7 ft and 148 ft.
