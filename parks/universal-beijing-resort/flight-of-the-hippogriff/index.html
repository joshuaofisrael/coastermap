Flight of the Hippogriff

Universal Studios Beijing · Tongzhou, Beijing, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in), or 99 to 122 cm (about 39 to 48 in) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 40 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Flight of the Hippogriff is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Beijing in Tongzhou, Beijing, China, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 20 Sep 2021. The ride stands 40 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1017.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flight of the Hippogriff is like

Flight of the Hippogriff pairs a 40 ft structure with 1,017.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2021, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Universal Studios Beijing. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Youngstar Coaster / Hippogriff design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Hollywood. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 122 cm.

Its 122 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Beijing (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 99 to 122 cm (about 39 to 48 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flight of the Hippogriff sits on the east side of Universal Studios Beijing's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Loop-Dee-Doop-Dee, roughly 310 m away (122 cm minimum). Next closest is Jurassic Flyers at about 420 m. How Flight of the Hippogriff compares

On our intensity ranking Flight of the Hippogriff is number 2 of 4 at Universal Studios Beijing, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Flight of the Hippogriff

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in), and riders from 99 to 122 cm (about 39 to 48 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Guests under 99 cm cannot ride; children between 99 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Studios Beijing app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flight of the Hippogriff?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Flight of the Hippogriff, or 99 to 122 cm (about 39 to 48 in) with a companion. Guests under 99 cm cannot ride; children between 99 cm and 122 cm must be accompanied by an adult. Weight must not exceed 136 kg, according to Universal Beijing Resort, official ride page (accessibility information) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flight of the Hippogriff?

Flight of the Hippogriff is 40 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flight of the Hippogriff go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flight of the Hippogriff go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Flight of the Hippogriff open?

Flight of the Hippogriff opened on 20 Sep 2021.

Who built Flight of the Hippogriff?

Flight of the Hippogriff was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Flight of the Hippogriff?

RCDB lists 1017.1 ft of track.

How does Flight of the Hippogriff get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph; the minimum height is 122 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Beijing the same as Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Hollywood?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Youngstar Coaster / Hippogriff coasters on RCDB; the Universal Studios Beijing ride opened in 2021 and the Universal Studios Hollywood ride in 2016; both list a 122 cm minimum.
