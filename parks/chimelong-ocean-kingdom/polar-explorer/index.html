Polar Explorer

Chimelong Ocean Kingdom · Hengqin, Zhuhai, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 150 cm (about 59.1 in), or 120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in) with a companion; top speed 43.5 mph; 73.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Polar Explorer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom in Hengqin, Zhuhai, China, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 28 Feb 2014. The ride stands 73.1 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 2919.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Polar Explorer is like

With 2,919.9 ft of track for a 73.1 ft structure, Polar Explorer is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom.

Its 150 cm minimum is the strictest at Ocean Kingdom. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Polar Explorer sits on the south-west side of Ocean Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Penguin Coaster, roughly 260 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jungle Coaster at about 370 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 150 cm (about 59.1 in) tall, or 120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Polar Explorer compares

On our intensity ranking Polar Explorer is number 2 of 5 at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Polar Explorer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 150 cm (about 59.1 in), and riders from 120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Children 1.2 m to 1.5 m (inclusive) must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Chimelong Ocean Kingdom app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Polar Explorer?

Riders must be at least 150 cm (about 59.1 in) tall to ride Polar Explorer, or 120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in) with a companion. Children 1.2 m to 1.5 m (inclusive) must ride with an adult, according to Chimelong Ocean Kingdom, official attraction page (safety rules) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Polar Explorer?

Polar Explorer is 73.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Polar Explorer go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Polar Explorer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Polar Explorer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Polar Explorer open?

Polar Explorer opened on 28 Feb 2014.

Who built Polar Explorer?

Polar Explorer was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Polar Explorer?

RCDB lists 2919.9 ft of track.

How does Polar Explorer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Polar Explorer at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is Polar Explorer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 150 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
