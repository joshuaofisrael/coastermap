Chimelong Ocean Kingdom height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Hengqin, Zhuhai, China · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Chimelong Ocean Kingdom in Hengqin, Zhuhai, China has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Penguin Coaster to 150 cm (about 59.1 in) on Polar Explorer. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Chimelong Ocean Kingdom a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 20% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 52 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Parrot Coaster, which opened in 2014.

Chimelong Ocean Kingdom publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom? By height

At Chimelong Ocean Kingdom the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Penguin Coaster at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Penguin Coaster (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)) and Jungle Coaster (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Walrus Splash at 132 cm (about 52 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Penguin Coaster and Jungle Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Walrus Splash (120 to 132 cm (about 47.2 to 52 in)) and Polar Explorer (120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Parrot Coaster at 132 cm (about 52 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Walrus Splash and Parrot Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Polar Explorer (120 to 150 cm (about 47.2 to 59.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Parrot Coaster (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom

These are the rules Chimelong Ocean Kingdom states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Penguin Coaster: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Chimelong Ocean Kingdom for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Chimelong Ocean Kingdom, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Jungle Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Walrus Splash, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (132 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Parrot Coaster, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 164.1 ft tall, 67.1 mph, 3 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
