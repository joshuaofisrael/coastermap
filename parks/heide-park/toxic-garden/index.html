Toxic Garden

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1999.

Toxic Garden is a steel inverted roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1999 and was previously known as Limit. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Toxic Garden is like

Toxic Garden pairs a 109.3 ft structure with 2,260.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1999, it is the 4th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort. It has run under other names (Limit), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 22 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Heide Park sets 130 cm, while Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in, Six Flags New England sets 52 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 52 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Heide Park, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Toxic Garden sits on the north-east side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Bobbahn, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Loop at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Toxic Garden

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders must be at least 10 years old.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Heide Park Resort app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Toxic Garden?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Toxic Garden. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders must be at least 10 years old, according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Toxic Garden?

Toxic Garden is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Toxic Garden go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Toxic Garden go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Toxic Garden scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Toxic Garden open?

Toxic Garden opened in 1999 (previously known as Limit).

Who built Toxic Garden?

Toxic Garden was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Toxic Garden?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Toxic Garden get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Toxic Garden at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Toxic Garden good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Toxic Garden at Heide Park the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Heide Park ride opened in 1999 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; Heide Park sets 130 cm and Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in.
