Colossos: Kampf der Giganten

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 68.4 mph; 164 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Colossos: Kampf der Giganten is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 13 Apr 2001 and was previously known as Colossos. The ride stands 164 ft tall, drops 159.1 ft, reaches 68.4 mph and runs 4409.4 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Colossos: Kampf der Giganten is like

Colossos: Kampf der Giganten pairs a 164 ft structure with 4,409.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2001, it is the 5th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort. It has run under other names (Colossos), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Wooden Coaster (Prefabricated Track) design, and the first of them to open. Others include El Toro at Six Flags Great Adventure and Balder at Liseberg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Heide Park sets 140 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in and Liseberg sets 130 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Heide Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Colossos: Kampf der Giganten sits on the south-east side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Grottenblitz, roughly 250 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Desert Race at about 310 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Colossos: Kampf der Giganten compares

On our intensity ranking Colossos: Kampf der Giganten is number 3 of 9 at Heide Park Resort, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 9 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Colossos: Kampf der Giganten?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Colossos: Kampf der Giganten. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders must be at least 10 years old, according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Colossos: Kampf der Giganten?

Colossos: Kampf der Giganten is 164 ft tall, with a drop of 159.1 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Colossos: Kampf der Giganten go?

Its top speed is 68.4 mph (110 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Colossos: Kampf der Giganten go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Colossos: Kampf der Giganten scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Colossos: Kampf der Giganten open?

Colossos: Kampf der Giganten opened on 13 Apr 2001 (previously known as Colossos).

Who built Colossos: Kampf der Giganten?

Colossos: Kampf der Giganten was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Colossos: Kampf der Giganten?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 25 seconds over 4409.4 ft of track.

How does Colossos: Kampf der Giganten get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Colossos: Kampf der Giganten at once?

Up to 30 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,030 riders.

Is Colossos: Kampf der Giganten good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 68.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Colossos: Kampf der Giganten at Heide Park the same as Balder at Liseberg?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Wooden Coaster (Prefabricated Track) coasters on RCDB; the Heide Park ride opened in 2001 and the Liseberg ride in 2003; Heide Park sets 140 cm and Liseberg sets 130 cm; RCDB lists heights of 164 ft and 118.1 ft.
