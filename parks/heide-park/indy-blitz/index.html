Indy-Blitz

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 100 cm (about 39.4 in); top speed 18.6 mph; 14.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Indy-Blitz is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Zierer. It first opened on 24 May 2008 and was previously known as Indianer-Achterbahn. The ride stands 14.8 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 413.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Indy-Blitz is like

Indy-Blitz pairs a 14.8 ft structure with 413.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2008, it is the 7th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort. It has run under other names (Indianer-Achterbahn), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 100 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Heide Park, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Indy-Blitz sits on the south-west side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Krake, roughly 190 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grottenblitz at about 240 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Indy-Blitz?

Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall to ride Indy-Blitz. Children under 8 years and under 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Indy-Blitz?

Indy-Blitz is 14.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Indy-Blitz go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Indy-Blitz go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Indy-Blitz scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Indy-Blitz open?

Indy-Blitz opened on 24 May 2008 (previously known as Indianer-Achterbahn).

Who built Indy-Blitz?

Indy-Blitz was manufactured by Zierer.
