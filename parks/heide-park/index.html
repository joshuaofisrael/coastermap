Heide Park Resort height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Soltau, Germany · 9 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany has 9 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Indy-Blitz to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Colossos: Kampf der Giganten. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Heide Park Resort are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes Heide Park Resort a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 9 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Colossos: Kampf der Giganten); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Desert Race).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Big Loop, which opened in 1983.

Heide Park Resort publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Bobbahn: Riders must be at least 6 years old.
  • Toxic Garden: Riders must be at least 10 years old.
  • Colossos: Kampf der Giganten: Riders must be at least 10 years old.

What can my child ride at Heide Park Resort? By height

At Heide Park Resort the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Indy-Blitz at 100 cm (about 39.4 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Indy-Blitz. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Bobbahn at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Bobbahn and Big Loop. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Toxic Garden at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Toxic Garden. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Colossos: Kampf der Giganten at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Big Loop (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Toxic Garden (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Colossos: Kampf der Giganten (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Desert Race (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Flug der Dämonen (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Krake (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Heide Park Resort for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Heide Park Resort, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Indy-Blitz, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (100 cm minimum; 14.8 ft tall, 18.6 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Bobbahn, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 88.6 ft tall, 32.9 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Flug der Dämonen, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 131.2 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
