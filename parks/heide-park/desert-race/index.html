Desert Race

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 63.4 mph; 62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Desert Race is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 15 May 2007. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall, reaches 63.4 mph and runs 2132.5 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Desert Race is like

Desert Race spreads 2,132.5 ft of track across a layout only 62.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2007, it is the 6th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the 8th of them to open. Others include Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Xcelerator at Knott's and Rita at Alton Towers. Height rules for this model differ by park: Heide Park sets 140 cm, while Cedar Point sets 52 in, Hersheypark sets 54 in and Knott's sets 52 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Heide Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Desert Race sits on the south side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Grottenblitz, roughly 100 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Indy-Blitz at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Desert Race compares

On our intensity ranking Desert Race is number 6 of 9 at Heide Park Resort, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 9 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Bobbahn (88.6 ft tall, 32.9 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Big Loop (98.4 ft tall, 39.2 mph, 4 inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Desert Race?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Desert Race. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Desert Race?

Desert Race is 62.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Desert Race go?

Its top speed is 63.4 mph (102 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Desert Race go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Desert Race scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Desert Race open?

Desert Race opened on 15 May 2007.

Who built Desert Race?

Desert Race was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Desert Race?

RCDB lists a ride time of 49 seconds over 2132.5 ft of track.

Does Desert Race launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Desert Race at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Desert Race good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 63.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Desert Race at Heide Park the same as Xcelerator at Knott's?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Heide Park ride opened in 2007 and the Knott's ride in 2002; Heide Park sets 140 cm and Knott's sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 62.3 ft and 205 ft.
