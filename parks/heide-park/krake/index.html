Krake

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 64 mph; 134.5 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2011.

Krake is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 16 Apr 2011. The ride stands 134.5 ft tall, reaches 64 mph and runs 1561.7 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Krake is like

At 134.5 ft tall with 1,561.7 ft of track, Krake balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 87°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 1 time across 1,561.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2011, it is the 8th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Heide Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Krake sits on the west side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Loop, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flug der Dämonen at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Krake

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Heide Park Resort app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Krake?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Krake. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Krake?

Krake is 134.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Krake go?

Its top speed is 64 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Krake go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Krake scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Krake open?

Krake opened on 16 Apr 2011.

Who built Krake?

Krake was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Krake?

RCDB lists 1561.7 ft of track.

How does Krake get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Krake at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Krake good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 64 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
