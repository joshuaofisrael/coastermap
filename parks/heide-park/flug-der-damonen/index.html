Flug der Dämonen

Heide Park Resort · Soltau, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2014.

Flug der Dämonen is a steel wing roller coaster at Heide Park Resort in Soltau, Germany, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 29 Mar 2014. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 2532.8 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Flug der Dämonen is like

Flug der Dämonen pairs a 131.2 ft structure with 2,532.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,532.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2014, it is the newest of the 9 coasters we list at Heide Park Resort.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Heide Park, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flug der Dämonen sits on the north side of Heide Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Bobbahn, roughly 50 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Loop at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Flug der Dämonen

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Heide Park Resort app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flug der Dämonen?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Flug der Dämonen. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Heide Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flug der Dämonen?

Flug der Dämonen is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flug der Dämonen go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flug der Dämonen go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Flug der Dämonen scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Flug der Dämonen open?

Flug der Dämonen opened on 29 Mar 2014.

Who built Flug der Dämonen?

Flug der Dämonen was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
