Roller Skater

Kijima Kogen Park · Beppu, Oita, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Roller Skater is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kijima Kogen Park in Beppu, Oita, Japan, built by Vekoma. It opened in 2000. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Roller Skater is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Roller Skater balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2000, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Kijima Kogen Park. It is one of 14 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 11th of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America and Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kijima Kogen sets 110 cm, while Magic Mountain sets 36 in, Universal Studios Florida sets 36 in and Six Flags Great America sets 42 in.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Kijima Kogen, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Roller Skater sits on the north-west side of Kijima Kogen's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gold Rush, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon at about 110 m. Roller Skater layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

How Roller Skater compares

On our intensity ranking Roller Skater is number 4 of 5 at Kijima Kogen Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Dragon (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Gold Rush (42.7 ft tall, 28.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Roller Skater

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum age 4; riders 105 to 110 cm must ride with a guardian.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Kijima Kogen Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Roller Skater?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Roller Skater, or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Minimum age 4; riders 105 to 110 cm must ride with a guardian, according to Kijima Kogen Park, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Roller Skater?

Roller Skater is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Roller Skater go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Roller Skater go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Roller Skater scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Roller Skater open?

Roller Skater opened in 2000.

Who built Roller Skater?

Roller Skater was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Roller Skater?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 679.1 ft of track.

How does Roller Skater get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Roller Skater at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

Is Roller Skater good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Roller Skater operate before?

RCDB records it at Tokiwa Park (as unknown).

Is Roller Skater at Kijima Kogen the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Kijima Kogen ride opened in 2000 and the Disneyland ride in 1993; Kijima Kogen sets 110 cm and Disneyland sets 35 in.
