Jupiter

Kijima Kogen Park · Beppu, Oita, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 56.5 mph; 137.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

Jupiter is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kijima Kogen Park in Beppu, Oita, Japan, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 21 Jul 1992. The ride stands 137.8 ft tall, reaches 56.5 mph and runs 5249.3 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jupiter is like

With 5,249.3 ft of track for a 137.8 ft structure, Jupiter is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.7 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1992, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Kijima Kogen Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Wooden Coaster design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include American Eagle at Six Flags Great America and Elf at Hirakata Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kijima Kogen sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great America sets 48 in and Hirakata Park sets 110 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Kijima Kogen, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jupiter sits on the south-east side of Kijima Kogen's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Super LS Coaster, roughly 280 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon at about 350 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Jupiter compares

On our intensity ranking Jupiter is number 2 of 5 at Kijima Kogen Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Gold Rush (42.7 ft tall, 28.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Super LS Coaster (48.5 mph, 3 inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Jupiter?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Jupiter. Not for guests aged 65 or older, according to Kijima Kogen Park, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Jupiter?

Jupiter is 137.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Jupiter go?

Its top speed is 56.5 mph (91 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Jupiter go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jupiter scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Jupiter open?

Jupiter opened on 21 Jul 1992.

Who built Jupiter?

Jupiter was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Jupiter?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 34 seconds over 5249.3 ft of track.

How does Jupiter get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Jupiter at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Jupiter good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 56.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Jupiter at Kijima Kogen the same as American Eagle at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Wooden Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Kijima Kogen ride opened in 1992 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1981; Kijima Kogen sets 120 cm and Six Flags Great America sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 137.8 ft and 127 ft.
