Kijima Kogen Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Beppu, Oita, Japan · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kijima Kogen Park in Beppu, Oita, Japan has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Roller Skater to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Jupiter. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Kijima Kogen Park are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Kijima Kogen Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Jupiter).

The most common builder is Meisho Amusement Machines, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Super LS Coaster, which opened in 1983.

Kijima Kogen Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Kijima Kogen Park? By height

At Kijima Kogen Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Kijima Kogen Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Kijima Kogen Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dragon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Gold Rush, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 28.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Super LS Coaster, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 48.5 mph, 3 inversions).
