Beech Bend Amusement Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bowling Green, Kentucky · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Beech Bend Amusement Park in Bowling Green, Kentucky has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 47 in (119 cm) on Spinning Out to 48 in (122 cm) on Kentucky Rumbler. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Beech Bend Amusement Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Beech Bend Amusement Park a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 42% of coasters across all 176 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Kentucky Rumbler).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Wild Mouse, which opened in 2005.

What can my child ride at Beech Bend Amusement Park? By height

At Beech Bend Amusement Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
