Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park in Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Skyflyer to 48 in (122 cm) on Phoenix. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 38 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sea Serpent, which opened in 1999.

What can my child ride at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park? By height

At Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Skyflyer. With a companion they can also ride Sea Serpent (36 to 38 in (91 to 97 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Phoenix at 48 in (122 cm).

Companion rules at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park

These are the rules Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.
