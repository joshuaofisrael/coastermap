Skyflyer

Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park · Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Skyflyer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park in Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2021. The ride runs 91.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Skyflyer is like

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Deno's, which makes it a natural first coaster there.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Skyflyer sits near the middle of Deno's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sea Serpent, roughly 50 m away (38 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Phoenix at about 100 m. Skyflyer layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Skyflyer trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 3 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 12 riders.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Skyflyer?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Skyflyer, according to Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Skyflyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Skyflyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster. It is one of the gentlest coasters you can ride.

When did Skyflyer open?

Skyflyer opened in 2021.

Who built Skyflyer?

Skyflyer was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Skyflyer?

RCDB lists 91.9 ft of track.

How does Skyflyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Skyflyer at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Skyflyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as kiddie; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
