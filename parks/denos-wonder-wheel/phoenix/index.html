Phoenix

Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park · Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 34 mph; 68 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Phoenix is a steel suspended roller coaster at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park in Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York, built by Vekoma. It opened on 2 Jul 2021. The ride stands 68 ft tall, reaches 34 mph and runs 1220.5 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Phoenix is like

Phoenix pairs a 68 ft structure with 1,220.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2021, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Deno's. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Phoenix sits near the middle of Deno's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sea Serpent, roughly 80 m away (38 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Skyflyer at about 100 m. Before you ride Phoenix

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Phoenix?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Phoenix, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion. 48 inches to ride alone; 39 inches with a responsible person, according to Deno's Wonder Wheel Amusement Park, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Phoenix?

Phoenix is 68 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Phoenix go?

Its top speed is 34 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Phoenix go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Phoenix scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Phoenix open?

Phoenix opened on 2 Jul 2021.

Who built Phoenix?

Phoenix was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Phoenix?

RCDB lists 1220.5 ft of track.

How does Phoenix get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Phoenix at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Phoenix good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
