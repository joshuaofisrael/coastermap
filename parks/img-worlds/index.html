IMG Worlds of Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Dubai, United Arab Emirates · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

IMG Worlds of Adventure in Dubai, United Arab Emirates has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 125 cm (about 49.2 in) on Predator to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Velociraptor. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at IMG Worlds of Adventure are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes IMG Worlds of Adventure a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 40% across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, 2 of the 3 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Velociraptor).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Spider-Man Doc Ock's Revenge, which opened in 2016.

IMG Worlds of Adventure publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at IMG Worlds of Adventure? By height

At IMG Worlds of Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at IMG Worlds of Adventure for first-timers
