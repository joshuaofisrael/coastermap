Predator

IMG Worlds of Adventure · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2016.

Predator is a steel sit-down roller coaster at IMG Worlds of Adventure in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 31 Aug 2016. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1184.4 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Predator is like

At 72.2 ft tall with 1,184.4 ft of track, Predator balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. With 3 inversions in 1,184.4 ft, it flips riders roughly every 395 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2016, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at IMG Worlds of Adventure. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Euro-Fighter / 320+ design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Untamed at Canobie Lake, Rage at Adventure Island and Falcon at Duinrell. Height rules for this model differ by park: IMG Worlds sets 125 cm, while Canobie Lake sets 48 in, Adventure Island sets 120 cm and Duinrell sets 120 cm.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Predator compares

On our intensity ranking Predator is number 2 of 3 at IMG Worlds of Adventure, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Spider-Man Doc Ock's Revenge (no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Velociraptor (124.7 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 4 inversions, 3.8 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Predator

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the IMG Worlds of Adventure app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Predator?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Predator, according to IMG Worlds of Adventure, official rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Predator?

Predator is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Predator go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Predator go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Predator scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Predator open?

Predator opened on 31 Aug 2016.

Who built Predator?

Predator was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Predator?

RCDB lists 1184.4 ft of track.

How does Predator get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

Is Predator good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Predator at IMG Worlds the same as Rage at Adventure Island?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Euro-Fighter / 320+ coasters on RCDB; the IMG Worlds ride opened in 2016 and the Adventure Island ride in 2007; IMG Worlds sets 125 cm and Adventure Island sets 120 cm.
