Cyclone

Animália Park · Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Cyclone is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Animália Park in Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2025. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cyclone is like

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Animália Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Cyclon Coaster (MX49) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Taka Waka at Geiselwind. Height rules for this model differ by park: Animália Park sets 130 cm, while Geiselwind sets 120 cm.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Cyclone

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Below 1.30 m riders may not ride; no companion option is listed. The park sets no maximum height but says riders over 1.95 m or 120 kg may not fit the seat.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Animália Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cyclone?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Cyclone. Below 1.30 m riders may not ride; no companion option is listed. The park sets no maximum height but says riders over 1.95 m or 120 kg may not fit the seat, according to Animália Park, official attraction page (ride rules graphic) (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Cyclone go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cyclone scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cyclone open?

Cyclone opened in 2025.

Who built Cyclone?

Cyclone was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Cyclone get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Cyclone good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Cyclone operate before?

RCDB records it at Parc Magique (as Montanha Russa).

Is Cyclone at Animália Park the same as Taka Waka at Geiselwind?

Both are SBF Visa Group Cyclon Coaster (MX49) coasters on RCDB; the Animália Park ride opened in 2025 and the Geiselwind ride in 2022; Animália Park sets 130 cm and Geiselwind sets 120 cm.
