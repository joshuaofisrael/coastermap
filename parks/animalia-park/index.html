Animália Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Animália Park in Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Bear Mountain to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Cyclone. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Animália Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Animália Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is SBF Visa Group, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Bear Mountain, which opened in 2023.

Animália Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

Companion rules at Animália Park

These are the rules Animália Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Bear Mountain: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Cyclone: Below 1.30 m riders may not ride; no companion option is listed. The park sets no maximum height but says riders over 1.95 m or 120 kg may not fit the seat.
