Big Air Coaster

Animália Park · Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2025.

Big Air Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Animália Park in Cotia, São Paulo, Brazil, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2025. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Air Coaster is like

Opened in 2025, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Animália Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Big Air Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Xolo Loca at Casino Pier.

Written from RCDB statistics and the park's published height rule; it describes the layout, not a review.

Big Air Coaster layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Big Air Coaster:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

TrainsSingle train with 6 cars.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Frequently asked questions

Does Big Air Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Air Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Big Air Coaster open?

Big Air Coaster opened in 2025.

Who built Big Air Coaster?

Big Air Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Big Air Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Big Air Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Big Air Coaster at Animália Park the same as Xolo Loca at Casino Pier?

Both are SBF Visa Group Big Air Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Animália Park ride opened in 2025 and the Casino Pier ride in 2021.
