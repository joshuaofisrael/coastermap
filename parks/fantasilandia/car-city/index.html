Car City

Fantasilandia · Santiago, Chile

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Car City is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fantasilandia in Santiago, Chile, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 19 Dec 2024. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Car City is like

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Fantasilandia. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Double Mine Train Extended design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Flotter Otto at Skyline Park. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Fantasilandia, out of 5 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Car City layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Car City trains and ride system

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Car City

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders 0.90 to 1.20 m must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Car City?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Car City, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders 0.90 to 1.20 m must ride with an adult, according to Fantasilandia, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Car City go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Car City scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Car City open?

Car City opened on 19 Dec 2024.

Who built Car City?

Car City was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Car City get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Car City at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Car City good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Car City at Fantasilandia the same as Flotter Otto at Skyline Park?

Both are SBF Visa Group Double Mine Train Extended coasters on RCDB; the Fantasilandia ride opened in 2024 and the Skyline Park ride in 2023; both list a 120 cm minimum.
