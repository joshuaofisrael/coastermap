Tren Minero

Fantasilandia · Santiago, Chile

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 30.1 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Tren Minero is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fantasilandia in Santiago, Chile, built by Vekoma. It opened on 14 Nov 2015. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 30.1 mph and runs 2575.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tren Minero is like

Tren Minero spreads 2,575.5 ft of track across a layout only 45.9 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2015, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Fantasilandia. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Mine Train / 785m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Calamity Mine at Walibi Belgium. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fantasilandia sets 140 cm, while Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Fantasilandia, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tren Minero sits on the south side of Fantasilandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Mouse, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Raptor at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tren Minero?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Tren Minero, or 90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riders 0.90 to 1.40 m must ride with an adult, according to Fantasilandia, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tren Minero?

Tren Minero is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tren Minero go?

Its top speed is 30.1 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tren Minero go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tren Minero scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tren Minero open?

Tren Minero opened on 14 Nov 2015.

Who built Tren Minero?

Tren Minero was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Tren Minero?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 2575.5 ft of track.

How does Tren Minero get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tren Minero at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Tren Minero good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 30.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Tren Minero operate before?

RCDB records it at Ratanga Junction (as Diamond Devil Run).

Is Tren Minero at Fantasilandia the same as Calamity Mine at Walibi Belgium?

Both are Vekoma Mine Train / 785m coasters on RCDB; the Fantasilandia ride opened in 2015 and the Walibi Belgium ride in 1992; Fantasilandia sets 140 cm and Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm.
