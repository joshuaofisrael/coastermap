Fantasilandia height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Santiago, Chile · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Fantasilandia in Santiago, Chile has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Montaña Dragón to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Raptor. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Fantasilandia are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Fantasilandia a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Boomerang, which opened in 1996.

Fantasilandia publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Fantasilandia? By height

At Fantasilandia the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Montaña Dragón. With a companion they can also ride Tren Minero (90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in)) and Car City (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Wild Mouse at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Montaña Dragón. With a companion they can also ride Tren Minero (90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in)) and Car City (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Wild Mouse at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Wild Mouse, Car City and Boomerang. With a companion they can also ride Tren Minero (90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Raptor at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

With a companion they can also ride Tren Minero (90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Raptor at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Car City (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Fantasilandia

These are the rules Fantasilandia states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Tren Minero: riders 90 to 140 cm (about 35.4 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Car City: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Fantasilandia for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Fantasilandia, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Car City, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wild Mouse, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: Raptor, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Fantasilandia?

Roller coaster height requirements at Fantasilandia run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) (Montaña Dragón) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Raptor), according to official park sources checked 11 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Fantasilandia have?

Fantasilandia has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 11 Nov 2026.

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Raptor (109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Boomerang, at 116.5 ft tall (RCDB).
