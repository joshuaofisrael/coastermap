De Waarbeek height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Hengelo, Overijssel, Netherlands · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

De Waarbeek in Hengelo, Overijssel, Netherlands has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Rupsje Mae to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Rodelbaan. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at De Waarbeek are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes De Waarbeek a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rodelbaan, which opened in 1930.

De Waarbeek publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at De Waarbeek? By height

At De Waarbeek the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Rupsje Mae (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Raptor Adventure at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Rodelbaan (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Rupsje Mae (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)), Raptor Adventure (95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in)) and Missie 18 (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Rupsje Mae, Raptor Adventure and Missie 18. With a companion they can also ride Rodelbaan (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Rodelbaan. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at De Waarbeek

These are the rules De Waarbeek states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Rodelbaan: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Rupsje Mae: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Raptor Adventure: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Missie 18: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at De Waarbeek for first-timers

  1. Start here: Missie 18, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
