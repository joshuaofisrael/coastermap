Raptor Adventure

De Waarbeek · Hengelo, Overijssel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Raptor Adventure is a steel sit-down roller coaster at De Waarbeek in Hengelo, Overijssel, Netherlands, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2023. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Raptor Adventure is like

Opened in 2023, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at De Waarbeek. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Mine Train Coaster (MX601) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mine Train Coaster at Dalmaland. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at De Waarbeek (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Raptor Adventure sits on the south side of De Waarbeek's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rodelbaan, roughly 130 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Missie 18 at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Raptor Adventure

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park defines a companion as an adult aged at least 18.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the De Waarbeek app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Raptor Adventure?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Raptor Adventure, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park defines a companion as an adult aged at least 18, according to De Waarbeek, official attractions page (height requirements per attraction) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Raptor Adventure go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Raptor Adventure scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Raptor Adventure open?

Raptor Adventure opened in 2023.

Who built Raptor Adventure?

Raptor Adventure was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How many people ride Raptor Adventure at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Raptor Adventure good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Raptor Adventure operate before?

RCDB records it at Speelparadijs Pannenkoekenhuis Voorst (as Panky's Adventure).

Is Raptor Adventure at De Waarbeek the same as Mine Train Coaster at Dalmaland?

Both are SBF Visa Group Mine Train Coaster (MX601) coasters on RCDB; the De Waarbeek ride opened in 2023 and the Dalmaland ride in 2017; both list a 120 cm minimum.
