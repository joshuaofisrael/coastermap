Kozmo's Kurves

Knoebels Amusement Resort · Elysburg, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Kozmo's Kurves is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knoebels Amusement Resort in Elysburg, Pennsylvania, built by E&F Miler Industries. It opened on 1 Aug 2009. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kozmo's Kurves is like

Opened in 2009, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Knoebels Amusement Resort.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Knoebels, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kozmo's Kurves sits on the north-east side of Knoebels' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Impulse, roughly 90 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flying Turns at about 280 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kozmo's Kurves?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Kozmo's Kurves, according to Knoebels, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Kozmo's Kurves go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kozmo's Kurves scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Kozmo's Kurves open?

Kozmo's Kurves opened on 1 Aug 2009.

Who built Kozmo's Kurves?

Kozmo's Kurves was manufactured by E&F Miler Industries.

How does Kozmo's Kurves get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kozmo's Kurves at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Kozmo's Kurves use?

RCDB lists: t-bar.

Is Kozmo's Kurves good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
