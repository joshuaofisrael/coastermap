Flying Turns

Knoebels Amusement Resort · Elysburg, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 24 mph; 50 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Flying Turns is a wood roller coaster at Knoebels Amusement Resort in Elysburg, Pennsylvania. It opened on 5 Oct 2013. The ride stands 50 ft tall, reaches 24 mph and runs 1,300 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flying Turns is like

At 50 ft tall with 1,300 ft of track, Flying Turns balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2013, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Knoebels Amusement Resort.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Knoebels, out of 5 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flying Turns sits near the middle of Knoebels' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Phoenix, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twister at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flying Turns?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Flying Turns, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Knoebels, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flying Turns?

Flying Turns is 50 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flying Turns go?

Its top speed is 24 mph (39 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flying Turns go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flying Turns scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Flying Turns open?

Flying Turns opened on 5 Oct 2013.

How long is the track on Flying Turns?

RCDB lists 1,300 ft (396 m) of track.

How does Flying Turns get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Flying Turns at once?

Each train carries 6 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Flying Turns good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
