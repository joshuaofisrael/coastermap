Twister

Knoebels Amusement Resort · Elysburg, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 51.5 mph; 101.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Twister is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Knoebels Amusement Resort in Elysburg, Pennsylvania. It opened on 24 Jul 1999. The ride stands 101.6 ft tall, drops 89.6 ft, reaches 51.5 mph and runs 3,900 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twister is like

With 3,900 ft of track for a 101.6 ft structure, Twister is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1999, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Knoebels Amusement Resort.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Knoebels, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twister sits on the south side of Knoebels' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flying Turns, roughly 180 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Phoenix at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twister?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Twister, according to Knoebels, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twister?

Twister is 101.6 ft tall, with a drop of 89.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Twister go?

Its top speed is 51.5 mph (83 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Twister go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Twister open?

Twister opened on 24 Jul 1999.

How long is the ride on Twister?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 3,900 ft of track.

How does Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twister at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 51.5 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Twister at Knoebels the same as Twister at Gröna Lund?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Knoebels ride opened in 1999 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2011; Knoebels sets 42 in and Gröna Lund sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 101.6 ft and 51 ft.
