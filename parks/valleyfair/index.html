Valleyfair roller coasters

Shakopee, Minnesota · 8 coasters

Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Wild Thing at 207 ft, which is also the fastest at 74 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Cosmic Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Valleyfair have?

Valleyfair has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Valleyfair?

Wild Thing, at 207 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Valleyfair?

Wild Thing, with a top speed of 74 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Valleyfair?

By height requirement, Cosmic Coaster has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Valleyfair coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Cosmic Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Valleyfair coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: High Roller, Corkscrew, Excalibur, Wild Thing, Mad Mouse, Renegade, Cosmic Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Valleyfair do not go upside down?

High Roller, Excalibur, Wild Thing, Mad Mouse, Steel Venom, Renegade, Cosmic Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
