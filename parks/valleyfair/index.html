Valleyfair height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Shakopee, Minnesota · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Cosmic Coaster to 52 in (132 cm) on Steel Venom. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 7 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at Valleyfair are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Valleyfair a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 62% of its lineup, against 42% across all 183 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 8 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (High Roller and Renegade); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Steel Venom).

The most common builder is Arrow Dynamics, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is High Roller, which opened in 1976.

What can my child ride at Valleyfair? By height

At Valleyfair the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Cosmic Coaster at 42 in (107 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: High Roller, Corkscrew, Excalibur, Wild Thing, Mad Mouse and Renegade. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Steel Venom at 52 in (132 cm).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Steel Venom. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Valleyfair for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Valleyfair, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Cosmic Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Excalibur, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 100 ft tall, 54.5 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Steel Venom, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 185 ft tall, 70 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Valleyfair?

Roller coaster height requirements at Valleyfair run from 42 in (107 cm) (Cosmic Coaster) to 52 in (132 cm) (Steel Venom), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Valleyfair have?

Valleyfair has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Valleyfair?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Steel Venom (185 ft tall, 70 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Valleyfair?

Wild Thing, at 207 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Valleyfair?

Wild Thing, with a top speed of 74 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Valleyfair for first-timers?

Cosmic Coaster is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 42 in (107 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Valleyfair?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Cosmic Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Mad Mouse.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Valleyfair?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: High Roller, Corkscrew, Excalibur, Wild Thing, Mad Mouse, Renegade, Cosmic Coaster.

Which coasters at Valleyfair do not go upside down?

High Roller, Excalibur, Wild Thing, Mad Mouse, Steel Venom, Renegade, Cosmic Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
