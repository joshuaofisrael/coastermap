Renegade

Valleyfair · Shakopee, Minnesota

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 51.3 mph; 97.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Renegade is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 12 May 2007. The ride stands 97.5 ft tall, drops 91.4 ft, reaches 51.3 mph and runs 3,113 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Renegade?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Renegade, according to Valleyfair, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Renegade?

Renegade is 97.5 ft tall, with a drop of 91.4 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Renegade go?

Its top speed is 51.3 mph (83 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Renegade go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Renegade scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Renegade open?

Renegade opened on 12 May 2007.

Who built Renegade?

Renegade was manufactured by Great Coasters International.
