Steel Venom

Valleyfair · Shakopee, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 70 mph; 185 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Steel Venom is a steel inverted roller coaster at Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 17 May 2003. The ride stands 185 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 630 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Steel Venom is like

Steel Venom packs its 630 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (185 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2003, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Valleyfair. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Impulse Coaster / Twist & Spike (56m) design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Six Flags Great America, The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Discovery Kingdom and Possessed at Dorney Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Valleyfair sets 52 in, while Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Valleyfair. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Venom sits on the east side of Valleyfair's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Thing, roughly 100 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 400 m. Steel Venom layout, trains and ride system

Steel Venom starts with a linear induction motor (LIM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Steel Venom, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LIM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. Twisted Vertical Rollback
  3. Vertical Rollback

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Venom?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Steel Venom, according to Valleyfair, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Venom?

Steel Venom is 185 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Venom go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Venom go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steel Venom scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Steel Venom open?

Steel Venom opened on 17 May 2003.

Who built Steel Venom?

Steel Venom was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Steel Venom?

RCDB lists 630 ft (192 m) of track.

Does Steel Venom launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Steel Venom at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 950 riders.

Is Steel Venom good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Steel Venom at Valleyfair the same as The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Impulse Coaster / Twist & Spike (56m) coasters on RCDB; the Valleyfair ride opened in 2003 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2001; Valleyfair sets 52 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 185 ft and 150 ft.
