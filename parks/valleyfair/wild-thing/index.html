Wild Thing

Valleyfair · Shakopee, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 74 mph; 207 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Wild Thing is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota, built by D.H. Morgan Manufacturing, Inc. It opened on 11 May 1996. The ride stands 207 ft tall, drops 196 ft, reaches 74 mph and runs 5,460 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wild Thing is like

At 207 ft tall with 5,460 ft of track, Wild Thing balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1996, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Valleyfair.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Valleyfair, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wild Thing sits on the south-east side of Valleyfair's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steel Venom, roughly 100 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wild Thing?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wild Thing, according to Valleyfair, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wild Thing?

Wild Thing is 207 ft tall, with a drop of 196 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wild Thing go?

Its top speed is 74 mph (119 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wild Thing go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wild Thing scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wild Thing open?

Wild Thing opened on 11 May 1996.

Who built Wild Thing?

Wild Thing was manufactured by D.H. Morgan Manufacturing, Inc.

How long is the ride on Wild Thing?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 5,460 ft of track.

How does Wild Thing get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wild Thing at once?

Up to 36 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,700 riders.

Is Wild Thing good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 74 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
