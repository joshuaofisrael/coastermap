High Roller

Valleyfair · Shakopee, Minnesota

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50 mph; 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1976.

High Roller is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Valleyfair in Shakopee, Minnesota, built by International Amusement Devices, Inc.. It opened in 1976. The ride stands 70 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,982 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for High Roller?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride High Roller, according to Valleyfair, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is High Roller?

High Roller is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does High Roller go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does High Roller go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is High Roller scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Valleyfair rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did High Roller open?

High Roller opened on 1976.

Who built High Roller?
