Riddler's Revenge

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 65 mph; 156 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 1998.

Riddler's Revenge is a steel stand-up roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 4 Apr 1998. The ride stands 156 ft tall, drops 146 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 4,370 ft of track. On a stand-up coaster riders travel standing, supported by a bicycle-style seat and a shoulder harness. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding Riddler's Revenge is like

At 156 ft tall with 4,370 ft of track, Riddler's Revenge balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 6 times across 4,370 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the 6th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Stand-Up Coaster design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Rougarou at Cedar Point, Georgia Scorcher at Six Flags Over Georgia, Vortex at Carowinds and Patriot at California's Great America. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Magic Mountain, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Riddler's Revenge sits on the west side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wonder Woman Flight of Courage, roughly 130 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batman The Ride at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Riddler's Revenge

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Magic Mountain app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Riddler's Revenge?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Riddler's Revenge, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Riddler's Revenge?

Riddler's Revenge is 156 ft tall, with a drop of 146 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Riddler's Revenge go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Riddler's Revenge go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Riddler's Revenge scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Riddler's Revenge open?

Riddler's Revenge opened on 4 Apr 1998.

Who built Riddler's Revenge?

Riddler's Revenge was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Riddler's Revenge?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 4,370 ft of track.

How does Riddler's Revenge get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Riddler's Revenge at once?

Each train carries 32 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,610 riders.

Is Riddler's Revenge good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Riddler's Revenge at Magic Mountain the same as Patriot at California's Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Stand-Up Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Magic Mountain ride opened in 1998 and the California's Great America ride in 1991; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 156 ft and 91 ft.
