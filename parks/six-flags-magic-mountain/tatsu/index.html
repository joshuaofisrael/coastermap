Tatsu

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 62 mph; 170 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2006.

Tatsu is a steel flying roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 13 May 2006. The ride stands 170 ft tall, drops 111 ft, reaches 62 mph and runs 3,602 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tatsu?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Tatsu, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tatsu?

Tatsu is 170 ft tall, with a drop of 111 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tatsu go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tatsu go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Tatsu scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tatsu open?

Tatsu opened on 13 May 2006.

Who built Tatsu?

Tatsu was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
