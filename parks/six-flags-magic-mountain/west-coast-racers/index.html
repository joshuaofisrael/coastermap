West Coast Racers

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 55 mph; 67 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2020.

West Coast Racers is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 9 Jan 2020. The ride stands 67 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 4,000 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding West Coast Racers is like

With 4,000 ft of track for a 67 ft structure, West Coast Racers is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 4 times across 4,000 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2020, it is the 15th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Magic Mountain, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, West Coast Racers sits on the south-west side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ninja, roughly 130 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Apocalypse the Ride at about 180 m. West Coast Racers layout, trains and ride system

West Coast Racers starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride West Coast Racers

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Magic Mountain app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for West Coast Racers?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride West Coast Racers, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is West Coast Racers?

West Coast Racers is 67 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does West Coast Racers go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does West Coast Racers go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is West Coast Racers scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did West Coast Racers open?

West Coast Racers opened on 9 Jan 2020.

Who built West Coast Racers?

West Coast Racers was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on West Coast Racers?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 4,000 ft of track.

Does West Coast Racers launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride West Coast Racers at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is West Coast Racers good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
