Wonder Woman Flight of Courage

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 58 mph; 131 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2022.

Wonder Woman Flight of Courage is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 16 Jul 2022. The ride stands 131 ft tall, reaches 58 mph and runs 3,300 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Wonder Woman Flight of Courage is like

At 131 ft tall with 3,300 ft of track, Wonder Woman Flight of Courage balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,300 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Magic Mountain, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wonder Woman Flight of Courage sits on the north-west side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman The Ride, roughly 100 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Riddler's Revenge at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Wonder Woman Flight of Courage

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Magic Mountain app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wonder Woman Flight of Courage?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wonder Woman Flight of Courage, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wonder Woman Flight of Courage?

Wonder Woman Flight of Courage is 131 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wonder Woman Flight of Courage go?

Its top speed is 58 mph (93 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wonder Woman Flight of Courage go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Wonder Woman Flight of Courage scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wonder Woman Flight of Courage open?

Wonder Woman Flight of Courage opened on 16 Jul 2022.

Who built Wonder Woman Flight of Courage?

Wonder Woman Flight of Courage was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Wonder Woman Flight of Courage?

RCDB lists 3,300 ft (1,006 m) of track.

How does Wonder Woman Flight of Courage get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Wonder Woman Flight of Courage good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 58 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
