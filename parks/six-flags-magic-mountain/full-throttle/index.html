Full Throttle

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 70 mph; 164 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2013.

Full Throttle is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 22 Jun 2013. The ride stands 164 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 2,200 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Full Throttle is like

At 164 ft tall with 2,200 ft of track, Full Throttle balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,200 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2013, it is the 13th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Magic Mountain, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Full Throttle sits on the south-east side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tatsu, roughly 100 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Great American Revolution at about 110 m. Full Throttle layout, trains and ride system

Full Throttle starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch and a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once (2 launch sections listed) rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

  1. LSM Launch
  2. Loop (127.1 ft diameter 160 ft tall)
  3. Dive Drop
  4. LSM Launch (multi-pass)

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Full Throttle

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Magic Mountain app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Full Throttle?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Full Throttle, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Full Throttle?

Full Throttle is 164 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Full Throttle go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Full Throttle go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Full Throttle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Full Throttle open?

Full Throttle opened on 22 Jun 2013.

Who built Full Throttle?

Full Throttle was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Full Throttle?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2,200 ft of track.

Does Full Throttle launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch and a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Full Throttle at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Full Throttle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
