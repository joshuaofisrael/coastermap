Gold Rusher

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 35 mph; 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1971.

Gold Rusher is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 29 May 1971. The ride stands 70 ft tall, reaches 35 mph and runs 2,590 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Rusher is like

With 2,590 ft of track for a 70 ft structure, Gold Rusher is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1971, it is the oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Adventure Express at Kings Island, Trailblazer at Hersheypark and Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Great Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Magic Mountain sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 44 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Magic Mountain, out of 13 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Rusher sits near the middle of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Full Throttle, roughly 160 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wonder Woman Flight of Courage at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Rusher?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Gold Rusher, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gold Rusher?

Gold Rusher is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gold Rusher go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gold Rusher go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Rusher scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Gold Rusher open?

Gold Rusher opened on 29 May 1971.

Who built Gold Rusher?

Gold Rusher was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

Is Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Magic Mountain ride opened in 1971 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 70 ft and 35 ft.
