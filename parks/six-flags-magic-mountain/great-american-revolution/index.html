Great American Revolution

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 55 mph; 113 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1976.

Great American Revolution is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Schwarzkopf. It first opened on 8 May 1976 and was previously known as New Revolution and later Revolution and later La Revolución. The ride stands 113 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 3,457 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Great American Revolution is like

Great American Revolution spreads 3,457 ft of track across a layout only 113 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 1 time across 3,457 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.9 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1976, it is the 2nd oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain. It has run under other names (New Revolution, then Revolution, then La Revolución), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Great American Revolution sits on the south-east side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tatsu, roughly 90 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Full Throttle at about 110 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.
