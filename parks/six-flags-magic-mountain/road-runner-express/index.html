Road Runner Express

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Road Runner Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 May 2011. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Road Runner Express is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Road Runner Express balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2011, it is the 12th oldest of the 16 coasters we list at Six Flags Magic Mountain. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America, Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland and Tami-Tami at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Magic Mountain sets 36 in, while Six Flags Great America sets 42 in, Disneyland sets 35 in and PortAventura Park sets 130 cm.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Magic Mountain, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Road Runner Express sits on the north-east side of Magic Mountain's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Canyon Cruiser, roughly 100 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twisted Colossus at about 190 m. Road Runner Express layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 1 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

How Road Runner Express compares

On our intensity ranking Road Runner Express is number 15 of 16 at Six Flags Magic Mountain, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 15th tallest of the park's 16 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Canyon Cruiser (16 ft tall, no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Gold Rusher (70 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Road Runner Express?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Road Runner Express, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Road Runner Express?

Road Runner Express is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Road Runner Express go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Road Runner Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Road Runner Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 1 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Road Runner Express open?

Road Runner Express opened on 28 May 2011.

Who built Road Runner Express?

Road Runner Express was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Road Runner Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 44 seconds over 679.1 ft of track.

How does Road Runner Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Road Runner Express at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

Is Road Runner Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Road Runner Express operate before?

RCDB records it at Six Flags New Orleans (as Road Runner's Express).

Is Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Magic Mountain ride opened in 2011 and the Disneyland ride in 1993; Magic Mountain sets 36 in and Disneyland sets 35 in.
