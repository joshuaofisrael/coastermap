Batman The Ride

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 105 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1994.

Batman The Ride is a steel inverted roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 26 Mar 1994. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,700 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.
