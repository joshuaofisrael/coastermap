Apocalypse the Ride

Six Flags Magic Mountain · Valencia, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50.1 mph; 95 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Apocalypse the Ride is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Magic Mountain in Valencia, California, built by Great Coasters International. It first opened on 23 May 2009 and was previously known as Terminator Salvation: The Ride. The ride stands 95 ft tall, drops 87.3 ft, reaches 50.1 mph and runs 2,877 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Apocalypse the Ride?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Apocalypse the Ride, according to Six Flags Magic Mountain, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Apocalypse the Ride?

Apocalypse the Ride is 95 ft tall, with a drop of 87.3 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Apocalypse the Ride go?

Its top speed is 50.1 mph (81 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Apocalypse the Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Apocalypse the Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Magic Mountain rates it 3 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Apocalypse the Ride open?

Apocalypse the Ride opened on 23 May 2009 (previously known as Terminator Salvation: The Ride).

Who built Apocalypse the Ride?

Apocalypse the Ride was manufactured by Great Coasters International.
