Legoland New York height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Goshen, New York · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Legoland New York in Goshen, New York has 2 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Dragon's Apprentice to 48 in (122 cm) on The Dragon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Legoland New York are like

Of the ride types, none of the 2 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Dragon, which opened in 2021.

What can my child ride at Legoland New York? By height

At Legoland New York the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Dragon's Apprentice (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being The Dragon at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Dragon's Apprentice. With a companion they can also ride The Dragon (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: The Dragon. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: The Dragon and Dragon's Apprentice. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: The Dragon (up to 76 in (193 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Legoland New York

These are the rules Legoland New York states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • The Dragon: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Dragon's Apprentice: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Legoland New York for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Legoland New York, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Dragon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
