Twister Rollercoaster

Crealy Theme Park · Exeter, Devon, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 133 cm (about 52.4 in), or 120 to 133 cm (about 47.2 to 52.4 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Twister Rollercoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Crealy Theme Park in Exeter, Devon, England, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 19 Jul 2015. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twister Rollercoaster is like

Opened in 2015, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Crealy Theme Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coaster (MX52) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Viking Roller Coaster at Energylandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Crealy sets 133 cm, while Energylandia sets 140 cm.

Its 133 cm minimum is the strictest at Crealy. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 133 cm (about 47.2 to 52.4 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is Maximus, roughly 160 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 133 cm (about 52.4 in) tall, or 120 to 133 cm (about 47.2 to 52.4 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twister Rollercoaster?

Riders must be at least 133 cm (about 52.4 in) tall to ride Twister Rollercoaster, or 120 to 133 cm (about 47.2 to 52.4 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Twister, according to Crealy Theme Park, official ride height restrictions (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Twister Rollercoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twister Rollercoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Twister Rollercoaster open?

Twister Rollercoaster opened on 19 Jul 2015.

Who built Twister Rollercoaster?

Twister Rollercoaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

Is Twister Rollercoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 133 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Twister Rollercoaster at Crealy the same as Viking Roller Coaster at Energylandia?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coaster (MX52) coasters on RCDB; the Crealy ride opened in 2015 and the Energylandia ride in 2014; Crealy sets 133 cm and Energylandia sets 140 cm.
