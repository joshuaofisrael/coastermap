Royal Scotsman

Hansa-Park · Sierksdorf, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 31.1 mph; 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Royal Scotsman is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hansa-Park in Sierksdorf, Germany, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1993 and was previously known as Rasender Roland. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 1712.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Royal Scotsman is like

With 1,712.6 ft of track for a 52.5 ft structure, Royal Scotsman is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1993, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Hansa-Park. It has run under other names (Rasender Roland), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Royal Scotsman sits on the west side of Hansa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Nessie, roughly 40 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornwall Coaster at about 80 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Royal Scotsman layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Royal Scotsman compares

On our intensity ranking Royal Scotsman is number 6 of 8 at Hansa-Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Die Schlange von Midgard (32.8 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions; 90 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Cornwall Coaster (59.1 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions; 90 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Royal Scotsman?

Royal Scotsman is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Royal Scotsman go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Royal Scotsman go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Royal Scotsman scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Royal Scotsman open?

Royal Scotsman opened in 1993 (previously known as Rasender Roland).

Who built Royal Scotsman?

Royal Scotsman was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Royal Scotsman?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 10 seconds over 1712.6 ft of track.

How does Royal Scotsman get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Royal Scotsman at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Royal Scotsman good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
