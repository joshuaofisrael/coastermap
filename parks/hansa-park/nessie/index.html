Nessie

Hansa-Park · Sierksdorf, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 49.1 mph; 85.3 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1980.

Nessie is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hansa-Park in Sierksdorf, Germany, built by Schwarzkopf. It first opened on Apr 1980 and was previously known as Nessie Superrollercoaster. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall, reaches 49.1 mph and runs 2431.1 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Nessie is like

At 85.3 ft tall with 2,431.1 ft of track, Nessie balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,431.1 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1980, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Hansa-Park. It has run under other names (Nessie Superrollercoaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Nessie sits on the west side of Hansa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Royal Scotsman, roughly 40 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornwall Coaster at about 80 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Nessie compares

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Nessie?

Nessie is 85.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nessie go?

Its top speed is 49.1 mph (79 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nessie go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Nessie scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Nessie open?

Nessie opened on Apr 1980 (previously known as Nessie Superrollercoaster).

Who built Nessie?

Nessie was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Nessie?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 2431.1 ft of track.

How does Nessie get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Nessie at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,700 riders.

Is Nessie good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 49.1 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
