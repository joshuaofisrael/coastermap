Der kleine Zar

Hansa-Park · Sierksdorf, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 12.3 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Der kleine Zar is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hansa-Park in Sierksdorf, Germany, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 7 Apr 2017. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 12.3 mph and runs 176.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Der kleine Zar is like

At 10.8 ft tall with 176.5 ft of track, Der kleine Zar balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2017, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Hansa-Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Preston & Barbieri's Mini Coaster / Mine Vagon design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Stampbanan at Liseberg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hansa-Park sets 120 cm, while Liseberg sets 110 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Hansa-Park, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Der kleine Zar sits on the south side of Hansa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flucht von Novgorod, roughly 40 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornwall Coaster at about 90 m. Der kleine Zar layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Der kleine Zar?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Der kleine Zar, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm may only ride in the child seat next to an accompanying adult, according to Hansa-Park, official attraction page (size and age limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Der kleine Zar?

Der kleine Zar is 10.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Der kleine Zar go?

Its top speed is 12.3 mph (20 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Der kleine Zar go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Der kleine Zar scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Der kleine Zar open?

Der kleine Zar opened on 7 Apr 2017.

Who built Der kleine Zar?

Der kleine Zar was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How long is the track on Der kleine Zar?

RCDB lists 176.5 ft of track.

How does Der kleine Zar get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Der kleine Zar at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 280 riders.

Is Der kleine Zar good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 12.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Der kleine Zar at Hansa-Park the same as Stampbanan at Liseberg?

Both are Preston & Barbieri Mini Coaster / Mine Vagon coasters on RCDB; the Hansa-Park ride opened in 2017 and the Liseberg ride in 2013; Hansa-Park sets 120 cm and Liseberg sets 110 cm.
