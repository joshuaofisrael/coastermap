Flucht von Novgorod

Hansa-Park · Sierksdorf, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2009.

Flucht von Novgorod is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hansa-Park in Sierksdorf, Germany, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on 9 Apr 2009 and was previously known as Fluch von Novgorod. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 2296.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Flucht von Novgorod is like

Flucht von Novgorod pairs a 131.2 ft structure with 2,296.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,296.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2009, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Hansa-Park. It has run under other names (Fluch von Novgorod), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Hansa-Park, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flucht von Novgorod sits on the south side of Hansa-Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Der kleine Zar, roughly 40 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornwall Coaster at about 90 m. Flucht von Novgorod layout, trains and ride system

Flucht von Novgorod starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. It also uses a vertical chain lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. Dark/Show Section
  2. LSM Launch (rolling) (from 0 mph - 62.1 mph in 1.4 s)
  3. Heartline Roll
  4. Vertical Chain Lift Hill

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Flucht von Novgorod

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Hansa-Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flucht von Novgorod?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Flucht von Novgorod, according to Hansa-Park, official attraction page (size and age limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flucht von Novgorod?

Flucht von Novgorod is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flucht von Novgorod go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flucht von Novgorod go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Flucht von Novgorod scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Flucht von Novgorod open?

Flucht von Novgorod opened on 9 Apr 2009 (previously known as Fluch von Novgorod).

Who built Flucht von Novgorod?

Flucht von Novgorod was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Flucht von Novgorod?

RCDB lists 2296.6 ft of track.

Does Flucht von Novgorod launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Flucht von Novgorod at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Flucht von Novgorod good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
