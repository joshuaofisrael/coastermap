Hong Kong Disneyland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Lantau Island, Hong Kong · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Hong Kong Disneyland in Lantau Island, Hong Kong has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 95 cm (about 37.4 in) on Wandering Oaken’s Sliding Sleighs to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on RC Racer. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Hong Kong Disneyland are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Hong Kong Disneyland a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 52% of coasters across all 69 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 44.1 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Hyperspace Mountain, which opened in 2005.

Hong Kong Disneyland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Hong Kong Disneyland? By height

At Hong Kong Disneyland the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Wandering Oaken’s Sliding Sleighs at 95 cm (about 37.4 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Hyperspace Mountain and Wandering Oaken’s Sliding Sleighs. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars at 112 cm (about 44.1 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars and RC Racer. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Hyperspace Mountain, Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars, RC Racer and Wandering Oaken’s Sliding Sleighs. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Hong Kong Disneyland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Hong Kong Disneyland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wandering Oaken’s Sliding Sleighs, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (95 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Hyperspace Mountain, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (102 cm minimum; 100 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars, the most intense coaster in the park (112 cm minimum; 34.8 mph, no inversions).
