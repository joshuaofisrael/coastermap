Hyperspace Mountain

Hong Kong Disneyland · Lantau Island, Hong Kong

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 102 cm (about 40.2 in); 100 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Hyperspace Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hong Kong Disneyland in Lantau Island, Hong Kong, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 12 Sep 2005 and was previously known as Space Mountain. The ride stands 100 ft tall and runs 3,773 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hyperspace Mountain is like

With 3,773 ft of track for a 100 ft structure, Hyperspace Mountain is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2005, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hong Kong Disneyland. It has run under other names (Space Mountain), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 102 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Hong Kong Disneyland, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hyperspace Mountain sits on the north side of Hong Kong Disneyland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars, roughly 450 m away (112 cm minimum). Next closest is RC Racer at about 460 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hyperspace Mountain?

Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall to ride Hyperspace Mountain, according to Hong Kong Disneyland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hyperspace Mountain?

Hyperspace Mountain is 100 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Hyperspace Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hyperspace Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Hyperspace Mountain open?

Hyperspace Mountain opened on 12 Sep 2005 (previously known as Space Mountain).

Who built Hyperspace Mountain?

Hyperspace Mountain was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Hyperspace Mountain?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 3,773 ft of track.

How does Hyperspace Mountain get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hyperspace Mountain at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Hyperspace Mountain good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 102 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
