Beto Carrero World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Penha, Santa Catarina, Brazil · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Beto Carrero World in Penha, Santa Catarina, Brazil has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 80 cm (about 31.5 in) on Jacaré Dum-Dum to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on FireWhip. By the park's own centimetre lines: at 80 cm a child can ride 1 alone coaster; at 95 cm, 2 alone; at 130 cm, all 3.

What the coasters at Beto Carrero World are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Beto Carrero World a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 37.4 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tigor Mountain, which opened in 2007.

Beto Carrero World publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Beto Carrero World? By height

At Beto Carrero World the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Jacaré Dum-Dum. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Tigor Mountain at 95 cm (about 37.4 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Tigor Mountain. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being FireWhip at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Jacaré Dum-Dum and Tigor Mountain. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being FireWhip at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: FireWhip. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.
