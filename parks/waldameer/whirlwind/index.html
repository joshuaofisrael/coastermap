Whirlwind

Waldameer Park · Erie, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Whirlwind is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Waldameer Park in Erie, Pennsylvania, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 3 Jul 2020. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Whirlwind is like

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Waldameer Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Krazy Koaster at Silverwood. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Waldameer, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Whirlwind sits near the middle of Waldameer's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Comet, roughly 110 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Steel Dragon at about 110 m. Whirlwind layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Before you ride Whirlwind

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 77 in (196 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Whirlwind?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Whirlwind, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Waldameer, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Whirlwind go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Whirlwind scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Whirlwind open?

Whirlwind opened on 3 Jul 2020.

Who built Whirlwind?

Whirlwind was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Whirlwind get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Whirlwind at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Whirlwind good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Whirlwind at Waldameer the same as Krazy Koaster at Silverwood?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A coasters on RCDB; the Waldameer ride opened in 2020 and the Silverwood ride in 2014; both list a 48 in minimum.
