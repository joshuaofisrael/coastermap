Ravine Flyer 3

Waldameer Park · Erie, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Ravine Flyer 3 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Waldameer Park in Erie, Pennsylvania, built by E&F Miler Industries. It opened in 2000. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ravine Flyer 3 is like

Opened in 2000, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Waldameer Park.

Its 40 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Waldameer, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ravine Flyer 3 sits near the middle of Waldameer's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ravine Flyer II, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Comet at about 110 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ravine Flyer 3?

Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall to ride Ravine Flyer 3, according to Waldameer, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Ravine Flyer 3 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ravine Flyer 3 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Ravine Flyer 3 open?

Ravine Flyer 3 opened in 2000.

Who built Ravine Flyer 3?

Ravine Flyer 3 was manufactured by E&F Miler Industries.
