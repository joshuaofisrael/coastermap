Ravine Flyer II

Waldameer Park · Erie, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57 mph; 80 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Ravine Flyer II is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Waldameer Park in Erie, Pennsylvania. It opened on 17 May 2008. The ride stands 80 ft tall, drops 118 ft, reaches 57 mph and runs 2,900 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ravine Flyer II is like

Ravine Flyer II spreads 2,900 ft of track across a layout only 80 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. Its 118 ft drop is longer than its 80 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 2008, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Waldameer Park.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Waldameer, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ravine Flyer II sits on the north side of Waldameer's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ravine Flyer 3, roughly 50 m away (40 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Whirlwind at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ravine Flyer II?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Ravine Flyer II, according to Waldameer, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ravine Flyer II?

Ravine Flyer II is 80 ft tall, with a drop of 118 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ravine Flyer II go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ravine Flyer II go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ravine Flyer II scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ravine Flyer II open?

Ravine Flyer II opened on 17 May 2008.
