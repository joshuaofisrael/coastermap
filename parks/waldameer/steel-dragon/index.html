Steel Dragon

Waldameer Park · Erie, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm), or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 50.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Steel Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Waldameer Park in Erie, Pennsylvania, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 2 Jul 2004. The ride stands 50.8 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1391.1 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Steel Dragon is like

Steel Dragon pairs a 50.8 ft structure with 1,391.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2004, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Waldameer Park. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Laff Trakk at Hersheypark, Spider at Lagoon, Phoenix at Adventureland and Whirlwind at Seabreeze. Height rules for this model differ by park: Waldameer sets 50 in, while Hersheypark sets 42 in, Adventureland sets 48 in and Seabreeze sets 48 in.

Its 50 in minimum is the strictest at Waldameer. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Dragon sits on the west side of Waldameer's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Whirlwind, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ravine Flyer II at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

How Steel Dragon compares

Before you ride Steel Dragon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 50 in (127 cm), and riders from 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 78 in (198 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Waldameer Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Dragon?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Steel Dragon, or 46 to 50 in (117 to 127 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Waldameer, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Dragon?

Steel Dragon is 50.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Dragon go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Dragon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steel Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Steel Dragon open?

Steel Dragon opened on 2 Jul 2004.

Who built Steel Dragon?

Steel Dragon was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Steel Dragon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 24 seconds over 1391.1 ft of track.

How does Steel Dragon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steel Dragon at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 930 riders.

Is Steel Dragon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Steel Dragon at Waldameer the same as Spider at Lagoon?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 coasters on RCDB; the Waldameer ride opened in 2004 and the Lagoon ride in 2003; both list a 50 in minimum.
