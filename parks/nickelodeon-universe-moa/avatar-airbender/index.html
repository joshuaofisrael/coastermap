Avatar Airbender

Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) · Bloomington, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Avatar Airbender is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) in Bloomington, Minnesota, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 15 Mar 2008. The ride stands 70 ft tall. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Avatar Airbender is like

Opened in 2008, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America). It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Surfrider / 20m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Flash: Speed Force at Movie World and Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc at Nickelodeon Universe NJ. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Nickelodeon Universe MOA, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Avatar Airbender layout, trains and ride system

Avatar Airbender starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Avatar Airbender?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Avatar Airbender, according to Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America), official FAQ (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Avatar Airbender?

Avatar Airbender is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Avatar Airbender go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Avatar Airbender scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Avatar Airbender open?

Avatar Airbender opened on 15 Mar 2008.

Who built Avatar Airbender?

Avatar Airbender was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Avatar Airbender launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

Is Avatar Airbender good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Avatar Airbender at Nickelodeon Universe MOA the same as Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc at Nickelodeon Universe NJ?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Surfrider / 20m coasters on RCDB; the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride opened in 2008 and the Nickelodeon Universe NJ ride in 2019; both list a 48 in minimum.
