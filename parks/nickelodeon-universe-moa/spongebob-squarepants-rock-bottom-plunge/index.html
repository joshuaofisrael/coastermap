SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge

Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) · Bloomington, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 43.5 mph; 74.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2008.

SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) in Bloomington, Minnesota, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 15 Mar 2008. The ride stands 74.5 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1371.4 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge is like

At 74.5 ft tall with 1,371.4 ft of track, SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,371.4 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2008, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America).

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Nickelodeon Universe MOA, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge, according to Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America), official FAQ (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge?

SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge is 74.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge open?

SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge opened on 15 Mar 2008.

Who built SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge?

SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 1371.4 ft of track.

How does SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
