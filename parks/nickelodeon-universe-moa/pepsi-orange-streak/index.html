Pepsi Orange Streak

Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) · Bloomington, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion; top speed 30 mph; 60 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

Pepsi Orange Streak is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) in Bloomington, Minnesota, built by Zierer. It first opened on 11 Aug 1992 and was previously known as Pepsi Ripsaw. The ride stands 60 ft tall, drops 40 ft, reaches 30 mph and runs 2,680 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pepsi Orange Streak is like

Pepsi Orange Streak spreads 2,680 ft of track across a layout only 60 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a 40 ft drop against a 60 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 1992, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America). It has run under other names (Pepsi Ripsaw), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 47 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nickelodeon Universe MOA (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Pepsi Orange Streak layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels (60 ft tall, with a 40 ft drop). RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pepsi Orange Streak?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Pepsi Orange Streak, or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion. Riders 42 to 47 inches must ride with a chaperone, according to Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America), official FAQ (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pepsi Orange Streak?

Pepsi Orange Streak is 60 ft tall, with a drop of 40 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pepsi Orange Streak go?

Its top speed is 30 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pepsi Orange Streak go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pepsi Orange Streak scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Pepsi Orange Streak open?

Pepsi Orange Streak opened on 11 Aug 1992 (previously known as Pepsi Ripsaw).

Who built Pepsi Orange Streak?

Pepsi Orange Streak was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Pepsi Orange Streak?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 2,680 ft of track.

How does Pepsi Orange Streak get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pepsi Orange Streak at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train.

Is Pepsi Orange Streak good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 30 mph; the minimum height is 47 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
