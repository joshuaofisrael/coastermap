Fairly Odd Coaster

Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) · Bloomington, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion; top speed 31 mph; 54 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Fairly Odd Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) in Bloomington, Minnesota, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on 15 Mar 2004 and was previously known as Timberland Twister. The ride stands 54 ft tall, reaches 31 mph and runs 1,345 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Fairly Odd Coaster is like

Fairly Odd Coaster pairs a 54 ft structure with 1,345 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2004, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America). It has run under other names (Timberland Twister), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / 420/4 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Spinning Dragons at Worlds of Fun. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 47 in, while Worlds of Fun sets 48 in.

Its 47 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nickelodeon Universe MOA (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Fairly Odd Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 47 in (119 cm), and riders from 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 43 to 47 inches must ride with a chaperone.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fairly Odd Coaster?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Fairly Odd Coaster, or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion. Riders 43 to 47 inches must ride with a chaperone, according to Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America), official FAQ (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fairly Odd Coaster?

Fairly Odd Coaster is 54 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fairly Odd Coaster go?

Its top speed is 31 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fairly Odd Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fairly Odd Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Fairly Odd Coaster open?

Fairly Odd Coaster opened on 15 Mar 2004 (previously known as Timberland Twister).

Who built Fairly Odd Coaster?

Fairly Odd Coaster was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Fairly Odd Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1,345 ft of track.

How does Fairly Odd Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Fairly Odd Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Fairly Odd Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31 mph; the minimum height is 47 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Fairly Odd Coaster at Nickelodeon Universe MOA the same as Spinning Dragons at Worlds of Fun?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / 420/4 coasters on RCDB; the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride opened in 2004 and the Worlds of Fun ride in 2004; Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 47 in and Worlds of Fun sets 48 in.
