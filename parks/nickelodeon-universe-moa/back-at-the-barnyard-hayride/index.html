Back at the Barnyard Hayride

Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) · Bloomington, Minnesota

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 1995.

Back at the Barnyard Hayride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America) in Bloomington, Minnesota, built by Zamperla. It first opened in 1995 and was previously known as Li'l Shaver. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Back at the Barnyard Hayride is like

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Nickelodeon Universe (Mall of America). It has run under other names (Li'l Shaver), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Powered Coaster / Mini Mine Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Tiny Toot at Silverwood.

Frequently asked questions

Does Back at the Barnyard Hayride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Back at the Barnyard Hayride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Back at the Barnyard Hayride open?

Back at the Barnyard Hayride opened in 1995 (previously known as Li'l Shaver).

Who built Back at the Barnyard Hayride?

Back at the Barnyard Hayride was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Back at the Barnyard Hayride?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds.

How many people ride Back at the Barnyard Hayride at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Back at the Barnyard Hayride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Back at the Barnyard Hayride at Nickelodeon Universe MOA the same as Tiny Toot at Silverwood?

Both are Zamperla Powered Coaster / Mini Mine Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride opened in 1995 and the Silverwood ride in 1998.
