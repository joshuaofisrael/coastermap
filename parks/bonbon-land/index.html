BonBon-Land height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Holme-Olstrup, Denmark · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

BonBon-Land in Holme-Olstrup, Denmark has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 115 cm (about 45.3 in) on Hundeprutten to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Vildsvinet. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at BonBon-Land are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes BonBon-Land a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 217 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Hundeprutten, which opened in 1993.

BonBon-Land publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at BonBon-Land? By height

At BonBon-Land the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Viktor Vandorm (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Hundeprutten at 115 cm (about 45.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Hundeprutten (95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in)) and Viktor Vandorm (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Hankatten at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Hundeprutten and Viktor Vandorm. With a companion they can also ride Hankatten (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Vildsvinet at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Hankatten. With a companion they can also ride Vildsvinet (125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at BonBon-Land

These are the rules BonBon-Land states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Hundeprutten: riders 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Vildsvinet: riders 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Hankatten: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Viktor Vandorm: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at BonBon-Land for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for BonBon-Land, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Hundeprutten, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (115 cm minimum; 14.8 ft tall, 18.6 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Hankatten, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions, 3.3 g).
  3. The big one: Vildsvinet, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 72 ft tall, 45 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
