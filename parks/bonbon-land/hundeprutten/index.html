Hundeprutten

BonBon-Land · Holme-Olstrup, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 115 cm (about 45.3 in), or 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) with a companion; top speed 18.6 mph; 14.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Hundeprutten is a steel sit-down roller coaster at BonBon-Land in Holme-Olstrup, Denmark, built by Zierer. It opened in 1993. The ride stands 14.8 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 413.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hundeprutten is like

Hundeprutten pairs a 14.8 ft structure with 413.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1993, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at BonBon-Land. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Force / One design, and the first of them to open. Others include Indy-Blitz at Heide Park and Draken at Furuvik. Height rules for this model differ by park: BonBon-Land sets 115 cm, while Heide Park sets 100 cm and Furuvik sets 120 cm.

Its 115 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at BonBon-Land, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hundeprutten sits on the south side of BonBon-Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vildsvinet, roughly 120 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hankatten at about 120 m. Hundeprutten layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 115 cm (about 45.3 in) tall, or 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Hundeprutten

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 115 cm (about 45.3 in), and riders from 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 95 to 115 cm must ride with a companion aged 15 or older.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hundeprutten?

Riders must be at least 115 cm (about 45.3 in) tall to ride Hundeprutten, or 95 to 115 cm (about 37.4 to 45.3 in) with a companion. Riders 95 to 115 cm must ride with a companion aged 15 or older, according to BonBon-Land, official height requirements (Højdekrav, PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hundeprutten?

Hundeprutten is 14.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hundeprutten go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hundeprutten go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hundeprutten scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Hundeprutten open?

Hundeprutten opened in 1993.

Who built Hundeprutten?

Hundeprutten was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Hundeprutten?

RCDB lists 413.4 ft of track.

How does Hundeprutten get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hundeprutten at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 650 riders.

Is Hundeprutten good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph; the minimum height is 115 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Hundeprutten at BonBon-Land the same as Indy-Blitz at Heide Park?

Both are Zierer Force / One coasters on RCDB; the BonBon-Land ride opened in 1993 and the Heide Park ride in 2008; BonBon-Land sets 115 cm and Heide Park sets 100 cm.
