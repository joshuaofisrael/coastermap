Bobsleigh

Parc de la Vallée · Massais (Val-en-Vignes), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Bobsleigh is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc de la Vallée in Massais (Val-en-Vignes), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened in May 2018. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bobsleigh is like

Opened in 2018, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Parc de la Vallée.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bobsleigh sits near the middle of Parc de la Vallée's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Chenille Vallée, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grand Canyon at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Bobsleigh

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Le Bobsleigh: from 1.20 m, and children under 1.40 m must be accompanied. The park's booking site gives the same rule.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parc de la Vallée app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bobsleigh?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Bobsleigh, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Le Bobsleigh: from 1.20 m, and children under 1.40 m must be accompanied. The park's booking site gives the same rule, according to Parc de la Vallée, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Bobsleigh go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bobsleigh scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Bobsleigh open?

Bobsleigh opened in May 2018.

Who built Bobsleigh?

Bobsleigh was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Bobsleigh?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds.

How many people ride Bobsleigh at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Bobsleigh good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Bobsleigh operate before?

RCDB records it at Walibi Holland (as City Jet); Drayton Manor (as Jumbo Jet Star); Nigloland (as Bobsleigh).
