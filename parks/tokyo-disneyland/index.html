Tokyo Disneyland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Urayasu, Japan · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tokyo Disneyland in Urayasu, Japan has 2 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Gadget's Go Coaster to 102 cm (about 40.2 in) on Big Thunder Mountain. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Tokyo Disneyland are like

Of the ride types, none of the 2 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Big Thunder Mountain, which opened in 1987.

Tokyo Disneyland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Tokyo Disneyland? By height

At Tokyo Disneyland the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Gadget's Go Coaster. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Big Thunder Mountain at 102 cm (about 40.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Big Thunder Mountain. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Big Thunder Mountain and Gadget's Go Coaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Tokyo Disneyland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Tokyo Disneyland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Gadget's Go Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.7 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Big Thunder Mountain, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (102 cm minimum; no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
