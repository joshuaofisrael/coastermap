Tokyo Disneyland roller coasters

Urayasu, Japan · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tokyo Disneyland in Urayasu, Japan has 2 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Gadget's Go Coaster at 27.9 ft, which is also the fastest at 21.7 mph. The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), for Gadget's Go Coaster.
