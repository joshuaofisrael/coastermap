Gadget's Go Coaster

Tokyo Disneyland · Urayasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Gadget's Go Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tokyo Disneyland in Urayasu, Japan, built by Vekoma. It opened on 15 Apr 1996. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gadget's Go Coaster is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Gadget's Go Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

It is one of 14 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America and Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Tokyo Disneyland sets 90 cm, while Six Flags Great America sets 42 in, PortAventura Park sets 130 cm and Universal Studios Japan sets 122 cm.

Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Big Thunder Mountain, roughly 530 m away (102 cm minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gadget's Go Coaster?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Gadget's Go Coaster, according to Tokyo Disney Resort, official FAQ on attractions with height requirements (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gadget's Go Coaster?

Gadget's Go Coaster is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gadget's Go Coaster go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gadget's Go Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gadget's Go Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Gadget's Go Coaster open?

Gadget's Go Coaster opened on 15 Apr 1996.

Who built Gadget's Go Coaster?

Gadget's Go Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Gadget's Go Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 56 seconds over 679.1 ft of track.

How does Gadget's Go Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Gadget's Go Coaster at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

What restraints does Gadget's Go Coaster use?

RCDB lists: single position lap bar.

Is Gadget's Go Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Gadget's Go Coaster at Tokyo Disneyland the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Tokyo Disneyland ride opened in 1996 and the Disneyland ride in 1993; both list a 90 cm minimum.
