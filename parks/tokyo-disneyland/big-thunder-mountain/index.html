Big Thunder Mountain

Tokyo Disneyland · Urayasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 102 cm (about 40.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 1987.

Big Thunder Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tokyo Disneyland in Urayasu, Japan. It opened on 4 Jul 1987. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Thunder Mountain is like

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Gadget's Go Coaster, roughly 530 m away (90 cm minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Thunder Mountain?

Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall to ride Big Thunder Mountain, according to Tokyo Disney Resort, official FAQ on attractions with height requirements (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Big Thunder Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Thunder Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Thunder Mountain open?

Big Thunder Mountain opened on 4 Jul 1987.

How long is the ride on Big Thunder Mountain?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 40 seconds.

Is Big Thunder Mountain good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 102 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Big Thunder Mountain at Tokyo Disneyland the same as Big Thunder Mountain at Disneyland Paris?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Tokyo Disneyland ride opened in 1987 and the Disneyland Paris ride in 1992; both list a 102 cm minimum.
