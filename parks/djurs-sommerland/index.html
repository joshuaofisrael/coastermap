Djurs Sommerland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Nimtofte, Denmark · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Djurs Sommerland in Nimtofte, Denmark has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Vilde Hønsejagt to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Piraten. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Djurs Sommerland are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 4 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Djurs Sommerland a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 12% of its lineup, against 43% across all 159 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 8 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Juvelen).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Thor's Hammer, which opened in 2002.

Djurs Sommerland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Djurs Sommerland? By height

At Djurs Sommerland the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Vilde Hønsejagt (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)), Jungle Rally (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and T-Rex Family Coaster (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Thor's Hammer at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Thor's Hammer (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Vilde Hønsejagt (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)), Jungle Rally (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and T-Rex Family Coaster (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Skatteøen at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Vilde Hønsejagt and Jungle Rally. With a companion they can also ride Thor's Hammer (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)), Skatteøen (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)), Juvelen (120 to 132 cm (about 47.2 to 52 in)), DrageKongen (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)) and T-Rex Family Coaster (90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Piraten at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Thor's Hammer, Skatteøen, Juvelen, DrageKongen and T-Rex Family Coaster. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Piraten at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Piraten (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)) and DrageKongen (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Djurs Sommerland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Djurs Sommerland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Jungle Rally, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Skatteøen, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 91.9 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Piraten, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 101.7 ft tall, 52.8 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
