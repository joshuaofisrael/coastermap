Hopi Hari height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Hopi Hari in Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Speedi '64 to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Montezum. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 1 of them.

What the coasters at Hopi Hari are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Hopi Hari a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Montezum).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Montezum, which opened in 1999.

Hopi Hari publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Hopi Hari? By height

At Hopi Hari the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Hopi Hari for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Hopi Hari, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Speedi '64, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Vurang, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Montezum, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 64 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Hopi Hari has 4 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 11 Nov 2026.
