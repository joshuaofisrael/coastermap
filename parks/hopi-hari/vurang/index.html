Vurang

Hopi Hari · Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Vurang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hopi Hari in Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened in 1999. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vurang is like

Opened in 1999, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Hopi Hari.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Hopi Hari, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vurang sits near the middle of Hopi Hari's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speedi '64, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Katapul at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Before you ride Vurang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Hopi Hari app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vurang?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Vurang, according to Hopi Hari, official thrill attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Vurang go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vurang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Vurang open?

Vurang opened in 1999.

Who built Vurang?

Vurang was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Is Vurang good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
