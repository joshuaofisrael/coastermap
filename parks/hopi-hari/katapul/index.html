Katapul

Hopi Hari · Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 53 mph; 137.8 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1999.

Katapul is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hopi Hari in Vinhedo, São Paulo, Brazil, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened in 1999. The ride stands 137.8 ft tall, reaches 53 mph and runs 722 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Katapul is like

For a 137.8 ft ride, 722 ft of track is short: Katapul is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. It goes upside down 1 time across 722 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1999, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hopi Hari. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Schwarzkopf's Shuttle Loop / Weight Drop design, and the first of them to open. Others include Golden Loop at Gold Reef City. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hopi Hari sets 140 cm, while Gold Reef City sets 130 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Hopi Hari, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Katapul sits on the north-west side of Hopi Hari's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speedi '64, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Vurang at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Katapul

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Hopi Hari app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Katapul?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Katapul, according to Hopi Hari, official thrill attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Katapul?

Katapul is 137.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Katapul go?

Its top speed is 53 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Katapul go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Katapul scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Katapul open?

Katapul opened in 1999.

Who built Katapul?

Katapul was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the track on Katapul?

RCDB lists 722 ft (220 m) of track.

Does Katapul launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a launch.

Is Katapul good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 53 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Katapul operate before?

RCDB records it at Kings Dominion (as King Kobra); Jolly Roger Amusement Park (as King Kobra); Alton Towers (as Thunderlooper).

Is Katapul at Hopi Hari the same as Golden Loop at Gold Reef City?

Both are Schwarzkopf Shuttle Loop / Weight Drop coasters on RCDB; Hopi Hari sets 140 cm and Gold Reef City sets 130 cm.
