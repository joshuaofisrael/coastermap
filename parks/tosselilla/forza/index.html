Forza

Tosselilla Sommarland · Tomelilla, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 40 mph; 55 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Forza is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tosselilla Sommarland in Tomelilla, Sweden, built by Zierer. It opened on 2 Jul 2022. The ride stands 55 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 1,260 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Forza is like

Forza pairs a 55 ft structure with 1,260 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Tosselilla Sommarland.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Tosselilla (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Forza sits on the west side of Tosselilla's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kålmasken, roughly 180 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mad Mouse at about 250 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Forza?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Forza, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion. 120 cm, or 110 cm with an adult, according to Tosselilla, official activity page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Forza?

Forza is 55 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Forza go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Forza go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Forza scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Forza open?

Forza opened on 2 Jul 2022.

Who built Forza?

Forza was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Forza?

RCDB lists 1,260 ft (384 m) of track.

How does Forza get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Forza at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Forza good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Forza operate before?

RCDB records it at Bowcraft Playland (as Crossbow); Tosselilla (as Forza).
