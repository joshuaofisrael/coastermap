Tosselilla Sommarland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Tomelilla, Sweden · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tosselilla Sommarland in Tomelilla, Sweden has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Kålmasken to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Mad Mouse. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Tosselilla Sommarland are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Tosselilla Sommarland a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Kålmasken, which opened in 2004.

Tosselilla Sommarland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Tosselilla Sommarland? By height

At Tosselilla Sommarland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Tosselilla Sommarland
