Speedy Bob

Bobbejaanland · Lichtaart, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Speedy Bob is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bobbejaanland in Lichtaart, Belgium, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 2 Jul 1998. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Speedy Bob is like

At 45.9 ft tall with 1,213.9 ft of track, Speedy Bob balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Bobbejaanland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Wild Mouse at Hersheypark and Double Wild Mouse at Nagashima Spa Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bobbejaanland sets 140 cm, while Hersheypark sets 48 in and Nagashima Spa Land sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Bobbejaanland, shared with 5 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Speedy Bob sits on the south-east side of Bobbejaanland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Revolution, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dreamcatcher at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Speedy Bob compares

On our intensity ranking Speedy Bob is number 7 of 8 at Bobbejaanland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Speedy Bob

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Speedy Bob?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Speedy Bob, or 110 to 140 cm (about 43.3 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Bobbejaanland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Speedy Bob?

Speedy Bob is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Speedy Bob go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Speedy Bob go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Speedy Bob scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Speedy Bob open?

Speedy Bob opened on 2 Jul 1998.

Who built Speedy Bob?

Speedy Bob was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Speedy Bob?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 1213.9 ft of track.

How does Speedy Bob get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Speedy Bob at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,120 riders.

Is Speedy Bob good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Speedy Bob at Bobbejaanland the same as Double Wild Mouse at Nagashima Spa Land?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile coasters on RCDB; the Bobbejaanland ride opened in 1998 and the Nagashima Spa Land ride in 1996; Bobbejaanland sets 140 cm and Nagashima Spa Land sets 120 cm.
