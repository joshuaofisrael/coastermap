Dreamcatcher

Bobbejaanland · Lichtaart, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 82 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1987.

Dreamcatcher is a steel suspended roller coaster at Bobbejaanland in Lichtaart, Belgium, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 30 Apr 1987 and was previously known as Air Race. The ride stands 82 ft tall and runs 1968.5 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dreamcatcher is like

Dreamcatcher pairs a 82 ft structure with 1,968.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1987, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Bobbejaanland. It has run under other names (Air Race), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Swinging Turns design, and the first of them to open. Others include Grampus Jet at Greenland and Sky Coaster at Dream World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bobbejaanland sets 130 cm, while Greenland sets 110 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Bobbejaanland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dreamcatcher sits on the east side of Bobbejaanland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Oki Doki, roughly 170 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Speedy Bob at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dreamcatcher?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Dreamcatcher, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Bobbejaanland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dreamcatcher?

Dreamcatcher is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Dreamcatcher go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dreamcatcher scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Dreamcatcher open?

Dreamcatcher opened on 30 Apr 1987 (previously known as Air Race).

Who built Dreamcatcher?

Dreamcatcher was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Dreamcatcher?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 44 seconds over 1968.5 ft of track.

How does Dreamcatcher get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dreamcatcher at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Dreamcatcher good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Dreamcatcher at Bobbejaanland the same as Sky Coaster at Dream World?

Both are Vekoma Swinging Turns coasters on RCDB; the Bobbejaanland ride opened in 1987 and the Dream World ride in 1994; both list a 130 cm minimum.
