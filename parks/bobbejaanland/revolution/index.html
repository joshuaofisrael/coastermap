Revolution

Bobbejaanland · Lichtaart, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 31.1 mph; 60.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1989.

Revolution is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bobbejaanland in Lichtaart, Belgium, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 15 Jun 1989 and was previously known as Evolution. The ride stands 60.8 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 2362.2 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Revolution is like

With 2,362.2 ft of track for a 60.8 ft structure, Revolution is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1989, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Bobbejaanland. It has run under other names (Evolution), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Bobbejaanland, shared with 5 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Revolution sits on the south side of Bobbejaanland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speedy Bob, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Fury at about 140 m. Revolution layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

How Revolution compares

On our intensity ranking Revolution is number 6 of 8 at Bobbejaanland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Revolution?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Revolution, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Bobbejaanland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Revolution?

Revolution is 60.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Revolution go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Revolution go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Revolution scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Revolution open?

Revolution opened on 15 Jun 1989 (previously known as Evolution).

Who built Revolution?

Revolution was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Revolution?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 2362.2 ft of track.

How does Revolution get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Revolution at once?

RCDB's train details give 60 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Revolution good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
