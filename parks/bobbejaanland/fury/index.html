Fury

Bobbejaanland · Lichtaart, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 66.2 mph; 141.1 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2019.

Fury is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bobbejaanland in Lichtaart, Belgium, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 24 Jun 2019. The ride stands 141.1 ft tall, reaches 66.2 mph and runs 1968.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Fury is like

Fury pairs a 141.1 ft structure with 1,968.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,968.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Bobbejaanland.

Its 130 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Bobbejaanland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fury sits on the south-west side of Bobbejaanland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Naga Bay, roughly 80 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Typhoon at about 110 m. Fury layout, trains and ride system

Fury starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (8 entries):

  2. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  3. Jr. Scorpion Tail
  4. Top Hat - Outside
  5. Wraparound Corkscrew
  6. Corkscrew
  7. Jr. Scorpion Tail
  8. Turntable

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

General guidance from how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule. See best seat on a roller coaster.

How Fury compares

On our intensity ranking Fury is number 2 of 8 at Bobbejaanland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Oki Doki (53.5 ft tall, 36 mph, no inversions, 2.8 g; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Typhoon (84.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 4 inversions, 5 g; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Fury

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Bobbejaanland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fury?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Fury, according to Bobbejaanland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fury?

Fury is 141.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fury go?

Its top speed is 66.2 mph (107 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fury go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Fury scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fury open?

Fury opened on 24 Jun 2019.

Who built Fury?

Fury was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Fury?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1968.5 ft of track.

Does Fury launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

Is Fury good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 66.2 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
