Typhoon

Bobbejaanland · Lichtaart, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 49.7 mph; 84.3 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2004.

Typhoon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bobbejaanland in Lichtaart, Belgium, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 10 Apr 2004. The ride stands 84.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2198.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Typhoon is like

Typhoon pairs a 84.3 ft structure with 2,198.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,198.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2004, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Bobbejaanland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Bobbejaanland, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Typhoon sits on the south-west side of Bobbejaanland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Fury, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Naga Bay at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Typhoon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Bobbejaanland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Typhoon?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Typhoon, or 125 to 140 cm (about 49.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Bobbejaanland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Typhoon?

Typhoon is 84.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Typhoon go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Typhoon go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Typhoon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Typhoon open?

Typhoon opened on 10 Apr 2004.

Who built Typhoon?

Typhoon was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Typhoon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2198.2 ft of track.

How does Typhoon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Typhoon at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Typhoon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
