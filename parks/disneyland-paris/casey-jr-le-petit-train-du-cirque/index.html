Casey Jr., le Petit Train du Cirque

Disneyland Park (Paris) · Marne-la-Vallée, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 1994.

Casey Jr., le Petit Train du Cirque is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park (Paris) in Marne-la-Vallée, France, built by Vekoma. It opened on Mar 1994. The ride runs 984.3 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Casey Jr., le Petit Train du Cirque is like

Opened in 1994, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Disneyland Park (Paris).

Going by RCDB's map positions, Casey Jr., le Petit Train du Cirque sits on the north side of Disneyland Paris' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Star Wars Hyperspace Mountain: Rebel Mission, roughly 360 m away (120 cm minimum). Next closest is Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril at about 470 m.
