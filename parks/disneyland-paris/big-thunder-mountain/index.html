Big Thunder Mountain

Disneyland Park (Paris) · Marne-la-Vallée, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 102 cm (about 40.2 in); top speed 40.4 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

Big Thunder Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park (Paris) in Marne-la-Vallée, France, built by Vekoma. It opened on 12 Apr 1992. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, drops 39.3 ft, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 3953.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Thunder Mountain is like

Big Thunder Mountain spreads 3,953.4 ft of track across a layout only 72.2 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a 39.3 ft drop against a 72.2 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 1992, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Disneyland Park (Paris).

Its 102 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Disneyland Paris, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Thunder Mountain sits on the south side of Disneyland Paris' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril, roughly 310 m away (140 cm minimum). Next closest is Star Wars Hyperspace Mountain: Rebel Mission at about 480 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Thunder Mountain?

Riders must be at least 102 cm (about 40.2 in) tall to ride Big Thunder Mountain, according to Disneyland Paris, official attractions page (height filter) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Big Thunder Mountain?

Big Thunder Mountain is 72.2 ft tall, with a drop of 39.3 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Big Thunder Mountain go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Thunder Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Thunder Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Thunder Mountain open?

Big Thunder Mountain opened on 12 Apr 1992.

Who built Big Thunder Mountain?

Big Thunder Mountain was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Big Thunder Mountain?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 56 seconds over 3953.4 ft of track.

How does Big Thunder Mountain get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Big Thunder Mountain at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,424 riders.

Is Big Thunder Mountain good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 102 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Big Thunder Mountain at Disneyland Paris the same as Big Thunder Mountain at Tokyo Disneyland?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Disneyland Paris ride opened in 1992 and the Tokyo Disneyland ride in 1987; both list a 102 cm minimum.
