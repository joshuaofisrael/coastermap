La Récré des 3 Curés height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Milizac, Brittany, France · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

La Récré des 3 Curés in Milizac, Brittany, France has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Train de la Mine to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Vertika. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at La Récré des 3 Curés are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes La Récré des 3 Curés a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 41.3 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Train de la Mine, which opened in 2001.

La Récré des 3 Curés publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at La Récré des 3 Curés? By height

At La Récré des 3 Curés the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Jeepo Dino (90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Train de la Mine at 100 cm (about 39.4 in).

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Train de la Mine and Jeepo Dino. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Vertika at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

Best coasters at La Récré des 3 Curés for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for La Récré des 3 Curés, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Jeepo Dino, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (105 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Train de la Mine, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (100 cm minimum; 32.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Vertika, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 88.6 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 2 inversions).
