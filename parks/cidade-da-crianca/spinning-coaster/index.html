Spinning Coaster

Cidade da Criança · São Bernardo do Campo, São Paulo, Brazil

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Spinning Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cidade da Criança in São Bernardo do Campo, São Paulo, Brazil, built by Zhengzhou Shangdian Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd. It opened on Jan 2023. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinning Coaster is like

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Cidade da Criança.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Cidade da Criança, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Spinning Coaster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Spinning Coaster:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Spinning Coaster trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 4 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 16 riders.
Riders per train16

For families checking heights: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36 and 42 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Spinning Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum 0.90 m, and a companion is needed below 1.10 m.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Cidade da Criança app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinning Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Spinning Coaster, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Minimum 0.90 m, and a companion is needed below 1.10 m, according to Cidade da Criança, official attractions height table (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Spinning Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinning Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Spinning Coaster open?

Spinning Coaster opened on Jan 2023.

Who built Spinning Coaster?

Spinning Coaster was manufactured by Zhengzhou Shangdian Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd.

How does Spinning Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinning Coaster at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Spinning Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Spinning Coaster at Cidade da Criança the same as Spinning Coaster at Cosmoworld?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Cidade da Criança ride opened in 2023 and the Cosmoworld ride in 1998; Cidade da Criança sets 110 cm and Cosmoworld sets 140 cm.
