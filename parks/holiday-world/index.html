Holiday World roller coasters

Santa Claus, Indiana · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Holiday World in Santa Claus, Indiana has 6 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is The Voyage at 159 ft, which is also the fastest at 67 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for The Howler.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Holiday World have?

Holiday World has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Holiday World?

The Voyage, at 159 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Holiday World?

The Voyage, with a top speed of 67 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Holiday World?

By height requirement, The Howler has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Holiday World coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: The Howler. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Holiday World coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Good Gravy!, The Raven, The Howler, The Legend, The Voyage, Thunderbird. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Holiday World do not go upside down?

Good Gravy!, The Raven, The Howler, The Legend, The Voyage have no inversions, per RCDB.
