Holiday World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Santa Claus, Indiana · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Holiday World in Santa Claus, Indiana has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on The Howler to 48 in (122 cm) on Good Gravy!. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Holiday World are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Holiday World a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 52% across all 69 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 6 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (The Raven, The Legend and The Voyage).

The most common builder is Custom Coasters International, Inc., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Raven, which opened in 1995.

What can my child ride at Holiday World? By height

At Holiday World the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride The Howler (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Good Gravy! at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: The Howler. With a companion they can also ride Good Gravy! (38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being The Raven at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Good Gravy!, The Raven, The Legend, The Voyage and Thunderbird. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Good Gravy!, The Raven, The Howler, The Legend, The Voyage and Thunderbird. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Thunderbird (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Holiday World

These are the rules Holiday World states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Good Gravy!: riders 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Howler: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Holiday World for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Holiday World, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Howler, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: The Raven, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 80 ft tall, 48 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Thunderbird, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 140 ft tall, 60 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
