The Raven

Holiday World · Santa Claus, Indiana

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 48 mph; 80 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

The Raven is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Holiday World in Santa Claus, Indiana, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc.. It opened on 6 May 1995. The ride stands 80 ft tall, drops 85 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,800 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Raven?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride The Raven, according to Holiday World, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Raven?

The Raven is 80 ft tall, with a drop of 85 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Raven go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Raven go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Raven scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Raven open?

The Raven opened on 6 May 1995.

Who built The Raven?

The Raven was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc..
