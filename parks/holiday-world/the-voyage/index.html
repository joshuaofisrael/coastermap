The Voyage

Holiday World · Santa Claus, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 67 mph; 159 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2006.

The Voyage is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Holiday World in Santa Claus, Indiana. It opened on 6 May 2006. The ride stands 159 ft tall, drops 154 ft, reaches 67 mph and runs 6,442 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Voyage is like

With 6,442 ft of track for a 159 ft structure, The Voyage is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2006, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Holiday World.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Holiday World, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Voyage sits on the north-east side of Holiday World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Howler, roughly 290 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Legend at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Voyage?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Voyage, according to Holiday World, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Voyage?

The Voyage is 159 ft tall, with a drop of 154 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Voyage go?

Its top speed is 67 mph (108 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Voyage go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Voyage scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Voyage open?

The Voyage opened on 6 May 2006.

How long is the ride on The Voyage?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 45 seconds over 6,442 ft of track.

How does The Voyage get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Voyage at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is The Voyage good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 67 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
