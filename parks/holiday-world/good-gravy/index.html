Good Gravy!

Holiday World · Santa Claus, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 37 mph; 77 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Good Gravy! is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Holiday World in Santa Claus, Indiana, built by Vekoma. It opened on 11 May 2024. The ride stands 77 ft tall, reaches 37 mph and runs 721.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Good Gravy! is like

At 77 ft tall with 721.8 ft of track, Good Gravy! balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Holiday World.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Holiday World, shared with 4 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Good Gravy!

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Holiday World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Good Gravy!?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Good Gravy!, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Holiday World, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Good Gravy!?

Good Gravy! is 77 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Good Gravy! go?

Its top speed is 37 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Good Gravy! go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Good Gravy! scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Good Gravy! open?

Good Gravy! opened on 11 May 2024.

Who built Good Gravy!?

Good Gravy! was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Good Gravy!?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 5 seconds over 721.8 ft of track.

How many people ride Good Gravy! at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Good Gravy! good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
