Vortex

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 90 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1992.

Vortex is a steel stand-up roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 14 Mar 1992. The ride stands 90 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,040 ft of track. On a stand-up coaster riders travel standing, supported by a bicycle-style seat and a shoulder harness. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Vortex is like

Vortex pairs a 90 ft structure with 2,040 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,040 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1992, it is the 4th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Stand-Up Coaster design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Rougarou at Cedar Point, Riddler's Revenge at Magic Mountain, Georgia Scorcher at Six Flags Over Georgia and Patriot at California's Great America. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Carowinds, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vortex sits on the north side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Carolina Goldrusher, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ricochet at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Vortex compares

On our intensity ranking Vortex is number 7 of 14 at Carowinds, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 12 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Hurler (83 ft tall, 50 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Carolina Cyclone (95 ft tall, 41 mph, 4 inversions; 48 in minimum).

Before you ride Vortex

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Carowinds app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vortex?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Vortex, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vortex?

Vortex is 90 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Vortex go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vortex go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Vortex scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Vortex open?

Vortex opened on 14 Mar 1992.

Who built Vortex?

Vortex was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Vortex?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 19 seconds over 2,040 ft of track.

How does Vortex get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vortex at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Vortex good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Vortex at Carowinds the same as Patriot at California's Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Stand-Up Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 1992 and the California's Great America ride in 1991; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 90 ft and 91 ft.
