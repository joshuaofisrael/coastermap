Carolina Cyclone

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 41 mph; 95 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1980.

Carolina Cyclone is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 22 Mar 1980. The ride stands 95 ft tall, drops 65 ft, reaches 41 mph and runs 2,100 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Carolina Cyclone is like

At 95 ft tall with 2,100 ft of track, Carolina Cyclone balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a 65 ft drop against a 95 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,100 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1980, it is the 3rd oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Carowinds, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Carolina Cyclone sits on the north-west side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ricochet, roughly 70 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Carolina Goldrusher at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Carolina Cyclone

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Carowinds app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Carolina Cyclone?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Carolina Cyclone, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Carolina Cyclone?

Carolina Cyclone is 95 ft tall, with a drop of 65 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Carolina Cyclone go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Carolina Cyclone go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Carolina Cyclone scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Carolina Cyclone open?

Carolina Cyclone opened on 22 Mar 1980.

Who built Carolina Cyclone?

Carolina Cyclone was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Carolina Cyclone?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2,100 ft of track.

How does Carolina Cyclone get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Carolina Cyclone at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Carolina Cyclone good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 41 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
