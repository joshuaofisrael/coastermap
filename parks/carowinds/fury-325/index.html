Fury 325

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 95 mph; 325 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Fury 325 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 28 Mar 2015. The ride stands 325 ft tall, drops 320 ft, reaches 95 mph and runs 6,602 ft of track. That height makes it a giga coaster (300 to 399 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Fury 325 is like

Fury 325 pairs a 325 ft structure with 6,602 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 81°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2015, it is the 12th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the 13th of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island and Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Carowinds sets 54 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, Europa-Park sets 140 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Carowinds, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fury 325 sits on the north-east side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurler, roughly 360 m away (48 in minimum). Next closest is Vortex at about 390 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fury 325?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Fury 325, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fury 325?

Fury 325 is 325 ft tall, with a drop of 320 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fury 325 go?

Its top speed is 95 mph (153 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fury 325 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fury 325 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fury 325 open?

Fury 325 opened on 28 Mar 2015.

Who built Fury 325?

Fury 325 was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Fury 325?

RCDB lists 6,602 ft (2,012 m) of track.

How does Fury 325 get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Fury 325 at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,470 riders.

Is Fury 325 good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 95 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Fury 325 at Carowinds the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 2015 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; Carowinds sets 54 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 325 ft and 170 ft.
