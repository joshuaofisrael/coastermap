Fury 325

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 95 mph; 325 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Fury 325 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 28 Mar 2015. The ride stands 325 ft tall, drops 320 ft, reaches 95 mph and runs 6,602 ft of track. That height makes it a giga coaster (300 to 399 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fury 325?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Fury 325, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fury 325?

Fury 325 is 325 ft tall, with a drop of 320 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fury 325 go?

Its top speed is 95 mph (153 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fury 325 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fury 325 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fury 325 open?

Fury 325 opened on 28 Mar 2015.

Who built Fury 325?

Fury 325 was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
