Wilderness Run

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); 16 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Wilderness Run is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by E&F Miler Industries. It first opened in 1998 and was previously known as Taxi Jam and later Hey Arnold's Taxi Chase and later Lucy's Crabbie Cabbie. The ride stands 16 ft tall and runs 416 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wilderness Run is like

At 16 ft tall with 416 ft of track, Wilderness Run balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 6th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It has run under other names (Taxi Jam, then Hey Arnold's Taxi Chase, then Lucy's Crabbie Cabbie), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to E&F Miler Industries's Family Coaster / 16ft Oval w/helix on left design, and the first of them to open. Others include Canyon Cruiser at Magic Mountain.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Carowinds, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wilderness Run sits on the south-east side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock Express, roughly 60 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Thunder Striker at about 60 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wilderness Run?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Wilderness Run, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wilderness Run?

Wilderness Run is 16 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Wilderness Run go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wilderness Run scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Wilderness Run open?

Wilderness Run opened in 1998 (previously known as Taxi Jam and later Hey Arnold's Taxi Chase and later Lucy's Crabbie Cabbie).

Who built Wilderness Run?

Wilderness Run was manufactured by E&F Miler Industries.

How long is the track on Wilderness Run?

RCDB lists 416 ft (127 m) of track.

How does Wilderness Run get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wilderness Run at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Wilderness Run good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wilderness Run at Carowinds the same as Canyon Cruiser at Magic Mountain?

Both are E&F Miler Industries Family Coaster / 16ft Oval w/helix on left coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 1998 and the Magic Mountain ride in 1999.
