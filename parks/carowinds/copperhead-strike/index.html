Copperhead Strike

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 50 mph; 82 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2019.

Copperhead Strike is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 23 Mar 2019. The ride stands 82 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 3,255 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Copperhead Strike is like

With 3,255 ft of track for a 82 ft structure, Copperhead Strike is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 5 times across 3,255 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the 13th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 8 other coasters at Carowinds, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Copperhead Strike sits on the south-west side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flying Cobras, roughly 160 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Afterburn at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Copperhead Strike

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Carowinds app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Copperhead Strike?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Copperhead Strike, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Copperhead Strike?

Copperhead Strike is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Copperhead Strike go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Copperhead Strike go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Copperhead Strike scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Copperhead Strike open?

Copperhead Strike opened on 23 Mar 2019.

Who built Copperhead Strike?

Copperhead Strike was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Copperhead Strike?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 24 seconds over 3,255 ft of track.

Does Copperhead Strike launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Copperhead Strike at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Copperhead Strike good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
