Carowinds roller coasters

Charlotte, North Carolina · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina has 14 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Fury 325 at 325 ft, which is also the fastest at 95 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Wilderness Run.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Carowinds have?

Carowinds has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Carowinds?

Fury 325, at 325 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Carowinds?

Fury 325, with a top speed of 95 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Carowinds?

By height requirement, Wilderness Run has the lowest minimum at 36 in (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Carowinds coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Wilderness Run. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Carowinds coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Carolina Cyclone, Woodstock Express, Carolina Goldrusher, Hurler, Wilderness Run, Flying Cobras, Snoopy's Racing Railway, Kiddy Hawk. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Carowinds do not go upside down?

Woodstock Express, Carolina Goldrusher, Hurler, Wilderness Run, Thunder Striker, Fury 325, Snoopy's Racing Railway, Ricochet, Kiddy Hawk have no inversions, per RCDB.
