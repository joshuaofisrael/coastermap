Carowinds height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Charlotte, North Carolina · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina has 14 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Wilderness Run to 54 in (137 cm) on Vortex. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 8 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 14 of them.

What the coasters at Carowinds are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 8 as extreme, which makes Carowinds a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 57% of its lineup, against 53% across all 62 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 11 of the 14 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 14 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Woodstock Express and Hurler); 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Copperhead Strike and Snoopy's Racing Railway).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Carolina Goldrusher, which opened in 1973.

Fury 325 is the 3rd tallest of the 410 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Fury 325 is the 3rd fastest of the 412 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Carowinds? By height

At Carowinds the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 7 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 14 coasters with a verified rule: Wilderness Run. With a companion they can also ride Snoopy's Racing Railway (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 12 coasters, the nearest being Kiddy Hawk at 44 in (112 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 14 coasters with a verified rule: Wilderness Run. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)) and Snoopy's Racing Railway (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Kiddy Hawk at 44 in (112 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 14 coasters with a verified rule, 7 more than at the height below: Carolina Cyclone, Woodstock Express, Carolina Goldrusher, Hurler, Flying Cobras, Snoopy's Racing Railway and Kiddy Hawk. With a companion they can also ride Ricochet (44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Copperhead Strike at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 14 of 14 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: Vortex, Afterburn, Thunder Striker, Fury 325, Copperhead Strike and Ricochet. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Flying Cobras (up to 80 in (203 cm)), Snoopy's Racing Railway (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and Kiddy Hawk (up to 76 in (193 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

For a personal list, try the ride height checker.

Companion rules at Carowinds

These are the rules Carowinds states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Woodstock Express: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Snoopy's Racing Railway: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Ricochet: riders 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Carowinds for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Carowinds, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Woodstock Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (46 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Snoopy's Racing Railway, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 39.4 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Fury 325, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 325 ft tall, 95 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Carowinds?

Roller coaster height requirements at Carowinds run from 36 in (91 cm) (Wilderness Run) to 54 in (137 cm) (Vortex), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Carowinds have?

Carowinds has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Carowinds?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Fury 325 (325 ft tall, 95 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Carowinds?

Fury 325, at 325 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Carowinds?

Fury 325, with a top speed of 95 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Carowinds for first-timers?

Woodstock Express is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 46 in (117 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Carowinds?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Wilderness Run. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express, Snoopy's Racing Railway.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Carowinds?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Carolina Cyclone, Woodstock Express, Carolina Goldrusher, Hurler, Wilderness Run, Flying Cobras, Snoopy's Racing Railway, Kiddy Hawk. With a companion they can also ride Ricochet.

Which coasters at Carowinds do not go upside down?

Woodstock Express, Carolina Goldrusher, Hurler, Wilderness Run, Thunder Striker, Fury 325, Snoopy's Racing Railway, Ricochet, Kiddy Hawk have no inversions, per RCDB.
