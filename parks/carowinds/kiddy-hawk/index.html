Kiddy Hawk

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 44 in (112 cm); top speed 26 mph; 48.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Kiddy Hawk is a steel inverted roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 22 Mar 2003 and was previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar. The ride stands 48.6 ft tall, reaches 26 mph and runs 1,122 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kiddy Hawk is like

At 48.6 ft tall with 1,122 ft of track, Kiddy Hawk balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2003, it is the 9th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It has run under other names (Flying Ace Aerial Chase, then Rugrats Runaway Reptar), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 342m design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Woodstock's Air Rail at Kings Island, Silver Streak at Canada's Wonderland, The Bat at Lagoon and Kenny's Forest Flyer at Dreamworld. Height rules for this model differ by park: Carowinds sets 44 in, while Lagoon sets 42 in and Dreamworld sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 44 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Carowinds, out of 13 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kiddy Hawk sits on the south-east side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock Express, roughly 160 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Vortex at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kiddy Hawk?

Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall to ride Kiddy Hawk. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kiddy Hawk?

Kiddy Hawk is 48.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kiddy Hawk go?

Its top speed is 26 mph (42 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kiddy Hawk go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kiddy Hawk scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Kiddy Hawk open?

Kiddy Hawk opened on 22 Mar 2003 (previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar).

Who built Kiddy Hawk?

Kiddy Hawk was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Kiddy Hawk at Carowinds the same as Silver Streak at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 342m coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 2003 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 2001; both list a 44 in minimum.
