Kiddy Hawk

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 44 in (112 cm); top speed 26 mph; 48.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Kiddy Hawk is a steel inverted roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 22 Mar 2003 and was previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar. The ride stands 48.6 ft tall, reaches 26 mph and runs 1,122 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kiddy Hawk?

Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall to ride Kiddy Hawk. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kiddy Hawk?

Kiddy Hawk is 48.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kiddy Hawk go?

Its top speed is 26 mph (42 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kiddy Hawk go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kiddy Hawk scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Kiddy Hawk open?

Kiddy Hawk opened on 22 Mar 2003 (previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar).

Who built Kiddy Hawk?

Kiddy Hawk was manufactured by Vekoma.
