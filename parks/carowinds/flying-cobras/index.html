Flying Cobras

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2009.

Flying Cobras is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 28 Mar 2009 and was previously known as Carolina Cobra. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Flying Cobras is like

For a 116.5 ft ride, 935 ft of track is short: Flying Cobras is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2009, it is the 10th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It has run under other names (Carolina Cobra), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 28 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 26th of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland, Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas and Flashback at Six Flags New England. Height rules for this model differ by park: Carowinds sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Walibi Belgium sets 130 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Carowinds, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flying Cobras sits on the west side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Carolina Goldrusher, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Copperhead Strike at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Flying Cobras compares

On our intensity ranking Flying Cobras is number 5 of 14 at Carowinds, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 12 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Flying Cobras

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 80 in (203 cm). See can you be too tall?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flying Cobras?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Flying Cobras. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flying Cobras?

Flying Cobras is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flying Cobras go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flying Cobras go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Flying Cobras scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Carowinds rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Flying Cobras open?

Flying Cobras opened on 28 Mar 2009 (previously known as Carolina Cobra).

Who built Flying Cobras?

Flying Cobras was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Flying Cobras?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Flying Cobras get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Flying Cobras at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Flying Cobras good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Flying Cobras operate before?

RCDB records it at Geauga Lake & Wildwater Kingdom (as Head Spin).

Is Flying Cobras at Carowinds the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 2009 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; Carowinds sets 48 in and Bellewaerde sets 130 cm.
