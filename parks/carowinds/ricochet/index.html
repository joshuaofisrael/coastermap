Ricochet

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion; top speed 28 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Ricochet is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened in 2002. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 28 mph and runs 1213.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ricochet is like

Ricochet pairs a 45.9 ft structure with 1,213.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the 8th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Carowinds. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Park design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Ghost Chasers at Movie Park Germany and Tiger Express at Mer de Sable. Height rules for this model differ by park: Carowinds sets 54 in, while Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm and Mer de Sable sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Carowinds, shared with 4 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ricochet sits on the north-west side of Carowinds' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Carolina Cyclone, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Vortex at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ricochet?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Ricochet, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ricochet?

Ricochet is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ricochet go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ricochet go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ricochet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Ricochet open?

Ricochet opened in 2002.

Who built Ricochet?

Ricochet was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Ricochet?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds over 1213.9 ft of track.

How does Ricochet get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ricochet at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,120 riders.

Is Ricochet good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Ricochet at Carowinds the same as Ghost Chasers at Movie Park Germany?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Park coasters on RCDB; the Carowinds ride opened in 2002 and the Movie Park Germany ride in 2000; Carowinds sets 54 in and Movie Park Germany sets 120 cm.
