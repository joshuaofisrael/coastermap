Carolina Goldrusher

Carowinds · Charlotte, North Carolina

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 0 inversions; opened 1973.

Carolina Goldrusher is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Carowinds in Charlotte, North Carolina, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened in 1973. The ride runs 2,397 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Carolina Goldrusher?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Carolina Goldrusher, according to Carowinds, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Carolina Goldrusher go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Carolina Goldrusher scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Carowinds rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Carolina Goldrusher open?

Carolina Goldrusher opened on 1973.

Who built Carolina Goldrusher?

Carolina Goldrusher was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.
