Hades 360

Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park · Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 60 mph; 136 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2005.

Hades 360 is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park in Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin. It first opened on 14 May 2005 and was previously known as Hades. The ride stands 136 ft tall, drops 140 ft, reaches 60 mph and runs 4,746 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Hades 360 is like

With 4,746 ft of track for a 136 ft structure, Hades 360 is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 1 time across 4,746 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2005, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park. It has run under other names (Hades), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Mt. Olympus, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hades 360 sits on the north side of Mt. Olympus' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Zeus, roughly 60 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cyclops at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Hades 360

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hades 360?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Hades 360, according to Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park, Outdoor Theme Park ride list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hades 360?

Hades 360 is 136 ft tall, with a drop of 140 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hades 360 go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hades 360 go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Hades 360 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hades 360 open?

Hades 360 opened on 14 May 2005 (previously known as Hades).

How long is the track on Hades 360?

RCDB lists 4,746 ft (1,447 m) of track.

How does Hades 360 get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hades 360 at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Hades 360 good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
