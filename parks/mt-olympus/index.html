Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park in Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 40 in (102 cm) on Little Titans to 48 in (122 cm) on Cyclops. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 60% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down; 4 are wooden coasters (Cyclops, Pegasus, Zeus and Hades 360).

The most common builder is Custom Coasters International, Inc., with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Cyclops, which opened in 1995.

What can my child ride at Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park? By height

At Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Little Titans at 40 in (102 cm).

Best coasters at Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Little Titans, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (40 in minimum; 16 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Pegasus, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 60 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Hades 360, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 136 ft tall, 60 mph, 1 inversion).
