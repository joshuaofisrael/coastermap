Checkers' Sky Flyer

COTALAND · Austin, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Checkers' Sky Flyer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at COTALAND in Austin, Texas, built by SBF Visa Group. It first opened on 27 Nov 2020 and was previously known as Skyflyer. The ride runs 91.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Checkers' Sky Flyer is like

Opened in 2020, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at COTALAND. It has run under other names (Skyflyer), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Micro Coaster (MX615) design, and the first of them to open. Others include Skyflyer at Deno's. Height rules for this model differ by park: COTALAND sets 47 in, while Deno's sets 36 in.

A rider who just meets its 47 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at COTALAND, out of 4 with verified rules. Among kiddie-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 36 in (91 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Checkers' Sky Flyer layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Checkers' Sky Flyer:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Checkers' Sky Flyer trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 3 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 12 riders.
Riders per train12

For families checking heights: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36 and 42 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

Gentle (kiddie)

RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Checkers' Sky Flyer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 47 in (119 cm), and riders from 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 77 in (196 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders from 36 inches may ride accompanied by a supervising companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Checkers' Sky Flyer?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Checkers' Sky Flyer, or 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm). Riders from 36 inches may ride accompanied by a supervising companion, according to COTALAND, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Checkers' Sky Flyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Checkers' Sky Flyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a kiddie coaster. It is one of the gentlest coasters you can ride.

When did Checkers' Sky Flyer open?

Checkers' Sky Flyer opened on 27 Nov 2020 (previously known as Skyflyer).

Who built Checkers' Sky Flyer?

Checkers' Sky Flyer was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Checkers' Sky Flyer?

RCDB lists 91.9 ft of track.

How does Checkers' Sky Flyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Checkers' Sky Flyer at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Checkers' Sky Flyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as kiddie; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 47 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Checkers' Sky Flyer at COTALAND the same as Skyflyer at Deno's?

Both are SBF Visa Group Micro Coaster (MX615) coasters on RCDB; the COTALAND ride opened in 2020 and the Deno's ride in 2021; COTALAND sets 47 in and Deno's sets 36 in.
