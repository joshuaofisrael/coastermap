Circuit Breaker

COTALAND · Austin, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57.2 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2025.

Circuit Breaker is a steel sit-down roller coaster at COTALAND in Austin, Texas, built by Vekoma. It opened on 17 Oct 2025. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 57.2 mph and runs 2188.3 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Circuit Breaker is like

Circuit Breaker pairs a 131.2 ft structure with 2,188.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,188.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2025, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at COTALAND.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at COTALAND, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Circuit Breaker?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Circuit Breaker. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to COTALAND, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Circuit Breaker?

Circuit Breaker is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Circuit Breaker go?

Its top speed is 57.2 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Circuit Breaker go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Circuit Breaker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Circuit Breaker open?

Circuit Breaker opened on 17 Oct 2025.

Who built Circuit Breaker?

Circuit Breaker was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Circuit Breaker?

RCDB lists 2188.3 ft of track.

How does Circuit Breaker get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Circuit Breaker at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Circuit Breaker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 57.2 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
