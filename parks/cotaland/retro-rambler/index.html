Retro Rambler

COTALAND · Austin, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Retro Rambler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at COTALAND in Austin, Texas, built by Schwarzkopf. It first opened on 24 Dec 2021 and was previously known as Texas Wildcat. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Retro Rambler is like

Opened in 2021, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at COTALAND. It has run under other names (Texas Wildcat), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at COTALAND, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Retro Rambler?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Retro Rambler, according to COTALAND, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Retro Rambler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Retro Rambler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Retro Rambler open?

Retro Rambler opened on 24 Dec 2021 (previously known as Texas Wildcat).

Who built Retro Rambler?

Retro Rambler was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How does Retro Rambler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Retro Rambler at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Retro Rambler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Retro Rambler operate before?

RCDB records it at Bobbejaanland (as Achtbaan); Manning's Amusement Park (as Wild Cat); Lightwater Valley (as Raptor Attack); Cotaland (as Retro Rambler).
