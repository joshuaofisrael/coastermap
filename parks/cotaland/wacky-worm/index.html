Wacky Worm

COTALAND · Austin, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Wacky Worm is a steel sit-down roller coaster at COTALAND in Austin, Texas, built by Levent Lunapark. It first opened in 2021 and was previously known as Big Apple. The ride runs 452.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wacky Worm is like

Opened in 2021, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at COTALAND. It has run under other names (Big Apple), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 47 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at COTALAND, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Wacky Worm

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 47 in (119 cm), and riders from 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 78 in (198 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders from 39 inches may ride accompanied by a supervising companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wacky Worm?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Wacky Worm, or 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm). Riders from 39 inches may ride accompanied by a supervising companion, according to COTALAND, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Wacky Worm go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wacky Worm scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wacky Worm open?

Wacky Worm opened in 2021 (previously known as Big Apple).

Who built Wacky Worm?

Wacky Worm was manufactured by Levent Lunapark.

How long is the track on Wacky Worm?

RCDB lists 452.8 ft of track.

How many people ride Wacky Worm at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Wacky Worm good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 47 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
