COTALAND height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Austin, Texas · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

COTALAND in Austin, Texas has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Retro Rambler to 48 in (122 cm) on Palindrome. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at COTALAND are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes COTALAND a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Checkers' Sky Flyer, which opened in 2020.

What can my child ride at COTALAND? By height

At COTALAND the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Checkers' Sky Flyer (36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Retro Rambler at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Retro Rambler. With a companion they can also ride Checkers' Sky Flyer (36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm)) and Wacky Worm (39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Palindrome at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Checkers' Sky Flyer, Wacky Worm, Palindrome and Circuit Breaker. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Checkers' Sky Flyer, Wacky Worm, Retro Rambler, Palindrome and Circuit Breaker. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Checkers' Sky Flyer (up to 77 in (196 cm)), Wacky Worm (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Palindrome (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and Circuit Breaker (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at COTALAND

These are the rules COTALAND states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Checkers' Sky Flyer: riders 36 to 47 in (91 to 119 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wacky Worm: riders 39 to 47 in (99 to 119 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at COTALAND for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for COTALAND, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Checkers' Sky Flyer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (47 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Retro Rambler, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Circuit Breaker, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 131.2 ft tall, 57.2 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
