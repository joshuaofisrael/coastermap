Palindrome

COTALAND · Austin, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 51 mph; 95 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2026.

Palindrome is a steel sit-down roller coaster at COTALAND in Austin, Texas, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 26 Sep 2026. The ride stands 95 ft tall, reaches 51 mph and runs 1,230 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Palindrome is like

Palindrome pairs a 95 ft structure with 1,230 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,230 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at COTALAND.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at COTALAND, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Palindrome?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Palindrome. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to COTALAND, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Palindrome?

Palindrome is 95 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Palindrome go?

Its top speed is 51 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Palindrome go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Palindrome scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Palindrome open?

Palindrome opened on 26 Sep 2026.

Who built Palindrome?

Palindrome was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Palindrome?

RCDB lists 1,230 ft (375 m) of track.

How does Palindrome get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Palindrome at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Palindrome good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 51 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
