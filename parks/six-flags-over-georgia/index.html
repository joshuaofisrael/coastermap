Six Flags Over Georgia height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Austell, Georgia · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Joker Funhouse Coaster to 54 in (137 cm) on Batman The Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 7 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 11 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Over Georgia are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 9 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Over Georgia a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 82% of its lineup, against 45% across all 138 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 11 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 7 of the 11 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Great American Scream Machine).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Dahlonega Mine Train, which opened in 1967.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Over Georgia? By height

At Six Flags Over Georgia the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Joker Funhouse Coaster (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being The Riddler Mindbender at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 11 coasters with a verified rule: Joker Funhouse Coaster and The Riddler Mindbender. Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Blue Hawk at 48 in (122 cm).

Best coasters at Six Flags Over Georgia for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Over Georgia, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Joker Funhouse Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 30 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Dahlonega Mine Train, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 37 ft tall, 29 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Blue Hawk, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 122 ft tall, 52 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
