Six Flags Over Georgia roller coasters

Austell, Georgia · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia has 11 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Goliath at 200 ft, which is also the fastest at 70 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Joker Funhouse Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Over Georgia have?

Six Flags Over Georgia has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia?

Goliath, at 200 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia?

Goliath, with a top speed of 70 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Over Georgia?

By height requirement, Joker Funhouse Coaster has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Over Georgia coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Joker Funhouse Coaster, The Riddler Mindbender. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Over Georgia coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Blue Hawk, Great American Scream Machine, Dahlonega Mine Train, Joker Funhouse Coaster, Dare Devil Dive, Twisted Cyclone, The Riddler Mindbender. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Over Georgia do not go upside down?

Great American Scream Machine, Dahlonega Mine Train, Joker Funhouse Coaster, Goliath have no inversions, per RCDB.
