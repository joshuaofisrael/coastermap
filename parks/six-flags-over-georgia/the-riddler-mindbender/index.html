The Riddler Mindbender

Six Flags Over Georgia · Austell, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 50 mph; 80 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1978.

The Riddler Mindbender is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia, built by Schwarzkopf. It first opened on 31 Mar 1978 and was previously known as Mind Bender. The ride stands 80 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 3,253 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding The Riddler Mindbender is like

The Riddler Mindbender spreads 3,253 ft of track across a layout only 80 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 2 times across 3,253 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1978, it is the 3rd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Georgia. It has run under other names (Mind Bender), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Riddler Mindbender sits on the north-east side of Six Flags Over Georgia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dare Devil Dive, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batman The Ride at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride The Riddler Mindbender

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 42 in (107 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Over Georgia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Riddler Mindbender?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride The Riddler Mindbender, according to Six Flags Over Georgia, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Riddler Mindbender?

The Riddler Mindbender is 80 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Riddler Mindbender go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Riddler Mindbender go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is The Riddler Mindbender scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Riddler Mindbender open?

The Riddler Mindbender opened on 31 Mar 1978 (previously known as Mind Bender).

Who built The Riddler Mindbender?

The Riddler Mindbender was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on The Riddler Mindbender?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 33 seconds over 3,253 ft of track.

How does The Riddler Mindbender get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Riddler Mindbender at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

What restraints does The Riddler Mindbender use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar.

Is The Riddler Mindbender good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
