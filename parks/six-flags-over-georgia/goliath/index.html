Goliath

Six Flags Over Georgia · Austell, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 70 mph; 200 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2006.

Goliath is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 1 Apr 2006. The ride stands 200 ft tall, drops 175 ft, reaches 70 mph and runs 4,480 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Goliath is like

At 200 ft tall with 4,480 ft of track, Goliath balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2006, it is the 9th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Georgia. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island and Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Over Georgia sets 54 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, Europa-Park sets 140 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Over Georgia, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Goliath sits on the north side of Six Flags Over Georgia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Georgia Scorcher, roughly 100 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dare Devil Dive at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Goliath compares

On our intensity ranking Goliath is number 3 of 11 at Six Flags Over Georgia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Dare Devil Dive (95 ft tall, 52 mph, 3 inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Batman The Ride (105 ft tall, 50 mph, 5 inversions, 4 g; 54 in minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Goliath?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Goliath, according to Six Flags Over Georgia, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Goliath?

Goliath is 200 ft tall, with a drop of 175 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Goliath go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Goliath go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Goliath scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Goliath open?

Goliath opened on 1 Apr 2006.

Who built Goliath?

Goliath was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

Is Goliath at Six Flags Over Georgia the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Georgia ride opened in 2006 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; Six Flags Over Georgia sets 54 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 200 ft and 170 ft.
