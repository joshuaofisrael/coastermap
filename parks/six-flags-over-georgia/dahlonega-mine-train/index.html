Dahlonega Mine Train

Six Flags Over Georgia · Austell, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 29 mph; 37 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1967.

Dahlonega Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 16 Jun 1967. The ride stands 37 ft tall, reaches 29 mph and runs 2,327 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dahlonega Mine Train is like

With 2,327 ft of track for a 37 ft structure, Dahlonega Mine Train is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1967, it is the oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Georgia. It is one of 15 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Adventure Express at Kings Island and Trailblazer at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Over Georgia sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 44 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Six Flags Over Georgia, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dahlonega Mine Train sits on the south-west side of Six Flags Over Georgia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Joker Funhouse Coaster, roughly 90 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Superman - Ultimate Flight at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dahlonega Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Dahlonega Mine Train, according to Six Flags Over Georgia, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dahlonega Mine Train?

Dahlonega Mine Train is 37 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dahlonega Mine Train go?

Its top speed is 29 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dahlonega Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dahlonega Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Dahlonega Mine Train open?

Dahlonega Mine Train opened on 16 Jun 1967.

Who built Dahlonega Mine Train?

Dahlonega Mine Train was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Dahlonega Mine Train?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 51 seconds over 2,327 ft of track.

How does Dahlonega Mine Train get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dahlonega Mine Train at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,200 riders.

Is Dahlonega Mine Train good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Dahlonega Mine Train at Six Flags Over Georgia the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Georgia ride opened in 1967 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 37 ft and 35 ft.
