Great American Scream Machine

Six Flags Over Georgia · Austell, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57 mph; 105 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1973.

Great American Scream Machine is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened on 31 Mar 1973. The ride stands 105 ft tall, drops 89 ft, reaches 57 mph and runs 3,450 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Great American Scream Machine is like

Great American Scream Machine spreads 3,450 ft of track across a layout only 105 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1973, it is the 2nd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Georgia.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Six Flags Over Georgia, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Great American Scream Machine sits on the south side of Six Flags Over Georgia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Blue Hawk, roughly 180 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Superman - Ultimate Flight at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Great American Scream Machine?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Great American Scream Machine, according to Six Flags Over Georgia, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Great American Scream Machine?

Great American Scream Machine is 105 ft tall, with a drop of 89 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Great American Scream Machine go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Great American Scream Machine go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Great American Scream Machine scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Great American Scream Machine open?

Great American Scream Machine opened on 31 Mar 1973.

Who built Great American Scream Machine?

Great American Scream Machine was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the ride on Great American Scream Machine?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 3,450 ft of track.

How does Great American Scream Machine get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Great American Scream Machine at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

What restraints does Great American Scream Machine use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar, headrest, seatbelt.

Is Great American Scream Machine good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 57 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
