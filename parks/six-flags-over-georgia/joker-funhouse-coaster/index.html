Joker Funhouse Coaster

Six Flags Over Georgia · Austell, Georgia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; top speed 30 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Joker Funhouse Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia in Austell, Georgia, built by Chance Rides. It first opened on Aug 2004 and was previously known as Wile E. Coyote Canyon Blaster. The ride reaches 30 mph and runs 900 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Joker Funhouse Coaster is like

Opened in 2004, it is the 8th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Georgia. It has run under other names (Wile E. Coyote Canyon Blaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Joker Funhouse Coaster sits on the south-west side of Six Flags Over Georgia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dahlonega Mine Train, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Blue Hawk at about 250 m. Joker Funhouse Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Joker Funhouse Coaster?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Joker Funhouse Coaster, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Over Georgia, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Joker Funhouse Coaster go?

Its top speed is 30 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Joker Funhouse Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Joker Funhouse Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Georgia rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Joker Funhouse Coaster open?

Joker Funhouse Coaster opened on Aug 2004 (previously known as Wile E. Coyote Canyon Blaster).

Who built Joker Funhouse Coaster?

Joker Funhouse Coaster was manufactured by Chance Rides.

How long is the track on Joker Funhouse Coaster?

RCDB lists 900 ft (274 m) of track.

How does Joker Funhouse Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Joker Funhouse Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Joker Funhouse Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 30 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
