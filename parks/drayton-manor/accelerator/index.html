Accelerator

Drayton Manor · Tamworth, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Accelerator is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Drayton Manor in Tamworth, England, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 21 Apr 2011 and was previously known as Ben 10 - Ultimate Mission. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 643 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Accelerator is like

Accelerator pairs a 65.6 ft structure with 643 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2011, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Drayton Manor. It has run under other names (Ben 10 - Ultimate Mission), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Drayton Manor, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Accelerator sits on the south-east side of Drayton Manor's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gold Rush, roughly 70 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jormungandr at about 110 m. Accelerator layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Before you ride Accelerator

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders under 120 cm must be accompanied by a responsible person aged 16 or over.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Drayton Manor app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Accelerator?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Accelerator, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders under 120 cm must be accompanied by a responsible person aged 16 or over, according to Drayton Manor, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Accelerator?

Accelerator is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Accelerator go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Accelerator go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Accelerator scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Accelerator open?

Accelerator opened on 21 Apr 2011 (previously known as Ben 10 - Ultimate Mission).

Who built Accelerator?

Accelerator was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Accelerator?

RCDB lists 643 ft (196 m) of track.

How does Accelerator get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Accelerator at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 750 riders.

Is Accelerator good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
