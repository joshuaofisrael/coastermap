Pindsvinet

Fårup Sommerland · Blokhus, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 105 cm (about 41.3 in), or 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) with a companion; 13.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Pindsvinet is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fårup Sommerland in Blokhus, Denmark, built by Zamperla. It opened on 30 Apr 2012. The ride stands 13.1 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pindsvinet is like

At 13.1 ft tall with 262.5 ft of track, Pindsvinet balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2012, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Fårup Sommerland. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 10th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fårup sets 105 cm, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 42 in and Silver Dollar City sets 42 in.

Its 105 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Fårup (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pindsvinet sits on the north-west side of Fårup's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Falken, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Orkanen at about 190 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 105 cm (about 41.3 in) tall, or 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) with a companion.

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 105 cm (about 41.3 in), and riders from 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pindsvinet?

Riders must be at least 105 cm (about 41.3 in) tall to ride Pindsvinet, or 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 105 cm must ride with an adult, according to Fårup Sommerland, official height and age guide (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pindsvinet?

Pindsvinet is 13.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Pindsvinet go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pindsvinet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Pindsvinet open?

Pindsvinet opened on 30 Apr 2012.

Who built Pindsvinet?

Pindsvinet was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Pindsvinet?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Pindsvinet get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pindsvinet at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Pindsvinet good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 105 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Pindsvinet at Fårup the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Fårup ride opened in 2012 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; Fårup sets 105 cm and Holiday World sets 42 in; RCDB lists heights of 13.1 ft and 13 ft.
