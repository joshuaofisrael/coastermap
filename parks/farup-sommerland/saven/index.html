Saven

Fårup Sommerland · Blokhus, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 79.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Saven is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fårup Sommerland in Blokhus, Denmark, built by Vekoma. It opened on 5 Jun 2020. The ride stands 79.4 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 780.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Saven is like

At 79.4 ft tall with 780.8 ft of track, Saven balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2020, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Fårup Sommerland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Boomerang / 238m (Spirit) design, and the first of them to open. Others include Light Explorers at Energylandia and The Quest at Emerald Park. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Fårup, out of 7 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Saven sits on the east side of Fårup's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mine-Expressen, roughly 110 m away (105 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flagermusen at about 220 m. Saven layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

How Saven compares

On our intensity ranking Saven is number 5 of 8 at Fårup Sommerland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Saven

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 95 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fårup Sommerland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Saven?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Saven, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 95 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Fårup Sommerland, official height and age guide (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Saven?

Saven is 79.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Saven go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Saven go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Saven scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Saven open?

Saven opened on 5 Jun 2020.

Who built Saven?

Saven was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Saven?

RCDB lists 780.8 ft of track.

How does Saven get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Saven at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 686 riders.

Is Saven good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Saven at Fårup the same as Light Explorers at Energylandia?

Both are Vekoma Family Boomerang / 238m (Spirit) coasters on RCDB; the Fårup ride opened in 2020 and the Energylandia ride in 2021; both list a 120 cm minimum.
