Falken

Fårup Sommerland · Blokhus, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 46.6 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Falken is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Fårup Sommerland in Blokhus, Denmark, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened in May 2004. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 2040.7 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Falken is like

Falken spreads 2,040.7 ft of track across a layout only 65.6 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2004, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Fårup Sommerland.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Fårup, out of 7 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Falken sits on the west side of Fårup's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Orkanen, roughly 90 m away (105 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pindsvinet at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Falken compares

On our intensity ranking Falken is number 3 of 8 at Fårup Sommerland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Falken

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 110 to 130 cm must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Falken?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Falken, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 110 to 130 cm must ride with an adult, according to Fårup Sommerland, official height and age guide (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Falken?

Falken is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Falken go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Falken go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Falken scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Falken open?

Falken opened in May 2004.

Who built Falken?

Falken was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the ride on Falken?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 54 seconds over 2040.7 ft of track.

How does Falken get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Falken at once?

Each train carries 30 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Falken good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
