Gyeongju World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Gyeongju, South Korea · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Gyeongju World in Gyeongju, South Korea has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on Wizard Race to 145 cm (about 57.1 in) on Phaethon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Gyeongju World are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Gyeongju World a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 60% of its lineup, against 37% across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 49.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Phaethon, which opened in 2007.

Gyeongju World publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Gyeongju World? By height

At Gyeongju World the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Wizard Race at 105 cm (about 41.3 in).

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Wizard Race. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Valkyrie at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Valkyrie. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Sköll & Hati at 125 cm (about 49.2 in).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Draken and Sköll & Hati. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Phaethon at 145 cm (about 57.1 in).

Best coasters at Gyeongju World for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Gyeongju World, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Valkyrie, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wizard Race, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (105 cm minimum; 16.2 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Phaethon, the most intense coaster in the park (145 cm minimum; 147.7 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 6 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
