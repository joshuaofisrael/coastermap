Sköll & Hati

Gyeongju World · Gyeongju, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 52 mph; 105 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2024.

Sköll & Hati is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gyeongju World in Gyeongju, South Korea, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 30 Nov 2024. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,950 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Sköll & Hati is like

At 105 ft tall with 1,950 ft of track, Sköll & Hati balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,950 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2024, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Gyeongju World. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Rocky Mountain Coasters's Raptor Track / Stunt Pilot design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Stunt Pilot at Silverwood and Fire Runner at Lost Island. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gyeongju World sets 125 cm, while Silverwood sets 48 in and Lost Island sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Gyeongju World, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Sköll & Hati layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Sköll & Hati's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Dive Loop
  3. Cutback
  4. Corkscrew
  5. Over-Banked Curve

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Sköll & Hati compares

On our intensity ranking Sköll & Hati is number 3 of 5 at Gyeongju World, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Wizard Race (16.2 mph, no inversions; 105 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Draken (206.7 ft tall, 2 inversions; 135 cm minimum).

Before you ride Sköll & Hati

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Gyeongju World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sköll & Hati?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Sköll & Hati, according to Gyeongju World, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sköll & Hati?

Sköll & Hati is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sköll & Hati go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sköll & Hati go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Sköll & Hati scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Sköll & Hati open?

Sköll & Hati opened on 30 Nov 2024.

Who built Sköll & Hati?

Sköll & Hati was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Sköll & Hati?

RCDB lists 1,950 ft (594 m) of track.

How does Sköll & Hati get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Sköll & Hati good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Sköll & Hati at Gyeongju World the same as Stunt Pilot at Silverwood?

Both are Rocky Mountain Coasters Raptor Track / Stunt Pilot coasters on RCDB; the Gyeongju World ride opened in 2024 and the Silverwood ride in 2021; Gyeongju World sets 125 cm and Silverwood sets 48 in.
