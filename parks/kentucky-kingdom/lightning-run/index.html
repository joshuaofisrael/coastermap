Lightning Run

Kentucky Kingdom · Louisville, Kentucky

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 55 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Lightning Run is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kentucky Kingdom in Louisville, Kentucky, built by Chance Rides. It opened on 24 May 2014. The ride drops 100 ft, reaches 55 mph and runs 2,500 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Lightning Run is like

At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2014, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Kentucky Kingdom.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Kentucky Kingdom, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Lightning Run sits on the east side of Kentucky Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kentucky Flyer, roughly 280 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hollyhock and Roll at about 310 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Lightning Run?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Lightning Run, according to Kentucky Kingdom, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Lightning Run go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Lightning Run go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Lightning Run scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Lightning Run open?

Lightning Run opened on 24 May 2014.

Who built Lightning Run?

Lightning Run was manufactured by Chance Rides.

How long is the ride on Lightning Run?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 2,500 ft of track.

How does Lightning Run get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Lightning Run at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Lightning Run good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
