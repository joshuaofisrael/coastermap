Flying Fox

Kentucky Kingdom · Louisville, Kentucky

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 37 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Flying Fox is a steel suspended roller coaster at Kentucky Kingdom in Louisville, Kentucky, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 May 2026. The ride stands 65 ft tall, reaches 37 mph and runs 1,380 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flying Fox is like

At 65 ft tall with 1,380 ft of track, Flying Fox balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Kentucky Kingdom.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Kentucky Kingdom, out of 5 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Flying Fox layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion, and no taller than 81 in (206 cm).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flying Fox?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Flying Fox, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 81 in (206 cm). Riders 38 inches and up may ride with a supervising companion; 48 inches to ride alone, according to Kentucky Kingdom, official rides and attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Flying Fox?

Flying Fox is 65 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Flying Fox go?

Its top speed is 37 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Flying Fox go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flying Fox scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Flying Fox open?

Flying Fox opened on 28 May 2026.

Who built Flying Fox?

Flying Fox was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Flying Fox?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 3 seconds over 1,380 ft of track.

How does Flying Fox get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Flying Fox at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Flying Fox use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Flying Fox good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
