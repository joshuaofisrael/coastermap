Terra Mítica height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Benidorm, Spain · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Terra Mítica in Benidorm, Spain has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Alucinakis to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Inferno. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Terra Mítica are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Terra Mítica a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 45% across all 136 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Alucinakis, which opened in 2000.

Terra Mítica publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Terra Mítica? By height

At Terra Mítica the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
