Speedblaze

Dream Island · Moscow, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 3 inversions; opened 2026.

Speedblaze is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dream Island in Moscow, Russia, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 1 Aug 2026. The ride reaches 43.5 mph and runs 2070.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Speedblaze is like

It goes upside down 3 times across 2,070.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2026, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Dream Island. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Hot Racer / Mahuka design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mahuka at Walibi Rhône-Alpes. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Dream Island (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Treasure Mine, roughly 230 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Speedblaze

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 196 cm (about 77.2 in). See can you be too tall?
  • The park lists it as Пламя скорости, for riders 120 cm to 196 cm. No companion rule is stated.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Dream Island app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Speedblaze?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Speedblaze. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in). The park lists it as Пламя скорости, for riders 120 cm to 196 cm. No companion rule is stated, according to Dream Island, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Speedblaze go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Speedblaze go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Speedblaze scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Speedblaze open?

Speedblaze opened on 1 Aug 2026.

Who built Speedblaze?

Speedblaze was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Speedblaze?

RCDB lists 2070.2 ft of track.

Is Speedblaze good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Speedblaze at Dream Island the same as Mahuka at Walibi Rhône-Alpes?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Hot Racer / Mahuka coasters on RCDB; the Dream Island ride opened in 2026 and the Walibi Rhône-Alpes ride in 2024; both list a 120 cm minimum.
