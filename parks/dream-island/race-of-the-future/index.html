Race of the Future

Dream Island · Moscow, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 9.3 mph; 36.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Race of the Future is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dream Island in Moscow, Russia, built by Fabbri. It opened in 2020. The ride stands 36.1 ft tall and reaches 9.3 mph. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Race of the Future is like

Opened in 2020, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Dream Island. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Fabbri's Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse Midi design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Neo's Twister at PowerPark, Formula 1 at Magic Park Land and Family Star at Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Dream Island, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

How Race of the Future compares

Before you ride Race of the Future

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Гонка будущего: 110 cm to 130 cm with a companion, 130 cm and up without.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Race of the Future?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Race of the Future, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Гонка будущего: 110 cm to 130 cm with a companion, 130 cm and up without, according to Dream Island, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Race of the Future?

Race of the Future is 36.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Race of the Future go?

Its top speed is 9.3 mph (15 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Race of the Future go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Race of the Future scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Race of the Future open?

Race of the Future opened in 2020.

Who built Race of the Future?

Race of the Future was manufactured by Fabbri.

How does Race of the Future get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Race of the Future good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 9.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Race of the Future at Dream Island the same as Neo's Twister at PowerPark?

Both are Fabbri Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse Midi coasters on RCDB; the Dream Island ride opened in 2020 and the PowerPark ride in 2011; both list a 130 cm minimum.
