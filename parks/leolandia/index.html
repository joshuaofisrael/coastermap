Leolandia height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Capriate San Gervasio, Italy · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Leolandia in Capriate San Gervasio, Italy has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on Mine Train to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Reversum. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Leolandia are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Leolandia a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Twister Mountain, which opened in 2008.

Leolandia publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Leolandia? By height

At Leolandia the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Mine Train (90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in)) and Vroom (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Twister Mountain at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Mine Train. With a companion they can also ride Twister Mountain (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)), Vroom (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and Reversum (105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Twister Mountain and Vroom. With a companion they can also ride Reversum (105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Reversum. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Leolandia

These are the rules Leolandia states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

Best coasters at Leolandia for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Leolandia, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (105 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Reversum, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Twister Mountain, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 34.4 ft tall, 28.6 mph, no inversions, 2.3 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
