Vroom

Leolandia · Capriate San Gervasio, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 22.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Vroom is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Leolandia in Capriate San Gervasio, Italy, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 1 Aug 2018. The ride stands 22.9 ft tall and runs 1148.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vroom is like

Vroom spreads 1,148.3 ft of track across a layout only 22.9 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2018, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Leolandia.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Leolandia, out of 3 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vroom sits on the west side of Leolandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Reversum, roughly 240 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twister Mountain at about 310 m. Vroom layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Vroom

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vroom?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Vroom, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Leolandia, official rides and attractions page (ride detail panels) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vroom?

Vroom is 22.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Vroom go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vroom scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Vroom open?

Vroom opened on 1 Aug 2018.

Who built Vroom?

Vroom was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How long is the track on Vroom?

RCDB lists 1148.3 ft of track.

How does Vroom get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vroom at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Vroom good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
