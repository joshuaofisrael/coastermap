Dutch Wonderland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Lancaster, Pennsylvania · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Dutch Wonderland in Lancaster, Pennsylvania has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 39 in (99 cm) on Merlin's Mayhem to 46 in (117 cm) on Kingdom Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Dutch Wonderland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Dutch Wonderland a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 42 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Kingdom Coaster).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Kingdom Coaster, which opened in 1992.

What can my child ride at Dutch Wonderland? By height

At Dutch Wonderland the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Joust (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Merlin's Mayhem at 39 in (99 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Joust and Merlin's Mayhem. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Kingdom Coaster at 46 in (117 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Kingdom Coaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Kingdom Coaster, Joust and Merlin's Mayhem. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Merlin's Mayhem (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Dutch Wonderland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Dutch Wonderland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Joust, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 16.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Kingdom Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (46 in minimum; 55 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
