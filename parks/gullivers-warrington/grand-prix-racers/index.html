Grand Prix Racers

Gulliver's World Warrington · Warrington, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Grand Prix Racers is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gulliver's World Warrington in Warrington, England, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2023. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Grand Prix Racers is like

Opened in 2023, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Gulliver's World Warrington. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Race Coaster (MX611) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land and Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's Land. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Gulliver's World (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Grand Prix Racers sits on the east side of Gulliver's World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Antelope, roughly 110 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Wriggler at about 190 m. Grand Prix Racers layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Grand Prix Racers trains and ride system

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Grand Prix Racers

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • At Gulliver's World it sits in the Gulliver's Gears area; riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, and riders over 195 cm cannot ride.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Gulliver's World Warrington app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Grand Prix Racers?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Grand Prix Racers, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). At Gulliver's World it sits in the Gulliver's Gears area; riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, and riders over 195 cm cannot ride, according to Gulliver's World Resort, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Grand Prix Racers go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Grand Prix Racers scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Grand Prix Racers open?

Grand Prix Racers opened in 2023.

Who built Grand Prix Racers?

Grand Prix Racers was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Grand Prix Racers get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Grand Prix Racers at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Grand Prix Racers good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's World the same as Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land?

Both are SBF Visa Group Race Coaster (MX611) coasters on RCDB; the Gulliver's World ride opened in 2023 and the Ferrari Land ride in 2018; both list a 120 cm minimum.
