Gulliver's World Warrington height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Warrington, England · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Gulliver's World Warrington in Warrington, England has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Grand Prix Racers to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on The Antelope. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Gulliver's World Warrington are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Gulliver's World Warrington a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 42% of coasters across all 176 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (The Antelope).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Antelope, which opened in 1995.

Gulliver's World Warrington publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Gulliver's World Warrington? By height

At Gulliver's World Warrington the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

54 in (137 cm) tall

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Grand Prix Racers (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and The Wriggler (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Gulliver's World Warrington

These are the rules Gulliver's World Warrington states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Grand Prix Racers: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Wriggler: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
