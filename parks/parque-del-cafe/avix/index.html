Avix

Parque del Café · Montenegro, Quindío, Colombia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; 85.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Avix is a steel inverted roller coaster at Parque del Café in Montenegro, Quindío, Colombia, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 17 Dec 2024. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall and runs 1164.7 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Avix is like

Avix pairs a 85.3 ft structure with 1,164.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Parque del Café.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Parque del Café, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Avix compares

On our intensity ranking Avix is number 2 of 3 at Parque del Café, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Yippe (no inversions; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Kráter (100 ft tall, 52 mph, 3 inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Avix?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Avix, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Required height 120 to 200 cm; children 120 to 140 cm must sit in the same row as an adult, according to Parque del Café, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Avix?

Avix is 85.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Avix go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Avix scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Avix open?

Avix opened on 17 Dec 2024.

Who built Avix?

Avix was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Avix?

RCDB lists 1164.7 ft of track.

How does Avix get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Avix at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Avix good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
