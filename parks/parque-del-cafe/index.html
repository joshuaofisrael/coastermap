Parque del Café height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Montenegro, Quindío, Colombia · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parque del Café in Montenegro, Quindío, Colombia has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Kráter to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Yippe. By the park's own centimetre lines: at 130 cm a child can ride 1 alone (3 with a companion); at 140 cm, all 3.

What the coasters at Parque del Café are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Parque del Café a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Kráter, which opened in 2014.

Parque del Café publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Kráter: The park rates it extreme; riders over 110 kg may not fit the seats.
  • Yippe: Minimum 100 cm; children 100 to 140 cm must ride in the same row as an adult.
  • Avix: Required height 120 to 200 cm; children 120 to 140 cm must sit in the same row as an adult.

What can my child ride at Parque del Café? By height

At Parque del Café the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Parque del Café

These are the rules Parque del Café states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Yippe: riders 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Avix: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Kráter: The park rates it extreme; riders over 110 kg may not fit the seats.

Best coasters at Parque del Café for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parque del Café, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Yippe, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (140 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Avix, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 85.3 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Kráter, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 100 ft tall, 52 mph, 3 inversions).
