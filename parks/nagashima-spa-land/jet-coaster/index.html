Jet Coaster

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 1966.

Jet Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Meiwa Komuten. It opened on 29 Apr 1966. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jet Coaster is like

Opened in 1966, it is the oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jet Coaster sits near the middle of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Looping Star, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Shuttle Loop at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Does Jet Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jet Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Jet Coaster open?

Jet Coaster opened on 29 Apr 1966.

Who built Jet Coaster?

Jet Coaster was manufactured by Meiwa Komuten.

How long is the ride on Jet Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 2 seconds.

How does Jet Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Jet Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Jet Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
