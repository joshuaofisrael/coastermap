Acrobat

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 135 cm (about 53.1 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 141.1 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2015.

Acrobat is a steel flying roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 18 Jul 2015. The ride stands 141.1 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 3349.8 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Acrobat is like

Acrobat pairs a 141.1 ft structure with 3,349.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,349.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.1 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2015, it is the 9th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Flying Coaster / Manta design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Manta at SeaWorld Orlando. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nagashima Spa Land sets 135 cm, while SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 135 cm minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Nagashima Spa Land, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Acrobat sits on the north-west side of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steel Dragon 2000, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Corkscrew at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall, and no taller than 198 cm (about 78 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Acrobat compares

On our intensity ranking Acrobat is number 3 of 11 at Nagashima Spa Land, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 9 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Acrobat

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 135 cm (about 53.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 198 cm (about 78 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Nagashima Spa Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Acrobat?

Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall to ride Acrobat. The maximum rider height is 198 cm (about 78 in), according to Nagashima Spa Land, official attraction restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Acrobat?

Acrobat is 141.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Acrobat go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Acrobat go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Acrobat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Acrobat open?

Acrobat opened on 18 Jul 2015.

Who built Acrobat?

Acrobat was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Acrobat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 3349.8 ft of track.

How does Acrobat get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Acrobat at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train.

Is Acrobat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 135 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Acrobat at Nagashima Spa Land the same as Manta at SeaWorld Orlando?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Flying Coaster / Manta coasters on RCDB; the Nagashima Spa Land ride opened in 2015 and the SeaWorld Orlando ride in 2009; Nagashima Spa Land sets 135 cm and SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 141.1 ft and 140 ft.
