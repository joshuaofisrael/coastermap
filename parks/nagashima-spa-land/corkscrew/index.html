Corkscrew

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); 2 inversions; opened 2012.

Corkscrew is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 17 Mar 2012. The ride runs 1279.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Corkscrew is like

It goes upside down 2 times across 1,279.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2012, it is the 7th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure, Corkscrew at Silverwood, Corkscrew at Rusutsu and Magnus at Salitre Mágico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm, while Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in, Silverwood sets 48 in and Rusutsu sets 125 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nagashima Spa Land, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Corkscrew sits on the north side of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Looping Star, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Arashi at about 90 m. Corkscrew layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on Corkscrew's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Double Corkscrew

RCDB counts 2 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Corkscrew compares

On our intensity ranking Corkscrew is number 7 of 11 at Nagashima Spa Land, where 1 is the most intense.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Double Wild Mouse (45.9 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Arashi (120 ft tall, 38 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Corkscrew

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Nagashima Spa Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Corkscrew?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Corkscrew, according to Nagashima Spa Land, official attraction restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Corkscrew go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Corkscrew scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Corkscrew open?

Corkscrew opened on 17 Mar 2012.

Who built Corkscrew?

Corkscrew was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Corkscrew?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 16 seconds over 1279.5 ft of track.

How does Corkscrew get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Corkscrew at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Corkscrew use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Corkscrew good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Nagashima Spa Land ride opened in 2012 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm and Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in.
