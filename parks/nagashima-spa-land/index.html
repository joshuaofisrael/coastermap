Nagashima Spa Land height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Kuwana, Mie, Japan · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Corkscrew to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Steel Dragon 2000. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Nagashima Spa Land are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 7 as extreme, which makes Nagashima Spa Land a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 64% of its lineup, against 45% across all 138 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 11 go upside down.

The most common builder is Schwarzkopf, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Jet Coaster, which opened in 1966.

Steel Dragon 2000 is the 5th tallest of the 767 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Steel Dragon 2000 is the 5th fastest of the 757 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

Nagashima Spa Land publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Nagashima Spa Land? By height

At Nagashima Spa Land the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Corkscrew at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Corkscrew at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Double Wild Mouse, Arashi, Corkscrew and Shuttle Loop. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Hakugei at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Acrobat, Hakugei and Looping Star. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Steel Dragon 2000 at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Acrobat (up to 198 cm (about 78 in)) and Steel Dragon 2000 (up to 185 cm (about 72.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Nagashima Spa Land for first-timers

What are the height requirements at Nagashima Spa Land?

Roller coaster height requirements at Nagashima Spa Land run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) (Corkscrew) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Steel Dragon 2000), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Nagashima Spa Land have?

Nagashima Spa Land has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Steel Dragon 2000 (318.2 ft tall, 95 mph, no inversions).
