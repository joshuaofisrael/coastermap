Shuttle Loop

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 57 mph; 137.8 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1980.

Shuttle Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened on 1 Mar 1980. The ride stands 137.8 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 863 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Shuttle Loop is like

For a 137.8 ft ride, 863 ft of track is short: Shuttle Loop is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1980, it is the 2nd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Schwarzkopf's Shuttle Loop / Flywheel design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress at Knott's and Turbine at Walibi Belgium. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nagashima Spa Land sets 120 cm, while Knott's sets 48 in and Walibi Belgium sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Nagashima Spa Land, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Shuttle Loop sits on the west side of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steel Dragon 2000, roughly 110 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jet Coaster at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Shuttle Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Nagashima Spa Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Shuttle Loop?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Shuttle Loop, according to Nagashima Spa Land, official attraction restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Shuttle Loop?

Shuttle Loop is 137.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Shuttle Loop go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Shuttle Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Shuttle Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Shuttle Loop open?

Shuttle Loop opened on 1 Mar 1980.

Who built Shuttle Loop?

Shuttle Loop was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Shuttle Loop?

RCDB lists a ride time of 30 seconds over 863 ft of track.

Does Shuttle Loop launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a flywheel launch.

How many people ride Shuttle Loop at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

What restraints does Shuttle Loop use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar, seatbelt.

Is Shuttle Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 57 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Shuttle Loop at Nagashima Spa Land the same as MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress at Knott's?

Both are Schwarzkopf Shuttle Loop / Flywheel coasters on RCDB; the Nagashima Spa Land ride opened in 1980 and the Knott's ride in 1978; Nagashima Spa Land sets 120 cm and Knott's sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 137.8 ft and 148 ft.
