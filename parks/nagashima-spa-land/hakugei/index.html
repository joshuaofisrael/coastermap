Hakugei

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 66.5 mph; 180.4 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2019.

Hakugei is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 28 Mar 2019. The ride stands 180.4 ft tall, reaches 66.5 mph and runs 5019.7 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Hakugei is like

Hakugei pairs a 180.4 ft structure with 5,019.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 5,019.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Nagashima Spa Land, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hakugei sits on the east side of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Double Wild Mouse, roughly 180 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Looping Star at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Hakugei

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Nagashima Spa Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hakugei?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Hakugei, according to Nagashima Spa Land, official attraction restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hakugei?

Hakugei is 180.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hakugei go?

Its top speed is 66.5 mph (107 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hakugei go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Hakugei scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hakugei open?

Hakugei opened on 28 Mar 2019.

Who built Hakugei?

Hakugei was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Hakugei?

RCDB lists 5019.7 ft of track.

How does Hakugei get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hakugei at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Hakugei use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar, seatbelt.

Is Hakugei good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 66.5 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
