Looping Star

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 47.8 mph; 80.4 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1982.

Looping Star is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened on 1 Mar 1982. The ride stands 80.4 ft tall, reaches 47.8 mph and runs 1942.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Looping Star is like

Looping Star pairs a 80.4 ft structure with 1,942.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 1 time across 1,942.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1982, it is the 3rd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Schwarzkopf's Looping Star design, and the first of them to open. Others include Silver Bullet at Frontier City. Height rules for this model differ by park: Nagashima Spa Land sets 130 cm, while Frontier City sets 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Nagashima Spa Land, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Looping Star sits near the middle of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jet Coaster, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Double Wild Mouse at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Looping Star

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Nagashima Spa Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Looping Star?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Looping Star, according to Nagashima Spa Land, official attraction restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Looping Star?

Looping Star is 80.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Looping Star go?

Its top speed is 47.8 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Looping Star go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Looping Star scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Looping Star open?

Looping Star opened on 1 Mar 1982.

Who built Looping Star?

Looping Star was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Looping Star?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 18 seconds over 1942.3 ft of track.

How does Looping Star get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Looping Star good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 47.8 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Looping Star at Nagashima Spa Land the same as Silver Bullet at Frontier City?

Both are Schwarzkopf Looping Star coasters on RCDB; the Nagashima Spa Land ride opened in 1982 and the Frontier City ride in 1986; Nagashima Spa Land sets 130 cm and Frontier City sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 80.4 ft and 80 ft.
