Peter Rabbit Coaster

Nagashima Spa Land · Kuwana, Mie, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 15.5 mph; 14.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Peter Rabbit Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Mie, Japan, built by Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd. It opened on 25 Apr 2012. The ride stands 14.1 ft tall, reaches 15.5 mph and runs 502 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Peter Rabbit Coaster is like

Peter Rabbit Coaster spreads 502 ft of track across a layout only 14.1 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2012, it is the 8th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Nagashima Spa Land.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Peter Rabbit Coaster sits on the south side of Nagashima Spa Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Children Coaster, roughly 100 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jet Coaster at about 150 m.
