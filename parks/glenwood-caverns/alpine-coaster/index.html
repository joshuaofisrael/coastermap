Alpine Coaster

Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park · Glenwood Springs, Colorado

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 38 to 54 in (97 to 137 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Alpine Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park in Glenwood Springs, Colorado, built by Wiegand. It opened in 2005. The ride runs 3198.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Alpine Coaster is like

Opened in 2005, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Wiegand's Alpine Coaster / Gen 1 design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Trapper Slider at Fort Fun and Eifel-Coaster at Eifelpark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Glenwood Caverns sets 54 in, while Fort Fun sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 54 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Glenwood Caverns, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 54 in (97 to 137 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Alpine Coaster sits on the west side of Glenwood Caverns' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Defiance, roughly 110 m away (56 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wild West Express at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 38 to 54 in (97 to 137 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm), and riders from 38 to 54 in (97 to 137 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Alpine Coaster?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Alpine Coaster, or 38 to 54 in (97 to 137 cm) with a companion. 54 inches to ride alone; from 38 inches (and at least 3 years old) as the front rider with a taller driver; maximum combined weight 330 lb, according to Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Alpine Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Alpine Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Alpine Coaster open?

Alpine Coaster opened in 2005.

Who built Alpine Coaster?

Alpine Coaster was manufactured by Wiegand.

How long is the track on Alpine Coaster?

RCDB lists 3198.8 ft of track.

How does Alpine Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a cable lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Alpine Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 2 seats per train.

Is Alpine Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Alpine Coaster at Glenwood Caverns the same as Trapper Slider at Fort Fun?

Both are Wiegand Alpine Coaster / Gen 1 coasters on RCDB; the Glenwood Caverns ride opened in 2005 and the Fort Fun ride in 2005; Glenwood Caverns sets 54 in and Fort Fun sets 130 cm.
