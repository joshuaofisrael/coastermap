Cliffhanger

Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park · Glenwood Springs, Colorado

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 56 in (142 cm), or 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) with a companion; 60 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Cliffhanger is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park in Glenwood Springs, Colorado, built by S&MC. It opened on 15 Jun 2012. The ride stands 60 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cliffhanger is like

Opened in 2012, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park.

Its 56 in minimum is the strictest at Glenwood Caverns, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cliffhanger sits on the east side of Glenwood Caverns' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild West Express, roughly 100 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Defiance at about 250 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 56 in (142 cm) tall, or 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Cliffhanger compares

On our intensity ranking Cliffhanger is number 2 of 4 at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Cliffhanger

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 56 in (142 cm), and riders from 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 48 to 56 inches must ride with a Supervising Companion (16 or older).
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cliffhanger?

Riders must be at least 56 in (142 cm) tall to ride Cliffhanger, or 48 to 56 in (122 to 142 cm) with a companion. Riders 48 to 56 inches must ride with a Supervising Companion (16 or older), according to Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cliffhanger?

Cliffhanger is 60 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Cliffhanger go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cliffhanger scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cliffhanger open?

Cliffhanger opened on 15 Jun 2012.

Who built Cliffhanger?

Cliffhanger was manufactured by S&MC.

How many people ride Cliffhanger at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Cliffhanger good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 56 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Cliffhanger operate before?

RCDB records it at Celebration City (as Thunderbolt).
