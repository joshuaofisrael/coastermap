Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Glenwood Springs, Colorado · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park in Glenwood Springs, Colorado has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Wild West Express to 56 in (142 cm) on Cliffhanger. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 56 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Alpine Coaster, which opened in 2005.

What can my child ride at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park? By height

At Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wild West Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 10.8 ft tall, 16.2 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Cliffhanger, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (56 in minimum; 60 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Defiance, the most intense coaster in the park (56 in minimum; 75 ft tall, 56 mph, 2 inversions).
