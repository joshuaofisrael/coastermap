Suzuka Circuit Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Suzuka, Mie, Japan · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Suzuka Circuit Park in Suzuka, Mie, Japan has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Adventure Drive to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Rocky Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Suzuka Circuit Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Suzuka Circuit Park a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rocky Coaster, which opened in 1992.

Suzuka Circuit Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Suzuka Circuit Park? By height

At Suzuka Circuit Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
