Family Star

Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach · Great Yarmouth, Norfolk, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Family Star is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach in Great Yarmouth, Norfolk, England, built by Fabbri. It opened in 2013. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Star is like

Opened in 2013, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Fabbri's Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse Midi design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Neo's Twister at PowerPark and Formula 1 at Magic Park Land. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Star sits on the north side of Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Apple, roughly 20 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Roller Coaster at about 140 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Before you ride Family Star

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • No companion rule is stated.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Family Star?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Family Star. No companion rule is stated, according to Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach, official rides page (ride restrictions) (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Family Star go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Star scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Family Star open?

Family Star opened in 2013.

Who built Family Star?

Family Star was manufactured by Fabbri.

Is Family Star good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Family Star operate before?

RCDB records it at Trampolino Familien- und Freizeitpark (as Family Star); Sommerland Syd (as Family Star).

Is Family Star at Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach the same as Neo's Twister at PowerPark?

Both are Fabbri Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse Midi coasters on RCDB; the Great Yarmouth Pleasure Beach ride opened in 2013 and the PowerPark ride in 2011; both list a 130 cm minimum.
