Tony's Express

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 26.1 mph; 49.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Tony's Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on Sep 2022. The ride stands 49.9 ft tall, reaches 26.1 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tony's Express is like

Tony's Express pairs a 49.9 ft structure with 1,312.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Junior Coaster / J2SK 400m Combo design, and the first of them to open. Others include Flying Viking at Adventureland.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tony's Express sits on the west side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Thunderbolt, roughly 70 m away (50 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Soarin' Eagle at about 190 m. Tony's Express layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Tony's Express?

Tony's Express is 49.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tony's Express go?

Its top speed is 26.1 mph (42 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tony's Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tony's Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tony's Express open?

Tony's Express opened on Sep 2022.

Who built Tony's Express?

Tony's Express was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Tony's Express?

RCDB lists 1312.3 ft of track.

How does Tony's Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Tony's Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 26.1 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Tony's Express at Luna Park the same as Flying Viking at Adventureland?

Both are Zamperla Junior Coaster / J2SK 400m Combo coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2022 and the Adventureland ride in 2023; RCDB lists heights of 49.9 ft and 50 ft.
