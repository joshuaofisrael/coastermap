Luna Park in Coney Island height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Brooklyn, New York · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on The Tickler to 54 in (137 cm) on Coney Island Cyclone. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Luna Park in Coney Island are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Luna Park in Coney Island a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 42% across all 176 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Coney Island Cyclone).

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 7 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Coney Island Cyclone, which opened in 1927.

What can my child ride at Luna Park in Coney Island? By height

At Luna Park in Coney Island the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Luna Park in Coney Island

These are the rules Luna Park in Coney Island states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Circus Coaster: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Luna Park in Coney Island for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Luna Park in Coney Island, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Mini Mouse, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Steeplechase, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Thunderbolt, the most intense coaster in the park (50 in minimum; 114.8 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 4 inversions, 4.3 g).

What are the height requirements at Luna Park in Coney Island?
