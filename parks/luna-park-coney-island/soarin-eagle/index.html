Soarin' Eagle

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 25.7 mph; 50 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2011.

Soarin' Eagle is a steel flying roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on Apr 2011. The ride stands 50 ft tall, reaches 25.7 mph and runs 1282.8 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Soarin' Eagle is like

At 50 ft tall with 1,282.8 ft of track, Soarin' Eagle balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,282.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2011, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Volare / 391m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Hero at Flamingo Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Luna Park sets 52 in, while Flamingo Land sets 127 cm.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Luna Park, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Soarin' Eagle sits near the middle of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steeplechase, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Circus Coaster at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Soarin' Eagle

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Luna Park in Coney Island app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Soarin' Eagle?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Soarin' Eagle, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Soarin' Eagle?

Soarin' Eagle is 50 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Soarin' Eagle go?

Its top speed is 25.7 mph (41 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Soarin' Eagle go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Soarin' Eagle scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Soarin' Eagle open?

Soarin' Eagle opened on Apr 2011.

Who built Soarin' Eagle?

Soarin' Eagle was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Soarin' Eagle?

RCDB lists a ride time of 48 seconds over 1282.8 ft of track.

How does Soarin' Eagle get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a spiral lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Soarin' Eagle at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Soarin' Eagle good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 25.7 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Soarin' Eagle operate before?

RCDB records it at Elitch Gardens (as Flying Coaster); Luna Park (as Soarin' Eagle).

Is Soarin' Eagle at Luna Park the same as Hero at Flamingo Land?

Both are Zamperla Volare / 391m coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2011 and the Flamingo Land ride in 2013; Luna Park sets 52 in and Flamingo Land sets 127 cm.
