Mini Mouse

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Mini Mouse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2021. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mini Mouse is like

Opened in 2021, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Mini Mouse design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Swampwater Snake at Wild Adventures and Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Luna Park (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mini Mouse sits on the north-east side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Tickler, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Coney Island Cyclone at about 60 m. Mini Mouse layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mini Mouse trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 8 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 16 riders.
Riders per train16
Previous locationsVictorian Gardens (as Mini Mouse); Luna Park (as Mini Mouse)

Mini Mouse has been relocated: RCDB records it operating at Victorian Gardens as Mini Mouse and Luna Park as Mini Mouse before Luna Park.

For families checking heights: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster.

Elsewhere, Zamperla also built Dragon (Canobie Lake), Whistle Punk Chaser (Dollywood), Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster (Knott's) and Frankie's Mine Train (Frontier City).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mini Mouse?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Mini Mouse, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mini Mouse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mini Mouse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Mini Mouse open?

Mini Mouse opened in 2021.

Who built Mini Mouse?

Mini Mouse was manufactured by Zamperla.

How does Mini Mouse get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mini Mouse at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Mini Mouse good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Mini Mouse operate before?

RCDB records it at Victorian Gardens (as Mini Mouse); Luna Park (as Mini Mouse).

Is Mini Mouse at Luna Park the same as Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund?

Both are Zamperla Mini Mouse coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2021 and the Gröna Lund ride in 2010.
