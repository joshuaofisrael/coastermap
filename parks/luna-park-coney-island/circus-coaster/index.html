Circus Coaster

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 18.6 mph; 29.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Circus Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on 29 May 2010. The ride stands 29.5 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 574.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Circus Coaster is like

Circus Coaster pairs a 29.5 ft structure with 574.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2010, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Speedy Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Kiddi Koasta at Adventure Island.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Luna Park (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Circus Coaster sits on the east side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steeplechase, roughly 100 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Tickler at about 110 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Circus Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Circus Coaster, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Circus Coaster?

Circus Coaster is 29.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Circus Coaster go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Circus Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Circus Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Circus Coaster open?

Circus Coaster opened on 29 May 2010.

Who built Circus Coaster?

Circus Coaster was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Circus Coaster?

RCDB lists 574.2 ft of track.

How does Circus Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Circus Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Circus Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Circus Coaster at Luna Park the same as Kiddi Koasta at Adventure Island?

Both are Zamperla Speedy Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2010 and the Adventure Island ride in 2011.
