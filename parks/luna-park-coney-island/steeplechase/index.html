Steeplechase

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 37.3 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Steeplechase is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on Apr 2011. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1194.2 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Steeplechase is like

Steeplechase pairs a 42.7 ft structure with 1,194.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2011, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Moto Coaster / 364m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Moto Coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake and MotoGee at Särkänniemi. Height rules for this model differ by park: Luna Park sets 48 in, while Särkänniemi sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Luna Park (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steeplechase sits near the middle of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Soarin' Eagle, roughly 40 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Circus Coaster at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steeplechase?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Steeplechase, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steeplechase?

Steeplechase is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steeplechase go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steeplechase go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steeplechase scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Steeplechase open?

Steeplechase opened on Apr 2011.

Who built Steeplechase?

Steeplechase was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Steeplechase?

RCDB lists 1194.2 ft of track.

Does Steeplechase launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a flywheel launch.

How many people ride Steeplechase at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Steeplechase good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Steeplechase at Luna Park the same as Moto Coaster at Six Flags Darien Lake?

Both are Zamperla Moto Coaster / 364m coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2011 and the Six Flags Darien Lake ride in 2008; both list a 48 in minimum.
