Thunderbolt

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 50 in (127 cm); top speed 55.9 mph; 114.8 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2014.

Thunderbolt is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on 14 Jun 2014. The ride stands 114.8 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 2234.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Thunderbolt is like

Thunderbolt pairs a 114.8 ft structure with 2,234.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,234.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2014, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island.

A rider who just meets its 50 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Luna Park, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thunderbolt sits on the west side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tony's Express, roughly 70 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Soarin' Eagle at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Thunderbolt

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 50 in (127 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Thunderbolt?

Riders must be at least 50 in (127 cm) tall to ride Thunderbolt, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Thunderbolt?

Thunderbolt is 114.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Thunderbolt go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thunderbolt go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Thunderbolt scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Thunderbolt open?

Thunderbolt opened on 14 Jun 2014.

Who built Thunderbolt?

Thunderbolt was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Thunderbolt?

RCDB lists 2234.3 ft of track.

How does Thunderbolt get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Thunderbolt at once?

Up to 9 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Thunderbolt good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 50 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
