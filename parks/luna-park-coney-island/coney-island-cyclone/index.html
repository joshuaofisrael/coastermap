Coney Island Cyclone

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 60 mph; 75 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1927.

Coney Island Cyclone is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York. It opened on 26 Jun 1927. The ride stands 75 ft tall, reaches 60 mph and runs 2850.3 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Coney Island Cyclone is like

With 2,850.3 ft of track for a 75 ft structure, Coney Island Cyclone is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1927, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Luna Park. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Coney Island Cyclone sits on the east side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Mouse, roughly 60 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Tickler at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Coney Island Cyclone?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Coney Island Cyclone, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Coney Island Cyclone?

Coney Island Cyclone is 75 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Coney Island Cyclone go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Coney Island Cyclone go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Coney Island Cyclone scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Coney Island Cyclone open?

Coney Island Cyclone opened on 26 Jun 1927.

How long is the ride on Coney Island Cyclone?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds over 2850.3 ft of track.

What restraints does Coney Island Cyclone use?

RCDB lists: single position lap bar.

Is Coney Island Cyclone good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
