The Tickler

Luna Park in Coney Island · Brooklyn, New York

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

The Tickler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island in Brooklyn, New York, built by Zamperla. It opened on 29 May 2010. The ride runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Tickler is like

Opened in 2010, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Luna Park in Coney Island. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Twister Coaster / 420m design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Sidewinder Safari at Discovery Kingdom, Galaxy Spin at Fun Spot Kissimmee, Wild Mouse at Beech Bend and Wild Mouse at Fantasilandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Luna Park sets 48 in, while Fantasilandia sets 120 cm, Friheden sets 120 cm and Parque Diversiones sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Luna Park (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Tickler sits on the north-east side of Luna Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Mouse, roughly 40 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Coney Island Cyclone at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride The Tickler

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Tickler?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Tickler, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Luna Park in Coney Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does The Tickler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Tickler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Tickler open?

The Tickler opened on 29 May 2010.

Who built The Tickler?

The Tickler was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on The Tickler?

RCDB lists 1377.9 ft of track.

How does The Tickler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Tickler at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is The Tickler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is The Tickler at Luna Park the same as Wild Mouse at Beech Bend?

Both are Zamperla Twister Coaster / 420m coasters on RCDB; the Luna Park ride opened in 2010 and the Beech Bend ride in 2005; both list a 48 in minimum.
