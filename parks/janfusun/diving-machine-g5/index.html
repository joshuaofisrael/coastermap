Diving Machine G5

Janfusun Fancyworld · Gukeng, Yunlin County, Taiwan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 68.3 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Diving Machine G5 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Janfusun Fancyworld in Gukeng, Yunlin County, Taiwan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 29 Mar 2000. The ride drops 179 ft, reaches 68.3 mph and runs 1,250 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Diving Machine G5 is like

At 87.5°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Dive Coaster / Oblivion design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Oblivion at Alton Towers. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 140 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Janfusun, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Diving Machine G5 sits on the south side of Janfusun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Insane Speed, roughly 150 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is KuKu Coaster at about 250 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Diving Machine G5?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Diving Machine G5. Chinese name 飛天潛艇G5. The park's wording: riders 140 cm and under may not ride, according to Janfusun Fancyworld, official ride restrictions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Diving Machine G5 go?

Its top speed is 68.3 mph (110 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Diving Machine G5 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Diving Machine G5 scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Diving Machine G5 open?

Diving Machine G5 opened on 29 Mar 2000.

Who built Diving Machine G5?

Diving Machine G5 was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Diving Machine G5?

RCDB lists 1,250 ft (381 m) of track.

How does Diving Machine G5 get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Diving Machine G5 at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Diving Machine G5 good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 68.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Diving Machine G5 at Janfusun the same as Oblivion at Alton Towers?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Dive Coaster / Oblivion coasters on RCDB; the Janfusun ride opened in 2000 and the Alton Towers ride in 1998; both list a 140 cm minimum.
