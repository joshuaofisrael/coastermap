Insane Speed

Janfusun Fancyworld · Gukeng, Yunlin County, Taiwan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 53.4 mph; 4 inversions; opened 2001.

Insane Speed is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Janfusun Fancyworld in Gukeng, Yunlin County, Taiwan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened in 2001. The ride drops 131.3 ft, reaches 53.4 mph and runs 2677.2 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Insane Speed is like

It goes upside down 4 times across 2,677.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Janfusun, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Insane Speed sits on the west side of Janfusun's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Diving Machine G5, roughly 150 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is KuKu Coaster at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Insane Speed?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Insane Speed. Chinese name 衝瘋飛車. The park's wording: riders 140 cm and under may not ride, according to Janfusun Fancyworld, official ride restrictions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Insane Speed go?

Its top speed is 53.4 mph (86 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Insane Speed go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Insane Speed scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Insane Speed open?

Insane Speed opened in 2001.

Who built Insane Speed?

Insane Speed was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Insane Speed?

RCDB lists 2677.2 ft of track.

How does Insane Speed get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Insane Speed at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Insane Speed good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 53.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
