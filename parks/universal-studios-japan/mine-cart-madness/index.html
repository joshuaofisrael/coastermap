Mine Cart Madness

Universal Studios Japan · Osaka, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in), or 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Mine Cart Madness is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, Japan, built by Setpoint. It opened on 11 Dec 2024. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mine Cart Madness is like

Opened in 2024, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Universal Studios Japan.

Its 122 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Japan (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall, or 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Mine Cart Madness

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in), and riders from 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Supervising companions must be of junior high school age or older. Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Studios Japan app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mine Cart Madness?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Mine Cart Madness, or 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) with a companion. Supervising companions must be of junior high school age or older. Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page, according to Universal Studios Japan, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mine Cart Madness go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mine Cart Madness scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Mine Cart Madness open?

Mine Cart Madness opened on 11 Dec 2024.

Who built Mine Cart Madness?

Mine Cart Madness was manufactured by Setpoint.

How does Mine Cart Madness get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mine Cart Madness at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Mine Cart Madness good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 122 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
