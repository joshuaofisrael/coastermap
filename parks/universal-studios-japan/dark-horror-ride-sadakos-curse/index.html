Dark Horror Ride Sadako's Curse

Universal Studios Japan · Osaka, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Dark Horror Ride Sadako's Curse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, Japan, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 19 Mar 2010. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall and runs 1919.3 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dark Horror Ride Sadako's Curse is like

With 1,919.3 ft of track for a 62.3 ft structure, Dark Horror Ride Sadako's Curse is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2010, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Universal Studios Japan.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dark Horror Ride Sadako's Curse sits on the south-east side of Universal Studios Japan's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hollywood Dream: The Ride, roughly 240 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure at about 380 m.
