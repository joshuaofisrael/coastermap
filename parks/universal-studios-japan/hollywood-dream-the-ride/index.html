Hollywood Dream: The Ride

Universal Studios Japan · Osaka, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 144.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Hollywood Dream: The Ride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, Japan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 9 Mar 2007. The ride stands 144.3 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hollywood Dream: The Ride?

Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page, according to Universal Studios Japan, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). How tall is Hollywood Dream: The Ride?

Hollywood Dream: The Ride is 144.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Hollywood Dream: The Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Hollywood Dream: The Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.
