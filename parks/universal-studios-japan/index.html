Universal Studios Japan height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Osaka, Japan · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, Japan has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 122 cm (about 48 in) on Flight of the Hippogriff to 132 cm (about 52 in) on Hollywood Dream: The Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Studios Japan are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Universal Studios Japan a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 44% across all 148 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 6 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Hollywood Dream: The Ride, which opened in 2007.

Universal Studios Japan publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Universal Studios Japan? By height

At Universal Studios Japan the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Flight of the Hippogriff at 122 cm (about 48 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Flight of the Hippogriff (92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in)) and Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure (92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Mine Cart Madness at 122 cm (about 48 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Flight of the Hippogriff (92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in)), Mine Cart Madness (107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in)) and Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure (92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Hollywood Dream: The Ride at 132 cm (about 52 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Hollywood Dream: The Ride, Flight of the Hippogriff, The Flying Dinosaur, Mine Cart Madness and Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Flight of the Hippogriff (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and The Flying Dinosaur (up to 198 cm (about 78 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Universal Studios Japan

These are the rules Universal Studios Japan states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Flight of the Hippogriff: riders 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Mine Cart Madness: riders 107 to 122 cm (about 42.1 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure: riders 92 to 122 cm (about 36.2 to 48 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Hollywood Dream: The Ride: The Backdrop version, which runs backwards, has the same height rule. Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page.
  • The Flying Dinosaur: Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page.

Best coasters at Universal Studios Japan for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Studios Japan, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Flight of the Hippogriff, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (122 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Mine Cart Madness, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (122 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: The Flying Dinosaur, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 150.9 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
