The Flying Dinosaur

Universal Studios Japan · Osaka, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 150.9 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2016.

The Flying Dinosaur is a steel flying roller coaster at Universal Studios Japan in Osaka, Japan, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 18 Mar 2016. The ride stands 150.9 ft tall, drops 124.7 ft, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 3687.7 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding The Flying Dinosaur is like

At 150.9 ft tall with 3,687.7 ft of track, The Flying Dinosaur balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 3,687.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2016, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Universal Studios Japan.

Its 132 cm minimum is the strictest at Universal Studios Japan, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Flying Dinosaur sits on the south-west side of Universal Studios Japan's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Snoopy's Flying Ace Adventure, roughly 330 m away (122 cm minimum). Next closest is Hollywood Dream: The Ride at about 380 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall, and no taller than 198 cm (about 78 in).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride The Flying Dinosaur

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 198 cm (about 78 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Universal Studios Japan app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Flying Dinosaur?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride The Flying Dinosaur. The maximum rider height is 198 cm (about 78 in). Universal Studios Japan lists this ride on its Child Switch (rider swap) page, according to Universal Studios Japan, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Flying Dinosaur?

The Flying Dinosaur is 150.9 ft tall, with a drop of 124.7 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Flying Dinosaur go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Flying Dinosaur go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is The Flying Dinosaur scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Flying Dinosaur open?

The Flying Dinosaur opened on 18 Mar 2016.

Who built The Flying Dinosaur?

The Flying Dinosaur was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
