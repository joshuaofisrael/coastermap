Batflyer

Nasu Highland Park · Nasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 22.4 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Batflyer is a steel suspended roller coaster at Nasu Highland Park in Nasu, Japan, built by Caripro. It opened in 2001. The ride reaches 22.4 mph and runs 482.3 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Batflyer is like

Opened in 2001, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Nasu Highland Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Caripro's Batflyer design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Ziggy's Blast Quest at The Milky Way.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Batflyer sits near the middle of Nasu Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is F² Fright Flight, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Shinpi at about 130 m. Frequently asked questions

How fast does Batflyer go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Batflyer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Batflyer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Batflyer open?

Batflyer opened in 2001.

Who built Batflyer?

Batflyer was manufactured by Caripro.

How long is the ride on Batflyer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 41 seconds over 482.3 ft of track.

How does Batflyer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Batflyer at once?

RCDB's train details give 2 seats per train.

Is Batflyer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Batflyer at Nasu Highland the same as Ziggy's Blast Quest at The Milky Way?

Both are Caripro Batflyer coasters on RCDB; the Nasu Highland ride opened in 2001 and the The Milky Way ride in 1997.
