Shinpi

Nasu Highland Park · Nasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2009.

Shinpi is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nasu Highland Park in Nasu, Japan, built by Zamperla. It opened in Apr 2009. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Shinpi is like

Opened in 2009, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Nasu Highland Park.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Shinpi sits on the west side of Nasu Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is F² Fright Flight, roughly 60 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batflyer at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shinpi go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Shinpi scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Shinpi open?

Shinpi opened in Apr 2009.

Who built Shinpi?

Shinpi was manufactured by Zamperla.

How many people ride Shinpi at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Shinpi good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
