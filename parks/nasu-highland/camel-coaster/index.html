Camel Coaster

Nasu Highland Park · Nasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 94.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1983.

Camel Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nasu Highland Park in Nasu, Japan, built by Meisho Amusement Machines. It opened in 1983. The ride stands 94.8 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Camel Coaster is like

With 3,937 ft of track for a 94.8 ft structure, Camel Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.9 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1983, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Nasu Highland Park.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Nasu Highland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Camel Coaster sits on the south-east side of Nasu Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spinturn Coaster, roughly 100 m away (135 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Boom at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Camel Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Camel Coaster is number 3 of 7 at Nasu Highland Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Spinturn Coaster (69.9 ft tall, 29.2 mph, no inversions; 135 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Big Boom (126 ft tall, 54.7 mph, 1 inversion, 4.8 g; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Camel Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Camel Coaster. The park lists it as Camel Coaster Goku. Children under elementary-school age ride with a guardian, according to Nasu Highland Park, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Camel Coaster?

Camel Coaster is 94.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Camel Coaster go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Camel Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Camel Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Camel Coaster open?

Camel Coaster opened in 1983.

Who built Camel Coaster?

Camel Coaster was manufactured by Meisho Amusement Machines.

How long is the ride on Camel Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 54 seconds over 3,937 ft of track.

Is Camel Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
