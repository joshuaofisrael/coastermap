Famous Jack

Parc Bagatelle · Merlimont, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Famous Jack is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Bagatelle in Merlimont, France, built by Reverchon. It opened on 25 Apr 2015. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Famous Jack is like

Famous Jack spreads 1,377.9 ft of track across a layout only 42.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2015, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parc Bagatelle. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Reverchon's Spinning Coaster / Classic design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Barrels O' Fun at Six Flags Great Adventure, Exterminator at Kennywood, Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park and RidderStrijd at Hellendoorn. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bagatelle sets 130 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 59 in, Kennywood sets 59 in and Hellendoorn sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Bagatelle, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Famous Jack sits on the north-west side of Bagatelle's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spirale Express, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Triops at about 160 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Famous Jack

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parc Bagatelle app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Famous Jack?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Famous Jack, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Parc Bagatelle, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Famous Jack?

Famous Jack is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Famous Jack go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Famous Jack go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Famous Jack scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Famous Jack open?

Famous Jack opened on 25 Apr 2015.

Who built Famous Jack?

Famous Jack was manufactured by Reverchon.

How long is the ride on Famous Jack?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1377.9 ft of track.

How many people ride Famous Jack at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Famous Jack good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Famous Jack operate before?

RCDB records it at Pleasurewood Hills (as Mouse Trap); Bagatelle (as Ragondingue); Bagatelle (as Famous Jack).

Is Famous Jack at Bagatelle the same as Spin Mouse at Greenland?

Both are Reverchon Spinning Coaster / Classic coasters on RCDB; the Bagatelle ride opened in 2015 and the Greenland ride in 1998; Bagatelle sets 130 cm and Greenland sets 120 cm.
