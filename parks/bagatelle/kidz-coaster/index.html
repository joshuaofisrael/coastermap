Kid'z Coaster

Parc Bagatelle · Merlimont, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

12.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Kid'z Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc Bagatelle in Merlimont, France, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 25 Apr 2015. The ride stands 12.3 ft tall and runs 452.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kid'z Coaster is like

With 452.8 ft of track for a 12.3 ft structure, Kid'z Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2015, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Parc Bagatelle. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Wriggler at Gulliver's Land, Lokolo at Lost Island and Family Coaster at Plohn.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kid'z Coaster sits on the east side of Bagatelle's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gaz Express, roughly 180 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spirale Express at about 270 m. Frequently asked questions

How tall is Kid'z Coaster?

Kid'z Coaster is 12.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Kid'z Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kid'z Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Kid'z Coaster open?

Kid'z Coaster opened on 25 Apr 2015.

Who built Kid'z Coaster?

Kid'z Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the ride on Kid'z Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 55 seconds over 452.8 ft of track.

How does Kid'z Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kid'z Coaster at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is Kid'z Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle the same as Family Coaster at Plohn?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Bagatelle ride opened in 2015 and the Plohn ride in 2006.
