Triops

Parc Bagatelle · Merlimont, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 145 cm (about 57.1 in), or 135 to 145 cm (about 53.1 to 57.1 in) with a companion; top speed 50 mph; 131.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2012.

Triops is a steel inverted roller coaster at Parc Bagatelle in Merlimont, France, built by Vekoma. It opened on 30 Jun 2012. The ride stands 131.2 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 1013.8 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Triops is like

For a 131.2 ft ride, 1,013.8 ft of track is short: Triops is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 1,013.8 ft, it flips riders roughly every 338 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2012, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parc Bagatelle. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Invertigo design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Invertigo at Kings Island. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bagatelle sets 145 cm, while Kings Island sets 54 in.

Its 145 cm minimum is the strictest at Bagatelle. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 135 to 145 cm (about 53.1 to 57.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Triops sits on the south-west side of Bagatelle's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gaz Express, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Famous Jack at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 145 cm (about 57.1 in) tall, or 135 to 145 cm (about 53.1 to 57.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Triops compares

On our intensity ranking Triops is number 1 of 5 at Parc Bagatelle, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Triops

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 145 cm (about 57.1 in), and riders from 135 to 145 cm (about 53.1 to 57.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parc Bagatelle app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Triops?

Riders must be at least 145 cm (about 57.1 in) tall to ride Triops, or 135 to 145 cm (about 53.1 to 57.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in), according to Parc Bagatelle, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Triops?

Triops is 131.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Triops go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Triops go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Triops scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Triops open?

Triops opened on 30 Jun 2012.

Who built Triops?

Triops was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Triops?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1013.8 ft of track.

How does Triops get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Triops at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Triops good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 145 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Triops operate before?

RCDB records it at Liseberg (as HangOver); Allou Fun Park (as unknown); Sommerland Syd (as Tornado); Bagatelle (as Triops).

Is Triops at Bagatelle the same as Invertigo at Kings Island?

Both are Vekoma Invertigo coasters on RCDB; the Bagatelle ride opened in 2012 and the Kings Island ride in 1999; Bagatelle sets 145 cm and Kings Island sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 131.2 ft and 131.3 ft.
