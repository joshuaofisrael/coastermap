Texas Twister

Julianatoren · Apeldoorn, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Texas Twister is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Julianatoren in Apeldoorn, Netherlands, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 15 Apr 2022. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Texas Twister is like

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Julianatoren. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Krazy Koaster at Silverwood, Whirlwind at Waldameer, Spin-O-Rama at Cliff's and Slingshot at Camden Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Julianatoren sets 120 cm, while Silverwood sets 48 in, Waldameer sets 48 in and Cliff's sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Julianatoren, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Texas Twister layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Texas Twister trains and ride system

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Texas Twister

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • The park's height chart: from 120 cm alone, 95 to 120 cm with an adult; maximum 195 cm.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Texas Twister?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Texas Twister, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). The park's height chart: from 120 cm alone, 95 to 120 cm with an adult; maximum 195 cm, according to Julianatoren, official attraction page (height chart) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Texas Twister go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Texas Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Texas Twister open?

Texas Twister opened on 15 Apr 2022.

Who built Texas Twister?

Texas Twister was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Texas Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Texas Twister at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Texas Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Texas Twister at Julianatoren the same as Krazy Koaster at Silverwood?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A coasters on RCDB; the Julianatoren ride opened in 2022 and the Silverwood ride in 2014; Julianatoren sets 120 cm and Silverwood sets 48 in.
