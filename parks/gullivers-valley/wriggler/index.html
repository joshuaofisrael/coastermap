Wriggler

Gulliver's Valley · Rotherham, South Yorkshire, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Wriggler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gulliver's Valley in Rotherham, South Yorkshire, England, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 11 Feb 2023. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wriggler is like

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Gulliver's Valley. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Wriggler at Gulliver's Land, Lokolo at Lost Island and Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gulliver's Valley sets 120 cm, while Winnoland sets 80 cm and Didi'Land sets 130 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Gulliver's Valley, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wriggler sits near the middle of Gulliver's Valley's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rocky Ridge Railway, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grand Prix Racers at about 90 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wriggler?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Wriggler, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). The park lists it as The Wriggler, according to Gulliver's Valley, official park map (height key) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Wriggler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wriggler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wriggler open?

Wriggler opened on 11 Feb 2023.

Who built Wriggler?

Wriggler was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

Is Wriggler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wriggler at Gulliver's Valley the same as Family Coaster at Plohn?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Gulliver's Valley ride opened in 2023 and the Plohn ride in 2006.
