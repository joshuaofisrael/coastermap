Rocky Ridge Railway

Gulliver's Valley · Rotherham, South Yorkshire, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Rocky Ridge Railway is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gulliver's Valley in Rotherham, South Yorkshire, England, built by Zamperla. It opened on 11 Jul 2020. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rocky Ridge Railway is like

Opened in 2020, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Gulliver's Valley. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Mini Mouse design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Swampwater Snake at Wild Adventures, Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund, Mini Mouse at Luna Park and Mini Express at Calaway Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gulliver's Valley sets 120 cm, while Luna Park sets 48 in, Calaway Park sets 36 in and Parc Saint Paul sets 100 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Gulliver's Valley, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rocky Ridge Railway sits near the middle of Gulliver's Valley's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wriggler, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grand Prix Racers at about 100 m. Rocky Ridge Railway layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Rocky Ridge Railway trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 8 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 16 riders.

Previous locationsGulliver's World Resort (as Runaway Train); Gulliver's World Resort (as Runaway Mine Carts); Gulliver's Valley Resort (as Rocky Ridge Railway)

Rocky Ridge Railway has been relocated: RCDB records it operating at Gulliver's World Resort as Runaway Train, Gulliver's World Resort as Runaway Mine Carts and Gulliver's Valley Resort as Rocky Ridge Railway before Gulliver's Valley.

Kid-height check: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 36 and 42 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

Elsewhere, Zamperla also built Dragon (Canobie Lake), Blackhole Coaster (Greenland), Forest Train (Adventureland Sharjah) and Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster (Knott's).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rocky Ridge Railway?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Rocky Ridge Railway, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Gulliver's Valley, official park map (height key) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Rocky Ridge Railway go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rocky Ridge Railway scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Rocky Ridge Railway open?

Rocky Ridge Railway opened on 11 Jul 2020.

Who built Rocky Ridge Railway?

Rocky Ridge Railway was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the ride on Rocky Ridge Railway?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds.

How does Rocky Ridge Railway get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rocky Ridge Railway at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Rocky Ridge Railway good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Rocky Ridge Railway operate before?

RCDB records it at Gulliver's World Resort (as Runaway Train); Gulliver's World Resort (as Runaway Mine Carts); Gulliver's Valley Resort (as Rocky Ridge Railway).

Is Rocky Ridge Railway at Gulliver's Valley the same as Mini Mouse Cartoon at Parc Saint Paul?

Both are Zamperla Mini Mouse coasters on RCDB; the Gulliver's Valley ride opened in 2020 and the Parc Saint Paul ride in 2004; Gulliver's Valley sets 120 cm and Parc Saint Paul sets 100 cm.
