Parque Diversiones height requirements and roller coasters ranked

La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parque Diversiones in La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 80 cm (about 31.5 in) on Montana Ghibli to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Búmeran. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Parque Diversiones are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Parque Diversiones a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 40% of its lineup, against 40% across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Montana Ghibli, which opened in 1984.

Parque Diversiones publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parque Diversiones? By height

At Parque Diversiones the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Montana Ghibli. With a companion they can also ride Teletren (80 to 110 cm (about 31.5 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Bocaraca at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Búmeran. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Parque Diversiones for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parque Diversiones, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Montana Ghibli, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (80 cm minimum; 11.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Torbellino, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Búmeran, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
