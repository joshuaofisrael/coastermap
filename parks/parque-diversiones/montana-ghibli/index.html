Montana Ghibli

Parque Diversiones · La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 80 cm (about 31.5 in); 11.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1984.

Montana Ghibli is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque Diversiones in La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica, built by Lucrezia Di Cartoceto. It opened on May 1984. The ride stands 11.1 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Montana Ghibli is like

Opened in 1984, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parque Diversiones.

Its 80 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Parque Diversiones, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Montana Ghibli sits on the south-east side of Parque Diversiones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Búmeran, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Torbellino at about 130 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Montana Ghibli?

Riders must be at least 80 cm (about 31.5 in) tall to ride Montana Ghibli, according to Parque Diversiones, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Montana Ghibli?

Montana Ghibli is 11.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Montana Ghibli go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Montana Ghibli scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Montana Ghibli open?

Montana Ghibli opened on May 1984.

Who built Montana Ghibli?

Montana Ghibli was manufactured by Lucrezia Di Cartoceto.

How many people ride Montana Ghibli at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 262 riders.

Is Montana Ghibli good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 80 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
