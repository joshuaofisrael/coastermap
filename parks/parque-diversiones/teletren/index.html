Teletren

Parque Diversiones · La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 80 to 110 cm (about 31.5 to 43.3 in) with a companion; 13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Teletren is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque Diversiones in La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica, built by Zamperla. It opened on Dec 2002. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Teletren is like

Teletren pairs a 13 ft structure with 262.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parque Diversiones. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark, Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City and Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque Diversiones sets 110 cm, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 42 in and Silver Dollar City sets 42 in.

A rider who just meets its 110 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Parque Diversiones, out of 4 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 80 to 110 cm (about 31.5 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Teletren sits on the north-west side of Parque Diversiones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Torbellino, roughly 140 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Bocaraca at about 150 m. Who is it for?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Teletren?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Teletren, or 80 to 110 cm (about 31.5 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Riders 0.80 to 1.10 m need a supervising companion, according to Parque Diversiones, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Teletren?

Teletren is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Teletren go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Teletren scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Teletren open?

Teletren opened on Dec 2002.

Who built Teletren?

Teletren was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Teletren?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Teletren get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Teletren at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 300 riders.

Is Teletren good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Teletren at Parque Diversiones the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Parque Diversiones ride opened in 2002 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; Parque Diversiones sets 110 cm and Holiday World sets 42 in.
