Bocaraca

Parque Diversiones · La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 64 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2005.

Bocaraca is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque Diversiones in La Uruca, San José, Costa Rica, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Nov 2005. The ride stands 64 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1148.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Bocaraca is like

Bocaraca pairs a 64 ft structure with 1,148.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,148.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2005, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parque Diversiones. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Whirlwind design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include SpeedSnake FREE at Fort Fun and Rallus at Salitre Mágico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque Diversiones sets 120 cm, while Fort Fun sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Parque Diversiones, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bocaraca sits on the north side of Parque Diversiones' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Torbellino, roughly 140 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Teletren at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Bocaraca compares

On our intensity ranking Bocaraca is number 2 of 5 at Parque Diversiones, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Torbellino (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Búmeran (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 130 cm minimum).

Site-wide, Bocaraca is taller than 41% and faster than 33% of the 1454 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Bocaraca

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parque Diversiones app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bocaraca?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Bocaraca, according to Parque Diversiones, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Bocaraca?

Bocaraca is 64 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Bocaraca go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Bocaraca go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Bocaraca scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Bocaraca open?

Bocaraca opened on 27 Nov 2005.

Who built Bocaraca?

Bocaraca was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Bocaraca?

RCDB lists 1148.3 ft of track.

How does Bocaraca get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Bocaraca at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Bocaraca good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Bocaraca operate before?

RCDB records it at Playland Park (as Whirlwind); Knoebels Amusement Resort (as Whirlwind).

Is Bocaraca at Parque Diversiones the same as SpeedSnake FREE at Fort Fun?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Whirlwind coasters on RCDB; the Parque Diversiones ride opened in 2005 and the Fort Fun ride in 1981; Parque Diversiones sets 120 cm and Fort Fun sets 140 cm.
