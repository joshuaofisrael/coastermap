Tilt of Thunder

VinWonders Nha Trang · Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang, Vietnam

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 58.4 mph; 160.1 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2026.

Tilt of Thunder is a steel sit-down roller coaster at VinWonders Nha Trang in Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang, Vietnam, built by Vekoma. It opened on 10 Jul 2026. The ride stands 160.1 ft tall, reaches 58.4 mph and runs 2965.9 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Tilt of Thunder is like

Tilt of Thunder pairs a 160.1 ft structure with 2,965.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,965.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at VinWonders Nha Trang. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Tilt Coaster / Cliffhanger design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Siren’s Curse at Cedar Point. Height rules for this model differ by park: VinWonders Nha Trang sets 120 cm, while Cedar Point sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at VinWonders Nha Trang. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Tilt of Thunder

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the VinWonders Nha Trang app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tilt of Thunder?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tilt of Thunder, according to VinWonders, official attraction requirements at VinWonders Nha Trang (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tilt of Thunder?

Tilt of Thunder is 160.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tilt of Thunder go?

Its top speed is 58.4 mph (94 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tilt of Thunder go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Tilt of Thunder scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tilt of Thunder open?

Tilt of Thunder opened on 10 Jul 2026.

Who built Tilt of Thunder?

Tilt of Thunder was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Tilt of Thunder?

RCDB lists 2965.9 ft of track.

How does Tilt of Thunder get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tilt of Thunder at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Tilt of Thunder good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 58.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Tilt of Thunder at VinWonders Nha Trang the same as Siren’s Curse at Cedar Point?

Both are Vekoma Tilt Coaster / Cliffhanger coasters on RCDB; the VinWonders Nha Trang ride opened in 2026 and the Cedar Point ride in 2025; VinWonders Nha Trang sets 120 cm and Cedar Point sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 160.1 ft and 160 ft.
