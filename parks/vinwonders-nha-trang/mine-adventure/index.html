Mine Adventure

VinWonders Nha Trang · Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang, Vietnam

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Mine Adventure is a steel sit-down roller coaster at VinWonders Nha Trang in Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang, Vietnam, built by Vekoma. It opened in 2016. The ride runs 2575.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mine Adventure is like

Opened in 2016, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at VinWonders Nha Trang. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Mine Train / 785m design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Calamity Mine at Walibi Belgium, Tren Minero at Fantasilandia and Lost Valley at VinWonders Nam Hoi An. Height rules for this model differ by park: VinWonders Nha Trang sets 110 cm, while Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm, Fantasilandia sets 140 cm and VinWonders Nam Hoi An sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 110 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at VinWonders Nha Trang, out of 2 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Alpine Coaster, roughly 130 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mine Adventure?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Mine Adventure, according to VinWonders, official attraction requirements at VinWonders Nha Trang (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mine Adventure go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mine Adventure scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Mine Adventure open?

Mine Adventure opened in 2016.

Who built Mine Adventure?

Mine Adventure was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Mine Adventure?

RCDB lists 2575.5 ft of track.

How does Mine Adventure get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Mine Adventure good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Mine Adventure at VinWonders Nha Trang the same as Calamity Mine at Walibi Belgium?

Both are Vekoma Mine Train / 785m coasters on RCDB; the VinWonders Nha Trang ride opened in 2016 and the Walibi Belgium ride in 1992; VinWonders Nha Trang sets 110 cm and Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm.
