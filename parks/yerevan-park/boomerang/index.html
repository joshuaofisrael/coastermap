Boomerang

Yerevan Park · Yerevan, Armenia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Boomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yerevan Park in Yerevan, Armenia, built by Vekoma. It opened on 15 May 2021. The ride runs 672.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Boomerang is like

Opened in 2021, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Yerevan Park. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Snoopy's Soap Box Racers at Kings Island, Boomerang at Energylandia, Speedway Stunt Coaster at Six Flags Mexico and Fireball at Furuvik. Height rules for this model differ by park: Yerevan Park sets 120 cm, while Kings Island sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Yerevan Park (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Boomerang sits near the middle of Yerevan Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Yan Coaster, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Happy Coaster at about 130 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Boomerang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Yerevan Park writes centimetres as "sm". The park describes this Boomerang as a family coaster in its Looys' Zone.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Yerevan Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Boomerang?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Boomerang, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Yerevan Park writes centimetres as "sm". The park describes this Boomerang as a family coaster in its Looys' Zone, according to Yerevan Park, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Boomerang go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Boomerang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Boomerang open?

Boomerang opened on 15 May 2021.

Who built Boomerang?

Boomerang was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Boomerang?

RCDB lists 672.6 ft of track.

Is Boomerang good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Boomerang at Yerevan Park the same as Boomerang at Energylandia?

Both are Vekoma Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) coasters on RCDB; the Yerevan Park ride opened in 2021 and the Energylandia ride in 2017; both list a 120 cm minimum.
