Eejanaika

Fuji-Q Highland · Fujiyoshida, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 78.3 mph; 249.3 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2006.

Eejanaika is a steel wing roller coaster at Fuji-Q Highland in Fujiyoshida, Japan, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 19 Jul 2006. The ride stands 249.3 ft tall, reaches 78.3 mph and runs 3782.8 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Eejanaika is like

Eejanaika pairs a 249.3 ft structure with 3,782.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,782.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2006, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Fuji-Q Highland.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Fuji-Q Highland, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Eejanaika sits on the north side of Fuji-Q Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Zokkon, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gaspard et Lisa: Voyage Dans Le Ciel at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Eejanaika compares

On our intensity ranking Eejanaika is number 2 of 6 at Fuji-Q Highland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Eejanaika

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?

  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Eejanaika?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Eejanaika. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Not available to guests over 55, according to Fuji-Q Highland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Eejanaika?

Eejanaika is 249.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Eejanaika go?

Its top speed is 78.3 mph (126 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Eejanaika go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Eejanaika scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Eejanaika open?

Eejanaika opened on 19 Jul 2006.

Who built Eejanaika?

Eejanaika was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.
