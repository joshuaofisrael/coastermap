Fuji-Q Highland roller coasters

Fujiyoshida, Japan · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Fuji-Q Highland in Fujiyoshida, Japan has 6 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Fujiyama at 259.2 ft, which is also the fastest at 80.8 mph. The lowest height requirement is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), for Gaspard et Lisa: Voyage Dans Le Ciel.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Fuji-Q Highland have?

Fuji-Q Highland has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Fuji-Q Highland?

Fujiyama, at 259.2 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Fuji-Q Highland?

Fujiyama, with a top speed of 80.8 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Fuji-Q Highland?

By height requirement, Gaspard et Lisa: Voyage Dans Le Ciel has the lowest minimum at 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Fuji-Q Highland coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Gaspard et Lisa: Voyage Dans Le Ciel, Zokkon. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Fuji-Q Highland do not go upside down?

Nia and Animal Coaster, Fujiyama, Gaspard et Lisa: Voyage Dans Le Ciel, Zokkon have no inversions, per RCDB.
