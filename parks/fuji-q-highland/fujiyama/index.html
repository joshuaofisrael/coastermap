Fujiyama

Fuji-Q Highland · Fujiyoshida, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 80.8 mph; 259.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Fujiyama is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fuji-Q Highland in Fujiyoshida, Japan, built by Togo. It opened in Jul 1996. The ride stands 259.2 ft tall, drops 229.7 ft, reaches 80.8 mph and runs 6708.7 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Fujiyama is like

Fujiyama pairs a 259.2 ft structure with 6,708.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1996, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Fuji-Q Highland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Togo's Sitdown design, and the first of them to open. Others include Go Go Sneaker at Rusutsu and Jungle Coaster at Enakyo Wonderland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fuji-Q Highland sets 130 cm, while Rusutsu sets 110 cm and Enakyo Wonderland sets 110 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Fuji-Q Highland. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fujiyama sits on the south-west side of Fuji-Q Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Takabisha, roughly 250 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Zokkon at about 330 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Fujiyama compares

On our intensity ranking Fujiyama is number 3 of 6 at Fuji-Q Highland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Zokkon (45.4 mph, no inversions, 3.1 g; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Eejanaika (249.3 ft tall, 78.3 mph, 3 inversions; 125 cm minimum).

Before you ride Fujiyama

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders between 110 and 130 cm must be accompanied by someone of junior high school age or older. Not available to guests over 65.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fuji-Q Highland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fujiyama?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Fujiyama, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders between 110 and 130 cm must be accompanied by someone of junior high school age or older. Not available to guests over 65, according to Fuji-Q Highland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fujiyama?

Fujiyama is 259.2 ft tall, with a drop of 229.7 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fujiyama go?

Its top speed is 80.8 mph (130 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fujiyama go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fujiyama scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fujiyama open?

Fujiyama opened in Jul 1996.

Who built Fujiyama?

Fujiyama was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the ride on Fujiyama?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 36 seconds over 6708.7 ft of track.

How many people ride Fujiyama at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Fujiyama good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 80.8 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Fujiyama at Fuji-Q Highland the same as Jungle Coaster at Enakyo Wonderland?

Both are Togo Sitdown coasters on RCDB; the Fuji-Q Highland ride opened in 1996 and the Enakyo Wonderland ride in 1999; Fuji-Q Highland sets 130 cm and Enakyo Wonderland sets 110 cm.
