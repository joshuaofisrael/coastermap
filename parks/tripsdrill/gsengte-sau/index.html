G'sengte Sau

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill · Cleebronn, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 100 cm (about 39.4 in); 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

G'sengte Sau is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill in Cleebronn, Germany, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened in 1998. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall and runs 1574.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding G'sengte Sau is like

At 52.5 ft tall with 1,574.8 ft of track, G'sengte Sau balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Bobsled Coaster / 480/4 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Thor's Hammer at Djurs Sommerland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Tripsdrill sets 100 cm, while Djurs Sommerland sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 100 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Tripsdrill, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, G'sengte Sau sits on the east side of Tripsdrill's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mammut, roughly 160 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Karacho at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for G'sengte Sau?

Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall to ride G'sengte Sau. Minimum age 4 with an adult, 8 to ride alone, according to Erlebnispark Tripsdrill, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is G'sengte Sau?

G'sengte Sau is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does G'sengte Sau go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is G'sengte Sau scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did G'sengte Sau open?

G'sengte Sau opened in 1998.

Who built G'sengte Sau?

G'sengte Sau was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on G'sengte Sau?

RCDB lists 1574.8 ft of track.

How does G'sengte Sau get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride G'sengte Sau at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is G'sengte Sau good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 100 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is G'sengte Sau at Tripsdrill the same as Thor's Hammer at Djurs Sommerland?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Bobsled Coaster / 480/4 coasters on RCDB; the Tripsdrill ride opened in 1998 and the Djurs Sommerland ride in 2002; Tripsdrill sets 100 cm and Djurs Sommerland sets 130 cm.
