Mammut

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill · Cleebronn, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 52.8 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Mammut is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill in Cleebronn, Germany, built by Cordes Holzbau. It opened on 28 Apr 2008. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 52.8 mph and runs 2821.5 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mammut is like

At 98.4 ft tall with 2,821.5 ft of track, Mammut balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2008, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Tripsdrill, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mammut sits on the north-east side of Tripsdrill's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Karacho, roughly 100 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is G'sengte Sau at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mammut?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Mammut. Minimum age 6 with an adult, 8 to ride alone, according to Erlebnispark Tripsdrill, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mammut?

Mammut is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mammut go?

Its top speed is 52.8 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mammut go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mammut scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mammut open?

Mammut opened on 28 Apr 2008.

Who built Mammut?

Mammut was manufactured by Cordes Holzbau.

How long is the track on Mammut?

RCDB lists 2821.5 ft of track.

How does Mammut get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mammut at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

What restraints does Mammut use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is Mammut good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 52.8 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Mammut at Tripsdrill the same as Mammut at Gardaland?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Tripsdrill ride opened in 2008 and the Gardaland ride in 2008; Tripsdrill sets 120 cm and Gardaland sets 130 cm; RCDB lists heights of 98.4 ft and 45.9 ft.
