Erlebnispark Tripsdrill height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Cleebronn, Germany · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill in Cleebronn, Germany has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 95 cm (about 37.4 in) on Volldampf to 125 cm (about 49.2 in) on Karacho. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Erlebnispark Tripsdrill a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Mammut).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rasender Tausendfüßler, which opened in 1986.

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill? By height

At Erlebnispark Tripsdrill the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Erlebnispark Tripsdrill, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Volldampf, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (95 cm minimum; 72.2 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: G'sengte Sau, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (100 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, no inversions, 3 g).
  3. The big one: Karacho, the most intense coaster in the park (125 cm minimum; 98.4 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 4 inversions).
