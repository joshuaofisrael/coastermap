Volldampf

Erlebnispark Tripsdrill · Cleebronn, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Volldampf is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill in Cleebronn, Germany, built by Vekoma. It opened on 26 Jun 2020. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 780.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Volldampf is like

At 72.2 ft tall with 780.8 ft of track, Volldampf balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Erlebnispark Tripsdrill.

Its 95 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Tripsdrill, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Volldampf sits on the south-west side of Tripsdrill's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hals-über-Kopf, roughly 60 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rasender Tausendfüßler at about 190 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Volldampf?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Volldampf. Minimum age 4 with an adult, 8 to ride alone, according to Erlebnispark Tripsdrill, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Volldampf?

Volldampf is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Volldampf go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Volldampf go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Volldampf scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Volldampf open?

Volldampf opened on 26 Jun 2020.

Who built Volldampf?

Volldampf was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Volldampf?

RCDB lists 780.8 ft of track.

How many people ride Volldampf at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 500 riders.

Is Volldampf good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 95 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
