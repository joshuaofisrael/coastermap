Mine Train Coaster

Dalmaland · Biograd na Moru, Zadar County, Croatia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Mine Train Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dalmaland in Biograd na Moru, Zadar County, Croatia, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 15 Jul 2017. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mine Train Coaster is like

Opened in 2017, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Dalmaland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Mine Train Coaster (MX601) design, and the first of them to open. Others include Raptor Adventure at De Waarbeek. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Dalmaland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mine Train Coaster sits on the south-east side of Dalmaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Alien, roughly 120 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Blue at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Before you ride Mine Train Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • The park also lists a minimum age of 6.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Dalmaland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mine Train Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Mine Train Coaster, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). The park also lists a minimum age of 6, according to Dalmaland, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mine Train Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mine Train Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Mine Train Coaster open?

Mine Train Coaster opened on 15 Jul 2017.

Who built Mine Train Coaster?

Mine Train Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How many people ride Mine Train Coaster at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Mine Train Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Mine Train Coaster at Dalmaland the same as Raptor Adventure at De Waarbeek?

Both are SBF Visa Group Mine Train Coaster (MX601) coasters on RCDB; the Dalmaland ride opened in 2017 and the De Waarbeek ride in 2023; both list a 120 cm minimum.
