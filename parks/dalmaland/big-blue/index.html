Big Blue

Dalmaland · Biograd na Moru, Zadar County, Croatia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 41 mph; 60 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2017.

Big Blue is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dalmaland in Biograd na Moru, Zadar County, Croatia, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened on 15 Jul 2017. The ride stands 60 ft tall, reaches 41 mph and runs 1,817 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Big Blue is like

With 1,817 ft of track for a 60 ft structure, Big Blue is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 1 time across 1,817 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2017, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Dalmaland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Dalmaland. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Blue sits near the middle of Dalmaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Alien, roughly 50 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mine Train Coaster at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Big Blue

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park also lists a minimum age of 10.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Dalmaland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Blue?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Big Blue, or 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The park also lists a minimum age of 10, according to Dalmaland, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Big Blue?

Big Blue is 60 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Big Blue go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Blue go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Big Blue scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Big Blue open?

Big Blue opened on 15 Jul 2017.

Who built Big Blue?

Big Blue was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the ride on Big Blue?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 1,817 ft of track.

How many people ride Big Blue at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,500 riders.

Is Big Blue good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 41 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Big Blue operate before?

RCDB records it at OK Corral (as Looping Star); Ocean Beach Amusement Park (as Looping Star); Camelot Theme Park (as Tower of Terror); Dreamland (as Looping Star); Loudoun Castle (as Twist and Shout).
