Tivoli Friheden height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Aarhus, Denmark · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tivoli Friheden in Aarhus, Denmark has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Tyfonen to 125 cm (about 49.2 in) on Vindfald. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Tivoli Friheden are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Tivoli Friheden a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Dragen, which opened in 2001.

Tivoli Friheden publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Tivoli Friheden? By height

At Tivoli Friheden the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Bisværmen (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Tyfonen at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Bisværmen (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Tyfonen at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Tyfonen and Bisværmen. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Vindfald at 125 cm (about 49.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Vindfald. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Bisværmen (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Vindfald (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Tivoli Friheden for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Tivoli Friheden, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Bisværmen, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 23 ft tall, 18.6 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Tyfonen, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: Vindfald, the most intense coaster in the park (125 cm minimum; 75.5 ft tall, 44.7 mph, 2 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
