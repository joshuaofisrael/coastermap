Dragen

Tivoli Friheden · Aarhus, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2001.

Dragen is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tivoli Friheden in Aarhus, Denmark, built by Pinfari. It first opened in 2001 and was previously known as Caterpillar. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dragen is like

Opened in 2001, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Tivoli Friheden. It has run under other names (Caterpillar), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragen sits near the middle of Friheden's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vindfald, roughly 70 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tyfonen at about 110 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Frequently asked questions

Does Dragen go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dragen scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Dragen open?

Dragen opened in 2001 (previously known as Caterpillar).

Who built Dragen?

Dragen was manufactured by Pinfari.

Is Dragen good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
