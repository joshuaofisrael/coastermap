Brucomela

Fabrikus World · Vias, Occitanie, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 100 cm (about 39.4 in); 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Brucomela is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fabrikus World in Vias, Occitanie, France, built by Kılıç Lunapark. It opened in 2024. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Brucomela is like

Opened in 2024, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fabrikus World.

Its 100 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Fabrikus World, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Written from RCDB statistics and the park's published height rule; it describes the layout, not a review.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall.

Before you ride Brucomela

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 100 cm (about 39.4 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as La Pomme: over 100 cm alone; under 100 cm only with an adult. No lower limit is stated.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fabrikus World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Brucomela?

Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall to ride Brucomela. The park lists it as La Pomme: over 100 cm alone; under 100 cm only with an adult. No lower limit is stated, according to Fabrikus World, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Brucomela go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Brucomela scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Brucomela open?

Brucomela opened in 2024.

Who built Brucomela?

Brucomela was manufactured by Kılıç Lunapark.

How does Brucomela get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Brucomela good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 100 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
