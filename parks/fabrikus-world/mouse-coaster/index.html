Mouse Coaster

Fabrikus World · Vias, Occitanie, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Mouse Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fabrikus World in Vias, Occitanie, France, built by Interpark. It opened on 19 Apr 2026. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mouse Coaster is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Fabrikus World.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Fabrikus World, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mouse Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Mouse Coaster, according to Fabrikus World, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Mouse Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mouse Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Mouse Coaster open?

Mouse Coaster opened on 19 Apr 2026.

Who built Mouse Coaster?

Mouse Coaster was manufactured by Interpark.

How many people ride Mouse Coaster at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Mouse Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Mouse Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at EuroPark Milano Idroscalo (as Funny Mouse).
