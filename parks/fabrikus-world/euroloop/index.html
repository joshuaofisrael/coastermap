EuroLoop

Fabrikus World · Vias, Occitanie, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 40.4 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2004.

EuroLoop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fabrikus World in Vias, Occitanie, France, built by Vekoma. It opened in 2004. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 2460.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding EuroLoop is like

At 98.4 ft tall with 2,460.6 ft of track, EuroLoop balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,460.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2004, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fabrikus World. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Python at Efteling and Shaman at Gardaland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fabrikus World sets 140 cm, while Efteling sets 120 cm and Gardaland sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Fabrikus World. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Eurospeed, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

EuroLoop layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on EuroLoop's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop ×2
  3. Double Corkscrew

RCDB counts 4 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride EuroLoop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fabrikus World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for EuroLoop?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride EuroLoop, according to Fabrikus World, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is EuroLoop?

EuroLoop is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does EuroLoop go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does EuroLoop go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is EuroLoop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did EuroLoop open?

EuroLoop opened in 2004.

Who built EuroLoop?

EuroLoop was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on EuroLoop?

RCDB lists 2460.6 ft of track.

How does EuroLoop get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride EuroLoop at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB.

Is EuroLoop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did EuroLoop operate before?

RCDB records it at Mirapolis (as Miralooping); Spreepark (as Mega Looping Bahn).

Is EuroLoop at Fabrikus World the same as Python at Efteling?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Fabrikus World ride opened in 2004 and the Efteling ride in 1981; Fabrikus World sets 140 cm and Efteling sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 98.4 ft and 95 ft.
