Aquila

Mandoria · Rzgów, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 95 cm (about 37.4 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 52.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Aquila is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Mandoria in Rzgów, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 19 Jan 2025. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1,460 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Aquila is like

At 52.5 ft tall with 1,460 ft of track, Aquila balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2025, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Mandoria.

A rider who just meets its 95 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Mandoria, out of 2 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Aquila layout, trains and ride system

Aquila starts with a linear induction motor (LIM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists one element for Aquila:

  1. LIM Launch (from 0 mph - 43.5 mph in 3 s)

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Aquila?

Riders must be at least 95 cm (about 37.4 in) tall to ride Aquila. The park says children as short as 95 cm can ride; it does not state a companion rule, according to Mandoria, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Aquila?

Aquila is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Aquila go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Aquila go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Aquila scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Aquila open?

Aquila opened on 19 Jan 2025.

Who built Aquila?

Aquila was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Aquila?

RCDB lists 1,460 ft (445 m) of track.

Does Aquila launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

How many people ride Aquila at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Aquila good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 95 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
