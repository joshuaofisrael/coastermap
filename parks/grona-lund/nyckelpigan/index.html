Nyckelpigan

Gröna Lund · Stockholm, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 16.2 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1976.

Nyckelpigan is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden, built by Zierer. It opened in 1976. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 16.2 mph and runs 197.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Nyckelpigan is like

At 10.8 ft tall with 197.5 ft of track, Nyckelpigan balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1976, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gröna Lund. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Small design, and the first of them to open. Others include Hoot N Holler at Six Flags Darien Lake, Puff the Little Fire Dragon at Lagoon, Mariehønen at Bakken and Froschbahn at Bayern-Park.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Nyckelpigan sits on the north-east side of Gröna Lund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tuff-Tuff Tåget, roughly 40 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Monster at about 100 m. Nyckelpigan layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Nyckelpigan compares

On our intensity ranking Nyckelpigan is number 5 of 6 at Gröna Lund, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Nyckelpigan?

Nyckelpigan is 10.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nyckelpigan go?

Its top speed is 16.2 mph (26 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nyckelpigan go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Nyckelpigan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Nyckelpigan open?

Nyckelpigan opened in 1976.

Who built Nyckelpigan?

Nyckelpigan was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Nyckelpigan?

RCDB lists 197.5 ft of track.

How does Nyckelpigan get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Nyckelpigan at once?

RCDB's train details give 10 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Nyckelpigan good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 16.2 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Nyckelpigan at Gröna Lund the same as Mariehønen at Bakken?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Small coasters on RCDB; the Gröna Lund ride opened in 1976 and the Bakken ride in 1981.
