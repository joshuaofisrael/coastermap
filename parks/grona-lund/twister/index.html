Twister

Gröna Lund · Stockholm, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 38 mph; 51 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Twister is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden, built by Gravitykraft Corporation. It opened on 7 May 2011. The ride stands 51 ft tall, drops 46 ft, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,544 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twister is like

With 1,544 ft of track for a 51 ft structure, Twister is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2011, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gröna Lund.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Gröna Lund (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twister sits on the south-west side of Gröna Lund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kvasten, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Insane at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Twister compares

On our intensity ranking Twister is number 3 of 6 at Gröna Lund, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twister?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Twister, according to Gröna Lund, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twister?

Twister is 51 ft tall, with a drop of 46 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Twister go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Twister go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Twister open?

Twister opened on 7 May 2011.

Who built Twister?

Twister was manufactured by Gravitykraft Corporation.

How long is the track on Twister?

RCDB lists 1,544 ft (471 m) of track.

How does Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twister at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 38 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Twister at Gröna Lund the same as Twister at Knoebels?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Gröna Lund ride opened in 2011 and the Knoebels ride in 1999; Gröna Lund sets 120 cm and Knoebels sets 42 in; RCDB lists heights of 51 ft and 101.6 ft.
