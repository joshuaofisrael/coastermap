Insane

Gröna Lund · Stockholm, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 34.2 mph; 108.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Insane is a steel wing roller coaster at Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 25 Apr 2009 and was previously known as Tele2 Insane. The ride stands 108.3 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 810.4 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Insane is like

For a 108.3 ft ride, 810.4 ft of track is short: Insane is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2009, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gröna Lund. It has run under other names (Tele2 Insane), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Gröna Lund, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Insane sits near the middle of Gröna Lund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Monster, roughly 50 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twister at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Insane?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Insane. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Gröna Lund, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Insane?

Insane is 108.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Insane go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Insane go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Insane scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Insane open?

Insane opened on 25 Apr 2009 (previously known as Tele2 Insane).

Who built Insane?

Insane was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Insane?

RCDB lists 810.4 ft of track.

How does Insane get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Insane at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Insane good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
