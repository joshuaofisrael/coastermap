Tuff-Tuff Tåget

Gröna Lund · Stockholm, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 4.5 mph; 8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Tuff-Tuff Tåget is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden, built by Zamperla. It opened on 24 Apr 2010. The ride stands 8 ft tall, reaches 4.5 mph and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tuff-Tuff Tåget is like

With 262.5 ft of track for a 8 ft structure, Tuff-Tuff Tåget is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2010, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gröna Lund. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Mini Mouse design, and the first of them to open. Others include Swampwater Snake at Wild Adventures and Mini Mouse at Luna Park.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tuff-Tuff Tåget sits on the north-east side of Gröna Lund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Nyckelpigan, roughly 40 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Monster at about 90 m. Tuff-Tuff Tåget layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Tuff-Tuff Tåget?

Tuff-Tuff Tåget is 8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tuff-Tuff Tåget go?

Its top speed is 4.5 mph (7 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tuff-Tuff Tåget go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tuff-Tuff Tåget scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tuff-Tuff Tåget open?

Tuff-Tuff Tåget opened on 24 Apr 2010.

Who built Tuff-Tuff Tåget?

Tuff-Tuff Tåget was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Tuff-Tuff Tåget?

RCDB lists 262.5 ft of track.

How does Tuff-Tuff Tåget get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tuff-Tuff Tåget at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Tuff-Tuff Tåget good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 4.5 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Tuff-Tuff Tåget at Gröna Lund the same as Swampwater Snake at Wild Adventures?

Both are Zamperla Mini Mouse coasters on RCDB; the Gröna Lund ride opened in 2010 and the Wild Adventures ride in 2019.
