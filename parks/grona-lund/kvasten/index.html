Kvasten

Gröna Lund · Stockholm, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Kvasten is a steel suspended roller coaster at Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 Apr 2007. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1295.9 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kvasten is like

At 65.6 ft tall with 1,295.9 ft of track, Kvasten balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gröna Lund. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 395m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Freedom Flyer at Fun Spot Orlando. Height rules for this model differ by park: Gröna Lund sets 120 cm, while Fun Spot Orlando sets 52 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Gröna Lund (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kvasten sits on the south side of Gröna Lund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Twister, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Insane at about 130 m. Kvasten layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

Before you ride Kvasten

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders must also be at least 4 years old.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kvasten?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Kvasten, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders must also be at least 4 years old, according to Gröna Lund, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kvasten?

Kvasten is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kvasten go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kvasten go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kvasten scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Kvasten open?

Kvasten opened on 28 Apr 2007.

Who built Kvasten?

Kvasten was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Kvasten?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 3 seconds over 1295.9 ft of track.

How does Kvasten get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kvasten at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 650 riders.

Is Kvasten good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Kvasten at Gröna Lund the same as Freedom Flyer at Fun Spot Orlando?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 395m coasters on RCDB; the Gröna Lund ride opened in 2007 and the Fun Spot Orlando ride in 2013; Gröna Lund sets 120 cm and Fun Spot Orlando sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 65.6 ft and 64.3 ft.
