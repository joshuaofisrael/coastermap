Gröna Lund height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Stockholm, Sweden · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Gröna Lund in Stockholm, Sweden has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Kvasten to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Insane. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Gröna Lund are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Gröna Lund a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Twister).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Nyckelpigan, which opened in 1976.

Gröna Lund publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Gröna Lund? By height

At Gröna Lund the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Gröna Lund for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Gröna Lund, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Kvasten, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  2. Step up: Twister, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 51 ft tall, 38 mph, no inversions, 3 g).
  3. The big one: Monster, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 111.5 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 3 inversions, 4.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
