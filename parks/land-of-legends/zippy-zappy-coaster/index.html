Zippy Zappy Coaster

The Land of Legends · Belek, Antalya, Turkey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 31.1 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Zippy Zappy Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at The Land of Legends in Belek, Antalya, Turkey, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2023. The ride reaches 31.1 mph and runs 426.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Zippy Zappy Coaster is like

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at The Land of Legends. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Race Coaster (MX611) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land, Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's World and Grand Prix Racers at Gulliver's Land. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Land of Legends (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Zippy Zappy Coaster sits on the north side of Land of Legends' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hyper Coaster, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Family Coaster at about 210 m. Zippy Zappy Coaster layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Zippy Zappy Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Zippy Zappy Coaster. The park notes that parental supervision is required, according to The Land of Legends, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Zippy Zappy Coaster go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Zippy Zappy Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Zippy Zappy Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Zippy Zappy Coaster open?

Zippy Zappy Coaster opened in 2023.

Who built Zippy Zappy Coaster?

Zippy Zappy Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Zippy Zappy Coaster?

RCDB lists 426.5 ft of track.

How does Zippy Zappy Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Zippy Zappy Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Zippy Zappy Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Land of Legends Theme Park (as Zippy Zappy Coaster); Land of Legends Theme Park (as Zippy Zappy Coaster).

Is Zippy Zappy Coaster at Land of Legends the same as Junior Red Force at Ferrari Land?

Both are SBF Visa Group Race Coaster (MX611) coasters on RCDB; the Land of Legends ride opened in 2023 and the Ferrari Land ride in 2018; both list a 120 cm minimum.
