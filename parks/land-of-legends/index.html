The Land of Legends height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Belek, Antalya, Turkey · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

The Land of Legends in Belek, Antalya, Turkey has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Family Coaster to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Typhoon Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at The Land of Legends are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes The Land of Legends a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 40% across all 221 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Typhoon Coaster, which opened in 2016.

The Land of Legends publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at The Land of Legends? By height

At The Land of Legends the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at The Land of Legends for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for The Land of Legends, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Family Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Zippy Zappy Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Hyper Coaster, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 200.2 ft tall, 71.5 mph, 2 inversions, 4.2 g).
