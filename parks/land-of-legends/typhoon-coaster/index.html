Typhoon Coaster

The Land of Legends · Belek, Antalya, Turkey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 53.4 mph; 141.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Typhoon Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at The Land of Legends in Belek, Antalya, Turkey, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened in 2016. The ride stands 141.1 ft tall, reaches 53.4 mph and runs 1722.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Typhoon Coaster is like

At 141.1 ft tall with 1,722.4 ft of track, Typhoon Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2016, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at The Land of Legends.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Land of Legends, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Typhoon Coaster sits on the south-east side of Land of Legends' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Family Coaster, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Zippy Zappy Coaster at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Typhoon Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Typhoon Coaster is number 2 of 4 at The Land of Legends, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Zippy Zappy Coaster (31.1 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Hyper Coaster (200.2 ft tall, 71.5 mph, 2 inversions, 4.2 g; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Typhoon Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Typhoon Coaster. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in), according to The Land of Legends, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Typhoon Coaster?

Typhoon Coaster is 141.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Typhoon Coaster go?

Its top speed is 53.4 mph (86 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Typhoon Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Typhoon Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Typhoon Coaster open?

Typhoon Coaster opened in 2016.

Who built Typhoon Coaster?

Typhoon Coaster was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Typhoon Coaster?

RCDB lists 1722.4 ft of track.

How does Typhoon Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Typhoon Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 10 seats per train.

Is Typhoon Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 53.4 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
