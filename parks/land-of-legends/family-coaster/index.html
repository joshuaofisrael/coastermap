Family Coaster

The Land of Legends · Belek, Antalya, Turkey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Family Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at The Land of Legends in Belek, Antalya, Turkey, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened in 2018. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Coaster is like

Opened in 2018, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at The Land of Legends. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Family Coaster / 360 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Dragonfly at Duinrell and Cobra des Amun Ra at Belantis. Height rules for this model differ by park: Land of Legends sets 120 cm, while Duinrell sets 110 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Land of Legends (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Coaster sits on the south-west side of Land of Legends' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Typhoon Coaster, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hyper Coaster at about 180 m. Before you ride Family Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Family Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Family Coaster. The park notes that parental supervision is required, according to The Land of Legends, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Family Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Family Coaster open?

Family Coaster opened in 2018.

Who built Family Coaster?

Family Coaster was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How does Family Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Family Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Family Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Family Coaster at Land of Legends the same as Dragonfly at Duinrell?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Family Coaster / 360 coasters on RCDB; the Land of Legends ride opened in 2018 and the Duinrell ride in 2012; Land of Legends sets 120 cm and Duinrell sets 110 cm.
