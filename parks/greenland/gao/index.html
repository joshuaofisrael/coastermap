Gao

Greenland · Arao, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 60.9 mph; 131.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1979.

Gao is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Greenland in Arao, Japan, built by Meisho Amusement Machines. It first opened on 8 Mar 1979 and was previously known as Hurricane. The ride stands 131.3 ft tall, reaches 60.9 mph and runs 5457.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gao is like

Gao spreads 5,457.7 ft of track across a layout only 131.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1979, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Greenland. It has run under other names (Hurricane), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Greenland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gao sits near the middle of Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ladybug Coaster, roughly 150 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Grampus Jet at about 180 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gao?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Gao. The park lists it as Dinosaur Coaster GAO. Riders must be 64 or younger, according to Greenland (Kumamoto), official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gao?

Gao is 131.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gao go?

Its top speed is 60.9 mph (98 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gao go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gao scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Gao open?

Gao opened on 8 Mar 1979 (previously known as Hurricane).

Who built Gao?

Gao was manufactured by Meisho Amusement Machines.

How long is the ride on Gao?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 56 seconds over 5457.7 ft of track.

How does Gao get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Gao at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Gao good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 60.9 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
