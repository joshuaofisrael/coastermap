Blackhole Coaster

Greenland · Arao, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2007.

Blackhole Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Greenland in Arao, Japan, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2007. The ride runs 1312.3 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Blackhole Coaster is like

Opened in 2007, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Greenland.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Blackhole Coaster sits on the north-east side of Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gao, roughly 250 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Nio at about 260 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

Does Blackhole Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Blackhole Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Blackhole Coaster open?

Blackhole Coaster opened in 2007.

Who built Blackhole Coaster?

Blackhole Coaster was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Blackhole Coaster?

RCDB lists 1312.3 ft of track.

How many people ride Blackhole Coaster at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Blackhole Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
