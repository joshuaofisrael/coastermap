Greenland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Arao, Japan · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Greenland in Arao, Japan has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Grampus Jet to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Nio. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Greenland are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Greenland a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 8 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Gao, which opened in 1979.

Greenland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Spin Mouse: Riders must be 64 or younger.
  • Nio: Riders must be 64 or younger.
  • Gao: The park lists it as Dinosaur Coaster GAO. Riders must be 64 or younger.
  • Milky Way: The seated side (Orihime) needs 120 cm; the stand-up side (Hikoboshi) needs 130 cm. Riders must be 64 or younger.
  • Grampus Jet: No age limit is listed.

We could not verify a height rule on an official Greenland source for Sphinx Coaster, Ladybug Coaster and Blackhole Coaster, so they are left out of the height lists below. Ask the park before you queue.

What can my child ride at Greenland? By height

At Greenland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Greenland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Greenland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Spin Mouse, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  2. Step up: Grampus Jet, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (110 cm minimum; 49.7 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Nio, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
