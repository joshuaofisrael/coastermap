Spin Mouse

Greenland · Arao, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Spin Mouse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Greenland in Arao, Japan, built by Reverchon. It opened on 9 Jul 1998. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spin Mouse is like

With 1,377.9 ft of track for a 42.7 ft structure, Spin Mouse is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Greenland. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Reverchon's Spinning Coaster / Classic design, and the first of them to open. Others include Barrels O' Fun at Six Flags Great Adventure, Exterminator at Kennywood, Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park and RidderStrijd at Hellendoorn. Height rules for this model differ by park: Greenland sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 59 in, Kennywood sets 59 in and Hirakata Park sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Greenland, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spin Mouse sits on the north side of Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Blackhole Coaster, roughly 310 m away. Next closest is Sphinx Coaster at about 360 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Spin Mouse

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders must be 64 or younger.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spin Mouse?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Spin Mouse. Riders must be 64 or younger, according to Greenland (Kumamoto), official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spin Mouse?

Spin Mouse is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spin Mouse go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spin Mouse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spin Mouse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spin Mouse open?

Spin Mouse opened on 9 Jul 1998.

Who built Spin Mouse?

Spin Mouse was manufactured by Reverchon.

How long is the ride on Spin Mouse?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 56 seconds over 1377.9 ft of track.

How does Spin Mouse get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spin Mouse at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Spin Mouse good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Spin Mouse at Greenland the same as Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park?

Both are Reverchon Spinning Coaster / Classic coasters on RCDB; the Greenland ride opened in 1998 and the Hirakata Park ride in 1998; Greenland sets 120 cm and Hirakata Park sets 130 cm.
