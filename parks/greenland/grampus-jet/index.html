Grampus Jet

Greenland · Arao, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Grampus Jet is a steel suspended roller coaster at Greenland in Arao, Japan, built by Vekoma. It opened on 11 Mar 1995. The ride reaches 49.7 mph and runs 1968.5 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Grampus Jet is like

Opened in 1995, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Greenland. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Swinging Turns design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Dreamcatcher at Bobbejaanland and Sky Coaster at Dream World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Greenland sets 110 cm, while Bobbejaanland sets 130 cm and Dream World sets 130 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Greenland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Grampus Jet sits on the south side of Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ladybug Coaster, roughly 50 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Milky Way at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Grampus Jet?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Grampus Jet. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). No age limit is listed, according to Greenland (Kumamoto), official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Grampus Jet go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Grampus Jet go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Grampus Jet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Grampus Jet open?

Grampus Jet opened on 11 Mar 1995.

Who built Grampus Jet?

Grampus Jet was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Grampus Jet?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 1968.5 ft of track.

How does Grampus Jet get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Grampus Jet at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Grampus Jet use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Grampus Jet good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Grampus Jet at Greenland the same as Dreamcatcher at Bobbejaanland?

Both are Vekoma Swinging Turns coasters on RCDB; the Greenland ride opened in 1995 and the Bobbejaanland ride in 1987; Greenland sets 110 cm and Bobbejaanland sets 130 cm.
