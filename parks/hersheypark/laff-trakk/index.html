Laff Trakk

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 40 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Laff Trakk is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 23 May 2015. The ride reaches 40 mph and runs 1,400 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Laff Trakk?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Laff Trakk, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Laff Trakk go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Laff Trakk go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Laff Trakk scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Laff Trakk open?

Laff Trakk opened on 23 May 2015.

Who built Laff Trakk?

Laff Trakk was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.
