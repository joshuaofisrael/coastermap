Hersheypark height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Hershey, Pennsylvania · 14 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania has 14 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Trailblazer to 54 in (137 cm) on Great Bear. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 5, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 9 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 14 of them.

What the coasters at Hersheypark are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 10 as extreme, which makes Hersheypark a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 71% of its lineup, against 45% across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 14 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 6 of the 14 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Comet and Lightning Racer); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Storm Runner).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Comet, which opened in 1946.

What can my child ride at Hersheypark? By height

At Hersheypark the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Hersheypark for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Hersheypark, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Cocoa Cruiser, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wild Mouse, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 45.9 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Fahrenheit, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 121 ft tall, 58 mph, 6 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Hersheypark?

Roller coaster height requirements at Hersheypark run from 36 in (91 cm) (Trailblazer) to 54 in (137 cm) (Great Bear), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Hersheypark have?

Hersheypark has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Hersheypark?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Fahrenheit (121 ft tall, 58 mph, 6 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Hersheypark?

Candymonium, at 210 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Hersheypark?

Candymonium, with a top speed of 76 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Hersheypark for first-timers?

Cocoa Cruiser is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 36 in (91 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Hersheypark?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Comet, Trailblazer, Sooperdooperlooper, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Hersheypark?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Comet, Trailblazer, Sooperdooperlooper, Jolly Rancher Remix, Wild Mouse, Lightning Racer, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk, Wildcat's Revenge.

Which coasters at Hersheypark do not go upside down?

Comet, Trailblazer, Wild Mouse, Lightning Racer, Skyrush, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk, Candymonium have no inversions, per RCDB.
