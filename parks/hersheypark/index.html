Hersheypark roller coasters

Hershey, Pennsylvania · 14 coasters

Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania has 14 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Candymonium at 210 ft, which is also the fastest at 76 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Trailblazer.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Hersheypark have?

Hersheypark has 14 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Hersheypark?

Candymonium, at 210 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Hersheypark?

Candymonium, with a top speed of 76 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Hersheypark?

By height requirement, Trailblazer has the lowest minimum at 36 inches (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Hersheypark coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Comet, Trailblazer, Sooperdooperlooper, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Hersheypark coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Comet, Trailblazer, Sooperdooperlooper, Jolly Rancher Remix, Wild Mouse, Lightning Racer, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk, Wildcat's Revenge. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Hersheypark do not go upside down?

Comet, Trailblazer, Wild Mouse, Lightning Racer, Skyrush, Cocoa Cruiser, Laff Trakk, Candymonium have no inversions, per RCDB.
