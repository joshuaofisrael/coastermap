Fahrenheit

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 58 mph; 121 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2008.

Fahrenheit is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 24 May 2008. The ride stands 121 ft tall, reaches 58 mph and runs 2,700 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 6 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fahrenheit?

Riders must be at least 54 inches (137 cm) tall to ride Fahrenheit, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fahrenheit?

Fahrenheit is 121 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fahrenheit go?

Its top speed is 58 mph (93 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fahrenheit go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Fahrenheit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fahrenheit open?

Fahrenheit opened on 24 May 2008.

Who built Fahrenheit?

Fahrenheit was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
