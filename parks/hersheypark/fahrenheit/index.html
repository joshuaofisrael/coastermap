Fahrenheit

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 58 mph; 121 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2008.

Fahrenheit is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 24 May 2008. The ride stands 121 ft tall, reaches 58 mph and runs 2,700 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 6 times. Its steepest drop is 97°, past vertical.

What riding Fahrenheit is like

Fahrenheit pairs a 121 ft structure with 2,700 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 97°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 6 times across 2,700 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2008, it is the 9th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Hersheypark.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Hersheypark, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fahrenheit sits on the north side of Hersheypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Mouse, roughly 170 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jolly Rancher Remix at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Fahrenheit

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Hersheypark app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fahrenheit?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Fahrenheit, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fahrenheit?

Fahrenheit is 121 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fahrenheit go?

Its top speed is 58 mph (93 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fahrenheit go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Fahrenheit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fahrenheit open?

Fahrenheit opened on 24 May 2008.

Who built Fahrenheit?

Fahrenheit was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Fahrenheit?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 25 seconds over 2,700 ft of track.

How does Fahrenheit get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Fahrenheit at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 850 riders.

Is Fahrenheit good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 58 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
