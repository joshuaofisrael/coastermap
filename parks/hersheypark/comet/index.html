Comet

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 50 mph; 84 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1946.

Comet is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened on 30 May 1946. The ride stands 84 ft tall, drops 78 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 3,360 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Comet is like

With 3,360 ft of track for a 84 ft structure, Comet is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1946, it is the oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Hersheypark.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Hersheypark, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Comet sits on the south side of Hersheypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sooperdooperlooper, roughly 90 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Skyrush at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Comet?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Comet, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Comet?

Comet is 84 ft tall, with a drop of 78 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Comet go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Comet go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Comet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Comet open?

Comet opened on 30 May 1946.

Who built Comet?

Comet was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the ride on Comet?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 3,360 ft of track.

How does Comet get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Comet at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 950 riders.

Is Comet good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Comet at Hersheypark the same as Comet at Waldameer?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Hersheypark ride opened in 1946 and the Waldameer ride in 1951; Hersheypark sets 42 in and Waldameer sets 46 in; RCDB lists heights of 84 ft and 37 ft.
