Cocoa Cruiser

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Cocoa Cruiser is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Zamperla. It opened on 10 May 2014. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cocoa Cruiser is like

Opened in 2014, it is the 11th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Hersheypark. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Lil' Devil Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, Grand Exposition Coaster at Silver Dollar City, Roadrunner Express at Discovery Kingdom and Woodstock Express at Dorney Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hersheypark sets 36 in, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 42 in, Silver Dollar City sets 42 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 42 in.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Hersheypark (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cocoa Cruiser sits on the south side of Hersheypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Trailblazer, roughly 140 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Storm Runner at about 170 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cocoa Cruiser?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Cocoa Cruiser, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Cocoa Cruiser go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cocoa Cruiser scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Cocoa Cruiser open?

Cocoa Cruiser opened on 10 May 2014.

Who built Cocoa Cruiser?

Cocoa Cruiser was manufactured by Zamperla.

How does Cocoa Cruiser get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Cocoa Cruiser at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Cocoa Cruiser good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Cocoa Cruiser at Hersheypark the same as The Howler at Holiday World?

Both are Zamperla Family Coaster / Gravity 80STD coasters on RCDB; the Hersheypark ride opened in 2014 and the Holiday World ride in 1999; Hersheypark sets 36 in and Holiday World sets 42 in.
