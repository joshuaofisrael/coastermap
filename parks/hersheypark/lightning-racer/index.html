Lightning Racer

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 51.1 mph; 91.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Lightning Racer is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 13 May 2000. The ride stands 91.6 ft tall, drops 90 ft, reaches 51.1 mph and runs 3,393 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Lightning Racer is like

Lightning Racer spreads 3,393 ft of track across a layout only 91.6 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.6 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2000, it is the 7th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Hersheypark.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 8 other coasters at Hersheypark, out of 13 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Lightning Racer sits on the north side of Hersheypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Mouse, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Laff Trakk at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Lightning Racer?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Lightning Racer, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Lightning Racer?

Lightning Racer is 91.6 ft tall, with a drop of 90 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Lightning Racer go?

Its top speed is 51.1 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Lightning Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Lightning Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Lightning Racer open?

Lightning Racer opened on 13 May 2000.

Who built Lightning Racer?

Lightning Racer was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Lightning Racer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 3,393 ft of track.

How does Lightning Racer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Lightning Racer at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Lightning Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 51.1 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
