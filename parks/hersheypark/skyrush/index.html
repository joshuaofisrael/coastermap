Skyrush

Hersheypark · Hershey, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 75 mph; 200 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Skyrush is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hersheypark in Hershey, Pennsylvania, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 26 May 2012. The ride stands 200 ft tall, reaches 75 mph and runs 3,600 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Skyrush is like

Skyrush pairs a 200 ft structure with 3,600 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 85°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2012, it is the 10th oldest of the 14 coasters we list at Hersheypark.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Hersheypark, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Skyrush sits on the south-west side of Hersheypark's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Comet, roughly 120 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sooperdooperlooper at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Skyrush compares

On our intensity ranking Skyrush is number 5 of 14 at Hersheypark, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Skyrush?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Skyrush, according to Hershey Entertainment & Resorts, Hersheypark Roller Coaster Guide 2026 (lowest height category that may ride) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Skyrush?

Skyrush is 200 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Skyrush go?

Its top speed is 75 mph (121 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Skyrush go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Skyrush scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Skyrush open?

Skyrush opened on 26 May 2012.

Who built Skyrush?

Skyrush was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
