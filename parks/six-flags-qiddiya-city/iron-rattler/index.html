Iron Rattler

Six Flags Qiddiya City · Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 73.3 mph; 208 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2025.

Iron Rattler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Qiddiya City in Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, built by Vekoma. It opened on 31 Dec 2025. The ride stands 208 ft tall, reaches 73.3 mph and runs 4068.2 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Iron Rattler is like

At 208 ft tall with 4,068.2 ft of track, Iron Rattler balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 4,068.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2025, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Qiddiya City.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Qiddiya, shared with 7 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Iron Rattler sits on the north-west side of Six Flags Qiddiya's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Twilight Express, roughly 160 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sea Stallion at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 205 cm (about 80.7 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Iron Rattler

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 205 cm (about 80.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion. Recommended minimum age to ride unaccompanied: 10.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Qiddiya City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Rattler?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Iron Rattler, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 205 cm (about 80.7 in). Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion. Recommended minimum age to ride unaccompanied: 10, according to Six Flags Qiddiya City, official ride accessibility guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Rattler?

Iron Rattler is 208 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Rattler go?

Its top speed is 73.3 mph (118 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Rattler go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Iron Rattler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Iron Rattler open?

Iron Rattler opened on 31 Dec 2025.

Who built Iron Rattler?

Iron Rattler was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Iron Rattler?

RCDB lists 4068.2 ft of track.

How does Iron Rattler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Iron Rattler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 73.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Iron Rattler at Six Flags Qiddiya the same as Iron Rattler at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Six Flags Qiddiya ride opened in 2025 and the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride in 2013; Six Flags Qiddiya sets 130 cm and Six Flags Fiesta Texas sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 208 ft and 179 ft.
