Six Flags Qiddiya City height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Qiddiya City in Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Sea Stallion. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Qiddiya City are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Qiddiya City a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 38% across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 8 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Colossus); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Spitfire).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sea Stallion, which opened in 2025.

Falcons Flight is the tallest of the 1055 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Falcons Flight is the fastest of the 1007 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

Six Flags Qiddiya City publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Qiddiya City? By height

At Six Flags Qiddiya City the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 8 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Sea Stallion (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Twilight Express (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)), Adrena-Line (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)), Iron Rattler (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)), Colossus (up to 190 cm (about 74.8 in)) and Falcons Flight (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Six Flags Qiddiya City for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Qiddiya City, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Twilight Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; 41 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Saw Mill Falls, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 73.8 ft tall, 46 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Falcons Flight, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 534.8 ft tall, 155.3 mph, no inversions).
