Sea Stallion

Six Flags Qiddiya City · Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 43.5 mph; 39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Sea Stallion is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Qiddiya City in Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 31 Dec 2025. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1673.2 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sea Stallion is like

With 1,673.2 ft of track for a 39.4 ft structure, Sea Stallion is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 1.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2025, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Qiddiya City.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Qiddiya, shared with 7 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sea Stallion sits on the south-west side of Six Flags Qiddiya's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Adrena-Line, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Iron Rattler at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Sea Stallion

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion. The park recommends a minimum age of 6.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Qiddiya City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sea Stallion?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Sea Stallion, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion. The park recommends a minimum age of 6, according to Six Flags Qiddiya City, official ride accessibility guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sea Stallion?

Sea Stallion is 39.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sea Stallion go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sea Stallion go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sea Stallion scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Sea Stallion open?

Sea Stallion opened on 31 Dec 2025.

Who built Sea Stallion?

Sea Stallion was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Sea Stallion?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 3 seconds over 1673.2 ft of track.

How many people ride Sea Stallion at once?

Each train carries 2 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Sea Stallion use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt.

Is Sea Stallion good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
