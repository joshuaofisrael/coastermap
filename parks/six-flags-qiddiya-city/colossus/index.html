Colossus

Six Flags Qiddiya City · Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 50.2 mph; 94.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Colossus is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Qiddiya City in Qiddiya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 31 Dec 2025. The ride stands 94.5 ft tall, drops 88.6 ft, reaches 50.2 mph and runs 2918.6 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Colossus is like

Colossus spreads 2,918.6 ft of track across a layout only 94.5 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2025, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Six Flags Qiddiya City.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Qiddiya, shared with 7 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Colossus sits on the south-east side of Six Flags Qiddiya's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spitfire, roughly 230 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twilight Express at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion, and no taller than 190 cm (about 74.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Colossus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Colossus?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Colossus, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). Riders under 130 cm must ride with an adult companion, according to Six Flags Qiddiya City, official ride accessibility guide (PDF) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Colossus?

Colossus is 94.5 ft tall, with a drop of 88.6 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Colossus go?

Its top speed is 50.2 mph (81 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Colossus go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Colossus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Colossus open?

Colossus opened on 31 Dec 2025.

Who built Colossus?

Colossus was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the track on Colossus?

RCDB lists 2918.6 ft of track.

How many people ride Colossus at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Colossus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 50.2 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Colossus at Six Flags Qiddiya the same as Colossus at Thorpe Park?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Six Flags Qiddiya ride opened in 2025 and the Thorpe Park ride in 2002; Six Flags Qiddiya sets 130 cm and Thorpe Park sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 94.5 ft and 98.4 ft.
