VinWonders Nam Hoi An height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Thăng Bình, near Hội An, Vietnam · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

VinWonders Nam Hoi An in Thăng Bình, near Hội An, Vietnam has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Family Coaster to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Lost Valley. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at VinWonders Nam Hoi An are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes VinWonders Nam Hoi An a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Lost Valley, which opened in 2018.

VinWonders Nam Hoi An publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at VinWonders Nam Hoi An? By height

At VinWonders Nam Hoi An the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights
