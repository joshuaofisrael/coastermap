Plopsaland De Panne height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Plopsaland De Panne in Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on K3 Roller Skater to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Anubis: The Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Plopsaland De Panne are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Plopsaland De Panne a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 44% across all 139 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Heidi The Ride); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Anubis: The Ride).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is #LikeMe Coaster, which opened in 1976.

Plopsaland De Panne publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Plopsaland De Panne? By height

At Plopsaland De Panne the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Plopsaland De Panne for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Plopsaland De Panne, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: #LikeMe Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: K3 Roller Skater, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 28.5 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Anubis: The Ride, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 111.5 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 3 inversions).
