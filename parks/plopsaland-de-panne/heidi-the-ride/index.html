Heidi The Ride

Plopsaland De Panne · Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 44.3 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Heidi The Ride is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Plopsaland De Panne in Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 2 Apr 2017. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, drops 67.3 ft, reaches 44.3 mph and runs 2057.1 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Heidi The Ride is like

At 72.2 ft tall with 2,057.1 ft of track, Heidi The Ride balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2017, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Plopsaland De Panne.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Plopsaland De Panne, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Heidi The Ride sits on the south side of Plopsaland De Panne's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Draconis, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is K3 Roller Skater at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Heidi The Ride?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Heidi The Ride, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riders 120 to 140 cm must ride accompanied, according to Plopsaland De Panne, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Heidi The Ride?

Heidi The Ride is 72.2 ft tall, with a drop of 67.3 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Heidi The Ride go?

Its top speed is 44.3 mph (71 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Heidi The Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Heidi The Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Heidi The Ride open?

Heidi The Ride opened on 2 Apr 2017.

Who built Heidi The Ride?

Heidi The Ride was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the track on Heidi The Ride?

RCDB lists 2057.1 ft of track.

How does Heidi The Ride get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Heidi The Ride at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 635 riders.

Is Heidi The Ride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 44.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
