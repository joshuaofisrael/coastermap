#LikeMe Coaster

Plopsaland De Panne · Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1976.

#LikeMe Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Plopsaland De Panne in Adinkerke-De Panne, Belgium, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1976 and was previously known as Keverbaan and later Dongo's Race and later Viktor's Race. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding #LikeMe Coaster is like

With 1,181.1 ft of track for a 26.3 ft structure, #LikeMe Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1976, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Plopsaland De Panne. It has run under other names (Keverbaan, then Dongo's Race, then Viktor's Race), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 12 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the first of them to open. Others include Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure, Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom and Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Plopsaland De Panne (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, #LikeMe Coaster sits on the north-west side of Plopsaland De Panne's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is K3 Roller Skater, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Anubis: The Ride at about 170 m. #LikeMe Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride #LikeMe Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride accompanied.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for #LikeMe Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride #LikeMe Coaster, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 120 cm must ride accompanied, according to Plopsaland De Panne, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is #LikeMe Coaster?

#LikeMe Coaster is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does #LikeMe Coaster go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does #LikeMe Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is #LikeMe Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did #LikeMe Coaster open?

#LikeMe Coaster opened in 1976 (previously known as Keverbaan and later Dongo's Race and later Viktor's Race).

Who built #LikeMe Coaster?

#LikeMe Coaster was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on #LikeMe Coaster?

RCDB lists 1181.1 ft of track.

How does #LikeMe Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride #LikeMe Coaster at once?

Each train carries 40 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,250 riders.

Is #LikeMe Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne the same as Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Plopsaland De Panne ride opened in 1976 and the Six Flags Mexico ride in 1981; both list a 120 cm minimum.
