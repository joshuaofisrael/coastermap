Wrath Of Zeus

VinWonders Phu Quoc · Gành Dầu, Phú Quốc, Vietnam

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 71.5 mph; 164 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2020.

Wrath Of Zeus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at VinWonders Phu Quoc in Gành Dầu, Phú Quốc, Vietnam, built by Vekoma. It opened on 1 Jun 2020. The ride stands 164 ft tall, reaches 71.5 mph and runs 3444.9 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Wrath Of Zeus is like

Wrath Of Zeus pairs a 164 ft structure with 3,444.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,444.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at VinWonders Phu Quoc.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at VinWonders Phu Quoc, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wrath Of Zeus sits on the north-east side of VinWonders Phu Quoc's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Eagle Warrior, roughly 110 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ipanema Skate Ride at about 180 m. Wrath Of Zeus layout, trains and ride system

Wrath Of Zeus starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 6 elements RCDB records on Wrath Of Zeus' track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch
  2. Top Hat - Outside
  3. Heartline Roll
  4. Loop
  5. Corkscrew
  6. Over-Banked Curve

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Wrath Of Zeus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as The Wrath of Zeus in its 'guests over 140 cm' group.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the VinWonders Phu Quoc app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wrath Of Zeus?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Wrath Of Zeus. The park lists it as The Wrath of Zeus in its 'guests over 140 cm' group, according to VinWonders, official height requirements at VinWonders Phu Quoc (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wrath Of Zeus?

Wrath Of Zeus is 164 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wrath Of Zeus go?

Its top speed is 71.5 mph (115 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wrath Of Zeus go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Wrath Of Zeus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wrath Of Zeus open?

Wrath Of Zeus opened on 1 Jun 2020.

Who built Wrath Of Zeus?

Wrath Of Zeus was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Wrath Of Zeus?

RCDB lists 3444.9 ft of track.

Does Wrath Of Zeus launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Wrath Of Zeus at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wrath Of Zeus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 71.5 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
