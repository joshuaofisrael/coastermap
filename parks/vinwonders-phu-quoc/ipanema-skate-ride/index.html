Ipanema Skate Ride

VinWonders Phu Quoc · Gành Dầu, Phú Quốc, Vietnam

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Ipanema Skate Ride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at VinWonders Phu Quoc in Gành Dầu, Phú Quốc, Vietnam, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 1 Jun 2020. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ipanema Skate Ride is like

Opened in 2020, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at VinWonders Phu Quoc. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Surfrider / 20m design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Flash: Speed Force at Movie World, Avatar Airbender at Nickelodeon Universe MOA and Timmy's Half-Pipe Havoc at Nickelodeon Universe NJ. Height rules for this model differ by park: VinWonders Phu Quoc sets 140 cm, while Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 48 in and Nickelodeon Universe NJ sets 48 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at VinWonders Phu Quoc, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ipanema Skate Ride sits on the west side of VinWonders Phu Quoc's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Eagle Warrior, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wrath Of Zeus at about 180 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ipanema Skate Ride?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Ipanema Skate Ride. Listed in the park's 'guests over 140 cm' group, according to VinWonders, official height requirements at VinWonders Phu Quoc (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Ipanema Skate Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ipanema Skate Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Ipanema Skate Ride open?

Ipanema Skate Ride opened on 1 Jun 2020.

Who built Ipanema Skate Ride?

Ipanema Skate Ride was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Is Ipanema Skate Ride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Ipanema Skate Ride at VinWonders Phu Quoc the same as Avatar Airbender at Nickelodeon Universe MOA?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Surfrider / 20m coasters on RCDB; the VinWonders Phu Quoc ride opened in 2020 and the Nickelodeon Universe MOA ride in 2008; VinWonders Phu Quoc sets 140 cm and Nickelodeon Universe MOA sets 48 in.
