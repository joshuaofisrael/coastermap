Minifigure Speedway

Legoland Billund · Billund, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.8 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Minifigure Speedway is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Billund in Billund, Denmark, built by Zierer. It opened on 28 Mar 2026. The ride reaches 34.8 mph. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Minifigure Speedway is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Legoland Billund. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Force / Dueling Reverse Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Minifigure Speedway at Legoland Windsor. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland Billund sets 130 cm, while Legoland Windsor sets 105 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Legoland Billund, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Minifigure Speedway sits on the north-east side of Legoland Billund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Dragon, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is X-treme Racers at about 130 m. Minifigure Speedway layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Before you ride Minifigure Speedway

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 110 to 130 cm must be accompanied by a responsible adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland Billund app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Minifigure Speedway?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Minifigure Speedway, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 110 to 130 cm must be accompanied by a responsible adult, according to Legoland Billund, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Minifigure Speedway go?

Its top speed is 34.8 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Minifigure Speedway go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Minifigure Speedway scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Minifigure Speedway open?

Minifigure Speedway opened on 28 Mar 2026.

Who built Minifigure Speedway?

Minifigure Speedway was manufactured by Zierer.

How does Minifigure Speedway get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Minifigure Speedway at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Minifigure Speedway good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.8 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Minifigure Speedway at Legoland Billund the same as Minifigure Speedway at Legoland Windsor?

Both are Zierer Force / Dueling Reverse Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Legoland Billund ride opened in 2026 and the Legoland Windsor ride in 2024; Legoland Billund sets 130 cm and Legoland Windsor sets 105 cm.
