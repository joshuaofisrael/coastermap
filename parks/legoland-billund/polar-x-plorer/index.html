Polar X-plorer

Legoland Billund · Billund, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 40.4 mph; 62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Polar X-plorer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Billund in Billund, Denmark, built by Zierer. It opened on 29 Apr 2012. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 1633.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Polar X-plorer is like

Polar X-plorer pairs a 62.3 ft structure with 1,633.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2012, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Legoland Billund.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Legoland Billund, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Polar X-plorer sits on the north-west side of Legoland Billund's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is X-treme Racers, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Minifigure Speedway at about 200 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Polar X-plorer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 120 to 130 cm must be accompanied by a responsible adult.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Polar X-plorer?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Polar X-plorer, or 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 120 to 130 cm must be accompanied by a responsible adult, according to Legoland Billund, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Polar X-plorer?

Polar X-plorer is 62.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Polar X-plorer go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Polar X-plorer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Polar X-plorer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Polar X-plorer open?

Polar X-plorer opened on 29 Apr 2012.

Who built Polar X-plorer?

Polar X-plorer was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Polar X-plorer?

RCDB lists 1633.8 ft of track.

How does Polar X-plorer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Polar X-plorer at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 848 riders.

Is Polar X-plorer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
