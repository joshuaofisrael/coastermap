Galaxi

Cliff's Amusement Park · Albuquerque, New Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1977.

Galaxi is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Cliff's Amusement Park in Albuquerque, New Mexico, built by S.D.C. It opened in 1977. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Galaxi is like

Opened in 1977, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Cliff's Amusement Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Cliff's, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Galaxi sits near the middle of Cliff's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is New Mexico Rattler, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spin-O-Rama at about 160 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Galaxi

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • No one under 36 inches; riders under 48 inches need a Supervising Companion at least 54 inches tall.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Cliff's Amusement Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Galaxi?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Galaxi, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion. No one under 36 inches; riders under 48 inches need a Supervising Companion at least 54 inches tall, according to Cliff's Amusement Park, official attraction height requirements (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Galaxi go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Galaxi scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Galaxi open?

Galaxi opened in 1977.

Who built Galaxi?

Galaxi was manufactured by S.D.C.

How does Galaxi get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Galaxi at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is Galaxi good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
