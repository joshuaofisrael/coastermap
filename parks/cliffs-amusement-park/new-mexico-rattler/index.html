New Mexico Rattler

Cliff's Amusement Park · Albuquerque, New Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 80 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

New Mexico Rattler is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Cliff's Amusement Park in Albuquerque, New Mexico, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It opened on 28 Sep 2002. The ride stands 80 ft tall, drops 75 ft, reaches 47 mph and runs 2,750 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding New Mexico Rattler is like

With 2,750 ft of track for a 80 ft structure, New Mexico Rattler is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Cliff's Amusement Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Cliff's, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, New Mexico Rattler sits near the middle of Cliff's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Galaxi, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spin-O-Rama at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for New Mexico Rattler?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride New Mexico Rattler, according to Cliff's Amusement Park, official attraction height requirements (PDF) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is New Mexico Rattler?

New Mexico Rattler is 80 ft tall, with a drop of 75 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does New Mexico Rattler go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does New Mexico Rattler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is New Mexico Rattler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did New Mexico Rattler open?

New Mexico Rattler opened on 28 Sep 2002.

Who built New Mexico Rattler?

New Mexico Rattler was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.

How long is the ride on New Mexico Rattler?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 2,750 ft of track.

How does New Mexico Rattler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride New Mexico Rattler at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is New Mexico Rattler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
