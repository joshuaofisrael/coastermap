1066

Festyland · Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 40.4 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

1066 is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Festyland in Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France, built by Soquet. It opened on 27 Mar 2005. The ride drops 55.8 ft, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding 1066 is like

Opened in 2005, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Festyland.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Festyland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is Drakkar Express, roughly 270 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for 1066?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride 1066, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Festyland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does 1066 go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does 1066 go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

When did 1066 open?

1066 opened on 27 Mar 2005.

Who built 1066?

1066 was manufactured by Soquet.

How long is the ride on 1066?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 1312.3 ft of track.

How many people ride 1066 at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.
