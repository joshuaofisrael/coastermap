Valkyrie

Festyland · Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 40.4 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Valkyrie is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Festyland in Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France, built by Vekoma. It opened on 4 Apr 2026. The ride reaches 40.4 mph and runs 754.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Valkyrie is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Festyland.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Festyland. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Valkyrie layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Valkyrie?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Valkyrie, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Festyland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Valkyrie go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Valkyrie go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Valkyrie scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Valkyrie open?

Valkyrie opened on 4 Apr 2026.

Who built Valkyrie?

Valkyrie was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Valkyrie?

RCDB lists 754.6 ft of track.

How does Valkyrie get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Valkyrie at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Valkyrie good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Valkyrie at Festyland the same as Valkyrie at Gyeongju World?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Festyland ride opened in 2026 and the Gyeongju World ride in 2021; Festyland sets 130 cm and Gyeongju World sets 120 cm.
