Drakkar Express

Festyland · Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Drakkar Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Festyland in Bretteville-sur-Odon, Normandy, France, built by Soquet. It opened in 1997. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Drakkar Express is like

Opened in 1997, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Festyland.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Festyland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is 1066, roughly 270 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Drakkar Express

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Drakkar Express?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Drakkar Express, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Festyland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Drakkar Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Drakkar Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Drakkar Express open?

Drakkar Express opened in 1997.

Who built Drakkar Express?

Drakkar Express was manufactured by Soquet.

How many people ride Drakkar Express at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Drakkar Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
