Jaguar!

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 35 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Jaguar! is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Zierer. It opened on 17 Jun 1995. The ride stands 65 ft tall, drops 45 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 2,602 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jaguar! is like

With 2,602 ft of track for a 65 ft structure, Jaguar! is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a 45 ft drop against a 65 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Knott's, out of 9 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jaguar! sits on the north-east side of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sierra Sidewinder, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress at about 50 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Jaguar!?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Jaguar!, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Jaguar!?

Jaguar! is 65 ft tall, with a drop of 45 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Jaguar! go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Jaguar! go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jaguar! scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Jaguar! open?

Jaguar! opened on 17 Jun 1995.

Who built Jaguar!?

Jaguar! was manufactured by Zierer.
