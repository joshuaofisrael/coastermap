HangTime

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57 mph; 150 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2018.

HangTime is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 18 May 2018. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 2,198 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 5 times. Its steepest drop is 96°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Knott's Berry Farm app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for HangTime?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride HangTime. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is HangTime?

HangTime is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does HangTime go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does HangTime go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is HangTime scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did HangTime open?

HangTime opened on 18 May 2018.

Who built HangTime?

HangTime was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.
