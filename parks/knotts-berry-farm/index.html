Knott's Berry Farm roller coasters

Buena Park, California · 10 coasters

Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California has 10 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Xcelerator at 205 ft, which is also the fastest at 82 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Knott's Berry Farm have?

Knott's Berry Farm has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm?

Xcelerator, at 205 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm?

Xcelerator, with a top speed of 82 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Knott's Berry Farm?

By height requirement, Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Knott's Berry Farm coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Knott's Berry Farm coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Jaguar!, MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress, GhostRider, Sierra Sidewinder, Pony Express, HangTime, Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Knott's Berry Farm do not go upside down?

Jaguar!, GhostRider, Xcelerator, Sierra Sidewinder, Pony Express, Coast Rider, Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
