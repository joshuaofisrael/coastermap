Knott's Berry Farm height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Buena Park, California · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster to 54 in (137 cm) on Silver Bullet. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 7 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 10 of them.

What the coasters at Knott's Berry Farm are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Knott's Berry Farm a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 37% across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 10 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 10 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (GhostRider); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Xcelerator).

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress, which opened in 1978.

What can my child ride at Knott's Berry Farm? By height

At Knott's Berry Farm the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Jaguar! at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 10 coasters with a verified rule: Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Sierra Sidewinder (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Jaguar! at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: Jaguar!, MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress, GhostRider, Sierra Sidewinder, Pony Express and HangTime. With a companion they can also ride Coast Rider (44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Xcelerator at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 10 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Xcelerator, Silver Bullet and Coast Rider. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress (up to 79 in (201 cm)) and HangTime (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Knott's Berry Farm

These are the rules Knott's Berry Farm states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Sierra Sidewinder: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Coast Rider: riders 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Knott's Berry Farm for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Knott's Berry Farm, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Pony Express, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 44.3 ft tall, 38 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: HangTime, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 150 ft tall, 57 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Knott's Berry Farm?

Roller coaster height requirements at Knott's Berry Farm run from 42 in (107 cm) (Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster) to 54 in (137 cm) (Silver Bullet), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Knott's Berry Farm have?

Knott's Berry Farm has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is HangTime (150 ft tall, 57 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm?

Xcelerator, at 205 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm?

Xcelerator, with a top speed of 82 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Knott's Berry Farm for first-timers?

Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 42 in (107 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Knott's Berry Farm?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Sierra Sidewinder.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Knott's Berry Farm?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Jaguar!, MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress, GhostRider, Sierra Sidewinder, Pony Express, HangTime, Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Coast Rider.

Which coasters at Knott's Berry Farm do not go upside down?

Jaguar!, GhostRider, Xcelerator, Sierra Sidewinder, Pony Express, Coast Rider, Snoopy's Tenderpaw Twister Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
