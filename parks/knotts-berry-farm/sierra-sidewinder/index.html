Sierra Sidewinder

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 36.9 mph; 64 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Sierra Sidewinder is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 26 May 2007. The ride stands 64 ft tall, drops 41 ft, reaches 36.9 mph and runs 1505.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Sierra Sidewinder is like

At 64 ft tall with 1,505.9 ft of track, Sierra Sidewinder balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a 41 ft drop against a 64 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 6th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Spinning Coaster / Sierra Sidewinder design, and the first of them to open. Others include Spider-Man Doc Ock's Revenge at IMG Worlds and Reversum at Leolandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Knott's sets 48 in, while Leolandia sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Knott's, out of 9 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sierra Sidewinder sits on the east side of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jaguar!, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is MonteZOOMa: The Forbidden Fortress at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Sierra Sidewinder

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Knott's Berry Farm app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sierra Sidewinder?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Sierra Sidewinder, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sierra Sidewinder?

Sierra Sidewinder is 64 ft tall, with a drop of 41 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sierra Sidewinder go?

Its top speed is 36.9 mph (59 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sierra Sidewinder go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Sierra Sidewinder scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Sierra Sidewinder open?

Sierra Sidewinder opened on 26 May 2007.

Who built Sierra Sidewinder?

Sierra Sidewinder was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Sierra Sidewinder?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 56 seconds over 1505.9 ft of track.

How does Sierra Sidewinder get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Sierra Sidewinder at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Sierra Sidewinder good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 36.9 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Sierra Sidewinder at Knott's the same as Spider-Man Doc Ock's Revenge at IMG Worlds?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Spinning Coaster / Sierra Sidewinder coasters on RCDB; the Knott's ride opened in 2007 and the IMG Worlds ride in 2016.
