Pony Express

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 38 mph; 44.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Pony Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Zamperla. It opened on 22 May 2008. The ride stands 44.3 ft tall, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,250 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pony Express is like

Pony Express pairs a 44.3 ft structure with 1,250 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2008, it is the 7th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Knott's, out of 9 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pony Express sits on the south-west side of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is HangTime, roughly 200 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Silver Bullet at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pony Express?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Pony Express, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pony Express?

Pony Express is 44.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pony Express go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pony Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pony Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Pony Express open?

Pony Express opened on 22 May 2008.

Who built Pony Express?

Pony Express was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Pony Express?

RCDB lists 1,250 ft (381 m) of track.

Does Pony Express launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a flywheel launch.

How many people ride Pony Express at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Pony Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 38 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
