Xcelerator

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 82 mph; 205 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

Xcelerator is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 22 Jun 2002. The ride stands 205 ft tall, reaches 82 mph and runs 2,202 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Xcelerator is like

Xcelerator pairs a 205 ft structure with 2,202 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2002, it is the 4th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Rita at Alton Towers and Stealth at Thorpe Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Knott's sets 52 in, while Hersheypark sets 54 in, Alton Towers sets 140 cm and Thorpe Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Knott's, out of 9 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Xcelerator sits on the north-west side of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Coast Rider, roughly 120 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is HangTime at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Xcelerator compares

On our intensity ranking Xcelerator is number 2 of 10 at Knott's Berry Farm, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 9 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Xcelerator?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Xcelerator, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Xcelerator?

Xcelerator is 205 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Xcelerator go?

Its top speed is 82 mph (132 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Xcelerator go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Xcelerator scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Xcelerator open?

Xcelerator opened on 22 Jun 2002.

Who built Xcelerator?

Xcelerator was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Xcelerator?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 2 seconds over 2,202 ft of track.

Does Xcelerator launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Xcelerator at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,330 riders.

Is Xcelerator good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 82 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Xcelerator at Knott's the same as Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Knott's ride opened in 2002 and the Cedar Point ride in 2003; both list a 52 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 205 ft and 420 ft.
