Silver Bullet

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 55 mph; 146 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2004.

Silver Bullet is a steel inverted roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 Dec 2004. The ride stands 146 ft tall, drops 109 ft, reaches 55 mph and runs 3,125 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding Silver Bullet is like

At 146 ft tall with 3,125 ft of track, Silver Bullet balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 6 times across 3,125 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2004, it is the 5th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Knott's, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Silver Bullet sits near the middle of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jaguar!, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sierra Sidewinder at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Silver Bullet

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Knott's Berry Farm app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Silver Bullet?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Silver Bullet, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Silver Bullet?

Silver Bullet is 146 ft tall, with a drop of 109 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Silver Bullet go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Silver Bullet go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Silver Bullet scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Silver Bullet open?

Silver Bullet opened on 7 Dec 2004.

Who built Silver Bullet?

Silver Bullet was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

Is Silver Bullet at Knott's the same as Silver Bullet at Frontier City?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Knott's ride opened in 2004 and the Frontier City ride in 1986; Knott's sets 54 in and Frontier City sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 146 ft and 80 ft.
