Coast Rider

Knott's Berry Farm · Buena Park, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm), or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion; 52 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Coast Rider is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Knott's Berry Farm in Buena Park, California, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 25 May 2013. The ride stands 52 ft tall and runs 1,339 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Coast Rider is like

Coast Rider pairs a 52 ft structure with 1,339 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2013, it is the 8th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Knott's Berry Farm. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Large Park design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Apple Zapple at Kings Dominion, The Fly at Canada's Wonderland, The Great LEGO Race at Legoland Florida and LEGO Technic Coaster at Legoland California. Height rules for this model differ by park: Knott's sets 54 in, while Legoland Florida sets 48 in and Legoland California sets 48 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Knott's, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Coast Rider sits on the west side of Knott's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is HangTime, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Xcelerator at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott's Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Coast Rider?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Coast Rider, or 44 to 54 in (112 to 137 cm) with a companion, according to Knott's Berry Farm, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Coast Rider?

Coast Rider is 52 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Coast Rider go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Coast Rider scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Knott&#x27;s Berry Farm rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Coast Rider open?

Coast Rider opened on 25 May 2013.

Who built Coast Rider?

Coast Rider was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

Is Coast Rider at Knott's the same as The Fly at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Large Park coasters on RCDB; the Knott's ride opened in 2013 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1999; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 52 ft and 51.8 ft.
