The Great LEGO Race

Legoland Florida · Winter Haven, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 51.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

The Great LEGO Race is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Florida in Winter Haven, Florida, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 15 Oct 2011 and was previously known as Project X. The ride stands 51.8 ft tall, drops 49.8 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Great LEGO Race is like

At 51.8 ft tall with 1,312.3 ft of track, The Great LEGO Race balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2011, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland Florida. It has run under other names (Project X), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Large Park design, and the 6th of them to open. Others include Coast Rider at Knott's, Apple Zapple at Kings Dominion, The Fly at Canada's Wonderland and LEGO Technic Coaster at Legoland California. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland Florida sets 48 in, while Knott's sets 54 in, Kings Dominion sets 54 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Legoland Florida, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Great LEGO Race sits near the middle of Legoland Florida's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Coastersaurus, roughly 80 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Galacticoaster at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride The Great LEGO Race

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Companions must be at least 14 years old and at least 48 in tall.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland Florida app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Great LEGO Race?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Great LEGO Race, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion. Companions must be at least 14 years old and at least 48 in tall, according to Legoland Florida, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Great LEGO Race?

The Great LEGO Race is 51.8 ft tall, with a drop of 49.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Great LEGO Race go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Great LEGO Race go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Great LEGO Race scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Great LEGO Race open?

The Great LEGO Race opened on 15 Oct 2011 (previously known as Project X).

Who built The Great LEGO Race?

The Great LEGO Race was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on The Great LEGO Race?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 35 seconds over 1312.3 ft of track.

How does The Great LEGO Race get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Great LEGO Race at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is The Great LEGO Race good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did The Great LEGO Race operate before?

RCDB records it at Legoland Windsor (as Jungle Coaster).

Is The Great LEGO Race at Legoland Florida the same as The Fly at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Large Park coasters on RCDB; the Legoland Florida ride opened in 2011 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1999; Legoland Florida sets 48 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in.
