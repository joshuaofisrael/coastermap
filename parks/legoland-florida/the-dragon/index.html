The Dragon

Legoland Florida · Winter Haven, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 28.5 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

The Dragon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland Florida in Winter Haven, Florida, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 26 Nov 2004 and was previously known as Okeechobee Rampage. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 28.5 mph and runs 1496.1 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Dragon is like

With 1,496.1 ft of track for a 42.7 ft structure, The Dragon is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland Florida. It has run under other names (Okeechobee Rampage), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 335m design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Cedar Point, Flight of the Hippogriff at Islands of Adventure, Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Japan and Energuś at Energylandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland Florida sets 48 in, while Islands of Adventure sets 36 in, Energylandia sets 120 cm and Movie World sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Legoland Florida, shared with 2 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Dragon sits on the south side of Legoland Florida's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Coastersaurus, roughly 300 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Great LEGO Race at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

How The Dragon compares

On our intensity ranking The Dragon is number 3 of 4 at Legoland Florida, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Galacticoaster (no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Coastersaurus (40 ft tall, 32 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g; 42 in minimum).

Before you ride The Dragon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Companions must be at least 14 years old.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland Florida app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Dragon?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Dragon, or 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) with a companion. Companions must be at least 14 years old, according to Legoland Florida, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Dragon?

The Dragon is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Dragon go?

Its top speed is 28.5 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Dragon go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Dragon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did The Dragon open?

The Dragon opened on 26 Nov 2004 (previously known as Okeechobee Rampage).

Who built The Dragon?

The Dragon was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is The Dragon at Legoland Florida the same as Woodstock Express at Cedar Point?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 335m coasters on RCDB; the Legoland Florida ride opened in 2004 and the Cedar Point ride in 1999; both list a 48 in minimum.
