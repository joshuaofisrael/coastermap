Legoland Florida height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Winter Haven, Florida · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Legoland Florida in Winter Haven, Florida has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Coastersaurus to 48 in (122 cm) on The Dragon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Legoland Florida are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Legoland Florida a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 49% of coasters across all 102 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Coastersaurus).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Dragon, which opened in 2004.

What can my child ride at Legoland Florida? By height

At Legoland Florida the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Galacticoaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Coastersaurus at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Coastersaurus. With a companion they can also ride The Dragon (40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm)), The Great LEGO Race (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Galacticoaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: The Dragon, The Great LEGO Race and Galacticoaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: The Dragon, Coastersaurus, The Great LEGO Race and Galacticoaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Legoland Florida

These are the rules Legoland Florida states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • The Dragon: riders 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Great LEGO Race: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Galacticoaster: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Legoland Florida for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Legoland Florida, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Galacticoaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Coastersaurus, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 40 ft tall, 32 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: The Great LEGO Race, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 51.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
