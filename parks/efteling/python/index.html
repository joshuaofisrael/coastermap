Python

Efteling · Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 46.6 mph; 95 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1981.

Python is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, built by Vekoma. It opened on 12 Apr 1981. The ride stands 95 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 2460.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Python is like

Python pairs a 95 ft structure with 2,460.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,460.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1981, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Efteling. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew design, and the first of them to open. Others include Shaman at Gardaland and EuroLoop at Fabrikus World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Efteling sets 120 cm, while Fabrikus World sets 140 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Efteling (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Python sits on the south-east side of Efteling's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Joris en de Draak, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is De Vliegende Hollander at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Python compares

On our intensity ranking Python is number 1 of 6 at Efteling, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Baron 1898 (98.4 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 2 inversions; 132 cm minimum).

Before you ride Python

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Efteling app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Python?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Python, according to Efteling, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Python?

Python is 95 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Python go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Python go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Python scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Python open?

Python opened on 12 Apr 1981.

Who built Python?

Python was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Python?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 8 seconds over 2460.6 ft of track.

How does Python get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Python at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Python good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Python at Efteling the same as Shaman at Gardaland?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Efteling ride opened in 1981 and the Gardaland ride in 1985; both list a 120 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 95 ft and 98.4 ft.
