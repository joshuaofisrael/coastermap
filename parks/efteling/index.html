Efteling roller coasters

Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands · 6 coasters

Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands has 6 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Baron 1898 at 98.4 ft, which is also the fastest at 55.9 mph. The lowest height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), for Python.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Efteling have?

Efteling has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Efteling?

Baron 1898, at 98.4 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Efteling?

Baron 1898, with a top speed of 55.9 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Efteling?

By height requirement, Python has the lowest minimum at 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Efteling coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Python, Vogel Rok, De Vliegende Hollander, Joris en de Draak. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Efteling do not go upside down?

Vogel Rok, De Vliegende Hollander, Joris en de Draak, Max + Moritz have no inversions, per RCDB.
