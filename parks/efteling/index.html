Efteling height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Python to 132 cm (about 52 in) on Baron 1898. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Efteling are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Efteling a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 43% across all 159 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Joris en de Draak).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Python, which opened in 1981.

Efteling publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Efteling? By height

At Efteling the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Efteling

These are the rules Efteling states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Joris en de Draak: riders 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Max + Moritz: riders 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Efteling for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Efteling, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Max + Moritz, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; 19.7 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions, 1.8 g).
  2. Step up: De Vliegende Hollander, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 73.8 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions, 3 g).
  3. The big one: Python, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 95 ft tall, 46.6 mph, 4 inversions, 3.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
