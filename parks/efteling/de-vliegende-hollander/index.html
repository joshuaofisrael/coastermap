De Vliegende Hollander

Efteling · Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 73.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

De Vliegende Hollander is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, built by KumbaK The Amusement Engineers. It opened on 1 Apr 2007. The ride stands 73.8 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

What is the height requirement for De Vliegende Hollander?

How tall is De Vliegende Hollander?

De Vliegende Hollander is 73.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does De Vliegende Hollander go?

Does De Vliegende Hollander go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.
