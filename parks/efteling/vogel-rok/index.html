Vogel Rok

Efteling · Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 40.4 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Vogel Rok is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, built by Vekoma. It opened on 9 Apr 1998. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, drops 62.3 ft, reaches 40.4 mph and runs 2460.6 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vogel Rok is like

Vogel Rok spreads 2,460.6 ft of track across a layout only 65.6 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1998, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Efteling.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Efteling (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vogel Rok sits on the north side of Efteling's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Baron 1898, roughly 540 m away (132 cm minimum). Next closest is De Vliegende Hollander at about 610 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Vogel Rok layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

How Vogel Rok compares

On our intensity ranking Vogel Rok is number 5 of 6 at Efteling, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Max + Moritz (19.7 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions, 1.8 g; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is De Vliegende Hollander (73.8 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions, 3 g; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vogel Rok?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Vogel Rok, according to Efteling, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vogel Rok?

Vogel Rok is 65.6 ft tall, with a drop of 62.3 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Vogel Rok go?

Its top speed is 40.4 mph (65 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vogel Rok go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vogel Rok scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Vogel Rok open?

Vogel Rok opened on 9 Apr 1998.

Who built Vogel Rok?

Vogel Rok was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Vogel Rok?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 41 seconds over 2460.6 ft of track.

How does Vogel Rok get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vogel Rok at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Vogel Rok good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
