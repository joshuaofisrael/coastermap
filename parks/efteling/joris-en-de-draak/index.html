Joris en de Draak

Efteling · Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 46.6 mph; 72.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Joris en de Draak is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 1 Jul 2010. The ride stands 72.4 ft tall, drops 72.4 ft, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 2,585 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Joris en de Draak is like

With 2,585 ft of track for a 72.4 ft structure, Joris en de Draak is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2010, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Efteling.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Efteling (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Joris en de Draak sits on the south side of Efteling's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is De Vliegende Hollander, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Python at about 110 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Joris en de Draak compares

On our intensity ranking Joris en de Draak is number 3 of 6 at Efteling, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is De Vliegende Hollander (73.8 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions, 3 g; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Baron 1898 (98.4 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 2 inversions; 132 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Joris en de Draak?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Joris en de Draak, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders below the ride-alone height must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or over, according to Efteling, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Joris en de Draak?

Joris en de Draak is 72.4 ft tall, with a drop of 72.4 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Joris en de Draak go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Joris en de Draak go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Joris en de Draak scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Joris en de Draak open?

Joris en de Draak opened on 1 Jul 2010.

Who built Joris en de Draak?

Joris en de Draak was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the track on Joris en de Draak?

RCDB lists 2,585 ft (788 m) of track.

How does Joris en de Draak get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Joris en de Draak at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,750 riders.

Is Joris en de Draak good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
