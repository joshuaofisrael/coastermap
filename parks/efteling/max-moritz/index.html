Max + Moritz

Efteling · Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 19.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Max + Moritz is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Efteling in Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 20 Jun 2020. The ride stands 19.7 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 984.3 ft of track on each track. It runs on two tracks side by side. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Max + Moritz is like

With 984.3 ft of track for a 19.7 ft structure, Max + Moritz is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a peak force of 1.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2020, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Efteling.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Efteling, out of 5 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Max + Moritz sits on the west side of Efteling's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Baron 1898, roughly 220 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is De Vliegende Hollander at about 290 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Max + Moritz?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Max + Moritz, or 90 to 130 cm (about 35.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders below the ride-alone height must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or over, according to Efteling, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Max + Moritz?

Max + Moritz is 19.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Max + Moritz go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Max + Moritz go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Max + Moritz scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Max + Moritz open?

Max + Moritz opened on 20 Jun 2020.

Who built Max + Moritz?

Max + Moritz was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
