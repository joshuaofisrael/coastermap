Hurricane

Himeji Central Park · Himeji, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 3 inversions; opened 2007.

Hurricane is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Himeji Central Park in Himeji, Japan, built by Togo. It opened on 24 Mar 2007. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Hurricane is like

Opened in 2007, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Himeji Central Park.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Himeji Central Park (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hurricane sits on the east side of Himeji Central Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Venus GP, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Imorinth at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 185 cm (about 72.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Hurricane compares

On our intensity ranking Hurricane is number 3 of 6 at Himeji Central Park, where 1 is the most intense.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Go! Go! Jet (72.2 ft tall, 40.4 mph, no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Venus GP (131.3 ft tall, 53.4 mph, 1 inversion, 5.2 g; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Hurricane

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 185 cm (about 72.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Himeji Central Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hurricane?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Hurricane. The maximum rider height is 185 cm (about 72.8 in), according to Himeji Central Park, official attraction list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Hurricane go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Hurricane scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hurricane open?

Hurricane opened on 24 Mar 2007.

Who built Hurricane?

Hurricane was manufactured by Togo.

How does Hurricane get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hurricane at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Hurricane good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Hurricane operate before?

RCDB records it at Kintetsu Ayameike (as Tornado).
