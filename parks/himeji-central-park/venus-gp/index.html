Venus GP

Himeji Central Park · Himeji, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 53.4 mph; 131.3 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2022.

Venus GP is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Himeji Central Park in Himeji, Japan, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 16 Jul 2022. The ride stands 131.3 ft tall, reaches 53.4 mph and runs 3412.1 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Venus GP is like

At 131.3 ft tall with 3,412.1 ft of track, Venus GP balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 1 time across 3,412.1 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2022, it is the newest of the 6 coasters we list at Himeji Central Park.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Himeji Central Park (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Venus GP sits on the east side of Himeji Central Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurricane, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Imorinth at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

On our intensity ranking Venus GP is number 2 of 6 at Himeji Central Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Venus GP

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 65 and over may not ride; children under elementary-school age ride with a guardian.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Himeji Central Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Venus GP?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Venus GP. Riders 65 and over may not ride; children under elementary-school age ride with a guardian, according to Himeji Central Park, official attraction list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Venus GP?

Venus GP is 131.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Venus GP go?

Its top speed is 53.4 mph (86 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Venus GP go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Venus GP scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Venus GP open?

Venus GP opened on 16 Jul 2022.

Who built Venus GP?

Venus GP was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Venus GP?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 15 seconds over 3412.1 ft of track.

How does Venus GP get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Venus GP at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Venus GP good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 53.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Venus GP operate before?

RCDB records it at Space World (as Venus GP); Rusutsu Resort (as unknown).
