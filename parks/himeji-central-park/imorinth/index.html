Imorinth

Himeji Central Park · Himeji, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 13.7 mph; 16.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Imorinth is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Himeji Central Park in Himeji, Japan, built by Pinfari. It opened in Mar 1998. The ride stands 16.4 ft tall, reaches 13.7 mph and runs 429.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Imorinth is like

At 16.4 ft tall with 429.8 ft of track, Imorinth balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1998, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Himeji Central Park.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Imorinth sits on the south side of Himeji Central Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurricane, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Go! Go! Jet at about 170 m. Frequently asked questions

How tall is Imorinth?

Imorinth is 16.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Imorinth go?

Its top speed is 13.7 mph (22 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Imorinth go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Imorinth scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Imorinth open?

Imorinth opened in Mar 1998.

Who built Imorinth?

Imorinth was manufactured by Pinfari.

How long is the ride on Imorinth?

RCDB lists a ride time of 58 seconds over 429.8 ft of track.

How does Imorinth get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Imorinth at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

What restraints does Imorinth use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, single position lap bar.

Is Imorinth good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 13.7 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
