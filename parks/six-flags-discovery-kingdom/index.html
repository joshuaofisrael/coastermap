Six Flags Discovery Kingdom height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Vallejo, California · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom in Vallejo, California has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Roadrunner Express to 54 in (137 cm) on Medusa. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 10 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 7 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Discovery Kingdom a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 70% of its lineup, against 45% across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 10 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 6 of the 10 go upside down; 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (The Flash: Vertical Velocity and Superman Ultimate Flight).

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Boomerang, which opened in 1998.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom? By height

At Six Flags Discovery Kingdom the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being Roadrunner Express at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 10 coasters with a verified rule: Roadrunner Express. With a companion they can also ride Cobra (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)) and Sidewinder Safari (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Cobra, Boomerang, The Joker, Batman The Ride and Sidewinder Safari. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Kong at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 10 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Medusa, The Flash: Vertical Velocity, Kong and Superman Ultimate Flight. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: The Flash: Vertical Velocity (up to 75 in (190 cm)), Kong (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Batman The Ride (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom

These are the rules Six Flags Discovery Kingdom states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Cobra: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Superman Ultimate Flight: riders 52 to 54 in (132 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Sidewinder Safari: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Discovery Kingdom, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Roadrunner Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 13 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Sidewinder Safari, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: Medusa, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 150 ft tall, 65 mph, 7 inversions, 4.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

What are the height requirements at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom?

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Medusa (150 ft tall, 65 mph, 7 inversions, 4.5 g).
