Six Flags Discovery Kingdom roller coasters

Vallejo, California · 10 coasters

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom in Vallejo, California has 10 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Medusa at 150 ft, which is also the fastest at 65 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Roadrunner Express.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Discovery Kingdom have?

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom?

Medusa, at 150 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom?

Medusa, with a top speed of 65 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom?

By height requirement, Roadrunner Express has the lowest minimum at 42 in (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Discovery Kingdom coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Roadrunner Express. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Discovery Kingdom coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Roadrunner Express, Cobra, Boomerang, The Joker, Batman The Ride, Sidewinder Safari. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom do not go upside down?

Roadrunner Express, Cobra, Batman The Ride, Sidewinder Safari have no inversions, per RCDB.
