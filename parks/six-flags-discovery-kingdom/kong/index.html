Kong

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom · Vallejo, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1998.

Kong is a steel inverted roller coaster at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom in Vallejo, California, built by Vekoma. It opened on May 1998. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Kong is like

At 109.3 ft tall with 2,260.5 ft of track, Kong balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1998, it is the 2nd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom. It is one of 15 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure and Mind Eraser at Six Flags Darien Lake. Height rules for this model differ by park: Discovery Kingdom sets 52 in, while Energylandia sets 140 cm, Heide Park sets 130 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Discovery Kingdom, out of 9 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kong sits on the north-west side of Discovery Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cobra, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Medusa at about 110 m. Kong layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Kong's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Double In-Line Twist

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Discovery Kingdom rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Kong

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 78 in (198 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Discovery Kingdom app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kong?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Kong. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Six Flags Discovery Kingdom, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kong?

Kong is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kong go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kong go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Kong scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Discovery Kingdom rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Kong open?

Kong opened on May 1998.

Who built Kong?

Kong was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Kong?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Kong get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kong at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Kong good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Kong operate before?

RCDB records it at Opryland USA (as Hangman).

Is Kong at Discovery Kingdom the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Discovery Kingdom ride opened in 1998 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; both list a 52 in minimum.
