Roadrunner Express

Six Flags Discovery Kingdom · Vallejo, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Roadrunner Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Discovery Kingdom in Vallejo, California, built by Zamperla. It opened in 1999. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Discovery Kingdom rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Roadrunner Express?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Roadrunner Express, according to Six Flags Discovery Kingdom, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Roadrunner Express?

Roadrunner Express is 13 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Roadrunner Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Roadrunner Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Discovery Kingdom rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Roadrunner Express open?

Roadrunner Express opened on 1999.

Who built Roadrunner Express?

Roadrunner Express was manufactured by Zamperla.
