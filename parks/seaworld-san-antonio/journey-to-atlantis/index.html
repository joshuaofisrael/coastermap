Journey to Atlantis

SeaWorld San Antonio · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 39 in (99 cm); 100 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Journey to Atlantis is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 11 May 2007. The ride stands 100 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Journey to Atlantis is like

Opened in 2007, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at SeaWorld San Antonio.

Its 39 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Journey to Atlantis sits on the west side of SeaWorld San Antonio's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Beach Rescue Racer, roughly 240 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Texas Stingray at about 350 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 39 in (99 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Journey to Atlantis compares

On our intensity ranking Journey to Atlantis is number 5 of 7 at SeaWorld San Antonio, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Barracuda Strike (90 ft tall, 44 mph, no inversions; 42 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Texas Stingray (96 ft tall, 55 mph, no inversions; 46 in minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Journey to Atlantis?

Riders must be at least 39 in (99 cm) tall to ride Journey to Atlantis, according to SeaWorld San Antonio, Rides and Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Journey to Atlantis?

Journey to Atlantis is 100 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Journey to Atlantis go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Journey to Atlantis scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Journey to Atlantis open?

Journey to Atlantis opened on 11 May 2007.

Who built Journey to Atlantis?

Journey to Atlantis was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Journey to Atlantis?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 30 seconds.

How does Journey to Atlantis get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Journey to Atlantis at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Journey to Atlantis good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 39 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Journey to Atlantis at SeaWorld San Antonio the same as Journey to Atlantis at SeaWorld Orlando?

They share a name but are separate rides; the SeaWorld San Antonio ride opened in 2007 and the SeaWorld Orlando ride in 1998; SeaWorld San Antonio sets 39 in and SeaWorld Orlando sets 42 in.
