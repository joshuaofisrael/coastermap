SeaWorld San Antonio height requirements and roller coasters ranked

San Antonio, Texas · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 39 in (99 cm) on Journey to Atlantis to 54 in (137 cm) on Great White. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes SeaWorld San Antonio a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 29% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Texas Stingray); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Great White, which opened in 1997.

What can my child ride at SeaWorld San Antonio? By height

At SeaWorld San Antonio the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Journey to Atlantis at 39 in (99 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Journey to Atlantis and Barracuda Strike. With a companion they can also ride Beach Rescue Racer (38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Texas Stingray at 46 in (117 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, Texas Stingray, Beach Rescue Racer and Steel Eel. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Great White at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Great White. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for SeaWorld San Antonio, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Beach Rescue Racer, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Texas Stingray, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (46 in minimum; 96 ft tall, 55 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Great White, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 108 ft tall, 50 mph, 5 inversions, 4.6 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Roller coaster height requirements at SeaWorld San Antonio run from 39 in (99 cm) (Journey to Atlantis) to 54 in (137 cm) (Great White), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does SeaWorld San Antonio have?

SeaWorld San Antonio has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Great White (108 ft tall, 50 mph, 5 inversions, 4.6 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Steel Eel, at 150 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Steel Eel, with a top speed of 65 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio for first-timers?

Beach Rescue Racer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Journey to Atlantis, Barracuda Strike. With a companion they can also ride Beach Rescue Racer.

What can a 48-inch child ride at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Journey to Atlantis, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, Texas Stingray, Beach Rescue Racer, Barracuda Strike, Steel Eel.

Which coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio do not go upside down?

Journey to Atlantis, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, Texas Stingray, Beach Rescue Racer, Barracuda Strike, Steel Eel have no inversions, per RCDB.
