SeaWorld San Antonio roller coasters

San Antonio, Texas · 7 coasters

SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas has 7 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Steel Eel at 150 ft, which is also the fastest at 65 mph. The lowest height requirement is 39 in (99 cm), for Journey to Atlantis.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does SeaWorld San Antonio have?

SeaWorld San Antonio has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Steel Eel, at 150 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio?

Steel Eel, with a top speed of 65 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at SeaWorld San Antonio?

By height requirement, Journey to Atlantis has the lowest minimum at 39 in (99 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which SeaWorld San Antonio coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis, Barracuda Strike. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which SeaWorld San Antonio coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Journey to Atlantis, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, Texas Stingray, Beach Rescue Racer, Barracuda Strike, Steel Eel. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio do not go upside down?

Journey to Atlantis, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, Texas Stingray, Beach Rescue Racer, Barracuda Strike, Steel Eel have no inversions, per RCDB.
