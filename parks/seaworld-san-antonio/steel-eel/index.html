Steel Eel

SeaWorld San Antonio · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 65 mph; 150 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Steel Eel is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas, built by D.H. Morgan Manufacturing, Inc. It opened on 6 Mar 1999. The ride stands 150 ft tall, drops 150 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 3,700 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Steel Eel is like

Steel Eel pairs a 150 ft structure with 3,700 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at SeaWorld San Antonio.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Eel sits on the east side of SeaWorld San Antonio's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Great White, roughly 230 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Eel?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Steel Eel, according to SeaWorld San Antonio, Rides and Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Eel?

Steel Eel is 150 ft tall, with a drop of 150 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Eel go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Eel go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Steel Eel scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Steel Eel open?

Steel Eel opened on 6 Mar 1999.

Who built Steel Eel?

Steel Eel was manufactured by D.H. Morgan Manufacturing, Inc.

How long is the ride on Steel Eel?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds over 3,700 ft of track.

How does Steel Eel get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Steel Eel at once?

Up to 36 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is Steel Eel good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
