Great White

SeaWorld San Antonio · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 108 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1997.

Great White is a steel inverted roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on Feb 1997. The ride stands 108 ft tall, drops 81.2 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,562 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Great White is like

Great White pairs a 108 ft structure with 2,562 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,562 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.6 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1997, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at SeaWorld San Antonio. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Inverted Coaster / Batman design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Batman The Ride at Magic Mountain, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great Adventure, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Over Texas. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at SeaWorld San Antonio. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Great White sits on the south-east side of SeaWorld San Antonio's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster, roughly 190 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Steel Eel at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Great White

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the SeaWorld San Antonio app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Great White?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Great White, according to SeaWorld San Antonio, Rides and Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Great White?

Great White is 108 ft tall, with a drop of 81.2 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Great White go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Great White go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Great White scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Great White open?

Great White opened on Feb 1997.

Who built Great White?

Great White was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

Is Great White at SeaWorld San Antonio the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Inverted Coaster / Batman coasters on RCDB; the SeaWorld San Antonio ride opened in 1997 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1992; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 108 ft and 100 ft.
