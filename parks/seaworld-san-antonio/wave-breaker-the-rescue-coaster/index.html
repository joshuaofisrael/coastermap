Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster

SeaWorld San Antonio · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 44 mph; 61 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at SeaWorld San Antonio in San Antonio, Texas, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 16 Jun 2017. The ride stands 61 ft tall, reaches 44 mph and runs 2,600 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster is like

With 2,600 ft of track for a 61 ft structure, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2017, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at SeaWorld San Antonio.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at SeaWorld San Antonio, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wave Breaker: The Rescue Coaster sits near the middle of SeaWorld San Antonio's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Barracuda Strike, roughly 120 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Great White at about 190 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.
