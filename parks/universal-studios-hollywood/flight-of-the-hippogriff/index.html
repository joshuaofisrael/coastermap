Flight of the Hippogriff

Universal Studios Hollywood · Universal City, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Flight of the Hippogriff is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Hollywood in Universal City, California, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 7 Apr 2016. The ride runs 984.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flight of the Hippogriff is like

Opened in 2016, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Universal Studios Hollywood.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Hollywood (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, roughly 410 m away (48 in minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Flight of the Hippogriff

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Flight of the Hippogriff?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Flight of the Hippogriff, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Universal Studios Hollywood, Guide for Rider Safety and Accessibility (Height Requirements Quick Reference Guide) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Flight of the Hippogriff go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flight of the Hippogriff scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Flight of the Hippogriff open?

Flight of the Hippogriff opened on 7 Apr 2016.

Who built Flight of the Hippogriff?

Flight of the Hippogriff was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.
