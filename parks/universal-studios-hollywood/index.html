Universal Studios Hollywood roller coasters

Universal City, California · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Hollywood in Universal City, California has 3 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride at 44.4 ft and the fastest is Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift at 72 mph. The lowest height requirement is 48 in (122 cm), for Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Universal Studios Hollywood have?

Universal Studios Hollywood has 3 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Universal Studios Hollywood?

Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, at 44.4 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Universal Studios Hollywood?

Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift, with a top speed of 72 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Universal Studios Hollywood?

By height requirement, Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride has the lowest minimum at 48 in (122 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Universal Studios Hollywood coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, Flight of the Hippogriff. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Universal Studios Hollywood do not go upside down?

Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, Flight of the Hippogriff have no inversions, per RCDB.
