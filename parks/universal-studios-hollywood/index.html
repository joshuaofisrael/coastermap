Universal Studios Hollywood height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Universal City, California · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Hollywood in Universal City, California has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride to 51 in (130 cm) on Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Studios Hollywood are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Universal Studios Hollywood a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, which opened in 2004.

What can my child ride at Universal Studios Hollywood? By height

At Universal Studios Hollywood the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Flight of the Hippogriff (39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride at 48 in (122 cm).

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride and Flight of the Hippogriff. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift at 51 in (130 cm).

Best coasters at Universal Studios Hollywood for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Studios Hollywood, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Flight of the Hippogriff, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. The big one: Revenge of the Mummy: The Ride, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 44.4 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
