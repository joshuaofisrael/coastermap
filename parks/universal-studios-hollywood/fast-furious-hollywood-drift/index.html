Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift

Universal Studios Hollywood · Universal City, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 51 in (130 cm); top speed 72 mph; 4 inversions; opened 2026.

Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Hollywood in Universal City, California, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 16 Sep 2026. The ride reaches 72 mph and runs 4,100 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift?

Riders must be at least 51 in (130 cm) tall to ride Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift, according to Universal Studios Hollywood, Guide for Rider Safety and Accessibility (Height Requirements Quick Reference Guide) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift go?

Its top speed is 72 mph (116 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

When did Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift open?

Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift opened on 16 Sep 2026.

Who built Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift?

Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
