Furuvik height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Furuvik, Sweden · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Furuvik in Furuvik, Sweden has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Fireball. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Furuvik are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Furuvik a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 43% of coasters across all 159 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Lightning).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Fireball, which opened in 2017.

Furuvik publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
