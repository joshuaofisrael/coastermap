Twin Spin

Enchanted Kingdom · Santa Rosa, Laguna, Philippines

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 36.7 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Twin Spin is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa, Laguna, Philippines, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 5 Jun 2021. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, reaches 36.7 mph and runs 1259.8 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twin Spin is like

At 55.8 ft tall with 1,259.8 ft of track, Twin Spin balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Enchanted Kingdom.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Enchanted Kingdom (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twin Spin sits on the south-east side of Enchanted Kingdom's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Roller Skater, roughly 220 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Space Shuttle at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Twin Spin

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Guests 39 to 48 inches must be accompanied by a supervising companion.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Enchanted Kingdom app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twin Spin?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Twin Spin, or 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) with a companion. Guests 39 to 48 inches must be accompanied by a supervising companion, according to Enchanted Kingdom, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twin Spin?

Twin Spin is 55.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Twin Spin go?

Its top speed is 36.7 mph (59 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Twin Spin go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twin Spin scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Twin Spin open?

Twin Spin opened on 5 Jun 2021.

Who built Twin Spin?

Twin Spin was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Twin Spin?

RCDB lists 1259.8 ft of track.

Is Twin Spin good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 36.7 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
