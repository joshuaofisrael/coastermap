Enchanted Kingdom height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Santa Rosa, Laguna, Philippines · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa, Laguna, Philippines has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Space Shuttle to 52 in (132 cm) on Roller Skater. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Enchanted Kingdom are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Enchanted Kingdom a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Space Shuttle, which opened in 1995.

What can my child ride at Enchanted Kingdom? By height

At Enchanted Kingdom the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Roller Skater (36 to 52 in (91 to 132 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Space Shuttle at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Roller Skater (36 to 52 in (91 to 132 cm)) and Twin Spin (39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Space Shuttle at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Space Shuttle and Twin Spin. With a companion they can also ride Roller Skater (36 to 52 in (91 to 132 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Roller Skater. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Enchanted Kingdom

These are the rules Enchanted Kingdom states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Roller Skater: riders 36 to 52 in (91 to 132 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Twin Spin: riders 39 to 48 in (99 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Enchanted Kingdom for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Enchanted Kingdom, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Roller Skater, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (52 in minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.7 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Twin Spin, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 55.8 ft tall, 36.7 mph, no inversions, 3.3 g).
  3. The big one: Space Shuttle, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
