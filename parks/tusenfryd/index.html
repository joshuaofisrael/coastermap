TusenFryd height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Vinterbro, Norway · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

TusenFryd in Vinterbro, Norway has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Loopen to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Speed Monster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at TusenFryd are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes TusenFryd a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 57% of its lineup, against 42% across all 189 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Thundercoaster); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Speed Monster).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Loopen, which opened in 1988.

TusenFryd publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at TusenFryd? By height

At TusenFryd the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at TusenFryd for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for TusenFryd, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: HuriHuri, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; 18 ft tall, 15.5 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: SuperSplash, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 95.2 ft tall, 46.6 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Speed Monster, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 101.8 ft tall, 55.9 mph, 3 inversions, 4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
