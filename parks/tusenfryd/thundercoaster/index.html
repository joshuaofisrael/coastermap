Thundercoaster

TusenFryd · Vinterbro, Norway

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 57.8 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Thundercoaster is a wood sit-down roller coaster at TusenFryd in Vinterbro, Norway, built by Vekoma. It opened on 1 May 2001. The ride drops 105 ft, reaches 57.8 mph and runs 3116.8 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Thundercoaster is like

Opened in 2001, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at TusenFryd. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Wooden design, and the first of them to open. Others include Loup-Garou at Walibi Belgium. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at TusenFryd, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thundercoaster sits near the middle of TusenFryd's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is HuriHuri, roughly 100 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Loopen at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Thundercoaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Thundercoaster, according to TusenFryd, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Thundercoaster go?

Its top speed is 57.8 mph (93 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thundercoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Thundercoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Thundercoaster open?

Thundercoaster opened on 1 May 2001.

Who built Thundercoaster?

Thundercoaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Thundercoaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 3116.8 ft of track.

How does Thundercoaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Thundercoaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Thundercoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 57.8 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Thundercoaster at TusenFryd the same as Loup-Garou at Walibi Belgium?

Both are Vekoma Wooden coasters on RCDB; the TusenFryd ride opened in 2001 and the Walibi Belgium ride in 2001; both list a 120 cm minimum.
