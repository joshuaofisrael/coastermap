SuperSplash

TusenFryd · Vinterbro, Norway

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 46.6 mph; 95.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

SuperSplash is a steel sit-down roller coaster at TusenFryd in Vinterbro, Norway, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened in 2003. The ride stands 95.2 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 1049.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding SuperSplash is like

At 95.2 ft tall with 1,049.8 ft of track, SuperSplash balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2003, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at TusenFryd.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at TusenFryd, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, SuperSplash sits on the south-east side of TusenFryd's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Western-Expressen, roughly 260 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Thundercoaster at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How SuperSplash compares

On our intensity ranking SuperSplash is number 5 of 7 at TusenFryd, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for SuperSplash?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride SuperSplash, according to TusenFryd, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is SuperSplash?

SuperSplash is 95.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does SuperSplash go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does SuperSplash go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is SuperSplash scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did SuperSplash open?

SuperSplash opened in 2003.

Who built SuperSplash?

SuperSplash was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on SuperSplash?

RCDB lists 1049.8 ft of track.

How does SuperSplash get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride SuperSplash at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is SuperSplash good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
