Western-Expressen

TusenFryd · Vinterbro, Norway

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 95 to 110 cm (about 37.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 28.5 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

Western-Expressen is a steel sit-down roller coaster at TusenFryd in Vinterbro, Norway, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 28 Apr 2012 and was previously known as Steampunk Hunters. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 28.5 mph and runs 1099.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Western-Expressen is like

Western-Expressen pairs a 42.7 ft structure with 1,099.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2012, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at TusenFryd. It has run under other names (Steampunk Hunters), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 335m design, and the 13th of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Cedar Point, Flight of the Hippogriff at Islands of Adventure, Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Japan and The Dragon at Legoland Florida. Height rules for this model differ by park: TusenFryd sets 110 cm, while Cedar Point sets 48 in, Islands of Adventure sets 36 in and Universal Studios Japan sets 122 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at TusenFryd (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 110 cm (about 37.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Western-Expressen sits on the south-west side of TusenFryd's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Storm: The Dragon Legend, roughly 200 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Thundercoaster at about 240 m. Western-Expressen layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 95 to 110 cm (about 37.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

How Western-Expressen compares

On our intensity ranking Western-Expressen is number 6 of 7 at TusenFryd, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Western-Expressen?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Western-Expressen, or 95 to 110 cm (about 37.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion, according to TusenFryd, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Western-Expressen?

Western-Expressen is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Western-Expressen go?

Its top speed is 28.5 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Western-Expressen go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Western-Expressen scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Western-Expressen open?

Western-Expressen opened on 28 Apr 2012 (previously known as Steampunk Hunters).

Who built Western-Expressen?

Western-Expressen was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Western-Expressen?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 1099.1 ft of track.

How does Western-Expressen get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Western-Expressen at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Western-Expressen good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.5 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Western-Expressen operate before?

RCDB records it at Mirabilandia (as Family Adventure).

Is Western-Expressen at TusenFryd the same as K3 Roller Skater at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 335m coasters on RCDB; the TusenFryd ride opened in 2012 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1991; TusenFryd sets 110 cm and Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm.
