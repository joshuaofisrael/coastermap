Speed Monster

TusenFryd · Vinterbro, Norway

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 101.8 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2006.

Speed Monster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at TusenFryd in Vinterbro, Norway, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 23 Apr 2006. The ride stands 101.8 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 2263.8 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Speed Monster is like

At 101.8 ft tall with 2,263.8 ft of track, Speed Monster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,263.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2006, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at TusenFryd. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Xcelerator at Knott's and Rita at Alton Towers. Height rules for this model differ by park: TusenFryd sets 140 cm, while Cedar Point sets 52 in, Hersheypark sets 54 in and Knott's sets 52 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at TusenFryd. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Speed Monster sits on the north-east side of TusenFryd's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Loopen, roughly 120 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is HuriHuri at about 160 m. Speed Monster layout, trains and ride system

Speed Monster starts with a hydraulic launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 3 elements RCDB records on Speed Monster's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Hydraulic Launch (from 0 mph - 52.8 mph in 2.2 s)
  2. Norwegian Loop
  3. Corkscrew

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Speed Monster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the TusenFryd app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Speed Monster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Speed Monster, according to TusenFryd, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Speed Monster?

Speed Monster is 101.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Speed Monster go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Speed Monster go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Speed Monster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Speed Monster open?

Speed Monster opened on 23 Apr 2006.

Who built Speed Monster?

Speed Monster was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Speed Monster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 9 seconds over 2263.8 ft of track.

Does Speed Monster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Speed Monster at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 700 riders.

Is Speed Monster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Speed Monster at TusenFryd the same as Xcelerator at Knott's?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the TusenFryd ride opened in 2006 and the Knott's ride in 2002; TusenFryd sets 140 cm and Knott's sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 101.8 ft and 205 ft.
