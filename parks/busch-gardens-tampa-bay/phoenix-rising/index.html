Phoenix Rising

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 44 mph; 80 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Phoenix Rising is a steel inverted roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 21 Jul 2024. The ride stands 80 ft tall, reaches 44 mph and runs 1,831 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Phoenix Rising is like

At 80 ft tall with 1,831 ft of track, Phoenix Rising balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa, out of 7 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Phoenix Rising sits on the north side of Busch Gardens Tampa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tigris, roughly 290 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SheiKra at about 340 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Phoenix Rising?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Phoenix Rising, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Phoenix Rising?

Phoenix Rising is 80 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Phoenix Rising go?

Its top speed is 44 mph (71 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Phoenix Rising go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Phoenix Rising scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Phoenix Rising open?

Phoenix Rising opened on 21 Jul 2024.

Who built Phoenix Rising?

Phoenix Rising was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Phoenix Rising?

RCDB lists 1,831 ft (558 m) of track.

How does Phoenix Rising get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Phoenix Rising good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 44 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
