Busch Gardens Tampa Bay roller coasters

Tampa, Florida · 8 coasters

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Iron Gwazi at 206 ft, which is also the fastest at 76 mph. The lowest height requirement is 38 in (97 cm), for Air Grover.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Busch Gardens Tampa Bay have?

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay?

Iron Gwazi, at 206 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay?

Iron Gwazi, with a top speed of 76 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay?

By height requirement, Air Grover has the lowest minimum at 38 in (97 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Busch Gardens Tampa Bay coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Air Grover, Phoenix Rising. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Busch Gardens Tampa Bay coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Air Grover, Cheetah Hunt, Cobra's Curse, Iron Gwazi, Phoenix Rising. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay do not go upside down?

Air Grover, Cobra's Curse, Phoenix Rising have no inversions, per RCDB.
