Busch Gardens Tampa Bay height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Tampa, Florida · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 38 in (97 cm) on Air Grover to 54 in (137 cm) on Montu. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Busch Gardens Tampa Bay a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 62% of its lineup, against 45% across all 131 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 8 go upside down; 2 use a launch instead of a lift hill (Cheetah Hunt and Tigris).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Montu, which opened in 1996.

What can my child ride at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay? By height

At Busch Gardens Tampa Bay the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Air Grover at 38 in (97 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: Air Grover and Phoenix Rising. With a companion they can also ride Cobra's Curse (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Cheetah Hunt at 48 in (122 cm).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Montu, SheiKra and Tigris. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Air Grover, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (38 in minimum; 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Cobra's Curse, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 70 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Iron Gwazi, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 206 ft tall, 76 mph, 2 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
