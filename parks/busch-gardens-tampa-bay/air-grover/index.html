Air Grover

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 38 in (97 cm); top speed 24.9 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Air Grover is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Zierer. It opened on 27 Mar 2010. The ride reaches 24.9 mph and runs 629.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Air Grover is like

Opened in 2010, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Force / 190 design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Grover's Alpine Express at Busch Gardens Williamsburg and Fun Pilot at Walibi Belgium. Height rules for this model differ by park: Busch Gardens Tampa sets 38 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 41 in and Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm.

Its 38 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Air Grover sits on the west side of Busch Gardens Tampa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Iron Gwazi, roughly 190 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SheiKra at about 230 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Air Grover?

Riders must be at least 38 in (97 cm) tall to ride Air Grover, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Air Grover go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Air Grover go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Air Grover scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Air Grover open?

Air Grover opened on 27 Mar 2010.

Who built Air Grover?

Air Grover was manufactured by Zierer.

Is Air Grover at Busch Gardens Tampa the same as Grover's Alpine Express at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Zierer Force / 190 coasters on RCDB; the Busch Gardens Tampa ride opened in 2010 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 2009; Busch Gardens Tampa sets 38 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 41 in.
