SheiKra

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 70 mph; 200 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2005.

SheiKra is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 21 May 2005. The ride stands 200 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 3,188 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding SheiKra is like

At 200 ft tall with 3,188 ft of track, SheiKra balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 1 time across 3,188 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2005, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Busch Gardens Tampa, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, SheiKra sits on the west side of Busch Gardens Tampa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tigris, roughly 130 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Air Grover at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

How SheiKra compares

On our intensity ranking SheiKra is number 4 of 8 at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Cheetah Hunt (102 ft tall, 60 mph, 1 inversion, 4 g; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Tigris (150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion; 54 in minimum).

Before you ride SheiKra

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Busch Gardens Tampa Bay app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for SheiKra?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride SheiKra, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is SheiKra?

SheiKra is 200 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does SheiKra go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does SheiKra go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is SheiKra scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did SheiKra open?

SheiKra opened on 21 May 2005.

Who built SheiKra?

SheiKra was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on SheiKra?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 3,188 ft of track.

How does SheiKra get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride SheiKra at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is SheiKra good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
