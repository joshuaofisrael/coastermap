Cheetah Hunt

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 60 mph; 102 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2011.

Cheetah Hunt is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 27 May 2011. The ride stands 102 ft tall, drops 130 ft, reaches 60 mph and runs 4,429 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Busch Gardens Tampa Bay app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cheetah Hunt?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Cheetah Hunt, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cheetah Hunt?

Cheetah Hunt is 102 ft tall, with a drop of 130 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Cheetah Hunt go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Cheetah Hunt go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Cheetah Hunt scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Cheetah Hunt open?

Cheetah Hunt opened on 27 May 2011.

Who built Cheetah Hunt?

Cheetah Hunt was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
