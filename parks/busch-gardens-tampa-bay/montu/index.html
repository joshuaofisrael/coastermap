Montu

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 60 mph; 150 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 1996.

Montu is a steel inverted roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 16 May 1996. The ride stands 150 ft tall, drops 128 ft, reaches 60 mph and runs 3,983 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Montu?

Riders must be at least 54 inches (137 cm) tall to ride Montu, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Montu?

Montu is 150 ft tall, with a drop of 128 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Montu go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Montu go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Montu scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Montu open?

Montu opened on 16 May 1996.

Who built Montu?

Montu was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
