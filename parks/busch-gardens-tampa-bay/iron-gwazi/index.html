Iron Gwazi

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 76 mph; 206 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2022.

Iron Gwazi is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 11 Mar 2022. The ride stands 206 ft tall, drops 206 ft, reaches 76 mph and runs 4,075 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 91°, past vertical.

What riding Iron Gwazi is like

Iron Gwazi pairs a 206 ft structure with 4,075 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. The steepest drop is 91°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 2 times across 4,075 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2022, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Busch Gardens Tampa, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Iron Gwazi sits on the south-west side of Busch Gardens Tampa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Air Grover, roughly 190 m away (38 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cheetah Hunt at about 260 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Iron Gwazi

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Busch Gardens Tampa Bay app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Gwazi?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Iron Gwazi, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Gwazi?

Iron Gwazi is 206 ft tall, with a drop of 206 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Gwazi go?

Its top speed is 76 mph (122 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Gwazi go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Iron Gwazi scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Iron Gwazi open?

Iron Gwazi opened on 11 Mar 2022.

Who built Iron Gwazi?

Iron Gwazi was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the ride on Iron Gwazi?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds over 4,075 ft of track.

How does Iron Gwazi get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Iron Gwazi at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Iron Gwazi good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 76 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
