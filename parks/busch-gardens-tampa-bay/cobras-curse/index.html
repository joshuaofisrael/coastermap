Cobra's Curse

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Tampa, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 40 mph; 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Cobra's Curse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay in Tampa, Florida, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 17 Jun 2016. The ride stands 70 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 2,100 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cobra's Curse is like

With 2,100 ft of track for a 70 ft structure, Cobra's Curse is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2016, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Busch Gardens Tampa, out of 7 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cobra's Curse sits on the east side of Busch Gardens Tampa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Montu, roughly 110 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cheetah Hunt at about 230 m. Cobra's Curse layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses an elevator lift and a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Cobra's Curse

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Busch Gardens Tampa Bay app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cobra's Curse?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Cobra's Curse, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cobra's Curse?

Cobra's Curse is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Cobra's Curse go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Cobra's Curse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cobra's Curse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cobra's Curse open?

Cobra's Curse opened on 17 Jun 2016.

Who built Cobra's Curse?

Cobra's Curse was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Cobra's Curse?

RCDB lists 2,100 ft (640 m) of track.

How does Cobra's Curse get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift and a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Cobra's Curse at once?

Up to 8 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Cobra's Curse good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
