Kettle Creek Mine

Playland (Vancouver) · Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Kettle Creek Mine is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Playland (Vancouver) in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, built by E&F Miler Industries. It opened in 2004. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kettle Creek Mine is like

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Playland (Vancouver). It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to E&F Miler Industries's Family Coaster / 16ft Outside Spiral CCW design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Great Chase at Six Flags New England, Blazin' Buckaroo at Elitch Gardens and Diamond Mine Run at Magic Springs. Height rules for this model differ by park: Playland sets 36 in, while Elitch Gardens sets 42 in and Magic Springs sets 42 in.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Playland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kettle Creek Mine sits on the south-west side of Playland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Bug Whirled, roughly 110 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Coaster at about 120 m. Before you ride Kettle Creek Mine

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kettle Creek Mine?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Kettle Creek Mine. The park lists it as Kettle Creek Mine Coaster: 36 inches and taller. It does not state a companion rule, according to Playland (PNE), official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Kettle Creek Mine go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kettle Creek Mine scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Kettle Creek Mine open?

Kettle Creek Mine opened in 2004.

Who built Kettle Creek Mine?

Kettle Creek Mine was manufactured by E&F Miler Industries.

How does Kettle Creek Mine get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kettle Creek Mine at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Kettle Creek Mine good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Kettle Creek Mine at Playland the same as Great Chase at Six Flags New England?

Both are E&F Miler Industries Family Coaster / 16ft Outside Spiral CCW coasters on RCDB; the Playland ride opened in 2004 and the Six Flags New England ride in 1996; both list a 36 in minimum.
