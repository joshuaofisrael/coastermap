Playland (Vancouver) height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Playland (Vancouver) in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Kettle Creek Mine to 52 in (132 cm) on ThunderVolt. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Playland (Vancouver) are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Playland (Vancouver) a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Coaster); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (ThunderVolt).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Coaster, which opened in 1958.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Coaster: The park calls it the Wooden Roller Coaster.
  • Kettle Creek Mine: The park lists it as Kettle Creek Mine Coaster: 36 inches and taller. It does not state a companion rule.
  • Bug Whirled: Minimum 36 inches; riders from 36 to 48 inches must be accompanied by an adult. Minimum age 4.

What can my child ride at Playland (Vancouver)? By height

At Playland (Vancouver) the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Kettle Creek Mine. With a companion they can also ride Bug Whirled (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Coaster at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Kettle Creek Mine. With a companion they can also ride Bug Whirled (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Coaster at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Coaster and Bug Whirled. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being ThunderVolt at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: ThunderVolt. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

This family or kiddie coaster has an upper limit: Bug Whirled (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Playland (Vancouver)

These are the rules Playland (Vancouver) states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Coaster: The park calls it the Wooden Roller Coaster.
  • Kettle Creek Mine: The park lists it as Kettle Creek Mine Coaster: 36 inches and taller. It does not state a companion rule.

Best coasters at Playland (Vancouver) for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Playland (Vancouver), picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Bug Whirled, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 68 ft tall, 47 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: ThunderVolt, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 59.1 ft tall, no inversions, 1.3 g).
