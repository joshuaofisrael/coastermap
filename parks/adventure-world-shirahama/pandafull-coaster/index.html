Pandafull Coaster

Adventure World · Shirahama, Wakayama, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

Pandafull Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventure World in Shirahama, Wakayama, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It first opened in 1986 and was previously known as Mad Mouse. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pandafull Coaster is like

Opened in 1986, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Adventure World. It has run under other names (Mad Mouse), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Adventure World (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pandafull Coaster sits near the middle of Adventure World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Little Adventure, roughly 30 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Big Adventure Coaster at about 130 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Pandafull Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pandafull Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Pandafull Coaster, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Riders from 90 to 110 cm need a companion aged 16 or older (or of high-school age), according to Adventure World, official Play Zone page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Pandafull Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

When did Pandafull Coaster open?

Pandafull Coaster opened in 1986 (previously known as Mad Mouse).

Who built Pandafull Coaster?

Pandafull Coaster was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the ride on Pandafull Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds.

How does Pandafull Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pandafull Coaster at once?

Up to 2 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.
