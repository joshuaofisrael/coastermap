Big Adventure Coaster

Adventure World · Shirahama, Wakayama, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in), or 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1990.

Big Adventure Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventure World in Shirahama, Wakayama, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It opened in 1990. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Adventure Coaster is like

Opened in 1990, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Adventure World.

Its 125 cm minimum is the strictest at Adventure World. Children from 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Adventure Coaster sits on the south-west side of Adventure World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pandafull Coaster, roughly 130 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Little Adventure at about 140 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall, or 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Big Adventure Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in), and riders from 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders from 105 to 125 cm need a companion aged 16 or older (or of high-school age) who meets the ride rules.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Adventure Coaster?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Big Adventure Coaster, or 105 to 125 cm (about 41.3 to 49.2 in) with a companion. Riders from 105 to 125 cm need a companion aged 16 or older (or of high-school age) who meets the ride rules, according to Adventure World, official Play Zone page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Big Adventure Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

When did Big Adventure Coaster open?

Big Adventure Coaster opened in 1990.

Who built Big Adventure Coaster?

Big Adventure Coaster was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the ride on Big Adventure Coaster?
