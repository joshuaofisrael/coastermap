Wipeout

Palace Playland · Old Orchard Beach, Maine

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm), or 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Wipeout is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Palace Playland in Old Orchard Beach, Maine, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 19 Jul 2018. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wipeout is like

Opened in 2018, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Palace Playland. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608/E Higher Model A design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Wild Whizzer at Morey's Piers, Ladybird Loop at Emerald Park, Whirlwind at Castaway Cove and Spinning Coaster at VinWonders Nam Hoi An. Height rules for this model differ by park: Palace Playland sets 46 in, while Morey's Piers sets 48 in, Castaway Cove sets 48 in and VinWonders Nam Hoi An sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 46 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Palace Playland, out of 2 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

The nearest other coaster is Sea Viper, roughly 40 m away (51 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Wipeout layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall, or 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Wipeout

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 46 in (117 cm), and riders from 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders from 36 inches may ride with a paying adult.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Palace Playland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wipeout?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Wipeout, or 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) with a companion. Riders from 36 inches may ride with a paying adult, according to Palace Playland, official rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Wipeout go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wipeout scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wipeout open?

Wipeout opened on 19 Jul 2018.

Who built Wipeout?

Wipeout was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Wipeout get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wipeout at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wipeout good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 46 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Wipeout at Palace Playland the same as Whirlwind at Castaway Cove?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608/E Higher Model A coasters on RCDB; the Palace Playland ride opened in 2018 and the Castaway Cove ride in 2016; Palace Playland sets 46 in and Castaway Cove sets 48 in.
