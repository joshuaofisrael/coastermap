Palace Playland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Old Orchard Beach, Maine · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Palace Playland in Old Orchard Beach, Maine has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Gold Rush to 51 in (130 cm) on Sea Viper. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Palace Playland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Palace Playland a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 46 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sea Viper, which opened in 2018.

What can my child ride at Palace Playland? By height

At Palace Playland the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Wipeout (36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Gold Rush at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Gold Rush. With a companion they can also ride Wipeout (36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Sea Viper at 51 in (130 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Wipeout. With a companion they can also ride Sea Viper (48 to 51 in (122 to 130 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Sea Viper. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Gold Rush (up to 79 in (201 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Palace Playland

These are the rules Palace Playland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Sea Viper: riders 48 to 51 in (122 to 130 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wipeout: riders 36 to 46 in (91 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Palace Playland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Palace Playland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wipeout, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (46 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Sea Viper, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (51 in minimum; 68.8 ft tall, 41.1 mph, no inversions).
