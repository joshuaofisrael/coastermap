Six Flags Fiesta Texas roller coasters

San Antonio, Texas · 11 coasters

Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas has 11 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Iron Rattler at 179 ft, which is also the fastest at 70 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Batgirl Coaster Chase.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Fiesta Texas have?

Six Flags Fiesta Texas has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Iron Rattler, at 179 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Iron Rattler, with a top speed of 70 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

By height requirement, Batgirl Coaster Chase has the lowest minimum at 36 inches (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Fiesta Texas coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Batgirl Coaster Chase. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Fiesta Texas coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Iron Rattler, Batman The Ride, Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster, Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger, Batgirl Coaster Chase, Road Runner Express, Boomerang Coast to Coaster, Pandemonium. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Fiesta Texas do not go upside down?

Batman The Ride, Batgirl Coaster Chase, Road Runner Express, Pandemonium have no inversions, per RCDB.
