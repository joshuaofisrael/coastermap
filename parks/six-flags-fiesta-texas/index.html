Six Flags Fiesta Texas height requirements and roller coasters ranked

San Antonio, Texas · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Batgirl Coaster Chase to 54 in (137 cm) on Poltergeist. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 8 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 11 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Fiesta Texas are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 8 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Fiesta Texas a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 73% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 9 of the 11 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 7 of the 11 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Poltergeist).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Batgirl Coaster Chase, which opened in 1992.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas? By height

At Six Flags Fiesta Texas the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 7 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 11 coasters with a verified rule: Batgirl Coaster Chase. Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being Pandemonium at 47 in (119 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 11 coasters with a verified rule: Batgirl Coaster Chase. With a companion they can also ride Pandemonium (42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm)). Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Iron Rattler at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 7 more than at the height below: Iron Rattler, Batman The Ride, Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster, Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger, Road Runner Express, Boomerang Coast to Coaster and Pandemonium. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Poltergeist at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 11 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Poltergeist, Superman Krypton Coaster and Chupacabra. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Batman The Ride (up to 77 in (196 cm)), Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Boomerang Coast to Coaster (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Six Flags Fiesta Texas for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Fiesta Texas, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Batgirl Coaster Chase, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Pandemonium, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (47 in minimum; 48 ft tall, 31 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Superman Krypton Coaster, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 168 ft tall, 70 mph, 6 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
