Poltergeist

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 60 mph; 78.8 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1999.

Poltergeist is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 28 May 1999. The ride stands 78.8 ft tall, reaches 60 mph and runs 2,705 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Poltergeist is like

With 2,705 ft of track for a 78.8 ft structure, Poltergeist is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,705 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 4th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's Catapult Launch Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Flight of Fear at Kings Island and Flight of Fear at Kings Dominion. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Fiesta Texas, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Poltergeist sits on the east side of Six Flags Fiesta Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman The Ride, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batgirl Coaster Chase at about 180 m. Poltergeist layout, trains and ride system

Poltergeist starts with a linear induction motor (LIM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Poltergeist's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LIM Launch
  2. Cobra Roll
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 4 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Poltergeist compares

On our intensity ranking Poltergeist is number 2 of 11 at Six Flags Fiesta Texas, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 8th tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger (150 ft tall, 60 mph, 2 inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Superman Krypton Coaster (168 ft tall, 70 mph, 6 inversions; 54 in minimum).

Site-wide, Poltergeist is taller than 47% and faster than 78% of the 917 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Poltergeist

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Fiesta Texas app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Poltergeist?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Poltergeist, according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Poltergeist?

Poltergeist is 78.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Poltergeist go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Poltergeist go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Poltergeist scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Poltergeist open?

Poltergeist opened on 28 May 1999.

Who built Poltergeist?

Poltergeist was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Poltergeist?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 2,705 ft of track.

Does Poltergeist launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

How many people ride Poltergeist at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Poltergeist use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar.

Is Poltergeist good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 60 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Poltergeist at Six Flags Fiesta Texas the same as Flight of Fear at Kings Dominion?

Both are Premier Rides Catapult Launch Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride opened in 1999 and the Kings Dominion ride in 1996; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 78.8 ft and 74.2 ft.
