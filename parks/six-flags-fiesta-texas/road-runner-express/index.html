Road Runner Express

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 73 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1997.

Road Runner Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on May 1997. The ride stands 73 ft tall and runs 2,400 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Road Runner Express is like

With 2,400 ft of track for a 73 ft structure, Road Runner Express is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1997, it is the 2nd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 12th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Adventure Express at Kings Island and Trailblazer at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Fiesta Texas sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 44 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Six Flags Fiesta Texas, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Road Runner Express sits on the north-west side of Six Flags Fiesta Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Iron Rattler, roughly 130 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Road Runner Express?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Road Runner Express, according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Road Runner Express?

Road Runner Express is 73 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Road Runner Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Road Runner Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Road Runner Express open?

Road Runner Express opened on May 1997.

Who built Road Runner Express?

Road Runner Express was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Road Runner Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 24 seconds over 2,400 ft of track.

How does Road Runner Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Road Runner Express at once?

RCDB's train details give 36 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,800 riders.

Is Road Runner Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Road Runner Express at Six Flags Fiesta Texas the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride opened in 1997 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 73 ft and 35 ft.
