Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52 mph; 113 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2018.

Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 12 May 2018. The ride stands 113 ft tall, drops 100 ft, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,800 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster is like

Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster pairs a 113 ft structure with 1,800 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,800 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the 10th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Rocky Mountain Coasters's Raptor Track / Prototype design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include RailBlazer at California's Great America. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Six Flags Fiesta Texas, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster sits on the south-east side of Six Flags Fiesta Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pandemonium, roughly 130 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Superman Krypton Coaster at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster, according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster?

Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster is 113 ft tall, with a drop of 100 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster open?

Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster opened on 12 May 2018.

Who built Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster?

Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster?

RCDB lists 1,800 ft (549 m) of track.

How does Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas the same as RailBlazer at California's Great America?

Both are Rocky Mountain Coasters Raptor Track / Prototype coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride opened in 2018 and the California's Great America ride in 2018; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 113 ft and 106 ft.
