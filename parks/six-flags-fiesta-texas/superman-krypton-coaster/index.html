Superman Krypton Coaster

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 70 mph; 168 ft tall; 6 inversions; opened 2000.

Superman Krypton Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 11 Mar 2000. The ride stands 168 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 4,025 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 6 times.

What riding Superman Krypton Coaster is like

At 168 ft tall with 4,025 ft of track, Superman Krypton Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 6 times across 4,025 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2000, it is the 5th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Six Flags Fiesta Texas.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Fiesta Texas, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Superman Krypton Coaster sits on the south side of Six Flags Fiesta Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batgirl Coaster Chase at about 150 m. Superman Krypton Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (6 entries):

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Loop
  3. Zero-G Roll
  4. Cobra Roll (78 ft tall)
  5. Block Brake
  6. Interlocking Corkscrews

RCDB counts 6 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 6 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Superman Krypton Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 6 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Fiesta Texas app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Superman Krypton Coaster?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Superman Krypton Coaster, according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Superman Krypton Coaster?

Superman Krypton Coaster is 168 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Superman Krypton Coaster go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Superman Krypton Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 6 inversions.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Superman Krypton Coaster open?

Superman Krypton Coaster opened on 11 Mar 2000.

Who built Superman Krypton Coaster?

Superman Krypton Coaster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Superman Krypton Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 35 seconds over 4,025 ft of track.

How does Superman Krypton Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Superman Krypton Coaster at once?

Up to 32 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 6 times; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas the same as Superman Krypton Coaster at Six Flags Mexico?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride opened in 2000 and the Six Flags Mexico ride in 1993; RCDB lists heights of 168 ft and 27.9 ft.
