Iron Rattler

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 70 mph; 179 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2013.

Iron Rattler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 25 May 2013. The ride stands 179 ft tall, drops 171 ft, reaches 70 mph and runs 3,266 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Rattler?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Iron Rattler, according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Rattler?

Iron Rattler is 179 ft tall, with a drop of 171 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Rattler go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Rattler go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Iron Rattler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Iron Rattler open?

Iron Rattler opened on 25 May 2013.

Who built Iron Rattler?

Iron Rattler was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.
