Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger

Six Flags Fiesta Texas · San Antonio, Texas

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 60 mph; 150 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2022.

Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 30 Jul 2022. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 60 mph and runs 2,501 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 95°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Six Flags Fiesta Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger?

Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger go?

Its top speed is 60 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Fiesta Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger open?

Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger opened on 30 Jul 2022.

Who built Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger?

Dr. Diabolical's Cliffhanger was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
