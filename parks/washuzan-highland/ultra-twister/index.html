Ultra Twister

Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland · Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 3 inversions; opened 1991.

Ultra Twister is a steel roller coaster at Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland in Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan, built by Togo. It opened on 19 Jul 1991. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Ultra Twister is like

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Washuzan Highland, out of 2 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ultra Twister sits on the west side of Washuzan Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster, roughly 190 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Chupy Coaster at about 290 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Ultra Twister

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ultra Twister?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Ultra Twister, according to Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Ultra Twister go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Ultra Twister scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ultra Twister open?

Ultra Twister opened on 19 Jul 1991.

Who built Ultra Twister?

Ultra Twister was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the ride on Ultra Twister?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds.

How does Ultra Twister get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ultra Twister at once?

Each train carries 6 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Ultra Twister good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
