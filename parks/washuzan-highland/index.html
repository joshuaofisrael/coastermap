Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland in Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Chupy Coaster to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Togo, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster, which opened in 1986.

Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
