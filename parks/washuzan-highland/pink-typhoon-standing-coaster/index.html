Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster

Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland · Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 95.1 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1986.

Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster is a steel stand-up roller coaster at Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland in Kurashiki, Okayama, Japan, built by Togo. It first opened on 9 May 1986 and was previously known as Star Jet. The ride stands 95.1 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2132.5 ft of track. On a stand-up coaster riders travel standing, supported by a bicycle-style seat and a shoulder harness. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster is like

At 95.1 ft tall with 2,132.5 ft of track, Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 1 time across 2,132.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

It has run under other names (Star Jet), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Togo's Standing Coaster / Astro Comet design, and the first of them to open. Others include Freestyle at Cavallino Matto. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 140 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Washuzan Highland. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster sits near the middle of Washuzan Highland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Chupy Coaster, roughly 110 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ultra Twister at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster. The park lists it as the Standing Coaster, according to Brazilian Park Washuzan Highland, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster?

Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster is 95.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster open?

Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster opened on 9 May 1986 (previously known as Star Jet).

Who built Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster?

Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the track on Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster?

RCDB lists 2132.5 ft of track.

How does Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at International Science and Technology Expo (as Star Jet).

Is Pink Typhoon Standing Coaster at Washuzan Highland the same as Freestyle at Cavallino Matto?

Both are Togo Standing Coaster / Astro Comet coasters on RCDB; the Washuzan Highland ride opened in 1986 and the Cavallino Matto ride in 2015; both list a 140 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 95.1 ft and 88 ft.
