Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets

Disney's Hollywood Studios · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57 mph; 80 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1999.

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disney's Hollywood Studios in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 29 Jul 1999 and was previously known as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster. The ride stands 80 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 3267.7 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets is like

With 3,267.7 ft of track for a 80 ft structure, Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,267.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

It has run under other names (Rock 'n' Roller Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's LSM Coaster / 1,037m (Disney) design, and the first of them to open. Others include Avengers Assemble: Flight Force at Disney Adventure World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Hollywood Studios sets 48 in, while Disney Adventure World sets 120 cm.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Slinky Dog Dash, roughly 390 m away (38 in minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets layout, trains and ride system

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 57 mph in 2.8 s)
  2. Sea Serpent
  3. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Before you ride Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Disney's Hollywood Studios app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets, according to Walt Disney World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets?

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets is 80 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets open?

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets opened on 29 Jul 1999 (previously known as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster).

Who built Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets?

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 22 seconds over 3267.7 ft of track.

Does Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,800 riders.

Is Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 57 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets at Hollywood Studios the same as Avengers Assemble: Flight Force at Disney Adventure World?

Both are Vekoma LSM Coaster / 1,037m (Disney) coasters on RCDB; the Hollywood Studios ride opened in 1999 and the Disney Adventure World ride in 2002; Hollywood Studios sets 48 in and Disney Adventure World sets 120 cm.
