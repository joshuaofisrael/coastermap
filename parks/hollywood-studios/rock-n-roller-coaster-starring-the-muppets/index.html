Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets

Disney's Hollywood Studios · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 57 mph; 80 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1999.

Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disney's Hollywood Studios in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 29 Jul 1999 and was previously known as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster. The ride stands 80 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 3267.7 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.
