Disney's Hollywood Studios roller coasters

Lake Buena Vista, Florida · 2 coasters

Disney's Hollywood Studios in Lake Buena Vista, Florida has 2 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets at 80 ft, which is also the fastest at 57 mph. The lowest height requirement is 38 in (97 cm), for Slinky Dog Dash.
