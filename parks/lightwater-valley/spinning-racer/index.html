Spinning Racer

Lightwater Valley · North Stainley, Ripon, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 50.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Spinning Racer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lightwater Valley in North Stainley, Ripon, England, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 3 Apr 2026. The ride stands 50.9 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1,378 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinning Racer is like

At 50.9 ft tall with 1,378 ft of track, Spinning Racer balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Lightwater Valley. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Laff Trakk at Hersheypark, Spider at Lagoon, Phoenix at Adventureland and Steel Dragon at Waldameer. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lightwater Valley sets 120 cm, while Hersheypark sets 42 in, Lagoon sets 50 in and Adventureland sets 48 in.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinning Racer?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Spinning Racer, according to Lightwater Valley, official rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spinning Racer?

Spinning Racer is 50.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spinning Racer go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spinning Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinning Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spinning Racer open?

Spinning Racer opened on 3 Apr 2026.

Who built Spinning Racer?

Spinning Racer was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Spinning Racer?

RCDB lists 1,378 ft (420 m) of track.

How does Spinning Racer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinning Racer at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Spinning Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Spinning Racer operate before?

RCDB records it at Fantasy Island (as Spinning Racer); South Sabahiya Park (as Spinning Racer).

Is Spinning Racer at Lightwater Valley the same as Spider at Lagoon?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 coasters on RCDB; the Lightwater Valley ride opened in 2026 and the Lagoon ride in 2003; Lightwater Valley sets 120 cm and Lagoon sets 50 in; RCDB lists heights of 50.9 ft and 50.8 ft.
