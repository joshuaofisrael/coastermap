Camden Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Huntington, West Virginia · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Camden Park in Huntington, West Virginia has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 48 in (122 cm) on Big Dipper. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Camden Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Camden Park a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Big Dipper and Lil' Dipper).

The most common builder is National Amusement Device Company, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Big Dipper, which opened in 1958.

What can my child ride at Camden Park? By height

At Camden Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
