Gold Rush

Kingoland · Plumelin, Brittany, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 24.6 mph; 30.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Gold Rush is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kingoland in Plumelin, Brittany, France, built by Pinfari. It opened on 20 Jun 2020. The ride stands 30.2 ft tall, reaches 24.6 mph and runs 764.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Rush is like

At 30.2 ft tall with 764.4 ft of track, Gold Rush balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2020, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Kingoland. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Pinfari's Mini Mega Coaster MM29 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mighty Mini Mega at Adventure Island. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kingoland sets 130 cm, while Adventure Island sets 120 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Kingoland (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Rush sits on the south-east side of Kingoland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speed Chenille, roughly 150 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Maranello Twist at about 150 m. Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Rush?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Gold Rush, or 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. From 0.95 m; children up to 1.30 m must be accompanied, according to Kingoland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gold Rush?

Gold Rush is 30.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gold Rush go?

Its top speed is 24.6 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gold Rush go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Rush scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Gold Rush open?

Gold Rush opened on 20 Jun 2020.

Who built Gold Rush?

Gold Rush was manufactured by Pinfari.

How long is the ride on Gold Rush?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 764.4 ft of track.

How many people ride Gold Rush at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 700 riders.

Is Gold Rush good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.6 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Where did Gold Rush operate before?

RCDB records it at Gulliver's Land Resort (as Python).

Is Gold Rush at Kingoland the same as Mighty Mini Mega at Adventure Island?

Both are Pinfari Mini Mega Coaster MM29 coasters on RCDB; the Kingoland ride opened in 2020 and the Adventure Island ride in 2003; Kingoland sets 130 cm and Adventure Island sets 120 cm.
