Kingoland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Plumelin, Brittany, France · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kingoland in Plumelin, Brittany, France has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Apollo Steamrocket to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Maranello Twist. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Kingoland are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Kingoland a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Pinfari, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Apollo Steamrocket, which opened in 2014.

Kingoland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Kingoland? By height

At Kingoland the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Kingoland

These are the rules Kingoland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Speed Chenille: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Gold Rush: riders 95 to 130 cm (about 37.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Kingoland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Kingoland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Speed Chenille, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Gold Rush, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 30.2 ft tall, 24.6 mph, no inversions, 2.2 g).
  3. The big one: Apollo Steamrocket, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
