Abyssus

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 62.1 mph; 126.3 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2021.

Abyssus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 14 Jul 2021. The ride stands 126.3 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 4317.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Abyssus is like

With 4,317.6 ft of track for a 126.3 ft structure, Abyssus is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 4 times across 4,317.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2021, it is the 16th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Energylandia, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Abyssus sits on the west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Light Explorers, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Choco Chip Creek at about 140 m. Abyssus layout, trains and ride system

Abyssus starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Abyssus' track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch
  2. LSM Boost
  3. Loop
  4. Batwing
  5. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 4 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Abyssus compares

On our intensity ranking Abyssus is number 3 of 19 at Energylandia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 18 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Abyssus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Energylandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Abyssus?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Abyssus, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Energylandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Abyssus?

Abyssus is 126.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Abyssus go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Abyssus go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Abyssus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Abyssus open?

Abyssus opened on 14 Jul 2021.

Who built Abyssus?

Abyssus was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Abyssus?

RCDB lists 4317.6 ft of track.

Does Abyssus launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Abyssus at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Abyssus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
