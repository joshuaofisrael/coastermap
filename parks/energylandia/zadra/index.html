Zadra

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 75.2 mph; 206 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2019.

Zadra is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 22 Aug 2019. The ride stands 206 ft tall, reaches 75.2 mph and runs 4317.6 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Zadra is like

Zadra pairs a 206 ft structure with 4,317.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 4,317.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2019, it is the 15th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Energylandia, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Zadra sits on the north-west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Draken, roughly 130 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Roller Coaster Mayan at about 130 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Zadra

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Energylandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Zadra?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Zadra, according to Energylandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Zadra?

Zadra is 206 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Zadra go?

Its top speed is 75.2 mph (121 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Zadra go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Zadra scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Zadra open?

Zadra opened on 22 Aug 2019.

Who built Zadra?

Zadra was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the ride on Zadra?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 50 seconds over 4317.6 ft of track.

How does Zadra get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Zadra at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,050 riders.

Is Zadra good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 75.2 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
