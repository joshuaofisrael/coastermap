Frida

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 27.7 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Frida is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 20 Jul 2019. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 27.7 mph and runs 810.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Frida is like

At 42.7 ft tall with 810.4 ft of track, Frida balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2019, it is the 14th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 247m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Rookie Racer at Mid America Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Energylandia sets 120 cm, while Mid America Adventure sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Energylandia (tied with 3 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Frida sits on the north-west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Draken, roughly 120 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Light Explorers at about 150 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Frida layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Frida?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Frida, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Energylandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Frida?

Frida is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Frida go?

Its top speed is 27.7 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Frida go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Frida scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Frida open?

Frida opened on 20 Jul 2019.

Who built Frida?

Frida was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Frida?

RCDB lists 810.4 ft of track.

How does Frida get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Frida good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 27.7 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Frida at Energylandia the same as Rookie Racer at Mid America Adventure?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 247m coasters on RCDB; the Energylandia ride opened in 2019 and the Mid America Adventure ride in 2023; Energylandia sets 120 cm and Mid America Adventure sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 42.7 ft and 41 ft.
