Viking Roller Coaster

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 26.7 mph; 36.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Viking Roller Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 14 Jul 2014. The ride stands 36.1 ft tall, reaches 26.7 mph and runs 1017.1 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Viking Roller Coaster is like

Viking Roller Coaster pairs a 36.1 ft structure with 1,017.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2014, it is the 3rd oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Energylandia, shared with 6 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Viking Roller Coaster sits on the north-east side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Boomerang, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Roller Coaster Mayan at about 110 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Before you ride Viking Roller Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders must be at least 12 years old.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Viking Roller Coaster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Viking Roller Coaster. Riders must be at least 12 years old, according to Energylandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Viking Roller Coaster?

Viking Roller Coaster is 36.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Viking Roller Coaster go?

Its top speed is 26.7 mph (43 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Viking Roller Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Viking Roller Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Viking Roller Coaster open?

Viking Roller Coaster opened on 14 Jul 2014.

Who built Viking Roller Coaster?

Viking Roller Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the ride on Viking Roller Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds over 1017.1 ft of track.

How does Viking Roller Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Viking Roller Coaster at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 700 riders.

Is Viking Roller Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 26.7 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
