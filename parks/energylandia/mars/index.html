Mars

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 15.3 mph; 19.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Mars is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 19.7 ft tall, reaches 15.3 mph and runs 492.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mars is like

Mars pairs a 19.7 ft structure with 492.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mars sits on the south-east side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Circus Coaster, roughly 30 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Speed at about 70 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Mars layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Mars:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Mars trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 6 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 24 riders.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Mars?

Mars is 19.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mars go?

Its top speed is 15.3 mph (25 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mars go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mars scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Mars open?

Mars opened in 2014.

Who built Mars?

Mars was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Mars?

RCDB lists 492.1 ft of track.

How does Mars get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mars at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Mars good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 15.3 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
