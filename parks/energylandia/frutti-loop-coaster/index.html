Frutti Loop Coaster

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 12.4 mph; 12.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Frutti Loop Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 12.1 ft tall, reaches 12.4 mph and runs 295.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Frutti Loop Coaster is like

At 12.1 ft tall with 295.3 ft of track, Frutti Loop Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2014, it is the oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Wriggler at Gulliver's Land.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Frutti Loop Coaster sits on the east side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Energuś, roughly 30 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mars at about 140 m. Frequently asked questions

How tall is Frutti Loop Coaster?

Frutti Loop Coaster is 12.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Frutti Loop Coaster go?

Its top speed is 12.4 mph (20 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Frutti Loop Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Frutti Loop Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Frutti Loop Coaster open?

Frutti Loop Coaster opened in 2014.

Who built Frutti Loop Coaster?

Frutti Loop Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Frutti Loop Coaster?

How does Frutti Loop Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Frutti Loop Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 12.4 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia the same as Wriggler at Gulliver's Land?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Energylandia ride opened in 2014 and the Gulliver's Land ride in 2026.
