Honey Harbour

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 28.6 mph; 38.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Honey Harbour is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Apr 2024. The ride stands 38.4 ft tall, reaches 28.6 mph and runs 833.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Honey Harbour is like

Honey Harbour pairs a 38.4 ft structure with 833.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.9 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Coaster / Kalypso design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Dino Dash at Emerald Park and Wild West Coaster at Winnoland.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Honey Harbour sits on the south-west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Formuła, roughly 170 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Choco Chip Creek at about 170 m. Honey Harbour layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Honey Harbour compares

On our intensity ranking Honey Harbour is number 14 of 19 at Energylandia, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 13th tallest of the park's 18 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Draken (24 ft tall, 21.4 mph, no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Frida (42.7 ft tall, 27.7 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Honey Harbour?

Honey Harbour is 38.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Honey Harbour go?

Its top speed is 28.6 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Honey Harbour go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Honey Harbour scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Honey Harbour open?

Honey Harbour opened on 27 Apr 2024.

Who built Honey Harbour?

Honey Harbour was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Honey Harbour?

RCDB lists a ride time of 56 seconds over 833.3 ft of track.

How does Honey Harbour get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Honey Harbour at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 791 riders.

Is Honey Harbour good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.6 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Honey Harbour at Energylandia the same as Dino Dash at Emerald Park?

Both are Vekoma Family Coaster / Kalypso coasters on RCDB; the Energylandia ride opened in 2024 and the Emerald Park ride in 2022.
