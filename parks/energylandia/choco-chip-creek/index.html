Choco Chip Creek

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 34.2 mph; 54.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Choco Chip Creek is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 Apr 2024. The ride stands 54.1 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Choco Chip Creek is like

With 3,937 ft of track for a 54.1 ft structure, Choco Chip Creek is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2024, it is the 18th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Choco Chip Creek sits on the west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Light Explorers, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Abyssus at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Choco Chip Creek?

Choco Chip Creek is 54.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Choco Chip Creek go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Choco Chip Creek go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Choco Chip Creek scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Choco Chip Creek open?

Choco Chip Creek opened on 27 Apr 2024.

Who built Choco Chip Creek?

Choco Chip Creek was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Choco Chip Creek?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 30 seconds over 3,937 ft of track.

How does Choco Chip Creek get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Choco Chip Creek good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
