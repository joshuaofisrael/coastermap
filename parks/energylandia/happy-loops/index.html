Happy Loops

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 18.6 mph; 23 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Happy Loops is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2017. The ride stands 23 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 370.8 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Happy Loops is like

Happy Loops pairs a 23 ft structure with 370.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2017, it is the 10th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Kids Spin at Skyline Park and Spinning Out at Beech Bend.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Happy Loops sits on the south-east side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speed, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mars at about 120 m. Happy Loops layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Happy Loops?

Happy Loops is 23 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Happy Loops go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Happy Loops go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Happy Loops scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Happy Loops open?

Happy Loops opened in 2017.

Who built Happy Loops?

Happy Loops was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Happy Loops?

RCDB lists 370.8 ft of track.

How does Happy Loops get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Happy Loops at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 200 riders.

Is Happy Loops good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Happy Loops at Energylandia the same as Spinning Out at Beech Bend?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A coasters on RCDB; the Energylandia ride opened in 2017 and the Beech Bend ride in 2016.
