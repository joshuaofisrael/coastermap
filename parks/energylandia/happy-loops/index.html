Happy Loops

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 18.6 mph; 23 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Happy Loops is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2017. The ride stands 23 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 370.8 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Happy Loops is like

Happy Loops pairs a 23 ft structure with 370.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2017, it is the 10th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Happy Loops sits on the south-east side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speed, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mars at about 120 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.
