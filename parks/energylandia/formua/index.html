Formuła

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 49.2 mph; 81.4 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2016.

Formuła is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 25 Jun 2016 and was previously known as Rollercoaster Formula 1. The ride stands 81.4 ft tall, reaches 49.2 mph and runs 1837.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Formuła is like

Formuła pairs a 81.4 ft structure with 1,837.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,837.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.8 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2016, it is the 7th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia. It has run under other names (Rollercoaster Formula 1), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Energylandia, shared with 6 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Formuła sits on the south side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon Roller Coaster, roughly 130 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Honey Harbour at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Formuła

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Energylandia app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Formuła?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Formuła, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, according to Energylandia, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Formuła?

Formuła is 81.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Formuła go?

Its top speed is 49.2 mph (79 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Formuła go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Formuła scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Formuła open?

Formuła opened on 25 Jun 2016 (previously known as Rollercoaster Formula 1).

Who built Formuła?

Formuła was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Formuła?

RCDB lists 1837.3 ft of track.

Does Formuła launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Formuła at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Formuła good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 49.2 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
