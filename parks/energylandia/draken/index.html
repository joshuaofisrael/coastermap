Draken

Energylandia · Zator, Poland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 21.4 mph; 24 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Draken is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Energylandia in Zator, Poland, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 20 Jul 2019. The ride stands 24 ft tall, reaches 21.4 mph and runs 459.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Draken is like

Draken pairs a 24 ft structure with 459.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2019, it is the 13th oldest of the 19 coasters we list at Energylandia.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Draken sits on the north-west side of Energylandia's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Frida, roughly 120 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Zadra at about 130 m. Draken layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Draken?

Draken is 24 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Draken go?

Its top speed is 21.4 mph (34 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Draken go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Draken scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Draken open?

Draken opened on 20 Jul 2019.

Who built Draken?

Draken was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How long is the track on Draken?

RCDB lists 459.3 ft of track.

How does Draken get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Draken at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Draken good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.4 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
