Nigloland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Dolancourt, France · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Nigloland in Dolancourt, France has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Krampus Expédition to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Spatiale Expérience. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Nigloland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Nigloland a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 17% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 6 go upside down.

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 5 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Gold Mine Train, which opened in 1992.

Nigloland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Nigloland? By height

At Nigloland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Noisette Express (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Krampus Expédition at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Gold Mine Train (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)), Descente en Schlitt' (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)) and Noisette Express (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Krampus Expédition at 110 cm (about 43.3 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Gold Mine Train, Descente en Schlitt', Noisette Express and Krampus Expédition. With a companion they can also ride Spatiale Expérience (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Alpina Blitz at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Alpina Blitz. With a companion they can also ride Spatiale Expérience (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Nigloland

These are the rules Nigloland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Gold Mine Train: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Spatiale Expérience: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Descente en Schlitt': riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Noisette Express: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Nigloland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Nigloland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Gold Mine Train, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Spatiale Expérience, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions, 4.5 g).
  3. The big one: Alpina Blitz, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 108.3 ft tall, 51.6 mph, no inversions, 4.3 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Nigloland?

Roller coaster height requirements at Nigloland run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) (Krampus Expédition) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Spatiale Expérience), according to official park sources checked 11 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Nigloland have?

Nigloland has 6 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 11 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Nigloland?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Alpina Blitz (108.3 ft tall, 51.6 mph, no inversions, 4.3 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Nigloland?

Alpina Blitz, at 108.3 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Nigloland?

Alpina Blitz, with a top speed of 51.6 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Nigloland for first-timers?

Gold Mine Train is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 120 cm (about 47.2 in) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Nigloland?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Gold Mine Train, Descente en Schlitt', Noisette Express.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Nigloland?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Gold Mine Train, Descente en Schlitt', Noisette Express, Krampus Expédition. With a companion they can also ride Spatiale Expérience.

Which coasters at Nigloland do not go upside down?

Gold Mine Train, Spatiale Expérience, Descente en Schlitt', Alpina Blitz, Noisette Express, Krampus Expédition have no inversions, per RCDB.
