Alpina Blitz

Nigloland · Dolancourt, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 51.6 mph; 108.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Alpina Blitz is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Nigloland in Dolancourt, France, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 12 Apr 2014. The ride stands 108.3 ft tall, reaches 51.6 mph and runs 2358.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Alpina Blitz is like

Alpina Blitz pairs a 108.3 ft structure with 2,358.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2014, it is the 4th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Nigloland.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Nigloland, out of 5 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Alpina Blitz sits near the middle of Nigloland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Krampus Expédition, roughly 60 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spatiale Expérience at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Alpina Blitz?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Alpina Blitz, according to Nigloland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Alpina Blitz?

Alpina Blitz is 108.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Alpina Blitz go?

Its top speed is 51.6 mph (83 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Alpina Blitz go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Alpina Blitz scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Alpina Blitz open?

Alpina Blitz opened on 12 Apr 2014.

Who built Alpina Blitz?

Alpina Blitz was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Alpina Blitz?

RCDB lists 2358.9 ft of track.

How does Alpina Blitz get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Alpina Blitz at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Alpina Blitz good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 51.6 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
