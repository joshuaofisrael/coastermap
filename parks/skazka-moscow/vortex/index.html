Vortex

Skazka Family Park · Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 29.8 mph; 1 inversion; opened 2026.

Vortex is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Skazka Family Park in Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia, built by Jinma Rides. It opened on 15 Jul 2026. The ride reaches 29.8 mph. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Vortex is like

Opened in 2026, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Skazka Family Park.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Skazka, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 190 cm (about 74.8 in).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vortex?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Vortex. The maximum rider height is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). The park lists it as Вихрь. Its rules sheet lists 140 cm to 190 cm to ride alone and a minimum age of 10, according to Skazka park, official ride rules sheet for Вихрь (PDF) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Vortex go?

Its top speed is 29.8 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vortex go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Vortex scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Vortex open?

Vortex opened on 15 Jul 2026.

Who built Vortex?

Vortex was manufactured by Jinma Rides.

How does Vortex get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vortex at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Vortex good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 29.8 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
