Nitro

Skazka Family Park · Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 23.6 mph; 31.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Nitro is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Skazka Family Park in Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia, built by Jinma Rides. It opened in 2025. The ride stands 31.2 ft tall, reaches 23.6 mph and runs 869.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Nitro is like

At 31.2 ft tall with 869.4 ft of track, Nitro balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2025, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Skazka Family Park.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Skazka, shared with 1 other coaster. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Nitro

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The ride's rules sheet lists 100 cm to 140 cm with a companion over 18, and 140 cm to ride alone. The ride page shows only the 100 cm minimum.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Nitro?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Nitro, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The ride's rules sheet lists 100 cm to 140 cm with a companion over 18, and 140 cm to ride alone. The ride page shows only the 100 cm minimum, according to Skazka park, official ride rules sheet for Нитро (PDF) (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Nitro?

Nitro is 31.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nitro go?

Its top speed is 23.6 mph (38 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nitro go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Nitro scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Nitro open?

Nitro opened in 2025.

Who built Nitro?

Nitro was manufactured by Jinma Rides.

How long is the track on Nitro?

RCDB lists 869.4 ft of track.

How does Nitro get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Nitro at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Nitro good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 23.6 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
