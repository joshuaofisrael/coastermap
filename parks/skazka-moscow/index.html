Skazka Family Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Skazka Family Park in Krylatskoye, Moscow, Russia has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Caterpillar to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Nitro. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Skazka Family Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Skazka Family Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 43% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 7 go upside down.

The most common builder is Jinma Rides, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Lightning, which opened in 2017.

Skazka Family Park publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Nitro: The ride's rules sheet lists 100 cm to 140 cm with a companion over 18, and 140 cm to ride alone. The ride page shows only the 100 cm minimum.
  • Caterpillar: The park lists it as Гусеница: height from 50 cm, and children under 110 cm ride with an adult.
  • Boomerang: The ride's rules sheet lists 130 cm to 195 cm to ride alone and riders up to 60 years old.
  • Vortex: The park lists it as Вихрь. Its rules sheet lists 140 cm to 190 cm to ride alone and a minimum age of 10.

We could not verify a height rule on an official Skazka Family Park source for Lightning, Formula and Way of the Dragon, so they are left out of the height lists below. Ask the park before you queue.

What can my child ride at Skazka Family Park? By height

At Skazka Family Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Caterpillar (50 to 110 cm (about 19.7 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Nitro (100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in)) and Caterpillar (50 to 110 cm (about 19.7 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Caterpillar. With a companion they can also ride Nitro (100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Boomerang. With a companion they can also ride Nitro (100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Vortex at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides here also have an official maximum height: Boomerang (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Vortex (up to 190 cm (about 74.8 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Skazka Family Park

These are the rules Skazka Family Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Nitro: riders 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Caterpillar: riders 50 to 110 cm (about 19.7 to 43.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Boomerang: The ride's rules sheet lists 130 cm to 195 cm to ride alone and riders up to 60 years old.
  • Vortex: The park lists it as Вихрь. Its rules sheet lists 140 cm to 190 cm to ride alone and a minimum age of 10.

Best coasters at Skazka Family Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Skazka Family Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Caterpillar, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Nitro, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 31.2 ft tall, 23.6 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Boomerang, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 120.7 ft tall, 53 mph, 3 inversions).
