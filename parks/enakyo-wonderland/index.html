Enakyo Wonderland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ena, Gifu, Japan · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Enakyo Wonderland in Ena, Gifu, Japan has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Camel Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Enakyo Wonderland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Enakyo Wonderland a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Togo, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Camel Coaster, which opened in 1989.

Enakyo Wonderland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Enakyo Wonderland? By height

At Enakyo Wonderland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Enakyo Wonderland has 3 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 11 Nov 2026.
