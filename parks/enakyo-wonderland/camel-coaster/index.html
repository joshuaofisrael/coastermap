Camel Coaster

Enakyo Wonderland · Ena, Gifu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); 85.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1989.

Camel Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Enakyo Wonderland in Ena, Gifu, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It opened in 1989. The ride stands 85.3 ft tall and runs 2296.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Camel Coaster is like

Camel Coaster pairs a 85.3 ft structure with 2,296.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Camel Coaster sits on the west side of Enakyo Wonderland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Cycle Coaster, roughly 100 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jungle Coaster at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Camel Coaster?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Camel Coaster, according to Enakyo Wonderland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Camel Coaster?

Camel Coaster is 85.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Camel Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Camel Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Camel Coaster open?

Camel Coaster opened in 1989.

Who built Camel Coaster?

Camel Coaster was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the track on Camel Coaster?

RCDB lists 2296.6 ft of track.

How many people ride Camel Coaster at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Camel Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Camel Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Gifu Chubu Mirai Expo (as Camel Coaster).

Is Camel Coaster at Enakyo Wonderland the same as Camel Coaster at Nasu Highland?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Enakyo Wonderland ride opened in 1989 and the Nasu Highland ride in 1983; both list a 110 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 85.3 ft and 94.8 ft.
