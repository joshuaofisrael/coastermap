Hokkaido Greenland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Iwamizawa, Japan · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Hokkaido Greenland in Iwamizawa, Japan has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 115 cm (about 45.3 in) on GO-ON to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Dragon King. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Hokkaido Greenland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Hokkaido Greenland a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 40% across all 221 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Dragon King, which opened in 1986.

Hokkaido Greenland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
