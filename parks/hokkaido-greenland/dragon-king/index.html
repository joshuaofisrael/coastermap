Dragon King

Hokkaido Greenland · Iwamizawa, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 3 inversions; opened 1986.

Dragon King is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hokkaido Greenland in Iwamizawa, Japan, built by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd. It first opened on 3 Jun 1986 and was previously known as San Kaiten. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Dragon King is like

Opened in 1986, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hokkaido Greenland. It has run under other names (San Kaiten), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Hokkaido Greenland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragon King sits near the middle of Hokkaido Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wani Wani Coaster, roughly 100 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Torokko Coaster at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Dragon King

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Ryuo (龍王).
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Hokkaido Greenland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragon King?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Dragon King. The park lists it as Ryuo (龍王), according to Hokkaido Greenland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Dragon King go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Dragon King scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Dragon King open?

Dragon King opened on 3 Jun 1986 (previously known as San Kaiten).

Who built Dragon King?

Dragon King was manufactured by Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the ride on Dragon King?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds.

How many people ride Dragon King at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Dragon King good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
