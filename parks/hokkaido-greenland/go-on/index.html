GO-ON

Hokkaido Greenland · Iwamizawa, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 115 cm (about 45.3 in); top speed 46.6 mph; 108.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

GO-ON is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Hokkaido Greenland in Iwamizawa, Japan. It first opened on 18 Jun 1986 and was previously known as Camel Coaster and later Big Boom Coaster. The ride stands 108.3 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 4133.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding GO-ON is like

With 4,133.9 ft of track for a 108.3 ft structure, GO-ON is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1986, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Hokkaido Greenland. It has run under other names (Camel Coaster, then Big Boom Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 115 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Hokkaido Greenland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, GO-ON sits on the east side of Hokkaido Greenland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wani Wani Coaster, roughly 220 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon King at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 115 cm (about 45.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for GO-ON?

Riders must be at least 115 cm (about 45.3 in) tall to ride GO-ON. The park lists it as 轟音 (Go-on), according to Hokkaido Greenland, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is GO-ON?

GO-ON is 108.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does GO-ON go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does GO-ON go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is GO-ON scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did GO-ON open?

GO-ON opened on 18 Jun 1986 (previously known as Camel Coaster and later Big Boom Coaster).

How long is the ride on GO-ON?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 30 seconds over 4133.9 ft of track.

How does GO-ON get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride GO-ON at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does GO-ON use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is GO-ON good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 115 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
