Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts

Universal Studios Florida · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Florida in Orlando, Florida, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 8 Jul 2014. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts is like

Opened in 2014, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Universal Studios Florida.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Universal Studios Florida, out of 2 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts sits on the north-west side of Universal Studios Florida's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Revenge of the Mummy, roughly 380 m away (48 in minimum). Next closest is Trolls Trollercoaster at about 420 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts open?

Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts opened on 8 Jul 2014.

Who built Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts?

Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
