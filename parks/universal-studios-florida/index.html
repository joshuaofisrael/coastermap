Universal Studios Florida roller coasters

Orlando, Florida · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Florida in Orlando, Florida has 3 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Revenge of the Mummy at 44.4 ft, which is also the fastest at 40 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Trolls Trollercoaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Universal Studios Florida have?

Universal Studios Florida has 3 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Universal Studios Florida?

Revenge of the Mummy, at 44.4 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Universal Studios Florida?

Revenge of the Mummy, with a top speed of 40 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Universal Studios Florida?

By height requirement, Trolls Trollercoaster has the lowest minimum at 36 in (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Universal Studios Florida coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, Trolls Trollercoaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Universal Studios Florida coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, Trolls Trollercoaster, Revenge of the Mummy. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Universal Studios Florida do not go upside down?

Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, Trolls Trollercoaster, Revenge of the Mummy have no inversions, per RCDB.
