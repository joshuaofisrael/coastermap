Universal Studios Florida height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Orlando, Florida · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Universal Studios Florida in Orlando, Florida has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Trolls Trollercoaster to 48 in (122 cm) on Revenge of the Mummy. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Universal Studios Florida are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Universal Studios Florida a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 45% across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 42 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Trolls Trollercoaster, which opened in 1999.

What can my child ride at Universal Studios Florida? By height

At Universal Studios Florida the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Trolls Trollercoaster. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts at 42 in (107 cm).

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Revenge of the Mummy at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Revenge of the Mummy. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, Trolls Trollercoaster and Revenge of the Mummy. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Best coasters at Universal Studios Florida for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Universal Studios Florida, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Trolls Trollercoaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.7 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Revenge of the Mummy, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 44.4 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
