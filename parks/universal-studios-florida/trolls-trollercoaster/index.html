Trolls Trollercoaster

Universal Studios Florida · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 36 in (91 cm); top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Trolls Trollercoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Florida in Orlando, Florida, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 13 Mar 1999 and was previously known as Woody Woodpecker's Nuthouse Coaster. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Trolls Trollercoaster is like

Trolls Trollercoaster pairs a 27.9 ft structure with 679.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 1999, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Universal Studios Florida. It has run under other names (Woody Woodpecker's Nuthouse Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 11 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America, Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland and Tami-Tami at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Universal Studios Florida sets 36 in, while Six Flags Great America sets 42 in, Disneyland sets 35 in and PortAventura Park sets 130 cm.

Its 36 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Universal Studios Florida, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Trolls Trollercoaster sits on the east side of Universal Studios Florida's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Revenge of the Mummy, roughly 400 m away (48 in minimum). Next closest is Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts at about 420 m. Trolls Trollercoaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Trolls Trollercoaster?

Riders must be at least 36 in (91 cm) tall to ride Trolls Trollercoaster, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Trolls Trollercoaster?

Trolls Trollercoaster is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Trolls Trollercoaster go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Trolls Trollercoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Trolls Trollercoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Trolls Trollercoaster open?

Trolls Trollercoaster opened on 13 Mar 1999 (previously known as Woody Woodpecker's Nuthouse Coaster).

Who built Trolls Trollercoaster?

Trolls Trollercoaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Trolls Trollercoaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 44 seconds over 679.2 ft of track.

How does Trolls Trollercoaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Trolls Trollercoaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

Is Trolls Trollercoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph; the minimum height is 36 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Universal Studios Florida ride opened in 1999 and the Disneyland ride in 1993; Universal Studios Florida sets 36 in and Disneyland sets 35 in.
