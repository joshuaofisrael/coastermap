Revenge of the Mummy

Universal Studios Florida · Orlando, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 44.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Revenge of the Mummy is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Universal Studios Florida in Orlando, Florida, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 21 May 2004. The ride stands 44.4 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 2,200 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Revenge of the Mummy is like

Revenge of the Mummy spreads 2,200 ft of track across a layout only 44.4 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Universal Studios Florida.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Universal Studios Florida. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Revenge of the Mummy sits on the south-west side of Universal Studios Florida's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts, roughly 380 m away (42 in minimum). Next closest is Trolls Trollercoaster at about 400 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Revenge of the Mummy?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Revenge of the Mummy, according to Visit Orlando, Orlando Roller Coaster Guide (updated 1 Jun 2026) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Revenge of the Mummy?

Revenge of the Mummy is 44.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Revenge of the Mummy go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Revenge of the Mummy go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Revenge of the Mummy scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Revenge of the Mummy open?

Revenge of the Mummy opened on 21 May 2004.

Who built Revenge of the Mummy?

Revenge of the Mummy was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Revenge of the Mummy?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 57 seconds over 2,200 ft of track.

Does Revenge of the Mummy launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

How many people ride Revenge of the Mummy at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Revenge of the Mummy good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Revenge of the Mummy at Universal Studios Florida the same as Revenge of the Mummy at Universal Studios Singapore?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Universal Studios Florida ride opened in 2004 and the Universal Studios Singapore ride in 2010; both list a 48 in minimum.
