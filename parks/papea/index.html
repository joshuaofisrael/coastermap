Papéa Parc height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Yvré-l'Évêque, Pays de la Loire, France · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Papéa Parc in Yvré-l'Évêque, Pays de la Loire, France has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Train de la Mine to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Speedy Nuts. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Papéa Parc are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Papéa Parc a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Train de la Mine, which opened in 1995.

Papéa Parc publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Papéa Parc? By height

At Papéa Parc the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Train de la Mine at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

Companion rules at Papéa Parc

These are the rules Papéa Parc states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Train de la Mine: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Roller Coaster: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Chenille: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Speedy Nuts: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Papéa Parc for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Papéa Parc, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Chenille, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Roller Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Speedy Nuts, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 49.3 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
