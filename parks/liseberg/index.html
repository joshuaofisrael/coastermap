Liseberg height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Gothenburg, Sweden · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Liseberg in Gothenburg, Sweden has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Rabalder to 132 cm (about 52 in) on Valkyria. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Liseberg are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Liseberg a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 43% of its lineup, against 45% across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Balder); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Helix).

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Lisebergbanan, which opened in 1987.

Liseberg publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Liseberg? By height

At Liseberg the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Rabalder (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)) and Stampbanan (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Luna at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Rabalder (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)), Stampbanan (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)) and Luna (95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Lisebergbanan at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Rabalder, Stampbanan and Luna. With a companion they can also ride Lisebergbanan (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Helix at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Lisebergbanan, Helix, Valkyria and Balder. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Valkyria (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Liseberg

These are the rules Liseberg states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Lisebergbanan: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Rabalder: riders 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Stampbanan: riders 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Luna: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Helix: Riders must also be at least 7 years old.
  • Valkyria: Riders must also be at least 7 years old.
  • Balder: Riders must also be at least 7 years old.

Best coasters at Liseberg for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Liseberg, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Stampbanan, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; 10.8 ft tall, 12.3 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Luna, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 109.9 ft tall, 42.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Helix, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 134.5 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions, 4.3 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
