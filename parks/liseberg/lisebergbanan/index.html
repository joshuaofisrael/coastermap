Lisebergbanan

Liseberg · Gothenburg, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 49.7 mph; 147.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1987.

Lisebergbanan is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Liseberg in Gothenburg, Sweden, built by Zierer. It opened on 18 Apr 1987. The ride stands 147.7 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 4,397 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Lisebergbanan?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Lisebergbanan, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Liseberg, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Lisebergbanan?

Lisebergbanan is 147.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Lisebergbanan go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Lisebergbanan go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Lisebergbanan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.
