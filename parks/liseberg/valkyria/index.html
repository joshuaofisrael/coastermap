Valkyria

Liseberg · Gothenburg, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 65.2 mph; 154.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2018.

Valkyria is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Liseberg in Gothenburg, Sweden, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 10 Aug 2018. The ride stands 154.2 ft tall, drops 164 ft, reaches 65.2 mph and runs 2296.6 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Valkyria is like

Valkyria pairs a 154.2 ft structure with 2,296.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. Its 164 ft drop is longer than its 154.2 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,296.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Liseberg.

Its 132 cm minimum is the strictest at Liseberg. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Valkyria sits on the south-east side of Liseberg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Balder, roughly 70 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Luna at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Valkyria compares

On our intensity ranking Valkyria is number 2 of 7 at Liseberg, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Balder (118.1 ft tall, 55.9 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Helix (134.5 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions, 4.3 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Valkyria

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders must also be at least 7 years old.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Liseberg app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Valkyria?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Valkyria. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders must also be at least 7 years old, according to Liseberg, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Valkyria?

Valkyria is 154.2 ft tall, with a drop of 164 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Valkyria go?

Its top speed is 65.2 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Valkyria go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Valkyria scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Valkyria open?

Valkyria opened on 10 Aug 2018.

Who built Valkyria?

Valkyria was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
