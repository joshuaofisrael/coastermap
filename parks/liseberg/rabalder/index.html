Rabalder

Liseberg · Gothenburg, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 24.2 mph; 29.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Rabalder is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Liseberg in Gothenburg, Sweden, built by Zierer. It opened on 25 Apr 2009. The ride stands 29.5 ft tall, reaches 24.2 mph and runs 728.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rabalder is like

At 29.5 ft tall with 728.3 ft of track, Rabalder balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2009, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Liseberg. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Force / Two design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Vilde Hønsejagt at Djurs Sommerland, Wanted Dalton at Parc Spirou, Tabaluga's Achterbahn at Plopsaland Deutschland and Donderstenen at Hellendoorn. Height rules for this model differ by park: Liseberg sets 110 cm, while Djurs Sommerland sets 120 cm, Parc Spirou sets 130 cm and Plopsaland Deutschland sets 120 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Liseberg (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rabalder sits near the middle of Liseberg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Stampbanan, roughly 80 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Helix at about 100 m. Rabalder layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

How Rabalder compares

On our intensity ranking Rabalder is number 6 of 7 at Liseberg, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rabalder?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Rabalder, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion, according to Liseberg, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rabalder?

Rabalder is 29.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rabalder go?

Its top speed is 24.2 mph (39 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rabalder go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rabalder scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Rabalder open?

Rabalder opened on 25 Apr 2009.

Who built Rabalder?

Rabalder was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Rabalder?

RCDB lists 728.3 ft of track.

How does Rabalder get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rabalder at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Rabalder good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.2 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Rabalder at Liseberg the same as Potts Blitz at Potts Park?

Both are Zierer Force / Two coasters on RCDB; the Liseberg ride opened in 2009 and the Potts Park ride in 1993.
