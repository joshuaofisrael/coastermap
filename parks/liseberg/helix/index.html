Helix

Liseberg · Gothenburg, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 134.5 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2014.

Helix is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Liseberg in Gothenburg, Sweden, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 26 Apr 2014. The ride stands 134.5 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 4530.8 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding Helix is like

With 4,530.8 ft of track for a 134.5 ft structure, Helix is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 7 times across 4,530.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.3 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2014, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Liseberg.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Liseberg, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Helix sits on the north-west side of Liseberg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Lisebergbanan, roughly 100 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Rabalder at about 100 m. Helix layout, trains and ride system

Helix starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Helix

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders must also be at least 7 years old.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 7 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Liseberg app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Helix?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Helix. Riders must also be at least 7 years old, according to Liseberg, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Helix?

Helix is 134.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Helix go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Helix go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Helix scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Helix open?

Helix opened on 26 Apr 2014.

Who built Helix?

Helix was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on Helix?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 4530.8 ft of track.

Does Helix launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Helix at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,350 riders.

Is Helix good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 7 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
