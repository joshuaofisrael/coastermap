Griffon

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 71 mph; 205 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2007.

Griffon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 18 May 2007. The ride stands 205 ft tall, reaches 71 mph and runs 3,108 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Griffon is like

Griffon pairs a 205 ft structure with 3,108 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 2 times across 3,108 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 4th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Williamsburg.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Griffon sits on the west side of Busch Gardens Williamsburg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Alpengeist, roughly 100 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Loch Ness Monster at about 150 m. Griffon layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a chain lift hill.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Griffon's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Immelmann
  3. Hill (with a 130 ft drop at 90 °)
  4. Immelmann
  5. Splash Effect

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Griffon compares

On our intensity ranking Griffon is number 3 of 11 at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Tempesto (150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion; 54 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Alpengeist (195 ft tall, 67 mph, 6 inversions, 3.7 g; 54 in minimum).

Before you ride Griffon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Busch Gardens Williamsburg app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Griffon?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Griffon, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Griffon?

Griffon is 205 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Griffon go?

Its top speed is 71 mph (114 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Griffon go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Griffon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Griffon open?

Griffon opened on 18 May 2007.

Who built Griffon?

Griffon was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Griffon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes over 3,108 ft of track.

How does Griffon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Griffon at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Griffon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 71 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
