Apollo's Chariot

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 73 mph; 170 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Apollo's Chariot is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 27 Mar 1999. The ride stands 170 ft tall, drops 210 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 4,882 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Apollo's Chariot is like

At 170 ft tall with 4,882 ft of track, Apollo's Chariot balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 210 ft drop is longer than its 170 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.1 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 3rd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Orion at Kings Island and Candymonium at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, while SeaWorld Orlando sets 54 in, Kings Island sets 54 in and Kings Island sets 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 7 other coasters at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, out of 10 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Apollo's Chariot sits on the east side of Busch Gardens Williamsburg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tempesto, roughly 240 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pantheon at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Apollo's Chariot?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Apollo's Chariot, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Apollo's Chariot?

Apollo's Chariot is 170 ft tall, with a drop of 210 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Apollo's Chariot go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Apollo's Chariot go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Apollo's Chariot scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Apollo's Chariot open?

Apollo's Chariot opened on 27 Mar 1999.

Who built Apollo's Chariot?

Apollo's Chariot was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Apollo's Chariot?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 15 seconds over 4,882 ft of track.

How does Apollo's Chariot get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Apollo's Chariot at once?

Each train carries 36 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,750 riders.

Is Apollo's Chariot good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 73 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg the same as Raging Bull at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride opened in 1999 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1999; Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in and Six Flags Great America sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 170 ft and 202 ft.
