Apollo's Chariot

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 73 mph; 170 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Apollo's Chariot is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 27 Mar 1999. The ride stands 170 ft tall, drops 210 ft, reaches 73 mph and runs 4,882 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Apollo's Chariot?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Apollo's Chariot, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Apollo's Chariot?

Apollo's Chariot is 170 ft tall, with a drop of 210 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Apollo's Chariot go?

Its top speed is 73 mph (117 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Apollo's Chariot go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Apollo's Chariot scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Apollo's Chariot open?

Apollo's Chariot opened on 27 Mar 1999.

Who built Apollo's Chariot?

Apollo's Chariot was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
