InvadR

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 48 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

InvadR is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 7 Apr 2017. The ride drops 74 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,118 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for InvadR?

Riders must be at least 46 inches (117 cm) tall to ride InvadR, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does InvadR go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does InvadR go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is InvadR scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did InvadR open?

InvadR opened on 7 Apr 2017.

Who built InvadR?

InvadR was manufactured by Great Coasters International.
