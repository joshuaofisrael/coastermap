InvadR

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 48 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

InvadR is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 7 Apr 2017. The ride drops 74 ft, reaches 48 mph and runs 2,118 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding InvadR is like

Opened in 2017, it is the 8th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Williamsburg.

A rider who just meets its 46 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, InvadR sits on the west side of Busch Gardens Williamsburg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Alpengeist, roughly 140 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Griffon at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for InvadR?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride InvadR, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does InvadR go?

Its top speed is 48 mph (77 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does InvadR go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is InvadR scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did InvadR open?

InvadR opened on 7 Apr 2017.

Who built InvadR?

InvadR was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the track on InvadR?

RCDB lists 2,118 ft (646 m) of track.

How does InvadR get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride InvadR at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is InvadR good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 48 mph; the minimum height is 46 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
