Busch Gardens Williamsburg height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Williamsburg, Virginia · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 41 in (104 cm) on Grover's Alpine Express to 54 in (137 cm) on Griffon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 11 of them.

What the coasters at Busch Gardens Williamsburg are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 7 as extreme, which makes Busch Gardens Williamsburg a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 64% of its lineup, against 52% across all 80 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 8 of the 11 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 5 of the 11 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (InvadR); 3 use a launch instead of a lift hill (DarKoaster, Tempesto and Pantheon).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Loch Ness Monster, which opened in 1978.

What can my child ride at Busch Gardens Williamsburg? By height

At Busch Gardens Williamsburg the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Grover's Alpine Express at 41 in (104 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 11 coasters with a verified rule: Grover's Alpine Express and The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge. Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being InvadR at 46 in (117 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: DarKoaster, InvadR, Verbolten and Loch Ness Monster. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Pantheon at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 11 of 11 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Griffon, Tempesto, Pantheon, Alpengeist and Apollo's Chariot. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: DarKoaster (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Busch Gardens Williamsburg for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Busch Gardens Williamsburg, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Grover's Alpine Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (41 in minimum; 24 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: DarKoaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 36 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Pantheon, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 178 ft tall, 73 mph, 2 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
