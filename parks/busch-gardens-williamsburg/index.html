Busch Gardens Williamsburg roller coasters

Williamsburg, Virginia · 11 coasters

Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia has 11 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Griffon at 205 ft and the fastest is Pantheon at 73 mph. The lowest height requirement is 41 in (104 cm), for Grover's Alpine Express.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Busch Gardens Williamsburg have?

Busch Gardens Williamsburg has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Griffon, at 205 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Pantheon, with a top speed of 73 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

By height requirement, Grover's Alpine Express has the lowest minimum at 41 inches (104 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Busch Gardens Williamsburg coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Grover's Alpine Express, The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Busch Gardens Williamsburg coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Grover's Alpine Express, DarKoaster, InvadR, Verbolten, The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge, Loch Ness Monster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Busch Gardens Williamsburg do not go upside down?

Grover's Alpine Express, DarKoaster, InvadR, Verbolten, The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge, Apollo's Chariot have no inversions, per RCDB.
