The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 40 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge is a steel inverted roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 23 May 2025. The ride reaches 40 mph and runs 2,583 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge open?

The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge opened on 23 May 2025.

Who built The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge?

The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge?

RCDB lists 2,583 ft (787 m) of track.

How does The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is The Big Bad Wolf: The Wolf's Revenge good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
