Tempesto

Busch Gardens Williamsburg · Williamsburg, Virginia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 62 mph; 150 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2015.

Tempesto is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Busch Gardens Williamsburg in Williamsburg, Virginia, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 25 Apr 2015. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 62 mph and runs 863 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Tempesto is like

Tempesto packs its 863 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (150 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2015, it is the 7th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Busch Gardens Williamsburg. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's Sky Rocket / II design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Tigris at Busch Gardens Tampa, Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom, Electric Eel at SeaWorld San Diego and Phobia Phear Coaster at Lake Compounce. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tempesto sits on the north-east side of Busch Gardens Williamsburg's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pantheon, roughly 120 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Apollo's Chariot at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Tempesto

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Busch Gardens Williamsburg app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tempesto?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Tempesto, according to Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Roller Coasters page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tempesto?

Tempesto is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tempesto go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tempesto go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Tempesto scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tempesto open?

Tempesto opened on 25 Apr 2015.

Who built Tempesto?

Tempesto was manufactured by Premier Rides.

Is Tempesto at Busch Gardens Williamsburg the same as Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Premier Rides Sky Rocket / II coasters on RCDB; the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride opened in 2015 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2012; both list a 54 in minimum.
