Tiki-Waka

Walibi Belgium · Wavre, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 68.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Tiki-Waka is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 7 Apr 2018. The ride stands 68.9 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1850.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tiki-Waka is like

Tiki-Waka pairs a 68.9 ft structure with 1,850.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2018, it is the 7th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Walibi Belgium. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Bobsled Coaster / 515/4 design, and the first of them to open. Others include Valtor at Mandoria. Height rules for this model differ by park: Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm, while Mandoria sets 100 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Belgium (tied with 5 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tiki-Waka sits on the south-west side of Walibi Belgium's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mecalodon, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kondaa at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Tiki-Waka compares

On our intensity ranking Tiki-Waka is number 8 of 10 at Walibi Belgium, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Calamity Mine (45.9 ft tall, 30.1 mph, no inversions, 2.2 g; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Mecalodon (49.2 ft tall, 40.4 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tiki-Waka?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tiki-Waka, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Walibi Belgium, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tiki-Waka?

Tiki-Waka is 68.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tiki-Waka go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tiki-Waka go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tiki-Waka scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tiki-Waka open?

Tiki-Waka opened on 7 Apr 2018.

Who built Tiki-Waka?

Tiki-Waka was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Tiki-Waka?

RCDB lists 1850.4 ft of track.

How does Tiki-Waka get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tiki-Waka at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Tiki-Waka good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Tiki-Waka at Walibi Belgium the same as Valtor at Mandoria?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Bobsled Coaster / 515/4 coasters on RCDB; the Walibi Belgium ride opened in 2018 and the Mandoria ride in 2026; Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm and Mandoria sets 100 cm.
