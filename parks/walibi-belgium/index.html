Walibi Belgium height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Wavre, Belgium · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Mecalodon to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Turbine. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 6 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 9 of them.

What the coasters at Walibi Belgium are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes Walibi Belgium a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 60% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 10 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 10 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Loup-Garou).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Turbine, which opened in 1982.

Walibi Belgium publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Walibi Belgium? By height

At Walibi Belgium the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Fun Pilot (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Mecalodon at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Mecalodon (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)), Tiki-Waka (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)), Fun Pilot (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and Calamity Mine (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Pulsar at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 10 coasters with a verified rule: Mecalodon, Pulsar, Tiki-Waka, Fun Pilot, Calamity Mine and Loup-Garou. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Kondaa at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 9 of 10 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Kondaa, Vampire and Cobra. With a companion they can also ride Turbine (130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides here also have an official maximum height: Kondaa (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Vampire (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Walibi Belgium

These are the rules Walibi Belgium states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Mecalodon: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Tiki-Waka: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Fun Pilot: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Turbine: riders 130 to 140 cm (about 51.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Calamity Mine: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Walibi Belgium for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Walibi Belgium, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Fun Pilot, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 31.2 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Tiki-Waka, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 68.9 ft tall, 34.2 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Vampire, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Walibi Belgium?

Roller coaster height requirements at Walibi Belgium run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Mecalodon) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Turbine), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.
