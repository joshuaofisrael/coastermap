Kondaa

Walibi Belgium · Wavre, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 70.2 mph; 164 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2021.

Kondaa is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 8 May 2021. The ride stands 164 ft tall, reaches 70.2 mph and runs 3,937 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kondaa is like

At 164 ft tall with 3,937 ft of track, Kondaa balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2021, it is the 9th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Walibi Belgium.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 8 other coasters at Walibi Belgium, out of 9 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kondaa sits on the south-west side of Walibi Belgium's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tiki-Waka, roughly 150 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mecalodon at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Kondaa compares

On our intensity ranking Kondaa is number 2 of 10 at Walibi Belgium, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kondaa?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Kondaa. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Walibi Belgium, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kondaa?

Kondaa is 164 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kondaa go?

Its top speed is 70.2 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kondaa go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kondaa scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Kondaa open?

Kondaa opened on 8 May 2021.

Who built Kondaa?

Kondaa was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Kondaa?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 3,937 ft of track.

How does Kondaa get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Kondaa at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,080 riders.

Is Kondaa good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 70.2 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
