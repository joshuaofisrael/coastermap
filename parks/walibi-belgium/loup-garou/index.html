Loup-Garou

Walibi Belgium · Wavre, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 91.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Loup-Garou is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 Apr 2001. The ride stands 91.8 ft tall, drops 77.1 ft, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 3395.7 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Loup-Garou is like

Loup-Garou spreads 3,395.7 ft of track across a layout only 91.8 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2001, it is the 5th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Walibi Belgium. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Wooden design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Thundercoaster at TusenFryd. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Belgium (tied with 5 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Loup-Garou sits on the north side of Walibi Belgium's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pulsar, roughly 200 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Vampire at about 210 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Loup-Garou compares

On our intensity ranking Loup-Garou is number 6 of 10 at Walibi Belgium, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 10 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Loup-Garou?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Loup-Garou, according to Walibi Belgium, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Loup-Garou?

Loup-Garou is 91.8 ft tall, with a drop of 77.1 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Loup-Garou go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Loup-Garou go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Loup-Garou scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Loup-Garou open?

Loup-Garou opened on 28 Apr 2001.

Who built Loup-Garou?

Loup-Garou was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Loup-Garou?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 5 seconds over 3395.7 ft of track.

How does Loup-Garou get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Loup-Garou at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Loup-Garou good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Loup-Garou at Walibi Belgium the same as Thundercoaster at TusenFryd?

Both are Vekoma Wooden coasters on RCDB; the Walibi Belgium ride opened in 2001 and the TusenFryd ride in 2001; both list a 120 cm minimum.
