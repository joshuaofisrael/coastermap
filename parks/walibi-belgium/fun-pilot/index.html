Fun Pilot

Walibi Belgium · Wavre, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 24.9 mph; 31.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Fun Pilot is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium, built by Zierer. It opened on 25 May 2019. The ride stands 31.2 ft tall, reaches 24.9 mph and runs 629.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fun Pilot?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Fun Pilot, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Walibi Belgium, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fun Pilot?

Fun Pilot is 31.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fun Pilot go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fun Pilot go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Fun Pilot scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Fun Pilot open?

Fun Pilot opened on 25 May 2019.

Who built Fun Pilot?

Fun Pilot was manufactured by Zierer.
