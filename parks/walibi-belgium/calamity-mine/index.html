Calamity Mine

Walibi Belgium · Wavre, Belgium

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 30.1 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1992.

Calamity Mine is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Walibi Belgium in Wavre, Belgium, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 16 Jul 1992 and was previously known as Colorado. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 30.1 mph and runs 2575.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Calamity Mine is like

Calamity Mine spreads 2,575.5 ft of track across a layout only 45.9 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1992, it is the 2nd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Walibi Belgium. It has run under other names (Colorado), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Mine Train / 785m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Tren Minero at Fantasilandia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm, while Fantasilandia sets 140 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Walibi Belgium (tied with 5 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Calamity Mine sits on the west side of Walibi Belgium's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mecalodon, roughly 160 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tiki-Waka at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Calamity Mine?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Calamity Mine, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Walibi Belgium, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Calamity Mine?

Calamity Mine is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Calamity Mine go?

Its top speed is 30.1 mph (48 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Calamity Mine go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Calamity Mine scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Calamity Mine open?

Calamity Mine opened on 16 Jul 1992 (previously known as Colorado).

Who built Calamity Mine?

Calamity Mine was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Calamity Mine?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 2575.5 ft of track.

How does Calamity Mine get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Calamity Mine good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 30.1 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Calamity Mine at Walibi Belgium the same as Tren Minero at Fantasilandia?

Both are Vekoma Mine Train / 785m coasters on RCDB; the Walibi Belgium ride opened in 1992 and the Fantasilandia ride in 2015; Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm and Fantasilandia sets 140 cm.
