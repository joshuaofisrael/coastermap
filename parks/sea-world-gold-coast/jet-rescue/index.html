Jet Rescue

Sea World (Gold Coast) · Main Beach, Gold Coast, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 43.5 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Jet Rescue is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Sea World (Gold Coast) in Main Beach, Gold Coast, Australia, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 26 Dec 2008. The ride reaches 43.5 mph. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jet Rescue is like

RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2008, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Sea World (Gold Coast).

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jet Rescue sits near the middle of Sea World Gold Coast's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Storm Coaster, roughly 50 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SpongeBob's Boating School Blast at about 70 m. Jet Rescue layout, trains and ride system

Jet Rescue starts with a tyre-driven launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Tire Propelled Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on Jet Rescue's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Tire Propelled Launch
  2. Tire Propelled Boost

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does Jet Rescue go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Jet Rescue go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jet Rescue scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Jet Rescue open?

Jet Rescue opened on 26 Dec 2008.

Who built Jet Rescue?

Jet Rescue was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Jet Rescue launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Jet Rescue at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Jet Rescue good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
