Sea World (Gold Coast) height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Main Beach, Gold Coast, Australia · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Sea World (Gold Coast) in Main Beach, Gold Coast, Australia has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on SpongeBob's Boating School Blast to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Storm Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Sea World (Gold Coast) are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Sea World (Gold Coast) a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Leviathan); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Jet Rescue).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Jet Rescue, which opened in 2008.

Sea World (Gold Coast) publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Sea World (Gold Coast)? By height

At Sea World (Gold Coast) the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride SpongeBob's Boating School Blast (90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Leviathan at 122 cm (about 48 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: SpongeBob's Boating School Blast. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Leviathan at 122 cm (about 48 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: SpongeBob's Boating School Blast. With a companion they can also ride Storm Coaster (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Leviathan at 122 cm (about 48 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Storm Coaster and Leviathan. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Storm Coaster (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)) and Leviathan (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Sea World (Gold Coast)

These are the rules Sea World (Gold Coast) states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Storm Coaster: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • SpongeBob's Boating School Blast: riders 90 to 105 cm (about 35.4 to 41.3 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Sea World (Gold Coast) for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Sea World (Gold Coast), picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: SpongeBob's Boating School Blast, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (105 cm minimum; 13.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Storm Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Leviathan, the most intense coaster in the park (122 cm minimum; 105 ft tall, 49.7 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
