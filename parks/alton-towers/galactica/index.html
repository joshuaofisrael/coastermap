Galactica

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 46.6 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2002.

Galactica is a steel flying roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 16 Mar 2002 and was previously known as Air. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 2755.9 ft of track. On a flying coaster riders are tipped forward so they travel face down under the track, as if flying. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Galactica is like

With 2,755.9 ft of track for a 65.6 ft structure, Galactica is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,755.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 4th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers. It has run under other names (Air), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Alton Towers, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Galactica sits on the east side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Nemesis Reborn, roughly 160 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Runaway Mine Train at about 500 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

How Galactica compares

On our intensity ranking Galactica is number 4 of 11 at Alton Towers, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Oblivion (65 ft tall, 68 mph, no inversions, 4.5 g; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Nemesis Reborn (42.6 ft tall, 50 mph, 4 inversions, 3.5 g; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Galactica

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Alton Towers app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Galactica?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Galactica, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Galactica?

Galactica is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Galactica go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Galactica go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Galactica scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Galactica open?

Galactica opened on 16 Mar 2002 (previously known as Air).

Who built Galactica?

Galactica was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Galactica?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds over 2755.9 ft of track.

How does Galactica get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Galactica at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,500 riders.

Is Galactica good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
