Alton Towers roller coasters

Alton, England · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Alton Towers in Alton, England has 11 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Rita at 68.8 ft and the fastest is Oblivion at 68 mph. The lowest height requirement is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), for Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Alton Towers have?

Alton Towers has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Alton Towers?

Rita, at 68.8 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Alton Towers?

Oblivion, with a top speed of 68 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Alton Towers?

By height requirement, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure has the lowest minimum at 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Alton Towers coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Spinball Whizzer, TH13TEEN, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, Wicker Man, Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies!. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Alton Towers do not go upside down?

Oblivion, Runaway Mine Train, Spinball Whizzer, Rita, TH13TEEN, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, Wicker Man, Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies! have no inversions, per RCDB.
