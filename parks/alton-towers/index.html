Alton Towers height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Alton, England · 11 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Alton Towers in Alton, England has 11 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Nemesis Reborn. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Alton Towers are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Alton Towers a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 45% of its lineup, against 44% across all 139 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 11 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 11 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Wicker Man); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Rita).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Runaway Mine Train, which opened in 1992.

Alton Towers publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Alton Towers? By height

At Alton Towers the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Spinball Whizzer (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Rita (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and TH13TEEN (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Alton Towers for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Alton Towers, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies!, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Spinball Whizzer, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 55.8 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: The Smiler, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 52.8 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Alton Towers?

Roller coaster height requirements at Alton Towers run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) (Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Nemesis Reborn), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Alton Towers have?

Alton Towers has 11 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Alton Towers?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is The Smiler (52.8 mph, 1 inversion).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Alton Towers?

Rita, at 68.8 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Alton Towers?

Oblivion, with a top speed of 68 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Alton Towers for first-timers?

Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies! is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 110 cm (about 43.3 in) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Alton Towers?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies!.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Alton Towers?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Spinball Whizzer, TH13TEEN, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, Wicker Man, Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies!. With a companion they can also ride Runaway Mine Train.

Which coasters at Alton Towers do not go upside down?

Oblivion, Runaway Mine Train, Spinball Whizzer, Rita, TH13TEEN, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, Wicker Man, Bluey the Ride: Here Come the Grannies! have no inversions, per RCDB.
