Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 18.6 mph; 22 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2015.

Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Zamperla. It opened on 21 Mar 2015. The ride stands 22 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 469.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure is like

At 22 ft tall with 469.2 ft of track, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2015, it is the 9th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Alton Towers (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure sits on the north-west side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spinball Whizzer, roughly 50 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Smiler at about 200 m. Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure compares

On our intensity ranking Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure is number 10 of 11 at Alton Towers, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders below the ride-alone height must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or over.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Riders below the ride-alone height must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or over, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure?

Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure is 22 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure open?

Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure opened on 21 Mar 2015.

Who built Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure?

Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure?

RCDB lists 469.2 ft of track.

How does Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure at once?

Up to 14 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 580 riders.

Is Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
