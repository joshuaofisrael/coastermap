Nemesis Reborn

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 50 mph; 42.6 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1994.

Nemesis Reborn is a steel inverted roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 19 Mar 1994 and was previously known as Nemesis. The ride stands 42.6 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,349 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Nemesis Reborn is like

With 2,349 ft of track for a 42.6 ft structure, Nemesis Reborn is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,349 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1994, it is the 2nd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers. It has run under other names (Nemesis), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Alton Towers, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Nemesis Reborn sits on the east side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Galactica, roughly 160 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Runaway Mine Train at about 340 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Nemesis Reborn compares

On our intensity ranking Nemesis Reborn is number 3 of 11 at Alton Towers, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 6th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Nemesis Reborn

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Alton Towers app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Nemesis Reborn?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Nemesis Reborn, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Nemesis Reborn?

Nemesis Reborn is 42.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nemesis Reborn go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nemesis Reborn go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Nemesis Reborn scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Nemesis Reborn open?

Nemesis Reborn opened on 19 Mar 1994 (previously known as Nemesis).

Who built Nemesis Reborn?

Nemesis Reborn was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Nemesis Reborn?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds over 2,349 ft of track.

How does Nemesis Reborn get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Nemesis Reborn at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Nemesis Reborn good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
