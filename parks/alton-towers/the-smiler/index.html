The Smiler

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 52.8 mph; 1 inversion; opened 2013.

The Smiler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 31 May 2013. The ride drops 98.4 ft, reaches 52.8 mph and runs 3838.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding The Smiler is like

It goes upside down 1 time across 3,838.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2013, it is the 8th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Alton Towers, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Smiler sits on the west side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Oblivion, roughly 130 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spinball Whizzer at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride The Smiler

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Alton Towers app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Smiler?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride The Smiler, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does The Smiler go?

Its top speed is 52.8 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Smiler go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is The Smiler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Smiler open?

The Smiler opened on 31 May 2013.

Who built The Smiler?

The Smiler was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.
