Spinball Whizzer

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Spinball Whizzer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It first opened on 27 Mar 2004 and was previously known as Sonic Spinball. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall and runs 1476.4 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinball Whizzer is like

Spinball Whizzer pairs a 55.8 ft structure with 1,476.4 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2004, it is the 5th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers. It has run under other names (Sonic Spinball), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Alton Towers, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spinball Whizzer sits on the north-west side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Octonauts Rollercoaster Adventure, roughly 50 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Smiler at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinball Whizzer?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Spinball Whizzer. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spinball Whizzer?

Spinball Whizzer is 55.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Spinball Whizzer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinball Whizzer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spinball Whizzer open?

Spinball Whizzer opened on 27 Mar 2004 (previously known as Sonic Spinball).

Who built Spinball Whizzer?

Spinball Whizzer was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Spinball Whizzer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 1476.4 ft of track.

How does Spinball Whizzer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinball Whizzer at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 950 riders.

Is Spinball Whizzer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
