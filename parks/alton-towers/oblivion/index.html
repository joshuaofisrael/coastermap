Oblivion

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 68 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Oblivion is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 14 Mar 1998. The ride stands 65 ft tall, drops 180 ft, reaches 68 mph and runs 1,222 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Oblivion is like

At 65 ft tall with 1,222 ft of track, Oblivion balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 180 ft drop is longer than its 65 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. At 87.5°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the 3rd oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Alton Towers, shared with 4 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Oblivion sits on the west side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Smiler, roughly 130 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spinball Whizzer at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Oblivion?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Oblivion, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Oblivion?

Oblivion is 65 ft tall, with a drop of 180 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Oblivion go?

Its top speed is 68 mph (109 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Oblivion go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Oblivion scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Oblivion open?

Oblivion opened on 14 Mar 1998.

Who built Oblivion?

Oblivion was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Oblivion?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 1,222 ft of track.

How does Oblivion get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Oblivion at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,820 riders.

Is Oblivion good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 68 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
