Wicker Man

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.6 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Wicker Man is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 20 Mar 2018. The ride drops 72.2 ft, reaches 43.6 mph and runs 2608.3 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wicker Man is like

Opened in 2018, it is the 10th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Alton Towers, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wicker Man sits on the north-east side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Runaway Mine Train, roughly 180 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Spinball Whizzer at about 380 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wicker Man?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Wicker Man, according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Wicker Man go?

Its top speed is 43.6 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wicker Man go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wicker Man scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Wicker Man open?

Wicker Man opened on 20 Mar 2018.

Who built Wicker Man?

Wicker Man was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Wicker Man?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 30 seconds over 2608.3 ft of track.

How does Wicker Man get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wicker Man at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 952 riders.

Is Wicker Man good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
