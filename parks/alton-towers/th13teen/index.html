TH13TEEN

Alton Towers · Alton, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 41.6 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

TH13TEEN is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Alton Towers in Alton, England, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 20 Mar 2010. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 41.6 mph and runs 2480.3 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding TH13TEEN is like

TH13TEEN spreads 2,480.3 ft of track across a layout only 65.6 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2010, it is the 7th oldest of the 11 coasters we list at Alton Towers. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Multi Dimension Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Uncharted: The Enigma of Penitence at PortAventura Park and Studio Tour at Movie Park Germany. Height rules for this model differ by park: Alton Towers sets 120 cm, while Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Alton Towers, out of 10 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, TH13TEEN sits on the south side of Alton Towers' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rita, roughly 70 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Oblivion at about 450 m. TH13TEEN layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (3 entries):

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill ×2
  2. Vertical Drop Track (with a 16.4 ft drop)

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 196 cm (about 77.2 in).

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for TH13TEEN?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride TH13TEEN. The maximum rider height is 196 cm (about 77.2 in), according to Alton Towers Resort, Height Restrictions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is TH13TEEN?

TH13TEEN is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does TH13TEEN go?

Its top speed is 41.6 mph (67 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does TH13TEEN go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is TH13TEEN scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did TH13TEEN open?

TH13TEEN opened on 20 Mar 2010.

Who built TH13TEEN?

TH13TEEN was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on TH13TEEN?

RCDB lists 2480.3 ft of track.

How does TH13TEEN get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride TH13TEEN at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,100 riders.

Is TH13TEEN good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 41.6 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is TH13TEEN at Alton Towers the same as Studio Tour at Movie Park Germany?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Multi Dimension Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Alton Towers ride opened in 2010 and the Movie Park Germany ride in 2021; Alton Towers sets 120 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm; RCDB lists heights of 65.6 ft and 39.4 ft.
