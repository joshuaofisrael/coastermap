Rollo Coaster

Idlewild & SoakZone · Ligonier, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 27 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1938.

Rollo Coaster is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Idlewild & SoakZone in Ligonier, Pennsylvania, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened in 1938. The ride stands 27 ft tall and runs 900 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rollo Coaster is like

Rollo Coaster spreads 900 ft of track across a layout only 27 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Wild Mouse, roughly 150 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rollo Coaster?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Rollo Coaster. The park lists an adult companion as required, according to Idlewild & SoakZone, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rollo Coaster?

Rollo Coaster is 27 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Rollo Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rollo Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Rollo Coaster open?

Rollo Coaster opened in 1938.

Who built Rollo Coaster?

Rollo Coaster was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the track on Rollo Coaster?

RCDB lists 900 ft (274 m) of track.

Is Rollo Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
