Wonder Woman Coaster

Six Flags Mexico · Mexico City, Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in); top speed 38 mph; 120 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Wonder Woman Coaster is a steel wing roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 1 Jun 2018. The ride stands 120 ft tall, drops 54 ft, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,019 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wonder Woman Coaster is like

For a 120 ft ride, 1,019 ft of track is short: Wonder Woman Coaster is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. RCDB lists a 54 ft drop against a 120 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2018, it is the 9th oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Six Flags Mexico. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's 4D Free Spin / 110 design, and the 7th of them to open. Others include The Joker at Six Flags Great Adventure, The Joker at Six Flags Great America, The Joker at Six Flags Over Texas and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Mexico sets 122 cm, while Nagashima Spa Land sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 122 cm minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Six Flags Mexico, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wonder Woman Coaster sits on the east side of Six Flags Mexico's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman The Ride, roughly 120 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Superman el Último Escape at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wonder Woman Coaster?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Wonder Woman Coaster, according to Six Flags Mexico, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wonder Woman Coaster?

Wonder Woman Coaster is 120 ft tall, with a drop of 54 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wonder Woman Coaster go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wonder Woman Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wonder Woman Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wonder Woman Coaster open?

Wonder Woman Coaster opened on 1 Jun 2018.

Who built Wonder Woman Coaster?

Wonder Woman Coaster was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the track on Wonder Woman Coaster?

RCDB lists 1,019 ft (311 m) of track.

How does Wonder Woman Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wonder Woman Coaster at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Wonder Woman Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 38 mph; the minimum height is 122 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Wonder Woman Coaster at Six Flags Mexico the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Both are S&S Worldwide 4D Free Spin / 110 coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Mexico ride opened in 2018 and the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride in 2015; both list a 122 cm minimum.
