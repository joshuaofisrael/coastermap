Speedway Stunt Coaster

Six Flags Mexico · Mexico City, Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Speedway Stunt Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico, built by Vekoma. It opened on 31 Jan 2026. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Speedway Stunt Coaster is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 10 coasters we list at Six Flags Mexico. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Snoopy's Soap Box Racers at Kings Island, Boomerang at Energylandia and Fireball at Furuvik. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm, while Kings Island sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Mexico (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Speedway Stunt Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Speedway Stunt Coaster:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Speedway Stunt Coaster trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 10 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 20 riders.
Riders per train20

For families checking heights: it appears on our 48-inch list and every taller list (54 in) as a ride-alone coaster; a child of 42 in fits its companion band, so they can ride with an adult rather than alone.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Speedway Stunt Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Mexico app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Speedway Stunt Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Speedway Stunt Coaster, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Six Flags Mexico, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Speedway Stunt Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Speedway Stunt Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Speedway Stunt Coaster open?

Speedway Stunt Coaster opened on 31 Jan 2026.

Who built Speedway Stunt Coaster?

Speedway Stunt Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How does Speedway Stunt Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Speedway Stunt Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Speedway Stunt Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Speedway Stunt Coaster at Six Flags Mexico the same as Boomerang at Energylandia?

Both are Vekoma Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Mexico ride opened in 2026 and the Energylandia ride in 2017; both list a 120 cm minimum.
