Six Flags Mexico height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Mexico City, Mexico · 10 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico has 10 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Tsunami to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Batman The Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 9 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Mexico are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Mexico a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 45% across all 134 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 9 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 10 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 4 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tsunami, which opened in 1981.

Six Flags Mexico publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Mexico? By height

At Six Flags Mexico the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 9 coasters, the nearest being Tsunami at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Tsunami (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)), Dark Knight Coaster (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)), The Joker (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)) and Speedway Stunt Coaster (95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Batgirl Batarang at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 9 coasters with a verified rule: Tsunami, Batgirl Batarang, Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker and Speedway Stunt Coaster. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Wonder Woman Coaster at 122 cm (about 48 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 9 of 9 coasters with a verified rule, 4 more than at the height below: Batman The Ride, Superman el Último Escape, Medusa Steel Coaster and Wonder Woman Coaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at Six Flags Mexico

These are the rules Six Flags Mexico states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Tsunami: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Dark Knight Coaster: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • The Joker: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Speedway Stunt Coaster: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags Mexico for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Mexico, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Speedway Stunt Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Dark Knight Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Superman el Último Escape, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 219.8 ft tall, 74.6 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Six Flags Mexico?

Roller coaster height requirements at Six Flags Mexico run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Tsunami) to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) (Batman The Ride), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Mexico have?

Six Flags Mexico has 10 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Superman el Último Escape (219.8 ft tall, 74.6 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico?

Superman el Último Escape, at 219.8 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico?

Superman el Último Escape, with a top speed of 74.6 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Six Flags Mexico for first-timers?

What can a 42-inch child ride at Six Flags Mexico?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Tsunami, Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Speedway Stunt Coaster.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Six Flags Mexico?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Tsunami, Batgirl Batarang, Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Speedway Stunt Coaster.

Which coasters at Six Flags Mexico do not go upside down?

Tsunami, Superman Krypton Coaster, Superman el Último Escape, Dark Knight Coaster, The Joker, Wonder Woman Coaster, Speedway Stunt Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
