Tsunami

Six Flags Mexico · Mexico City, Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1981.

Tsunami is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1981 and was previously known as Catarina Voladora. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tsunami is like

Tsunami spreads 1,181.1 ft of track across a layout only 26.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1981, it is the oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Six Flags Mexico. It has run under other names (Catarina Voladora), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 12 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure, Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom and Tom y Jerry at Parque Warner. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Mexico (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tsunami sits near the middle of Six Flags Mexico's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Joker, roughly 60 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batman The Ride at about 120 m. Tsunami layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Mild (family)

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Tsunami

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Mexico app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tsunami?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tsunami, or 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Six Flags Mexico, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tsunami?

Tsunami is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tsunami go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tsunami go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tsunami scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Tsunami open?

Tsunami opened in 1981 (previously known as Catarina Voladora).

Who built Tsunami?

Tsunami was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Tsunami?

RCDB lists 1181.1 ft of track.

How does Tsunami get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tsunami at once?

Up to 30 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,250 riders.

Is Tsunami good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico the same as #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Mexico ride opened in 1981 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1976; both list a 120 cm minimum.
