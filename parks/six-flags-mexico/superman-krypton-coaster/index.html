Superman Krypton Coaster

Six Flags Mexico · Mexico City, Mexico

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Superman Krypton Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1993 and was previously known as Roller Skater and later Roller. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Superman Krypton Coaster is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Superman Krypton Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1993, it is the 3rd oldest of the 10 coasters we list at Six Flags Mexico. It has run under other names (Roller Skater, then Roller), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America and Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Superman Krypton Coaster sits on the south-west side of Six Flags Mexico's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dark Knight Coaster, roughly 90 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Medusa Steel Coaster at about 190 m. Superman Krypton Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How Superman Krypton Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Superman Krypton Coaster is number 9 of 10 at Six Flags Mexico, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Speedway Stunt Coaster (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Tsunami (26.3 ft tall, 22.4 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Superman Krypton Coaster?

Superman Krypton Coaster is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Superman Krypton Coaster go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Superman Krypton Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Superman Krypton Coaster open?

Superman Krypton Coaster opened in 1993 (previously known as Roller Skater and later Roller).

Who built Superman Krypton Coaster?

Superman Krypton Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Superman Krypton Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 44 seconds over 679.1 ft of track.

How does Superman Krypton Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Superman Krypton Coaster at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Superman Krypton Coaster at Six Flags Mexico the same as Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Mexico ride opened in 1993 and the Disneyland ride in 1993.
