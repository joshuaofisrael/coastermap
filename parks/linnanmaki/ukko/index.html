Ukko

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 65.3 mph; 151.6 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2011.

Ukko is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 27 May 2011. The ride stands 151.6 ft tall, reaches 65.3 mph and runs 492.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Ukko is like

Ukko packs its 492.2 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (151.6 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. With 2 inversions in 492.2 ft, it flips riders roughly every 246 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2011, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's SkyLoop / XT 150 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Sky Wheel at Skyline Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Linnanmäki sets 140 cm, while Skyline Park sets 150 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Linnanmäki, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ukko sits on the north-east side of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Vuoristorata, roughly 70 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kirnu at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Ukko compares

On our intensity ranking Ukko is number 2 of 8 at Linnanmäki, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Kirnu (82 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions, 2.6 g; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Taiga (170.6 ft tall, 65.9 mph, 4 inversions; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Ukko

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Linnanmäki app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ukko?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Ukko, according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ukko?

Ukko is 151.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ukko go?

Its top speed is 65.3 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ukko go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Ukko scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ukko open?

Ukko opened on 27 May 2011.

Who built Ukko?

Ukko was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Ukko?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 492.2 ft of track.

How does Ukko get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical chain lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ukko at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 550 riders.

Is Ukko good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 65.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Ukko at Linnanmäki the same as Sky Wheel at Skyline Park?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH SkyLoop / XT 150 coasters on RCDB; the Linnanmäki ride opened in 2011 and the Skyline Park ride in 2004; Linnanmäki sets 140 cm and Skyline Park sets 150 cm.
