Taiga

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 65.9 mph; 170.6 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2019.

Taiga is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 18 Jun 2019. The ride stands 170.6 ft tall, drops 105 ft, reaches 65.9 mph and runs 3,622 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Taiga is like

At 170.6 ft tall with 3,622 ft of track, Taiga balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. A free fall of 105 ft would give about 56 mph; Taiga reaches 65.9 mph because its launch adds the speed rather than the drop. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,622 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2019, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Linnanmäki, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Taiga sits near the middle of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tulireki, roughly 60 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kirnu at about 90 m. Taiga layout, trains and ride system

Taiga starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists 7 track elements for Taiga, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (rolling)
  2. Zero-G Winder
  3. LSM Boost
  4. Top Hat - Outside (170.6 ft tall)
  5. Zero-G Stall
  6. Immelmann
  7. Barrel Roll

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Taiga compares

Before you ride Taiga

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Linnanmäki app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Taiga?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Taiga. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Taiga?

Taiga is 170.6 ft tall, with a drop of 105 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Taiga go?

Its top speed is 65.9 mph (106 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Taiga go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Taiga scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Taiga open?

Taiga opened on 18 Jun 2019.

Who built Taiga?

Taiga was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Taiga?

RCDB lists 3,622 ft (1,104 m) of track.

Does Taiga launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Taiga at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Taiga good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 65.9 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
