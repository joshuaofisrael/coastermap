Linnanmäki height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Helsinki, Finland · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Linnunrata to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Vuoristorata. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Linnanmäki are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Linnanmäki a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 44% across all 139 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Vuoristorata); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Taiga).

The most common builder is Maurer Rides GmbH, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Vuoristorata, which opened in 1951.

Linnanmäki publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • Linnunrata: Riders must be over 6 to ride without a guardian.
  • Vuoristorata: Riders 120 to 140 cm must ride with a guardian.
  • Pikajuna: Riding without a guardian also requires being over 10.
  • Tulireki: Riding alone requires being over 8; riding with a guardian requires being over 6.

What can my child ride at Linnanmäki? By height

At Linnanmäki the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Linnunrata (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Kirnu (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Salama (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Taiga (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Linnanmäki

These are the rules Linnanmäki states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Vuoristorata: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Pikajuna: riders 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Tulireki: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Salama: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Linnunrata: Riders must be over 6 to ride without a guardian.

Best coasters at Linnanmäki for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Linnanmäki, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Pikajuna, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; 23 ft tall, 23 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Salama, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 55.8 ft tall, 37.3 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Taiga, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 170.6 ft tall, 65.9 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
