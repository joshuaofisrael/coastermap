Kirnu

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 82 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Kirnu is a steel wing roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 27 Apr 2007. The ride stands 82 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 465.9 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kirnu is like

For a 82 ft ride, 465.9 ft of track is short: Kirnu is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.6 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Linnanmäki, shared with 5 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Kirnu sits near the middle of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Linnunrata, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Taiga at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Kirnu?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Kirnu. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Kirnu?

Kirnu is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Kirnu go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Kirnu go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Kirnu scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Kirnu open?

Kirnu opened on 27 Apr 2007.

Who built Kirnu?

Kirnu was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
