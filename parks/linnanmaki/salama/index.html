Salama

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Salama is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 25 Apr 2008. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Salama is like

At 55.8 ft tall with 1,377.9 ft of track, Salama balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2008, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand and Dragon's Fury at Chessington. Height rules for this model differ by park: Linnanmäki sets 140 cm, while Chessington sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Linnanmäki, shared with 5 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Salama sits on the south side of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pikajuna, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Linnunrata at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Salama

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Salama?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Salama, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Salama?

Salama is 55.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Salama go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Salama go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Salama scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Salama open?

Salama opened on 25 Apr 2008.

Who built Salama?

Salama was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

Is Salama at Linnanmäki the same as Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 coasters on RCDB; the Linnanmäki ride opened in 2008 and the Phantasialand ride in 2002; both list a 140 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 55.8 ft and 57.1 ft.
