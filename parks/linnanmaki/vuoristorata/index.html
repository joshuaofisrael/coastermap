Vuoristorata

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 75.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1951.

Vuoristorata is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland. It opened on 13 Jul 1951. The ride stands 75.2 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 3149.6 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vuoristorata is like

With 3,149.6 ft of track for a 75.2 ft structure, Vuoristorata is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1951, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Linnanmäki, shared with 5 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vuoristorata sits on the north-east side of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ukko, roughly 70 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Kirnu at about 100 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Vuoristorata compares

On our intensity ranking Vuoristorata is number 4 of 8 at Linnanmäki, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vuoristorata?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Vuoristorata, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Riders 120 to 140 cm must ride with a guardian, according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vuoristorata?

Vuoristorata is 75.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Vuoristorata go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vuoristorata go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vuoristorata scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Vuoristorata open?

Vuoristorata opened on 13 Jul 1951.

How long is the ride on Vuoristorata?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 10 seconds over 3149.6 ft of track.

How does Vuoristorata get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a cable lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vuoristorata at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,500 riders.

Is Vuoristorata good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
