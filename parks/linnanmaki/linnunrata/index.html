Linnunrata

Linnanmäki · Helsinki, Finland

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 24.9 mph; 23 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Linnunrata is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Linnanmäki in Helsinki, Finland, built by Zierer. It first opened on 1 May 2000 and was previously known as Space Express and later Linnunrata eXtra. The ride stands 23 ft tall, reaches 24.9 mph and runs 984.3 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Linnunrata is like

Linnunrata spreads 984.3 ft of track across a layout only 23 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Linnanmäki. It has run under other names (Space Express, then Linnunrata eXtra), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Linnanmäki, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Linnunrata sits near the middle of Linnanmäki's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kirnu, roughly 70 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Salama at about 80 m. Linnunrata layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

How Linnunrata compares

On our intensity ranking Linnunrata is number 7 of 8 at Linnanmäki, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 7th tallest of the park's 8 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Pikajuna (23 ft tall, 23 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Tulireki (54.2 ft tall, 36 mph, no inversions, 3.5 g; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Linnunrata

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Riders must be over 6 to ride without a guardian.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Linnanmäki app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Linnunrata?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Linnunrata. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Riders must be over 6 to ride without a guardian, according to Linnanmäki, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Linnunrata?

Linnunrata is 23 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Linnunrata go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Linnunrata go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Linnunrata scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Linnunrata open?

Linnunrata opened on 1 May 2000 (previously known as Space Express and later Linnunrata eXtra).

Who built Linnunrata?

Linnunrata was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Linnunrata?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 984.3 ft of track.

How does Linnunrata get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Linnunrata at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 800 riders.

Is Linnunrata good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.9 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
