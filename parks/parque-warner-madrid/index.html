Parque Warner Madrid height requirements and roller coasters ranked

San Martín de la Vega, Spain · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parque Warner Madrid in San Martín de la Vega, Spain has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 121 cm (about 47.6 in) on Coaster Express to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Batman Gotham City Escape. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Parque Warner Madrid are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Parque Warner Madrid a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 71% of its lineup, against 40% across all 225 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 52 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 7 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Coaster Express); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Batman Gotham City Escape).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Shadows of Arkham, which opened in 2002.

Parque Warner Madrid publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parque Warner Madrid? By height

At Parque Warner Madrid the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Coaster Express (up to 197 cm (about 77.6 in)), Stunt Fall (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Batman Gotham City Escape (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Parque Warner Madrid for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parque Warner Madrid, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Coaster Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (121 cm minimum; 120 ft tall, 59 mph, no inversions).
  2. The big one: Superman: La Atracción de Acero, the most intense coaster in the park (132 cm minimum; 164.1 ft tall, 62.1 mph, 7 inversions).
