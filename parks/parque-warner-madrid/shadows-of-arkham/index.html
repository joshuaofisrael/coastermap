Shadows of Arkham

Parque Warner Madrid · San Martín de la Vega, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 132 cm (about 52 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 105 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2002.

Shadows of Arkham is a steel inverted roller coaster at Parque Warner Madrid in San Martín de la Vega, Spain, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It first opened on 6 Apr 2002 and was previously known as Batman la Fuga and later Batman: Arkham Asylum. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2700.2 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Shadows of Arkham is like

At 105 ft tall with 2,700.2 ft of track, Shadows of Arkham balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,700.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.9 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Parque Warner Madrid. It has run under other names (Batman la Fuga, then Batman: Arkham Asylum), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 11 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Inverted Coaster / Batman design, and the 10th of them to open. Others include Batman The Ride at Magic Mountain, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great Adventure, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Over Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Parque Warner sets 132 cm, while Magic Mountain sets 54 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 54 in and Six Flags Great America sets 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 132 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Parque Warner, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Shadows of Arkham sits on the south-east side of Parque Warner's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Superman: La Atracción de Acero, roughly 160 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Batman Gotham City Escape at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Shadows of Arkham

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 132 cm (about 52 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parque Warner Madrid app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Shadows of Arkham?

Riders must be at least 132 cm (about 52 in) tall to ride Shadows of Arkham, according to Parque Warner Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Shadows of Arkham?

Shadows of Arkham is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Shadows of Arkham go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Shadows of Arkham go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Shadows of Arkham scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Shadows of Arkham open?

Shadows of Arkham opened on 6 Apr 2002 (previously known as Batman la Fuga and later Batman: Arkham Asylum).

Who built Shadows of Arkham?

Shadows of Arkham was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Shadows of Arkham?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 15 seconds over 2700.2 ft of track.

How does Shadows of Arkham get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Shadows of Arkham at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,300 riders.

Is Shadows of Arkham good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 132 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Shadows of Arkham at Parque Warner the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Inverted Coaster / Batman coasters on RCDB; the Parque Warner ride opened in 2002 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1992; Parque Warner sets 132 cm and Six Flags Great America sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 105 ft and 100 ft.
