Batman Gotham City Escape

Parque Warner Madrid · San Martín de la Vega, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 64 mph; 147.6 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2023.

Batman Gotham City Escape is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parque Warner Madrid in San Martín de la Vega, Spain, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 13 May 2023. The ride stands 147.6 ft tall, reaches 64 mph and runs 3313.6 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Batman Gotham City Escape is like

Batman Gotham City Escape pairs a 147.6 ft structure with 3,313.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 3,313.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2023, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Parque Warner Madrid.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Parque Warner. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Batman Gotham City Escape sits near the middle of Parque Warner's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Shadows of Arkham, roughly 180 m away (132 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tom y Jerry at about 220 m. Batman Gotham City Escape layout, trains and ride system

Batman Gotham City Escape starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (LSM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 10 elements RCDB records on Batman Gotham City Escape's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 37.3 mph in 2 s)
  2. Corkscrew
  3. LSM Boost
  4. Top Hat - Outside
  5. Reverse Sidewinder
  6. Corkscrew
  7. LSM Boost (from 43.5 mph - 55.9 mph in 1.1 s)
  8. Zero-G Stall
  9. Vertical Rollback
  10. Switch Track

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Batman Gotham City Escape compares

On our intensity ranking Batman Gotham City Escape is number 2 of 7 at Parque Warner Madrid, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Batman Gotham City Escape

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Parque Warner Madrid app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Batman Gotham City Escape?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Batman Gotham City Escape. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Parque Warner Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Batman Gotham City Escape?

Batman Gotham City Escape is 147.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Batman Gotham City Escape go?

Its top speed is 64 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Batman Gotham City Escape go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Batman Gotham City Escape scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Batman Gotham City Escape open?

Batman Gotham City Escape opened on 13 May 2023.

Who built Batman Gotham City Escape?

Batman Gotham City Escape was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Batman Gotham City Escape?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 51 seconds over 3313.6 ft of track.

Does Batman Gotham City Escape launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Batman Gotham City Escape at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Batman Gotham City Escape good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 64 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
