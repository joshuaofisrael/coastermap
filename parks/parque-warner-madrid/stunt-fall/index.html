Stunt Fall

Parque Warner Madrid · San Martín de la Vega, Spain

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 137 cm (about 53.9 in); top speed 61.5 mph; 177.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2002.

Stunt Fall is a steel inverted roller coaster at Parque Warner Madrid in San Martín de la Vega, Spain, built by Vekoma. It opened on 8 Aug 2002. The ride stands 177.2 ft tall, reaches 61.5 mph and runs 1204.1 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Stunt Fall is like

Stunt Fall packs its 1,204.1 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (177.2 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,204.1 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Parque Warner Madrid. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Giant Inverted Boomerang design, and the first of them to open. Others include Aftershock at Silverwood. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 137 cm.

A rider who just meets its 137 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Parque Warner, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Stunt Fall sits on the north-east side of Parque Warner's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Batman Gotham City Escape, roughly 270 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Shadows of Arkham at about 370 m. Stunt Fall layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a vertical catch-car lift.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 137 cm (about 53.9 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Stunt Fall

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 137 cm (about 53.9 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Stunt Fall?

Riders must be at least 137 cm (about 53.9 in) tall to ride Stunt Fall. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Parque Warner Madrid, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Stunt Fall?

Stunt Fall is 177.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Stunt Fall go?

Its top speed is 61.5 mph (99 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Stunt Fall go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Stunt Fall scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Stunt Fall open?

Stunt Fall opened on 8 Aug 2002.

Who built Stunt Fall?

Stunt Fall was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Stunt Fall?

RCDB lists 1204.1 ft of track.

How does Stunt Fall get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical catch-car lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Stunt Fall at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 870 riders.

Is Stunt Fall good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 61.5 mph; the minimum height is 137 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Stunt Fall at Parque Warner the same as Aftershock at Silverwood?

Both are Vekoma Giant Inverted Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Parque Warner ride opened in 2002 and the Silverwood ride in 2008; both list a 137 cm minimum.
