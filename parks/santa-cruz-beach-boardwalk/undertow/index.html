Undertow

Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk · Santa Cruz, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 50 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Undertow is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk in Santa Cruz, California, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 19 Oct 2013. The ride stands 50 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 1,410 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Undertow is like

Undertow pairs a 50 ft structure with 1,410 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2013, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Laff Trakk at Hersheypark, Spider at Lagoon, Phoenix at Adventureland and Steel Dragon at Waldameer. Height rules for this model differ by park: Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 42 in, Lagoon sets 50 in and Waldameer sets 50 in.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Undertow sits on the west side of Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Giant Dipper, roughly 250 m away (50 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sea Serpent at about 390 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Undertow

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Undertow?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Undertow. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk, official height requirements page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Undertow?

Undertow is 50 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Undertow go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Undertow go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Undertow scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Undertow open?

Undertow opened on 19 Oct 2013.

Who built Undertow?

Undertow was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

Is Undertow at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk the same as Spider at Lagoon?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 2000 coasters on RCDB; the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk ride opened in 2013 and the Lagoon ride in 2003; Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk sets 48 in and Lagoon sets 50 in; RCDB lists heights of 50 ft and 50.8 ft.
