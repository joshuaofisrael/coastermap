Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Santa Cruz, California · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk in Santa Cruz, California has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Sea Serpent to 50 in (127 cm) on Giant Dipper. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 38% of coasters across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Giant Dipper).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Giant Dipper, which opened in 1924.

What can my child ride at Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk? By height

At Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
