Ferrari Land roller coasters

Salou, Spain · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Ferrari Land in Salou, Spain has 2 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Red Force at 367.3 ft, which is also the fastest at 111.8 mph. The lowest height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), for Junior Red Force.
