Sky Rocket

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 55 in (140 cm), or 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) with a companion; top speed 50 mph; 95 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2010.

Sky Rocket is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 29 Jun 2010. The ride stands 95 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,100 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Sky Rocket is like

At 95 ft tall with 2,100 ft of track, Sky Rocket balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 2,100 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2010, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Kennywood.

A rider who just meets its 55 in minimum can also ride 5 other coasters at Kennywood, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sky Rocket sits on the south side of Kennywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Jack Rabbit, roughly 130 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Racer at about 240 m. Sky Rocket layout, trains and ride system

Sky Rocket starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 5 track elements for Sky Rocket, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 50 mph in 3 s)
  2. Top Hat - Outside
  3. Cutback
  4. Zero-G Roll
  5. Corkscrew

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 55 in (140 cm) tall, or 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Sky Rocket compares

On our intensity ranking Sky Rocket is number 2 of 8 at Kennywood, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Phantom's Revenge (160 ft tall, 85 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Steel Curtain (220 ft tall, 75 mph, 8 inversions; 52 in minimum).

Before you ride Sky Rocket

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 55 in (140 cm), and riders from 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Kennywood app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sky Rocket?

Riders must be at least 55 in (140 cm) tall to ride Sky Rocket, or 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) with a companion, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sky Rocket?

Sky Rocket is 95 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sky Rocket go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sky Rocket go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Sky Rocket scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Sky Rocket open?

Sky Rocket opened on 29 Jun 2010.

Who built Sky Rocket?

Sky Rocket was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the ride on Sky Rocket?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 5 seconds over 2,100 ft of track.

Does Sky Rocket launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Sky Rocket at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Sky Rocket good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 55 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
