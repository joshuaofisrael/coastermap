Jack Rabbit

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 45 mph; 40 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1920.

Jack Rabbit is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania. It opened on 18 Jun 1920. The ride stands 40 ft tall, drops 70 ft, reaches 45 mph and runs 2,132 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Jack Rabbit is like

With 2,132 ft of track for a 40 ft structure, Jack Rabbit is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 70 ft drop is longer than its 40 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 1920, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Kennywood.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Kennywood, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Jack Rabbit sits on the south-east side of Kennywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Racer, roughly 110 m away (46 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sky Rocket at about 130 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Jack Rabbit?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Jack Rabbit, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Jack Rabbit?

Jack Rabbit is 40 ft tall, with a drop of 70 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Jack Rabbit go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Jack Rabbit go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Jack Rabbit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Jack Rabbit open?

Jack Rabbit opened on 18 Jun 1920.

How long is the ride on Jack Rabbit?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2,132 ft of track.

How does Jack Rabbit get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Jack Rabbit at once?

RCDB's train details give 18 seats per train.

Is Jack Rabbit good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 45 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Jack Rabbit at Kennywood the same as Jack Rabbit at Seabreeze?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Kennywood ride opened in 1920 and the Seabreeze ride in 1920; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 40 ft and 75 ft.
