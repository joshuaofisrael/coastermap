Racer

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 40 mph; 72.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1927.

Racer is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania. It opened in 1927. The ride stands 72.5 ft tall, drops 50 ft, reaches 40 mph and runs 4,500 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Racer?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Racer, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Racer?

Racer is 72.5 ft tall, with a drop of 50 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Racer go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Racer open?

Racer opened on 1927.
