Racer

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 40 mph; 72.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1927.

Racer is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania. It opened in 1927. The ride stands 72.5 ft tall, drops 50 ft, reaches 40 mph and runs 4,500 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Racer is like

With 4,500 ft of track for a 72.5 ft structure, Racer is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a 50 ft drop against a 72.5 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 1927, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Kennywood.

Its 46 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Kennywood, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Racer sits on the south-east side of Kennywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steel Curtain, roughly 50 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jack Rabbit at about 110 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Racer compares

On our intensity ranking Racer is number 5 of 8 at Kennywood, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Jack Rabbit (40 ft tall, 45 mph, no inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Thunderbolt (70 ft tall, 55 mph, no inversions; 52 in minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Racer?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Racer, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Racer?

Racer is 72.5 ft tall, with a drop of 50 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Racer go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Racer open?

Racer opened in 1927.

How long is the ride on Racer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 32 seconds over 4,500 ft of track.

How does Racer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Racer at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 40 mph; the minimum height is 46 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
