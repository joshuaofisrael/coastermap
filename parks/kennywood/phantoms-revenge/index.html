Phantom's Revenge

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 85 mph; 160 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1991.

Phantom's Revenge is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 1991 and was previously known as Steel Phantom. The ride stands 160 ft tall, drops 228 ft, reaches 85 mph and runs 3,200 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Phantom's Revenge is like

At 160 ft tall with 3,200 ft of track, Phantom's Revenge balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. Its 228 ft drop is longer than its 160 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 1991, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Kennywood. It has run under other names (Steel Phantom), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Kennywood, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Phantom's Revenge sits on the north-west side of Kennywood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Thunderbolt, roughly 70 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Exterminator at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Phantom's Revenge?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Phantom's Revenge, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Phantom's Revenge?

Phantom's Revenge is 160 ft tall, with a drop of 228 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Phantom's Revenge go?

Its top speed is 85 mph (137 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Phantom's Revenge go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Phantom's Revenge scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Phantom's Revenge open?

Phantom's Revenge opened in 1991 (previously known as Steel Phantom).

Who built Phantom's Revenge?

Phantom's Revenge was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Phantom's Revenge?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 57 seconds over 3,200 ft of track.

How does Phantom's Revenge get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Phantom's Revenge at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Phantom's Revenge good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 85 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
