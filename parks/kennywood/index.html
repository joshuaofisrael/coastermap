Kennywood roller coasters

West Mifflin, Pennsylvania · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania has 8 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Steel Curtain at 220 ft and the fastest is Phantom's Revenge at 85 mph. The lowest height requirement is 46 in (117 cm), for Racer.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Kennywood have?

Kennywood has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kennywood?

Steel Curtain, at 220 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kennywood?

Phantom's Revenge, with a top speed of 85 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Kennywood?

By height requirement, Racer has the lowest minimum at 46 in (117 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Kennywood coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Jack Rabbit, Racer, Phantom's Revenge. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Kennywood do not go upside down?

Lil' Phantom, Exterminator, Jack Rabbit, Racer, Thunderbolt, Phantom's Revenge have no inversions, per RCDB.
