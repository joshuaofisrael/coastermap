Kennywood height requirements and roller coasters ranked

West Mifflin, Pennsylvania · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 46 in (117 cm) on Racer to 59 in (150 cm) on Exterminator. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Kennywood are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Kennywood a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 52 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Jack Rabbit, Racer and Thunderbolt); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Sky Rocket).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Jack Rabbit, which opened in 1920.

Phantom's Revenge is the 14th fastest of the 1020 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Kennywood? By height

At Kennywood the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Racer at 46 in (117 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Jack Rabbit (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Racer at 46 in (117 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Jack Rabbit, Racer and Phantom's Revenge. With a companion they can also ride Exterminator (46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Thunderbolt at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Thunderbolt and Steel Curtain. With a companion they can also ride Exterminator (46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm)) and Sky Rocket (52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

A ride here also has an official maximum height: Steel Curtain (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Kennywood

These are the rules Kennywood states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Exterminator: riders 46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Jack Rabbit: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Sky Rocket: riders 52 to 55 in (132 to 140 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Kennywood for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Kennywood, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Exterminator, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (59 in minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  2. Step up: Racer, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (46 in minimum; 72.5 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Steel Curtain, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 220 ft tall, 75 mph, 8 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Kennywood?

Roller coaster height requirements at Kennywood run from 46 in (117 cm) (Racer) to 59 in (150 cm) (Exterminator), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Kennywood have?

Kennywood has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Kennywood?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Steel Curtain (220 ft tall, 75 mph, 8 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kennywood?

Steel Curtain, at 220 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kennywood?

Phantom's Revenge, with a top speed of 85 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Kennywood for first-timers?

Exterminator is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 59 in (150 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Kennywood?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Jack Rabbit.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Kennywood?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Jack Rabbit, Racer, Phantom's Revenge. With a companion they can also ride Exterminator.

Do any coasters at Kennywood have a maximum height?

Yes. Steel Curtain has a maximum of 77 in (196 cm), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026.

Which coasters at Kennywood do not go upside down?

Lil' Phantom, Exterminator, Jack Rabbit, Racer, Thunderbolt, Phantom's Revenge have no inversions, per RCDB.
