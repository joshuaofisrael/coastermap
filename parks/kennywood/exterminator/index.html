Exterminator

Kennywood · West Mifflin, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 59 in (150 cm), or 46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Exterminator is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kennywood in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania, built by Reverchon. It opened on 18 Apr 1999. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall, or 46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Exterminator?

Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall to ride Exterminator, or 46 to 59 in (117 to 150 cm) with a companion, according to Kennywood, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Exterminator?

Exterminator is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Exterminator go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Exterminator go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Exterminator scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Exterminator open?

Exterminator opened on 18 Apr 1999.

Who built Exterminator?

Exterminator was manufactured by Reverchon.
