Vialand height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Eyüpsultan, Istanbul, Turkey · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Vialand in Eyüpsultan, Istanbul, Turkey has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Maceraperest to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Nefeskesen. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Vialand are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Vialand a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 40% across all 217 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Nefeskesen).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Küçük Madenciler, which opened in 2013.

Vialand publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Vialand? By height

At Vialand the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Maceraperest (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)) and Nefeskesen (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Vialand for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Vialand, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Maceraperest, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Küçük Madenciler, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Nefeskesen, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 164.1 ft tall, 68.3 mph, 2 inversions).
