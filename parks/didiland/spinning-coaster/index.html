Spinning Coaster

Didi'Land · Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 11.2 mph; 20 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2025.

Spinning Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Didi'Land in Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 5 Apr 2025. The ride stands 20 ft tall, reaches 11.2 mph and runs 393.7 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinning Coaster is like

Spinning Coaster pairs a 20 ft structure with 393.7 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Didi'Land.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Didi'Land (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spinning Coaster sits near the middle of Didi'Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Drakkar, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Family Coaster at about 240 m. Spinning Coaster layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Spinning Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Le Spinning Coaster and says its height bands can vary by season.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Didi'Land app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinning Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Spinning Coaster, or 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Le Spinning Coaster and says its height bands can vary by season, according to Didi'Land, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spinning Coaster?

Spinning Coaster is 20 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spinning Coaster go?

Its top speed is 11.2 mph (18 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spinning Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinning Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Spinning Coaster open?

Spinning Coaster opened on 5 Apr 2025.

Who built Spinning Coaster?

Spinning Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Spinning Coaster?

RCDB lists 393.7 ft of track.

How does Spinning Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinning Coaster at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Spinning Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 11.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
