Didi'Land height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Didi'Land in Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Drakkar to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Family Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Didi'Land are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Didi'Land a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 261 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is SBF Visa Group, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Drakkar, which opened in 2000.

Didi'Land publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Didi'Land? By height

At Didi'Land the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Didi'Land

These are the rules Didi'Land states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Drakkar: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Family Coaster: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Spinning Coaster: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Didi'Land for first-timers
