Family Coaster

Didi'Land · Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 12.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Family Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Didi'Land in Morsbronn-les-Bains, Grand Est, France, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 12.3 ft tall and runs 452.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Family Coaster is like

With 452.8 ft of track for a 12.3 ft structure, Family Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2014, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Didi'Land. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Wriggler at Gulliver's Land, Lokolo at Lost Island and Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle. Height rules for this model differ by park: Didi'Land sets 130 cm, while Gulliver's Land sets 120 cm, VinWonders Nam Hoi An sets 120 cm and Winnoland sets 80 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Didi'Land. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Family Coaster sits on the south side of Didi'Land's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spinning Coaster, roughly 240 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Drakkar at about 290 m. Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

How Family Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Family Coaster is number 2 of 3 at Didi'Land, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 2 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Drakkar (no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Spinning Coaster (20 ft tall, 11.2 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Family Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Family Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Family Coaster, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. The park's page for La Pomme describes it as its caterpillar-shaped Family Coaster, according to Didi'Land, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Family Coaster?

Family Coaster is 12.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Family Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Family Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Family Coaster open?

Family Coaster opened in 2014.

Who built Family Coaster?

Family Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the ride on Family Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 55 seconds over 452.8 ft of track.

How does Family Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Family Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is Family Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Family Coaster at Didi'Land the same as Family Coaster at Plohn?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Didi'Land ride opened in 2014 and the Plohn ride in 2006.
