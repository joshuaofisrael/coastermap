Yokohama Cosmoworld height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Yokohama, Japan · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Yokohama Cosmoworld in Yokohama, Japan has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Diving Coaster: Vanish to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Spinning Coaster. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Yokohama Cosmoworld are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Yokohama Cosmoworld a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 38% across all 256 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Senyo Kogyo Co., Ltd., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Spinning Coaster, which opened in 1998.

Yokohama Cosmoworld publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Yokohama Cosmoworld? By height

At Yokohama Cosmoworld the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Companion rules at Yokohama Cosmoworld

These are the rules Yokohama Cosmoworld states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Spinning Coaster: riders 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Family Banana Coaster: riders 95 to 120 cm (about 37.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Diving Coaster: Vanish: Riders must be elementary-school age to 64.

Best coasters at Yokohama Cosmoworld for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Yokohama Cosmoworld, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Family Banana Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 17.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Spinning Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: Diving Coaster: Vanish, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 115 ft tall, 62.1 mph, no inversions, 3.8 g).
