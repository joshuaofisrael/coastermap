Spinning Coaster

Yokohama Cosmoworld · Yokohama, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 29.1 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1998.

Spinning Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yokohama Cosmoworld in Yokohama, Japan, built by Reverchon. It opened on 28 Mar 1998. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 29.1 mph and runs 1377.9 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spinning Coaster is like

Spinning Coaster spreads 1,377.9 ft of track across a layout only 42.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1998, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Yokohama Cosmoworld. It is one of 8 rides on Coaster Map built to Reverchon's Spinning Coaster / Classic design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Barrels O' Fun at Six Flags Great Adventure, Exterminator at Kennywood, Crazy Mouse at Hirakata Park and RidderStrijd at Hellendoorn. Height rules for this model differ by park: Cosmoworld sets 140 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 59 in, Kennywood sets 59 in and Hirakata Park sets 130 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Cosmoworld. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spinning Coaster sits on the south-east side of Cosmoworld's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Diving Coaster: Vanish, roughly 70 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Family Banana Coaster at about 290 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

How Spinning Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Spinning Coaster is number 2 of 3 at Yokohama Cosmoworld, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Family Banana Coaster (17.1 ft tall, no inversions; 120 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Diving Coaster: Vanish (115 ft tall, 62.1 mph, no inversions, 3.8 g; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Spinning Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum 120 cm; riders under 140 cm need a companion aged 16 or older (who pays). Riders must be elementary-school age to 64.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Yokohama Cosmoworld app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spinning Coaster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Spinning Coaster, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Minimum 120 cm; riders under 140 cm need a companion aged 16 or older (who pays). Riders must be elementary-school age to 64, according to Yokohama Cosmoworld, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spinning Coaster?

Spinning Coaster is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spinning Coaster go?

Its top speed is 29.1 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spinning Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spinning Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spinning Coaster open?

Spinning Coaster opened on 28 Mar 1998.

Who built Spinning Coaster?

Spinning Coaster was manufactured by Reverchon.

How long is the ride on Spinning Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 51 seconds over 1377.9 ft of track.

How does Spinning Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spinning Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Spinning Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Spinning Coaster at Cosmoworld the same as Spin Mouse at Greenland?

Both are Reverchon Spinning Coaster / Classic coasters on RCDB; the Cosmoworld ride opened in 1998 and the Greenland ride in 1998; Cosmoworld sets 140 cm and Greenland sets 120 cm.
