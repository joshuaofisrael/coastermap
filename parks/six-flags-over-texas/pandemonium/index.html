Pandemonium

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 42 to 47 in with a companion; top speed 31 mph; 53 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Pandemonium is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on 17 May 2008 and was previously known as Big Spin and later Tony Hawk's Big Spin. The ride stands 53 ft tall, drops 27 ft, reaches 31 mph and runs 1,351 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 42 to 47 in with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pandemonium?

Riders must be at least 47 inches (119 cm) tall to ride Pandemonium, or 42 to 47 inches with a companion, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pandemonium?

Pandemonium is 53 ft tall, with a drop of 27 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pandemonium go?

Its top speed is 31 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pandemonium go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pandemonium scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Pandemonium open?

Pandemonium opened on 17 May 2008 (previously known as Big Spin and later Tony Hawk's Big Spin).

Who built Pandemonium?

Pandemonium was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.
