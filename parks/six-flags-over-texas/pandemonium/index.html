Pandemonium

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion; top speed 31 mph; 53 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Pandemonium is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on 17 May 2008 and was previously known as Tony Hawk's Big Spin and later Big Spin. The ride stands 53 ft tall, drops 27 ft, reaches 31 mph and runs 1,351 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pandemonium is like

At 53 ft tall with 1,351 ft of track, Pandemonium balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a 27 ft drop against a 53 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2008, it is the 9th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas. It has run under other names (Tony Hawk's Big Spin, then Big Spin), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / 420/4 (Extended) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Pandemonium at Six Flags Fiesta Texas and Pandemonium at Mid America Adventure. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 47 in.

A rider who just meets its 47 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Six Flags Over Texas, out of 12 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pandemonium sits on the east side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Aquaman: Power Wave, roughly 60 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Runaway Mine Train at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Pandemonium

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 47 in (119 cm), and riders from 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Over Texas app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pandemonium?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Pandemonium, or 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pandemonium?

Pandemonium is 53 ft tall, with a drop of 27 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pandemonium go?

Its top speed is 31 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pandemonium go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pandemonium scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Pandemonium open?

Pandemonium opened on 17 May 2008 (previously known as Tony Hawk's Big Spin and later Big Spin).

Who built Pandemonium?

Pandemonium was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the ride on Pandemonium?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 51 seconds over 1,351 ft of track.

How does Pandemonium get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pandemonium at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 720 riders.

Is Pandemonium good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31 mph; the minimum height is 47 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Pandemonium at Six Flags Over Texas the same as Pandemonium at Mid America Adventure?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / 420/4 (Extended) coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Texas ride opened in 2008 and the Mid America Adventure ride in 2007; both list a 47 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 53 ft and 48 ft.
