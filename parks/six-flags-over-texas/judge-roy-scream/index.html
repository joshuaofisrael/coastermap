Judge Roy Scream

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 45 mph; 71 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1980.

Judge Roy Scream is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas. It opened on 1 Mar 1980. The ride stands 71 ft tall, drops 65 ft, reaches 45 mph and runs 2,670 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Judge Roy Scream?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Judge Roy Scream, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Judge Roy Scream?

Judge Roy Scream is 71 ft tall, with a drop of 65 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Judge Roy Scream go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Judge Roy Scream go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Judge Roy Scream scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Judge Roy Scream open?

Judge Roy Scream opened on 1 Mar 1980.
