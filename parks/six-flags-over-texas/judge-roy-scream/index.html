Judge Roy Scream

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 45 mph; 71 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1980.

Judge Roy Scream is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas. It opened on 1 Mar 1980. The ride stands 71 ft tall, drops 65 ft, reaches 45 mph and runs 2,670 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Judge Roy Scream is like

With 2,670 ft of track for a 71 ft structure, Judge Roy Scream is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1980, it is the 3rd oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Over Texas, out of 12 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Judge Roy Scream sits on the south-east side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster, roughly 180 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aquaman: Power Wave at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Judge Roy Scream?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Judge Roy Scream, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Judge Roy Scream?

Judge Roy Scream is 71 ft tall, with a drop of 65 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Judge Roy Scream go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Judge Roy Scream go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Judge Roy Scream scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Judge Roy Scream open?

Judge Roy Scream opened on 1 Mar 1980.

How long is the ride on Judge Roy Scream?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2,670 ft of track.

How does Judge Roy Scream get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Judge Roy Scream at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Judge Roy Scream good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 45 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
