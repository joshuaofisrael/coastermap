Mini Mine Train

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 20 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1969.

Mini Mine Train is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened in 1969. The ride stands 20 ft tall and runs 910 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mini Mine Train?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Mini Mine Train, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mini Mine Train?

Mini Mine Train is 20 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Mini Mine Train go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mini Mine Train scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Mini Mine Train open?

Mini Mine Train opened on 1969.

Who built Mini Mine Train?

Mini Mine Train was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.
