Aquaman: Power Wave

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 62.1 mph; 146.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2023.

Aquaman: Power Wave is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 11 Mar 2023. The ride stands 146.3 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 708.7 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Aquaman: Power Wave is like

Aquaman: Power Wave packs its 708.7 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (146.3 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2023, it is the 12th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's PowerSplash / 217m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Pulsar at Walibi Belgium and Giant Splash at Lotte World Busan. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Over Texas sets 48 in, while Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm and Lotte World Busan sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Over Texas, out of 12 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Aquaman: Power Wave sits near the middle of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Pandemonium, roughly 60 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster at about 120 m. Aquaman: Power Wave layout, trains and ride system

Aquaman: Power Wave starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Aquaman: Power Wave?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Aquaman: Power Wave, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Aquaman: Power Wave?

Aquaman: Power Wave is 146.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Aquaman: Power Wave go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Aquaman: Power Wave go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Aquaman: Power Wave scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Aquaman: Power Wave open?

Aquaman: Power Wave opened on 11 Mar 2023.

Who built Aquaman: Power Wave?

Aquaman: Power Wave was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Aquaman: Power Wave?

RCDB lists 708.7 ft of track.

Does Aquaman: Power Wave launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Aquaman: Power Wave at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 950 riders.

Is Aquaman: Power Wave good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Aquaman: Power Wave at Six Flags Over Texas the same as Pulsar at Walibi Belgium?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG PowerSplash / 217m coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Texas ride opened in 2023 and the Walibi Belgium ride in 2016; Six Flags Over Texas sets 48 in and Walibi Belgium sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 146.3 ft and 147.7 ft.
