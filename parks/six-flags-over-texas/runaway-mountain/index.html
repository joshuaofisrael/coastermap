Runaway Mountain

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 65 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Runaway Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Premier Rides. It opened in 1996. The ride stands 65 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 1,500 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Runaway Mountain?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Runaway Mountain, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Runaway Mountain?

Runaway Mountain is 65 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Runaway Mountain go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Runaway Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Runaway Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Runaway Mountain open?

Runaway Mountain opened on 1996.

Who built Runaway Mountain?

Runaway Mountain was manufactured by Premier Rides.
