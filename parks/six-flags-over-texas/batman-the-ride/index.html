Batman The Ride

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 105 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1999.

Batman The Ride is a steel inverted roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 26 May 1999. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,700 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Batman The Ride is like

Batman The Ride pairs a 105 ft structure with 2,700 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,700 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 6th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas. It is one of 11 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Inverted Coaster / Batman design, and the 8th of them to open. Others include Batman The Ride at Magic Mountain, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great Adventure, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Over Georgia. Height rules for this model differ by park: Six Flags Over Texas sets 54 in, while Parque Warner sets 132 cm and Himeji Central Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Over Texas, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Batman The Ride sits on the north-east side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Joker, roughly 130 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mr. Freeze at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Batman The Ride

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags Over Texas app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Batman The Ride?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Batman The Ride, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Batman The Ride?

Batman The Ride is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Batman The Ride go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Batman The Ride go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Batman The Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Batman The Ride open?

Batman The Ride opened on 26 May 1999.

Who built Batman The Ride?

Batman The Ride was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the ride on Batman The Ride?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 2,700 ft of track.

How does Batman The Ride get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Batman The Ride at once?

Each train carries 32 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Batman The Ride good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Batman The Ride at Six Flags Over Texas the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Inverted Coaster / Batman coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Texas ride opened in 1999 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1992; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 105 ft and 100 ft.
