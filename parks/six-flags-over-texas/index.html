Six Flags Over Texas roller coasters

Arlington, Texas · 13 coasters

Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas has 13 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Tormenta Rampaging Run at 309 ft, which is also the fastest at 87 mph. The lowest height requirement is 42 in (107 cm), for Mini Mine Train.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Over Texas have?

Six Flags Over Texas has 13 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas?

Tormenta Rampaging Run, at 309 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas?

Tormenta Rampaging Run, with a top speed of 87 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags Over Texas?

By height requirement, Mini Mine Train has the lowest minimum at 42 inches (107 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags Over Texas coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Mini Mine Train, Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags Over Texas coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Judge Roy Scream, Runaway Mine Train, Runaway Mountain, Mini Mine Train, Titan, Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster, Pandemonium, New Texas Giant, The Joker, Aquaman: Power Wave, Tormenta Rampaging Run. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags Over Texas do not go upside down?

Judge Roy Scream, Runaway Mine Train, Runaway Mountain, Mini Mine Train, Titan, Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster, Pandemonium, New Texas Giant, The Joker, Aquaman: Power Wave have no inversions, per RCDB.
