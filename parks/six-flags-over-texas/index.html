Six Flags Over Texas height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Arlington, Texas · 13 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas has 13 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Mini Mine Train to 54 in (137 cm) on Mr. Freeze. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 11 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 13 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags Over Texas are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 4 as thrill and 7 as extreme, which makes Six Flags Over Texas a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 54% of its lineup, against 52% across all 69 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 10 of the 13 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 13 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Judge Roy Scream).

The most common builder is Premier Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Runaway Mine Train, which opened in 1966.

Titan is the 12th tallest of the 443 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Titan is the 10th fastest of the 442 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Six Flags Over Texas? By height

At Six Flags Over Texas the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 9 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Mini Mine Train (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)) and Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Pandemonium at 47 in (119 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 13 coasters with a verified rule: Mini Mine Train and Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster. With a companion they can also ride Pandemonium (42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm)). Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being Judge Roy Scream at 48 in (122 cm).

Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Mr. Freeze at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 13 of 13 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Mr. Freeze and Batman The Ride. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: The Joker (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and Tormenta Rampaging Run (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Six Flags Over Texas

These are the rules Six Flags Over Texas states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Mini Mine Train: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Pandemonium: riders 42 to 47 in (107 to 119 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags Over Texas for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags Over Texas, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; 16.1 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Pandemonium, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (47 in minimum; 53 ft tall, 31 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Tormenta Rampaging Run, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 309 ft tall, 87 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Six Flags Over Texas?

Roller coaster height requirements at Six Flags Over Texas run from 42 in (107 cm) (Mini Mine Train) to 54 in (137 cm) (Mr. Freeze), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Six Flags Over Texas have?

Six Flags Over Texas has 13 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Tormenta Rampaging Run (309 ft tall, 87 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas?

Which coasters at Six Flags Over Texas do not go upside down?
