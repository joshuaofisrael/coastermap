New Texas Giant

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 65 mph; 153 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

New Texas Giant is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 22 Apr 2011. The ride stands 153 ft tall, drops 147 ft, reaches 65 mph and runs 4,200 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding New Texas Giant is like

New Texas Giant pairs a 153 ft structure with 4,200 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2011, it is the 10th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 10 other coasters at Six Flags Over Texas, out of 12 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, New Texas Giant sits on the west side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Runaway Mountain, roughly 200 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tormenta Rampaging Run at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for New Texas Giant?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride New Texas Giant, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is New Texas Giant?

New Texas Giant is 153 ft tall, with a drop of 147 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does New Texas Giant go?

Its top speed is 65 mph (105 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does New Texas Giant go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is New Texas Giant scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did New Texas Giant open?

New Texas Giant opened on 22 Apr 2011.

Who built New Texas Giant?

New Texas Giant was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on New Texas Giant?

RCDB lists 4,200 ft (1,280 m) of track.

How does New Texas Giant get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride New Texas Giant at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

What restraints does New Texas Giant use?

RCDB lists: individual hydraulic lap bar.

Is New Texas Giant good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 65 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
