Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 16.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Chance Rides. It opened on 10 Mar 2001. The ride stands 16.1 ft tall. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster is like

Opened in 2001, it is the 8th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Chance Rides's Big Dipper / Standard design, and the first of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Michigan's Adventure. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 42 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Six Flags Over Texas (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster sits on the south side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Aquaman: Power Wave, roughly 120 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pandemonium at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster?

Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster is 16.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster open?

Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster opened on 10 Mar 2001.

Who built Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster?

Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster was manufactured by Chance Rides.

Is Wile E. Coyote's Grand Canyon Blaster at Six Flags Over Texas the same as Woodstock Express at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Chance Rides Big Dipper / Standard coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Texas ride opened in 2001 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 2021; both list a 42 in minimum.
