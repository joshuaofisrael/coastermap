Mr. Freeze

Six Flags Over Texas · Arlington, Texas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 70 mph; 218 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1998.

Mr. Freeze is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags Over Texas in Arlington, Texas, built by Premier Rides. It first opened in 1998 and was previously known as Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast. The ride stands 218 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 1,300 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Mr. Freeze is like

For a 218 ft ride, 1,300 ft of track is short: Mr. Freeze is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 1 time across 1,300 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1998, it is the 5th oldest of the 13 coasters we list at Six Flags Over Texas. It has run under other names (Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's LIM Shuttle Loop Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast at Mid America Adventure. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Six Flags Over Texas, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mr. Freeze sits on the north-east side of Six Flags Over Texas' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mini Mine Train, roughly 50 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Runaway Mine Train at about 80 m. Mr. Freeze layout, trains and ride system

Mr. Freeze starts with a linear induction motor (LIM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (LIM Boost) that add speed mid-course.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. LIM Launch (from 0 mph - 70 mph in 3.8 s)
  2. Top Hat - Inside
  3. Vertical Rollback
  4. LIM Boost

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mr. Freeze?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Mr. Freeze, according to Six Flags Over Texas, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mr. Freeze?

Mr. Freeze is 218 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mr. Freeze go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mr. Freeze go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Mr. Freeze scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags Over Texas rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Mr. Freeze open?

Mr. Freeze opened in 1998 (previously known as Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast).

Who built Mr. Freeze?

Mr. Freeze was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the track on Mr. Freeze?

RCDB lists 1,300 ft (396 m) of track.

Does Mr. Freeze launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear induction motor (LIM) launch.

What restraints does Mr. Freeze use?

RCDB lists: individual ratcheting lap bar.

Is Mr. Freeze good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 70 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Mr. Freeze at Six Flags Over Texas the same as Mr. Freeze Reverse Blast at Mid America Adventure?

Both are Premier Rides LIM Shuttle Loop Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Six Flags Over Texas ride opened in 1998 and the Mid America Adventure ride in 1998; both list a 54 in minimum.
