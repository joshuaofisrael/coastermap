Botton's Pleasure Beach height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Skegness, Lincolnshire, England · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Botton's Pleasure Beach in Skegness, Lincolnshire, England has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 100 cm (about 39.4 in) on Runaway Train to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Rockin' Roller. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 3, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Botton's Pleasure Beach are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Botton's Pleasure Beach a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 39.4 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Pinfari, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Big Apple, which opened in 1987.

Botton's Pleasure Beach publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Botton's Pleasure Beach? By height

At Botton's Pleasure Beach the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.
