Rockin' Roller

Botton's Pleasure Beach · Skegness, Lincolnshire, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 49.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2006.

Rockin' Roller is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Botton's Pleasure Beach in Skegness, Lincolnshire, England, built by Fabbri. It opened in Jun 2006. The ride stands 49.3 ft tall. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rockin' Roller is like

RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2006, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Botton's Pleasure Beach. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Fabbri's Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse design, and the first of them to open. Others include Mad Mouse at Tosselilla and Roof Junior Coaster at E-DA. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 130 cm.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Botton's. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rockin' Roller sits on the south side of Botton's' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Apple, roughly 40 m away (100 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Runaway Train at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Before you ride Rockin' Roller

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists Rockin Roller at 1.3m under its ride height restrictions, with no companion option.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rockin' Roller?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Rockin' Roller. The park lists Rockin Roller at 1.3m under its ride height restrictions, with no companion option, according to Botton's Pleasure Beach, official wristbands page (ride height restrictions) (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rockin' Roller?

Rockin' Roller is 49.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Rockin' Roller go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rockin' Roller scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rockin' Roller open?

Rockin' Roller opened in Jun 2006.

Who built Rockin' Roller?

Rockin' Roller was manufactured by Fabbri.

How does Rockin' Roller get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rockin' Roller at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Rockin' Roller good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rockin' Roller at Botton's the same as Mad Mouse at Tosselilla?

Both are Fabbri Spinning Coasters / Power Mouse coasters on RCDB; the Botton's ride opened in 2006 and the Tosselilla ride in 2009; both list a 130 cm minimum.
