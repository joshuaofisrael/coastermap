Pirate's Hideaway

Casino Pier · Seaside Heights, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2007.

Pirate's Hideaway is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Casino Pier in Seaside Heights, New Jersey, built by Wisdom Rides. It opened on 29 May 2007. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pirate's Hideaway is like

Opened in 2007, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Casino Pier.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Casino Pier, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pirate's Hideaway sits near the middle of Casino Pier's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Xolo Loca, roughly 40 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Hot Tamales at about 40 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Before you ride Pirate's Hideaway

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 38 to 48 inches must be accompanied by a paying adult (18+).
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pirate's Hideaway?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Pirate's Hideaway, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion. Riders 38 to 48 inches must be accompanied by a paying adult (18+), according to Casino Pier, official boardwalk rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Pirate's Hideaway go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pirate's Hideaway scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Pirate's Hideaway open?

Pirate's Hideaway opened on 29 May 2007.

Who built Pirate's Hideaway?

Pirate's Hideaway was manufactured by Wisdom Rides.

How does Pirate's Hideaway get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a spiral lift; it has no launch.

Is Pirate's Hideaway good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
