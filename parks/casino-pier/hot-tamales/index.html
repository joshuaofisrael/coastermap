Hot Tamales

Casino Pier · Seaside Heights, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 14.1 mph; 11.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Hot Tamales is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Casino Pier in Seaside Heights, New Jersey, built by L&T Systems. It opened in 2003. The ride stands 11.8 ft tall, reaches 14.1 mph and runs 196.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Hot Tamales is like

At 11.8 ft tall with 196.8 ft of track, Hot Tamales balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2003, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Casino Pier.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hot Tamales sits near the middle of Casino Pier's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Xolo Loca, roughly 10 m away (47 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Pirate's Hideaway at about 40 m.
