Comet Express

Lotte World Adventure · Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Comet Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lotte World Adventure in Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened in 1995. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Comet Express is like

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lotte World Adventure. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Twist and Turn Coaster design, and the first of them to open. Others include Vurang at Hopi Hari and Sahara Twist at Leofoo Village. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lotte World sets 120 cm, while Hopi Hari sets 140 cm and Leofoo Village sets 90 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Lotte World, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Comet Express sits on the south-east side of Lotte World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Atlantis Adventure, roughly 110 m away (135 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is French Revolution at about 440 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Comet Express?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Comet Express. The park lists it as Comet Express (혜성특급). Riders 120 cm or taller may ride; the park also asks for a waist of 38 inches or less, according to Lotte World Adventure, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Comet Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Comet Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Comet Express open?

Comet Express opened in 1995.

Who built Comet Express?

Comet Express was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Comet Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 15 seconds.

Is Comet Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Comet Express at Lotte World the same as Vurang at Hopi Hari?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Twist and Turn Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Lotte World ride opened in 1995 and the Hopi Hari ride in 1999; Lotte World sets 120 cm and Hopi Hari sets 140 cm.
