Lotte World Adventure height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Lotte World Adventure in Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Stone Express to 135 cm (about 53.1 in) on Atlantis Adventure. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Lotte World Adventure are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Lotte World Adventure a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 37% across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 1 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is French Revolution, which opened in 1989.

Lotte World Adventure publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • French Revolution: The park says riders 120 cm or taller may ride. No companion rule is stated.
  • Comet Express: The park lists it as Comet Express (혜성특급). Riders 120 cm or taller may ride; the park also asks for a waist of 38 inches or less.

  • Stone Express: 110 cm or taller may ride; children from 90 cm to 110 cm can ride when accompanied by a guardian.

What can my child ride at Lotte World Adventure? By height

At Lotte World Adventure the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Stone Express (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being French Revolution at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Stone Express (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being French Revolution at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: French Revolution, Comet Express and Stone Express. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Atlantis Adventure at 135 cm (about 53.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Atlantis Adventure. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

A ride here also has an official maximum height: Atlantis Adventure (up to 190 cm (about 74.8 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Best coasters at Lotte World Adventure for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Lotte World Adventure, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Stone Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; 37.7 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Comet Express, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: French Revolution, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 43.5 mph, 1 inversion).
