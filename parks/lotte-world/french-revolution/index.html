French Revolution

Lotte World Adventure · Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 14 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 1 inversion; opened 1989.

French Revolution is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lotte World Adventure in Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 12 Jul 1989 and was previously known as French Revolution2 VR. The ride reaches 43.5 mph and runs 2066.9 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding French Revolution is like

It goes upside down 1 time across 2,066.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1989, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lotte World Adventure. It has run under other names (French Revolution2 VR), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Lotte World, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, French Revolution sits on the north-west side of Lotte World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Atlantis Adventure, roughly 400 m away (135 cm minimum). Next closest is Comet Express at about 440 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride French Revolution

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park says riders 120 cm or taller may ride. No companion rule is stated.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for French Revolution?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride French Revolution. The park says riders 120 cm or taller may ride. No companion rule is stated, according to Lotte World Adventure, official attraction page (checked 14 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does French Revolution go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does French Revolution go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is French Revolution scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did French Revolution open?

French Revolution opened on 12 Jul 1989 (previously known as French Revolution2 VR).

Who built French Revolution?

French Revolution was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on French Revolution?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 2066.9 ft of track.

How does French Revolution get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride French Revolution at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB.

Is French Revolution good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
