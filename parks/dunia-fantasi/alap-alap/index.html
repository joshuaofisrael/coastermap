Alap Alap

Dunia Fantasi (Dufan) · Ancol, North Jakarta, Indonesia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 100 cm (about 39.4 in); top speed 19.9 mph; 19.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

Alap Alap is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dunia Fantasi (Dufan) in Ancol, North Jakarta, Indonesia, built by Zierer. It opened in 1986. The ride stands 19.7 ft tall, reaches 19.9 mph and runs 652.9 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Alap Alap is like

Alap Alap spreads 652.9 ft of track across a layout only 19.7 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1986, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Dunia Fantasi (Dufan). It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Medium design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Drako at Walibi Holland, SOS Tournevis at Parc Astérix, Ladybird at Lightwater Valley and Children Coaster at Nagashima Spa Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Dufan sets 100 cm, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm and Lightwater Valley sets 90 cm.

Its 100 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Dufan, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Alap Alap sits on the west side of Dufan's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Kereta Misteri, roughly 210 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Halilintar at about 330 m. Alap Alap layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Alap Alap?

Riders must be at least 100 cm (about 39.4 in) tall to ride Alap Alap. The park spells it Alap-Alap, according to Ancol Taman Impian, official Dufan ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Alap Alap?

Alap Alap is 19.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Alap Alap go?

Its top speed is 19.9 mph (32 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Alap Alap go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Alap Alap scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Alap Alap open?

Alap Alap opened in 1986.

Who built Alap Alap?

Alap Alap was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the ride on Alap Alap?

RCDB lists a ride time of 58 seconds over 652.9 ft of track.

How does Alap Alap get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Alap Alap at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is Alap Alap good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 19.9 mph; the minimum height is 100 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Alap Alap at Dufan the same as Children Coaster at Nagashima Spa Land?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Medium coasters on RCDB; the Dufan ride opened in 1986 and the Nagashima Spa Land ride in 1983.
