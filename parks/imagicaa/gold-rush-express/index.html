Gold Rush Express

Imagicaa · Khopoli, Maharashtra, India

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 107 cm (about 42.1 in); top speed 47.2 mph; 68.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Gold Rush Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Imagicaa in Khopoli, Maharashtra, India, built by Zamperla. It opened on 18 Apr 2013. The ride stands 68.9 ft tall, reaches 47.2 mph and runs 2329.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Rush Express is like

With 2,329.4 ft of track for a 68.9 ft structure, Gold Rush Express is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2013, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Imagicaa.

Its 107 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Imagicaa, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Rush Express sits on the north side of Imagicaa's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Chhota Bheem The Ride, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Nitro at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 107 cm (about 42.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Gold Rush Express compares

On our intensity ranking Gold Rush Express is number 3 of 4 at Imagicaa, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Rush Express?

Riders must be at least 107 cm (about 42.1 in) tall to ride Gold Rush Express, according to Imagicaa, official ride restrictions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gold Rush Express?

Gold Rush Express is 68.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gold Rush Express go?

Its top speed is 47.2 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gold Rush Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Rush Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Gold Rush Express open?

Gold Rush Express opened on 18 Apr 2013.

Who built Gold Rush Express?

Gold Rush Express was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Gold Rush Express?

RCDB lists 2329.4 ft of track.

How does Gold Rush Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is Gold Rush Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 47.2 mph; the minimum height is 107 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
