Vampire

Chessington World of Adventures · Chessington, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 45 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1990.

Vampire is a steel suspended roller coaster at Chessington World of Adventures in Chessington, England, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 11 Apr 1990. The ride reaches 45 mph and runs 2,200 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Vampire is like

Opened in 1990, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Chessington World of Adventures. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Suspended Coaster design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Iron Dragon at Cedar Point, Ninja at Magic Mountain, The Bat at Kings Island and Vortex at Canada's Wonderland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Chessington sets 130 cm, while Cedar Point sets 42 in, Magic Mountain sets 42 in and Kings Island sets 42 in.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Chessington, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vampire sits on the south-east side of Chessington's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mandrill Mayhem, roughly 110 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon's Fury at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Vampire

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vampire?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Vampire, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 110 to 130 cm must ride with an adult aged 16+, according to Chessington World of Adventures, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Vampire go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vampire go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Vampire scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Vampire open?

Vampire opened on 11 Apr 1990.

Who built Vampire?

Vampire was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the track on Vampire?

RCDB lists 2,200 ft (671 m) of track.

How does Vampire get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vampire at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Vampire good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 45 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Vampire at Chessington the same as Iron Dragon at Cedar Point?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Suspended Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Chessington ride opened in 1990 and the Cedar Point ride in 1987; Chessington sets 130 cm and Cedar Point sets 42 in.
