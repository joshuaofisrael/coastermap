Chase's Mountain Mission

Chessington World of Adventures · Chessington, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2026.

Chase's Mountain Mission is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chessington World of Adventures in Chessington, England, built by Zierer. It opened on 3 May 2026. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Chase's Mountain Mission is like

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Chessington World of Adventures.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Chessington, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Chase's Mountain Mission sits on the north side of Chessington's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rattlesnake, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Dragon's Fury at about 180 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Chase's Mountain Mission layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on Chase's Mountain Mission's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill
  2. Helix

Chase's Mountain Mission trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 8 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in a single row for a total of 16 riders.
Riders per train16

Who is it for?

Before you ride Chase's Mountain Mission

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 90 to 110 cm must ride with an adult (16+).
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Chase's Mountain Mission?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Chase's Mountain Mission, or 90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Riders 90 to 110 cm must ride with an adult (16+), according to Chessington World of Adventures, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Chase's Mountain Mission go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Chase's Mountain Mission scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Chase's Mountain Mission open?

Chase's Mountain Mission opened on 3 May 2026.

Who built Chase's Mountain Mission?

Chase's Mountain Mission was manufactured by Zierer.

How does Chase's Mountain Mission get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Chase's Mountain Mission at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Chase's Mountain Mission good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 110 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
