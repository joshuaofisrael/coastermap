Chessington World of Adventures height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Chessington, England · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Chessington World of Adventures in Chessington, England has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Chase's Mountain Mission to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Rattlesnake. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Chessington World of Adventures are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Chessington World of Adventures a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 20% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 5 go upside down.

The most common builder is Maurer Rides GmbH, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Vampire, which opened in 1990.

Chessington World of Adventures publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Chessington World of Adventures? By height

At Chessington World of Adventures the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Chase's Mountain Mission (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Dragon's Fury at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Chase's Mountain Mission (90 to 110 cm (about 35.4 to 43.3 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Dragon's Fury at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Dragon's Fury, Mandrill Mayhem and Chase's Mountain Mission. With a companion they can also ride Vampire (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Rattlesnake at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Vampire. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Rattlesnake at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides here also have an official maximum height: Dragon's Fury (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Mandrill Mayhem (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Rattlesnake (up to 196 cm (about 77.2 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Best coasters at Chessington World of Adventures for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Chessington World of Adventures, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Chase's Mountain Mission, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Rattlesnake, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 49.3 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Mandrill Mayhem, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 44.7 mph, 1 inversion).

What are the height requirements at Chessington World of Adventures?

Roller coaster height requirements at Chessington World of Adventures run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) (Chase's Mountain Mission) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Rattlesnake), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Chessington World of Adventures have?

Chessington World of Adventures has 5 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Chessington World of Adventures?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Mandrill Mayhem (65.6 ft tall, 44.7 mph, 1 inversion).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Chessington World of Adventures?
