Dragon's Fury

Chessington World of Adventures · Chessington, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 50.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Dragon's Fury is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chessington World of Adventures in Chessington, England, built by Maurer Rides GmbH. It opened on 27 Mar 2004. The ride stands 50.8 ft tall and runs 1,706 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Dragon's Fury is like

With 1,706 ft of track for a 50.8 ft structure, Dragon's Fury is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2004, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Chessington World of Adventures. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Maurer Rides GmbH's Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand, Salama at Linnanmäki, Naga Bay at Bobbejaanland and Tarántula at Parque de Atracciones. Height rules for this model differ by park: Chessington sets 120 cm, while Phantasialand sets 140 cm, Linnanmäki sets 140 cm and Bobbejaanland sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Chessington, out of 4 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Dragon's Fury sits near the middle of Chessington's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rattlesnake, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mandrill Mayhem at about 90 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Dragon's Fury?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Dragon's Fury. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Chessington World of Adventures, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Dragon's Fury?

Dragon's Fury is 50.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Dragon's Fury go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Dragon's Fury scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Dragon's Fury open?

Dragon's Fury opened on 27 Mar 2004.

Who built Dragon's Fury?

Dragon's Fury was manufactured by Maurer Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Dragon's Fury?

RCDB lists 1,706 ft (520 m) of track.

How does Dragon's Fury get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Dragon's Fury at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 950 riders.

Is Dragon's Fury good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Dragon's Fury at Chessington the same as Winja's Fear & Force at Phantasialand?

Both are Maurer Rides GmbH Spinning Coaster / SC 3000 coasters on RCDB; the Chessington ride opened in 2004 and the Phantasialand ride in 2002; Chessington sets 120 cm and Phantasialand sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 50.8 ft and 57.1 ft.
