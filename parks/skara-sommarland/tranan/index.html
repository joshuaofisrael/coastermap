Tranan

Skara Sommarland · Skara, Västra Götaland, Sweden

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 24.9 mph; 65.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Tranan is a steel suspended roller coaster at Skara Sommarland in Skara, Västra Götaland, Sweden, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 29 May 2009. The ride stands 65.6 ft tall, reaches 24.9 mph and runs 1640.4 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tranan is like

At 65.6 ft tall with 1,640.4 ft of track, Tranan balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2009, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Skara Sommarland.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Skara Sommarland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tranan sits on the east side of Skara Sommarland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spinner, roughly 60 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gruvbanan at about 230 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tranan?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Tranan. No companion rule is stated, according to Skara Sommarland, official attraction page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tranan?

Tranan is 65.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tranan go?

Its top speed is 24.9 mph (40 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tranan go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tranan scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tranan open?

Tranan opened on 29 May 2009.

Who built Tranan?

Tranan was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the track on Tranan?

RCDB lists 1640.4 ft of track.

How does Tranan get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tranan at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Tranan good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 24.9 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
