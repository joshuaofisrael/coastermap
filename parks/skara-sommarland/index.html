Skara Sommarland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Skara, Västra Götaland, Sweden · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Skara Sommarland in Skara, Västra Götaland, Sweden has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Tranan to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Spinner. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Skara Sommarland are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Skara Sommarland a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 276 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Gruvbanan, which opened in 1987.

Skara Sommarland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Skara Sommarland? By height

At Skara Sommarland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 3 coasters with a verified rule: Tranan. With a companion they can also ride Gruvbanan (100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in)) and Spinner (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 3 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Gruvbanan. With a companion they can also ride Spinner (120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

A ride here also has an official maximum height: Spinner (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Riders over the maximum are turned away because the restraints are sized for a range of body heights; the limit is about fit, not age.

Companion rules at Skara Sommarland

These are the rules Skara Sommarland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.
