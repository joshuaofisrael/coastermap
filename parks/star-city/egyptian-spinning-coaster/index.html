Egyptian Spinning Coaster

Star City · Pasay, Philippines

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Egyptian Spinning Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Star City in Pasay, Philippines, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 24 Feb 2022. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Egyptian Spinning Coaster is like

Opened in 2022, it is the 2nd oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Star City. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Happy Loops at Energylandia, Kids Spin at Skyline Park, Spinning Out at Beech Bend and Bisværmen at Friheden. Height rules for this model differ by park: Star City sets 42 in, while Skyline Park sets 120 cm, Beech Bend sets 47 in and Friheden sets 120 cm.

Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Egyptian Spinning Coaster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

RCDB lists one element for Egyptian Spinning Coaster:

  1. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Egyptian Spinning Coaster trains and ride system

TrainsSingle train with 4 cars. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 16 riders.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Before you ride Egyptian Spinning Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 42 in (107 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Star City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Egyptian Spinning Coaster?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Egyptian Spinning Coaster, according to Star City, official rides and attractions list (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Egyptian Spinning Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Egyptian Spinning Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Egyptian Spinning Coaster open?

Egyptian Spinning Coaster opened on 24 Feb 2022.

Who built Egyptian Spinning Coaster?

Egyptian Spinning Coaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How does Egyptian Spinning Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Egyptian Spinning Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Egyptian Spinning Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Egyptian Spinning Coaster at Star City the same as Spinning Out at Beech Bend?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX609 3 Loops Model A coasters on RCDB; the Star City ride opened in 2022 and the Beech Bend ride in 2016; Star City sets 42 in and Beech Bend sets 47 in.
