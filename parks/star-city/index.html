Star City height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Pasay, Philippines · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Star City in Pasay, Philippines has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Egyptian Spinning Coaster to 54 in (137 cm) on Star Flyer. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Star City are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Star City a family-leaning park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 33% of its lineup, against 38% across all 249 parks on Coaster Map.

Of the ride types, 1 of the 3 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Star Flyer, which opened in 2007.

What can my child ride at Star City? By height

At Star City the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Star City for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Star City, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Egyptian Spinning Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. The big one: Star Flyer, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 65.6 ft tall, 38 mph, 2 inversions, 4 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
