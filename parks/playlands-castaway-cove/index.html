Playland's Castaway Cove height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Ocean City, New Jersey · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Playland's Castaway Cove in Ocean City, New Jersey has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Pirates Gold Rush to 48 in (122 cm) on GaleForce. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Playland's Castaway Cove are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Playland's Castaway Cove a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 37% across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (GaleForce).

The most common builder is E&F Miler Industries, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Pirates Gold Rush, which opened in 2013.

What can my child ride at Playland's Castaway Cove? By height

At Playland's Castaway Cove the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Pirates Gold Rush. With a companion they can also ride Whirlwind (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being GaleForce at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: Pirates Gold Rush. Companion rules at Playland's Castaway Cove

Best coasters at Playland's Castaway Cove for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Playland's Castaway Cove, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Pirates Gold Rush, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Wild Waves, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 50 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: GaleForce, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 125 ft tall, 64 mph, no inversions, 4 g).
