GaleForce

Playland's Castaway Cove · Ocean City, New Jersey

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 64 mph; 125 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

GaleForce is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Playland's Castaway Cove in Ocean City, New Jersey, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 26 May 2017. The ride stands 125 ft tall, reaches 64 mph and runs 1,269 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding GaleForce is like

GaleForce pairs a 125 ft structure with 1,269 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2017, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Playland's Castaway Cove.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Castaway Cove, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, GaleForce sits near the middle of Castaway Cove's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Waves, roughly 10 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Whirlwind at about 30 m. GaleForce layout, trains and ride system

GaleForce starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on GaleForce's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. Kickflip

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for GaleForce?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride GaleForce, according to Playland's Castaway Cove, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is GaleForce?

GaleForce is 125 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does GaleForce go?

Its top speed is 64 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does GaleForce go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is GaleForce scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did GaleForce open?

GaleForce opened on 26 May 2017.

Who built GaleForce?

GaleForce was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the ride on GaleForce?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 1,269 ft of track.

Does GaleForce launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride GaleForce at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is GaleForce good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 64 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
