Boomerang

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1984.

Boomerang is a steel sit-down roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1984. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Boomerang is like

Boomerang packs its 935 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (116.5 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1984, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde. It is one of 28 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: La Ronde sets 48 in, while Walibi Holland sets 120 cm, Walibi Belgium sets 130 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at La Ronde, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Boomerang sits on the south-east side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Toboggan Nordique, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is La Marche du Mille-pattes at about 150 m. Boomerang layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. Catch Car Lift Hill
  2. Cobra Roll
  3. Loop
  4. Chain Lift Hill

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Boomerang compares

On our intensity ranking Boomerang is number 4 of 8 at La Ronde, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Boomerang

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the La Ronde app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Boomerang?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Boomerang, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Boomerang?

Boomerang is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Boomerang go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Boomerang go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Boomerang scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Boomerang open?

Boomerang opened in 1984.

Who built Boomerang?

Boomerang was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Boomerang?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Boomerang get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Boomerang at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Boomerang good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Boomerang operate before?

RCDB records it at Tivoli (as unknown).

Is Boomerang at La Ronde the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the La Ronde ride opened in 1984 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; La Ronde sets 48 in and Bellewaerde sets 130 cm.
