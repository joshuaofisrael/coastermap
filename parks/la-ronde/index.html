La Ronde height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Montréal, Canada · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

La Ronde in Montréal, Canada has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on La Marche du Mille-pattes to 54 in (137 cm) on Le Monstre. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 8 of them.

What the coasters at La Ronde are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes La Ronde a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 62% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 52 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 8 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Le Monstre).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is La Marche du Mille-pattes, which opened in 1967.

What can my child ride at La Ronde? By height

At La Ronde the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride La Marche du Mille-pattes (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 8 coasters with a verified rule: La Marche du Mille-pattes. With a companion they can also ride Dragon (42 to 52 in (107 to 132 cm)) and Toboggan Nordique (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Boomerang at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Boomerang and Toboggan Nordique. With a companion they can also ride Dragon (42 to 52 in (107 to 132 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Ednör at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 8 of 8 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Le Monstre, Dragon, Vampire, Goliath and Ednör. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Companion rules at La Ronde

These are the rules La Ronde states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Dragon: riders 42 to 52 in (107 to 132 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • La Marche du Mille-pattes: riders 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Toboggan Nordique: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at La Ronde for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for La Ronde, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Dragon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (52 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Toboggan Nordique, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 41 ft tall, 11.2 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Ednör, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at La Ronde?

Roller coaster height requirements at La Ronde run from 42 in (107 cm) (La Marche du Mille-pattes) to 54 in (137 cm) (Le Monstre), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does La Ronde have?

La Ronde has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at La Ronde?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Ednör (109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at La Ronde?

Goliath, at 174.8 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at La Ronde?

Goliath, with a top speed of 68.3 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at La Ronde for first-timers?

Dragon is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 52 in (132 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at La Ronde?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: La Marche du Mille-pattes. With a companion they can also ride Dragon, Toboggan Nordique.

What can a 48-inch child ride at La Ronde?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Boomerang, La Marche du Mille-pattes, Toboggan Nordique. With a companion they can also ride Dragon.

Which coasters at La Ronde do not go upside down?

Le Monstre, Dragon, La Marche du Mille-pattes, Toboggan Nordique, Goliath have no inversions, per RCDB.
