Le Monstre

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 59.7 mph; 130.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1985.

Le Monstre is a wood sit-down roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada. It opened on 20 Jul 1985. The ride stands 130.9 ft tall, reaches 59.7 mph and runs 3996.1 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Le Monstre is like

Le Monstre spreads 3,996.1 ft of track across a layout only 130.9 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1985, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at La Ronde, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Le Monstre sits on the east side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Toboggan Nordique, roughly 190 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is La Marche du Mille-pattes at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Le Monstre?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Le Monstre, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Le Monstre?

Le Monstre is 130.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Le Monstre go?

Its top speed is 59.7 mph (96 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Le Monstre go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Le Monstre scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Le Monstre open?

Le Monstre opened on 20 Jul 1985.

How long is the track on Le Monstre?

RCDB lists 3996.1 ft of track.

How does Le Monstre get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Le Monstre at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Le Monstre good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 59.7 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
