La Marche du Mille-pattes

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 1967.

La Marche du Mille-pattes is a steel sit-down roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened on 27 Apr 1967 and was previously known as Petites Montagnes Russes. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding La Marche du Mille-pattes is like

Opened in 1967, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde. It has run under other names (Petites Montagnes Russes), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mini Mine Train design, and the first of them to open. Others include Mini Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 42 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at La Ronde, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, La Marche du Mille-pattes sits on the south-east side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Toboggan Nordique, roughly 30 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Boomerang at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; La Ronde rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for La Marche du Mille-pattes?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride La Marche du Mille-pattes, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does La Marche du Mille-pattes go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is La Marche du Mille-pattes scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; La Ronde rates it 2 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did La Marche du Mille-pattes open?

La Marche du Mille-pattes opened on 27 Apr 1967 (previously known as Petites Montagnes Russes).

Who built La Marche du Mille-pattes?

La Marche du Mille-pattes was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How does La Marche du Mille-pattes get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride La Marche du Mille-pattes at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is La Marche du Mille-pattes good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is La Marche du Mille-pattes at La Ronde the same as Mini Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mini Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the La Ronde ride opened in 1967 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1969; both list a 42 in minimum.
