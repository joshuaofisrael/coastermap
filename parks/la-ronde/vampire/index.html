Vampire

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 105 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2002.

Vampire is a steel inverted roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 18 May 2002. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 2700.2 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Vampire is like

Vampire pairs a 105 ft structure with 2,700.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,700.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4 g, level with the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde. It is one of 11 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Inverted Coaster / Batman design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include Batman The Ride at Magic Mountain, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great Adventure, Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America and Batman The Ride at Six Flags Over Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: La Ronde sets 54 in, while Parque Warner sets 132 cm and Himeji Central Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at La Ronde, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Vampire sits on the south-west side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is La Marche du Mille-pattes, roughly 290 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Toboggan Nordique at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Vampire compares

On our intensity ranking Vampire is number 2 of 8 at La Ronde, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Goliath (174.8 ft tall, 68.3 mph, no inversions; 54 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Ednör (109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions; 52 in minimum).

Before you ride Vampire

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the La Ronde app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Vampire?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Vampire, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Vampire?

Vampire is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Vampire go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Vampire go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Vampire scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Vampire open?

Vampire opened on 18 May 2002.

Who built Vampire?

Vampire was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Vampire?

RCDB lists 2700.2 ft of track.

How does Vampire get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Vampire at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,400 riders.

Is Vampire good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Vampire at La Ronde the same as Batman The Ride at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Inverted Coaster / Batman coasters on RCDB; the La Ronde ride opened in 2002 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1992; both list a 54 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 105 ft and 100 ft.
