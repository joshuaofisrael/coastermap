Ednör

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2010.

Ednör is a steel inverted roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada, built by Vekoma. It opened on 15 May 2010. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Ednör is like

At 109.3 ft tall with 2,260.5 ft of track, Ednör balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2010, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde. It is one of 15 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 14th of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: La Ronde sets 52 in, while Energylandia sets 140 cm, Heide Park sets 130 cm and Movie Park Germany sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at La Ronde, out of 7 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ednör sits on the north-west side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon, roughly 60 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Goliath at about 220 m. Ednör layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Ednör's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Double In-Line Twist

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Before you ride Ednör

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the La Ronde app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ednör?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Ednör, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Ednör?

Ednör is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Ednör go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Ednör go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Ednör scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; La Ronde rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Ednör open?

Ednör opened on 15 May 2010.

Who built Ednör?

Ednör was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Ednör?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Ednör get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Ednör at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Ednör good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Ednör operate before?

RCDB records it at Six Flags AstroWorld (as Serial Thriller); Great Escape (as unknown).

Is Ednör at La Ronde the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the La Ronde ride opened in 2010 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; both list a 52 in minimum.
