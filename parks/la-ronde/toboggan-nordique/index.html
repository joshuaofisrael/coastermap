Toboggan Nordique

La Ronde · Montréal, Canada

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 11.2 mph; 41 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Toboggan Nordique is a steel sit-down roller coaster at La Ronde in Montréal, Canada, built by Zamperla. It opened on 17 May 2003. The ride stands 41 ft tall, reaches 11.2 mph and runs 885.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Toboggan Nordique is like

Toboggan Nordique pairs a 41 ft structure with 885.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2003, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at La Ronde. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Zamperla's Zig Zag Coaster / 270m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Doo Wopper at Morey's Piers. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at La Ronde, out of 7 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Toboggan Nordique sits on the south-east side of La Ronde's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is La Marche du Mille-pattes, roughly 30 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Boomerang at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; La Ronde rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Toboggan Nordique?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Toboggan Nordique, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to La Ronde, official All Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Toboggan Nordique?

Toboggan Nordique is 41 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Toboggan Nordique go?

Its top speed is 11.2 mph (18 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Toboggan Nordique go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Toboggan Nordique scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; La Ronde rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Toboggan Nordique open?

Toboggan Nordique opened on 17 May 2003.

Who built Toboggan Nordique?

Toboggan Nordique was manufactured by Zamperla.

Is Toboggan Nordique at La Ronde the same as Doo Wopper at Morey's Piers?

Both are Zamperla Zig Zag Coaster / 270m coasters on RCDB; the La Ronde ride opened in 2003 and the Morey's Piers ride in 2005; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 41 ft and 35 ft.
