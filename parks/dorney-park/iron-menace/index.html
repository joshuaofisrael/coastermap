Iron Menace

Dorney Park · Allentown, Pennsylvania

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 64 mph; 160 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 2024.

Iron Menace is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dorney Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 10 May 2024. The ride stands 160 ft tall, drops 152 ft, reaches 64 mph and runs 2,169 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times. Its steepest drop is 95°, past vertical.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Dorney Park rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Dorney Park app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Iron Menace?

Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall to ride Iron Menace. The maximum rider height is 78 inches, according to Dorney Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Iron Menace?

Iron Menace is 160 ft tall, with a drop of 152 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Iron Menace go?

Its top speed is 64 mph (103 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Iron Menace go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Iron Menace scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Dorney Park rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Iron Menace open?

Iron Menace opened on 10 May 2024.

Who built Iron Menace?

Iron Menace was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
