Possessed

Dorney Park · Allentown, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 70 mph; 185 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Possessed is a steel inverted roller coaster at Dorney Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on May 2008 and was previously known as Voodoo. The ride stands 185 ft tall, reaches 70 mph and runs 629.9 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Possessed is like

Possessed packs its 629.9 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (185 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2008, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Dorney Park. It has run under other names (Voodoo), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Impulse Coaster / Twist & Spike (56m) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Six Flags Great America, The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Discovery Kingdom and Steel Venom at Valleyfair. Height rules for this model differ by park: Dorney Park sets 52 in, while Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.

A rider who just meets its 52 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Dorney Park, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Possessed sits on the south-west side of Dorney Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Thunderhawk, roughly 120 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Steel Force at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Dorney Park rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Possessed?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Possessed. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Dorney Park, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Possessed?

Possessed is 185 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Possessed go?

Its top speed is 70 mph (113 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Possessed go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Possessed scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Dorney Park rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Possessed open?

Possessed opened on May 2008 (previously known as Voodoo).

Who built Possessed?

Possessed was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Is Possessed at Dorney Park the same as The Flash: Vertical Velocity at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Impulse Coaster / Twist & Spike (56m) coasters on RCDB; the Dorney Park ride opened in 2008 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2001; Dorney Park sets 52 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in; RCDB lists heights of 185 ft and 150 ft.
