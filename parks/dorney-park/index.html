Dorney Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Allentown, Pennsylvania · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Dorney Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Thunderhawk to 54 in (137 cm) on Talon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 7 of them.

What the coasters at Dorney Park are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Dorney Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 62% of its lineup, against 40% across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 8 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Thunderhawk); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Possessed).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Thunderhawk, which opened in 1924.

What can my child ride at Dorney Park? By height

At Dorney Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Thunderhawk at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Wild Mouse (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Thunderhawk at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Thunderhawk, Steel Force, Wild Mouse and Iron Menace. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Possessed at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Talon, Hydra the Revenge and Possessed. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Wild Mouse (up to 75 in (190 cm)), Possessed (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Iron Menace (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Dorney Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Dorney Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Wild Mouse, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; 49.3 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Thunderhawk, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 80 ft tall, 45 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Iron Menace, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 160 ft tall, 64 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Dorney Park?

Roller coaster height requirements at Dorney Park run from 48 in (122 cm) (Thunderhawk) to 54 in (137 cm) (Talon), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Dorney Park have?

Dorney Park has 8 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Dorney Park?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Iron Menace (160 ft tall, 64 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Dorney Park?

Steel Force, at 200 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Dorney Park?

Steel Force, with a top speed of 75 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Dorney Park for first-timers?

Wild Mouse is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 48 in (122 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Dorney Park?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less. With a companion they can also ride Wild Mouse.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Dorney Park?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Thunderhawk, Steel Force, Wild Mouse, Iron Menace.

Which coasters at Dorney Park do not go upside down?

Thunderhawk, Steel Force, Wild Mouse, Woodstock Express, Possessed have no inversions, per RCDB.
