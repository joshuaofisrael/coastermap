Woodstock Express

Dorney Park · Allentown, Pennsylvania

13 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Woodstock Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dorney Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2000. The ride stands 13 ft tall and runs 262.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
