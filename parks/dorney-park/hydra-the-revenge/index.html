Hydra the Revenge

Dorney Park · Allentown, Pennsylvania

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 53 mph; 95 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2005.

Hydra the Revenge is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Dorney Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 7 May 2005. The ride stands 95 ft tall, drops 105 ft, reaches 53 mph and runs 3,198 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

What riding Hydra the Revenge is like

With 3,198 ft of track for a 95 ft structure, Hydra the Revenge is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 105 ft drop is longer than its 95 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. It goes upside down 7 times across 3,198 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2005, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Dorney Park.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Dorney Park, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hydra the Revenge sits on the north-east side of Dorney Park's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wild Mouse, roughly 170 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Woodstock Express at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Dorney Park rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.
