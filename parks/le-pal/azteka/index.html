Azteka

Le PAL · Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2003.

Azteka is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Le PAL in Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France, built by Soquet. It opened on 13 Apr 2003. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 1640.4 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Azteka is like

At 55.8 ft tall with 1,640.4 ft of track, Azteka balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2003, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Le PAL.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Le PAL, shared with 3 other coasters. Children from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Azteka sits on the north-east side of Le PAL's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tigre de Sibérie, roughly 90 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twist at about 190 m. Who is it for?

It suits riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Azteka compares

On our intensity ranking Azteka is number 4 of 5 at Le PAL, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Azteka

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
