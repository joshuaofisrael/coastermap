Twist

Le PAL · Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 43.5 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2011.

Twist is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Le PAL in Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 16 Apr 2011. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 1,542 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twist is like

Twist pairs a 72.2 ft structure with 1,542 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2011, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Le PAL. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Spinning Coaster / Twist design, and the first of them to open. Others include Dwervelwind at Toverland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Le PAL sets 140 cm, while Toverland sets 120 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Le PAL, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twist sits on the north side of Le PAL's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Azteka, roughly 190 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tigre de Sibérie at about 260 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

How Twist compares

On our intensity ranking Twist is number 2 of 5 at Le PAL, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Fjord Explorer (88.6 ft tall, 49.1 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Yukon Quad (39.4 ft tall, 55.9 mph, no inversions; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Twist

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Le Twist. Under 100 cm not allowed; under 140 cm must be accompanied (one adult over 16 for 1 child).
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Le PAL app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twist?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Twist, or 100 to 140 cm (about 39.4 to 55.1 in) with a companion. The park lists it as Le Twist. Under 100 cm not allowed; under 140 cm must be accompanied (one adult over 16 for 1 child), according to Le PAL, official attraction page (access conditions) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twist?

Twist is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Twist go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Twist go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twist scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Twist open?

Twist opened on 16 Apr 2011.

Who built Twist?

Twist was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on Twist?

RCDB lists 1,542 ft (470 m) of track.

How does Twist get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twist at once?

Up to 16 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Twist good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Twist at Le PAL the same as Dwervelwind at Toverland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Spinning Coaster / Twist coasters on RCDB; the Le PAL ride opened in 2011 and the Toverland ride in 2012; Le PAL sets 140 cm and Toverland sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 72.2 ft and 65.6 ft.
