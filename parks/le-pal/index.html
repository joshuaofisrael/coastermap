Le PAL height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Le PAL in Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Fjord Explorer to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Azteka. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 0 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 1 of them.

What the coasters at Le PAL are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Le PAL a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Yukon Quad).

The most common builder is Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tigre de Sibérie, which opened in 1990.

Le PAL publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

Best coasters at Le PAL for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Le PAL, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Tigre de Sibérie, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (140 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 24.9 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Twist, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (140 cm minimum; 72.2 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Yukon Quad, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 39.4 ft tall, 55.9 mph, no inversions).
