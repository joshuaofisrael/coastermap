Yukon Quad

Le PAL · Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in), or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion; top speed 55.9 mph; 39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2018.

Yukon Quad is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Le PAL in Dompierre-sur-Besbre, France, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 7 Apr 2018. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, reaches 55.9 mph and runs 3280.8 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Yukon Quad is like

Yukon Quad spreads 3,280.8 ft of track across a layout only 39.4 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2018, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Le PAL. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Family Launch Coaster / Juvelen design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Juvelen at Djurs Sommerland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Le PAL sets 140 cm, while Djurs Sommerland sets 132 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Le PAL, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Yukon Quad sits on the south side of Le PAL's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tigre de Sibérie, roughly 340 m away (140 cm minimum). Next closest is Azteka at about 430 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Yukon Quad layout, trains and ride system

Yukon Quad starts with a tyre-driven launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Tire Propelled Boost) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on Yukon Quad's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Tire Propelled Launch
  2. Tire Propelled Boost

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Yukon Quad compares

On our intensity ranking Yukon Quad is number 1 of 5 at Le PAL, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Twist (72.2 ft tall, 43.5 mph, no inversions; 140 cm minimum).

Measured against every coaster on Coaster Map with a listed figure, Yukon Quad is taller than 19% and faster than 74% of them.

Before you ride Yukon Quad

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in), and riders from 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Under 120 cm not allowed; under 140 cm must be accompanied (one adult over 16 for 3 children).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Yukon Quad?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Yukon Quad, or 120 to 140 cm (about 47.2 to 55.1 in) with a companion. Under 120 cm not allowed; under 140 cm must be accompanied (one adult over 16 for 3 children), according to Le PAL, official attraction page (access conditions) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Yukon Quad?

Yukon Quad is 39.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Yukon Quad go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Yukon Quad go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Yukon Quad scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Yukon Quad open?

Yukon Quad opened on 7 Apr 2018.

Who built Yukon Quad?

Yukon Quad was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Yukon Quad?

RCDB lists 3280.8 ft of track.

Does Yukon Quad launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a tyre-driven launch.

How many people ride Yukon Quad at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 770 riders.

Is Yukon Quad good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Yukon Quad at Le PAL the same as Juvelen at Djurs Sommerland?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Family Launch Coaster / Juvelen coasters on RCDB; the Le PAL ride opened in 2018 and the Djurs Sommerland ride in 2013; Le PAL sets 140 cm and Djurs Sommerland sets 132 cm.
