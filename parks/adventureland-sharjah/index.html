Adventureland (Sharjah) height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Sahara Centre, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates · 3 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Adventureland (Sharjah) in Sahara Centre, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates has 3 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on Kukulcan to 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Rocket Cycles. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Adventureland (Sharjah) are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Adventureland (Sharjah) a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 37% of coasters across all 269 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 41.3 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 3 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zamperla, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Kukulcan, which opened in 2002.

Adventureland (Sharjah) publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
