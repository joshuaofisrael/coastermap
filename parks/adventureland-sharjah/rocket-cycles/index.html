Rocket Cycles

Adventureland (Sharjah) · Sahara Centre, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 12 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 29.2 mph; 44.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Rocket Cycles is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Adventureland (Sharjah) in Sahara Centre, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, built by Zamperla. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 44.9 ft tall, reaches 29.2 mph and runs 994.1 ft of track. Riders straddle their seats, motorbike style. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rocket Cycles is like

Rocket Cycles pairs a 44.9 ft structure with 994.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2014, it is the newest of the 3 coasters we list at Adventureland (Sharjah).

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Adventureland Sharjah. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rocket Cycles?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Rocket Cycles, according to Adventureland (Sharjah), official ride page (checked 12 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rocket Cycles?

Rocket Cycles is 44.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rocket Cycles go?

Its top speed is 29.2 mph (47 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rocket Cycles go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rocket Cycles scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rocket Cycles open?

Rocket Cycles opened in 2014.

Who built Rocket Cycles?

Rocket Cycles was manufactured by Zamperla.

How long is the track on Rocket Cycles?

RCDB lists 994.1 ft of track.

How many people ride Rocket Cycles at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Rocket Cycles good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 29.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
