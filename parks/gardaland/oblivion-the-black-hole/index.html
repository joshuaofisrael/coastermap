Oblivion: The Black Hole

Gardaland · Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 62.1 mph; 139.4 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2015.

Oblivion: The Black Hole is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gardaland in Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 28 Mar 2015. The ride stands 139.4 ft tall, reaches 62.1 mph and runs 1856.9 ft of track. The train has no floor, so riders' feet hang just above the track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Oblivion: The Black Hole is like

Oblivion: The Black Hole pairs a 139.4 ft structure with 1,856.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 87°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,856.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2015, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gardaland.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Gardaland, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Oblivion: The Black Hole sits on the north-east side of Gardaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Mammut, roughly 130 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Raptor at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

How Oblivion: The Black Hole compares

On our intensity ranking Oblivion: The Black Hole is number 2 of 7 at Gardaland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Oblivion: The Black Hole

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Oblivion: The Black Hole?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Oblivion: The Black Hole. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Gardaland, official Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Oblivion: The Black Hole?

Oblivion: The Black Hole is 139.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Oblivion: The Black Hole go?

Its top speed is 62.1 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Oblivion: The Black Hole go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Oblivion: The Black Hole scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Oblivion: The Black Hole open?

Oblivion: The Black Hole opened on 28 Mar 2015.

Who built Oblivion: The Black Hole?

Oblivion: The Black Hole was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Oblivion: The Black Hole?

RCDB lists 1856.9 ft of track.

How does Oblivion: The Black Hole get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Oblivion: The Black Hole at once?

RCDB's train details give 18 seats per train.

Is Oblivion: The Black Hole good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 62.1 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
