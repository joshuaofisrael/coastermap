Mammut

Gardaland · Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 31.1 mph; 45.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2008.

Mammut is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gardaland in Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy, built by Vekoma. It opened on 19 Mar 2008. The ride stands 45.9 ft tall, reaches 31.1 mph and runs 3264.4 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mammut is like

With 3,264.4 ft of track for a 45.9 ft structure, Mammut is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2008, it is the 3rd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gardaland.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Gardaland, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mammut sits on the east side of Gardaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Raptor, roughly 80 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Oblivion: The Black Hole at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Mammut compares

On our intensity ranking Mammut is number 5 of 7 at Gardaland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mammut?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Mammut, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion, according to Gardaland, official Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mammut?

Mammut is 45.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mammut go?

Its top speed is 31.1 mph (50 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Mammut go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Mammut scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Mammut open?

Mammut opened on 19 Mar 2008.

Who built Mammut?

Mammut was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Mammut?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 45 seconds over 3264.4 ft of track.

How does Mammut get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Mammut at once?

Up to 36 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Mammut good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.1 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Mammut at Gardaland the same as Mammut at Tripsdrill?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Gardaland ride opened in 2008 and the Tripsdrill ride in 2008; Gardaland sets 130 cm and Tripsdrill sets 120 cm; RCDB lists heights of 45.9 ft and 98.4 ft.
