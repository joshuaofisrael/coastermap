Gardaland height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Gardaland in Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Shaman to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Blue Tornado. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Gardaland are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Gardaland a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 57% of its lineup, against 46% across all 122 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 5 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 7 go upside down.

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Shaman, which opened in 1985.

Gardaland publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Gardaland? By height

At Gardaland the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Shaman at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Ortobruco Tour (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Shaman at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 7 coasters with a verified rule: Shaman and Ortobruco Tour. With a companion they can also ride Mammut (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)) and Dragon Rush (110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Blue Tornado at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 7 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Mammut and Dragon Rush. Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Blue Tornado at 140 cm (about 55.1 in).

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Blue Tornado (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)), Shaman (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)), Raptor (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)), Oblivion: The Black Hole (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Dragon Rush (up to 200 cm (about 78.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Gardaland

These are the rules Gardaland states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Ortobruco Tour: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Mammut: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Dragon Rush: riders 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Gardaland for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Gardaland, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Ortobruco Tour, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Mammut, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; 45.9 ft tall, 31.1 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Blue Tornado, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Gardaland?

Roller coaster height requirements at Gardaland run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) (Shaman) to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) (Blue Tornado), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Gardaland have?

Gardaland has 7 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Gardaland?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Blue Tornado (109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Gardaland?

Oblivion: The Black Hole, at 139.4 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Gardaland?

What is the best coaster at Gardaland for first-timers?

Which coasters at Gardaland do not go upside down?
