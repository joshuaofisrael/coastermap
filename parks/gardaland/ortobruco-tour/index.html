Ortobruco Tour

Gardaland · Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions.

Ortobruco Tour is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gardaland in Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy, built by Pinfari. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Ortobruco Tour is like

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Gardaland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Ortobruco Tour sits on the west side of Gardaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon Rush, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Shaman at about 140 m. Before you ride Ortobruco Tour

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Ortobruco Tour?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Ortobruco Tour, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Gardaland, official Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Ortobruco Tour go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Ortobruco Tour scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

Who built Ortobruco Tour?

Ortobruco Tour was manufactured by Pinfari.

How many people ride Ortobruco Tour at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Ortobruco Tour good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
