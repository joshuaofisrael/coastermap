Shaman

Gardaland · Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 43.5 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1985.

Shaman is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Gardaland in Castelnuovo del Garda, Italy, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1985 and was previously known as Magic Mountain. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 2460.6 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Shaman is like

Shaman pairs a 98.4 ft structure with 2,460.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,460.6 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1985, it is the oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Gardaland. It has run under other names (Magic Mountain), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Python at Efteling. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Gardaland (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Shaman sits on the west side of Gardaland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Dragon Rush, roughly 90 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Ortobruco Tour at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, and no taller than 205 cm (about 80.7 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Shaman compares

On our intensity ranking Shaman is number 4 of 7 at Gardaland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Shaman

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 205 cm (about 80.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Gardaland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Shaman?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Shaman. The maximum rider height is 205 cm (about 80.7 in), according to Gardaland, official Rides page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Shaman?

Shaman is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Shaman go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Shaman go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Shaman scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Shaman open?

Shaman opened in 1985 (previously known as Magic Mountain).

Who built Shaman?

Shaman was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Shaman at Gardaland the same as Python at Efteling?

Both are Vekoma MK-1200 / Double Loop Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Gardaland ride opened in 1985 and the Efteling ride in 1981; both list a 120 cm minimum; RCDB lists heights of 98.4 ft and 95 ft.
