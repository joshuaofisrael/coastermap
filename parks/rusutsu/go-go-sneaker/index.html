Go Go Sneaker

Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park · Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in), or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion; top speed 14.9 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Go Go Sneaker is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park in Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan, built by Togo. It opened on 28 Apr 2005. The ride reaches 14.9 mph. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Go Go Sneaker is like

Opened in 2005, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Togo's Sitdown design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Fujiyama at Fuji-Q Highland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Rusutsu sets 110 cm, while Fuji-Q Highland sets 130 cm.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Rusutsu (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Go Go Sneaker sits near the middle of Rusutsu's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Loop the Loop, roughly 120 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mountain Jet Coaster at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall, or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Go Go Sneaker

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in), and riders from 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Minimum age 4; riders 105 to 110 cm (age 4 or older) may ride with an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Go Go Sneaker?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Go Go Sneaker, or 105 to 110 cm (about 41.3 to 43.3 in) with a companion. Minimum age 4; riders 105 to 110 cm (age 4 or older) may ride with an adult, according to Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Go Go Sneaker go?

Its top speed is 14.9 mph (24 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Go Go Sneaker go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Go Go Sneaker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Go Go Sneaker open?

Go Go Sneaker opened on 28 Apr 2005.

Who built Go Go Sneaker?

Go Go Sneaker was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the ride on Go Go Sneaker?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute.

Is Go Go Sneaker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 14.9 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Go Go Sneaker operate before?

RCDB records it at Kintetsu Ayameike (as Go Go Sneaker); Rusutsu Resort (as Go Go Sneaker).

Is Go Go Sneaker at Rusutsu the same as Fujiyama at Fuji-Q Highland?

Both are Togo Sitdown coasters on RCDB; the Rusutsu ride opened in 2005 and the Fuji-Q Highland ride in 1996; Rusutsu sets 110 cm and Fuji-Q Highland sets 130 cm.
