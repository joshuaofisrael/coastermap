Hurricane

Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park · Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 1996.

Hurricane is a steel inverted roller coaster at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park in Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1996. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2260.5 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Hurricane is like

Hurricane pairs a 109.3 ft structure with 2,260.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,260.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1996, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park. It is one of 18 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 689m Standard design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland, Riddler Revenge at Six Flags New England, Kong at Discovery Kingdom and Thunderhawk at Michigan's Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Rusutsu sets 130 cm, while Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in, Six Flags New England sets 52 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 52 in.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Rusutsu, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Hurricane sits on the north-east side of Rusutsu's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Corkscrew, roughly 50 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Loop the Loop at about 80 m. Hurricane layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 200 cm (about 78.7 in).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Hurricane

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Minimum age 10.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 5 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Hurricane?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Hurricane. The maximum rider height is 200 cm (about 78.7 in). Minimum age 10, according to Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Hurricane?

Hurricane is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Hurricane go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Hurricane go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Hurricane scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Hurricane open?

Hurricane opened in 1996.

Who built Hurricane?

Hurricane was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Hurricane?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 2260.5 ft of track.

How does Hurricane get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Hurricane at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,040 riders.

Is Hurricane good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Hurricane at Rusutsu the same as DareDeviler at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 689m Standard coasters on RCDB; the Rusutsu ride opened in 1996 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1995; Rusutsu sets 130 cm and Canada's Wonderland sets 52 in.
