Corkscrew

Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park · Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in); top speed 39.1 mph; 69.9 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1983.

Corkscrew is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park in Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 10 Jun 1983. The ride stands 69.9 ft tall, reaches 39.1 mph and runs 1254.9 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Corkscrew is like

Corkscrew pairs a 69.9 ft structure with 1,254.9 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,254.9 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1983, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure, Corkscrew at Silverwood, Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land and Magnus at Salitre Mágico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Rusutsu sets 125 cm, while Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in, Silverwood sets 48 in and Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm.

A rider who just meets its 125 cm minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Rusutsu, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Corkscrew sits on the east side of Rusutsu's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurricane, roughly 50 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Loop the Loop at about 130 m. Corkscrew layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

These are the 2 elements RCDB records on Corkscrew's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Double Corkscrew

RCDB counts 2 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Corkscrew

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Corkscrew?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Corkscrew, according to Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Corkscrew?

Corkscrew is 69.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Corkscrew go?

Its top speed is 39.1 mph (63 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Corkscrew go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Corkscrew scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Corkscrew open?

Corkscrew opened on 10 Jun 1983.

Who built Corkscrew?

Corkscrew was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Corkscrew?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 20 seconds over 1254.9 ft of track.

How does Corkscrew get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Corkscrew at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Corkscrew use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Corkscrew good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 39.1 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Corkscrew operate before?

RCDB records it at Yatsu Yuenchi (as Corkscrew); Rusutsu Resort (as Corkscrew).

Is Corkscrew at Rusutsu the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Rusutsu ride opened in 1983 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; Rusutsu sets 125 cm and Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 69.9 ft and 70 ft.
