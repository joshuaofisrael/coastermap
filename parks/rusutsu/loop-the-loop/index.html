Loop the Loop

Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park · Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 110 cm (about 43.3 in); top speed 55.9 mph; 150.9 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 1983.

Loop the Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park in Rusutsu, Hokkaido, Japan, built by Meisho Amusement Machines. It opened on 10 Jun 1983. The ride stands 150.9 ft tall and reaches 55.9 mph. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Loop the Loop is like

At 80°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 1983, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park.

Its 110 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Rusutsu (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Loop the Loop sits on the north-east side of Rusutsu's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Hurricane, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Go Go Sneaker at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Loop the Loop compares

On our intensity ranking Loop the Loop is number 3 of 7 at Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Standing Coaster (82 ft tall, 46.6 mph, 1 inversion; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Ultra Twister (114.8 ft tall, 4 inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Loop the Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 110 cm (about 43.3 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Loop the Loop?

Riders must be at least 110 cm (about 43.3 in) tall to ride Loop the Loop, according to Rusutsu Resort Amusement Park, official attractions page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Loop the Loop?

Loop the Loop is 150.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Loop the Loop go?

Its top speed is 55.9 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Loop the Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Loop the Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Loop the Loop open?

Loop the Loop opened on 10 Jun 1983.

Who built Loop the Loop?

Loop the Loop was manufactured by Meisho Amusement Machines.

How does Loop the Loop get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Loop the Loop at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Loop the Loop use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness, single position lap bar.

Is Loop the Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 55.9 mph; the minimum height is 110 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Loop the Loop operate before?

RCDB records it at World Food Festival (as Loop the Loop); Rusutsu Resort (as Loop the Loop).
