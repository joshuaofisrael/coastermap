Piraten Spinner

Freizeit-Land Geiselwind · Geiselwind, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 18.6 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1994.

Piraten Spinner is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind in Geiselwind, Germany, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1994 and was previously known as Drehgondelbahn. The ride reaches 18.6 mph and runs 328.1 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Piraten Spinner is like

Opened in 1994, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind. It has run under other names (Drehgondelbahn), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Piraten Spinner sits on the south-east side of Geiselwind's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Fluch des Kraken, roughly 80 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Drachenhöhle at about 100 m. Piraten Spinner layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does Piraten Spinner go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Piraten Spinner go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Piraten Spinner scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Piraten Spinner open?

Piraten Spinner opened in 1994 (previously known as Drehgondelbahn).

Who built Piraten Spinner?

Piraten Spinner was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Piraten Spinner?

RCDB lists 328.1 ft of track.

How does Piraten Spinner get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Piraten Spinner at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Piraten Spinner good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
