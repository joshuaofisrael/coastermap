Fluch des Kraken

Freizeit-Land Geiselwind · Geiselwind, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2000.

Fluch des Kraken is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind in Geiselwind, Germany, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 2000 and was previously known as Boomerang. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Fluch des Kraken is like

For a 116.5 ft ride, 935 ft of track is short: Fluch des Kraken is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2000, it is the 3rd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind. It has run under other names (Boomerang), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 26 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 17th of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Geiselwind sets 130 cm, while Hersheypark sets 48 in, Carowinds sets 48 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 48 in.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Geiselwind, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Fluch des Kraken sits near the middle of Geiselwind's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Piraten Spinner, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Silverpony Express at about 160 m. Fluch des Kraken layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

RCDB lists 4 track elements for Fluch des Kraken, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. Catch Car Lift Hill
  2. Cobra Roll

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 190 cm (about 74.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Fluch des Kraken

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

  • Minimum age 10.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fluch des Kraken?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Fluch des Kraken. The maximum rider height is 190 cm (about 74.8 in). Minimum age 10, according to Freizeit-Land Geiselwind, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fluch des Kraken?

Fluch des Kraken is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fluch des Kraken go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fluch des Kraken go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Fluch des Kraken scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fluch des Kraken open?

Fluch des Kraken opened in 2000 (previously known as Boomerang).

Who built Fluch des Kraken?

Fluch des Kraken was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Fluch des Kraken?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Fluch des Kraken get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Fluch des Kraken at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Fluch des Kraken good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Fluch des Kraken at Geiselwind the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Geiselwind ride opened in 2000 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; both list a 130 cm minimum.
