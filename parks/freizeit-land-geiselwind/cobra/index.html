Cobra

Freizeit-Land Geiselwind · Geiselwind, Germany

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); 33 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2018.

Cobra is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind in Geiselwind, Germany, built by Interpark. It opened on 24 Mar 2018. The ride stands 33 ft tall and runs 656.2 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Cobra is like

Cobra pairs a 33 ft structure with 656.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 1 time across 656.2 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Geiselwind, shared with 2 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cobra sits on the north-west side of Geiselwind's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Silverpony Express, roughly 90 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Taka Waka at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

General guidance from how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule. See best seat on a roller coaster.

How Cobra compares

On our intensity ranking Cobra is number 2 of 8 at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Wilde Hilde (68.9 ft tall, no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Fluch des Kraken (116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Cobra

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Children under 12 must ride with a companion.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Freizeit-Land Geiselwind app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cobra?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Cobra. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Children under 12 must ride with a companion, according to Freizeit-Land Geiselwind, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cobra?

Cobra is 33 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Cobra go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Cobra scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Cobra open?

Cobra opened on 24 Mar 2018.

Who built Cobra?

Cobra was manufactured by Interpark.

How long is the ride on Cobra?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute over 656.2 ft of track.

How does Cobra get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Cobra at once?

Up to 10 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 900 riders.

Is Cobra good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
