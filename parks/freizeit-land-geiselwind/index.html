Freizeit-Land Geiselwind height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Geiselwind, Germany · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Freizeit-Land Geiselwind in Geiselwind, Germany has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 105 cm (about 41.3 in) on Doggy Dog to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Cobra. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Freizeit-Land Geiselwind a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 233 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 51.2 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 8 go upside down.

The most common builder is Zierer, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Silverpony Express, which opened in 1989.

Freizeit-Land Geiselwind publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind? By height

At Freizeit-Land Geiselwind the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. Still too short for 6 coasters, the nearest being Doggy Dog at 105 cm (about 41.3 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Doggy Dog. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Drachenhöhle at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Drachenhöhle and Taka Waka. With a companion they can also ride Wilde Hilde (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Cobra at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Cobra, Wilde Hilde and Fluch des Kraken. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Cobra (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)) and Fluch des Kraken (up to 190 cm (about 74.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind

These are the rules Freizeit-Land Geiselwind states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Wilde Hilde: riders 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Doggy Dog: Children aged 3 to 6 ride with a companion.
  • Cobra: Children under 12 must ride with a companion.
  • Drachenhöhle: Children aged 6 to 12 ride with a companion.
  • Taka Waka: Ages 6 and 7 ride with a companion; from age 8 alone.
  • Fluch des Kraken: Minimum age 10.

Best coasters at Freizeit-Land Geiselwind for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Freizeit-Land Geiselwind, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Doggy Dog, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (105 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Drachenhöhle, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (120 cm minimum; 22.7 ft tall, 25.5 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).
  3. The big one: Fluch des Kraken, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 116.5 ft tall, 47 mph, 3 inversions, 5.2 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
