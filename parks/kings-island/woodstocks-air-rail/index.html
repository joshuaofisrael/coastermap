Woodstock's Air Rail

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 44 in (112 cm); top speed 26 mph; 48.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Woodstock's Air Rail is a steel inverted roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 2001 and was previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar. The ride stands 48.6 ft tall, reaches 26 mph and runs 1,122 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Woodstock's Air Rail is like

At 48.6 ft tall with 1,122 ft of track, Woodstock's Air Rail balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.2 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2001, it is the 9th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It has run under other names (Flying Ace Aerial Chase, then Rugrats Runaway Reptar), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 6 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 342m design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Kiddy Hawk at Carowinds, Silver Streak at Canada's Wonderland, The Bat at Lagoon and Kenny's Forest Flyer at Dreamworld. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kings Island sets 44 in, while Lagoon sets 42 in and Dreamworld sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 44 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 46 in (117 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Woodstock's Air Rail sits on the south-west side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, roughly 30 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Great Pumpkin Coaster at about 130 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Woodstock's Air Rail?

Riders must be at least 44 in (112 cm) tall to ride Woodstock's Air Rail. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Woodstock's Air Rail?

Woodstock's Air Rail is 48.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Woodstock's Air Rail go?

Its top speed is 26 mph (42 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Woodstock's Air Rail go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Woodstock's Air Rail scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Woodstock's Air Rail open?

Woodstock's Air Rail opened in 2001 (previously known as Flying Ace Aerial Chase and later Rugrats Runaway Reptar).

Who built Woodstock's Air Rail?

Woodstock's Air Rail was manufactured by Vekoma.

Is Woodstock's Air Rail at Kings Island the same as Silver Streak at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Vekoma Suspended Family Coaster / 342m coasters on RCDB; the Kings Island ride opened in 2001 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 2001; both list a 44 in minimum.
