Banshee

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 68 mph; 167 ft tall; 7 inversions; opened 2014.

Banshee is a steel inverted roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 18 Apr 2014. The ride stands 167 ft tall, drops 150 ft, reaches 68 mph and runs 4,124 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 7 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 7 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Banshee?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Banshee. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Banshee?

Banshee is 167 ft tall, with a drop of 150 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Banshee go?

Its top speed is 68 mph (109 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Banshee go upside down?

Yes. It has 7 inversions.

Is Banshee scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Banshee open?

Banshee opened on 18 Apr 2014.

Who built Banshee?

Banshee was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.
