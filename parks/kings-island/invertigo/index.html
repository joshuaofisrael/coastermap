Invertigo

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 50 mph; 131.3 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 1999.

Invertigo is a steel inverted roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 17 Apr 1999 and was previously known as Face/Off. The ride stands 131.3 ft tall, reaches 50 mph and runs 1013.8 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Invertigo is like

Invertigo packs its 1,013.8 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (131.3 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. With 3 inversions in 1,013.8 ft, it flips riders roughly every 338 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 8th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It has run under other names (Face/Off), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Kings Island, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Invertigo sits on the north-west side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Bat, roughly 300 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Woodstock Express at about 310 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 78 in (198 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Before you ride Invertigo

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 54 in (137 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Invertigo?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Invertigo. The maximum rider height is 78 in (198 cm), according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Invertigo?

Invertigo is 131.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Invertigo go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Invertigo go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Invertigo scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Invertigo open?

Invertigo opened on 17 Apr 1999 (previously known as Face/Off).

Who built Invertigo?

Invertigo was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Invertigo?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 1013.8 ft of track.

How does Invertigo get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Invertigo at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Invertigo good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
