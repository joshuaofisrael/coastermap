The Bat

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 51 mph; 78 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

The Bat is a steel suspended roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened on 9 Apr 1993 and was previously known as Top Gun and later Flight Deck. The ride stands 78 ft tall, reaches 51 mph and runs 2,352 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Bat is like

The Bat spreads 2,352 ft of track across a layout only 78 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1993, it is the 6th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It has run under other names (Top Gun, then Flight Deck), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Suspended Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Iron Dragon at Cedar Point, Ninja at Magic Mountain, Vortex at Canada's Wonderland and Vampire at Chessington. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kings Island sets 42 in, while Chessington sets 130 cm.

A rider who just meets its 42 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Bat sits on the north side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Banshee, roughly 200 m away (52 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Invertigo at about 300 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Bat?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride The Bat, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Bat?

The Bat is 78 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Bat go?

Its top speed is 51 mph (82 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Bat go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Bat scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did The Bat open?

The Bat opened on 9 Apr 1993 (previously known as Top Gun and later Flight Deck).

Who built The Bat?

The Bat was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on The Bat?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 52 seconds over 2,352 ft of track.

How does The Bat get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Bat at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is The Bat good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 51 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is The Bat at Kings Island the same as Iron Dragon at Cedar Point?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Suspended Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Kings Island ride opened in 1993 and the Cedar Point ride in 1987; both list a 42 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 78 ft and 76 ft.
