Woodstock Express

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm), or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 38.6 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1972.

Woodstock Express is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.. It first opened on 29 Apr 1972 and was previously known as Fairly Odd Coaster and later Beastie and later Scooby Doo. The ride stands 38.6 ft tall, reaches 35 mph and runs 1,350 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Woodstock Express?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Woodstock Express, or 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) with a companion, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Woodstock Express?

Woodstock Express is 38.6 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Woodstock Express go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Woodstock Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Woodstock Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Woodstock Express open?

Woodstock Express opened on 29 Apr 1972 (previously known as Fairly Odd Coaster and later Beastie and later Scooby Doo).

Who built Woodstock Express?

Woodstock Express was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc..
