Kings Island roller coasters

Mason, Ohio · 15 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kings Island in Mason, Ohio has 15 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Orion at 287 ft, which is also the fastest at 91 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for The Great Pumpkin Coaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Kings Island have?

Kings Island has 15 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kings Island?

Orion, at 287 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kings Island?

Orion, with a top speed of 91 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Kings Island?

By height requirement, The Great Pumpkin Coaster has the lowest minimum at 36 in (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Kings Island coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Kings Island coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: The Beast, The Racer, Adventure Express, Woodstock Express, The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Mystic Timbers, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, Woodstock's Air Rail, Queen City Stunt Coaster. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Kings Island do not go upside down?

The Beast, The Racer, Adventure Express, Woodstock Express, The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Diamondback, Orion, Mystic Timbers, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, Woodstock's Air Rail, Queen City Stunt Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
