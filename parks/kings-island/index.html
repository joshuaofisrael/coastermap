Kings Island height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Mason, Ohio · 15 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Kings Island in Mason, Ohio has 15 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on The Great Pumpkin Coaster to 54 in (137 cm) on Flight of Fear. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 2, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 10 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 15 of them.

What the coasters at Kings Island are like

RCDB classes 4 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 8 as extreme, which makes Kings Island a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 53% of its lineup, against 40% across all 221 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 11 of the 15 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 15 go upside down; 4 are wooden coasters (The Beast, The Racer, Woodstock Express and Mystic Timbers); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Flight of Fear).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Racer, which opened in 1972.

Orion is the 10th tallest of the 999 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed height.

Orion is the 8th fastest of the 959 coasters on Coaster Map with a listed top speed.

What can my child ride at Kings Island? By height

At Kings Island the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 8 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 15 coasters with a verified rule: The Great Pumpkin Coaster. Still too short for 14 coasters, the nearest being The Bat at 42 in (107 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 15 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: The Bat. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)) and Snoopy's Soap Box Racers (38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Woodstock's Air Rail at 44 in (112 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 10 of 15 coasters with a verified rule, 8 more than at the height below: The Beast, The Racer, Adventure Express, Woodstock Express, Mystic Timbers, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, Woodstock's Air Rail and Queen City Stunt Coaster. Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Banshee at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 15 of 15 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Flight of Fear, Invertigo, Diamondback, Banshee and Orion. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Invertigo (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Banshee (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Woodstock's Air Rail (up to 76 in (193 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Kings Island

These are the rules Kings Island states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Woodstock Express: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Snoopy's Soap Box Racers: riders 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Kings Island for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Kings Island, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Great Pumpkin Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; 8 ft tall, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Queen City Stunt Coaster, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 45.2 ft tall, 40 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Banshee, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 167 ft tall, 68 mph, 7 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Kings Island?

Roller coaster height requirements at Kings Island run from 36 in (91 cm) (The Great Pumpkin Coaster) to 54 in (137 cm) (Flight of Fear), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Kings Island have?

Kings Island has 15 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Kings Island?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Banshee (167 ft tall, 68 mph, 7 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Kings Island?

Orion, at 287 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Kings Island?

Orion, with a top speed of 91 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Kings Island for first-timers?

The Great Pumpkin Coaster is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 36 in (91 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Kings Island?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Kings Island?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: The Beast, The Racer, Adventure Express, Woodstock Express, The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Mystic Timbers, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, Woodstock's Air Rail, Queen City Stunt Coaster.

Which coasters at Kings Island do not go upside down?

The Beast, The Racer, Adventure Express, Woodstock Express, The Bat, The Great Pumpkin Coaster, Diamondback, Orion, Mystic Timbers, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, Woodstock's Air Rail, Queen City Stunt Coaster have no inversions, per RCDB.
