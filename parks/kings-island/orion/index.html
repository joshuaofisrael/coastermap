Orion

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 91 mph; 287 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2020.

Orion is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 2 Jul 2020. The ride stands 287 ft tall, drops 300 ft, reaches 91 mph and runs 5,321 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Orion is like

Orion pairs a 287 ft structure with 5,321 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. Its 300 ft drop is longer than its 287 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. At 85°, the steepest drop is close to vertical.

Opened in 2020, it is the 14th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Bolliger & Mabillard's Hyper Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Mako at SeaWorld Orlando, Diamondback at Kings Island, Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg and Candymonium at Hersheypark. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kings Island sets 54 in, while Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in, Europa-Park sets 140 cm and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Kings Island, shared with 3 other coasters. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Orion sits on the south-east side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Racer, roughly 230 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flight of Fear at about 280 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Orion compares

On our intensity ranking Orion is number 2 of 15 at Kings Island, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 15 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Orion?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Orion, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Orion?

Orion is 287 ft tall, with a drop of 300 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Orion go?

Its top speed is 91 mph (146 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Orion go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Orion scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Orion open?

Orion opened on 2 Jul 2020.

Who built Orion?

Orion was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

Is Orion at Kings Island the same as Apollo's Chariot at Busch Gardens Williamsburg?

Both are Bolliger & Mabillard Hyper Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Kings Island ride opened in 2020 and the Busch Gardens Williamsburg ride in 1999; Kings Island sets 54 in and Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 52 in; RCDB lists heights of 287 ft and 170 ft.
