The Racer

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 53 mph; 88 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1972.

The Racer is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc. It opened on 29 Apr 1972. The ride stands 88 ft tall, reaches 53 mph and runs 3,415 ft of track on each track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Racer is like

The Racer spreads 3,415 ft of track across a layout only 88 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1972, it is the oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Racer sits on the east side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flight of Fear, roughly 130 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Queen City Stunt Coaster at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Racer?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Racer, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Racer?

The Racer is 88 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Racer go?

Its top speed is 53 mph (85 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Racer open?

The Racer opened on 29 Apr 1972.

Who built The Racer?

The Racer was manufactured by Philadelphia Toboggan Coasters, Inc.

How long is the ride on The Racer?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 3,415 ft of track.

How does The Racer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Racer at once?

Up to 30 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,640 riders.

Is The Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 53 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
