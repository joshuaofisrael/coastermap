Snoopy's Soap Box Racers

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 37.3 mph; 75.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Snoopy's Soap Box Racers is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Vekoma. It opened on 24 May 2024. The ride stands 75.5 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 672.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Snoopy's Soap Box Racers is like

Snoopy's Soap Box Racers packs its 672.6 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (75.5 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. RCDB lists a peak force of 3 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Boomerang at Energylandia and Speedway Stunt Coaster at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kings Island sets 48 in, while Energylandia sets 120 cm and Six Flags Mexico sets 120 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Snoopy's Soap Box Racers sits on the west side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Woodstock's Air Rail, roughly 30 m away (44 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is The Great Pumpkin Coaster at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion.

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Snoopy's Soap Box Racers?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Snoopy's Soap Box Racers, or 38 to 48 in (97 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Snoopy's Soap Box Racers?

Snoopy's Soap Box Racers is 75.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Snoopy's Soap Box Racers go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Snoopy's Soap Box Racers go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Snoopy's Soap Box Racers scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster; Kings Island rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. It is built for families.

When did Snoopy's Soap Box Racers open?

Snoopy's Soap Box Racers opened on 24 May 2024.

Who built Snoopy's Soap Box Racers?

Snoopy's Soap Box Racers was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Snoopy's Soap Box Racers?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 36 seconds over 672.6 ft of track.

How many people ride Snoopy's Soap Box Racers at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Snoopy's Soap Box Racers good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Snoopy's Soap Box Racers at Kings Island the same as Boomerang at Energylandia?

Both are Vekoma Family Boomerang / 208m (Rebound) coasters on RCDB; the Kings Island ride opened in 2024 and the Energylandia ride in 2017; Kings Island sets 48 in and Energylandia sets 120 cm.
