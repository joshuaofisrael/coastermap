The Beast

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 64.8 mph; 110 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1979.

The Beast is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio. It opened on 14 Apr 1979. The ride stands 110 ft tall, drops 141 ft, reaches 64.8 mph and runs 7,361 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding The Beast is like

The Beast spreads 7,361 ft of track across a layout only 110 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. Its 141 ft drop is longer than its 110 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside.

Opened in 1979, it is the 3rd oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, The Beast sits on the south side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Orion, roughly 350 m away (54 in minimum). Next closest is Diamondback at about 430 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Beast?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Beast, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Beast?

The Beast is 110 ft tall, with a drop of 141 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Beast go?

Its top speed is 64.8 mph (104 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Beast go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Beast scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Beast open?

The Beast opened on 14 Apr 1979.

How long is the ride on The Beast?

RCDB lists a ride time of 4 minutes 10 seconds over 7,361 ft of track.

How does The Beast get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride The Beast at once?

RCDB's train details give 36 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,200 riders.

Is The Beast good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 64.8 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
