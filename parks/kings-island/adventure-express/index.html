Adventure Express

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 35 mph; 63 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1991.

Adventure Express is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 13 Apr 1991. The ride stands 63 ft tall, reaches 35 mph and runs 2,963 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Adventure Express is like

Adventure Express spreads 2,963 ft of track across a layout only 63 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1991, it is the 4th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island. It is one of 14 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Mine Train design, and the 9th of them to open. Others include Cedar Creek Mine Ride at Cedar Point, Gold Rusher at Magic Mountain, Trailblazer at Hersheypark and Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Great Adventure. Height rules for this model differ by park: Kings Island sets 48 in, while Hersheypark sets 36 in, Six Flags Great Adventure sets 44 in and PortAventura Park sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Adventure Express sits on the north-east side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flight of Fear, roughly 140 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Banshee at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Adventure Express?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Adventure Express, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Adventure Express?

Adventure Express is 63 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Adventure Express go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Adventure Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Adventure Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Adventure Express open?

Adventure Express opened on 13 Apr 1991.

Who built Adventure Express?

Adventure Express was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Adventure Express?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 20 seconds over 2,963 ft of track.

How does Adventure Express get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Adventure Express at once?

RCDB's train details give 30 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,600 riders.

Is Adventure Express good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Adventure Express at Kings Island the same as Runaway Mine Train at Six Flags Over Texas?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Mine Train coasters on RCDB; the Kings Island ride opened in 1991 and the Six Flags Over Texas ride in 1966; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 63 ft and 35 ft.
