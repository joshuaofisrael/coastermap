Mystic Timbers

Kings Island · Mason, Ohio

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 53 mph; 109.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

Mystic Timbers is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 15 Apr 2017. The ride stands 109.2 ft tall, reaches 53 mph and runs 3,265 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Mystic Timbers is like

At 109.2 ft tall with 3,265 ft of track, Mystic Timbers balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2017, it is the 13th oldest of the 15 coasters we list at Kings Island.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 9 other coasters at Kings Island, out of 14 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Mystic Timbers sits on the south-west side of Kings Island's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Diamondback, roughly 130 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Woodstock's Air Rail at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Kings Island rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Mystic Timbers?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Mystic Timbers, according to Kings Island, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Mystic Timbers?

Mystic Timbers is 109.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Mystic Timbers go?

No. It has no inversions.

Mystic Timbers opened on 15 Apr 2017.
