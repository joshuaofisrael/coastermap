Psycho Mouse

California's Great America · Santa Clara, California

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 44 to 48 in (112 to 122 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Psycho Mouse is a steel sit-down roller coaster at California's Great America in Santa Clara, California, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened on 24 Mar 2001. The ride runs 1,257 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 44 to 48 in (112 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Psycho Mouse?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Psycho Mouse, or 44 to 48 in (112 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to California's Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Psycho Mouse go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Psycho Mouse scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Psycho Mouse open?

Psycho Mouse opened on 24 Mar 2001.

Who built Psycho Mouse?

Psycho Mouse was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.
