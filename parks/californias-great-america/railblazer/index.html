RailBlazer

California's Great America · Santa Clara, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52 mph; 106 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2018.

RailBlazer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at California's Great America in Santa Clara, California, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 14 Jun 2018. The ride stands 106 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,800 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding RailBlazer is like

At 106 ft tall with 1,800 ft of track, RailBlazer balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,800 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2018, it is the newest of the 9 coasters we list at California's Great America. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Rocky Mountain Coasters's Raptor Track / Prototype design, and the first of them to open. Others include Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 48 in.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at California's Great America, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, RailBlazer sits on the west side of California's Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Demon, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How RailBlazer compares

On our intensity ranking RailBlazer is number 2 of 9 at California's Great America, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride RailBlazer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the California's Great America app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for RailBlazer?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride RailBlazer, according to California's Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is RailBlazer?

RailBlazer is 106 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does RailBlazer go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does RailBlazer go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is RailBlazer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did RailBlazer open?

RailBlazer opened on 14 Jun 2018.

Who built RailBlazer?

RailBlazer was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on RailBlazer?

RCDB lists 1,800 ft (549 m) of track.

How does RailBlazer get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride RailBlazer at once?

Each train carries 8 riders, according to RCDB.

Is RailBlazer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is RailBlazer at California's Great America the same as Wonder Woman Golden Lasso Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas?

Both are Rocky Mountain Coasters Raptor Track / Prototype coasters on RCDB; the California's Great America ride opened in 2018 and the Six Flags Fiesta Texas ride in 2018; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 106 ft and 113 ft.
