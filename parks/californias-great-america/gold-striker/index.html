Gold Striker

California's Great America · Santa Clara, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 53.7 mph; 108.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2013.

Gold Striker is a wood sit-down roller coaster at California's Great America in Santa Clara, California, built by Great Coasters International. It opened on 31 May 2013. The ride stands 108.2 ft tall, reaches 53.7 mph and runs 3,197 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Gold Striker is like

At 108.2 ft tall with 3,197 ft of track, Gold Striker balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2013, it is the 8th oldest of the 9 coasters we list at California's Great America.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at California's Great America, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Gold Striker sits on the west side of California's Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is RailBlazer, roughly 180 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Patriot at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California's Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Gold Striker?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Gold Striker, according to California's Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Gold Striker?

Gold Striker is 108.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Gold Striker go?

Its top speed is 53.7 mph (86 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Gold Striker go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Gold Striker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California&#x27;s Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Gold Striker open?

Gold Striker opened on 31 May 2013.

Who built Gold Striker?

Gold Striker was manufactured by Great Coasters International.

How long is the ride on Gold Striker?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 3,197 ft of track.

How does Gold Striker get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Gold Striker at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 850 riders.

Is Gold Striker good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 53.7 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
