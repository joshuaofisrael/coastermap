California's Great America roller coasters

Santa Clara, California · 9 coasters

California's Great America in Santa Clara, California has 9 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Gold Striker at 108.2 ft and the fastest is Grizzly at 55 mph. The lowest height requirement is 40 in (102 cm), for Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does California's Great America have?

California's Great America has 9 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at California's Great America?

Gold Striker, at 108.2 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at California's Great America?

Grizzly, with a top speed of 55 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at California's Great America?

By height requirement, Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies has the lowest minimum at 40 inches (102 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which California's Great America coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which California's Great America coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Demon, Grizzly, Woodstock Express, Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies, Psycho Mouse, Gold Striker, RailBlazer. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at California's Great America do not go upside down?

Grizzly, Woodstock Express, Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies, Psycho Mouse, Gold Striker have no inversions, per RCDB.
