California's Great America height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Santa Clara, California · 9 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

California's Great America in Santa Clara, California has 9 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 40 in (102 cm) on Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies to 54 in (137 cm) on Patriot. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 7 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 9 of them.

What the coasters at California's Great America are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes California's Great America a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 50% across all 88 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 7 of the 9 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 9 go upside down; 2 are wooden coasters (Grizzly and Gold Striker).

The most common builder is Bolliger & Mabillard, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Demon, which opened in 1976.

What can my child ride at California's Great America? By height

At California's Great America the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies (36 to 40 in (91 to 102 cm)). Still too short for 8 coasters, the nearest being Woodstock Express at 46 in (117 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 9 coasters with a verified rule: Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express (40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm)). Still too short for 7 coasters, the nearest being Demon at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 9 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: Demon, Grizzly, Woodstock Express, Psycho Mouse, Gold Striker and RailBlazer. Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being Patriot at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 9 of 9 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Patriot and Flight Deck. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Patriot (up to 78 in (198 cm)) and Flight Deck (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at California's Great America

These are the rules California's Great America states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Woodstock Express: riders 40 to 46 in (102 to 117 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies: riders 36 to 40 in (91 to 102 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Psycho Mouse: riders 44 to 48 in (112 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at California's Great America for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for California's Great America, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Woodstock Express, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (46 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Psycho Mouse, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Demon, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 102 ft tall, 50 mph, 4 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at California's Great America?

Roller coaster height requirements at California's Great America run from 40 in (102 cm) (Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies) to 54 in (137 cm) (Patriot), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does California's Great America have?

California's Great America has 9 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at California's Great America?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Demon (102 ft tall, 50 mph, 4 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at California's Great America?

Gold Striker, at 108.2 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at California's Great America?

Grizzly, with a top speed of 55 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at California's Great America for first-timers?

Woodstock Express is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 46 in (117 cm) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at California's Great America?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies. With a companion they can also ride Woodstock Express.

What can a 48-inch child ride at California's Great America?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Demon, Grizzly, Woodstock Express, Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies, Psycho Mouse, Gold Striker, RailBlazer.

Which coasters at California's Great America do not go upside down?

Grizzly, Woodstock Express, Lucy's Crabbie Cabbies, Psycho Mouse, Gold Striker have no inversions, per RCDB.
