Demon

California's Great America · Santa Clara, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 50 mph; 102 ft tall; 4 inversions; opened 1976.

Demon is a steel sit-down roller coaster at California's Great America in Santa Clara, California, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened on 20 May 1976 and was previously known as Turn Of The Century. The ride stands 102 ft tall, drops 90 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 2,130 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 4 times.

What riding Demon is like

At 102 ft tall with 2,130 ft of track, Demon balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 4 times across 2,130 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1976, it is the oldest of the 9 coasters we list at California's Great America. It has run under other names (Turn Of The Century), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at California's Great America, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Demon sits on the south-west side of California's Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Grizzly, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is RailBlazer at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California's Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 4 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Demon

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 4 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the California's Great America app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Demon?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Demon, according to California's Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Demon?

Demon is 102 ft tall, with a drop of 90 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Demon go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Demon go upside down?

Yes. It has 4 inversions.

Is Demon scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California&#x27;s Great America rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Demon open?

Demon opened on 20 May 1976 (previously known as Turn Of The Century).

Who built Demon?

Demon was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Demon?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 45 seconds over 2,130 ft of track.

How does Demon get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Demon at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Demon use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Demon good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 4 times; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Demon at California's Great America the same as Demon at Six Flags Great America?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Custom Looping Coaster coasters on RCDB; the California's Great America ride opened in 1976 and the Six Flags Great America ride in 1976; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 102 ft and 103.7 ft.
