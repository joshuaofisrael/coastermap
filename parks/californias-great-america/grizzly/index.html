Grizzly

California's Great America · Santa Clara, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 55 mph; 91 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

Grizzly is a wood sit-down roller coaster at California's Great America in Santa Clara, California. It opened in 1986. The ride stands 91 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 3,250 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Grizzly is like

With 3,250 ft of track for a 91 ft structure, Grizzly is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1986, it is the 2nd oldest of the 9 coasters we list at California's Great America.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at California's Great America, out of 8 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Grizzly sits on the south side of California's Great America's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Demon, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Psycho Mouse at about 180 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California's Great America rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Grizzly?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Grizzly, according to California's Great America, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Grizzly?

Grizzly is 91 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Grizzly go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Grizzly go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Grizzly scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; California&#x27;s Great America rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Grizzly open?

Grizzly opened in 1986.

How long is the ride on Grizzly?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 40 seconds over 3,250 ft of track.

How does Grizzly get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Grizzly at once?

RCDB's train details give 28 seats per train.

Is Grizzly good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Grizzly at California's Great America the same as Grizzly at Kings Dominion?

They share a name but are separate rides; the California's Great America ride opened in 1986 and the Kings Dominion ride in 1982; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 91 ft and 87 ft.
