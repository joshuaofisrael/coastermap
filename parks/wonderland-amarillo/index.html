Wonderland Amusement Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Amarillo, Texas · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Wonderland Amusement Park in Amarillo, Texas has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Mouse Trap to 50 in (127 cm) on Texas Tornado. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Wonderland Amusement Park are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 1 as extreme, which makes Wonderland Amusement Park a balanced park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 25% of its lineup, against 40% across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Cyclone, which opened in 1968.

What can my child ride at Wonderland Amusement Park? By height

At Wonderland Amusement Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Wonderland Amusement Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Wonderland Amusement Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Cyclone, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Hornet, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 27.9 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Texas Tornado, the most intense coaster in the park (50 in minimum; 80 ft tall, 2 inversions).
