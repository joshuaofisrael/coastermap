LEGO Technic Coaster

Legoland California · Carlsbad, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 35 mph; 51.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

LEGO Technic Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland California in Carlsbad, California, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 7 Apr 2001. The ride stands 51.8 ft tall, drops 49.8 ft, reaches 35 mph and runs 1312.3 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding LEGO Technic Coaster is like

LEGO Technic Coaster pairs a 51.8 ft structure with 1,312.3 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2001, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland California. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Large Park design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Coast Rider at Knott's, Apple Zapple at Kings Dominion, The Fly at Canada's Wonderland and The Great LEGO Race at Legoland Florida. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland California sets 48 in, while Knott's sets 54 in, Kings Dominion sets 54 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Legoland California, shared with 3 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, LEGO Technic Coaster sits on the east side of Legoland California's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is The Dragon, roughly 120 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Coastersaurus at about 320 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride LEGO Technic Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders must be at least 4 years old, and at least 6 to ride alone.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland California app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for LEGO Technic Coaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride LEGO Technic Coaster, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion. Riders must be at least 4 years old, and at least 6 to ride alone, according to Legoland California, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is LEGO Technic Coaster?

LEGO Technic Coaster is 51.8 ft tall, with a drop of 49.8 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does LEGO Technic Coaster go?

Its top speed is 35 mph (56 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does LEGO Technic Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is LEGO Technic Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did LEGO Technic Coaster open?

LEGO Technic Coaster opened on 7 Apr 2001.

Who built LEGO Technic Coaster?

LEGO Technic Coaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on LEGO Technic Coaster?

RCDB lists 1312.3 ft of track.

How does LEGO Technic Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride LEGO Technic Coaster at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is LEGO Technic Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 35 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is LEGO Technic Coaster at Legoland California the same as The Fly at Canada's Wonderland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Large Park coasters on RCDB; the Legoland California ride opened in 2001 and the Canada's Wonderland ride in 1999; Legoland California sets 48 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 54 in.
