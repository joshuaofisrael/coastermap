Coastersaurus

Legoland California · Carlsbad, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 20.5 mph; 18.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Coastersaurus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Legoland California in Carlsbad, California, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 1 Apr 2004. The ride stands 18.1 ft tall, reaches 20.5 mph and runs 492.2 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Coastersaurus is like

Coastersaurus pairs a 18.1 ft structure with 492.2 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2004, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Legoland California. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH's Junior Coaster / Drachenjagd design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Drachenjagd at Legoland Deutschland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Legoland California sets 48 in, while Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the strictest at Legoland California, shared with 3 other coasters. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Coastersaurus sits on the south-west side of Legoland California's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is LEGO Technic Coaster, roughly 320 m away (48 in minimum). Next closest is The Dragon at about 350 m. Coastersaurus layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Before you ride Coastersaurus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Companions must be adults aged 18 or older.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Legoland California app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Coastersaurus?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Coastersaurus, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion. Companions must be adults aged 18 or older, according to Legoland California, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Coastersaurus?

Coastersaurus is 18.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Coastersaurus go?

Its top speed is 20.5 mph (33 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Coastersaurus go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Coastersaurus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Coastersaurus open?

Coastersaurus opened on 1 Apr 2004.

Who built Coastersaurus?

Coastersaurus was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Coastersaurus?

RCDB lists 492.2 ft of track.

How does Coastersaurus get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Coastersaurus at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Coastersaurus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 20.5 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Coastersaurus at Legoland California the same as Drachenjagd at Legoland Deutschland?

Both are Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH Junior Coaster / Drachenjagd coasters on RCDB; the Legoland California ride opened in 2004 and the Legoland Deutschland ride in 2003; Legoland California sets 48 in and Legoland Deutschland sets 120 cm.
