Legoland California height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Carlsbad, California · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Legoland California in Carlsbad, California has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 48 in (122 cm) on The Dragon. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Legoland California are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Legoland California a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 49% of coasters across all 100 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is The Dragon, which opened in 1999.

Other rider rules stated by the park

  • The Dragon: Companions must be at least 14 years old and at least 48 in tall.
  • LEGO Technic Coaster: Riders must be at least 4 years old, and at least 6 to ride alone.
  • Coastersaurus: Companions must be adults aged 18 or older.

What can my child ride at Legoland California? By height

At Legoland California the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 4 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Coastersaurus (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Galacticoaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 2 coasters, the nearest being The Dragon at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride The Dragon (40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm)), LEGO Technic Coaster (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)), Coastersaurus (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Galacticoaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: The Dragon, LEGO Technic Coaster, Coastersaurus and Galacticoaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 4 coasters with a verified rule: The Dragon, LEGO Technic Coaster, Coastersaurus and Galacticoaster. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Galacticoaster (up to 78 in (198 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Legoland California

These are the rules Legoland California states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • The Dragon: riders 40 to 48 in (102 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • LEGO Technic Coaster: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Coastersaurus: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Galacticoaster: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Legoland California for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Legoland California, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: The Dragon, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Coastersaurus, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (48 in minimum; 18.1 ft tall, 20.5 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: LEGO Technic Coaster, the most intense coaster in the park (48 in minimum; 51.8 ft tall, 35 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
