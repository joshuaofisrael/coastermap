DC Rivals HyperCoaster

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 71.5 mph; 202 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

DC Rivals HyperCoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 22 Sep 2017. The ride stands 202 ft tall, reaches 71.5 mph and runs 4,593 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding DC Rivals HyperCoaster is like

At 202 ft tall with 4,593 ft of track, DC Rivals HyperCoaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2017, it is the 5th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World.

A rider who just meets its 130 cm minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Movie World, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, DC Rivals HyperCoaster sits on the east side of Movie World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Green Lantern Coaster, roughly 210 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flash: Speed Force at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for DC Rivals HyperCoaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride DC Rivals HyperCoaster. Riders must be at least 7 years old, according to Warner Bros. Movie World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is DC Rivals HyperCoaster?

DC Rivals HyperCoaster is 202 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does DC Rivals HyperCoaster go?

Its top speed is 71.5 mph (115 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does DC Rivals HyperCoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is DC Rivals HyperCoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did DC Rivals HyperCoaster open?

DC Rivals HyperCoaster opened on 22 Sep 2017.

Who built DC Rivals HyperCoaster?

DC Rivals HyperCoaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the track on DC Rivals HyperCoaster?

RCDB lists 4,593 ft (1,400 m) of track.

How does DC Rivals HyperCoaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

Is DC Rivals HyperCoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 71.5 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
