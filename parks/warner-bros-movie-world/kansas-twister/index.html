Kansas Twister

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 36 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Kansas Twister is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Vekoma. It opened on 20 Dec 2024. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 36 mph and runs 738.2 ft of track on each track. It runs on two tracks side by side. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Kansas Twister is like

At 72.2 ft tall with 738.2 ft of track, Kansas Twister balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World.
