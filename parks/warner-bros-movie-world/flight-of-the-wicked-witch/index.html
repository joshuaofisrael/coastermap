Flight of the Wicked Witch

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 41.6 mph; 62.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2024.

Flight of the Wicked Witch is a steel suspended roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Vekoma. It opened on 20 Dec 2024. The ride stands 62.3 ft tall, reaches 41.6 mph and runs 1489.5 ft of track. On a suspended coaster the cars hang below the track on pivots and swing outward through the turns. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flight of the Wicked Witch is like

Flight of the Wicked Witch pairs a 62.3 ft structure with 1,489.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2024, it is the 7th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Suspended Family Coaster / 453m design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Dragonflier at Dollywood and Dragon Roller Coaster at Energylandia.
