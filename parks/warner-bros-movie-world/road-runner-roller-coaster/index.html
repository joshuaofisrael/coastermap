Road Runner Roller Coaster

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 28.5 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Road Runner Roller Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Vekoma. It opened on 26 Dec 2000. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 28.5 mph and runs 1099.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Road Runner Roller Coaster is like

Road Runner Roller Coaster pairs a 42.7 ft structure with 1,099.1 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2000, it is the oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 335m design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Cedar Point, Flight of the Hippogriff at Islands of Adventure, Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Japan and The Dragon at Legoland Florida. Height rules for this model differ by park: Movie World sets 120 cm, while Cedar Point sets 48 in, Islands of Adventure sets 36 in and Universal Studios Japan sets 122 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Movie World, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Road Runner Roller Coaster sits on the north-west side of Movie World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Superman Escape, roughly 230 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster at about 230 m. Road Runner Roller Coaster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Road Runner Roller Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Road Runner Roller Coaster, or 110 to 120 cm (about 43.3 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Warner Bros. Movie World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Road Runner Roller Coaster?

Road Runner Roller Coaster is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Road Runner Roller Coaster go?

Its top speed is 28.5 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Road Runner Roller Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Road Runner Roller Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Road Runner Roller Coaster open?

Road Runner Roller Coaster opened on 26 Dec 2000.

Who built Road Runner Roller Coaster?

Road Runner Roller Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Road Runner Roller Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 6 seconds over 1099.1 ft of track.

How does Road Runner Roller Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Road Runner Roller Coaster at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Road Runner Roller Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.5 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Road Runner Roller Coaster at Movie World the same as K3 Roller Skater at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 335m coasters on RCDB; the Movie World ride opened in 2000 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1991; both list a 120 cm minimum.
