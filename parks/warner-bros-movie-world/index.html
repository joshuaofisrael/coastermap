Warner Bros. Movie World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Oxenford, Australia · 8 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia has 8 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Road Runner Roller Coaster to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Superman Escape. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Warner Bros. Movie World are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 3 as extreme, which makes Warner Bros. Movie World a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 38% of its lineup, against 42% across all 176 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 55.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 1 of the 8 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Superman Escape).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Road Runner Roller Coaster, which opened in 2000.

Warner Bros. Movie World publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Warner Bros. Movie World? By height

At Warner Bros. Movie World the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Warner Bros. Movie World for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Warner Bros. Movie World, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Road Runner Roller Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 28.5 mph, no inversions).
  2. The big one: DC Rivals HyperCoaster, the most intense coaster in the park (130 cm minimum; 202 ft tall, 71.5 mph, no inversions).
