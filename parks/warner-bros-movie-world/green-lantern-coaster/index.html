Green Lantern Coaster

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 41 mph; 108.3 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2011.

Green Lantern Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 23 Dec 2011. The ride stands 108.3 ft tall, reaches 41 mph and runs 1601.1 ft of track. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 120.5°, past vertical.

What riding Green Lantern Coaster is like

At 108.3 ft tall with 1,601.1 ft of track, Green Lantern Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 120.5°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,601.1 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2011, it is the 4th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's El Loco design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach and Mumbo Jumbo at Flamingo Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Movie World sets 140 cm, while Indiana Beach sets 48 in and Flamingo Land sets 122 cm.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Movie World, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Green Lantern Coaster sits on the north-east side of Movie World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flash: Speed Force, roughly 130 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Superman Escape at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Green Lantern Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Green Lantern Coaster is number 3 of 8 at Warner Bros. Movie World, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 7 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster (55.8 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Superman Escape (131.3 ft tall, 62.1 mph, no inversions, 4.2 g; 140 cm minimum).

Before you ride Green Lantern Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Warner Bros. Movie World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Green Lantern Coaster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Green Lantern Coaster, according to Warner Bros. Movie World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Green Lantern Coaster?

Green Lantern Coaster is 108.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Green Lantern Coaster go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Green Lantern Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Green Lantern Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Green Lantern Coaster open?

Green Lantern Coaster opened on 23 Dec 2011.

Who built Green Lantern Coaster?

Green Lantern Coaster was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How long is the track on Green Lantern Coaster?

RCDB lists 1601.1 ft of track.

How does Green Lantern Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Green Lantern Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 8 seats per train.

Is Green Lantern Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 41 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Green Lantern Coaster at Movie World the same as Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach?

Both are S&S Worldwide El Loco coasters on RCDB; the Movie World ride opened in 2011 and the Indiana Beach ride in 2008; Movie World sets 140 cm and Indiana Beach sets 48 in; RCDB lists heights of 108.3 ft and 96 ft.
