Flash: Speed Force

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

0 inversions; opened 2024.

Flash: Speed Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 13 May 2024. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Flash: Speed Force is like

Opened in 2024, it is the 6th oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Flash: Speed Force sits near the middle of Movie World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Superman Escape, roughly 10 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Green Lantern Coaster at about 130 m. Flash: Speed Force layout, trains and ride system

Flash: Speed Force starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists one element for Flash: Speed Force:

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Frequently asked questions

Does Flash: Speed Force go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Flash: Speed Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Flash: Speed Force open?

Flash: Speed Force opened on 13 May 2024.

Who built Flash: Speed Force?

Flash: Speed Force was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

Does Flash: Speed Force launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

Is Flash: Speed Force good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Flash: Speed Force operate before?

RCDB records it at Wet'n'Wild Water World (as Surfrider).
