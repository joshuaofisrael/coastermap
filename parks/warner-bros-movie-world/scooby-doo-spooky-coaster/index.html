SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster

Warner Bros. Movie World · Oxenford, Australia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 28 mph; 55.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2002.

SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Warner Bros. Movie World in Oxenford, Australia, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It first opened on 17 Jun 2002 and was previously known as Scooby-Doo Spooky Coaster. The ride stands 55.8 ft tall, drops 7 ft, reaches 28 mph and runs 1738.8 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster is like

With 1,738.8 ft of track for a 55.8 ft structure, SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. RCDB lists a 7 ft drop against a 55.8 ft height, so the first drop does not fall the full height of the structure.

Opened in 2002, it is the 2nd oldest of the 8 coasters we list at Warner Bros. Movie World. It has run under other names (Scooby-Doo Spooky Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those.

Going by RCDB's map positions, SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster sits on the west side of Movie World's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Superman Escape, roughly 130 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Flash: Speed Force at about 140 m. Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster?

SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster is 55.8 ft tall, with a drop of 7 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster open?

SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster opened on 17 Jun 2002 (previously known as Scooby-Doo Spooky Coaster).

Who built SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster?

SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How long is the ride on SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 4 minutes 13 seconds over 1738.8 ft of track.

How does SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists an elevator lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,000 riders.

Is SCOOBY-DOO! Spooky Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
