Egg-Spress

Pleasurewood Hills · Lowestoft, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 125 cm (about 49.2 in), or 90 to 125 cm (about 35.4 to 49.2 in) with a companion; top speed 22.4 mph; 26.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1986.

Egg-Spress is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Pleasurewood Hills in Lowestoft, England, built by Zierer. It first opened in 1986 and was previously known as Ladybird and later Rattlesnake and later Snake In The Grass. The ride stands 26.3 ft tall, reaches 22.4 mph and runs 1181.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Egg-Spress is like

Egg-Spress spreads 1,181.1 ft of track across a layout only 26.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 1986, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Pleasurewood Hills. It has run under other names (Ladybird, then Rattlesnake, then Snake In The Grass), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 12 rides on Coaster Map built to Zierer's Tivoli / Large design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Harley Quinn Crazy Train at Six Flags Great Adventure, Catwoman Whip at Six Flags New England, Cobra at Discovery Kingdom and Tsunami at Six Flags Mexico. Height rules for this model differ by park: Pleasurewood Hills sets 125 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in, Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 48 in.

Its 125 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Pleasurewood Hills (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 90 to 125 cm (about 35.4 to 49.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Egg-Spress sits on the west side of Pleasurewood Hills' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wipeout, roughly 210 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Marble Madness at about 480 m. Egg-Spress layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall, or 90 to 125 cm (about 35.4 to 49.2 in) with a companion.

Before you ride Egg-Spress

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 125 cm (about 49.2 in), and riders from 90 to 125 cm (about 35.4 to 49.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Guests between 0.9 m and 1.25 m must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Pleasurewood Hills app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Egg-Spress?

Riders must be at least 125 cm (about 49.2 in) tall to ride Egg-Spress, or 90 to 125 cm (about 35.4 to 49.2 in) with a companion. Guests between 0.9 m and 1.25 m must be accompanied by an adult, according to Pleasurewood Hills, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Egg-Spress?

Egg-Spress is 26.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Egg-Spress go?

Its top speed is 22.4 mph (36 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Egg-Spress go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Egg-Spress scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Egg-Spress open?

Egg-Spress opened in 1986 (previously known as Ladybird and later Rattlesnake and later Snake In The Grass).

Who built Egg-Spress?

Egg-Spress was manufactured by Zierer.

How long is the track on Egg-Spress?

RCDB lists 1181.1 ft of track.

How does Egg-Spress get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Egg-Spress good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 22.4 mph; the minimum height is 125 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Egg-Spress at Pleasurewood Hills the same as #LikeMe Coaster at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Zierer Tivoli / Large coasters on RCDB; the Pleasurewood Hills ride opened in 1986 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1976; Pleasurewood Hills sets 125 cm and Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm.
