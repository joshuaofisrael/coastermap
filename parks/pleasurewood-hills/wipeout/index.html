Wipeout

Pleasurewood Hills · Lowestoft, England

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 47 mph; 116.5 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2007.

Wipeout is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Pleasurewood Hills in Lowestoft, England, built by Vekoma. It opened on 28 Jul 2007. The ride stands 116.5 ft tall, reaches 47 mph and runs 935 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Wipeout is like

Wipeout packs its 935 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (116.5 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. With 3 inversions in 935 ft, it flips riders roughly every 312 ft, one of the denser inversion sequences you will find. RCDB lists a peak force of 5.2 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2007, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Pleasurewood Hills. It is one of 24 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Boomerang design, and the 22nd of them to open. Others include Jolly Rancher Remix at Hersheypark, Flying Cobras at Carowinds, The Bat at Canada's Wonderland and Boomerang Coast to Coaster at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. Height rules for this model differ by park: Pleasurewood Hills sets 130 cm, while Hersheypark sets 48 in, Carowinds sets 48 in and Canada's Wonderland sets 48 in.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Pleasurewood Hills. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wipeout sits near the middle of Pleasurewood Hills' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Egg-Spress, roughly 210 m away (125 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Marble Madness at about 310 m. Wipeout layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Wipeout's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Catch Car Lift Hill
  2. Cobra Roll
  3. Loop
  4. Chain Lift Hill

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Wipeout

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Pleasurewood Hills app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wipeout?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Wipeout, according to Pleasurewood Hills, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wipeout?

Wipeout is 116.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wipeout go?

Its top speed is 47 mph (76 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wipeout go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Wipeout scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wipeout open?

Wipeout opened on 28 Jul 2007.

Who built Wipeout?

Wipeout was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Wipeout?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 48 seconds over 935 ft of track.

How does Wipeout get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a catch-car lift hill and a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wipeout at once?

Each train carries 28 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 760 riders.

Is Wipeout good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 47 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Wipeout operate before?

RCDB records it at Glasgow Garden Festival (as Coca-Cola Roller); American Adventure Theme Park (as Missile).

Is Wipeout at Pleasurewood Hills the same as Boomerang at Bellewaerde?

Both are Vekoma Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Pleasurewood Hills ride opened in 2007 and the Bellewaerde ride in 1984; both list a 130 cm minimum.
