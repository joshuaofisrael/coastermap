Disney Adventure World height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Formerly Walt Disney Studios Park · Marne-la-Vallée, France · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Disney Adventure World (formerly Walt Disney Studios Park) in Marne-la-Vallée, France has 2 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on RC Racer. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Disney Adventure World are like

Of the ride types, 1 of the 2 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, which opened in 2002.

Disney Adventure World publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Disney Adventure World? By height

At Disney Adventure World the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Disney Adventure World for first-timers

  2. The big one: Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 80 ft tall, 57 mph, 3 inversions, 5 g).

Frequently asked questions

Is Disney Adventure World the same park as Walt Disney Studios Park?

Yes. Disney Adventure World in Marne-la-Vallée, France is the park formerly called Walt Disney Studios Park; RCDB lists the park's former name as Walt Disney Studios Park, and Disneyland Paris's official attractions page lists it as Disney Adventure World (checked 10 Nov 2026).

What are the height requirements at Disney Adventure World?

RC Racer requires riders to be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in), according to the park (checked 10 Nov 2026). Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Disney Adventure World have?

Disney Adventure World has 2 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Disney Adventure World?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force (80 ft tall, 57 mph, 3 inversions, 5 g).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Disney Adventure World?

RC Racer, at 82 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Disney Adventure World?

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, with a top speed of 57 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Disney Adventure World for first-timers?

RC Racer is the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with a 120 cm (about 47.2 in) minimum height.

What can a 42-inch child ride at Disney Adventure World?

No coaster here has a ride-alone minimum of 42 in or less.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Disney Adventure World?

Riding alone, a 48-inch (122 cm) child meets the official minimum on: RC Racer, Avengers Assemble: Flight Force.

Which coasters at Disney Adventure World do not go upside down?

RC Racer have no inversions, per RCDB.
