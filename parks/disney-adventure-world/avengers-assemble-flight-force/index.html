Avengers Assemble: Flight Force

Disney Adventure World · Marne-la-Vallée, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 57 mph; 80 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2002.

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disney Adventure World in Marne-la-Vallée, France, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 16 Mar 2002 and was previously known as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster. The ride stands 80 ft tall, reaches 57 mph and runs 3267.7 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Avengers Assemble: Flight Force is like

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force spreads 3,267.7 ft of track across a layout only 80 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,267.7 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

It has run under other names (Rock 'n' Roller Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's LSM Coaster / 1,037m (Disney) design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets at Hollywood Studios. Height rules for this model differ by park: Disney Adventure World sets 120 cm, while Hollywood Studios sets 48 in.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is RC Racer, roughly 330 m away (120 cm minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force layout, trains and ride system

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (from 0 mph - 57 mph in 2.8 s)
  2. Sea Serpent
  3. Corkscrew

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Before you ride Avengers Assemble: Flight Force

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Disney Adventure World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Avengers Assemble: Flight Force?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, according to Disneyland Paris, official attractions page (height filter) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force?

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force is 80 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Avengers Assemble: Flight Force go?

Its top speed is 57 mph (92 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Avengers Assemble: Flight Force go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Avengers Assemble: Flight Force open?

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force opened on 16 Mar 2002 (previously known as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster).

Who built Avengers Assemble: Flight Force?

Avengers Assemble: Flight Force was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Avengers Assemble: Flight Force?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 10 seconds over 3267.7 ft of track.

Does Avengers Assemble: Flight Force launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Avengers Assemble: Flight Force at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,800 riders.

Is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 57 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force at Disney Adventure World the same as Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets at Hollywood Studios?

Both are Vekoma LSM Coaster / 1,037m (Disney) coasters on RCDB; the Disney Adventure World ride opened in 2002 and the Hollywood Studios ride in 1999; Disney Adventure World sets 120 cm and Hollywood Studios sets 48 in.
