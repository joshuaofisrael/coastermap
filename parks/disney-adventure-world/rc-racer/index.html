RC Racer

Disney Adventure World · Marne-la-Vallée, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); 82 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

RC Racer is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disney Adventure World in Marne-la-Vallée, France, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 17 Aug 2010. The ride stands 82 ft tall and runs 269 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding RC Racer is like

For a 82 ft ride, 269 ft of track is short: RC Racer is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements.

It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Surfrider / RC Racer design, and the first of them to open. Others include Rex's RC Racer at Shanghai Disneyland and RC Racer at Hong Kong Disneyland. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 120 cm.

Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Avengers Assemble: Flight Force, roughly 330 m away (120 cm minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

RC Racer layout, trains and ride system

RC Racer starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride RC Racer

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride runs backwards. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Disney Adventure World app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for RC Racer?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride RC Racer, according to Disneyland Paris, official attractions page (height filter) (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is RC Racer?

RC Racer is 82 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does RC Racer go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is RC Racer scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did RC Racer open?

RC Racer opened on 17 Aug 2010.

Who built RC Racer?

RC Racer was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on RC Racer?

RCDB lists 269 ft (82 m) of track.

Does RC Racer launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride RC Racer at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

Is RC Racer good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is RC Racer at Disney Adventure World the same as RC Racer at Hong Kong Disneyland?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Surfrider / RC Racer coasters on RCDB; the Disney Adventure World ride opened in 2010 and the Hong Kong Disneyland ride in 2011; both list a 120 cm minimum.
