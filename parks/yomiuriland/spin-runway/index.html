Spin Runway

Yomiuriland · Inagi, Tokyo, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion; top speed 28.3 mph; 44.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2016.

Spin Runway is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yomiuriland in Inagi, Tokyo, Japan, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It opened on 18 Mar 2016. The ride stands 44.9 ft tall, reaches 28.3 mph and runs 1469.8 ft of track. It combines coaster sections with themed show scenes, like a dark ride. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Spin Runway is like

With 1,469.8 ft of track for a 44.9 ft structure, Spin Runway is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2016, it is the 3rd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Yomiuriland.

Its 130 cm minimum is the strictest at Yomiuriland, shared with 1 other coaster. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Spin Runway sits on the south side of Yomiuriland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wan Wan Coaster Wandit, roughly 160 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Lipovitan Rocket Luna at about 220 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

How Spin Runway compares

On our intensity ranking Spin Runway is number 2 of 5 at Yomiuriland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Lipovitan Rocket Luna (50.9 ft tall, 24.2 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Bandit (167.3 ft tall, 68.4 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g; 120 cm minimum).

Before you ride Spin Runway

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 110 to 130 cm need a companion of junior-high age or older. Minimum age 4; under 6 must ride with a companion.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Yomiuriland app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Spin Runway?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Spin Runway, or 110 to 130 cm (about 43.3 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Riders 110 to 130 cm need a companion of junior-high age or older. Minimum age 4; under 6 must ride with a companion, according to Yomiuriland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Spin Runway?

Spin Runway is 44.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Spin Runway go?

Its top speed is 28.3 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Spin Runway go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Spin Runway scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Spin Runway open?

Spin Runway opened on 18 Mar 2016.

Who built Spin Runway?

Spin Runway was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How long is the track on Spin Runway?

RCDB lists 1469.8 ft of track.

How does Spin Runway get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a spiral lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Spin Runway at once?

Up to 4 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

What restraints does Spin Runway use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, t-bar.

Is Spin Runway good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.3 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
