Wan Wan Coaster Wandit

Yomiuriland · Inagi, Tokyo, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); top speed 18.6 mph; 18.1 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Wan Wan Coaster Wandit is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yomiuriland in Inagi, Tokyo, Japan, built by Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd. It opened on 23 Jul 2005. The ride stands 18.1 ft tall, reaches 18.6 mph and runs 485.6 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Wan Wan Coaster Wandit is like

Wan Wan Coaster Wandit pairs a 18.1 ft structure with 485.6 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2005, it is the 2nd oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Yomiuriland.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Yomiuriland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Wan Wan Coaster Wandit sits near the middle of Yomiuriland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Spin Runway, roughly 160 m away (130 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Bandit at about 300 m. It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall.

How Wan Wan Coaster Wandit compares

On our intensity ranking Wan Wan Coaster Wandit is number 4 of 5 at Yomiuriland, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is DinoRunner (no inversions; 110 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Lipovitan Rocket Luna (50.9 ft tall, 24.2 mph, no inversions; 130 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wan Wan Coaster Wandit?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Wan Wan Coaster Wandit. Minimum age 3; under 5 must ride with a companion of junior-high age or older, according to Yomiuriland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wan Wan Coaster Wandit?

Wan Wan Coaster Wandit is 18.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wan Wan Coaster Wandit go?

Its top speed is 18.6 mph (30 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wan Wan Coaster Wandit go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Wan Wan Coaster Wandit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Wan Wan Coaster Wandit open?

Wan Wan Coaster Wandit opened on 23 Jul 2005.

Who built Wan Wan Coaster Wandit?

Wan Wan Coaster Wandit was manufactured by Hoei Sangyo Co., Ltd.

How long is the ride on Wan Wan Coaster Wandit?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 46 seconds over 485.6 ft of track.

How does Wan Wan Coaster Wandit get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Wan Wan Coaster Wandit at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 400 riders.

What restraints does Wan Wan Coaster Wandit use?

RCDB lists: seatbelt, t-bar.

Is Wan Wan Coaster Wandit good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 18.6 mph; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
