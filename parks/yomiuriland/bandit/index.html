Bandit

Yomiuriland · Inagi, Tokyo, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 68.4 mph; 167.3 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1988.

Bandit is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Yomiuriland in Inagi, Tokyo, Japan, built by Togo. It opened on 25 Mar 1988. The ride stands 167.3 ft tall, reaches 68.4 mph and runs 5118.1 ft of track. The layout follows the natural hillside terrain. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Bandit is like

Bandit spreads 5,118.1 ft of track across a layout only 167.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1988, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Yomiuriland.

A rider who just meets its 120 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Yomiuriland, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Bandit sits on the north side of Yomiuriland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Wan Wan Coaster Wandit, roughly 300 m away (90 cm minimum). Next closest is Spin Runway at about 470 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Bandit?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Bandit. The park also requires riders to be elementary-school age or older, according to Yomiuriland, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Bandit?

Bandit is 167.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Bandit go?

Its top speed is 68.4 mph (110 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Bandit go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Bandit scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Bandit open?

Bandit opened on 25 Mar 1988.

Who built Bandit?

Bandit was manufactured by Togo.

How long is the ride on Bandit?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 18 seconds over 5118.1 ft of track.

How does Bandit get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Bandit at once?

Up to 28 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,260 riders.

What restraints does Bandit use?

RCDB lists: seat divider, shoulder harness.

Is Bandit good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 68.4 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
