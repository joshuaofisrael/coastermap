Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster

Disneyland Park · Anaheim, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 35 in (89 cm); top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California, built by Vekoma. It first opened in 1993 and was previously known as Gadget's Go Coaster. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster is like

At 27.9 ft tall with 679.1 ft of track, Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1993, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Disneyland Park. It has run under other names (Gadget's Go Coaster), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 16 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 207m design, and the first of them to open. Others include Road Runner Express at Magic Mountain, Trolls Trollercoaster at Universal Studios Florida, Sprocket Rockets at Six Flags Great America and Tami-Tami at PortAventura Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Disneyland sets 35 in, while Magic Mountain sets 36 in, Universal Studios Florida sets 36 in and Six Flags Great America sets 42 in.

Its 35 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Disneyland, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster sits on the north side of Disneyland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, roughly 240 m away (40 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Matterhorn Bobsleds at about 270 m. Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster layout, trains and ride system

Trains climb a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster?

Riders must be at least 35 in (89 cm) tall to ride Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster, according to Disneyland Resort, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster?

Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster is 27.9 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster go?

Its top speed is 21.7 mph (35 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster open?

Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster opened in 1993 (previously known as Gadget's Go Coaster).

Who built Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster?

Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 44 seconds over 679.1 ft of track.

How does Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 780 riders.

Is Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 21.7 mph; the minimum height is 35 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at Disneyland the same as Jul's RollerSkates at Julianatoren?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 207m coasters on RCDB; the Disneyland ride opened in 1993 and the Julianatoren ride in 1993; Disneyland sets 35 in and Julianatoren sets 120 cm.
