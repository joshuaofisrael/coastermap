Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster

Disneyland Park · Anaheim, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 35 in (89 cm); top speed 21.7 mph; 27.9 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California, built by Vekoma. It first opened on 1993 and was previously known as Gadget's Go Coaster. The ride stands 27.9 ft tall, reaches 21.7 mph and runs 679.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 35 in (89 cm) tall.
