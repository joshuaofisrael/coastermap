Disneyland Park roller coasters

Anaheim, California · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California has 4 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Matterhorn Bobsleds at 80 ft and the fastest is Space Mountain at 32 mph. The lowest height requirement is 35 in (89 cm), for Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Disneyland Park have?

Disneyland Park has 4 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Disneyland Park?

Matterhorn Bobsleds, at 80 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Disneyland Park?

Space Mountain, with a top speed of 32 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Disneyland Park?
