Disneyland Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Anaheim, California · 4 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California has 4 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 35 in (89 cm) on Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster to 42 in (107 cm) on Matterhorn Bobsleds. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 4, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 4 of them.

What the coasters at Disneyland Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Disneyland Park a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 231 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 40 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 4 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Matterhorn Bobsleds, which opened in 1959.

What can my child ride at Disneyland Park? By height

At Disneyland Park the biggest single step is reaching 42 in (107 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Disneyland Park for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Disneyland Park, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (35 in minimum; 27.9 ft tall, 21.7 mph, no inversions).
  2. Step up: Matterhorn Bobsleds, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (42 in minimum; 80 ft tall, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, the most intense coaster in the park (40 in minimum; 28 mph, no inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Disneyland Park?

Roller coaster height requirements at Disneyland Park run from 35 in (89 cm) (Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster) to 42 in (107 cm) (Matterhorn Bobsleds), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Disneyland Park have?

Disneyland Park has 4 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Disneyland Park?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad (28 mph, no inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Disneyland Park?

Matterhorn Bobsleds, at 80 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Disneyland Park?
