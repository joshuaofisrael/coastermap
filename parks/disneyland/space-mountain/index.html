Space Mountain

Disneyland Park · Anaheim, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); top speed 32 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1977.

Space Mountain is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California. It opened on 27 May 1977. The ride reaches 32 mph and runs 3,459 ft of track. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

TypeSteel, Sit Down, Family, Enclosed, Onboard Sound

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Because much of the ride is themed or in the dark, the front rows give the clearest view of the show scenes and effects, while the back rows feel the drops a little more.

Before you ride

  • Height is checked at the ride entrance. If your child is close to the line, ask the ride team before you queue.
  • Rides close for weather and maintenance. Check the Disneyland Park app or official site for today's status and line-skip options.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at each ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Space Mountain?

Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall to ride Space Mountain, according to Disneyland Resort, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Space Mountain go?

Its top speed is 32 mph (51 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Space Mountain go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Space Mountain scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Space Mountain open?

Space Mountain opened on 27 May 1977.
