Big Thunder Mountain Railroad

Disneyland Park · Anaheim, California

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 40 in (102 cm); top speed 28 mph; 0 inversions; opened 1979.

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Disneyland Park in Anaheim, California, built by Dynamic Attractions. It opened on 2 Sep 1979. The ride reaches 28 mph and runs 2,671 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Big Thunder Mountain Railroad is like

Opened in 1979, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Disneyland Park.

A rider who just meets its 40 in minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Disneyland, out of 3 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad sits on the west side of Disneyland's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Matterhorn Bobsleds, roughly 220 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster at about 240 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Big Thunder Mountain Railroad?

Riders must be at least 40 in (102 cm) tall to ride Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, according to Disneyland Resort, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad go?

Its top speed is 28 mph (45 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Big Thunder Mountain Railroad open?

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad opened on 2 Sep 1979.

Who built Big Thunder Mountain Railroad?

Big Thunder Mountain Railroad was manufactured by Dynamic Attractions.

How long is the track on Big Thunder Mountain Railroad?

RCDB lists 2,671 ft (814 m) of track.

How does Big Thunder Mountain Railroad get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at once?

Up to 30 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,400 riders.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28 mph; the minimum height is 40 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at Disneyland the same as Big Thunder Mountain Railroad at Magic Kingdom?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Disneyland ride opened in 1979 and the Magic Kingdom ride in 1980; Disneyland sets 40 in and Magic Kingdom sets 38 in.
