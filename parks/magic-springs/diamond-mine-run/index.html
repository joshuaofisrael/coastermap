Diamond Mine Run

Magic Springs Theme and Water Park · Hot Springs, Arkansas

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm), or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Diamond Mine Run is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Magic Springs Theme and Water Park in Hot Springs, Arkansas, built by E&F Miler Industries. It opened in 2000. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Diamond Mine Run is like

Opened in 2000, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Magic Springs Theme and Water Park. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to E&F Miler Industries's Family Coaster / 16ft Outside Spiral CCW design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Great Chase at Six Flags New England, Blazin' Buckaroo at Elitch Gardens and Kettle Creek Mine at Playland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Magic Springs sets 42 in, while Six Flags New England sets 36 in and Playland sets 36 in.

Its 42 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Magic Springs (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Diamond Mine Run sits near the middle of Magic Springs' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Big Bad John, roughly 130 m away (42 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gauntlet at about 170 m. Before you ride Diamond Mine Run

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 42 in (107 cm), and riders from 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • The park lists it as Diamond Mine Coaster: riders 36 to 42 inches must be accompanied by a supervising companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Diamond Mine Run?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Diamond Mine Run, or 36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm) with a companion. The park lists it as Diamond Mine Coaster: riders 36 to 42 inches must be accompanied by a supervising companion, according to Magic Springs Theme and Water Park, official family rides page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Diamond Mine Run go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Diamond Mine Run scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Diamond Mine Run open?

Diamond Mine Run opened in 2000.

Who built Diamond Mine Run?

Diamond Mine Run was manufactured by E&F Miler Industries.

How does Diamond Mine Run get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Diamond Mine Run at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Diamond Mine Run good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 42 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Diamond Mine Run at Magic Springs the same as Great Chase at Six Flags New England?

Both are E&F Miler Industries Family Coaster / 16ft Outside Spiral CCW coasters on RCDB; the Magic Springs ride opened in 2000 and the Six Flags New England ride in 1996; Magic Springs sets 42 in and Six Flags New England sets 36 in.
