Silverwood Theme Park height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Athol, Idaho · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 48 in (122 cm) on Tremors to 54 in (137 cm) on Aftershock. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Silverwood Theme Park are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 5 as extreme, which makes Silverwood Theme Park a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 71% of its lineup, against 43% across all 170 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 6 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 7 go upside down.

The most common builder is Custom Coasters International, Inc., with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Corkscrew, which opened in 1990.

What can my child ride at Silverwood Theme Park? By height

At Silverwood Theme Park the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Krazy Koaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 5 coasters, the nearest being Tremors at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Tremors (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)), Krazy Koaster (36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm)) and Timber Terror (42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Stunt Pilot at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 6 coasters with a verified rule: Tremors, Krazy Koaster, Stunt Pilot, Timber Terror and Corkscrew. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Aftershock at 54 in (137 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 6 of 6 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Aftershock. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Aftershock (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Silverwood Theme Park

These are the rules Silverwood Theme Park states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Tremors: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Krazy Koaster: riders 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Timber Terror: riders 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Silverwood Theme Park for first-timers
