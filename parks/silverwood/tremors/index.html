Tremors

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 50 mph; 85 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1999.

Tremors is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It opened on 15 May 1999. The ride stands 85 ft tall, drops 99 ft, reaches 50 mph and runs 3,158 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tremors is like

With 3,158 ft of track for a 85 ft structure, Tremors is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge. Its 99 ft drop is longer than its 85 ft height, which means the first drop falls below the level where it starts, into a trench, tunnel or down a hillside. RCDB lists a peak force of 2.5 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1999, it is the 4th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Silverwood (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tremors sits on the south-west side of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Timber Terror, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Stunt Pilot at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Tremors

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tremors?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Tremors, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tremors?

Tremors is 85 ft tall, with a drop of 99 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tremors go?

Its top speed is 50 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tremors go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tremors scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Tremors open?

Tremors opened on 15 May 1999.

Who built Tremors?

Tremors was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.

How long is the ride on Tremors?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 40 seconds over 3,158 ft of track.

How does Tremors get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tremors at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Tremors good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 50 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
