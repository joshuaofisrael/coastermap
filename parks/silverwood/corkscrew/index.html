Corkscrew

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 46 mph; 70 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 1990.

Corkscrew is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by Arrow Dynamics. It opened in 1990. The ride stands 70 ft tall, drops 62 ft, reaches 46 mph and runs 1,250 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Corkscrew is like

At 70 ft tall with 1,250 ft of track, Corkscrew balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,250 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 1990, it is the oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the 2nd of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure and Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Silverwood sets 48 in, while Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Silverwood (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Corkscrew sits on the north side of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Stunt Pilot, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tiny Toot at about 190 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Corkscrew compares

On our intensity ranking Corkscrew is number 3 of 7 at Silverwood Theme Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 5th tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Corkscrew

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Silverwood Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Corkscrew?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Corkscrew, according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Corkscrew?

Corkscrew is 70 ft tall, with a drop of 62 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Corkscrew go?

Its top speed is 46 mph (74 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Corkscrew go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Corkscrew scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Corkscrew open?

Corkscrew opened in 1990.

Who built Corkscrew?

Corkscrew was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the track on Corkscrew?

RCDB lists 1,250 ft (381 m) of track.

How does Corkscrew get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Corkscrew at once?

Each train carries 24 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 600 riders.

Is Corkscrew good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 46 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Corkscrew operate before?

RCDB records it at Knott's Berry Farm (as Corkscrew).

Is Corkscrew at Silverwood the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Silverwood ride opened in 1990 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; both list a 48 in minimum.
