Aftershock

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 61.5 mph; 177.2 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2008.

Aftershock is a steel inverted roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by Vekoma. It opened on 21 Jul 2008. The ride stands 177.2 ft tall, reaches 61.5 mph and runs 1204.1 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 3 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Aftershock is like

For a 177.2 ft ride, 1,204.1 ft of track is short: Aftershock is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,204.1 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2008, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Giant Inverted Boomerang design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Stunt Fall at Parque Warner. Every listed park with a verified rule sets the same minimum, 54 in.

Its 54 in minimum is the strictest at Silverwood. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Aftershock sits on the south-west side of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Timber Terror, roughly 160 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tremors at about 170 m. Aftershock layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a vertical catch-car lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (4 entries):

  1. Catch Car Vertical Lift
  2. Cobra Roll (110 ft tall)
  3. Loop (102 ft tall)
  4. Catch Car Vertical Lift

RCDB counts 3 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 80 in (203 cm).

Best seat to ride

Choose an outside seat for the most open feeling and the front row for a clear view; the back row feels the drops hardest because the train pulls it over the top at speed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Aftershock?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Aftershock. The maximum rider height is 80 in (203 cm), according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Aftershock?

Aftershock is 177.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Aftershock go?

Its top speed is 61.5 mph (99 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Aftershock go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Aftershock scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Aftershock open?

Aftershock opened on 21 Jul 2008.

Who built Aftershock?

Aftershock was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the track on Aftershock?

RCDB lists 1204.1 ft of track.

How does Aftershock get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a vertical catch-car lift; it has no launch.

How many people ride Aftershock at once?

RCDB's train details give 32 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 870 riders.

Is Aftershock good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 61.5 mph; the minimum height is 54 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Aftershock operate before?

RCDB records it at Six Flags Great America (as Déjà Vu).

Is Aftershock at Silverwood the same as Stunt Fall at Parque Warner?

Both are Vekoma Giant Inverted Boomerang coasters on RCDB; the Silverwood ride opened in 2008 and the Parque Warner ride in 2002; both list a 54 in minimum.
