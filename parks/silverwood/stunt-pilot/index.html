Stunt Pilot

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52 mph; 105 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2021.

Stunt Pilot is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 29 May 2021. The ride stands 105 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,800 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Stunt Pilot is like

Stunt Pilot pairs a 105 ft structure with 1,800 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,800 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2021, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Silverwood (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Stunt Pilot sits near the middle of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Corkscrew, roughly 80 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tiny Toot at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Stunt Pilot compares

On our intensity ranking Stunt Pilot is number 2 of 7 at Silverwood Theme Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 6 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Corkscrew (70 ft tall, 46 mph, 2 inversions; 48 in minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Aftershock (177.2 ft tall, 61.5 mph, 3 inversions, 4.5 g; 54 in minimum).

Before you ride Stunt Pilot

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Silverwood Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Stunt Pilot?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Stunt Pilot, according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Stunt Pilot?

Stunt Pilot is 105 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Stunt Pilot go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Stunt Pilot go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Stunt Pilot scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Stunt Pilot open?

Stunt Pilot opened on 29 May 2021.

Who built Stunt Pilot?

Stunt Pilot was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Stunt Pilot?

RCDB lists 1,800 ft (549 m) of track.

How does Stunt Pilot get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Stunt Pilot at once?

RCDB's train details give 10 seats per train.

Is Stunt Pilot good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
