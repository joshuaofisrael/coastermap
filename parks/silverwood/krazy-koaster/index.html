Krazy Koaster

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion; 14 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2014.

Krazy Koaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened in 2014. The ride stands 14 ft tall and runs 190 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Krazy Koaster is like

At 14 ft tall with 190 ft of track, Krazy Koaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2014, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park. It is one of 7 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A design, and the first of them to open. Others include Whirlwind at Waldameer, Spin-O-Rama at Cliff's, Texas Twister at Julianatoren and Slingshot at Camden Park. Height rules for this model differ by park: Silverwood sets 48 in, while Julianatoren sets 120 cm and Doha Quest sets 130 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Silverwood (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Krazy Koaster sits on the north-east side of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tiny Toot, roughly 80 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Stunt Pilot at about 200 m. Before you ride Krazy Koaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm), and riders from 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Silverwood Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Krazy Koaster?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Krazy Koaster, or 36 to 48 in (91 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Krazy Koaster?

Krazy Koaster is 14 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Krazy Koaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Krazy Koaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Krazy Koaster open?

Krazy Koaster opened in 2014.

Who built Krazy Koaster?

Krazy Koaster was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Krazy Koaster?

RCDB lists 190 ft (58 m) of track.

Is Krazy Koaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 48 in. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Krazy Koaster at Silverwood the same as Slingshot at Camden Park?

Both are SBF Visa Group Spinning Coasters / MX608 Park Model A coasters on RCDB; the Silverwood ride opened in 2014 and the Camden Park ride in 2016; both list a 48 in minimum.
