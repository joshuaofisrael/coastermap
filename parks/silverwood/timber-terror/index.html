Timber Terror

Silverwood Theme Park · Athol, Idaho

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm), or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion; top speed 55 mph; 85 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1996.

Timber Terror is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Silverwood Theme Park in Athol, Idaho, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It first opened in 1996 and was previously known as Grizzly. The ride stands 85 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 2,700 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Timber Terror is like

With 2,700 ft of track for a 85 ft structure, Timber Terror is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1996, it is the 2nd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Silverwood Theme Park. It has run under other names (Grizzly), so older guides and photos may use those.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Silverwood (tied with 4 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Timber Terror sits on the south side of Silverwood's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tremors, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Aftershock at about 160 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Timber Terror?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Timber Terror, or 42 to 48 in (107 to 122 cm) with a companion, according to Silverwood, official Size Guide (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Timber Terror?

Timber Terror is 85 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Timber Terror go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Timber Terror go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Timber Terror scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Timber Terror open?

Timber Terror opened in 1996 (previously known as Grizzly).

Who built Timber Terror?

Timber Terror was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.

How long is the ride on Timber Terror?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 32 seconds over 2,700 ft of track.

Is Timber Terror good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
