Rutschebanen

Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) · Klampenborg, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 34.2 mph; 72.2 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1932.

Rutschebanen is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) in Klampenborg, Denmark. It opened on 16 May 1932. The ride stands 72.2 ft tall, reaches 34.2 mph and runs 2795.3 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Rutschebanen is like

With 2,795.3 ft of track for a 72.2 ft structure, Rutschebanen is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1932, it is the oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken).

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Bakken, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Rutschebanen sits near the middle of Bakken's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is De Vilde Mus, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tornado at about 80 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Rutschebanen?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Rutschebanen, or 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 100 to 120 cm must ride with an adult, according to Bakken, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Rutschebanen?

Rutschebanen is 72.2 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Rutschebanen go?

Its top speed is 34.2 mph (55 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Rutschebanen go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Rutschebanen scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Rutschebanen open?

Rutschebanen opened on 16 May 1932.

How long is the ride on Rutschebanen?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes 30 seconds over 2795.3 ft of track.

How does Rutschebanen get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a cable lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Rutschebanen at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Rutschebanen use?

RCDB lists: t-bar.

Is Rutschebanen good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 34.2 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Rutschebanen at Bakken the same as Rutschebanen at Tivoli?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Bakken ride opened in 1932 and the Tivoli ride in 1914; RCDB lists heights of 72.2 ft and 39.4 ft.
