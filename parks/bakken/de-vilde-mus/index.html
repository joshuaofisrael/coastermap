De Vilde Mus

Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) · Klampenborg, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 107 to 120 cm (about 42.1 to 47.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2012.

De Vilde Mus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) in Klampenborg, Denmark, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 29 Mar 2012. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding De Vilde Mus is like

Opened in 2012, it is the newest of the 5 coasters we list at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken). It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG's Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile, Rev. 2 design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include The Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Great Adventure, The Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Great America, Dark Knight Coaster at Six Flags Mexico and Descente en Schlitt' at Nigloland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bakken sets 120 cm, while Six Flags Great Adventure sets 48 in and Six Flags Great America sets 48 in.

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Bakken, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 107 to 120 cm (about 42.1 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, De Vilde Mus sits on the west side of Bakken's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rutschebanen, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Tornado at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 107 to 120 cm (about 42.1 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride De Vilde Mus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 120 cm (about 47.2 in), and riders from 107 to 120 cm (about 42.1 to 47.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders 107 to 120 cm must ride with an adult; the park also sets a minimum age of 8 to ride without an adult.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for De Vilde Mus?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride De Vilde Mus, or 107 to 120 cm (about 42.1 to 47.2 in) with a companion. Riders 107 to 120 cm must ride with an adult; the park also sets a minimum age of 8 to ride without an adult, according to Bakken, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does De Vilde Mus go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is De Vilde Mus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did De Vilde Mus open?

De Vilde Mus opened on 29 Mar 2012.

Who built De Vilde Mus?

De Vilde Mus was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How does De Vilde Mus get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride De Vilde Mus at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is De Vilde Mus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is De Vilde Mus at Bakken the same as Descente en Schlitt' at Nigloland?

Both are Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG Wilde Maus / Compact Mobile, Rev. 2 coasters on RCDB; the Bakken ride opened in 2012 and the Nigloland ride in 2007; both list a 120 cm minimum.
