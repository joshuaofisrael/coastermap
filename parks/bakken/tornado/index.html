Tornado

Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) · Klampenborg, Denmark

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in); top speed 31.7 mph; 39.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2009.

Tornado is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) in Klampenborg, Denmark, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 9 Apr 2009. The ride stands 39.4 ft tall, reaches 31.7 mph and runs 1,122 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Tornado is like

Tornado pairs a 39.4 ft structure with 1,122 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it.

Opened in 2009, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken).

Its 120 cm minimum is the strictest at Bakken, shared with 2 other coasters. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Tornado sits near the middle of Bakken's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Rutschebanen, roughly 80 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Mariehønen at about 120 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Seat choice matters less on a spinning coaster: how much the car spins depends on how the weight is spread around it, so every ride is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Tornado?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Tornado, according to Bakken, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Tornado?

Tornado is 39.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Tornado go?

Its top speed is 31.7 mph (51 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Tornado go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Tornado scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Tornado open?

Tornado opened on 9 Apr 2009.

Who built Tornado?

Tornado was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the track on Tornado?

RCDB lists 1,122 ft (342 m) of track.

How does Tornado get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Tornado at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 480 riders.

Is Tornado good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 31.7 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
