Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Klampenborg, Denmark · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) in Klampenborg, Denmark has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. The height requirement is 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Tornado. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 3 of them.

What the coasters at Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 3 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) a balanced park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 232 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 0 of the 3 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Rutschebanen).

The most common builder is Intamin Amusement Rides, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Rutschebanen, which opened in 1932.

Bakken (Dyrehavsbakken) publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.
