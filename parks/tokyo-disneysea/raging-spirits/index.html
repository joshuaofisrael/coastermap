Raging Spirits

Tokyo DisneySea · Urayasu, Japan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 117 cm (about 46.1 in); top speed 37.3 mph; 52.5 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2005.

Raging Spirits is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Tokyo DisneySea in Urayasu, Japan, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 21 Jul 2005. The ride stands 52.5 ft tall, reaches 37.3 mph and runs 1968.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Raging Spirits is like

Raging Spirits spreads 1,968.5 ft of track across a layout only 52.5 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 1 time across 1,968.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster, roughly 150 m away (90 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 117 cm (about 46.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Raging Spirits

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 117 cm (about 46.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Tokyo DisneySea app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Raging Spirits?

Riders must be at least 117 cm (about 46.1 in) tall to ride Raging Spirits. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Tokyo Disney Resort, official FAQ on attractions with height requirements (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Raging Spirits?

Raging Spirits is 52.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Raging Spirits go?

Its top speed is 37.3 mph (60 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Raging Spirits go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Raging Spirits scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Raging Spirits open?

Raging Spirits opened on 21 Jul 2005.

Who built Raging Spirits?

Raging Spirits was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Raging Spirits?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 38 seconds over 1968.5 ft of track.

How does Raging Spirits get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Raging Spirits at once?

Each train carries 12 riders, according to RCDB.

What restraints does Raging Spirits use?

RCDB lists: shoulder harness.

Is Raging Spirits good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 37.3 mph; the minimum height is 117 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
