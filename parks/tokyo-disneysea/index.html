Tokyo DisneySea height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Urayasu, Japan · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tokyo DisneySea in Urayasu, Japan has 2 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 90 cm (about 35.4 in) on Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster to 117 cm (about 46.1 in) on Raging Spirits. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 2 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Tokyo DisneySea are like

Of the ride types, 1 of the 2 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster, which opened in 2001.

Tokyo DisneySea publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Tokyo DisneySea? By height

At Tokyo DisneySea the biggest single step is reaching 36 in (91 cm), which opens up 1 more coaster. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Raging Spirits at 117 cm (about 46.1 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Raging Spirits at 117 cm (about 46.1 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Raging Spirits. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 2 of 2 coasters with a verified rule: Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster and Raging Spirits. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Raging Spirits (up to 195 cm (about 76.8 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Tokyo DisneySea for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Tokyo DisneySea, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (90 cm minimum; 20 mph, no inversions).
  2. The big one: Raging Spirits, the most intense coaster in the park (117 cm minimum; 52.5 ft tall, 37.3 mph, 1 inversion).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
