Tokyo DisneySea roller coasters

Urayasu, Japan · 2 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Tokyo DisneySea in Urayasu, Japan has 2 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Raging Spirits at 52.5 ft, which is also the fastest at 37.3 mph. The lowest height requirement is 90 cm (about 35.4 in), for Flounder's Flying Fish Coaster.
