Timber Drop

Fraispertuis-City · Jeanménil, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 122 cm (about 48 in); top speed 41 mph; 96 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2011.

Timber Drop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Fraispertuis-City in Jeanménil, France, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 2 Jul 2011. The ride stands 96 ft tall and reaches 41 mph. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 113°, past vertical.

What riding Timber Drop is like

The steepest drop is 113°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down.

Opened in 2011, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Fraispertuis-City. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's El Loco design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Green Lantern Coaster at Movie World, Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach and Mumbo Jumbo at Flamingo Land. Height rules for this model differ by park: Fraispertuis-City sets 122 cm, while Movie World sets 140 cm.

Its 122 cm minimum is the strictest at Fraispertuis-City. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Timber Drop sits near the middle of Fraispertuis-City's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Ronde des Rondins, roughly 20 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Fireline Redwood at about 50 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

How Timber Drop compares

On our intensity ranking Timber Drop is number 1 of 4 at Fraispertuis-City, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Fireline Redwood (73.8 ft tall, 37.9 mph, no inversions, 2.7 g; 95 cm minimum).

Before you ride Timber Drop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 122 cm (about 48 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Fraispertuis-City app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Timber Drop?

Riders must be at least 122 cm (about 48 in) tall to ride Timber Drop, according to Fraispertuis-City, official attractions page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Timber Drop?

Timber Drop is 96 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Timber Drop go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Timber Drop go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Timber Drop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Timber Drop open?

Timber Drop opened on 2 Jul 2011.

Who built Timber Drop?

Timber Drop was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

How does Timber Drop get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Timber Drop at once?

Each train carries 4 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Timber Drop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 41 mph; the minimum height is 122 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Timber Drop at Fraispertuis-City the same as Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach?

Both are S&S Worldwide El Loco coasters on RCDB; the Fraispertuis-City ride opened in 2011 and the Indiana Beach ride in 2008; both list a 122 cm minimum.
