Young Star Coaster

Chimelong Paradise · Panyu, Guangzhou, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in), or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion; 0 inversions; opened 2010.

Young Star Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chimelong Paradise in Panyu, Guangzhou, China, built by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG. It opened on 8 Feb 2010. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Young Star Coaster is like

Opened in 2010, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Chimelong Paradise.

Its 130 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Chimelong Paradise (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class. Children from 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Young Star Coaster sits on the south-west side of Chimelong Paradise's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sky Rocket, roughly 400 m away (140 cm minimum). Next closest is Circus Roller Coaster at about 540 m. Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Young Star Coaster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Mild (family)

RCDB classes it as a family coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion.

Best seat to ride

On a small coaster any seat works. The front row gives the best view.

Before you ride Young Star Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in), and riders from 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Children 1 m to 1.3 m must ride with a parent.
  • Loose items, health warnings and accessibility rules are set by the park and posted at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Chimelong Paradise app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Young Star Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Young Star Coaster, or 100 to 130 cm (about 39.4 to 51.2 in) with a companion. Children 1 m to 1.3 m must ride with a parent, according to Chimelong Paradise, official attraction page (height limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Young Star Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Young Star Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Young Star Coaster open?

Young Star Coaster opened on 8 Feb 2010.

Who built Young Star Coaster?

Young Star Coaster was manufactured by Mack Rides GmbH & Co KG.

How does Young Star Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Young Star Coaster at once?

Up to 20 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Young Star Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 130 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.
