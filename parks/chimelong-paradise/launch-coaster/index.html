Launch Coaster

Chimelong Paradise · Panyu, Guangzhou, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 130 cm (about 51.2 in); top speed 60.4 mph; 118.8 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2025.

Launch Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chimelong Paradise in Panyu, Guangzhou, China, built by Jinma Rides. It opened in 2025. The ride stands 118.8 ft tall, reaches 60.4 mph and runs 1968.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Launch Coaster is like

Launch Coaster pairs a 118.8 ft structure with 1,968.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,968.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.1 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2025, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Chimelong Paradise.

Its 130 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Chimelong Paradise (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Launch Coaster layout, trains and ride system

Launch Coaster starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 3 track elements for Launch Coaster, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

  1. LSM Launch (rolling)
  2. Corkscrew ×2

Launch Coaster trains and ride system

Trains2 trains with 5 cars per train. Riders are arranged 2 across in 2 rows for a total of 20 riders per train.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Launch Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Launch Coaster is number 2 of 4 at Chimelong Paradise, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Circus Roller Coaster (196.8 ft tall, 69.6 mph, 1 inversion; 140 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Sky Rocket (150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion; 140 cm minimum).

Site-wide, Launch Coaster is taller than 79% and faster than 82% of the 1486 coasters we cover that have a listed figure.

Before you ride Launch Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 130 cm (about 51.2 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Children under 10 must ride with a parent.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Chimelong Paradise app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Launch Coaster?

Riders must be at least 130 cm (about 51.2 in) tall to ride Launch Coaster. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Children under 10 must ride with a parent, according to Chimelong Paradise, official attraction page (height limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Launch Coaster?

Launch Coaster is 118.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Launch Coaster go?

Its top speed is 60.4 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Launch Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Launch Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Launch Coaster open?

Launch Coaster opened in 2025.

Who built Launch Coaster?

Launch Coaster was manufactured by Jinma Rides.

How long is the track on Launch Coaster?

RCDB lists 1968.5 ft of track.

Does Launch Coaster launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that starts while the train is already moving.

How many people ride Launch Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train.

Is Launch Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 60.4 mph; the minimum height is 130 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
