Circus Roller Coaster

Chimelong Paradise · Panyu, Guangzhou, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 69.6 mph; 196.8 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2026.

Circus Roller Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chimelong Paradise in Panyu, Guangzhou, China, built by Bolliger & Mabillard. It opened on 30 Sep 2026. The ride stands 196.8 ft tall, reaches 69.6 mph and runs 3218.5 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 1 time. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Circus Roller Coaster is like

At 196.8 ft tall with 3,218.5 ft of track, Circus Roller Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 1 time across 3,218.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2026, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Chimelong Paradise.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Chimelong Paradise, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Circus Roller Coaster sits on the north-east side of Chimelong Paradise's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Sky Rocket, roughly 200 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Young Star Coaster at about 540 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Circus Roller Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Circus Roller Coaster is number 3 of 4 at Chimelong Paradise, where 1 is the most intense. It is the tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Young Star Coaster (no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Launch Coaster (118.8 ft tall, 60.4 mph, 2 inversions, 4.1 g; 130 cm minimum).

Before you ride Circus Roller Coaster

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Children under 12 must ride with a parent.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Chimelong Paradise app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Circus Roller Coaster?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Circus Roller Coaster. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). Children under 12 must ride with a parent, according to Chimelong Paradise, official attraction page (height limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Circus Roller Coaster?

Circus Roller Coaster is 196.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Circus Roller Coaster go?

Its top speed is 69.6 mph (112 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Circus Roller Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Circus Roller Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Circus Roller Coaster open?

Circus Roller Coaster opened on 30 Sep 2026.

Who built Circus Roller Coaster?

Circus Roller Coaster was manufactured by Bolliger & Mabillard.

How long is the track on Circus Roller Coaster?

RCDB lists 3218.5 ft of track.

How does Circus Roller Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Circus Roller Coaster at once?

Each train carries 30 riders, according to RCDB.

Is Circus Roller Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 69.6 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
