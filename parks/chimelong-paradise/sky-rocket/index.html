Sky Rocket

Chimelong Paradise · Panyu, Guangzhou, China

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 140 cm (about 55.1 in); top speed 62 mph; 150 ft tall; 1 inversion; opened 2017.

Sky Rocket is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Chimelong Paradise in Panyu, Guangzhou, China, built by Premier Rides. It opened on 26 Sep 2017. The ride stands 150 ft tall, reaches 62 mph and runs 863 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 1 time.

What riding Sky Rocket is like

Sky Rocket packs its 863 ft of track into a tall, compact footprint (150 ft high), so the big moments come quickly and close together. It goes upside down 1 time across 863 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2017, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Chimelong Paradise. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Premier Rides's Sky Rocket / II design, and the 5th of them to open. Others include Tigris at Busch Gardens Tampa, Tempesto at Busch Gardens Williamsburg, Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom and Electric Eel at SeaWorld San Diego. Height rules for this model differ by park: Chimelong Paradise sets 140 cm, while Busch Gardens Tampa sets 54 in, Busch Gardens Williamsburg sets 54 in and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.

Its 140 cm minimum is the strictest at Chimelong Paradise, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Sky Rocket sits on the east side of Chimelong Paradise's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Circus Roller Coaster, roughly 200 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Young Star Coaster at about 400 m. Sky Rocket layout, trains and ride system

Sky Rocket starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Element by element, as listed on RCDB (3 entries):

  1. LSM Launch (multi-pass)
  2. In-Line Twist
  3. Non Inverting Loop

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 1 time. Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall, and no taller than 195 cm (about 76.8 in).

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Before you ride Sky Rocket

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 140 cm (about 55.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 195 cm (about 76.8 in). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 1 inversion; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Chimelong Paradise app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Sky Rocket?

Riders must be at least 140 cm (about 55.1 in) tall to ride Sky Rocket. The maximum rider height is 195 cm (about 76.8 in), according to Chimelong Paradise, official attraction page (height limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Sky Rocket?

Sky Rocket is 150 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Sky Rocket go?

Its top speed is 62 mph (100 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Sky Rocket go upside down?

Yes. It has 1 inversion.

Is Sky Rocket scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Sky Rocket open?

Sky Rocket opened on 26 Sep 2017.

Who built Sky Rocket?

Sky Rocket was manufactured by Premier Rides.

How long is the track on Sky Rocket?

RCDB lists 863 ft (263 m) of track.

Does Sky Rocket launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

How many people ride Sky Rocket at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Sky Rocket good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 1 time; it tops out at 62 mph; the minimum height is 140 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Sky Rocket at Chimelong Paradise the same as Superman Ultimate Flight at Discovery Kingdom?

Both are Premier Rides Sky Rocket / II coasters on RCDB; the Chimelong Paradise ride opened in 2017 and the Discovery Kingdom ride in 2012; Chimelong Paradise sets 140 cm and Discovery Kingdom sets 54 in.
