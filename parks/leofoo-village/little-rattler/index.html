Little Rattler

Leofoo Village Theme Park · Guanxi, Hsinchu County, Taiwan

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 90 cm (about 35.4 in); top speed 28.5 mph; 42.7 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1993.

Little Rattler is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Leofoo Village Theme Park in Guanxi, Hsinchu County, Taiwan, built by Vekoma. It opened in 1993. The ride stands 42.7 ft tall, reaches 28.5 mph and runs 1099.1 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Little Rattler is like

At 42.7 ft tall with 1,099.1 ft of track, Little Rattler balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 1993, it is the oldest of the 3 coasters we list at Leofoo Village Theme Park. It is one of 13 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's Junior Coaster / 335m design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Woodstock Express at Cedar Point, Flight of the Hippogriff at Islands of Adventure, Flight of the Hippogriff at Universal Studios Japan and The Dragon at Legoland Florida. Height rules for this model differ by park: Leofoo Village sets 90 cm, while Cedar Point sets 48 in, Universal Studios Japan sets 122 cm and Legoland Florida sets 48 in.

Its 90 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Leofoo Village (tied with 1 other), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Little Rattler sits near the middle of Leofoo Village's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Screaming Condor, roughly 100 m away (140 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Sahara Twist at about 250 m. Little Rattler layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Little Rattler?

Riders must be at least 90 cm (about 35.4 in) tall to ride Little Rattler. Chinese name 西部瘋狂列車, according to Leofoo Village Theme Park, official park rules (ride height limits) (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Little Rattler?

Little Rattler is 42.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Little Rattler go?

Its top speed is 28.5 mph (46 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Little Rattler go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Little Rattler scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Little Rattler open?

Little Rattler opened in 1993.

Who built Little Rattler?

Little Rattler was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Little Rattler?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 6 seconds over 1099.1 ft of track.

How does Little Rattler get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride Little Rattler at once?

RCDB's train details give 16 seats per train.

Is Little Rattler good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 28.5 mph; the minimum height is 90 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Little Rattler at Leofoo Village the same as K3 Roller Skater at Plopsaland De Panne?

Both are Vekoma Junior Coaster / 335m coasters on RCDB; the Leofoo Village ride opened in 1993 and the Plopsaland De Panne ride in 1991; Leofoo Village sets 90 cm and Plopsaland De Panne sets 120 cm.
