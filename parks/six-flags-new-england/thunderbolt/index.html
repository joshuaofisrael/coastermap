Thunderbolt

Six Flags New England · Agawam, Massachusetts

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 40 mph; 70 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1941.

Thunderbolt is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts. It opened in 1941. The ride stands 70 ft tall, reaches 40 mph and runs 2,600 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Thunderbolt is like

With 2,600 ft of track for a 70 ft structure, Thunderbolt is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 1941, it is the oldest of the 12 coasters we list at Six Flags New England.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 6 other coasters at Six Flags New England, out of 11 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Thunderbolt sits near the middle of Six Flags New England's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Great Chase, roughly 70 m away (36 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum at about 150 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Thunderbolt?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Thunderbolt, according to Six Flags New England, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Thunderbolt?

Thunderbolt is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Thunderbolt go?

Its top speed is 40 mph (64 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Thunderbolt go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Thunderbolt scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Thunderbolt open?

Thunderbolt opened in 1941.

Is Thunderbolt at Six Flags New England the same as Thunderbolt at Kennywood?

They share a name but are separate rides; the Six Flags New England ride opened in 1941 and the Kennywood ride in 1924; Six Flags New England sets 48 in and Kennywood sets 52 in.
