Pandemonium

Six Flags New England · Agawam, Massachusetts

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 47 in (119 cm), or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion; 42 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2005.

Pandemonium is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts, built by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH. It first opened on 16 Apr 2005 and was previously known as Mr. Six's Pandemonium. The ride stands 42 ft tall. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pandemonium is like

Opened in 2005, it is the 8th oldest of the 12 coasters we list at Six Flags New England. It has run under other names (Mr. Six's Pandemonium), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 47 in minimum can also ride 1 other coaster at Six Flags New England, out of 11 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class. Children from 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pandemonium sits on the north-west side of Six Flags New England's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Flashback, roughly 70 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Wicked Cyclone at about 90 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall, or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion.

Before you ride Pandemonium

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 47 in (119 cm), and riders from 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) need a companion. Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • If you get motion sick, note that this ride spins. See our motion sickness tips.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Six Flags New England app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pandemonium?

Riders must be at least 47 in (119 cm) tall to ride Pandemonium, or 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) with a companion, according to Six Flags New England, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pandemonium?

Pandemonium is 42 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Pandemonium go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pandemonium scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 4 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Pandemonium open?

Pandemonium opened on 16 Apr 2005 (previously known as Mr. Six's Pandemonium).

Who built Pandemonium?

Pandemonium was manufactured by Gerstlauer Amusement Rides GmbH.

How does Pandemonium get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Pandemonium at once?

RCDB's train details give 4 seats per train.

Is Pandemonium good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; the minimum height is 47 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
