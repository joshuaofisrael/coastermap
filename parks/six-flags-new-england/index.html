Six Flags New England roller coasters

Agawam, Massachusetts · 12 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts has 12 operating roller coasters listed on RCDB as of 10 Nov 2026. The tallest is Superman the Ride at 208 ft, which is also the fastest at 77 mph. The lowest height requirement is 36 in (91 cm), for Great Chase.

Frequently asked questions

How many roller coasters does Six Flags New England have?

Six Flags New England has 12 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags New England?

Superman the Ride, at 208 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags New England?

Superman the Ride, with a top speed of 77 mph (RCDB).

What is the easiest coaster for kids at Six Flags New England?

By height requirement, Great Chase has the lowest minimum at 36 in (91 cm). Check the park for companion rules.

Which Six Flags New England coasters can a 42-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Great Chase. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which Six Flags New England coasters can a 48-inch child ride?

Based on official minimum heights checked 10 Nov 2026: Thunderbolt, Great Chase, Flashback, Catwoman Whip, Pandemonium, Wicked Cyclone, The Joker. Some rides also need a companion below a certain height; confirm at the ride.

Which coasters at Six Flags New England do not go upside down?

Thunderbolt, Great Chase, Superman the Ride, Catwoman Whip, Pandemonium, Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum, The Joker, Quantum Accelerator have no inversions, per RCDB.
