Six Flags New England height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Agawam, Massachusetts · 12 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts has 12 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 36 in (91 cm) on Great Chase to 54 in (137 cm) on Superman the Ride. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 7 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 12 of them.

What the coasters at Six Flags New England are like

RCDB classes 2 coasters here as kiddie or family, 4 as thrill and 6 as extreme, which makes Six Flags New England a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 50% of its lineup, against 46% across all 122 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 10 of the 12 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 12 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Thunderbolt); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Quantum Accelerator).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Thunderbolt, which opened in 1941.

What can my child ride at Six Flags New England? By height

At Six Flags New England the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 6 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 12 coasters with a verified rule: Great Chase. Still too short for 11 coasters, the nearest being Pandemonium at 47 in (119 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 12 coasters with a verified rule: Great Chase. With a companion they can also ride Catwoman Whip (41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm)). Still too short for 10 coasters, the nearest being Pandemonium at 47 in (119 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 7 of 12 coasters with a verified rule, 6 more than at the height below: Thunderbolt, Flashback, Catwoman Whip, Pandemonium, Wicked Cyclone and The Joker. With a companion they can also ride Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum (46 to 54 in (117 to 137 cm)) and Quantum Accelerator (48 to 52 in (122 to 132 cm)). Still too short for 3 coasters, the nearest being Riddler Revenge at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 12 of 12 coasters with a verified rule, 5 more than at the height below: Riddler Revenge, Superman the Ride, Batman: The Dark Knight, Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum and Quantum Accelerator. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Riddler Revenge (up to 78 in (198 cm)), Superman the Ride (up to 76 in (193 cm)), Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum (up to 75 in (190 cm)), The Joker (up to 77 in (196 cm)) and Quantum Accelerator (up to 77 in (196 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Six Flags New England

These are the rules Six Flags New England states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Catwoman Whip: riders 41 to 48 in (104 to 122 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Pandemonium: riders 43 to 47 in (109 to 119 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum: riders 46 to 54 in (117 to 137 cm) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Quantum Accelerator: riders 48 to 52 in (122 to 132 cm) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Six Flags New England for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Six Flags New England, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Great Chase, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (36 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Gotham City Gauntlet Escape from Arkham Asylum, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (54 in minimum; 49.3 ft tall, 28 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: Batman: The Dark Knight, the most intense coaster in the park (54 in minimum; 117.8 ft tall, 55 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

What are the height requirements at Six Flags New England?

Roller coaster height requirements at Six Flags New England run from 36 in (91 cm) (Great Chase) to 54 in (137 cm) (Superman the Ride), according to official park sources checked 10 Nov 2026. Rules are checked at each ride entrance.

How many roller coasters does Six Flags New England have?

Six Flags New England has 12 operating roller coasters according to RCDB, checked 10 Nov 2026.

What is the scariest roller coaster at Six Flags New England?

By our intensity ranking, which uses RCDB class, height, speed, inversions and launches, it is Batman: The Dark Knight (117.8 ft tall, 55 mph, 5 inversions).

What is the tallest roller coaster at Six Flags New England?

Superman the Ride, at 208 ft tall (RCDB).

What is the fastest roller coaster at Six Flags New England?

Superman the Ride, with a top speed of 77 mph (RCDB).

What is the best coaster at Six Flags New England for first-timers?

What can a 42-inch child ride at Six Flags New England?

Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child meets the official minimum on: Great Chase. With a companion they can also ride Catwoman Whip.

What can a 48-inch child ride at Six Flags New England?
