Superman the Ride

Six Flags New England · Agawam, Massachusetts

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 54 in (137 cm); top speed 77 mph; 208 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2000.

Superman the Ride is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It first opened on 5 May 2000 and was previously known as Bizarro and later Superman - Ride Of Steel. The ride stands 208 ft tall, drops 221 ft, reaches 77 mph and runs 5,400 ft of track. That height makes it a hyper coaster (200 to 299 ft tall). It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall, and no taller than 76 in (193 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Superman the Ride?

Riders must be at least 54 in (137 cm) tall to ride Superman the Ride. The maximum rider height is 76 in (193 cm), according to Six Flags New England, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Superman the Ride?

Superman the Ride is 208 ft tall, with a drop of 221 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does Superman the Ride go?

Its top speed is 77 mph (124 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Superman the Ride go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Superman the Ride scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Superman the Ride open?

Superman the Ride opened on 5 May 2000 (previously known as Bizarro and later Superman - Ride Of Steel).

Who built Superman the Ride?

Superman the Ride was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.
