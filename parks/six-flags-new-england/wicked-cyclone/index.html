Wicked Cyclone

Six Flags New England · Agawam, Massachusetts

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 55 mph; 109 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2015.

Wicked Cyclone is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 24 May 2015. The ride stands 109 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 3,320 ft of track. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Seat advice is general guidance based on how this style of coaster moves, not a park rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Wicked Cyclone?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Wicked Cyclone, according to Six Flags New England, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Wicked Cyclone?

Wicked Cyclone is 109 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Wicked Cyclone go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Wicked Cyclone go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Wicked Cyclone scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Wicked Cyclone open?

Wicked Cyclone opened on 24 May 2015.

Who built Wicked Cyclone?

Wicked Cyclone was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.
