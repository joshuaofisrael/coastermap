The Joker

Six Flags New England · Agawam, Massachusetts

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 38 mph; 120 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2017.

The Joker is a steel wing roller coaster at Six Flags New England in Agawam, Massachusetts, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 27 May 2017. The ride stands 120 ft tall, drops 54 ft, reaches 38 mph and runs 1,019 ft of track. On a wing coaster the seats stick out on either side of the track, so the outer riders have nothing above or below them. It does not turn riders upside down.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

The front row gives an unobstructed view, which is the main reason riders choose it on this style of coaster. Outer seats feel more open; the back row is pulled over the drops faster and so tends to give the strongest drop sensation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for The Joker?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride The Joker. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Six Flags New England, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is The Joker?

The Joker is 120 ft tall, with a drop of 54 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does The Joker go?

Its top speed is 38 mph (61 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does The Joker go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is The Joker scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster; Six Flags New England rates it 5 out of 5 on its own thrill scale. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did The Joker open?

The Joker opened on 27 May 2017.

Who built The Joker?

The Joker was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.
