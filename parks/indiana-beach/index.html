Indiana Beach height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Monticello, Indiana · 7 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana has 7 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 46 in (117 cm) on Cornball Express to 59 in (150 cm) on All American Triple Loop. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 5 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 6 of them.

What the coasters at Indiana Beach are like

RCDB classes 0 coasters here as kiddie or family, 5 as thrill and 2 as extreme, which makes Indiana Beach a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 29% of its lineup, against 50% across all 96 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 6 of the 7 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 2 of the 7 go upside down; 3 are wooden coasters (Hoosier Hurricane, Lost Coaster of Superstition Mountain and Cornball Express).

The most common builder is Custom Coasters International, Inc., with 3 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Tig'rr, which opened in 1984.

What can my child ride at Indiana Beach? By height

At Indiana Beach the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 5 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Indiana Beach for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Indiana Beach, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Cyclone, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (48 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Cornball Express, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (46 in minimum; 55 ft tall, 45 mph, no inversions).
  3. The big one: All American Triple Loop, the most intense coaster in the park (59 in minimum; 108.3 ft tall, 53.4 mph, 3 inversions, 6.1 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
