Steel Hawg

Indiana Beach · Monticello, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 41 mph; 96 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2008.

Steel Hawg is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana, built by S&S Worldwide. It opened on 5 Jul 2008. The ride stands 96 ft tall, reaches 41 mph and runs 1,276 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 111°, past vertical.

What riding Steel Hawg is like

At 96 ft tall with 1,276 ft of track, Steel Hawg balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. The steepest drop is 111°, past vertical, so riders tip slightly beyond straight down. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,276 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2008, it is the 5th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Indiana Beach. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to S&S Worldwide's El Loco design, and the first of them to open. Others include Green Lantern Coaster at Movie World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Indiana Beach sets 48 in, while Movie World sets 140 cm.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Indiana Beach, out of 6 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Steel Hawg sits on the north-west side of Indiana Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is All American Triple Loop, roughly 90 m away (59 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornball Express at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Before you ride Steel Hawg

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Riders may not sit on another rider's lap.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Indiana Beach app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Steel Hawg?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Steel Hawg. Riders may not sit on another rider's lap, according to Indiana Beach, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Steel Hawg?

Steel Hawg is 96 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Steel Hawg go?

Its top speed is 41 mph (66 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Steel Hawg go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Steel Hawg scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Steel Hawg open?

Steel Hawg opened on 5 Jul 2008.

Who built Steel Hawg?

Steel Hawg was manufactured by S&S Worldwide.

Is Steel Hawg at Indiana Beach the same as Green Lantern Coaster at Movie World?

Both are S&S Worldwide El Loco coasters on RCDB; the Indiana Beach ride opened in 2008 and the Movie World ride in 2011; Indiana Beach sets 48 in and Movie World sets 140 cm; RCDB lists heights of 96 ft and 108.3 ft.
