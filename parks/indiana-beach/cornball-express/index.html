Cornball Express

Indiana Beach · Monticello, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 46 in (117 cm); top speed 45 mph; 55 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2001.

Cornball Express is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana, built by Custom Coasters International, Inc. It opened on 18 May 2001. The ride stands 55 ft tall, reaches 45 mph and runs 2,100 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It is a hybrid coaster, mixing wooden and steel construction. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cornball Express is like

With 2,100 ft of track for a 55 ft structure, Cornball Express is a long, low-slung ride: most of the time is spent carving through turns and small hills rather than on one big plunge.

Opened in 2001, it is the 3rd oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Indiana Beach.

Its 46 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Indiana Beach, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cornball Express sits near the middle of Indiana Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Tig'rr, roughly 30 m away (54 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Steel Hawg at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cornball Express?

Riders must be at least 46 in (117 cm) tall to ride Cornball Express, according to Indiana Beach, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Cornball Express?

Cornball Express is 55 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Cornball Express go?

Its top speed is 45 mph (72 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Cornball Express go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cornball Express scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cornball Express open?

Cornball Express opened on 18 May 2001.

Who built Cornball Express?

Cornball Express was manufactured by Custom Coasters International, Inc.
