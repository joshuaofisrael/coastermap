All American Triple Loop

Indiana Beach · Monticello, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 59 in (150 cm); top speed 53.4 mph; 108.3 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2024.

All American Triple Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana, built by Schwarzkopf. It opened on 11 May 2024. The ride stands 108.3 ft tall, drops 100.9 ft, reaches 53.4 mph and runs 3379.3 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding All American Triple Loop is like

All American Triple Loop spreads 3,379.3 ft of track across a layout only 108.3 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 3 times across 3,379.3 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 6.1 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2024, it is the newest of the 7 coasters we list at Indiana Beach.

Its 59 in minimum is the strictest at Indiana Beach. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, All American Triple Loop sits on the north-west side of Indiana Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Steel Hawg, roughly 90 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornball Express at about 230 m. All American Triple Loop layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall, and no taller than 77 in (196 cm).

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride All American Triple Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 59 in (150 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Tall riders: the maximum is 77 in (196 cm). See can you be too tall?
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for All American Triple Loop?

Riders must be at least 59 in (150 cm) tall to ride All American Triple Loop. The maximum rider height is 77 in (196 cm), according to Indiana Beach, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is All American Triple Loop?

All American Triple Loop is 108.3 ft tall, with a drop of 100.9 ft, according to RCDB.

How fast does All American Triple Loop go?

Its top speed is 53.4 mph (86 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does All American Triple Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is All American Triple Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did All American Triple Loop open?

All American Triple Loop opened on 11 May 2024.

Who built All American Triple Loop?

All American Triple Loop was manufactured by Schwarzkopf.

How long is the track on All American Triple Loop?

RCDB lists 3379.3 ft of track.

How does All American Triple Loop get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

How many people ride All American Triple Loop at once?

Up to 12 per train, going by RCDB's train layout. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,500 riders.

Is All American Triple Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 53.4 mph; the minimum height is 59 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did All American Triple Loop operate before?

RCDB records it at Sunway Lagoon (as Triple Loop Coaster); Flamingo Land (as Magnum Force); Aztlán Parque Urbano (as Quimera).
