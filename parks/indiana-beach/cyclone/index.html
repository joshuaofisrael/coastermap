Cyclone

Indiana Beach · Monticello, Indiana

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Cyclone is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana. It opened on 30 Apr 2022. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Cyclone is like

Opened in 2022, it is the 6th oldest of the 7 coasters we list at Indiana Beach.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 4 other coasters at Indiana Beach, out of 6 with verified rules. Among thrill-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 48 in (122 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Cyclone sits on the east side of Indiana Beach's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Lost Coaster of Superstition Mountain, roughly 140 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Cornball Express at about 140 m. How intense is it?

Moderate (thrill)

RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Cyclone?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Cyclone, according to Indiana Beach, official ride page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

Does Cyclone go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Cyclone scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Cyclone open?

Cyclone opened on 30 Apr 2022.
