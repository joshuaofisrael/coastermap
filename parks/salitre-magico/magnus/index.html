Magnus

Salitre Mágico · Bogotá, Colombia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 135 cm (about 53.1 in); 70 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2000.

Magnus is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Salitre Mágico in Bogotá, Colombia, built by Arrow Dynamics. It first opened in 2000 and was previously known as Montaña Rusa. The ride stands 70 ft tall and runs 1,250 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Magnus is like

At 70 ft tall with 1,250 ft of track, Magnus balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,250 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2000, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Salitre Mágico. It has run under other names (Montaña Rusa), so older guides and photos may use those. It is one of 5 rides on Coaster Map built to Arrow Dynamics's Corkscrew design, and the 4th of them to open. Others include Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure, Corkscrew at Silverwood, Corkscrew at Nagashima Spa Land and Corkscrew at Rusutsu. Height rules for this model differ by park: Salitre Mágico sets 135 cm, while Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in, Silverwood sets 48 in and Nagashima Spa Land sets 110 cm.

A rider who just meets its 135 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Salitre Mágico, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Magnus sits on the north side of Salitre Mágico's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gusano Loco, roughly 270 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Doble Loop at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

Pick the front row for the view and the back rows for airtime: the train drags the rear cars over every crest faster than the front.

How Magnus compares

On our intensity ranking Magnus is number 4 of 5 at Salitre Mágico, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Gusano Loco (no inversions; 110 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Rallus (64 ft tall, 37.3 mph, 2 inversions).

Before you ride Magnus

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 135 cm (about 53.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Salitre Mágico app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Magnus?

Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall to ride Magnus, according to Salitre Mágico, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Magnus?

Magnus is 70 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Magnus go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Magnus scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Magnus open?

Magnus opened in 2000 (previously known as Montaña Rusa).

Who built Magnus?

Magnus was manufactured by Arrow Dynamics.

How long is the ride on Magnus?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 10 seconds over 1,250 ft of track.

How many people ride Magnus at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Magnus good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; the minimum height is 135 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Magnus operate before?

RCDB records it at Magic Harbor (as Corkscrew); Myrtle Beach Pavilion (as Corkscrew); Salitre Magico (as Magnus).

Is Magnus at Salitre Mágico the same as Corkscrew at Michigan's Adventure?

Both are Arrow Dynamics Corkscrew coasters on RCDB; the Salitre Mágico ride opened in 2000 and the Michigan's Adventure ride in 1979; Salitre Mágico sets 135 cm and Michigan's Adventure sets 48 in.
