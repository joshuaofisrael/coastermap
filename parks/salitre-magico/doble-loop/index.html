Doble Loop

Salitre Mágico · Bogotá, Colombia

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 135 cm (about 53.1 in); top speed 56 mph; 59.1 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2004.

Doble Loop is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Salitre Mágico in Bogotá, Colombia, built by Soquet. It opened on 12 Jul 2004. The ride stands 59.1 ft tall, reaches 56 mph and runs 2,822 ft of track. It turns riders upside down 2 times.

What riding Doble Loop is like

Doble Loop spreads 2,822 ft of track across a layout only 59.1 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. It goes upside down 2 times across 2,822 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2004, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Salitre Mágico.

A rider who just meets its 135 cm minimum can also ride 2 other coasters at Salitre Mágico, out of 3 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Doble Loop sits on the south-west side of Salitre Mágico's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Gusano Loco, roughly 130 m away (110 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Magnus at about 270 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Doble Loop compares

On our intensity ranking Doble Loop is number 2 of 5 at Salitre Mágico, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

Before you ride Doble Loop

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 135 cm (about 53.1 in). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Salitre Mágico app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Doble Loop?

Riders must be at least 135 cm (about 53.1 in) tall to ride Doble Loop, according to Salitre Mágico, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Doble Loop?

Doble Loop is 59.1 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Doble Loop go?

Its top speed is 56 mph (90 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Doble Loop go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Doble Loop scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Doble Loop open?

Doble Loop opened on 12 Jul 2004.

Who built Doble Loop?

Doble Loop was manufactured by Soquet.

How long is the track on Doble Loop?

RCDB lists 2,822 ft (860 m) of track.

How does Doble Loop get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Doble Loop at once?

Up to 24 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Doble Loop good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 56 mph; the minimum height is 135 cm. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Doble Loop operate before?

RCDB records it at Seabreeze (as Quantum Loop); Salitre Magico (as Doble Loop).
