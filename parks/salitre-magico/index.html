Salitre Mágico height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Bogotá, Colombia · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Salitre Mágico in Bogotá, Colombia has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 110 cm (about 43.3 in) on Gusano Loco to 140 cm (about 55.1 in) on Drakko: The Flying Beast. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 1 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 2 of them.

What the coasters at Salitre Mágico are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 0 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Salitre Mágico a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 80% of its lineup, against 40% across all 210 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 53.1 in, higher than the 48 in typical across the site, and 3 of the 4 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 4 of the 5 go upside down; 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Drakko: The Flying Beast).

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Magnus, which opened in 2000.

Salitre Mágico publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Salitre Mágico? By height

At Salitre Mágico the biggest single step is reaching 54 in (137 cm), which opens up 2 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

Best coasters at Salitre Mágico for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Salitre Mágico, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Gusano Loco, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (110 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. The big one: Drakko: The Flying Beast, the most intense coaster in the park (140 cm minimum; 150 ft tall, 62 mph, 1 inversion).
