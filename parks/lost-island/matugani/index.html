Matugani

Lost Island Theme Park · Waterloo, Iowa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 46.6 mph; 78.7 ft tall; 2 inversions; opened 2023.

Matugani is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lost Island Theme Park in Waterloo, Iowa, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 20 May 2023. The ride stands 78.7 ft tall, reaches 46.6 mph and runs 1443.5 ft of track. Instead of a slow chain lift, trains are launched up to speed. It turns riders upside down 2 times. Its steepest drop is 90°, straight down.

What riding Matugani is like

Matugani pairs a 78.7 ft structure with 1,443.5 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. At 90°, the steepest drop is close to vertical. It goes upside down 2 times across 1,443.5 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2023, it is the 3rd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lost Island Theme Park. It is one of 9 rides on Coaster Map built to Intamin Amusement Rides's Accelerator Coaster design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Top Thrill 2 at Cedar Point, Storm Runner at Hersheypark, Xcelerator at Knott's and Rita at Alton Towers. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lost Island sets 52 in, while Hersheypark sets 54 in, Alton Towers sets 140 cm and Thorpe Park sets 140 cm.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Lost Island, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Nopuko Air Coaster, roughly 320 m away (52 in minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Matugani layout, trains and ride system

Matugani starts with a hydraulic launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

RCDB lists 4 track elements for Matugani, shown here in the order RCDB lists them:

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 2 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

Before you ride Matugani

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 52 in (132 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 2 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lost Island Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Matugani?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Matugani, according to Lost Island Theme Park, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Matugani?

Matugani is 78.7 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Matugani go?

Its top speed is 46.6 mph (75 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Matugani go upside down?

Yes. It has 2 inversions.

Is Matugani scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Matugani open?

Matugani opened on 20 May 2023.

Who built Matugani?

Matugani was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Matugani?

RCDB lists a ride time of 50 seconds over 1443.5 ft of track.

Does Matugani launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a hydraulic launch.

How many people ride Matugani at once?

Each train carries 16 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 930 riders.

Is Matugani good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 2 times; it tops out at 46.6 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Matugani operate before?

RCDB records it at Liseberg (as Kanonen).

Is Matugani at Lost Island the same as Xcelerator at Knott's?

Both are Intamin Amusement Rides Accelerator Coaster coasters on RCDB; the Lost Island ride opened in 2023 and the Knott's ride in 2002; both list a 52 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 78.7 ft and 205 ft.
