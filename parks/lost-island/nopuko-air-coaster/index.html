Nopuko Air Coaster

Lost Island Theme Park · Waterloo, Iowa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 52 in (132 cm); top speed 49.7 mph; 109.3 ft tall; 5 inversions; opened 2022.

Nopuko Air Coaster is a steel inverted roller coaster at Lost Island Theme Park in Waterloo, Iowa, built by Vekoma. It opened on 18 Jun 2022. The ride stands 109.3 ft tall, reaches 49.7 mph and runs 2509.8 ft of track. On an inverted coaster the seats hang below the track, so riders' feet dangle free with nothing beneath them. It turns riders upside down 5 times.

What riding Nopuko Air Coaster is like

Nopuko Air Coaster pairs a 109.3 ft structure with 2,509.8 ft of track, a classic proportion that gives a clear first drop and a full layout after it. It goes upside down 5 times across 2,509.8 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2022, it is the 2nd oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lost Island Theme Park. It is one of 2 rides on Coaster Map built to Vekoma's SLC / 765m Standard w/Helix design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Blue Tornado at Gardaland. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lost Island sets 52 in, while Gardaland sets 140 cm.

Its 52 in minimum is the strictest at Lost Island, shared with 1 other coaster. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is higher than is typical for its class.

The nearest other coaster is Matugani, roughly 320 m away (52 in minimum). Distances are straight-line estimates from map coordinates, not walking routes.

Nopuko Air Coaster layout, trains and ride system

The ride begins with a chain lift hill.

These are the 4 elements RCDB records on Nopuko Air Coaster's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Chain Lift Hill
  2. Roll Over
  3. Sidewinder
  4. Double In-Line Twist

RCDB counts 5 inversions in total; some elements, such as a cobra roll, turn riders over more than once.

Intense (extreme)

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 5 times. Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

On this style of coaster, the front row has nothing in your line of sight, and the outermost seats feel the most exposed. Riders after the strongest drops pick the back row, which is pulled over each crest faster.

Before you ride Nopuko Air Coaster

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Nopuko Air Coaster?

Riders must be at least 52 in (132 cm) tall to ride Nopuko Air Coaster, according to Lost Island Theme Park, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Nopuko Air Coaster?

Nopuko Air Coaster is 109.3 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Nopuko Air Coaster go?

Its top speed is 49.7 mph (80 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Nopuko Air Coaster go upside down?

Yes. It has 5 inversions.

Is Nopuko Air Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Nopuko Air Coaster open?

Nopuko Air Coaster opened on 18 Jun 2022.

Who built Nopuko Air Coaster?

Nopuko Air Coaster was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Nopuko Air Coaster?

RCDB lists a ride time of 1 minute 30 seconds over 2509.8 ft of track.

How does Nopuko Air Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Nopuko Air Coaster at once?

RCDB's train details give 20 seats per train. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 1,010 riders.

Is Nopuko Air Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 5 times; it tops out at 49.7 mph; the minimum height is 52 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Where did Nopuko Air Coaster operate before?

RCDB records it at Ratanga Junction (as Cobra).

Is Nopuko Air Coaster at Lost Island the same as Blue Tornado at Gardaland?

Both are Vekoma SLC / 765m Standard w/Helix coasters on RCDB; the Lost Island ride opened in 2022 and the Gardaland ride in 1998; Lost Island sets 52 in and Gardaland sets 140 cm.
