Fire Runner

Lost Island Theme Park · Waterloo, Iowa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 52 mph; 92 ft tall; 3 inversions; opened 2025.

Fire Runner is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lost Island Theme Park in Waterloo, Iowa, built by Rocky Mountain Coasters. It opened on 18 Jul 2025. The ride stands 92 ft tall, reaches 52 mph and runs 1,950 ft of track. Riders sit single file on a train that runs on one narrow rail. It turns riders upside down 3 times.

What riding Fire Runner is like

At 92 ft tall with 1,950 ft of track, Fire Runner balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns. It goes upside down 3 times across 1,950 ft of track, so inversions are spaced out between other elements.

Opened in 2025, it is the newest of the 4 coasters we list at Lost Island Theme Park. It is one of 3 rides on Coaster Map built to Rocky Mountain Coasters's Raptor Track / Stunt Pilot design, and the most recent of them to open. Others include Stunt Pilot at Silverwood and Sköll & Hati at Gyeongju World. Height rules for this model differ by park: Lost Island sets 48 in, while Gyeongju World sets 125 cm.

Its 48 in minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Lost Island, which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders happy to go upside down 3 times. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

For the strongest airtime, sit near the back: the cars ahead pull the back of the train over each crest faster, so you leave your seat sooner on the drops. For the best view of what is coming, sit in the front row.

How Fire Runner compares

On our intensity ranking Fire Runner is number 3 of 4 at Lost Island Theme Park, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 2nd tallest of the park's 4 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Lokolo (12.5 ft tall, no inversions). Ready for more? The next step up is Matugani (78.7 ft tall, 46.6 mph, 2 inversions; 52 in minimum).

Before you ride Fire Runner

  • Measure first: the ride-alone minimum is 48 in (122 cm). Height is checked at the entrance, so ask the ride team before you queue if your child is close.
  • Secure glasses, phones and loose items before the 3 inversions; the park posts its loose-article rules at the ride.
  • Opening times and ride status: check the Lost Island Theme Park app or official site on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Fire Runner?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Fire Runner, according to Lost Island Theme Park, official ride page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Fire Runner?

Fire Runner is 92 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Fire Runner go?

Its top speed is 52 mph (84 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Fire Runner go upside down?

Yes. It has 3 inversions.

Is Fire Runner scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Fire Runner open?

Fire Runner opened on 18 Jul 2025.

Who built Fire Runner?

Fire Runner was manufactured by Rocky Mountain Coasters.

How long is the track on Fire Runner?

RCDB lists 1,950 ft (594 m) of track.

How does Fire Runner get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Fire Runner at once?

RCDB's train details give 12 seats per train.

Is Fire Runner good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it goes upside down 3 times; it tops out at 52 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.

Is Fire Runner at Lost Island the same as Stunt Pilot at Silverwood?

Both are Rocky Mountain Coasters Raptor Track / Stunt Pilot coasters on RCDB; the Lost Island ride opened in 2025 and the Silverwood ride in 2021; both list a 48 in minimum; RCDB lists heights of 92 ft and 105 ft.
