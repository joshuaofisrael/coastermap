Lokolo

Lost Island Theme Park · Waterloo, Iowa

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

12.5 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Lokolo is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Lost Island Theme Park in Waterloo, Iowa, built by SBF Visa Group. It opened on 18 Jun 2022. The ride stands 12.5 ft tall and runs 452.8 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Lokolo is like

Lokolo spreads 452.8 ft of track across a layout only 12.5 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop.

Opened in 2022, it is the oldest of the 4 coasters we list at Lost Island Theme Park. It is one of 10 rides on Coaster Map built to SBF Visa Group's Family Coaster / MX48 design, and the 8th of them to open. Others include Frutti Loop Coaster at Energylandia, Wriggler at Gulliver's Land, Kid'z Coaster at Bagatelle and Family Coaster at Plohn.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Lokolo?

Lokolo is 12.5 ft tall, according to RCDB.

Does Lokolo go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Lokolo scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Lokolo open?

Lokolo opened on 18 Jun 2022.

Who built Lokolo?

Lokolo was manufactured by SBF Visa Group.

How long is the track on Lokolo?

RCDB lists 452.8 ft of track.

How does Lokolo get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Lokolo at once?

RCDB's train details give 24 seats per train.

Is Lokolo good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Lokolo at Lost Island the same as Family Coaster at Plohn?

Both are SBF Visa Group Family Coaster / MX48 coasters on RCDB; the Lost Island ride opened in 2022 and the Plohn ride in 2006.
