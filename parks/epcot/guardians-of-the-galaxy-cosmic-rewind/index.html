Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind

EPCOT · Lake Buena Vista, Florida

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 42 in (107 cm); top speed 60.3 mph; 0 inversions; opened 2022.

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind is a steel sit-down roller coaster at EPCOT in Lake Buena Vista, Florida, built by Vekoma. It opened on 27 May 2022. The ride reaches 60.3 mph and runs 5577.4 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. Most or all of the ride is indoors. The train has an onboard soundtrack. It does not turn riders upside down.

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind layout, trains and ride system

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch rather than relying only on gravity from a lift. It also uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 2 booster sections (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

These are the 5 elements RCDB records on Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind's track, in RCDB's order:

  1. Dark/Show Section
  2. Booster Wheel Lift Hill
  3. Dark/Show Section
  4. LSM Launch
  5. Booster Wheel Lift Hill

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind?

Riders must be at least 42 in (107 cm) tall to ride Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind, according to Walt Disney World, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How fast does Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind go?

Its top speed is 60.3 mph (97 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind open?

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind opened on 27 May 2022.

Who built Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind?

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind was manufactured by Vekoma.

How long is the ride on Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind?

RCDB lists a ride time of 3 minutes 10 seconds over 5577.4 ft of track.

Does Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch.

How many people ride Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind at once?

Each train carries 20 riders, according to RCDB. RCDB lists an hourly capacity of about 2,000 riders.

Is Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 60.3 mph; the minimum height is 42 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
