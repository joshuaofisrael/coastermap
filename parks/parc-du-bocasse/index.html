Parc du Bocasse height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Le Bocasse, France · 5 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Parc du Bocasse in Le Bocasse, France has 5 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 120 cm (about 47.2 in) on Speedy Gonzales to 130 cm (about 51.2 in) on Orochi. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 0, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 3 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Parc du Bocasse are like

RCDB classes 3 coasters here as kiddie or family, 2 as thrill and 0 as extreme, which makes Parc du Bocasse a family-leaning park for roller coasters. None of its coasters are classed as extreme, against 40% of coasters across all 217 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 47.2 in, lower than the 48 in typical across the site, and 2 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, none of the 5 go upside down.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Fort d'Odin, which opened in 2004.

Parc du Bocasse publishes its height rules in centimetres. We show the official centimetre figure with an inch conversion, and every list on this page compares in centimetres, so a child exactly at the line counts as tall enough.

What can my child ride at Parc du Bocasse? By height

At Parc du Bocasse the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Pirate's Coaster (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Speedy Gonzales at 120 cm (about 47.2 in).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Speedy Gonzales (100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in)), Jurassic Twister (105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in)), Pirate's Coaster (90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in)) and Fort d'Odin (105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in)). Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Orochi at 130 cm (about 51.2 in).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 3 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Speedy Gonzales, Jurassic Twister and Pirate's Coaster. With a companion they can also ride Orochi (120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in)) and Fort d'Odin (105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in)). That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 2 more than at the height below: Orochi and Fort d'Odin. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Orochi (up to 205 cm (about 80.7 in)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Companion rules at Parc du Bocasse

These are the rules Parc du Bocasse states on its official pages for riding with a companion or other conditions. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by the park; check the linked source for the exact wording.

  • Speedy Gonzales: riders 100 to 120 cm (about 39.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Jurassic Twister: riders 105 to 120 cm (about 41.3 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Pirate's Coaster: riders 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Orochi: riders 120 to 130 cm (about 47.2 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.
  • Fort d'Odin: riders 105 to 130 cm (about 41.3 to 51.2 in) tall must ride with a companion.

Best coasters at Parc du Bocasse for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Parc du Bocasse, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Orochi, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  2. Step up: Fort d'Odin, a bigger ride that stops short of the park's most extreme coasters (130 cm minimum; no inversions).
  3. The big one: Jurassic Twister, the most intense coaster in the park (120 cm minimum; 42.7 ft tall, 29.1 mph, no inversions, 2.5 g).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
