Pirate's Coaster

Parc du Bocasse · Le Bocasse, France

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 11 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 120 cm (about 47.2 in), or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion; top speed 12.3 mph; 10.8 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2019.

Pirate's Coaster is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Parc du Bocasse in Le Bocasse, France, built by Preston & Barbieri. It opened on 7 Jul 2019. The ride stands 10.8 ft tall, reaches 12.3 mph and runs 176.5 ft of track. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Pirate's Coaster is like

At 10.8 ft tall with 176.5 ft of track, Pirate's Coaster balances height and length: a main drop followed by a full circuit of hills and turns.

Opened in 2019, it is the 4th oldest of the 5 coasters we list at Parc du Bocasse. It is one of 4 rides on Coaster Map built to Preston & Barbieri's Mini Coaster / Mine Vagon design, and the 3rd of them to open. Others include Stampbanan at Liseberg, Der kleine Zar at Hansa-Park and Little Nipper at Luna Park Sydney. Height rules for this model differ by park: Bocasse sets 120 cm, while Liseberg sets 110 cm and Luna Park Sydney sets 132 cm.

Its 120 cm minimum is the lowest of any coaster at Bocasse (tied with 2 others), which makes it a natural first coaster there. Among family-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 47 in (120 cm), so this rule is about typical for its class. Children from 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) can ride with a companion, so a family can often ride together before a child is old enough to ride alone.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Pirate's Coaster sits on the west side of Bocasse's coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Speedy Gonzales, roughly 210 m away (120 cm minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Jurassic Twister at about 250 m. Pirate's Coaster layout, trains and ride system

To gain height it uses a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels. RCDB also lists 1 booster section (Booster Wheel Lift Hill) that add speed mid-course.

Who is it for?

It suits young riders taking their first coasters and adults riding with them and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion.

How Pirate's Coaster compares

On our intensity ranking Pirate's Coaster is number 4 of 5 at Parc du Bocasse, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 3rd tallest of the park's 3 coasters with a listed height.

If it sounds like too much, the next gentler coaster here is Orochi (no inversions; 130 cm minimum). Ready for more? The next step up is Speedy Gonzales (19.7 ft tall, 21.3 mph, no inversions; 120 cm minimum).

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Pirate's Coaster?

Riders must be at least 120 cm (about 47.2 in) tall to ride Pirate's Coaster, or 90 to 120 cm (about 35.4 to 47.2 in) with a companion, according to Parc du Bocasse, official attraction page (checked 11 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Pirate's Coaster?

Pirate's Coaster is 10.8 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Pirate's Coaster go?

Its top speed is 12.3 mph (20 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Pirate's Coaster go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Pirate's Coaster scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a family coaster. It is built for families.

When did Pirate's Coaster open?

Pirate's Coaster opened on 7 Jul 2019.

Who built Pirate's Coaster?

Pirate's Coaster was manufactured by Preston & Barbieri.

How long is the track on Pirate's Coaster?

RCDB lists 176.5 ft of track.

How does Pirate's Coaster get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a lift hill driven by rubber booster wheels; it has no launch.

Is Pirate's Coaster good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as family; it has no inversions; it tops out at 12.3 mph; the minimum height is 120 cm. Those are the marks of a first-coaster ride: a gentle class and no inversions.

Is Pirate's Coaster at Bocasse the same as Stampbanan at Liseberg?

Both are Preston & Barbieri Mini Coaster / Mine Vagon coasters on RCDB; the Bocasse ride opened in 2019 and the Liseberg ride in 2013; Bocasse sets 120 cm and Liseberg sets 110 cm.
