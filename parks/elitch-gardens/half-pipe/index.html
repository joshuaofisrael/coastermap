Half Pipe

Elitch Gardens · Denver, Colorado

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

top speed 43.5 mph; 98.4 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 2004.

Half Pipe is a steel sit-down roller coaster at Elitch Gardens in Denver, Colorado, built by Intamin Amusement Rides. It opened on 27 May 2004. The ride stands 98.4 ft tall, reaches 43.5 mph and runs 229.7 ft of track. The cars can spin freely as they travel, so no two rides feel quite the same. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Half Pipe is like

For a 98.4 ft ride, 229.7 ft of track is short: Half Pipe is compact and vertical, with little time between its main elements. RCDB lists a peak force of 4.5 g, above the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 2004, it is the 5th oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Elitch Gardens.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Half Pipe sits on the south-west side of Elitch Gardens' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Boomerang, roughly 50 m away (48 in minimum), so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Twister III: Storm Chaser at about 130 m. Half Pipe layout, trains and ride system

Half Pipe starts with a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once rather than relying only on gravity from a lift.

Moderate (thrill)

Who is it for?

It suits riders who enjoy speed and turns but may not want the biggest drops in the park and riders who prefer not to go upside down.

How Half Pipe compares

On our intensity ranking Half Pipe is number 5 of 6 at Elitch Gardens, where 1 is the most intense. It is the 4th tallest of the park's 5 coasters with a listed height.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is Half Pipe?

Half Pipe is 98.4 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Half Pipe go?

Its top speed is 43.5 mph (70 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Half Pipe go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Half Pipe scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as a thrill coaster. Expect a lively ride that stops short of the most extreme coasters.

When did Half Pipe open?

Half Pipe opened on 27 May 2004.

Who built Half Pipe?

Half Pipe was manufactured by Intamin Amusement Rides.

How long is the ride on Half Pipe?

RCDB lists a ride time of 2 minutes over 229.7 ft of track.

Does Half Pipe launch?

Yes. RCDB lists a linear synchronous motor (LSM) launch that the train passes through more than once.

Is Half Pipe good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as thrill; it has no inversions; it tops out at 43.5 mph. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
