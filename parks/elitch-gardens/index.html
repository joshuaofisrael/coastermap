Elitch Gardens height requirements and roller coasters ranked

Denver, Colorado · 6 coasters

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Elitch Gardens in Denver, Colorado has 6 operating roller coasters on RCDB. Height requirements run from 42 in (107 cm) on Blazin' Buckaroo to 52 in (132 cm) on Mind Eraser. Riding alone, a 42-inch (107 cm) child can ride 1, a 48-inch (122 cm) child 4 and a 54-inch (137 cm) rider 5 of them.

What the coasters at Elitch Gardens are like

RCDB classes 1 coaster here as kiddie or family, 1 as thrill and 4 as extreme, which makes Elitch Gardens a thrill-heavy park for roller coasters. Extreme coasters make up 67% of its lineup, against 43% across all 159 parks on Coaster Map.

The typical (median) height requirement here is about 48 in, about the same as the 48 in typical across the site, and 4 of the 5 coasters with a verified rule need a rider of at least 48 in (122 cm).

Of the ride types, 3 of the 6 go upside down; 1 is a wooden coaster (Twister III: Storm Chaser); 1 uses a launch instead of a lift hill (Sidewinder).

The most common builder is Vekoma, with 2 of the coasters.

The longest-serving coaster in the lineup is Sidewinder, which opened in 1995.

What can my child ride at Elitch Gardens? By height

At Elitch Gardens the biggest single step is reaching 48 in (122 cm), which opens up 3 more coasters than the height below. Measure at home in the shoes your child will wear, since parks measure at the ride entrance and a child even slightly under the line is turned away.

36 in (91 cm) tall

A 36-inch rider is below the ride-alone minimum on every coaster here. With a companion they can also ride Blazin' Buckaroo (36 to 42 in (91 to 107 cm)). Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Sidewinder at 48 in (122 cm).

42 in (107 cm) tall

A 42-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 1 of 5 coasters with a verified rule: Blazin' Buckaroo. Still too short for 4 coasters, the nearest being Sidewinder at 48 in (122 cm).

48 in (122 cm) tall

A 48-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 4 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 3 more than at the height below: Sidewinder, Twister III: Storm Chaser and Boomerang. Still too short for 1 coaster, the nearest being Mind Eraser at 52 in (132 cm).

54 in (137 cm) tall

A 54-inch rider meets the ride-alone minimum on 5 of 5 coasters with a verified rule, 1 more than at the height below: Mind Eraser. That covers every coaster with a verified rule here.

Maximum heights

Some rides are built for children and have an upper limit: Mind Eraser (up to 80 in (203 cm)). Taller riders are turned away for restraint fit, not age.

Best coasters at Elitch Gardens for first-timers

Nervous riders usually do best working up in steps. Here is a three-step plan for Elitch Gardens, picked from our intensity ranking and the official height rules:

  1. Start here: Blazin' Buckaroo, the gentlest coaster here by our intensity ranking, with no inversions (42 in minimum; no inversions).
  2. The big one: Mind Eraser, the most intense coaster in the park (52 in minimum; 109.3 ft tall, 49.7 mph, 5 inversions).

Sit in the middle of the train for the softest ride, and watch a few cycles from the queue first. Our best first roller coasters guide has more tips.
