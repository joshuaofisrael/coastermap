Twister III: Storm Chaser

Elitch Gardens · Denver, Colorado

By the Coaster Map editorial team · Last verified 10 Nov 2026 · How we verify

Height requirement: 48 in (122 cm); top speed 55 mph; 100 ft tall; 0 inversions; opened 1995.

Twister III: Storm Chaser is a wood sit-down roller coaster at Elitch Gardens in Denver, Colorado, built by Hensel Phelps Construction Co. It first opened on 27 May 1995 and was previously known as Twister II. The ride stands 100 ft tall, reaches 55 mph and runs 4,640 ft of track. It is a wooden coaster, with a wooden track structure. It does not turn riders upside down.

What riding Twister III: Storm Chaser is like

Twister III: Storm Chaser spreads 4,640 ft of track across a layout only 100 ft tall, so expect a ride that keeps moving through turns and hills instead of saving everything for one drop. RCDB lists a peak force of 3.1 g, below the 4 g median for coasters on Coaster Map with a g-force figure.

Opened in 1995, it is the 2nd oldest of the 6 coasters we list at Elitch Gardens. It has run under other names (Twister II), so older guides and photos may use those.

A rider who just meets its 48 in minimum can also ride 3 other coasters at Elitch Gardens, out of 4 with verified rules. Among extreme-class coasters on Coaster Map the median ride-alone minimum is about 51 in (130 cm), so this rule is lower than is typical for its class.

Going by RCDB's map positions, Twister III: Storm Chaser sits on the south-west side of Elitch Gardens' coaster line-up. The nearest other coaster is Half Pipe, roughly 130 m away, so the two are easy to ride back to back. Next closest is Boomerang at about 170 m. How intense is it?

Intense (extreme)

RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster.

Who is it for?

It suits confident thrill seekers who are comfortable with big forces and riders who prefer not to go upside down. Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall.

Best seat to ride

The back rows get pulled over each hill at speed, which gives the sharpest drops and most airtime; the front row trades some of that for a clear view of the track ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the height requirement for Twister III: Storm Chaser?

Riders must be at least 48 in (122 cm) tall to ride Twister III: Storm Chaser, according to Elitch Gardens, official attraction page (checked 10 Nov 2026). Requirements can change, so confirm at the ride entrance.

How tall is Twister III: Storm Chaser?

Twister III: Storm Chaser is 100 ft tall, according to RCDB.

How fast does Twister III: Storm Chaser go?

Its top speed is 55 mph (88 km/h), according to RCDB.

Does Twister III: Storm Chaser go upside down?

No. It has no inversions.

Is Twister III: Storm Chaser scary?

It depends on the rider, but on intensity: RCDB classes it as an extreme coaster. That is RCDB's most intense class.

When did Twister III: Storm Chaser open?

Twister III: Storm Chaser opened on 27 May 1995 (previously known as Twister II).

Who built Twister III: Storm Chaser?

Twister III: Storm Chaser was manufactured by Hensel Phelps Construction Co.

How long is the track on Twister III: Storm Chaser?

RCDB lists 4,640 ft (1,414 m) of track.

How does Twister III: Storm Chaser get up its first hill?

RCDB lists a chain lift hill; it has no launch.

How many people ride Twister III: Storm Chaser at once?

Up to 18 per train, going by RCDB's train layout.

Is Twister III: Storm Chaser good for first-time riders?

On the numbers: RCDB classes it as extreme; it has no inversions; it tops out at 55 mph; the minimum height is 48 in. Most first-timers start with a family-class ride with no inversions, so this is better as a step up than a first coaster.
