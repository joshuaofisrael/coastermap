Ride rules change: check with the park

Height requirements, maximum heights, companion rules, health and accessibility restrictions, opening dates and ride status are set by each park and can change without notice. The figures on this site were correct on the source page on the date shown, but the park's posted rules and ride staff always take priority. Check the park's official website or app before your visit and confirm at the ride entrance.

No safety or medical advice

Nothing on this site is safety, medical or professional advice. Descriptions of intensity, "who it suits" and seat suggestions are general opinions to help planning. Riders with health conditions, pregnancy, injuries or other concerns should follow the park's posted warnings and consult a doctor.
