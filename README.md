# Coaster Map (coastermap.com)

Roller coasters at the big theme parks, mapped and explained: height requirements (official park pages), stats (RCDB), intensity, who it suits, seat guidance, park maps (Leaflet + OSM), a ride height checker and a coaster quiz.

- Repo: https://github.com/joshuaofisrael/coastermap  · Hosting: GitHub Pages, branch `main`, folder `/`, custom domain coastermap.com (CNAME file).
- Build: `python3 _build/build.py` then `python3 _build/check.py` (JSON-LD, beacon, legal line, broken links, duplicate titles). Commit the built HTML.
- Data (all in `_build/`):
  - `rcdb.json`: RCDB scrape (10 Nov 2026) for every park: operating coasters, stats, coordinates, former names. Scraper: `scrape_new.py` (park slug to RCDB id map in `NEW`; run `python3 scrape_new.py slug...`, caches raw pages in /workspace/coastermap-data/raw/).
  - `sf_heights.json`: sixflags.com heights for the original Cedar Point and Magic Mountain, keyed by ride name.
  - `sf_heights_new.json`: heights scraped by `sf_heights_new.py` from sixflags.com ride pages and Enchanted Parks ride pages (Worlds of Fun, Valleyfair, Michigan's Adventure, Mid America Adventure), keyed `park-slug|RCDB name`; `sf_extra_slugs.json` holds slug overrides. After a run, clear acc_min when it equals min.
  - `data.py`: `PARKS` (slug, names, location, official URL, group, optional `former` park name), `ORIGINAL` (launch parks, which use `sf_heights.json` and `H`), `DISPLAY` / `DISPLAY_PARK` name overrides, `H` (launch-park heights) and `HP2` (expansion-park heights keyed (park slug, RCDB name)), each with its official source tuple. Lookup order: sf_heights_new (non-original parks), then HP2, then H.
  - Height model: `min` = ride alone, `acc_min`/`acc_max` = companion range, `max` = maximum, `thrill` = park's 1 to 5 rating, optional `unit="cm"` when the park's official rule is metric (stored in cm, shown as cm with inches; the height checker compares in the rule's own unit).
  - Never add an unsourced number; if a rule cannot be verified on an official page, leave it out (the page then says "check park").

- Build modules, exec'd by build.py in this order: `_build/guides.py` (height, record, region and type guides, plus the guides hub), `_build/parkguide.py` (park height-requirement guides and coaster comparisons), `_build/explainers.py` (evergreen rider-question pages and manufacturer pages, called from `build_guides()` before the hub).
  - `coasterplus.py` (exec'd last): per-coaster uniqueness. `feel_html()` writes the "What riding X is like" section from RCDB layout stats (length-to-height ratio, drop vs height, launch speed vs free-fall speed, drop angle, inversion density, g-force vs the site median, age rank at the park, former names, same-model family and how its height rules differ by park, height-rule context at the park and in its intensity class). `where_html()` adds the compass side of the park and the nearest coasters from RCDB coordinates (straight-line, with a disclaimer). `clone_faqs()` answers "Is X the same as Y?" for same-model rides. `_pick()` chooses one of several phrasings per page from an md5 of park+slug so wording varies but stays stable between builds.
  - Audits (outside the repo, in /workspace/audit): `dup.py` (5-word shingle near-duplicate check over coaster `<article>` text; reports max pairwise Jaccard) and `seo.py` (title/description uniqueness and length, canonical, one h1, JSON-LD parse, sitemap coverage, orphans and click depth from the home page). Run both after big template changes.
  - Meta descriptions are clipped to 158 characters by `clip_desc()` in `page()`.
  - `explainers.py`: `qpage()` renders a page with a quick-answer box, FAQ schema, related links and a sources list. Pages: fear tips, stomach drop / g-forces, airtime, how coasters work, staying on the track, launches, brakes, restraints, best seat, height requirements explained, too tall (max heights), stuck coasters, motion sickness, health conditions, world records, glossary, and the manufacturers hub plus one page per maker in `MAKERS` (HQ and founding year from RCDB company pages).
  - World records (`REC_SPEED`, `REC_HEIGHT` and the other records) are hand-copied from https://rcdb.com/rhr.htm (`?m=1` height, `2` length, `3` drop, `4` inversions, `5` angle). Re-check that page and update the constants when re-verifying.
  - Health and motion sickness pages may only quote official park rules (Universal Orlando rider guide PDF) and government guidance (CDC), framed as general information. Do not add medical claims without such a source.
  - Coaster pages link to their manufacturer page, the best-seat page and, where relevant, the launch, max-height and motion-sickness pages (`related_html` in build.py).
- Wording must stay evergreen: no "this year", "currently", "new for", prices, wait times or events. Only the "last verified" date marks time.
- Region guides: `STATES` and `COUNTRIES` in guides.py (built when at least 2 parks); `REGION` maps country to continent for the record leaders.
