Ride height checker: which coasters can my child ride?

Enter your child's height and pick a park. We'll list every coaster they are tall enough for, the ones they can ride with a companion, and the ones to save for next year. Height rules come from official park pages, checked 10 Nov 2026.

Always confirm at the ride. Parks measure at the entrance, rules change, and a ride can refuse anyone for safety reasons. Coasters with no verified requirement are listed separately so you can ask the park.

How height requirements work

Each coaster has a minimum height set by the park and the ride maker. Some family coasters let shorter children ride with a companion (an adult or older rider), and a few kiddie coasters also have a maximum height. Our checker uses only those official numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Where is height checked?

At the ride entrance, by the park's ride team. Rules on footwear and re-measuring vary by park, so ask the ride team if your child is close to the line.

Where do these height requirements come from?

From each park's official ride pages, ride guides or FAQs (for Universal Orlando parks, Visit Orlando's official coaster guide), all checked 10 Nov 2026. Each coaster page links its exact source, and our sources and methods page lists them all.

Does the checker work in centimetres?

Yes. Choose cm or inches. Parks in Europe and Asia publish rules in centimetres, and the checker compares your child's height against that official centimetre figure, so a rider exactly at the line is shown as able to ride in either unit.

What does ride with a companion mean?

Some family coasters let a shorter child ride if an adult or older rider sits with them. The checker lists these separately, with the companion height range the park gives. Who counts as a companion (age or height) is set by each park.
